**Siberia** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in Northern Asia. It may be released after researching Pan-nationalism, while in control of its capital and three other core states. At the start date, most of its core states are controlled by [Russia](Russia.md), with [China](China.md) controlling Altai and Tuva.

## 核心州

Siberia has 18 core states, comprising the entire Siberia region and three states in the Northeast Asia region.
