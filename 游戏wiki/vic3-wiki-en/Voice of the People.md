**Voice of the People** is the first immersion pack for *Victoria 3*. It was announced on 2023-04-20[1] and was released alongside [patch 1.3](Patch 1.3.md) on 2023-05-22.[2]

## Game features

Relive the dramatic political events of the 19th century. Drawing from the historical struggles between reformists, reactionaries and revolutionaries, *Voice of the People* puts you in the middle of political crises as charismatic characters from history vigorously push their agendas.

**Over 60 New Historical Agitators**

- From the fiery American abolitionist John Brown to the German Socialist Rosa Luxembourg and the determined Italian nation-builder Giuseppe Mazzini.
- The new Agitator system brings history to life with over 60 new real world characters fighting for their beliefs in your nation.
- Famous historical figures may appear in your country to build their own power base and promote changes to help or hinder your current plans.

**Special Agitator Interactions**

- Co-opt Agitators into your state by appointing them leaders of an interest group or letting them command troops.
- Exile the troublemakers who are wrecking your stable country.
- Invite other people's exiles to promote their agendas in your more welcoming society, potentially giving you a leg up in pushing advanced reforms.

**The Dramatic History of France**

- From the House of Orleans, through the Second Bonaparte Empire to the Third Republic, the history of [France](France.md) in the time of Victoria 3 is one of tumult and adventure.
- Watch this exciting history spring into vivid color with a host of new events, decisions and journal entries that tell the story of one of the century's most powerful and pivotal empires.
- Advance French claims in North Africa, choose the true king of France or advance an aggressive policy on the continent.

**New Art**

- A new paper map puts you in the drawing rooms of power, as you plan your industrial and military expansion, wrapped in a new art nouveau inspired UI.
- France gets new building art.
- Revolutionary interest groups get unique on-map animations to underline which groups are threatening your regime.
- Many characters and cultures get new uniforms and clothing to better reflect the era.
