*"Van Diemen's Land is the primary penal colony in British Australia. What does the future hold for this colony of convicts?"*

**Tasmania**, called *Van Diemen's Land* while a colony of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), is an insignificant power located in Oceania. Tasmania is able to form [Australia](Australia.md).

## Scripted content

As an Australian country, Tasmania receives the journal entry and decision both named *Federate Australia*.

### Interest groups

The Armed Forces are in government. The Anglican Church, Rural Folk and Squattocracy are in opposition. The Petite Bourgeoisie, Intelligentsia, Trade Unions, and Industrialists are marginalized.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.12. |

Tasmania has the Peasant Levies law.

Tasmania starts with one Battalion and can call on no Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.
