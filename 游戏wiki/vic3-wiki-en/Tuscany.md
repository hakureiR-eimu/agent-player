**Tuscany** is an minor power in northern Italy. It is able to form [Italy](Italy.md).

## Formation

If it ceases to exist, Tuscany can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country controlling its capital and only core state of Tuscany.

### Interest groups

Tuscany begins with the Intelligentsia in government. The Landowners, Catholic Church, Rural Folk, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Armed Forces are in opposition. The Industrialists and Trade Unions are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Tuscany starts in the *Metternich System* power bloc, lead by [Austria](Austria.md). Austria also guarantees Tuscany's independence and Tuscany and Austria has mutual trade priviliges with the [Diplomatic Treaty](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Diplomatic_Treaty) *Treaty of Reciprocity*.

Tuscany has both a pro-country lobby () and anti-country lobby () towards Austria.

Due to its minute nature, Tuscany may be better served as a springboard to other Italian powers. Once the [Risorgimento](Italy.md) journal entry is active, create radicals of Italian culture, either via conquest or frequent changes of government. Do this until you get the event which changes Tuscany to a republican government. Continue radicalization and an event will fire, allowing another Italian power to annex Tuscany.

### Immediately after forming Italy

When Italy forms, especially when done so diplomatically, it is likely that your country will grow in size and population 4-6 fold. It is therefore *very important* to pause the game and thoroughly analyze the new situation.

There are likely going to be problems with market shortages, sub-optimal production methods, [infrastructure](Infrastructure.md) shortages, insufficient administration and an amount of [construction sectors](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Construction_sectors) that is either woefully insufficient or way too expensive.

Especially the state of Emilia has to be looked at, since it has come from three separate independent states, and is thus likely to be in a very strange state.
