**Government type** of a country depends on several factors, with the most important ones being the [Governance Principles and the Distribution of Power laws.](Power structure laws.md)

Government type determines country [ruler](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Ruler)'s title and how they are chosen. There are four possible types of transfer of power:

- Hereditary: the [heir](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Heir) inherits the throne when the ruler dies.
- Dictatorial: the leader of the [interest group](Interest group.md) with the highest clout becomes the ruler when the previous ruler dies.
- Presidential elective: the leader of the interest group with the highest clout within the winning party becomes the ruler at the end of every [election](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Election).
- Parliamentary elective: the leader of the interest group in [government](Government.md) with the highest clout is the ruler. Can change when the government is reformed.

Conditions for government types often match conditions for dynamic [country names](Dynamic country names.md), [map colors](Dynamic map colors.md), and [flags](List of flags.md). Some conditions for those dynamic elements also depend on having a certain government type.

## Government types

In the following tables, the first valid option is used (with default sorting). The tables below generally collapse government types with the same name and requirements but differing ruler titles. Ruler titles for female rulers are typically the same as the male title or the conventional counterpart; some government types do not have a title for female rulers.

### Monarchies

Most monarchy government types are not valid for Crown Land subjects. Monarchies can go into regencies, in which case the ruler is titled *Regent* and heir receives the appropriate ruler title. Some monarchy government types do not have a specific regency type and use a generic type instead.

The unique ruler titles tied to these conditions are kept if the country enacts Social Monarchy, but the government name is in *all* cases changed to *Social Monarchy.*
