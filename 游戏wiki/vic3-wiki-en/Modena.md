*"The Duchy of Modena is both ruled by, and politically subservient to, the Hapsburgs and the Austrian Empire. Will Modena shake off these chains of family loyalty, or do the ties that bind restrict too much?"*

**Modena** is an insignificant power in northern Italy. It is able to form [Italy](Italy.md)

### Interest groups

Modena begins with the Landowners in government. The Catholic Church, Intelligentsia, Rural Folk, and Armed Forces are in opposition, while the Petite Bourgeoisie, Trade Unions and Industrialists are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Modena starts in the *Metternich System* power bloc, lead by [Austria](Austria.md). Austria also guarantees Modena's independence.

Modena has both a pro-country lobby () and anti-country lobby () towards [Austria](Austria.md).

## Military

Modena has the Professional Army law.

Modena begins with four Battalions and can call upon two Conscripts, using No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| Esercito del Ducato di Modena e Reggio | 3 Line Infantry 1 Dragoon | — | Italy |

Modena has no flotillas at the start of the game.
