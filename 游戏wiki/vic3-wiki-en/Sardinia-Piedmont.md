**Sardinia-Piedmont** is a minor power in Italy. It borders [France](France.md) to the west, [Switzerland](Switzerland.md) to the north and [Austria](Austria.md), [Modena](Modena.md), and [Parma](Parma.md) to the east. It is the most technologically advanced nations on the Italian peninsula and is just beginning to tap into its vast industrial potential.

Sardinia-Piedmont is a prime candidate for unifying [Italy](Italy.md), which can be formed after all Italian countries have researched Nationalism.

## Historical background

After the Napoleonic Wars, Sardinia-Piedmont was troubled by political instability under many governments. Despite efforts to industrialize beginning in the 1830s, Sardinia-Piedmont was still a nation of old, with the nobility protected and the church holding considerable political power. The *Statuto Albertino*, a liberal constitution ratified in 1848, shaped Sardinian-Piedmontese politics for decades to come, taking inspiration from [France](France.md) and its style of constitutional and centralized monarchy.

### Industry and Resources

**Industry**

Almost all [industry buildings](Building.md) in Sardinia-Piedmont are located in the capital state, Piedmont. Piedmont accounts for 50% of the country's total GDP at game start. The only industry building not located in Piedmont, is single level of Shipyards in Sardinia.

**Resources**

Sardinia-Piedmont has the option to build Silk Plantations in Provence, thus being able to supply its own silk for luxury clothes. Other natural resources available to Sardinia-Piedmont include coal, lead, sulfur, and iron, as well as fish.

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.12. |

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Sardinia-Piedmont
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
You may wonder: why play Sardinia-Piedmont instead of the bigger [Two Sicilies](Two Sicilies.md)? The answer is that Sardinia-Piedmont is the most [technologically advanced](Technology.md) and politically progressive Italian minor at game start. With help from [France](France.md), you can rival [Austria](Austria.md) to take back your land in Lombardy and Venetia. First off, Sardinia starts in a bad spot with a low GDP at the start of the game.

### Economy

In the beginning of the game, the state of Piedmont is by far the most important and is where almost all of your initial construction will take place due to being the largest population, the only state with on the [Po River](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Po_River), having starting industry and having almost all the mining resources industry needs. Outside of sulfur there is really only reason to invest anywhere else, due to potential issues with [infrastructure](Infrastructure.md). Initially S-P has enough cheap iron to run the constructions sectors on iron-frame construction PM. Once you research Atmospheric Engine, you want to build some coal mines. Getting Railways early is a priority.

### Military and conquest

Sardinia-Piedmont has a decent army, but the Navy is very subpar to what a coastal nation of this power should be, so intercontinental invasions are difficult without an established front, before significant investment has been made in a proper navy. Initially the nearby Italian minors are guaranteed by [Austria](Austria.md) making attacking them difficult, and generally not worth it, until the formation of Italy is possible. This limits the potential targets quite a bit. [Lucca](Lucca.md) is not, however, and they will probably back down if you demand that they become a protectorate. [Switzerland](Switzerland.md) starts the game without allies or protectors, and one can either Puppet them or annex one of their two states for a quick boost in power. Attacking [Denmark](Denmark.md) for their colony in Ghana, and expanding in West Africa from there is possible despite S-P's initial lackluster navy, since Denmark does not station any troops in Ghana at the beginning of the game.

### Forming Italy

S-P can form Italy without any warfare, if it manages to outpace [Two Sicilies](Two Sicilies.md) and get a better rank than them, and then maintain good relations with everyone. All the independent countries as states enough between them.

Alternatively, one can keep a close eye on the Sicilian-Austrian relations, and attack if Two Sicilies stops being in Austria's power bloc.

#### Immediately after formation

When Italy forms, especially when done so diplomatically, it is likely that your country will grow in size and population 4-6 fold. It is therefore *very important* to pause the game and thoroughly analyze the new situation.

There are likely going to be problems with market shortages, sub-optimal production methods, [infrastructure](Infrastructure.md) shortages, insufficient administration and an amount of [construction sectors](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Construction_sectors) that is either woefully insufficient or way too expensive.

Especially the state of Emilia has to be looked at, since it has come from three separate independent states, and is thus likely to be in a very strange state.

### France's natural borders

|  | Available only with the [Voice of the People](Voice of the People.md) DLC enabled. |
|---|---|

If France activates the [journal entry](French content.md), it will receive a claim on Savoy. Needless to say, this makes France dangerous. It is advised to seek an ally or defensive pact with a GP rival of France (usually [Great Britain](Great Britain.md)).

As Savoy is not particular valuable, one could decide to take inspiration from historic events, and simply give Savoy and Nice away for an obligation via the [The Treaty of Turin](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/The_Treaty_of_Turin), and in turn use that to attack Austria for the much more valuable Lombardy. This will also remove Savoy from being an Italian homeland, and will thus not impact the possibility of forming Italy.
