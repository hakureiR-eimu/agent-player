All [characters](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Characters) have an individual ideology. When a character leads an interest group, this ideology adds to – and may override – that interest group's default stances on [laws](Laws.md). The ruler's ideology also provides support or opposition to law changes.

## List of character ideologies

When characters are created, they can receive a weighted random valid ideology or a preset ideology. Each [interest group](Interest group.md) has a list of valid character ideologies, which is further supplemented by [political movements](Political movement.md). This applies in addition to the specific requirements of each individual ideology. Characters defined with preset ideologies ignore the requirements and weights.

The chance for an ideology to be selected is equal to its weight divided by the total weight of all valid ideologies.

### Ideology restrictions

See also:
Ideology content
Certain journal entries and event chains can strongly reduce the potential ideologies for characters to only the related ideologies of that entry. These apply only to the interest group which is specifically affected by these entries or events.[5]

Specifically:

*The Spectre Haunting the World* restricts potential ideologies to just Anarchist, Communist, and Vanguardist ideologies;

Completion of the journal entry *The People's Revolution* restricts potential Trade Unions ideologies to a choice of just Anarchist, Communist, or Vanguardist ideology;

*The State and Revolution* journal event, *The Vanguard Advances*, restricts potential Armed Forces, Intelligentsia, or Rural Folk ideologies to Vanguardist ideology. The event can happen once for each interest group;

*Nation Above Self* journal event, *Night of the Long Knives*, restricts potential ideologies to just Fascist and Integralist ideologies;

*Nation Above Self* journal event, *Joining Our Cause*, restricts potential ideologies either to just Fascist and Integralist ideologies, or else to Ethno-Nationalist, Fascist, Integralist, Traditionalist, and Minoritarian Traditionalist ideologies. The event can repeat up to 3 times, once per interest group (if [Brazil](Brazil.md), Industrialists and Trade Unions cannot be affected).

Completion of the journal entry *Our Nation, Our Culture* restricts potential ideologies either to just Ethno-Nationalist ideology, or else to just Ethno-Nationalist, Fascist, Integralist, Traditionalist, and Minoritarian Traditionalist ideologies;

Newly generated characters draw from the pool of valid ideologies for their affiliated interest group. This includes the following ideologies, plus ideologies from [political movements](Political movement.md) that pressure the interest group.

Each ideology must still be valid individually to be selected; for example, Industrialists and Petite Bourgeoisie cannot generate a Protectionist until the country researches International Trade. The chance of an individual ideology is equal to its weight divided by the total weight of valid ideologies.
