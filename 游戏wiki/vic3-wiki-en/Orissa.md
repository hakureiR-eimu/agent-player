**Orissa** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in eastern India.

Orissa can form [India](India.md) (BHT).

## Formation

Orissa can be formed by the following countries when it fully the core state, Orissa.

| Country | Capital | Primary Cultures | State Religion | Tier | Type | Core states |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Jeypore** | Northern Circars | Oria | Hindu | Principality | Unrecognized | Split state in Northern Circars |
| **Mayurbhanj** | Orissa | Oria | Hindu | Principality | Unrecognized | Split state in Orissa |
| **Narsinghpur** | Orissa | Oria | Hindu | Principality | Unrecognized | Split state in Orissa |
| **Patna** | Orissa | Oria | Hindu | Principality | Unrecognized | Split state in Orissa |

All of the above exist at start and may be [released](Releasable countries.md) if they cease to exist. Orissa may also be released by any country that controls the state of Orissa

## Oria minors

The Oria minors are all unrecognized powers and have similar basic stats, starting technology, and politics. Each except Jeypore starts with a split state in Orissa and each is a puppet of [East India](East India.md). They are all Principalities and Hindu. They generally start with the Zamindars in government with the Hindu Priesthood, Armed Forces, Intelligentsia, and Ryots in opposition, and the remaining interest groups marginalized.

The Hindu Priesthood has the Hindu Moralist ideology instead of the default Moralist. The Petite Bourgeoisie has the Modernizer ideology instead of the default Reactionary. The Intelligentsia has the Constitutionalist ideology instead of the default Republican.

They all start with the following technology and laws:
