**Two Sicilies** is a minor power that controls the southern half of the Italian peninsula. It is surrounded by the Mediterranean Sea to the east, south and west. To the north, it borders the [Papal States](Papal States.md).

Two Sicilies is the largest and most powerful Italian state and is thus a prime candidate for unifying [Italy](Italy.md), which can be formed after Nationalism has been researched.

If Two Sicilies loses part of the [state region](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/State_region) of Campania while retaining all of Sicily, it will be renamed *Sicily*; on the other hand, if it loses part of Sicily while retaining all of Campania, it will be called *Naples*.

## Historical background

The Kingdom of the Two Sicilies was originally founded as the *Kingdom of Sicily* following the Norman conquest of the region in the 11th and 12th centuries. Following a revolt at the end of the 13th century, the island of Sicily became a separate kingdom under the Crown of Aragon, and the remaining peninsular kingdom was colloquially known as the *Kingdom of Naples*, but officially remained the Kingdom of Sicily. In the mid-15th century, this kingdom, too, came under the control of Aragon, and later Spain.

In the 18th century, the two kingdoms become Austrian possessions briefly, before returning to Spanish dynastic control. The kingdoms remained separate though joined in a personal union until 1816, when they were formally reunited following the Napoleonic Wars, after over 500 years of separation. The reunited kingdom did not last long, as it was annexed in 1861 to the Kingdom of Sardinia-Piedmont during Italian unification.

## Formation

If Two Sicilies ceases to exist, it can be released by any country that controls both Campania and Sicily.

### Interest groups

Two Sicilies starts with only the Landowners in government. The Catholic Church, Rural Folk, Intelligentsia, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Armed Forces are in opposition, while the Industrialists and Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Petite Bourgeoisie have the Mercantile ideology due to the starting Traditionalism.

### Diplomacy

Two Sicilies starts with three available interests, but only one of them is declared. It has an active interest in Italy and has a declared interest the Balkans.

Two Sicilies starts with cordial relations with most of the other Italian states.

Two Sicilies starts in the *Metternich System* power bloc, lead by [Austria](Austria.md). Austria guarantees Two Sicilies' independence and the two countries has mutual trade priviliges with the *Declaration of Caserta* [treaty](Treaty.md).

### Industry and Resources

**Industry**

Nearly all [industry buildings](Building.md) in Two Sicilies are located in the capital, Campania. The only industry buildings not located in Campania are a level 1 furniture Manufacturies in Abruzzo and a level 1 food Industry in Calabria.

**Resources**

Two Sicilies produces most of the basic resources. It does have access to some coal and iron in Calabria, but in the long run it is not enough. The state of Sicily has both 100 levels of potential sulfur mines as well as the Sicilian Sulfur Deposits state trait giving **+20%** sulfur mine throughput. This makes it the single best sulfur producing state in the entire world.

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.12. |

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Two Sicilies
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
Two Sicilies' biggest strength is its sulfur production in Sicily with both a [state trait](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/State_trait) increasing production, 100 possible mines and potentially a unique [prestige good](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Prestige_good): [Sicilian Sulfur](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Sicilian_Sulfur). Initially, however, there is actually no use for your Sulfur Mines except exporting it, so researching a tech that can use it is a priority, together with Stock Exchange and Nationalism.

Calabria is the only state with access to both coal and iron, so it is an obvious choice to develop first, although the capital, Campania, has a big issue with unemployment, so it should also be prioritized sooner rather than later.

Due to Two Sicilies' bad [literacy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Literacy), establishing a school system is a priority, and preferably immediately. Due to the influence of the Catholic Church, and the complete absence of any opposition to the law, Religious Schools should be trivial to pass. Afterwards, getting out of Traditionalism is important too. [MAPI](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/MAPI) is important for any country, but Two Sicilies are especially hit by bad [MAPI](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/MAPI) due to practically all industry being in Campania, while most available resources being in other states. Therefore Stock Exchange is very important too.

As Two Sicilies starts with bad laws, consider using the [Risorgimento](Italy.md) mechanic and allow another Italian power to annex it by generating radicals of Italian culture.

### Initial Expansion

Two Sicilies has many potential directions to expand in. They can:

- damage relations and puppet the [Papal States](Papal States.md), although having the Papal States in a customs union should eventually lead to to an event annexing it; annexation will also take place once Italy is formed. While Papal States has friendly relations with both Austria and France, they are not in Austria's [power bloc](Power bloc.md) unlike the weaker Italian states.
- expand into the Balkans through the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md) (especially if the [Dead Man of Europe](Turkish content.md) event has occurred) and [Montenegro](Montenegro.md). Alternatively, it is likely that various countries on the Balkan peninsula gets independence through revolts, in which case they are easy pickings assuming that Two Sicilies can reach them.
- activate an interest in North Africa and attack either [Morocco](Morocco.md) or the component states of [Algeria](Algeria.md); beware of French intervention, especially in Algeria.
- activate an interest in Niger and attack [Denmark](Denmark.md) for their colony in Ghana, and expand in West Africa from there.

However, Italian unification should be the focus until its completion. Two Sicilies start as part of Austria's power bloc, which has the *Defensive Cooperation* principle; this prevents bloc members from fighting each other. This includes [Tuscany](Tuscany.md), [Modena](Modena.md) and [Parma](Parma.md), so they are best dealt with, by diplomacy rather than warfare. Both because it is difficult to attack them, and because doing it diplomatically does not cost any [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy).

### Towards forming Italy

Nationalism is required to form Italy, but since it requires all Italian countries to have the tech, it probably does not take first priority, when it comes to technologies.

Assuming that Two Sicilies can outpace [Sardinia-Piedmont](Sardinia-Piedmont.md) to keep a higher rank, which is very likely due to the difference in population, Two Sicilies can form [Italy](Italy.md) without every firing a single shot. 12 states are needed, and the independent Italian states have 13 between them.[1] So maintaining good relations with all states should be enough.

### Immediately after forming Italy

When Italy forms, especially when done so diplomatically, it is likely that your country will grow in size and population 4-6 fold. It is therefore *very important* to pause the game and thoroughly analyze the new situation.

There are likely going to be problems with market shortages, sub-optimal production methods, [infrastructure](Infrastructure.md) shortages, insufficient administration and an amount of [construction sectors](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Construction_sectors) that is either woefully insufficient or way too expensive.

Especially the state of Emilia has to be looked at, since it has come from three separate independent states, and is thus likely to be in a very strange state.
