*"Cambodia has fallen far since the golden age of the Khmer Empire. Can the Khmer state rise once more, or will this dark age last forever?"*

**Cambodia** (initially called *Tran Tay Thanh*) is an unrecognized power in Indochina. Situated on the Indochina peninsula, Cambodia borders [Siam](Siam.md) and the Siamese subject [Champassak](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Champassak) to the north and north west, [Dai Nam](Dai Nam.md) to the east and the Gulf of Siam to the west. Cambodia starts as a vassal of Dai Nam.

## Historical background

Cambodia was once a major Asian influence in the past, but numerous wars with neighbors wore them down throughout the 16th and 17th centuries. In the 18th century, Cambodia was increasingly influenced and occupied by Siam and Vietnam. By the early 19th century, Cambodia became a subject of Vietnam. Though able to reassert independence briefly, Cambodia became a protectorate under France in 1863. The original monarchy was restored but optimism for a renewal of Cambodia did not last as France continued Cambodia's erosion of power, eventually limiting the monarch's influence to no further than the steps of the Royal Palace.

## Formation

If Cambodia no longer exists, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country which controls Cambodia. Its core states are the Khmer homelands, which are Cambodia and Mekong.

### Interest groups

Cambodia starts with the Landowners in government. The Theravada Monks, Armed Forces, and Intelligentsia are in opposition. The remaining interest groups are marginalized.

The Theravada Monks have the Buddhist Moralist ideology instead of the default Moralist.

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.10. |
|---|---|

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Cambodia
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
To start, as long as [Siam](Siam.md) and [Dai Nam](Dai Nam.md) have their rivalry, you are able to play them off of each other until you are powerful enough to take them on yourself. For starters, it is beneficial to declare independence from Dai Nam once Siam's truce is up with Dai Nam by swaying them with a humiliation [Casus Belli](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Casus_Belli). Rush line infantry at the start and play defensive while Siam moves its troops into Dai Nam, cutting the nation in two and earning a relatively easy victory. It is also beneficial to take the state of Mekong in this process to boost your economy and base population while gaining back a homeland for your culture.

Enacting a Citizenship better than Racial Segregation is recommended to reduce the number of radicals, as well as enacting a Greener Grass Campaign and Promote National Values [decree](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Decree) in your Cambodia state to promote assimilation. After a few years of economy building, military building and constant relation boosting with Dai Viet, you can then declare on Siam to gain back the rest of the Cambodia state, and call Dai Nam in with, once again, a humiliation casus belli against Siam if their rivalry is still intact.

To take the state of Nakhon Ratchasima off Siam is a risky move, as there is no homeland of the Khmer culture there despite pops being there, as well as an instant claim from Siam on the state, which, as long as your population is below theirs and military technology is not rushed, will result in it returning to Siam. After this, it is beneficial to establish an [interest](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Interest) in the Rhine region and obtain either an alliance, become a protectorate of or obtain a defensive pact with a European power with a national interest of either economic or political colonialism. This same strategy can be done with China, but as China is less stable than Europe, this route should be taken with caution. This will allow for a focus on economy without worrying too much about neighbors.
