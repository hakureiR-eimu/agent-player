**Uruguay** is an insignificant power in South America. It borders [Argentina](Argentina.md) to the west and the rebellious [Piratini](Piratini.md) to the northeast.

Uruguay is able to form [Rio de la Plata](Rio de la Plata.md), as long as it does not nationalize its culture.

## Formation

If Uruguay ceases to exist, it can be released by any country that controls its sole core state, Uruguay.

## Scripted content

Main article:
South America content
Uruguay shares several journal entries and events with its South American neighbors. These include journal entries related to Caudillismo, European immigration, and changing national culture.

## Politics

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.12. |

Uruguay is a Presidential Republic, with President Manuel Oribe leading the Landowners. Uruguay starts with the *Age of Caudillos Age of Caudillos Country modifier +250% Political Strength of Officers +50% Armed Forces Political Strength +10% Armed Forces Pop Attraction* modifier, increasing the political strength of the Armed Forces greatly.

Around 15% of Uruguay's population starts as radicals.

Uruguay begins with the [amendment](Amendments.md) Electoral Clientelism (), which increases authority but greatly reduces electoral confidence, representing the deeply entrenched electoral rigging system of Uruguayan politics.

### Interest groups

Due to Uruguay's lack of historical IG leaders for certain IGs and its
Wealth Voting law, the starting interest groups
in government can vary.
Uruguay typically begins with the Landowners and Catholic Church in government as the Blanco Party. The Armed Forces may join them or be in opposition as the Patriotic Party. Meanwhile, the Intelligentsia, Petite Bourgeoisie and Rural Folk form the Colorado Party, while the Trade Unions and Industrialists are marginalized.

The Armed Forces have the Caudillismo ideology in addition to their standard ideologies. The Landowners have the Elitist ideology instead of the default Paternalistic. The Landowners also have the Pro-Slavery ideology due to the starting Legacy Slavery.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Uruguay starts with only a small agriculture sector.

**Resources**

Uruguay has access to a small amount of whaling, and starts with a single logging camp, but no other starting resources. Eventually, they will get access to oil.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.12. |

Uruguay has the Professional Army law.

Uruguay starts with four Battalions and can call upon no Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ejército Nacional | 3 Line Infantry 1 Cuirassier | Fructuoso Rivera | Brazil |

Uruguay has no flotillas at the start of the game.
