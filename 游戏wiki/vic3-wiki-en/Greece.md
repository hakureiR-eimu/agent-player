*"Through a bloody struggle, Greece has won its independence from the Ottoman Empire. Can Greece expand its borders?"*

Greece is a minor power in the Eastern Mediterranean, having recently won its independence. It borders the [Ottoman Empire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey) as well as the [British](Great Britain.md) protectorate of the [Ionian Islands](Ionian Islands.md). Greece's relations with the Ottomans start off cold, and Greece can gain claims on several Ottoman states after researching Nationalism, through the event *The Greek State*. Nationalism also unlocks a decision to annex the Ionian Islands, if relations are 20 or above.

A monarchist Greece can form [Byzantium](Byzantium.md) after acquiring all 12 required states.

## Historical background

Greece is a young nation in 1836, having won its independence from the [Ottoman Empire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey) only a decade prior in the ![Military offense.png](/images/thumb/7/70/Military_offense.png/24px-Military_offense.png) Greek War of Independence. Greece became a ![Law monarchy.png](/images/thumb/5/5f/Law_monarchy.png/24px-Law_monarchy.png) Kingdom in 1832 with the ascension of Otto von Wittelsbach to the Greek Throne, coming of age in 1835 to rule his nation as an absolute monarch. As a former province in the Ottoman Empire, Greece is lacking many of the advancements of the modern age, having been held back by the rulers in Constantinople. Many arcane system of governance, mainly decentralized into local clans or families would prove difficult to reform. Externally, many native Greeks are still under the rule of the Ottoman yoke, Greece's border with the Empire being the Arta-Volos Line as part of the ![Event newspaper.png](/images/thumb/3/35/Event_newspaper.png/24px-Event_newspaper.png) London Conference of 1832.

## Scripted content

Main article:
Greek content
Greece has a journal entry and events related to the concept of the *Megali Idea* or *Greater Greece*, annexing the [Ionian Islands](Ionian Islands.md), and reviving the Olympic Games. Except for the last, these are shared with all Greek culture countries.

With National Awakening, Greece also starts with the Bavarocracy journal entry, dealing with Otto won Wittelsbach's unstable rule.

## Formation

If Greece ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country that controls its capital, Athens, and one other core state. Its core states are the Greek homelands.

### Interest groups

The Landowners start in government. The Orthodox Church, Rural Folk, Petite Bourgeoisie, Intelligentsia, Armed Forces, and Industrialists are in opposition. Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Orthodox Church has the Orthodox Patriarchy ideology instead of the default Patriarchal.

### Diplomacy

Greece starts with obligations to [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), [Russia](Russia.md) and [France](France.md) and also has claims on the [Turkish](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey) state of Thessaly and the [Ionian Islands](Ionian Islands.md).

Greece starts with pro-country lobbies towards Great Britain (), Russia (), and France ().

Greece has a Truce with the Ottoman Empire, ending on 1 January 1837.

Greece has an active interest in the Balkans and a declared interest in Italy.

### Industry and Resources

**Industry**

Greece has only one Shipyard. Greece thus lacks the domestic production of simple goods such as clothes and furniture.

**Resources**

Greece has the ability to build Tobacco Plantations, producing tobacco for [consumption](Needs.md) or export. Greece also has access to iron, lead, and sulfur, but not to coal.

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Greece
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

### General overview

Greece starts out with a relatively low population, not enough technology, and a large adversary surrounding it on all sides in the [Ottoman Empire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey) that owns many of its historical homelands. It does have a relatively solid base for industrialization and can foster good relations with the European powers.

Greece's trajectory is upwards, however, as the Ottoman Empire is sick and ready to be partitioned by the countries bordering it. The *Megali Idea* journal entry and the corresponding chain of events will give Greece mountains of claims on Ottoman land, making it relatively easy to form a large and powerful Greece, become a power broker at the intersection of Asia, Africa, and Europe, and potentially even form [Byzantium](Byzantium.md).

### The Phoenix rises

If you wish to do so, forming Byzantium is arguably the most satisfying formable nation in Victoria 3. It must be stated that it is not easy and requires quite a lot of skill, maneuvering, and luck.

To start, it is absolutely critical to increase relations with [Austria](Austria.md), [Russia](Russia.md), and (if possible) [France](France.md), and Great Britain. Other than that, some standard economy building should be done. It may be helpful to build some barracks for some extra security, but is not required. Military technology should be researched first.

One of the earliest scripted events in the entire game is the Turkish-Egyptian War. When the Ottomans starts a play for the Levant, start a play against Egypt for Crete. Most of the time, they will be preoccupied with fighting in Libya and Syria to actually garrison the island, and the war should just take up a single naval invasion. As soon as you've finished that, immediately turn around and declare war on the Turks for Thessaly and Macedonia - make sure to sway a Great Power, and ideally two for extra security. After all of this and said and done the Greek lands will be doubled.

These new lands are very valuable thanks to prior Ottoman development, and there should be little challenge in utilising these for yourself. Building up the army should be the priority, as future wars will have you fighting on two fronts. Other than that, you can essentially let the AI micromanage the economy for you. You'll have more than enough resources by the end of everything.

### March to Constantinople

When the truce runs out, immediately declare on the Ottomans for Western and Eastern Thrace. A great power will likely help you out for breadcrumbs, but this may not be entirely needed if the Turks had a shaky internal scene after it's wars. Marching onto the Walls of Constantinople should be incredibly easy, and they should capitulate after that. Finally, after 400 years of humiliation, the Golden City is in your hands. Due to the loss of their main city, the Ottomans are likely to be reduced to a Minor Power of perhaps even lower. If not, another war across the straits should be enough to cripple the Turks.

### One Last War

Now that the Sick Man of Europe is on its deathbed, it's time to finally put them to rest. There are either two ways of doing this:

The first is to protectorate the Ottomans and lower their autonomy to a puppet. While it may seem straight-forward, this may prove more trouble than the other option. It depends on the circumstances of your campaign.

The second is to take the needed States only from the Turks. Typically, Greece around this time will be a Major, if not Great, Power, and the diplomatic maneuvers provided can easily take the last few states with no problem in one or two wars. Unfortunately, a great power may feel as if you're going too far and intervene if your campaigns, perhaps even making a protectorate out of the Ottoman rump State diplomatically. Also, if your army is not properly up to size, the Turks may actually end up getting the upper hand and reconquer their claims. Once again, it depends on your campaigns.

Either way, after this is completed, you will have all of the States you need to reform Byzantium. Except for one: the Ionians. It is incredibly easy to get the Ionian Islands. Simply raise the opinion of the islands and their British overlords. Once nationalism is researched, a simple click of a button will see you annexing them.

Once you have finally done it, you can go over to the Cultures tab and fulfill every Paradox player's wet dream: forming Byzantium. Constantine XI has been avenged!

### Alternate - Starting as the Ottomans

An easier way to complete the *Megali Idea* as Greece is to play as the Ottoman Empire, annex Greece, and then release and play as it. Releasing Greece will cause it to own all the states which are homelands of the Greek culture. Because of that, the newly released Greece will already have all the states required for every event and for the *Megali Idea*. You're also only two states away from forming Byzantium. The Ottomans start with a truce with Greece until 1837 though, so you'd have to wait until that ends before declaring war.

Select [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md) on the world map and play as it. As mentioned you have a truce with Greece until 1837 so try to enact the laws you want before you switch as they transfer to the released nation. While you wait you can also build factories, mines, etc. in Greek territories already owned by you, such as Eastern and Western Thrace. Building iron mines, tooling workshops and coal mines to get the construction sector industry going is recommended.

Once you declare war on Greece, it is recommended to pick the lowest infamy option which is the Aegean Islands and then later select to annex the rest later to prevent people from opposing you in the war. Usually France joins the war against you, but sometimes they don't send any troops. If they do, just perform a naval invasion and the enemy will be confused. When you win, just select to release Greece and play as. It is also recommended to release other Balkan nations.
