**Sierra Leone** – called *British West Africa* at the start of the game – is an unrecognized power located in the Senegal [strategic region](Strategic region.md) of Africa, which starts as a colony of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md).

### Interest groups

Due to Sierra Leone's lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Sierra Leone starts with the Armed Forces and Landowners in government. Intelligentsia is in opposition. The rest are marginalized.

Sierra Leone has 1 undeclared interest.

## Military

Sierra Leone has the Peasant Levies law.

It has no Battalions and can call upon no Conscripts.

Sierra Leone has no flotillas at the start of the game.

Sierra Leone not only starts with small and fractured states, somewhat outdated technology, poor literacy, but is also a colony of the most powerful country on the planet, and that is not looking about to change anytime soon. Therefore it can not start any diplomatic plays, leaving colonization as the only means of expansion. On the other hand, it does not actually matter that Sierra Leone does not have a military. Additionally, the primary culture English and state religion Protestantism is a minority in its own country, meaning problems with radicalism and government buildings unable to hire unless the player either assimilates Africans or both passes at Multiculturalism and converts them into Protestants.
