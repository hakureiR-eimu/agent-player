## Hanoverian Succession

This journal entry reflects the succession of Victoria to the British throne and the end of the personal union with Hanover thereby.

id

The personal union between Great Britain and Hanover rests upon unstable ground. Different laws of succession guarantee that the union will end upon the death of King William, with the British throne passing to Princess Victoria.
