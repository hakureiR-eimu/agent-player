**Cherokee** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in North America. It may be released by any country that controls its capital.

Cherokee may form the [Turtle Island](Turtle Island.md).

## Historical background

By the 1700s, the Cherokee people lived in and around the southern Appalachian Mountains. As American settlers moved into the area, conflict grew and eventually resulted in a series of raids and battles called the Cherokee-American wars. In 1794, the United States recognized the Cherokee Nation as a sovereign tribe and guaranteed their territory. While the Cherokee Nation was initially a loose association of smaller tribes, over time it developed a more centralized government, modeled after the US government.

As more settlers moved into the frontier regions, the US government sought to remove the native peoples there, the Cherokee among them. Early efforts moved many Cherokee into Arkansas. In 1830, the US government passed the Indian Removal Act and shortly after signed the Treaty of New Echota with a minority faction of the Cherokee. The treaty first relied on incentives for the Cherokee to voluntarily move to the [Indian Territory](Indian Territory.md) in Oklahoma. By 1838, the US government sent troops to evict the remaining Cherokee. Due to the extreme hardship and many deaths among the Cherokee, this became known as the Trail of Tears. Though most were forced west, a small number of Cherokee were able to evade the federal troops and remain in the east.

The Cherokee Nation was unofficially dissolved in 1907 when Oklahoma became a US state. It was reconstituted in 1938
