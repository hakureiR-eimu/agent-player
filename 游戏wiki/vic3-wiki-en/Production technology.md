**Production technologies** typically increase a country's economic capacity in various ways. They tend to be concrete inventions that unlock new [buildings](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Buildings), [goods](Goods.md), and [production methods](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Production_methods). Some production technologies are more abstract, such as more effectively utilizing labor in manufacturing industries.
