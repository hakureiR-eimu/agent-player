**Bornu** is an unrecognized power located in the Niger [strategic region](Strategic region.md) of Africa. It borders it [Wadai](Wadai.md), [Bagirmi](Bagirmi.md), [Damagaram](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Damagaram), and [Sokoto](Sokoto.md), and the decentralized nation of [Air](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Air).

### Interest groups

Due to Bornu's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Bornu starts with the Landowners in government. The Sunni Ulema, and Armed Forces are in opposition, while the Rural Folk, Intelligentsia, Trade Unions, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Industrialists are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Bornu starts with no diplomatic relations of note, and its only active interest is in Niger.
