**Decisions** represent actions that can be taken under certain circumstances. Decisions can be found in the *decision* tab in the [journal](Journal.md) window.

## By country

See also:
Countries
Country-specific decisions can be found on the relevant country page or on the content pages in the navbox at the top of the page.

It may be pertinent to remove the ban on opium should the diplomatic or economic situation warrant it.

### Ban Liquor

The consumption of liquor is reducing our citizens to their base urges. Liquor must be banned to ensure a moral and civilized society.
---

| Shown conditions: Is the market leader Has active journal entry The Temperance Movement Is not banning liquor | Useable conditions: Does not have Free Trade |
|---|---|
| Effects: Bans liquor Adds modifier Liquor Ban Liquor Ban Country modifier −20% Authority permanently |  |

id

### Remove the Liquor Ban

Political or economic circumstances may require that we lift the liquor ban.
---

| Shown conditions: Is the market leader Is banning liquor | Useable conditions: Always |
|---|---|
| Effects: Stops banning liquor Removes modifier Liquor Ban Liquor Ban Country modifier −20% Authority |  |

## General

id

### Hold a Grand Exhibition

Hold a Grand Exhibition to show all the world your wonders. The more technologies you have to show, the more successful the exhibition will be.
---

| Shown conditions: Has not held a Grand Exhibition | Useable conditions: Is player controlled Is at peace Has researched International Relations Has researched Railways Any owned Arts Academy is at least level 10 Any owned University is at least level 10 If owns Île-de-France, Has built the Eiffel Tower Otherwise, capital state Urban Center is at least level 20 |
|---|---|
| Effects: Activates journal entry Awaiting the Grand Exhibition |  |

id

### Lowlands Land Reclamation

Through a combination of techniques, it is possible to drain certain areas of the lowlands, turning the sea into workable land.
---

| Shown conditions: Fully owns both Friesland and Holland Global land reclamation progress counter does not exist, or has reached at least 10 Has not already reclaimed land here (internal flag not yet set) | Useable conditions: Has researched Water-tube Boiler Has at least 200 bureaucracy |
|---|---|
| Effects: Adds modifier Reclaiming Land Reclaiming Land Country modifier −200 Bureaucracy Activates journal entry Land Reclamation |  |

id

### Pandemic Reponses

With a deadly flu ravaging the land, we must act to protect our citizens.
---

| Shown conditions: Any owned state has the modifier Flu Pandemic | Useable conditions: Has not changed the pandemic response in 6 months |
|---|---|
| Effects: Triggers event The [Country] Flu: Government Response |  |
