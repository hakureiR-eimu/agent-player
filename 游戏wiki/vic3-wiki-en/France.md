**France** is a great power in western Europe. It neighbors [Belgium](Belgium.md), [Prussia](Prussia.md), [Spain](Spain.md), [Carlist Spain](Carlist Spain.md), [Switzerland](Switzerland.md), [Bavaria](Bavaria.md), [Baden](Baden.md), [Sardinia-Piedmont](Sardinia-Piedmont.md), and the [Netherlands](Netherlands.md)'s junior partner in Personal Union, [Luxembourg](Luxembourg.md). France's colonies, treaty ports, and unincorporated states border [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), [Netherlands](Netherlands.md), and [Brazil](Brazil.md) in the Americas; the [Algeria](Algeria.md)n princes in North Africa; [Futa Toro](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Futa_Toro), [Ashanti](Ashanti.md), and assorted decentralized nations in West Africa; and [East India](East India.md) in India.

After its ultimate defeat in the Napoleonic wars, France is now another player in the Concert of Europe, aiming to keep its European competitors in check whilst growing itself without generating too much infamy that would unite the other powers to intervene against it.

France is one of four recommended countries for the *Egalitarian Society* [objective](Objective.md), the others being [Russia](Russia.md), [Great Qing](China.md), and [Mexico](Mexico.md).

## Historical background

To the Romans, this region was known as Gaul. After the fall of the western Roman Empire, the region became divided into multiple kingdoms by the major migration of tribes into the region. The Franks were one such tribe whose kingdom was based around the area of Northern France and Belgium. The first king of all the Franks was Clovis I, who expanded his kingdom greatly in all directions, converted to Christianity, and made Paris his capital. The Franks embraced the Christian Gallo-Roman culture, and ancient Gaul became known as *Francia* – "Land of the Franks". Though Clovis' kingdom was split after his death, the Carolingian Empire sprang up soon after and expanded the realm to its greatest extent, to the Elbe river in Germany, then down to Austria and Slovenia in the east, past Rome in Italy, and to the Pyrenees in Spain, and and everything in between. Although this empire did not last long either, its successor states evolved into the Holy Roman Empire and the kingdom of France. France remained a major actor in European history from this time, being involved in nearly every major conflict in Europe through the Middle Ages and Early Modern Era.

Arguably, France had the largest influence in setting the stage of the "long 19th century", through the French Revolution and the Napoleonic Wars, which – despite Napoleon's eventual defeat – reshaped Europe in many ways, resulting in the state of Europe at game start. Among these effects are rising sentiments of nationalism and republicanism, the dissolution of the Holy Roman Empire and consolidation of Germany into fewer, larger German states, and the Napoleonic Code as a civil code which remained influential in the areas ruled by Napoleon's empire.

France in 1836 is ruled by the July Monarchy, which took power in 1830 with its king, Louis-Philippe I, proclaiming himself "King of the French". By this time, France had largely recovered from its losses during the Napoleonic Wars. However, it is by no means a hegemon in Europe, and has fallen quite far from its arguable zenith just a quarter of a century ago as the French Empire under Napoleon, when France ruled much of Europe, directly or through client states, and occupied more, or even its earlier colonial extent, which encompassed parts of India under the French East India company and swathes of North America.

Now, its great historical rival, [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), continues to ascend as the second industrial revolution picks up steam. [Austria](Austria.md) and [Russia](Russia.md) dominate Eastern Europe, while a new rising power, [Prussia](Prussia.md), finds itself with a French border.

## Politics

France is a [Constitutional Monarchy](Government type.md), with King Louis-Philippe d'Orléans supporting the Industrialists. France, along with [Argentina](Argentina.md), [Brazil](Brazil.md), [Portugal](Portugal.md), and [Spain](Spain.md) are the only countries to start with National Guard. It is also one of a few countries, together with [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), [Montenegro](Montenegro.md) and [Portugal](Portugal.md), to start with any kind of Social Security.

### Interest groups

In government, the Movement Party (comprised of the Intelligentsia and the Industrialists) rules the country, opposed by the Landowners, Armed Forces and Petite Bourgeoisie of the Resistance Party. The Trade Unions and Rural Folk are part of the Republican Union, while the Catholic Church are the sole members of the Legitimist Party.

The Landowners have the Pro-Slavery (colonial) ideology due to the starting Colonial Slavery.

### Political Movements

France starts with unique Orléanist, Legitimist and Bonapartist movements. They lack access to the generic Royalist, Absolutist and Traditionalist movements, however.

They also start with a Radical movement.

### Diplomacy

France is owed an obligation from [Greece](Greece.md). [Tahiti](Tahiti.md) is a protectorate of France.

France has 5 active [treaties](Treaty.md).

- Two defensive pacts with [Spain](Spain.md) and [Portugal](Portugal.md), the *Treaty of the Quadruple Alliance*, bound for 8 years.
  - The treaty with Spain also provides them with military assistance.
- With [Belgium](Belgium.md), the *Declaration of Liége*, whereby France guarantees Belgium's independence. It is bound for 10 years.
- With [Egypt](Egypt.md), the *French Mission to Egypt*, whereby France provides military assistance and transfers 10 small arms and 5 artillery. In exchange, Egypt transfers 120 fabric and gives France investment rights. It is bound for 8 years.
- With [Switzerland](Switzerland.md), the *Swiss-French Transit Rights Agreement*, whereby France provides transit rights to Switzerland. It is bound for 10 years.[1]

France's starting interest levels are Pervasive in Southern Europe and Western Europe; Engaged in Central America, Central Europe, East Africa, Gran Colombia, North Africa, Northern Europe, South India, and West Africa; and Observant in Oceania.

France starts with both a pro-country lobby () and an anti-country lobby () towards [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), and an anti-country lobby towards [Prussia](Prussia.md) ().

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

France starts out with a decent consumer goods industrial base, focused mainly on the production of clothes and furniture. Heavy industry is much more limited, with two steel mills and an explosives factory in the entire country.

**Resources**

Metropolitan France has large deposits of coal (386 potential building levels in total) and a decent amount of iron, enough to kickstart the industrialization process. Gaining access to sizeable sources of rubber and oil will be a must by the middle of the game, as these are not present in the country's starting territory except for a few potential whaling stations. France is by a very large margin the largest wine producer in the world at the start of the game.

## Strategy and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.11. |
|---|---|

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
France
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

### Starting position

At the start of the game, France is a constitutional monarchy with a powerful military, a strong economy, and colonies in Africa and Asia. However, it faces several challenges, including internal political instability, nearby competitors of Prussia, Austria (or even a unified Germany) and Britain, and a growing population that strains its resources. However, with its starting laws, no pops in France are subjected to Violent Hostility, given enough time (base [acceptance](Acceptance.md) of **+20**).

Regarding [Tahiti](Tahiti.md), it is a good idea to exempt them from joining France's diplomatic play in order to lower their liberty desire, as Tahiti has no actual army that could help anyway.

#### Research

One of the first technologies that should be researched is Psychiatry, this is because the event To Venerate Mankind will fire in June 1842, and if you have Psychiatry you have the option to get a whopping 4150 progress in researching Philosophical Pragmatism.

#### Economy

France starts with Military Assistance to both [Spain](Spain.md) and [Egypt](Egypt.md); consider removing the clauses via negotiations.

However, France starts with a shortage of Iron and Engines and should move to increase the supply of these essential goods. It also starts within striking distance of African opium, and should seek to expand at the expense of [Futa Toro](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Futa_Toro) to reach [Kaarta](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kaarta) and beyond. France also starts with a shortage of fertilizer, but this can be fixed by just changing a few production methods.

The French Low Countries starts with insufficient [infrastructure](Infrastructure.md), but this can easily be temporarily fixed with road maintenance. Luckily, the private construction tends to quickly build a railroad there. This however opens another issue, as [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) is the only nation producing engines, and they are very unreliable to import from, as they often will declare France their rival. Therefore building a motor industry is one of the first priorities, once the initial iron shortage has been addressed.

The states of Burgundy, Rhône and Alsace-Lorraine are the best states to build in for industrialization. Burgundy should be prioritized for the motor industry in order to establish [Schneider et Cie](List of flavored companies.md). The [prestige artillery](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Prestige_goods) is important to match the German armies.

With the introduction of tolls, the lucrative Gibraltar toll makes Spanish Al-Rif and Gibraltar worthy targets; consider removing the starting defensive pact with Spain (*Treaty of the Quadruple Alliance*) via negotiations first. Note that besides the truce period, Spain cannot be attacked until their Carlist journal entry has been resolved. Unlike other tolls, the Gibraltar toll can only be collected when one power controls *both* ends.

### Germany

It is advantageous to prevent Prussia or Austria from forming [Germany](Germany.md), and to stop Prussia from forming the [North German Federation](North German Federation.md) or Austria from annexing the [South German Federation](South German Federation.md). While a Prussian-led Germany is already formidable, a "Grossdeutschland" formed by Austria is definitely a greater threat. After [Hanover](Hanover.md) ends its personal union with Great Britain, consider grabbing it as a protectorate, although other Great Powers are almost certain to help Hanover. Owning four or more German homeland states, directly or through a subject, will allow France to form the [Confederation of the Rhine](Confederation of the Rhine.md) (Alsace-Lorraine counts towards the requirements to form the Confederation, although the Confederation will not annex the state when formed), meaning Hanover will be enough.

Taking Hanover has the additional benefit of getting oil which France is generally lacking. From there [Hamburg](Hamburg.md) and [Bremen](Bremen.md) can be puppeted and be merged with the Confederation.

#### Breaking the Prussian Market apart

Be on the lookout in diplomatic plays involving Prussia and other members of the *Zollverein* power bloc. If possible, use a sway to get them to oppose Prussia in exchange for them leaving the Zollverein. This both leaves Prussia weaker economically, as well as weaken their case for uniting Germany.

Many of the German states will also see their [SoL](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/SoL) plummet once they are not part of a bigger market.

### Colonies

France can begin to establish colonies in Africa by attacking [Futa Jallon](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Futa_Jallon) and [Ashanti](Ashanti.md) as they share a border. Make sure to take both Ghana and Togo from Ashanti, so that you can continue on to [Dahomey](Dahomey.md) and beyond.

### Divided Monarchy

|  | Available only with the [Voice of the People](Voice of the People.md) DLC enabled. |
|---|---|

The *Divided Monarchy* journal entry is an early challenge for France, as it can dramatically increase the number of radicals within the country. Depending on the player's preferences, the Radical movement can be encouraged or suppressed. Generally, whichever monarch the player supports, will end up winning, so it leaves the player with some additional options to determine the direction, France is heading.

### France's natural borders

|  | Available only with the [Voice of the People](Voice of the People.md) DLC enabled. |
|---|---|

Once France activates the journal entry, it can receive claims on Savoy (typically held by [Sardinia-Piedmont](Sardinia-Piedmont.md) or [Italy](Italy.md)), Flanders and Wallonia (typically held by [Belgium](Belgium.md) or [United Netherlands](United Netherlands.md)), and Rhineland and North Rhine (typically held by [Prussia](Prussia.md) or [Germany](Germany.md)). While valuable, these states can easily incur high infamy for France. Combined with the reduced infamy reduction modifier, it can be difficult to keep infamy low. Once completed, Wallonian can become a primary culture of France, allowing it to form [Central Europe](Central Europe.md).[2] Note that France would lose French as a primary culture if Central Europe is formed, unless it is any type of Republic when formed.[3]

The first available ideology to start the JE is Bonapartist, which can be achieved by picking them at the Divided Monarchy JE, and getting Napoleon as king. Armed Forces are the likely candidate, which will be strengthened by conquering [Algeria](Algeria.md) anyway. The rest can be achieved by overpromoting and overhiring generals. The Bonapartists don't have to stay in power, only once must they cross the 20% threshold.

### Algeria

|  | Available only with the [Voice of the People](Voice of the People.md) DLC enabled. |
|---|---|

The Algerian journal entries may be hard to complete, relatively to how valuable the [Algeria](Algeria.md)n states seems to be, but failure for *The Conquest of Algeria* should be avoided as *The Algerian Fiasco* brings some nasty modifiers with it. Complications come if a great power (especially Great Britain) gains a protectorate in the region. Since completing *The Conquest of Algeria* grants claims on many Moroccan and Tunisian states, consider completing it quickly. [Constantine](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Constantine) should be conquered as direct access is required to establish colonies deeper in the Sahara.
