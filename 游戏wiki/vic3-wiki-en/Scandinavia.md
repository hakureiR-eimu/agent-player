**Scandinavia** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in northern Europe. It can be formed by any nation with a "Norse" Scandinavian primary culture (Danish, Icelandic, Norwegian, or Swedish). Although Finnish is also a Scandinavian culture, it is not included. Additionally, if Ostrobothnia and Uusimaa are owned at the time of formation, Finnish can be gained as a primary culture.

If Scandinavia is formed by [Denmark](Denmark.md), the country is called the *Kalmar Union* and receives an orange color. If the ruler's culture is Finnish, it receives a brown color and is called *Fennoscandia*. Both of these names require that the country remain a Monarchy.

## Historical background

Kalmar Union Flag
The three primary kingdoms of Scandinavia – Denmark, Norway, and Sweden – emerged in the early Middle Ages, along with the introduction of Christianity to the region. In the later Middle Ages, Scandinavia was mostly unified under Danish control as the Kalmar Union from 1397 to 1523, though with several rebellions in Sweden which ultimately led to the union's dissolution. Following the end of the union, the region was split between Swedish and Danish control, east and west. Sweden became a major player in the 17th century, but eventually lost ground in the 18th century. During the Napoleonic Wars, Sweden lost control of Finland to Russia, but gained control of Norway from Denmark.

Support for a united Scandinavian state, or *Scandinavianism*, originated in Sweden and Denmark at the beginning of the 19th century. The shared heritage and close linguistic, literary, and cultural similarities between the Nordic nations of Denmark, Sweden and Norway have historically made for closer cooperation between these nations. The movement really gained traction in the early 1840s, where several Danish and Swedish university students started calling for greater cooperation between the Scandinavian 'brother nations'. The political establishment of the two countries, both ruled by absolute monarchs at that time, viewed the movement with great suspicion. The movement mostly collapsed after the Second Schleswig-Holstein War broke out in 1864 and Sweden failed to help Denmark with the war. Although support for political union dwindled, ccooperation between the Nordic countries can be seen today, with the prime example being the Nordic Council.

11 of the 15 required states are needed in order to form Scandinavia
Scandinavia can be formed by the following nations when they control at least 11 of the 15 states in the [geographic region Scandinavia](Geographic region.md). As Scandinavia is a *major unification*, the states do not need to be held directly, but instead a unification candidate can gain the support of the other countries to form Scandinavia. The candidate must be at least a minor power, as long as there are no higher-rank candidates, and all nations with Scandinavia's cultures must have researched Pan-nationalism.

Of the 15 states in [Scandinavia](Geographic region.md), eight are controlled by Sweden, either directly or by being the overlord of Norway, four by [Russia](Russia.md), as overlord of [Finland](Finland.md), and two by Denmark, who also controls most of Schleswig-Holstein as overlord of Schleswig and [Holstein](Holstein.md). The German state [Lübeck](Lübeck.md) controls the rest of Schleswig-Holstein. If Denmark forms Scandinavia, the flag is that of the Kalmar Union.

Greenland, Iceland, Scania, and Jan Mayen do not exist at game start and each can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country which controls its capital. Scania and Jan Mayen can only be released with the [game rule](Game rule.md) *Releasable Nations: All*.

Unifying Scandinavia completes the *I am a Scandinavian* achievement.

### Core states

Scandinavia has 16 (or 15) core states, comprising the homelands of its four primary cultures. Except for Schleswig-Holstein, all core states are in strategic region of Northern Europe. Scandinavia's core states have a wealth of iron, but limited supply of other minable resources, limiting industrial potential. Many of its core states also have beneficial traits, particularly *Scandinavian Forests* and its various fjords, making early naval expansion more efficient.

In addition to the Scandinavian states, it is very likely that Scandinavia inherits the Danish colonies in the West Indies (present day US Virgin Islands), Togo, Carnatic, and Pegu.

## Strategy and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Scandinavia
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

### Schleswig-Holstein

In patches which exclude Iceland as a core, the state of Schleswig-Holstein is required to form Scandinavia if the player does not control any part of Finland; controlling the state in any manner typically brings [Prussia](Prussia.md) into conflict with the player. Later on, [Germany](Germany.md) can pose a threat and controlling Schleswig-Holstein helps prevents that threat from appearing, but it does not fully stop it as Germany can still be formed if they get the states from Austria and/or France. Schleswig-Holstein can be formed into the [Confederation of the Rhine](Confederation of the Rhine.md) through the related journal entry. While the Confederation of the Rhine exists, Germany cannot be formed. This method requires the player to become a Great Power and gather the required number of German subjects/states. Note that [Lübeck](Lübeck.md) is also required.

### Unification in 1855

At the start, [Sweden](Sweden.md) has the strongest army, navy, and economy in Scandinavia, and is the closest to forming Scandinavia, making it the ideal unification candidate. To start, demand protectorate from [Denmark](Denmark.md) while liberating Schleswig and Holstein from them, refraining from mobilizing one's army until the tail end of the negotiations will prevent Denmark from calling in other European powers. Begin researching Pan-nationalism. Once within the player's loving embrace; use improve relations, bankroll, and support regime (an overlord action) to lower their liberty desire and eventually turn Denmark into a puppet. Once Pan-Nationalism is researched the player can birth Scandinavia into existence with the click of a button; however, a willing player can go further.

If [Prussia](Prussia.md) has lost their war against [Austria](Austria.md); Lübeck, Schleswig, and Holstein are free for the taking. Starting a unification play declares war upon all three and [Finland](Finland.md). Despite Finland and Russia's personal union, Russia does not come to Finland's aid. Splitting Sweden's starting army allows the player beat all four, fully forming Scandinavia.

Rushing Pan-nationalism from day one incurs a hefty 43,750 research penalty, resulting in the twenty two years it takes to research two technologies. The player should consider whether delaying and researching a few production, military, society technologies would better suit their play style and long term goals. For example: Researching Intensive Agriculture if the player wishes to enter Prussia's grain starved economy, Screw Frigate if the player wishes to build a large wooden navy using the 8 *Scandinavian Forests* states, 4 *Nordic Fjords* states, and 2 *Natural Harbors* states within Scandinavia. Researching Corporate Charters for the company throughput bonus, Quinine to begin establishing a colony in Africa, or Labor Movement to increase the education institution are also potential avenues of research the player should consider. These research picks are suggestions, not meta/multiplayer optimal rules.

### Immediately after formation

When Scandinavia forms, especially when done so diplomatically, it is likely that your country will triple in size overnight. It is therefore *very important* to pause the game and thoroughly analyze the new situation.

There are likely going to be problems with market shortages, sub-optimal production methods, [infrastructure](Infrastructure.md) shortages, insufficient administration and an amount of [construction sectors](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Construction_sectors) that is either woefully insufficient or way too expensive.

### Finland

If the forming country owns the states of Ostrobothnia and Uusimaa at the time of formation, Finnish can be added as a primary culture as a choice, but it can't be added afterwards. As Finnish belong to a different [language group](List of cultures.md) this is not entirely insignificant.

### Scandinavian Industry

The various [state traits](State traits.md) and resource deposits in Scandinavia synergizes nicely, with Norway and Sweden being much better suited for industry, while Denmark is good for agriculture. Additionally Scandinavia likely inherits the Danish colonies, which can provide some of resources that can't be produced in Scandinavia, like dye and rubber, as well as various luxury goods.

The lack of coal is a long term concern, as there are only 8 levels available in Scania in all of Scandinavia.

### Expansion in Africa

It is very likely that Scandinavia inherits the Danish colonial possessions in Togo. From there, you have plenty of possibilities to attack [Ashanti](Ashanti.md), [Dahomey](Dahomey.md) and the other west African countries in the area before any other European colonizer gets to them. None of the West African nations should pose any problems, as long as no one else interferes, and [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) and [Netherlands](Netherlands.md) are the only European countries with an actual interest in the area at the game start.

If possible, slinking into south Africa via [Zulu](Zulu.md) to acquire [Transvaal](Transvaal.md) and [Oranje](Oranje.md) is a sold move. Those three states alone will grant the player vast coal reserves (226 Levels) and gold mines to subsidize any inefficiencies away. In Niger, going through [Benin](Benin.md) to eat most of [Sokoto](Sokoto.md) will also net the player coal reserves (121 Levels) and future oil reserves.

### POPs, Where are they?

Population will be an issue for Scandinavia and all the player can hope to do is lessen that. Migration friendly policies are recommended; Multiculturalism, Total Separation, No Migration Controls. Legal Guardianship for an easy 5% birth rate bonus is recommended. Finally, the Basic Food [company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Company) for another 5% birth rate (from its prosperity bonus) is also recommended.
