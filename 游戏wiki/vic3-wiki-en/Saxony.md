**Saxony** is a minor power and the fifth or sixth ranked German country, jockeying against [Württemberg](Württemberg.md). Its shares its capital and sole state, Saxony, with four other German minors: [Saxe-Coburg-Gotha](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Saxe-Coburg-Gotha), [Saxe-Meiningen](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Saxe-Meiningen), [Saxe-Weimar](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Saxe-Weimar), and [Schwarzburg](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Schwarzburg).

As a North German nation, Saxony and the other Saxon minors are able to form the [North German Federation](North German Federation.md) and [Germany](Germany.md). As an alternative, they can instead form [Confederation of the Rhine](Confederation of the Rhine.md).

## Historical background

Saxony has its origin as a stem duchy of the Holy Roman Empire and was made an elector with the Golden Bull. The House of Wetten gained Saxony in 1422, then partitioned it in 1485 into the Albertine line, whose lands eventually became the majority of the modern state of Saxony, and the Ernestine line, whose lands eventually became the modern state of Thuringia. The Albertine line gained control of the Electorship in 1567 and kept it until the empire was dissolved in 1806. In compensation for the loss of electorship, Electoral Saxony was elevated to a kingdom. The Ernestine line continued to divide its lands into smaller portions, though often recombining parts as various lines died out. By 1836, four duchies remained: Saxe-Weimar, Saxe-Meiningen, Saxe-Coburg-Gotha, and Saxe-Altenburg.

## Formation

If Saxony ceases to exist, it can be released by any country that controls the state of Saxony, its sole core state.

### Interest groups

Due to Saxony's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Saxony begins with the Landowners in government, the Evangelical Church may also be in government. The remaining interest groups are in opposition, except for the Industrialists and Trade Unions, which are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Saxony is a member of the *Zollverein* [power bloc](Power bloc.md), led by [Prussia](Prussia.md).

Saxony starts with an active interest in North Germany and a declared interest in South Germany.

## Saxon minors

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.9. |

The four Saxon minors are all insignificant powers typically and have the same basic stats, starting technology, and politics as Saxony, with only differences in clout for their interest groups. Each starts with only a split state in Saxony. They are all Principalities except Saxe-Weimar, which is a Grand Principality. All four are members of the Zollverein.
