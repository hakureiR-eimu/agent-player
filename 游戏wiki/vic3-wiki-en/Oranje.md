**Oranje** – called *Winburg* at the start of the game – is a [republic](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Republic) and unrecognized power in the South Africa region. Its capital is the city of Bloemfontein in the state of Vrystaat, next to its twin state [Transvaal](Transvaal.md) (*Potchefstroom*).

## Historical background

Oranje was founded by Boers, descendants of Dutch settlers in Cape Colony, who left after the British took formal ownership of the colony in 1815.

Historically, the Boers left after 1836, and Oranje was not officially founded until 1856, though there were several short-lived Boer republics established before then.

The town of Winburg was established by Voortrekkers in 1835, and in 1839 joined the Boer Republic of [Natalia](Natalia.md). In 1843, after Natal's annexation by [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), it became the first capital of Oranje Free State.

## Scripted content

Main article:
Southern Africa content
Oranje shares a journal entry with nearby countries related to the struggle for dominance over the *South Africa* [geographic region](Geographic region.md). Specifically, Oranje must ensure diplomatic recognition and coastal access to gain dominance over the other minor states in the region. Through this journal entry, Oranje may seek confederation with other Boer states, such as [Potchefstroom](Transvaal.md) and [Natalia](Natalia.md).

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Oranje has no starting industry and only one agricultural building, a Livestock Ranch.

**Resources**

Oranje starts with access to a large amount of coal and iron, and a bit of wood. This makes starting industrialization difficult initially, but easier once started. Oranje will also get gold soon.

### Culture and religion

Oranje's only accepted primary culture is Boer, who are the descendants of Dutch settlers in the cape colony that were living there for over a hundred years before the British seized the colony during the Napoleonic wars. Dissatisfied with British rule, these settlers ventured out of Cape Colony and established the Independent Republics of Transvaal and Oranje.

As such, despite being the sole primary culture, Boers represent only 3.16% of Oranje's starting population. The remaining 96.84% are comprised of the state's indigenous black African population.

Like their cultural make-up, the Oranje's religious order is also extremely lopsided. Though [Protestant](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Protestant) Christianity is the [state religion](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/State_religion), it is only adhered to by 3.16% of the population at the start of the game, whilst the vast African majority follows their traditional animist beliefs.

As hinted at earlier, Oranje's demographic woes are going to be the major source of political strife. Though a republic with a (limited) democratically elected government, it is a very conservative and exclusionary country. Not only is over 90% of their population unaccepted on both religious and cultural grounds, but a portion of the native Africans are also enslaved.

Though it's definitely possible to end the game with a sheer Protestant Christian majority, you are very unlikely to displace the indigenous Africans as the majority cultural group even with the help of later European immigrants (who themselves may also not be of an accepted cultural-religious group), and especially so if you intend to expand you reach across the South African region. Therefore, it's important to pass progressive reforms in order to placate the massive non-Boer population. In particular, Cultural Exclusion is a must, since in addition to increasing acceptance, it will allow pops in the Cultural Erasure acceptance status to work in government buildings, otherwise the player may see their construction sectors and government administrations largely empty.

### Economy and Trade

In this time period, gold isn't only worth a lot of money, it is money. Therefore, a country that's lucky enough to possess gold as an extractable resource is bound to be a ridiculously wealthy one if it works to develop the mines for it. Oranje is one of those nations, as the Vrystaat is loaded with gold, though it is not immediately discovered at the start of the game
