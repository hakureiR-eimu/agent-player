**Ecuador** is an insignificant power in South America.

Ecuador can form [Gran Colombia](Gran Colombia.md) after Nationalism has been researched, and [Federation of the Andes](Federation of the Andes.md) after Pan-nationalism has been researched.

## Historical background

Ecuador is one of the three successors to Gran Colombia, along with Colombia and Venezuela. Gran Colombia was proclaimed in 1819 after Simón Bolívar's victory over [Spanish](Spain.md) forces near Bogotá and officially formed two years later when its constitution was adopted. After the war with Spain concluded, regional frictions resurfaced and eventually led to the break-up of Gran Colombia in 1830.

## Formation

If Ecuador ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by a country controlling its capital state of Ecuador. Its core states are Ecuador and Pastaza.

### Interest groups

Due to Ecuador's lack of historical IG leaders for certain IGs and its
Landed Voting law, the starting interest groups
in government can vary.
Ecuador typically begins with the Landowners, Armed Forces, and Catholic Church in government as the Conservative Party. The Intelligentsia and Rural Folk are in opposition as the Liberal Party, while the Petite Bourgeoisie are unaffiliated. The Trade Unions and Industrialists are marginalized.

The Armed Forces have the Caudillismo ideology in addition to their standard ideologies. The Landowners have the Elitist ideology instead of the default Paternalistic. The Landowners also have the Pro-Slavery ideology due to the starting Legacy Slavery.

Ecuador borders [New Granada](Colombia.md) to the north and [North Peru](North Peru.md) to the south.

Ecuador starts with the modifier *Independence Not Recognized by Spain Independence Not Recognized by Spain Country modifier −10% Influence*, until [Spain](Spain.md) recognizes its independence, or Ecuador becomes strong enough to disregard Spain.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Ecuador has no industrial buildings at the start of the game.

**Resources**

The capital state of Ecuador has access to coal, wood, and iron, making it good for early industrialization. However, the *Andes* trait limits its development. The state of Pastaza also has access to rubber, but Ecuador completely lacks sulfur or lead, making mid-game development somewhat more difficult.

Agriculturally, Ecuador has decent potential: the *La Costa* trait in Ecuador increases plantation and agriculture output, and can support Coffee Plantations, Sugar Plantations, and Tobacco Plantations, with Ecuador starting with a few of the last one. However, it is likely not enough to compete with the agricultural giants of the continent like [Brazil](Brazil.md), [Argentina](Argentina.md) and [New Granada](Colombia.md).
