**Iberia** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in western Europe. It can be formed by any country with a Spanish or Portuguese primary culture. Countries with other Iberian cultures can also form it if certain journal entries have been completed.

## Historical background

The Iberian peninsula has been unified or nearly unified several times; initially under the Roman Empire, then later by the Umayyad Caliphate. Following the Reconquista, the peninsula was split between the kingdoms of Portugal and Spain. In 1581, the two kingdoms were united in a personal union which lasted until 1640. Before and after the union, the relations between Portugal and Spain were often tense, though open conflict was rare. Relations eventually warmed after World War I, and the two countries are now close allies.
