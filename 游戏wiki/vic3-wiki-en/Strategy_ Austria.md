. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

## Overview

Austria will have to decide early on if it should focus on forming Germany or its multicultural empire. Taking the fight to Prussia might take away the resources to make sure the Hungarians and others remain loyal to the Habsburgs. If Austria however forms Germany, they will automatically be the extremely powerful Greater Germany. Note that forming Germany will prevent the player from getting the "Habsburg Resurgence" achievement, and that to complete *The Matter of Hungary*, the player must either establish the Dual Monarchy or integrate the crown lands into the empire.

Austria starts much larger than [Prussia](Prussia.md), but has a worse military, worse techs, is less industrialized, and Prussia already have most of Germany in their [power bloc](Power bloc.md) *Zollverein*, so Prussia arguably has the upper hand to start with.

## First construction choices

There are multiple items regarding Austria's economy which require consideration. There are more [peasants](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Peasants) than you can possibly use in all parts of the empire. First and foremost, Austria starts with 26 [construction](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Construction), which is woefully insufficient for an empire of this size. A player will need to build both logging camps and iron mines to sustain them and then more Tooling Workshops and coal Mines to upgrade the PMs quickly afterwards. Luckily there are plenty of potential in both the Italian, Czech, Slovak and Polish parts of the empire, and when Hungary is annexed, also the Hungarian parts.

More Paper Mills and government administrations are also an early priority. There are very few places in the Austrian Empire that do *not* have insufficient [taxation capacity](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Taxation_capacity), which should be addressed. Being able to scale up the education [institution](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Institution) is not a bad byproduct either. Alternatively, Atmospheric Engine is also important, as is researching Nationalism due to its role in the [Journal entry German national identity](German content.md).

Note that Austria starts with an empty [company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Company) slot, which can be assigned immediately. Paper, groceries or mining are probably the best choices.

## The matter of Hungary

*The Matter of Hungary* journal entry gives Austria three options for integrating Hungary (and its cultural minorities more broadly): integrate their crown land subjects ( [Hungary](Hungary.md), [Transylvania](Transylvania.md) and [Croatia](Croatia.md), but not [Krakow](Krakow.md)) directly, elevate them to a Dual Monarchy, or raise cultural acceptance of Hungarian to 70.

Integrating the crown lands requires lowering their Liberty Desire to below 25, which in the case of [Hungary](Hungary.md) will require subject action concessions at the outset of the game in addition to improved relationships. The states can then be annexed via a journal entry decision. Significantly more difficult than annexing the crown lands is keeping turmoil low despite the many nationalist movements that will spring up. If the player delays annexing the crown lands until after the *Springtime of Nations* journal entry is completed, it will be significantly easier to avoid the explosion of revolutions and secessions.

Establishing the Dual Monarchy requires Austria to remain a Monarchy but either fail the *Autocracy in the Age of Liberty* or research Philosophical Pragmatism. Once available, it cedes Hungarian and South Slavic territories to Hungary (giving some agency to the player in the reorganization of the South Slavic territories), and potentially sets the stage for *The Federal Solution* (see below).

Raising the cultural acceptance of Hungarian as Austria essentially requires the adoption of Multiculturalism. This is extremely difficult as Austria, as none of its major cultural minorities will agitate for it.

## Where to build

Regarding the initial states of Austria, the most important places to build are Lombardy and Bohemia due to their unemployment and iron potentials, followed by the states of Austria and Venice due to the unemployment.

When Austria has properly annexed the entire empire, it will have so many states, one can really specialize each of them. Agriculture should of course be placed in Croatia, Hungary and Dalmatia, due to the *Terra Rossa* and *Pannonian Plain* [state traits](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/State_trait). The states of Bohemia, Slovakia, Great Hungarian Plain and Transdanubia are good choices for heavy industry early, because of the Danube (German: Donau) [river](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/State_trait) as well as iron and coal. Great Hungarian Plain and Transdanubia could also specialise in Food Industries, since they start with a few levels of those, as well as a [state trait](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/State_trait), *Pannonian Plain*, which boosts agriculture. Great Hungarian Plain and Bohemia also each have a potential [flavored company](List of flavored companies.md) containing Motors and either [prestige steel or tools](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Prestige_goods) (Škoda Works and MÁVAG respectively), as well as every resource required for Motor Industries. The Polish states has an abundance of coal, which is a good early specialization for these areas. Venetia is your only source of Sulfur so make it a priority to hold onto it, even if you have to concede Lombardia to Italian nationalism. Doubly so, because Venice is one of only 3 states with access to the sea and thus the [world market](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/World_market).

Due to cotton and silk, Lombardy and Venetia are good places for Textile Mills. Partium is best used for Food Industries as they lack crucial industrial resources, but also have the *Pannonian Plain* state trait.

## Prussia and breaking the Prussian market apart

Be on the lookout in diplomatic plays involving Prussia and other members of the *Zollverein* power bloc. If possible, use a sway to get them to oppose Prussia in exchange for them leaving the *Zollverein*.

It is likely that Prussia will be the one initiating the diplomatic play for German leadership. Before that, be sure to be up to date on Prussia's diplomacy, specifically any alliances with great powers. The best case scenario is that Prussia enters the war with no GP allies, while you are able to call on a GP due to a defensive pact or alliance. Since [Russia](Russia.md) starts with a defensive pact with both Austria and Prussia, they are especially important to know the allegiance of. Austria's starting armies are infantry-heavy and that Prussia's armies are usually better; set your generals to defensive stances and deplete Prussia's manpower. Depending on the citizenship law active during the war, Austria may be forced to rely on its German areas for replenishment of manpower. The smaller German states will join whichever side, they prefer (if any), so securing the support of [Bavaria](Bavaria.md), [Saxony](Saxony.md) and the other bigger ones, can turn the tide.

## Conquest and expansion

Honestly, Austria does not strictly need to expand; there is plenty of land already. But, there are plenty of options for expansion. [Puppeting](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Puppet) (or outright annexing) [Switzerland](Switzerland.md) or [Papal States](Papal States.md) can be worthy endeavors. Remember that without expansion, Austria cannot field a powerful navy, due to the lack of coastal states. It also has to expand to secure supplies of dyes, opium, tobacco and rubber; tobacco can be secured in Bosnia and Albania. Other than Italy, sulfur can be found in [Wallachia](Wallachia.md) and [Greece](Greece.md).

[Denmark](Denmark.md) can either be turned into a puppet or Austria can annex/release [Schleswig](Schleswig.md) and/or [Holstein](Holstein.md), because it solves the [Schleswig-Holstein Quetion](German content.md) [Journal Entry](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Journal_Entry). Danish Togo can also be taken to get a bridgehead to start colonizing Africa. In fact declaring the war for Danish Togo severely limits the amount of countries that can join. Attacking Denmark does however require a bit more planning, as Austria's navy is frankly pitiful, and getting military access all the way to Denmark is a very complicated endeavor. Consider annexing [Hamburg](Hamburg.md) or [Lübeck](Lübeck.md) to establish a front against Denmark. If it is something Austria pursues, it is best to do so, before the German Unification mechanics kicks in, as Denmark will by then actually have something to offer Prussia, namely German leadership.

Fully annexing [Krakow](Krakow.md) (which starts as an Austrian puppet) should be done at some point. It is worth it to wait with those two, since they will construct some valuable buildings in the meantime. They should be annexed, once they start hitting the [infrastructure](Infrastructure.md) limit.

The Ottoman Balkans and their protectorates [Wallachia](Wallachia.md) and [Moldavia](Moldavia.md) are worth going after; note that Wallachia and Moldavia have events surrounding their eventual formation of [Romania](Romania.md), and so should not be left as subjects. However, once Romania has been formed, it can be brought to heel as a subject. Alternatively [Bulgaria](Bulgaria.md) and [Bosnia-Herzegovina](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Bosnia-Herzegovina) can be released and with time be added to the Austrian power bloc. Keep in mind that in 1866 if the Ottoman [Tanzimat Reforms](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Tanzimat_Reforms) fail, all their subjects gain independence, and this can even happen during a war for those exact subjects, potentially invalidating any war goals. Finally, Prussian Upper and Lower Silesia is very valuable due to the *Upper Silesia Coalfield* [state trait](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/State_trait), and in general large deposits of every resource needed for industrialization, and on top it already has a large population (and the achievement of course).

Expanding into the Italian peninsula can help Austria maintain a strong navy. Of interest is that Austria starts with some Italian minors (making up most of the state of Emilia) within the *Metternich System* power bloc; turning the power bloc into a customs union should be considered. Note that holding onto Italian homelands will result in waves of Italian pops being radicalized due to unfulfilled unification; this is due to the Italian nations' [Risorgimento](Italy.md) journal entry which allows them to radicalize Italian pops in Italian homelands every three years as long as they are at least minor powers and support the movement. As [Sardinia-Piedmont](Sardinia-Piedmont.md) is usually a supporter of *Risorgimento*, consider grabbing the state of Piedmont to severely weaken them and hopefully reduce them to an insignificant power. However, other Italian powers may become minor powers via *Risorgimento* annexation (e.g. [Tuscany](Tuscany.md)); if [Two Sicilies](Two Sicilies.md) supports *Risorgimento*, it poses a dilemma on how to handle Italian unification without incurring high infamy. If such a situation happens, forming Central Europe may be the best solution. Italian nations with relations higher than **+20** will not be able to generate Italian radicals.

Another alternative is to eventually have all Italian states as part of the *Metternich System* so that even after consolidation, Italy is still part of the Austrian customs union. As a corollary, consider replacing the *Defensive Cooperation* principle with *Market Unification*, which creates a customs union at level 3. Removing *Defensive Cooperation* allows flexibility in declaring war to reduce autonomy of Italian subjects. However, it is advised not to incur too much infamy trying to make the larger Italian nations protectorates; Austria can do little to prevent the Italian nations from consolidating and the reduction in relations can allow eligible Italian nations to radicalize Italian pops in Italian homelands.

## South German Federation

Once Austria is the sole German unification candidate, has researched Nationalism, and the Schleswig-Holstein question has been resolved, Austria will then annex the South German minors, but will not form the [South German Federation](South German Federation.md).

## Forming Großdeutschland and Central Europe

After annexing the SGF, Austria should consider preparing for a unification play against France and the North German minors who do not support it for German unification. Needless to say, this play is a high risk, high reward move, as the opposing war leader receives a *Cut Down to Size* war goal on Austria. Assuming both France and Prussia are still great powers, Austria should minimally ensure the neutrality of the other great powers (mainly [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) and [Russia](Russia.md)); if conditions are right, Austria can sway them to join the war on its side. As for Prussia, Austria can improve relations, while trying to secure a trade agreement, defensive pact or alliance; this will allow relations to (eventually) get to **+80**. With an appropriate attitude, Prussia will vote for Austria for unification. Once Prussia agrees to unification, the resulting unification play can be seen as a means to annex all supporters, rather than a war to seize Alsace-Lorraine. Alternatively, Austria can wait until a unification play is not even needed, and then take Alsace-Lorraine once Germany has been formed and stabilized.

If Prussia is stubborn diplomatically and persists in having a negative attitude, consider liberating [Rhineland](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Rhineland) or [Westphalia](Westphalia.md) and [Pomerania](Pomerania.md) so that the states these minors control can be targeted with a unification play. Note that if Prussia has a successful revolution after losing its status as a unification candidate, it can also be targeted during a unification play. Thus, consider waging war on Prussia to destabilize it.

When Austria forms Germany while holding all Austrian lands, the new Greater Germany will get an [event](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Event), where they have to choose between releasing all non-German countries, keeping them and receiving **70** [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy), or releasing them as puppets and getting **40** infamy. Therefore it is important to time the formation, so that Prussia has no more than 29.9 infamy, as well as at least decent relations with most other [great powers](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Great_power). Within a few years of stabilization, Super-Germany should be powerful enough to take on the entire world, but a *cut down to size* war immediately will be a problem. As long as Germany does not have more than 30 infamy, they should be fine though.

Austria can form [Central Europe](Central Europe.md) after forming Germany.[1] It has incentive to do so as forming Central Europe makes North Italian a primary culture and thus the waves of radicalization of Italian pops will stop. Also, any North Italian dominions or puppets will be annexed when Central Europe is formed; this means that as long as Two Sicilies is annexed by any other Italian power beforehand, the entire Italian peninsula can be annexed with the formation of Central Europe. Another important note is that if Austria forms Central Europe *without* forming Germany, the infamy generating event will not trigger.

Note: If one waits to form [Central Europe](Central Europe.md) until the "The Dual Monarchy" event is currently active, it is possible to add Hungarian as a primary culture to [Central Europe](Central Europe.md). If Austria is also a Republic, then French can be added via the formation event as well. It is advised to Puppet [Switzerland](Switzerland.md), [Sardinia-Piedmont](Sardinia-Piedmont.md), [Netherlands](Netherlands.md) and the [Papal States](Papal States.md) instead of annexing them, as it is cheaper in [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy), and in the case of the Netherlands it means that the Dutch colonies will now be Central European.

## The Federal Solution

This is a [Journal Entry](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Journal_Entry) that allows you to form the Greater States of Austria. The Journal Entry will activate if you have researched Pan-nationalism, become the Dual/Trial Monarchy by finishing the Matter of Hungary, and if you are *not* a unification candidate for [Germany](Germany.md). Also, the citizen law cannot be National Supremacy or Ethnostate. Note that completing this Journal Entry will lock the player out of the Biedermeier achievement, as the Greater States of Austria is a separate tag.

As part of this Journal Entry, you get choices to integrate cultures, gaining new primary cultures.

Depending on your choices, some cultures will be disqualified. Do keep in mind that you need at least 5 cultures to agree, so the goal is probably to pick options that don’t lock you out of this, while also granting a large coverage for languages and heritages. If you have subjects, they may prove problematic by refusing while you wait for the cooldown.

It should be noted that you can only press all buttons once, and never if the culture is already disqualified or integrated. And you might want to annex Krakow beforehand, or befriend them (they need negative relations to remove Polish).

- **The Bohemian Question**

By addressing the Czechs, you can integrate them at no cost except for radicalizing South Germans. Alternatively, you can also grab the Slovaks in a 2-for-1-deal, at the cost of disqualifying (and angering) [Hungary](Hungary.md).

- **The Fate of Slovakia**

If you integrate the Slovaks, you cannot have the Hungarians. You’re also allowed to get the Czechs in one decision with this one. As with other options, you can choose to not integrate anyone here, which only grants radicals and loyalists.

- **The Future of Galicia**

With this decision, you can either integrate the Polish or the Ukrainians, or you can choose to integrate Polish while also disqualifying the Ukrainians. It should be noted that if [Krakow](Krakow.md) still exists, some decisions will impact relations.

- **The Partition of Hungary**

This one is very deciding. You can integrate Hungarian and Szekler (Transylvanian) while losing Slovak, Romanian and Serbian. With the other options, you can just disqualify varying cultures without integrating Hungarian. These being Romanian, Serbian and Croatian for the second option, Serbian and Slovak for the third one, or no disqualifications at all for the fourth option. As with Krakow, these decisions will impact your relations with Hungary. And they can choose to just deny federalization, blocking this option.

- **The Slovene Lands**

You can integrate Slovene at the cost of losing North Italian (but gaining loyalists). Or you can just take Slovenian at no cost whatsoever. Alternatively, you can take Slovene and Croatian in one go while losing Hungarian. Lastly, you can choose to integrate Slovene, Croatian and Serbian all at once, while disqualifying Hungary (and angering).

- **The Triune Kingdom**

You get the option to integrate Croatian, while losing Hungarian. A second option allows you to do the same, while also integrating Slovene. A third option allows you to also take Serbian along with the other two. All options will infuriate Hungary.

- **Beyond the forest**

This concerns Transylvania. You can get Romanian, while losing Szekler and Hungarian. Alternatively, you can integrate Romanian and Szekler, while losing Hungarian if their liberty desire is high. The second-to-last option allows you to get Szekler, while only losing Hungary if their [liberty desire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Liberty_desire) is high. Or you can decide to give everything to Hungary, which makes them happy, but doesn’t integrate them.

- **The Western Fringe**

You can, at no cost except for Slovene and South German radicals, get North Italian. Another option allows you to do the same, but while getting some loyalists (only enabled if you have either Lombardy or Venetia).

- **The Borderland**

The first option allows you to take Ukrainian, while losing Polish (but gaining lots of loyalists). You can also pick a variant of this option, which grants Ukrainian for free (just for a few radicals) – be careful though, as if you promised Bukovina to Romania before, you will lose Romania (so pick Ukrainian first!). Lastly, you can use the final option to integrate the Polish.

- **The Serbs of Delvidek**

You can either just pick Serbian and make Hungary angry, or you can take Serbian, Slovene and Croat all in one go, while majorly infuriating Hungary. For both options, you lose Hungarian.

### Coverage of Languages and Heritages

The West Slavic Heritage (European Trait) is shared between Polish, Czech and Slovak. The West Slavic Language (Slavic Trait) is shared between them as well. As such, these three Cultures are nearly identical, so you technically only need one of them (but Polish allows you to integrate the territory taken from [Russia](Russia.md) faster).

Hungarian has the Magyar Heritage (European Trait) and the Hungarian Language (Uralic Trait). It shares all of these properties with Szekler, so you only need either Szekler or Hungarian.

Slovenes are of South Slavic Heritage (European Trait). Their language is Slovene (Slavic Trait).

Croatians and Serbians have the South Slavic Heritage (European) and the South Slavic Language (Slavic Trait). You technically only need one of these two, and they even share their heritage with Slovene, but not their language. But Serbian allows the integration of more cultures.

Romanians have Romanian Heritage (European Trait), which is unique. Their Language Trait is Eastern Romance with the Romance trait, which is widespread. Getting Romanian also allows easier conquest of Wallachia and Moldova.

North Italian has Italic Heritage (European Trait) and the Italian Language, which has the Romance trait. So, getting either North Italian or Romanian will be useful. But North Italian will allow you to grab the rest of Italy a bit easier.

Ukrainian has the East Slavic Heritage (European) and their Language is East Slavic (Slavic Trait), both of which they share with Byelorussian and Russian. Considering the large amount of Ukrainian population and homelands, Ukrainian might be worth taking.

### The Goals

You want one of Czech, Slovak and Polish (preferably Polish).

One of Hungarian and Szekler (probably Szekler, as Hungarian is very restrictive).

You probably want Ukrainian.

You probably want at least one of Slovene, Croat and Serb. Preferably Serb, possibly Slovene too (optional).

You want either North Italian or Romanian, North Italian is preferable, but Romanian can also be gotten.

### The Best You Can Do, Normally

Before you start, Hungarian is not worth it. They ask for too much, and they might just immediately decline. And you want Krakow to be happy. Otherwise, they can decline and lock you out of Polish, which is something you might want to take (but is replaceable in an emergency). Also, the order does not matter (except for Ukraine before Romania). The only other important thing is that you might not want to pick all anti-Hungarian things at once. They might get angry.

1. Get **Polish**. Partition Galicia, but give Lwow to the Poles.
2. Get **Serbian**. Partition Delvidek off from Hungary. Alternatively, pick the **Slovene** or the **Croat** one with all three cultures, if you want as many of them as possible (and if you don’t care about Hungarian liberty desire).
3. Get **Ukrainian** by promising them Bukovina.
4. Get **North Italian** by promising one of Trento or Trieste. You can also give neither if you lost the Italian provinces and have no other choice.
5. Get the **Romanians** and **Szekler** by giving everything except for the Szekler land.

If desired, you can take the Czechs here, together with the Slovaks if you want to. Though it might be advisable to either skip Slovak, or to go for Czechoslovakia before the North Italians, as you will have to wait for Hungarian Liberty Desire to drop before the journal entry finishes.

This will give 10/11 cultures. Everything except for the Hungarians, which admittedly can become a problem. As such, there is some breathing room to pick other choices which grant more loyalists or reduce Hungary’s liberty desire (although sadly, the ones that reduce liberty desire lock you out of the more useful cultures).

### ALL of the Cultures?

This uses the '[Subject] Government Pledges Cooperation' event in order to circumvent Hungarian locking out Slovak and Serbian.

**Before activating the Federalization JE**, you will need :

1. **Hungary**, with liberty desire below 25, friendly, and a pro-overlord lobby in government.
2. **Serbia** and/or Montenegro with a liberty desire below 25, friendly, and a pro-overlord lobby in government.
3. The ability to press the **"Address Czech concerns"** button

(You can bump them up to friendly using an obligation, by giving them military access/assistance in exchange.)

(Don't worry, you can still shelve the Federalization button until you are ready.)

Once you start the Federalization JE, make sure to check that both Hungary and Serbia/Montenegro has the -0.20 Federalist Stance liberty desire modifier - if they don't, reload, retry the JE.

The steps typically ensure the highest odds for them to accept federalization, but is still susceptible to RNG.

Then wait. The [Serb] Government Pledges Cooperation can simply just be accepted as soon as it pops up.

[Hungarian] Government Pledges Cooperation needs close attention, though. As soon as it pops up, open the JE and address Czech concerns - give them Czechoslovakia to get Czech and Slovak.

Then, use the event to get Hungarian to bypass disqualification.

After this, it is just the regular options for the other cultures.

Note that the events may not pop up immediately - there's about a 2-3 month in-game delay inbetween the "pledge cooperation" event, and other subjects can gum it up.

*Do not accept Romanian from the event, as not doing it through the JE will cause you to be unable to get Szekler.*

### Being Frugal

If you want to go as fast as possible and get the important stuff, you can do the following:

You take Ukrainian, Polish, North Italian and Hungarian (which also grants Szekler). This gives the most important cultures and integrates Hungary quickly, as they need to be happy for the journal to finish. And you might want to befriend Krakow beforehand, or substitute Polish with Czech.
