**Geographic regions** are defined areas of the world, consisting of a number of [state regions](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/State_region), [strategic regions](Strategic region.md), or both.

Geographic regions are referenced in a number of mechanics, such as earthquakes which occur more frequently in certain geographic regions.
