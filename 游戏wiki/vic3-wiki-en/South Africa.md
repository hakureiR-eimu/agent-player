*Originally a Dutch trading post, the Cape Colony has recently become a British Crown Colony with most of its former autonomy curtailed. Now, tensions are rising between the British crown and the Boer states to the north, while in the east, the power of the Zulu Empire is growing. How will the Cape Colony weather the struggles ahead?*

**South Africa** is a minor power in Southern Africa. It is called the *Cape Colony* while a colony under [British](Great Britain.md) rule. South Africa starts as a Southern African [colonial administration](Colonization.md).

## Formation

If it ceases to exist, South Africa may be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country that controls its capital state.

## Scripted content

Main article:
Southern Africa content
South Africa shares a journal entry with nearby countries related to the struggle for dominance over the *South Africa* [geographic region](Geographic region.md). Specifically, Cape Colony must ensure British dominance (or its own control) over the entirety of South Africa. It may also gain Boer and Griqua as Primary Cultures through this content.

### Interest groups

Cape Colony begins with the Landowners in government. The Anglican Church, Armed Forces, Rural Folk, Intelligentsia, and Petite Bourgeoisie are in opposition. The Industrialists and Trade Unions are marginalized.

### Industry and resources

South Africa starts with no industry, and only a small agricultural sector and a single fishing wharf. There is a single Gold Fields in Northern Cape, which provides a small but meaningful boost to minting.

## Strategy and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.10. |
|---|---|

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
South Africa
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
An obvious place to expand is [Oranje](Oranje.md) and [Transvaal](Transvaal.md) to the north. The issue is that they are guaranteed by [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) for the next 25 years. Once that treaty runs out, you should be ready to take them on. Until then getting some colonization up and running and taking [Zulu](Zulu.md) and [Gaza](Gaza.md) is important.

A significant issue is that the primary cultures English and Boer and state religion Protestantism are a minority in their own country, meaning problems with radicalism and government buildings unable to hire unless the player passes at least Cultural Exclusion and Freedom of Conscience. Its primary culture's homelands are also far away in Europe (or already controlled by centralized countries), meaning that South Africa will have just a hard time colonizing as the Europeans do.
