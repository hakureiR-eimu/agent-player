The **Antillean Confederation** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in the Caribbean. It may be formed by any Caribeño or Afro-Caribeño country below Kingdom tier.

## Formation

The following countries can form the Antillean Confederation when they fully control four of its five core states and have researched Pan-nationalism.

| Country | Capital | Primary Cultures | State Religion | Tier | Type | Core States | Exists at start |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cuba** | Western Cuba | Caribeño | Catholic | Principality | Colonial | Western Cuba, Central Cuba, Eastern Cuba | ![Yes](/images/thumb/7/77/Yes.png/24px-Yes.png) |
| **Puerto Rico** | Porto Rico | Caribeño | Catholic | Principality | Colonial | Porto Rico | ![Yes](/images/thumb/7/77/Yes.png/24px-Yes.png) |
| **Santo Domingo** | Santo Domingo | Caribeño, Afro-Caribeño | Catholic | Principality | Recognized | Santo Domingo | ![No](/images/thumb/0/04/No.png/24px-No.png) |

[Haiti](Haiti.md) can gain Afro-Caribeño as a primary culture by completing the journal entry *One and Indivisible*, allowing it to form the Antillean Confederation.
