*The young Dalai Lama is the theocratic leader of Tibet. Will Buddhism remain at the center of political power, or will it be displaced?*

Tibet is an unrecognized regional power in the Himalayan plateau. It starts the game as a vassal of [Great Qing](China.md).

Tibet is located in the Himalayas, rapid building is not possible. This makes Tibet one of the difficult countries in Victoria 3.

## Formation

If Tibet ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by a country controlling its capital state of Lhasa. It uses the Tibetan homelands as its core states, which include several states not controlled by Tibet at game start.

### Interest groups

Tibet begins with the Vajrayana Monks in government.

The Vajrayana Monks have the Buddhist Moralist ideology instead of the default Moralist.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.10. |

Tibet has the Peasant Levies law.

It begins with eighteen Battalions. It may raise up to 27 Conscripts at the start. All Barracks use No Organized Training.

Tibet starts with no flotillas, as they are landlocked.
