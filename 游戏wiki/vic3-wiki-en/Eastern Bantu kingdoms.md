*The people of the Great Lakes have established fledgling empires, dominating both water and land. Will a great nation arise from among them?*

The eastern Bantu kingdoms are six small countries in the African Great Lakes region: Ankole, Buganda, Bunyoro, Burundi, Karagwe, and Rwanda. They represent the largest of the many historical kingdoms in this region.

In game, they are unrecognized powers in the northwest of the Zanj region, in the states of Uganda and Tanganyika. All of them are Animist and they each have one or two Eastern Bantu primary cultures. They each start with only a split state in their capital state region.

## Technology

All six countries start with the same technology, just one from each tree (the fewest starting techs of any countries in the game):

| Production technology | Military technology | Society technology |
|---|---|---|
| Enclosure | Standing Army | Urbanization |

All six countries have the same law set except [Ankole](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Ankole) which has No Migration Controls instead of Closed Borders.

### Interest groups

They generally start with the Landowners in government, the Devout and Armed Forces in opposition, and the remaining interest groups marginalized.

### Historical characters

Only [Ankole](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Ankole) has a historical character, Gasiyonga Nkole.
