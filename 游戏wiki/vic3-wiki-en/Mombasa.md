**Mombasa** is an unrecognized power located in the Zanj [strategic region](Strategic region.md) of Africa. It borders [Oman](Oman.md)i Kenya both to the north and south, as well as the decentralized nation of [Mijikenda](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Mijikenda) to the north west. It is one of only two Ibadi, with the other being [Oman](Oman.md).

With a GDP of just 1,700, Mombasa has the smallest economy of any country in the game.

### Interest groups

Due to Mombasa's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Mombasa starts with the Landowners in government. The the Armed Forces, Rural Folk, Petite Bourgeoisie, and the Trade Unions are in opposition, while the Intelligentsia, Ibadi Ulema, and Industrialists are marginalized.

Mombasa is one of very few countries that start with a non-marginalized Trade Unions.

The Ibadi Ulema have the Imamic and Shura ideologies instead of the default Moralist and Patriarchal.

## Strategies and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Mombasa
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
The starting condition of Mombasa does not offer much advantage. Terrible tech, mediocre laws, and an Inept leader.

One advantage it does have compared to its East African neighbors, is the fact that the country is a subject, thus able to use 'request knowledge sharing' with the overlord for the +25% tech spread. As the economy grow, however, the player will have to decide for themselves whether the tech spread is worth more than the 20% income they must pay to their overlord, unless Oman becomes recognized, thus turning Mombasa into a protectorate that no longer has to pay a portion of their income, which, under normal circumstance is very unlikely to happen.

**Technology**

The player will have to decide on picking Colonization first, which would take 5 years to finish, or Academia first, Colonization second, which would take 8 years.

**Expansion**

Mombasa can right away declare war on Oman to take Omani Kenya and Zanzibar, as Oman will not send its army to East Africa. The player can choose to also go independent, after maxing the liberty desire to 90, or stay on being a subject to make use of the 'request knowledge sharing' button for the +25% tech spread. After the two states are taken, it is very unlikely for the player to be puppeted by Oman afterwards, because the player will have eclipsed Oman in terms of economy, thus affecting the liberty desire.

After this, the player can expand northward into the horn of Africa. In expanding southward, Portugal's territories are free for the taking, while Gaza will likely be an obstacle until later.---- Wikipedia link references:
