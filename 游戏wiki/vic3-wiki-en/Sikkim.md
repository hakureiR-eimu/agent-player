**Sikkim** is an unrecognized power in northeastern India. It neighbors both the [Nepal](Nepal.md), [Bhutan](Bhutan.md), [Tibet](Tibet.md) and [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company).

### Interest groups

Due to Sikkim's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Sikkim starts with the Landowners in government. The Rural Folk, Vajrayana Monks, Intelligentsia, and Armed Forces are in opposition, while the Trade Unions, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Industrialists are marginalized.

The Vajrayana Monks have the Buddhist Moralist ideology instead of the default Moralist.

### Diplomacy

Sikkim starts with no diplomatic relations of note and its only active interest is in the Himalayas region itself.

## Strategies and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Sikkim
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
Due to being a tiny country stuck between [China](China.md) and [East India](East India.md), Sikkim is very difficult to succeed as. One strategy is to put an interest in the Persia region, attack [Herat](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Herat) and draw in [Persia](Persia.md) with returning the state of Herat, then using them to win the war for you. This'll give you Herati Hazaristan, and you can use that as a springboard to expand in the region, and you'll be able to continue to sway Persia in by promising them returns on their claimed states. You will need to move your market capital, though, or else your Persian states will have no market access.
