**Central Europe** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in Europe. It can be formed by any country with its capital in Europe that has a "central European" primary culture (North German, South German, Czech, Dutch, Flemish, Wallonian, Alemannic, North Italian, Slovene). Central Europe requires the [game rule](Game rule.md) Formable Nations: *All* to be formed.

Central Europe represents either a restored Holy Roman Empire, one of several hypothetical multi-ethnic European unifications, or "Greater Germany" variants. It is also one of the few Hegemony-tier countries, the others being [India](India.md) and [Federation of the Andes](Federation of the Andes.md).

## Historical background

The idea of a unified Europe has existed since the Roman Empire.

Prior to its dissolution in 1806, the Holy Roman Empire united Austria, Germany, and northern Italy, though actual unity was more symbolic than anything else, as most Italian states and the more powerful German states enjoyed *de facto* independence from the Austrian Habsburg emperors. After the empire's end, a specifically German confederation replaced the core of the Holy Roman Empire, and eventually most German states unified into the [North German Federation](North German Federation.md), then soon after into the [German Empire](Germany.md).

Through the 19th and early 20th centuries, many philosophers, politicians, and writers proposed various forms of European unification, often calling for a *United States of Europe*. Though no real union ever formed, most European countries eventually joined the European Union, which grew out of treaties and agreements made following World War II and through the Cold War.

35 of the 47 core states are needed to form Central Europe
Central Europe can be formed by any country that has one of its primary cultures when they control 35 of the 47 core states and have their capital in Europe. Central Europe can be formed by the following countries – or any of the countries that can form them, either directly or though the intermediate formation. Central Europe retains the state religion of the country that forms it. This means the state religion can be any of Protestant, Catholic, or Orthodox; even Sunni is possible if [Bosnia-Herzegovina](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Bosnia-Herzegovina) forms [Yugoslavia](Yugoslavia.md) and then Central Europe.

[France](France.md) can gain Wallonian as a primary culture by completing the journal entry *The Natural Borders of France*, allowing it to form Central Europe. With enabled, France can also add Wallonian as a primary culture through the journal entry *The Status of the Vernacular*.

If Central Europe is any type of Republic when formed, it can gain French as a primary culture; this includes Council Republics, but only if France is not also a Council Republic. This is noteworthy for France itself, as otherwise it would lose French as a primary culture.

[Austria](Austria.md) is in a good position to form Central Europe if it can form [Großdeutschland](Germany.md) as it will then have most of the required states. The second best candidate is [Prussia](Prussia.md) for the same reasons, although they will not necessarily get as many states, as convincing Austria to join after a unification war is close to impossible.

### As Austria

Austria has additional incentive to form Central Europe as it makes North Italian a primary culture and thus stops the waves of radicalization of Italian pops from Italian *Risorgimento* journal entry. However, Hungarian is not a primary culture in Central Europe and thus requires at least Racial Segregation to be accepted. If Two Sicilies (the only Italian nation with South Italian as the sole primary culture) is annexed by any other Italian power, it will then be possible to annex the entire Italian peninsula when forming Central Europe, by having the Italian nations as dominions or puppets.

Additionally, forming Central Europe instead of Germany can be used to bypass the [event The Seventy Million Reich](German content.md), which imposes a whopping **70** [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy) to whoever forms Germany with all the Austrian lands. The German Unification leadership play and unification plays can still be used, as long as Germany is not formed in the process.

### Tips for formation

It is generally cheaper in [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy) to [puppet](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Puppet) surrounding European nations before formation than it is to conquer them. It also has the added benefit of not taking time to integrate the states. The [Netherlands](Netherlands.md) is a particularly ripe target for this if you can drop them to a minor power, as it gives you the rather large boon of the [Dutch East Indies](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Dutch_East_Indies) without having to deal with the massive infamy cost of transferring the subject. If liberty desire proves problematic to get down, one can conquer bit and pieces of colonies and gift them to your protectorates. For example [Ashanti](Ashanti.md)'s part of Ghana can be taken and given to the Netherlands.

### France

When forming Central Europe, French culture can be added as primary if either 1) Central Europe is a Parliamentary Republic or Presidential Republic, or 2) if Central Europe is a Council Republic and [France](France.md) is *not*. Corporate State does not count. This comes with an increase in radicals, but is generally worth doing. Especially if France forms Central Europe; then it will be truly unstoppable. This is however a one-time opportunity, and must be done at formation. So have the correct government type ready before forming Central Europe.

### Italy

If Italy is formed and then puppeted, the entirety of Italy will be annexed by the formation including the lands of [Two Sicilies](Two Sicilies.md). This can be done by "vassal feeding"[3] an Italian subject until they can form Italy under your guidance. This has the added benefit of reducing liberty desire.

Even if the ambition is not to get the entirety of Italy, it is advised to puppet the [Papal States](Papal States.md) as they have both North and South Italian as primary cultures, so they will be annexed upon formation, but it is only the State of Romagna is considered a core state of Central Europe. Therefore doing it this way gives you Lazio and Umbria for free.

### Yugoslavia

Like with Italy, it is possible to vassal feed [Yugoslavia](Yugoslavia.md) and annex them upon formation due to Slovene primary culture being European. This involves taking states from the [Ottoman Empire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey). [Montenegro](Montenegro.md) can be puppeted and used for this purpose.
