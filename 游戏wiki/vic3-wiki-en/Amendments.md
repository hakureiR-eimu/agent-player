Laws
**Amendments** are modifications of laws, adding additional effects and making the law more or less acceptable to [interest groups](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Interest_groups). Many amendments have *allowed laws*, limiting which laws they can amend, as well as a *parent law* which sets their interest group stances. For example, the Second Amendment of the Constitution modifies Presidential Republic in [America](America.md) and inherits its interest group stances from National Guard.

## Historical Amendments

Historical amendments are enabled at the start of the game, althought some can be (re-)enacted as the game progresses. Most of them are country- or culture-specific, but a few are generic, like Colonial Racialization. For a list of countries that start with these amendments see [List of countries by starting laws](List of countries by starting laws.md).

## Enactment Amendments

Enactment Amendments are acquired through the enactment of new laws, during various advance, debate, or stall events. Any country can receive these amendments as long as the corresponding laws are enacted. These amendments can also sometimes be added through law negotations with interest groups who support the amendment.

The *Sponsors* column lists valid [Interest Groups](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Interest_Groups) that can sponsor the amendment via negotiations:

- Given interest groups
- Interest groups with ideology
- Interest groups with leader with ideology

## Political Concessions

Political Concessions are a set of generic amendments which all have the same name but slightly different effects. They can be added to any law, sponsored by any interest group, and enacted by any country. They are generally added only added through law negotations with interest groups, but a few events also can add the first variation.
