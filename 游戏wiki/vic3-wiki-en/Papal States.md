*"Though the political power of the Catholic Church has waned over the centuries, the Pope still presides over the Papal States. Does faith still rule the hearts of men?"*

The **Papal States** (called *Rome* if not a theocracy) is an minor power in Italy. It borders [Austria](Austria.md), [Modena](Modena.md), and [Tuscany](Tuscany.md) to the north and [Two Sicilies](Two Sicilies.md) to the south. It is the third largest Italian state behind [Sardinia-Piedmont](Sardinia-Piedmont.md) and Two Sicilies.

Papal States can form [Italy](Italy.md), which is then called the *Kingdom of Heaven* if it is a theocracy.

## Historical background

The Papacy has been in Rome since the fall of the Roman Empire, and has played an active role in European politics ever since. They have a long history of owning land and acting as a geopolitical player.

## Formation

If the Papal States ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country which controls Lazio.

### Interest groups

The Papal States starts with the Roman Curia in government. The Civic Nobility, Rural Folk, Armed Forces, Intelligentsia, and Petite Bourgeoisie are in opposition. The Industrialists and Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Roman Curia they have the Pontifical ideology instead of Moralist, which makes them support Theocracy over Monarchy. The Civic Nobility have the Baronial ideology instead of the Paternalistic ideology which means they also support Theocracy. The Intelligentsia lose the Anti-Clerical ideology. The Petite Bourgoisie have the Mercantile ideology, due to the starting Traditionalism law.

### Industry and Resources

**Industry**

Almost all [industry buildings](Building.md) in the Papal States are located in the capital Lazio, with the sole exception of a single level of Textile Mill in Romagna. Lazio accounts for 35% of the country's total GDP at game start.

Lazio has a level 2 Paper Mills.

**Resources**

Papal States has the option to build Silk Plantations in Lazio to produce silk, used to make luxury clothes. Some wood and iron are present, and there is an abundance of sulfur. But the lack of coal and lead may become an issue in the long term.

#### Vatican City

Lazio has the monument *Vatican City*. If monument effects are enabled in the [game rules](Game rule.md), Vatican City employs 500 clergymen and provides the following for Catholic countries:

- **+100** Authority
- **+100** Influence
- **+25%** Devout political strength
- **+20** [Urbanization](Urbanization.md)

Non-Catholic (including Atheist) countries gain **+25** prestige instead, with no employment required.

## Military

Papal States has the National Militia and Merchant Navy laws.

Papal States has seven regular battalions, and it can call upon 43 Conscripts battalions. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.

## Strategies and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Papal States
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
Papal States is arguably the third best candidate to form [Italy](Italy.md) behind [Two Sicilies](Two Sicilies.md) and [Sardinia-Piedmont](Sardinia-Piedmont.md).

Papal States' best chance to form [Italy](Italy.md) is by becoming the leading producer of Fine Art and possibly Luxury Clothes, in order to increase its rank to major power. Subjugating or conquering [Tuscany](Tuscany.md) may also be necessary to build up a sufficient market and GDP. It is important to notice who Austria is guaranteering, either through diplomatic agreements or their power bloc.

The capital state of Lazio has the largest population and thus infrastructure, but Umbria has the only source of iron. The lack of tools is also an issue that has to be solved. Very quickly, the complete lack of coal will also be an issue.

Weakening [Two Sicilies](Two Sicilies.md) is also as good as necessary, as they are almost certain to be a candidate to form Italy and so a war for unification leadership is typically necessary.

### Immediately after forming Italy

When Italy forms, especially when done so diplomatically, it is likely that your country will grow in size and population 4-6 fold. It is therefore *very important* to pause the game and thoroughly analyze the new situation.

There are likely going to be problems with market shortages, sub-optimal production methods, [infrastructure](Infrastructure.md) shortages, insufficient administration and an amount of [construction sectors](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Construction_sectors) that is either woefully insufficient or way too expensive.

Especially the state of Emilia has to be looked at, since it has come from three separate independent states, and is thus likely to be in a very strange state.
