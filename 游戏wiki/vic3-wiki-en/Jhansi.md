**Jhansi** is an unrecognized power in north central India. It neighbors both the [Gwalior](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Gwalior), [Orchha](Orchha.md) and [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company).

It starts the game as a puppet of [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company).

Jhansi is able to form [India](India.md).

### Interest groups

Jhansi begins with the Zamindars in government. The Armed Forces and Hindu Priesthood are in opposition. The remaining interest groups are marginalized.

The Hindu Priesthood has the Hindu Moralist ideology instead of the default Moralist. The Petite Bourgeoisie has the Modernizer ideology instead of the default Reactionary. The Intelligentsia has the Constitutionalist ideology instead of the default Republican.

### Diplomacy

Jhansi is a puppet of the [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company). Jhansi starts with no declarable interests, and its only active interest is in the Central India region.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.12. |

Jhansi has the Professional Army law.

Jhansi starts with 4 Battalions and cannot raise any Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jhansi Army | 2 Irregular Infantry 1 Line Infantry 1 Lancer | — | Central India |

Jhansi starts with no flotillas, as it is landlocked.
