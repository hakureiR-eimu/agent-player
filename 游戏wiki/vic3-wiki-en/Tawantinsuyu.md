**Tawantinsuyu** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in South America. It can be formed by any Quechua country below Kingdom tier (currently only [Iquicha](Iquicha.md)).

## Historical background

Tawantinsuyu is the Quechua name of the Inca Empire. It translates to *"Four Regions"* referring the four constituent regions of the empire. The Inca Empire was established in the 1400s and expanded to control most of the Andes by the beginning of the 1500s. However, the reign of the Inca Empire was cut short by [Spanish](Spain.md) conquistadors, who took advantage of a civil war to conquer the empire in 1532.

## Formation

[Iquicha](Iquicha.md) may form Tawantinsuyu when it controls four of its eight core states.

Alternatively, Tawantinsuyu can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country that controls its capital state.

### Core states

Tawantinsuyu uses the Quechua homelands as its core states. These are all in the Andes strategic region except Cauca which is in the Gran Colombia strategic region.
