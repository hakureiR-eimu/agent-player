**England** is a [releasable](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) and [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in Western Europe.

England may reform [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) if the latter ceases to exist.

## Formation

Five of the six core states are needed in order to form England.
England can be [formed](Formable countries.md) by a country below Kingdom tier with English as a primary culture when it controls 5 of the 6 core states. Alternatively, England can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country which controls Home Counties and two other core states.

## Core states
