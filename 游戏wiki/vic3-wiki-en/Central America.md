*"The Federal Republic consists of provinces which would largely prefer to be independent. Can Central America hold together?"*

**Central America** – officially, the Federal Republic of Central America or *Republica Federal de Centro America* – is a minor power in Central America. Its starting capital is the city of San Salvador in the state of El Salvador. Central America has one of the more progressive and liberal governments of all the starting countries in the game.

Central America borders [Mexico](Mexico.md) and [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) (province in Guatemala) in the north, [Miskito Kingdom](Miskitia.md) in the east and [New Granada](Colombia.md) in the south.

Typically, Central America disintegrates partially or entirely within a few years into the states that compose it: [Guatemala](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Guatemala), [El Salvador](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/El_Salvador), [Honduras](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Honduras), [Nicaragua](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Nicaragua), [Costa Rica](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Costa_Rica), and [Los Altos](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Los_Altos) (Chiapas).

When Central America ceases to exist, as is typical, it can be [formed](Formable countries.md) by the following countries when they control three of its six core states.

| **El Salvador** | San Salvador | Central American | Catholic | Principality | Recognized | San Salvador |

| **Honduras** | Honduras | Central American | Catholic | Principality | Recognized | Honduras |

Each of the above is [releasable](Releasable countries.md) by any country which controls their capital, which is their only core state. Central America is also releasable, by any country which holds San Salvador and two other core states.

### Core states

Central America's core states are the Central American homelands. These include all of its starting states, except Chiapas, plus the state of Panama.

## Scripted content

Central America starts with a journal entry that represents its dissolution. It can also activate an entry to track its reunification. Central America also gains the [Age of Caudillos](South America content.md) journal entry if it hires a general before it breaks up. Its breakaways cannot gain the *Age of Caudillos* entry. Central America also has content for attracting European migration, and for earning recognition from [Spain](Spain.md).

Central America begins with the [amendment](Amendments.md) Electoral Clientelism (), which increases authority but greatly reduces electoral confidence, representing the deeply entrenched electoral rigging system of Central American politics.

### Interest groups

Due to Central America's general lack of historical IG leaders and its
Census Suffrage law, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Central America typically starts with the Armed Forces in government, either as leader of the Patriotic Party or as a member of the Conservative Party. The Landowners, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Catholic Church are typically members of the Conservative Party, and so may be either in government or in opposition. The Rural Folk and Intelligentsia are typically unaffiliated with any party and in opposition. The Trade Unions, and Industrialists are marginalized.

The Armed Forces have the Caudillismo ideology in addition to their standard ideologies. The Landowners have the Elitist ideology instead of the default Paternalistic.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Central starts with almost no industry, just a single Textile Mill and a handful of agricultural buildings.

**Resources**

Central America can build many types of plantations, including coffee, tobacco, sugar and dyes. Iron can be mined in the state of Guatemala, and coal can be mined in the state of Costa Rica. Rubber can be discovered throughout the entire country. The country lacks lead and sulfur, which have to be imported.

### Surviving The Dissolution

Surviving the dissolution is a very difficult task that demands a sequence of changes to be made to reduce [turmoil](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turmoil) as quickly as possible and requires luck as well. Because the consequence of a seceding state results in increased radicals and turmoil on the remaining states, and because even without such penalties the task is already extremely difficult, if any state secedes, the player is recommended to either load a previous autosave, or just restart a new game entirely. Surviving the dissolution, despite its challenge, does not complete any achievement. It is all about reducing turmoil and therefore radicalism.

Before unpausing some steps can be taken to reduce radicals:

1. Lower taxation as low as possible;
2. Increase government and military wages as high as possible;
3. Activate Emergency Relief if applicable;
4. Reform government to maximum possible legitimacy. Try to include as many conservative interest groups as possible, as they are the most supported.

After these initial changes, a single day should be played to let the game refresh the interest groups approval and then paused again.

The primary objective is to reduce turmoil by increasing approval of interest groups through laws they like, and increasing standard of living by decreasing prices of basic needs and increasing employment. Not to develop country and increase GDP.

Alternatively, a player can cheese the dissolution event by releasing the Central American minors as puppets, leaving the player only with El Salvador. Note that releasing states as subjects will not trigger the events increasing radicals. After the dissolution event fires, pick the second option, then reform Central America for good.

### Economy

The goal of the economy is not just to expand it like any other country, but also to reduce radicalism in every available state. Therefore construction sectors should be more spread out than other countries would do.

Afterwards priority should be given to grain surplus. Expansion should be distributed through the central states: Guatemala, Honduras, El Salvador and Nicaragua.

Once grain prices are lowered, wood prices should be lowered too until there is a surplus. After stabilizing grain and wood prices, building a Furniture Manufacturies and Textile Mill to further access to consumer goods. Trade centers are also important to round out the market.

### Politics

Laws should generally changed only when they are popular and have both many supporters measured in POPs og IGs. There are some laws that should be considered:

1. Closed Borders to reduce immigration to have less mouths to feed
2. Religious Schools generally has few if any critics, and the [literacy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Literacy) needs improving
3. National Guard

Alongside the enactment of laws, there are some technologies that are more beneficial at this stage:

1. Nationalism
2. Lathe (Increase production of clothes and furniture to lower price and increase Standard of Living).

From now on until the journal is closed, these are the rules of thumbs to be followed:

- Any random event that has options that reduce radicalism or increase approval of interest groups, should be prioritized (or at least doesn't decrease it).
- After an election, should be chosen option to form government with the most legitimacy possible and include most interest groups possible.
- Prices of basic goods should continue to be balanced through construction: grain, wood, clothes, furniture.
- Do not fire anyone, not even from barracks. Firing people off their jobs turns them into radicals.
