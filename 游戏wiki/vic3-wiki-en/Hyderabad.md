*"Almost all of India is now under the direct or indirect control of the British East India Company. The Nawabs of the princely states still exert a strong degree of rule over their territories, but ultimately answer to the Company. Will India ever be free from British rule?"*

**Hyderabad** is an unrecognized regional power in India. It is one of the "princely states", a puppet of the [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company), and thus a part of the [British Empire](Great Britain.md).

As a Deccani country, Hyderabad is able to form [India](India.md).

### Interest groups

Hyderabad begins with the Zamindars as the sole interest group in government, with the Ryots, Armed Forces, Sunni Ulema, and Petite Bourgeoisie in opposition. The Intelligentsia, Trade Unions, and Industrialists are marginalized.

The Petite Bourgeoisie has the Modernizer ideology instead of the default Reactionary. The Intelligentsia has the Constitutionalist ideology instead of the default Republican.

## Strategy and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.10. |
|---|---|

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Hyderabad
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
**Starting Situation**

Hyderabad is one of the largest and most powerful princely states in the Indian region. It is underneath the [British East India Company](East India.md), and secondarily, the [British Empire](Great Britain.md). It is economically underdeveloped, but has a substantial population, and can gain more.

**Grand Strategy**

Hyderabad is all about gaining more of the Indian region, and gaining independence. As Hyderabad's material resources are not the best, an immediate military buildup for an attack is probably the best solution to gain port access. This works best if the British themselves are occupied elsewhere. Alternatively, the player can play tall, stick to their lands, and wait for their overlord to change from the East India Company to the British directly.

With the aggressive strategy, the Hyderabad player will gain independence and a port within the first 15 or so years of the game. After this, the player may wish to become a Protectorate or remain independent. For further expansion, it will be easier for the player to attack the other Princely States rather than the East India company to gain control of more of the Indian lands, resources, and population. Forcing the East India Company to release nations (such as Bangladesh) can cripple it, and open it up for further aggression. However, if the East India company beats down the player, it's often game over.

With the tall strategy, the issue here is that Hyderabad will eventually become dependent on resources it cannot produce and (due to a lack of port) it cannot trade for. On the flip side, it will give the player a chance to turn Hyderabad into a potent and modern economy as a part of the British Market. The way to form India here is to basically wait for the East India Company to collapse, which may or may not happen. Until the collapse comes, the player should aggressively build vertically to siphon population away from the rest of the British Market. A tall Hyderabad can be wealthy, but trapped.

**Internal Politics**

Hyderabad's starting internal politics are relatively poor. Getting good laws enacted will be challenging.

**Diplomacy**

Hyderabad's diplomacy is about gaining support (or at least neutrality) for its efforts in uniting India. The two foreign powers of greatest interest are the British and French. A distant third is the Russians, who can potentially gain a land border from the north. America is a bit of a wild card, but generally isn't overly interested in the Indian area.

**Potential**

A Hyderabad player can cripple the East India Company, and then use the East India Company's weakness to form India proper. While compensating for economic underdevelopment is challenging, the power spike from when the player's economy begins to hit its stride is enormous, considering the Indian area's enormous population and resources. India, once formed, is capable of being enormously powerful.

**Weaknesses**

Hyderabad's starting situation is extremely limiting, and correcting for that is extremely risky. If independence and a port are achieved, it still is in a very vulnerable situation. Breaking free is hard, and not getting crushed immediately after breaking free can be hard.
