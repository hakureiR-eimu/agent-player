**Ontario** (initially called *Upper Canada*) is an insignificant power located in the Canada [strategic region](Strategic region.md) of North America.

As an Anglo-Canadian country, Ontario can form [Canada](Canada.md).

## Formation

If Ontario ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country that controls its capital, Ontario.

### Interest groups

Ontario begins with the Landowners and the Anglican Church in government as the Liberal-Conservative Party. The Armed Forces also often join the Liberal-Conservative party. In opposition, the Rural Folk may form the Patrons of Industry or refrain from joining a party, the Industrialists will likely form the Free Trade party, the Petite Bourgeoisie and the Intelligentsia may form the Liberal party, and the Trade Unions will most likely form the Radical party.

### Political Movements

Ontario begins with a very Agitated Radical movement, due to the *Reformer Movement Fury Reformer Movement Fury Movement modifier +5% Political Movement Pop Attraction +20% Political Movement Activism* modifier, which lasts for 20 years. [Lower Canada](Quebec.md) starts in the same situation.

### Diplomacy

Ontario is a colony of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) and thus part of the *British Empire* power bloc. It is bordered to the east and north by other British colonies - [Lower Canada](Quebec.md) and the [Hudson’s Bay Company](Hudson’s Bay Company.md) respectively, - and by the [United States](America.md) to the south. It has an undeclared interest.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Ontario begins with no urban buildings, but has a couple agricultural buildings and even a Trade Center.

**Resources**

Ontario starts with 6 Sulfur Mines and has the potential for plenty more, as well as many Logging Camps. However, it lacks quick access to critical early resources like iron or coal, although nearby Canadian colonies have access to these resources. Being part of the [British](Great Britain.md) market will likely account for any needs Ontario - and Canada - may have in other regards.
