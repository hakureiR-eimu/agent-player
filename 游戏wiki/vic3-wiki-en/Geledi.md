**Geledi** is an unrecognized power located in the Ethiopia [strategic region](Strategic region.md) of Africa. It borders [Witu](Witu.md), [Borana](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Borana), [Harar](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Harar), and [Majerteen](Majerteen.md), and the decentralized nation of [Kikuyu](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kikuyu).

## Politics

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.10. |
|---|---|

Geledi's initial government is Monarchy with Autocracy, with Yuusuf Maxamuud Ibrahiim Gobroon as the sultan, a Moderate, supporting the Landowners. Geledi starts with Traditionalism and Isolationism, which prevents all trade and weakens the [Investment pool](Treasury.md), making economic growth very difficult. Additionally, Geledi starts with no [institutions](Institutions.md).

### Interest groups

Due to Geledi's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
The Landowners begin in a strongly dominant position, with almost no opposition and many laws also strongly favoring them.

- The Landowners start in government.
- The Armed Forces, Sunni Ulema, and Rural Folk are in opposition.
- The Intelligentsia , Petite Bourgeoisie, Trade Unions, and Industrialists are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Geledi starts with cordial relations with [Witu](Witu.md) (**+40**). Its only active interest is in the Ethiopia region itself.

[Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md), and [Egypt](Egypt.md) start with active or declared interests in the region, and other European powers are likely to declare interests in Ethiopia as well as they begin to colonize Africa.

## Military

Geledi has the Peasant Levies law.

Geledi starts with 5 Battalions but can raise no Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions comprise a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Geledi Army | 5 Irregulars | Yuusuf Maxamuud Ibrahiim Gobroon | Ethiopia |

Geledi starts with no flotillas.
