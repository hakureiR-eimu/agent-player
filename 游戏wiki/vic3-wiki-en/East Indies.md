The **East Indies** are a minor power in Southeast Asia. They are a dominion under the [Netherlands](Netherlands.md). In turn, they are the overlord of several Indonesian countries.

When a subject, they always have their name prefixed by their overlord's adjective, thus starting as the *Dutch East Indies*. East Indies can form [United Netherlands](United Netherlands.md).

## Historical background

The Dutch East Indies were originally founded as the United East India Company – or VOC from its Dutch initials. Although initially a commercial venture to trade spices, the VOC quickly gained territorial control in the Moluccan islands and at Batavia (modern Jakarta). The VOC slowly expanded its control over the next 200 years, also venturing into India and taking control of Ceylon and other coastal regions. However, in the 1700s, the company became increasingly dysfunctional and was officially disbanded at the end of 1799, with its holdings becoming direct Dutch colonies.

During the Napoleonic Wars, the British occupied much of the former VOC, with some parts being restored to the Netherlands in 1814, including the East Indies. Under direct colonial administration, the Dutch East Indies expanded rapidly in the 1800s, to encompass nearly the entire archipelago by 1900. The colony was massively profitable for the Dutch government.

Following World War II and Japanese occupation, Indonesian nationalists declared independence and won recognition after four years of fighting.

### Interest groups

Dutch East Indies starts with the Landowners and Armed Forces in government. The Rural Folk and Intelligentsia are in opposition. The remaining interest groups are all marginalized.

The Industrialists have the Colonialist instead of the default Laissez-Faire.

East Indies is colonizing Celebes, where it has a claim.

## Strategy and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.10. |

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
East Indies
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
**Starting Situation**

The Dutch East Indies aren't particularly strong, but can easily be positioned to dominate Southeast Asia. Their overlord, the Netherlands, are relatively weak when compared to the other colonial nations (such as [France](France.md) or the [United Kingdom](United Kingdom.md)), but are still strong enough to be useful against the Southeast Asian minors. Its economic development isn't horrid, but has significant room for improvement.

**Grand Strategy**

The Dutch East Indies start out with an assortment of puppets and nearby uncolonized lands. It's in the player's best interest to colonize the rest of Southeast Asia itself if possible to deny potential hostile powers a foothold. Annexing its puppets will also help consolidate its disorganized starting situation. After that, it can target the independent Southeast Asian minors around it.

The Dutch East Indies has great port access and the ability to create goods that the European majors often struggle with (such as Dye), so there is enormous profit potential from trade and exports. The player can then use these revenues to boost whatever they see fit. Investing in the Dutch East India company navy would be a good call, given the archipelago nature of the region.

A major choice for the player to make is whether or not to cut loose from the Dutch. The Dutch are too small and weak to have any sort of parity with the other European powers, so the player may not have a choice in this matter. It's not unheard of for the Netherlands to be conquered by the other European powers. If the Dutch get declared on by a major power, it may not be advisable to attempt to assist them, as the player may not be strong enough to tip the scales.

Whether the player is independent or not, the player may try to push into islands of Oceania (such as Hawaii) or into the Southeast Asian mainland later on in the game. It is probably best to avoid provoking the British colonies in India and Australia unless they go independent, but most other nations should be relatively easy conquests as long as the player can keep the European majors out.

**Internal Politics**

Similar in nature to [East India](East India.md), the Dutch East Indies' official religion and primary ethnicity do not align with the land that it is on. Uprisings and radicalism are a significant threat.

Other than that, its starting Laws are relatively poor: Debt Slavery, Serfdom and Extraction Economy (although it can switch to Interventionism easily). The player should not have too much trouble getting the laws they want if they industrialize (and repeal Extraction Economy quickly). On the other hand, they do start with Cultural Exclusion and Total Separation, which will allow government-funded buildings to hire most pops, since the primary culture and religion of Dutch and Protestant are tiny minorities in their own country.

**Diplomacy**

The primary diplomatic threats in the early game are usually the British and French. [Prussia](Prussia.md) and [Austria](Austria.md) are usually content with battering each other for the German minors, and [Russia](Russia.md) is usually content with its internal issues, Central Asia and its Great Game with Britain. If the player can keep the British and French out of their affairs, consolidating most of Southeast Asia should be easy. China may attempt to intervene, but their military (while enormous) is usually so far behind technologically as to not be a major threat.

**Potential**

The Dutch East Indies starts as the strongest nation in a weak area, and that gives a lot of potential for consolidation and conquest. The player will be able to consistently produce in-demand trade goods throughout the game to pad their economy. If the player is able to consolidate their position, they will be a nation that has good technology, a good population, and a good military when their neighbors might have none of those.

**Weaknesses**

The Dutch East Indies may have trouble with radicalism from discrimination until it can loosen up its tolerance laws (i.e. passing [Multiculturalism](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Multiculturalism)). Where Multiculturalism might be a law to pass to bring in more immigrants for the European nations, it can be a bare necessity for the Dutch East Indies for internal stability.

Gobbling up all those weak nations gives high [Infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy) when compared to the immediate wealth boost of the conquered territories, and its overlord is arguably impotent against the rest of Europe.

### Independence

Like [East India](East India.md), the East Indies can also form a native country (in this case, [Java](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Java) or [Indonesia](Indonesia.md)) after becoming independent. In order not to lose land to its subjects, the East Indies must also annex all subjects or research Pan-nationalism. As annexing subjects without an infamy penalty is dependent on their low Liberty Desire, expect a slow pace of puppet annexation or large increases in infamy. Also, expect more revolts from puppets as they join each other to resist annexation.

### United Netherlands

As East Indies has Dutch primary culture, it can form the [United Netherlands](United Netherlands.md) by controlling the required states.
