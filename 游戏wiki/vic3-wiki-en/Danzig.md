**Danzig** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in Eastern Europe which is released as a split state within West Prussia which, at the start of the game, is owned by [Prussia](Prussia.md). It is is able to form the [North German Federation](North German Federation.md) and [Germany](Germany.md). As an alternative, Danzig can also form the [Confederation of the Rhine](Confederation of the Rhine.md).

## Core States

Danzig is released as a split state within West Prussia. The state of West Prussia has access to fish, wood, and sulfur.
