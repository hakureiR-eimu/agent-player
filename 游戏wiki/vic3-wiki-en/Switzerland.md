**Switzerland** is an minor power in Central Europe, in the mountainous terrain of the ![State trait mountain.png](/images/thumb/f/f1/State_trait_mountain.png/24px-State_trait_mountain.png) Alps. It is landlocked and borders [Austria](Austria.md), [France](France.md), [Sardinia-Piedmont](Sardinia-Piedmont.md) and [Baden](Baden.md).

Switzerland is a unique country in a continent of monarchs, being a [Democratic Republic](Government type.md), where the citizens decide who gets to rule them by democratic means. Switzerland is also a multicultural nation, with German, French, and Italian minorities coming together in a federal system of cantons.

Switzerland can form [Central Europe](Central Europe.md).[1]

## Historical background

Following the ![Invention napoleonic warfare.png](/images/thumb/3/30/Invention_napoleonic_warfare.png/24px-Invention_napoleonic_warfare.png) Napoleonic Wars, when Switzerland was turned into a French Puppet state, the Swiss nation underwent a process of "Restoration and Regeneration". Restoration referred to undoing the reform done under Napoleon and returning to the ![Legitimacy.png](/images/thumb/2/25/Legitimacy.png/24px-Legitimacy.png) federal system (Ancien Régime). Regeneration was after the ![Alert radicalized.png](/images/thumb/e/e9/Alert_radicalized.png/24px-Alert_radicalized.png) July Revolution in France in 1830, where the Restoration of the old system was heavily opposed by liberals, mainly in Protestant Cantons, in Switzerland, with many calling for a redrawing of the Cantons and their constituencies. Meanwhile, conservative forces, mainly in Catholic Cantons, have been fighting the new liberal movement as much as they can. Hopefully, these two sides can come to an agreement or mediate before something catastrophic happens.

## Formation

If Switzerland ceases to exist, it can be released by any country which controls West Switzerland. Its core states are West Switzerland and East Switzerland.

## Politics

Switzerland is one of only two [Republics](Power structure laws.md) in Europe at the start date (excluding the [Free cities](Government type.md) in Germany), the other being the [Ionian Islands](Ionian Islands.md), and one of the three starting Parliamentary Republics in the world – [Circassia](Circassia.md) and [Kaurna](Kaurna.md) are the others. As a Parliamentary Republic with Census Suffrage, Switzerland holds [elections](Elections.md), with the next one being in 1839.

Switzerland starts with political parties: Conservative Party (), and Liberal Democrats ().

### Interest groups

The Landvolk start in government. In opposition, the Evangelical Church, Armed Forces, and Landowners form the Conservative Party, and the Intelligentsia and Kleinbürger make up the Liberal Democrats. The Industrialists and Trade Unions are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Switzerland starts with a [treaty](Treaty.md) providing transit rights through [France](France.md), binding for 10 years. This is the only way, Switzerland can access the [world market](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/World_market).

### Industry and Resources

**Industry**

Swiss industry isn't large, having a single level of Paper Mills and Textile Mills, with slightly more development in [agricultural buildings](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Agricultural_buildings) such as Rye Farms, Livestock Ranches and a Logging Camp.

**Resources**

Switzerland has access to large amounts of Coal, Iron, and Lead, although no mines are built at game start.

## Military

The National Militia law provides Switzerland with a large number of conscript battalions.

Switzerland starts with 10 battalions and can call upon 33 conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st Swiss Army | 10 Line Infantry | — | Central Europe |

Switzerland starts with no Flotillas, being a landlocked country.

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.10. |

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Switzerland
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
Switzerland starts in a relatively stable situation politically and economically, but lacks sea access. Sandwiched between Great Powers [France](France.md) and [Austria](Austria.md), Switzerland's best means of gaining coast access is an invasion of [Sardinia-Piedmont](Sardinia-Piedmont.md). Although the latter has more manpower, Swiss Skirmish Infantry should be able to defeat Piedmontese Line Infantry if wielded effectively. A successful invasion will allow Switzerland to gain the Franco-Provençal homeland state of Savoy, the coastal state of Piedmont, and a small piece of the split Provence state, which may be a useful bargaining chip when dealing with France.

After 10 years, France might withdraw from the transit rights [treaty](Treaty.md), so it is important to maintain good relations with them and, if possible, renegotiate it.
