*"The Congress of Vienna established the German Confederation, a loose assortment of German intended to keep the peace in Central Europe. The Confederation in practice is dominated by the rivalry between Austria and Prussia. Can the smaller nations survive the coming conflict?"*

**Holstein** is an insignificant power and one of the smaller countries in continental Europe. It is the junior member of a personal union with [Denmark](Denmark.md), together with its northern counterpart [Schleswig](Schleswig.md).

As a North German country, Holstein can potentially form the [North German Federation](North German Federation.md), [Germany](Germany.md), [Confederation of the Rhine](Confederation of the Rhine.md) and [Central Europe](Central Europe.md).

### Interest groups

Holstein starts with the Landowners in government. The Evangelical Church, Landvolk, Intelligentsia, Armed Forces, Industrialists and Kleinbürger are in opposition, while the Trade Unions are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Holstein begins as a junior member of a Personal Union under [Denmark](Denmark.md).

## Military

Holstein has the Professional Army law.

Holstein starts with 4 Battalions and can call upon 1 Conscript. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions comprise a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| Holsteinische Armee | 2 Line Infantry 1 Cannon Artillery 1 Lancer | — | North Germany |

Holstein starts with no flotillas.
