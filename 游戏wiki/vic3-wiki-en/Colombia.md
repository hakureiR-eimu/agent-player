*"With the dissolution of Gran Colombia in 1830, New Granada stands alone and divided between its liberal and conservative factions. Will Colombia achieve greatness once more?"*

**Colombia**, called *New Granada* initially, is a minor power in South America, making up the modern states of Colombia and [Panama](Panama.md). Owning the state of Panama means it can potentially build the Panama Canal, but it may also make it the target of the ambitions of the great powers.

Colombia can form [Gran Colombia](Gran Colombia.md) after Nationalism has been researched as well as the [Federation of the Andes](Federation of the Andes.md); both require that it does not nationalize its culture.

Colombia, along with [Sweden](Sweden.md), [Belgium](Belgium.md), and [Dai Nam](Dai Nam.md), is one of the recommended countries for the *Learn the Game* objective.

## Historical background

Colombia is one of the three successors to Gran Colombia, along with Ecuador and Venezuela. Gran Colombia was proclaimed in 1819 after Simón Bolívar's victory over [Spanish](Spain.md) forces near Bogotá and officially formed two years later when its constitution was adopted. After the war with Spain concluded, regional frictions resurfaced and eventually led to the break-up of Gran Colombia in 1830.

## Formation

If Colombia ceases to exist, it can be [formed](Formable countries.md) by any country below Kingdom tier with North Andean as a primary culture[1] that controls 80% of the North Andean homelands.

Colombia can also be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country that controls Cundinamarca and at least two of Antioquia, Cauca, and Guaviare.

When released, Colombia reverts to North Andean, even if it nationalized its culture.

### Interest groups

Colombia usually starts off with an illegitimate government, due to only the Petite Bourgeoisie being in government, without a starting party. In opposition, there are two parties: the Landowners, Catholic Church, and Armed Forces as the Conservative Party, and the Intelligentsia and Rural Folk as the Liberal Party. The Industrialists and Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Armed Forces have the Caudillismo ideology in addition to their standard ideologies. The Landowners have the Elitist ideology instead of the default Paternalistic. The Landowners also have the Pro-Slavery ideology due to the starting Legacy Slavery.

### Diplomacy

Colombia neighbors [Venezuela](Venezuela.md) to the east, [Central America](Central America.md) to the north, [Ecuador](Ecuador.md) and [North Peru](North Peru.md) to the west, and the [Brazil](Brazil.md)ian rebels [Grão-Pará](Grão-Pará.md) to the south.

Colombia has no diplomatic ties in 1836, but it starts off with poor relations towards [North Peru](North Peru.md), [South Peru](South Peru.md) and [Spain](Spain.md). Colombia also starts with the modifier *Independence Not Recognized by Spain Independence Not Recognized by Spain Country modifier −10% Influence*, until [Spain](Spain.md) recognizes its independence, or Colombia becomes strong enough to disregard Spain.

[Brazil](Brazil.md) has a claim on Colombian Amazonas and might be aggressive to get it.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Colombia begins with almost no industry, having only a single Textile Mill and Furniture Manufactury in their capital Cundinamarca.

**Resources**

Colombia has access to a small amount of iron and a good amount of coal, but no other mined resources. Colombia's agricultural sector is slightly developed with a small number of plantations, and it can grow cotton (fabric), coffee, dye, sugar, and tobacco. Both rubber and a small amount of oil can be discovered once the required technologies are researched. Cundinamarca is the only state in South America where opium can be grown.

Colombia's early development is greatly hampered by the *Andes* and*Amazon Rainforest* state traits, making Railways a priority for continuous growth.

### Amazonas

[Brazil](Brazil.md) has a claim on Colombian Amazonas. If they are aggressive in the early game (after they have won their starting civil wars), they are much stronger than Colombia. If you cannot negotiate powerful protectors, when they start a [diplomatic play](Diplomatic play.md), it is usually worth it to simply give up the state. It is not very valuable anyway. Even if e.g. [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) or [France](France.md) are willing to defend you for an obligation, it might not be worth it. Since it's part of the Brazil region, Amazonas isn't required to hold for *Grander Colombia* Achievement.

If Brazil does not attack, one should work on integrating it. There is more than enough surplus [bureaucracy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Bureaucracy) and it takes 20 years to fully integrate, so it should be done as early as possible.

Alternatively, if one is playing aggressive, [Grão-Pará](Grão-Pará.md) can be annexed day 1 if you can beat Brazil to the punch. Alternatively, you can simply wait and hope that it wins the war so that it may be attacked later without having to race to Pará with a disadvantage. It (or [Amazonia](Amazonia.md) if you keep your homelands) can then be released as a puppet.

### Forming Gran Colombia

To form [Gran Colombia](Gran Colombia.md), one needs to control at least seven of the eight starting North Andean Homelands; these comprise four of Colombia's initial provinces as well as those of [Venezuela](Venezuela.md) and [Ecuador](Ecuador.md). It is enough to make them puppets, as they will then be annexed without gaining [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy) when forming Gran Colombia. Ecuador can be made a protectorate right away, but Colombia first has to achieve a higher rank than Venezuela in order to make them a protectorate. Either by raising to [Major Power](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Major_Power) or by reducing Venezuela to [Insignificant power](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Insignificant_power).

It is advised to attack Ecuador as early as possible. Beating Ecuador is trivial. The only issue is if someone intervenes, which Brazil is likely to do, due to their claim on Amazonas. Therefore it should be done, before Brazil finishes their civil wars. But you have to activate the only available [interest](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Interest) in *The Andes* first. The combined forces of Ecuador and Colombia, should be able to defend themselves against Brazil. Afterwards, beating Venezuela and making them a protectorate is easy, as long as no great power intervenes.

Note that it might be worth it to delay unification – even once it is possible – as none of Colombia, Ecuador, or Venezuela starts with any construction sectors making the base construction of 10 more valuable, and even a puppetted Ecuador and Venezuela will still produce a few buildings.

### After unification

After unification, it is possible to expand into [USCA](Central America.md), [Peru](Peru.md), [Haiti](Haiti.md) and [Bolivia](Bolivia.md). Forming the Federation of the Andes is also possible, but note that this prevents you from obtaining the *Grander Colombia* achievement.
