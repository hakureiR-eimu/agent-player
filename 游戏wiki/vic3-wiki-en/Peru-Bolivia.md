**Peru-Bolivia** is a special formable country in South America. If formed, it usually borders [Chile](Chile.md), [Argentina](Argentina.md) and [Guarani (GNI)](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Guarani_(GNI)) to the south, [Ecuador](Ecuador.md) and [Colombia](Colombia.md) to the north and [Brazil](Brazil.md) to the east. Peru-Bolivia can only be formed by [Bolivia](Bolivia.md) through the journal entry *The Peru-Bolivian Confederation*.

Peru-Bolivia can form [Federation of the Andes](Federation of the Andes.md).

## Historical background

The Peru-Bolivian Confederation came about due to a civil war in Peru in 1835, when General Felipe Salaverry revolted against President Luis Orbegoso. Orbegoso sought the support of Bolivian president Andrés de Santa Cruz, and together they defeated Salaverry, though Orbegoso had to submit Peru to confederation under the leadership of Santa Cruz and Bolivia.

The Confederation was declared in 1836 and its legal framework established in 1837; however, the Confederation was unstable from the beginning and did not last long. Chile declared war at the end of 1836, in part out of fear of the Confederation's strength and in part for economic reasons. Argentina independently declared war in 1837 over the issue of the Tarija territory and Santa Cruz's support for the opponents of Argentina's *de facto* leader Juan Manuel de Rosas. While the initial conflict was indecisive, North Peru rebelled in 1838. This revolt was defeated by the Chilean forces, who then supported former Peruvian president Agustín Gamarra as the leader of Peru again.

The forces of Chile and Gamarra defeated the army of Santa Cruz in 1839, officially ending the Peru-Bolivian Confederation. Gamarra soon after sought to conquer Bolivia in 1841, but was killed in battle.
