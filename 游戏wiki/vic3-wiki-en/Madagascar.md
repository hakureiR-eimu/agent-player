**Madagascar** (initially called *Merina Kingdom*) is an unrecognized regional power in East Africa. Due to its status as an island nation, it does not share any land borders with other nations.

## Formation

If Madagascar ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country that controls its capital, North Madagascar. It uses the Malagasy homelands as its core states.

## Strategy and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Madagascar
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
Madagascar starts off very backward, and are unable to trade with the world market despite having Mercantilism, because they have no ports and could not construct one. Fortunately, it's a matter of giving an Investment Rights to Great Britain, which can be done at game start, and they will build the ports immediately. After connecting with the world market, the game becomes much easier for Madagascar.

They can expand into the mainland Africa through cheese, bluff, or plain old conquest (which can take a while to develop the navy).
