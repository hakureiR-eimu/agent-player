**Mecklenburg-Strelitz** is a minor power in North Germany. It borders [Mecklenburg](Mecklenburg.md) and [Prussia](Prussia.md) in game start.

As a North German nation, it is able to form the [North German Federation](North German Federation.md) and [Germany](Germany.md). Alternatively, they can form [Confederation of the Rhine](Confederation of the Rhine.md).

### Interest groups

Due to Mecklenburg-Strelitz' complete lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Mecklenburg-Strelitz begins with the Landowners in government. The Kleinbürger, Landvolk, Intelligentsia, Armed Forces and the Evangelical Church are in opposition. The Industrialists and the Trade unions are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Mecklenburg-Strelitz starts in the *Zollverein* power bloc, lead by [Prussia](Prussia.md).

## Military

Mecklenburg-Strelitz has the Professional Army law.

Mecklenburg-Strelitz begins with 1 Battalions and can call upon no Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training.
