**United Tribes** is an unrecognized power in New Zealand which starts as protectorate of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md).

United Tribes is the only centralized Māori nation, thus able to form [Aotearoa](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Aotearoa). It can also form [Polynesia](Polynesia.md).

## Scripted content

Although it cannot form [Australia](Australia.md), United Tribes does have access to the Federate Australia decision and journal entry. This can potentially allow it to annex the Australian colonies.

### Interest groups

Due to United Tribes' complete lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
United Tribes typically starts with the Armed Forces in government, while the Anglican Church, Landowners, and Rural Folk are in opposition. The Intelligentsia, Petite Bourgeoisie, Trade Unions, and Industrialists are marginalized.

### Historical characters

United Tribes starts with no historical characters

### Diplomacy

United Tribes is a Protectorate of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) and thus part of the *British Empire* power bloc.

## Strategy and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.10. |
|---|---|

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
United Tribes
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
As the United Tribes you start with not much, only the split state of North Island. You **must** rush Colonization and try to get South Island. This will lead to a split state, but you can still get a large part of it. Then it is best to rush Nationalism as a second or third technology, possibly after Stock Exchange, to make use of the *Federate Australia* decision. Afterwards they might want to rush Quinine in order to colonize the New Guineas and the Pacific Islands as soon as possible. Before those, though, it is important to stake a colonial claim on a province in Australia proper, granting you a claim and allowing you to colonize it and gain other Australian subjects as neighbours (making Federalization much easier than having to be richer than the richest starting subject, New South Wales). At one point, you must take over the entire island which can be won via a war by making sure to get a *support independence* treaty with as many nations as you can for a chance. Alternatively, the Australian neighboring countries can be annexed peacefully one at a time through the *Federate Australia* decision.

Because United Tribes is ineligible to form Australia, upon completing the *Federate Australia* entry, United Tribes will become independent from Great Britain instead of becoming its dominion. The player may want to hold off from completing the entry and prepare for a more independent economy first.
