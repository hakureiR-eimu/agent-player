**Maharashtra** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in central India. It represents a restored Maratha Empire or a new union of the Marathi people.

Maharashtra can form [India](India.md) (BHT).

## Historical background

The Maratha Empire formed at the end of the 17th century. Although nominally a vassal of the Mughal Empire, the Marathas came to control much of central India and even influenced Mughal politics as that empire declined.

By the end of the 18th century, the Maratha Empire came into conflict with the British East India Company and eventually was soundly defeated. The Empire was effectively dismantled, its constituents becoming princely states under British control.

## Marathi minors

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.10. |

The Marathi minors are all unrecognized powers and have similar basic stats, starting technology, and politics. Most start with a split state in the Maratha core states, and each is a puppet of [East India](East India.md). They are all Principalities and Hindu. They generally start with the Zamindars in government with the Hindu Priesthood, Armed Forces, Intelligentsia, and Ryots in opposition, and the remaining interest groups marginalized.

The Hindu Priesthood has the Hindu Moralist ideology instead of the default Moralist. The Petite Bourgeoisie has the Modernizer ideology instead of the default Reactionary. The Intelligentsia has the Constitutionalist ideology instead of the default Republican.
