*"Tsar Nicholas I started his rule by crushing a revolt and now rules Russia in a vise-like grip. He espouses the doctrines of Orthodoxy, Autocracy and Nationality. Will this authoritarian Russia thrive in the 19th century or will a new, different Russia emerge?"*

Russia is a great power located on the fringes of the European continent. Russia is a long-time rival of the neighboring [Ottoman Empire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey), fighting over control of the Black Sea and the Caucasus. Russia spans the length of Siberia, making it one of the largest countries in the world, although internally disconnected. Russia is still in many ways a feudal society, with serfs working the land of the elite aristocracy.

The potential of Russia is as vast as the country itself, but if that potential will ever be used to its full advantage, by a ruling elite that holds onto the past, is anyone's guess.

Russia is one of four recommended countries for the *Egalitarian Society* [objective](Objective.md), along with [France](France.md), [Great Qing](China.md), and [Mexico](Mexico.md); and one of six eligible countries for the *Great Game* objective, the others being [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), [Persia](Persia.md), and the Afghan minors of [Kabul](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kabul), [Herat](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Herat), and [Kandahar](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kandahar).

## Historical background

After the Congress of Vienna concluded the bloody Napoleonic Wars, Russia stood as one of the great powers of Europe. Tsar Alexander I oversaw a great expansion of the Russian Empire, finally securing hold of Poland and asserting its dominance in the Siberian tundra. As the Western powers began rapid liberalization and industrialization, Russia rested on its laurels, maintaining the institution of Serfdom and the privileges of the aristocracy. Not everyone in Russia saw this as the right way and revolutionary fervor began to take hold in intellectual circles, this fervor exploded when Alexander I died in 1825. The Decembrist Revolt, hoping to bring Russia closer to Western progress, liberalisation, and the abolition of Serfdom, was utterly and brutally crushed by the new Tsar, Nicholas I. Nicholas would centralise his power after the revolt, making sure nothing went past him. As Russian society stands ever still, Sergey Uvarov came up with the program of "Orthodoxy, Autocracy, and Nationality", laying the foundation of a new Russian national education program, and firmly solidifying Nicholas I's beliefs in the mind of his subjects.

## Formation

36 of the 47 core states are needed in order to form Russia; Kuban and Alaska can each gain a Russian homeland, increasing both amounts by 1
If Russia ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country that controls its capital state, Ingria, and four other Russian homeland states. It can also be [formed](Formable countries.md) by any Russian culture country that controls 36 of the 47 Russian homelands. All Russian homelands except Altai are owned at least partly by Russia at the start of the game.

### Interest groups

Russia starts with the Gentry Assembly in government. The Orthodox Church, Armed Forces and Intelligentsia are in opposition. The Rural Folk, Petite Bourgeoisie, Industrialists and Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Orthodox Church has the Russian Orthodox Patriarchy ideology instead of the default Patriarchal. The Petite Bourgeoisie have the Mercantile ideology due to the starting Traditionalism.

### Diplomacy

Russia starts the game with a mutual rivalry with [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) and the [Ottoman Empire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey). The [treaties](Treaty.md) of Holy Alliance grant it a defensive pact with [Austria](Austria.md) and [Prussia](Prussia.md), binding until 1850.10.26. Russia also guarantees [Montenegrin](Montenegro.md) independence, in the treaty *Protection of Orthodox Christians*, bound for 35 years. It starts the game with an obligation from [Greece](Greece.md).

Russia is the overlord of the three Zhuz nations of [Uly Zhuz](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Uly_Zhuz), [Kishi Zhuz](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kishi_Zhuz) and [Orta Zhuz](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Orta_Zhuz) in Central Asia, who are protectorates. [Finland](Finland.md) is also under Russian rule through a personal union, as is [Baltic Governorates](United Baltic Provinces.md) as a puppet. Russia also has the chartered company subject [Russian American Company](Alaska.md).

The Caucasian nations of [Circassia](Circassia.md) and the [Caucasian Imamate](Chechnya.md) both have a truce with Russia, ending on 1 January 1856. Russia has claims on both these nations. Russia also has a claim on the state of Sakhalin, and as [China](China.md) and [Japan](Japan.md) also have a claim on the state, it is likely to be a source of tension between the three countries.

Russia's starting interests are hegemonic (level 5) in Russia; pervasive (level 4) in Eastern Europe and Siberia, influential (level 3) in Balkans, Central Asia, Central Europe, Greater Persia, Near East, and Northern Europe; engaged (level 2) in Northeast Asia, and observant (level 1) in Pacific Coast and Western Europe.

Russia has a pro-country lobby towards [Prussia](Prussia.md) () and anti-country lobbies towards [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) () and the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md) ().

Russia starts as the leader of the *Rossiyskaya Imperiya* power bloc. All members are subjects of Russia. It starts with the principles Vassalization I and Defensive Cooperation I.

### Industry and Resources

**Industry**

Russia is lagging behind in terms of industrialization, with over 75% of its population being peasants working in [subsistence farming](List of buildings.md). The largest industrial hub is the capital state of Ingria, although it has only 8 urban centers.

**Resources**

Russia is very rich in wood, iron, coal, and grain, thanks to the state traits in the Russian heartland, Ukraine and the Siberian states. Russia also gets access to gold in many different states as the game goes on.

## Strategies and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Russia
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
Russia is undeveloped – very underdeveloped – but has arguably the biggest potential in the entire game. A massive population, access to more or less every important resource and many strong [State traits](State traits.md) that covers a wide range of resources.

### Initial Investments

Russia lacks sufficient Construction Sectors, so building many of these early is a must. Luckily, Russia has plenty of wood producing states to develop. Especially those with the Russian Taiga Forest [trait](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/State_trait), giving a bonus to logging camps. Alternating between logging camps and construction sectors will be the building queue for a very long time. Afterwards, developing sufficient Tooling Workshops and Iron Mines is needed for all the new logging camps, and with time upgrading the Construction PM. When the necessary techs are researched, getting coal and steel added will greatly add to the construction loop. The [Russian unique company](List of flavored companies.md) *New Russia Company Ltd.* is very good for this purpose. Russia has many states that works as construction hubs. Kharkiv or Taurida are an obvious choice due to their role in establishing *New Russia Company Ltd.*. Otherwise, Perm, Kiev, Tula or Ural (once integrated) are also good.

It can help to negotiate [investment treaties](Treaty.md) with richer countries such as Prussia or the United States to get some help employing all of the peasants. These should however be cancelled, once the industrial base starts to get going and the domestic capitalists can take over.

After construction is sufficient, getting Paper for universities and government administration is crucial. With a bit of luck [Finland](Finland.md) decides to establish [Nokia](List of flavored companies.md) to help with this. If that happens, giving Finland investment rights is not a bad idea. Textile Mills are an obvious investment since one of Russia's main exports is fabric and main imports is clothing. The states of Rostov, Kuban, Armenia, and the surrounding states has the potential for both cotton Plantations and Silk Plantations. Afterwards, the Ukranian states are a good place to establish Food Industries, due to their [state trait](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/State_trait) improving agriculture.

During the game many Gold Fields will appear and disappear making space for Gold Mines. Building the mines is also a priority to increase the potential construction.

### Politics and technology

Russia has many backwater laws, and modernizing early is of crucial importance. It starts with insufficient [taxation capacity](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Taxation_capacity) in about half of its states and [MAPI](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/MAPI) is hurting the country a lot. This is due in part to Traditionalism limiting the taxation capacity. If Russia doesn't abolish that laws, it would go bankrupt if it constructed sufficient administrative buildings. Unfortunately, Russia's landed elite are deeply entrenched, so this is a long, hard fought battle. To help the bureaucracy, it is worth considering the [techs](Society technology.md) that increase the base [taxation](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Taxation) capacity. Getting to Railways quickly is also important as Moscow has insufficient infrastructure. Serfdom also strongly impacts Russia's potential development as the many peasants are unable to gain qualifications for most professions and thus become more productive workers in new industries. Afterwards getting a education system going is crucial to help Russia's abysmal [literacy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Literacy).

One way to liberalise is to slowly industrialise while waiting for Tsar Nicholas I to pass away (There's an event which kills him or gives him the Sickly trait). With Alexander's ascension, the next Landowners leader is likely to have the Market Liberal ideology, allowing Serfdom and Traditionalism to be repealed with Landowner support.

Russia also needs to repeal State Religion in order to increase base [acceptance](Acceptance.md), especially of the Polish Catholics, and to allow for a better school system than Religious Schools.

Getting some kind of colonial affairs to get Turkmenistan and Sakhalin is also important, although it is not an immediate priority, because there are not many countries capable of colonizing near Turkmenistan and Russia has sole claims on Sakhalin until [Japan](Japan.md) completes the [Honorable Restoration](Japanese content.md). Afterwards, Russia can try to go for Africa, but otherwise the institution can either be downscaled or abolished completely.

#### Un-integrated states

Russia has a lot of un-integrated states, but early it is not advised to integrate immediately them as bureaucracy is very limited and establishing institutions is much more important. One exception to this is Ural due to all its resources (and state trait) crucial for the construction loop.

Once the third Administrative PM has been unlocked, Russia can probably integrate the remaining states, or at least the vast majority. Especially the Polish states as well as the ones with lacking gold mines are a priority.

### Targets of War

[Circassia](Circassia.md) and Russia starts with a very long truce, and as soon it runs out, it is advised to immediately take the provinces, as there is list of negative events associated with the [Journal Entry](Russian content.md). Russia has claims on the states, so it is cheap in [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy) to take.

Otherwise, Russia doesn't actually need to expand. It is already an absolutely massive country with more peasants than they could possibly feed. It is however worth it to consider expanding into Central Asia ( [Persia](Persia.md) and [Afghanistan](Afghanistan.md) especially) to gain access to their profitable Opium and Dye production, as these resources are not found naturally anywhere in Russia. Having states along the Indian Ocean will also be a great staging ground for Russia's navy. If Persia is taken as a [puppet](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Puppet), it is a good idea to *not* decrease their autonomy at the first opportunity, in the hopes that they will take [Herat](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Herat) which gives them a bunch of claims on Afghan states, which they will likely go far.

In Eastern [Austria](Austria.md), there are Ukrainian POPs and they might therefore also be a good target. Alternatively, siding with Austria in the German Brothers' War in exchange for Upper Silesia can be a good idea, as it is one of the best provinces in the game.

Russia is also involved in the unification of [Romania](Romania.md), in that it will be asked to cede territory to the fledgling new nation. Crushing [Wallachia](Wallachia.md) and [Moldavia](Moldavia.md) or otherwise dominating the Romanian states would almost certain pit Russia against Austria and the Ottomans. Turkish Kars can also be taken, as it is a split state and has decent agricultural potentials. Keep in mind that in 1866 if the Ottoman [Tanzimat Reforms](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Tanzimat_Reforms) fail, all their subjects gain independence, and this can even happen during a war for those exact subjects, potentially invalidating any [war goals](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/War_goals). Releasing [Bulgaria](Bulgaria.md) comes with an [event](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Event) giving instant Leverage, so adding them to the Russian power bloc, and potentially later subjugating them should be possible.

The Ottomans control the Bosporus, restricting the movement of Russia's Black Sea fleet. Grabbing control of the Bosporus should be considered, particular after the Ottomans become an unrecognized power.

Russia has a vested interest in preventing the formation of [Scandinavia](Scandinavia.md), if only to protect its personal union junior [Finland](Finland.md). Note that if a Scandinavian unification play is started, Russia is not automatically drawn in to protect Finland; you must join the war manually. Russia may choose to further dominate [Denmark](Denmark.md) and [Sweden](Sweden.md) for the Sound toll and control of Schleswig-Holstein (together with [Lübeck](Lübeck.md)) for the construction of the Kiel canal.

Russia has a unique opportunity to attack [Qing](China.md). When Great Britain starts a diplomatic play for a treaty port due to the opium ban, Russia can start her own play on any Qing objective. Britain won't side with Qing against Russia, while the opium addiction debuffs will devastate Qing armies even before the first shot is fired. When Nationalism is researched, Russia will get claims on some north eastern Chinese states (modern day Vladivostok), which contain Gold Mines. Taking [Korea](Korea.md) is also possible either directly owned or as a subject.

The [Pacification of the Steppes JE](Russian content.md) when completed gives claims on a list of Central Asian states. They are not particularly valuable, but the claims make it cheap in [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy) and as barely anyone has a declared interest in the area, taking them is likely to be completely problem free.

### War with Great Britain

Due to the mutual rivalry with [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), it is very likely that Russia will find themselves at war with them for one reason or another. Due to Britain's magnificent navy, and the fact that the Russian navy is split in two (Baltic Sea and Black Sea), facing naval invasions in Skt. Petersburg is a risk that has to be adressed in any war with Britain. It is therefore important not to send all armies to the front, and instead keeping some back to fight in the [capital](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Capital). With Russia travel time for armies is a serious concern, so when the naval invasion notification comes up, it might be too late to react.

An alternative is to grab Scania from Sweden, and make Denmark a protectorate so that Russia can lockdown the Sound.

### Late game industry

A major concern for Russia is the lack of potential rubber.

On the other hand, the state of Baku is one of the best producing states in the entire game, and it enables the [flavored company Branobel](List of flavored companies.md), which is very good.

### The Great Game

|  | Available only with the [Sphere of Influence](Sphere of Influence.md) DLC enabled. |
|---|---|

Russia has a slight advantage in the Great Game, in that it can more easily establish a land connection through the Central Asian and Persian regions. If it can grab Bampur from [Makran](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Makran) and Fars and Hormuz from [Persia](Persia.md) and [Oman](Oman.md), it can prevent Great Britain from reinforcing the regions by land and force the British to use naval invasions instead. To compliment this strategy, consider avoiding a land border with [East India](East India.md), as this allows East India to add its armies to assist Britain. Another opening move is to immediately puppet Persia and then either letting them expand due to their claims or taking states and giving them to Persia.

Also, consider failing the [Kazakh Zhuzes](Kazakhstan.md) Journal Entry to force a rebellion. By defeating the rebellion, Russia can annex their states quicker than the diplomatic route, and gain claims on surrounding states, hastening the creation of the land connection. Alternative, Russia can improve relations with them. Doing that and building maybe 1 or 2 buildings in each country should be enough to lower the liberty desire enough, to avoid a war altogether. Then Russia can instead focus on China or Persia.
