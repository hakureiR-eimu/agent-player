This article is [timeless](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Category:Timeless) and should be accurate for any version of the game.

**Map Modes** are various toggleable overlays which allow the player to view a specific property of a state or country. There are over 40 different map modes. Many map modes can be accessed a variety of ways and the descriptions below are not exhaustive. An orange-yellow lining marks the borders of countries at war or on opposite sides of a [diplomatic play](Diplomatic play.md).

## List of selectable map modes

Selectable map modes can be chosen and locked from the small world map button to the right of the trade lens at the bottom center of the screen, selecting a map mode this way will also open the ledger to the relevant page.

### Countries - At Peace

This map mode is the default option for times of peace. This map displays country names and colors countries according to owner (subjects share the color of their overlord).

The countries - at peace map mode.

### Country Attitude

Main article:
Diplomacy
This map shows the [attitude](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Attitude) to the selected country from other countries. Hovering over a country will show more information about their attitude and strategic desire. To see the attitudes of countries towards another country select the country and click the diplomacy tab.

Viewing attitudes towards Prussia as Great Britain.
The country attitude map mode viewed as Prussia.

### Country Relations

Main article:
Diplomacy
This map shows the [relations](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Relations) to the selected country from other countries. Hovering over a country will show more information about the attitude. To see the relations of countries towards another country select the country and click the diplomacy tab.

### Cultures Overview

Main article:
Culture
The cultures overview map mode and tooltip.
This map shows the most populous [culture](Culture.md) in a [state](State.md). Hovering over a state will show the population of each present culture and their political strength.

### Global GDP

This map shades states white to green according to their [gross domestic product](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/GDP) with higher GDP resulting in a darker green color. Hovering over a state will show the current annual GDP contribution, the percentage of that state's GDP in relation to the country's total GDP, and the top contributing [goods](Goods.md) to that state's GDP.

The Global GDP map mode and tooltip.

### Infamy

Main article:
Infamy
This map shades countries white to red according to their [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy) with higher infamy resulting in a darker red color. Hovering over a nation will give more information about it's infamy and it's effects.

### Literacy

Main article:
Literacy
This map shades states red to green according to the percentage of literate pops with higher literacy resulting in a darker green color. Hovering over a state will show the exact percentage of literate pops.

The literacy map mode and tooltip.

### Loyalists

This map shows how many Pops in your states **only** are [loyalists](Loyalist.md). Hovering over a state will show how many Pops are loyal.

### Markets

Main article:
Market
This map displays market names and colors countries by the market they belong to. Hovering over a country will show the flow of goods into and out of other markets as well as highlighting their used shipping lanes. When zooming in you will also see the specific goods and amounts of your owned trade routes over the market your route trades with.

The markets map mode showing the flow of goods and shipping lanes used by Great Britain.

### Migration

*Main article: Migration*

This map shades states red to green according to their migration attraction with higher attraction resulting in a darker green color. Hovering over a state gives a detailed breakdown of the migration attraction and it's value relative to other states in the same market.

### Military

This map shows your owned armies and fleets and their on map location. If you are at [war](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/War) it will highlight enemy and allied countries and show the locations of their armies and fleets. If you have an ongoing [diplomatic play](Diplomatic play.md) it will also highlight countries with an active interest in the region allowing them to participate in the play.

### National GDP

This map performs the same as Global GDP except it shades only your owned states.

### Pollution Impact

This map shades state white to red according to the impact of pollution. Hovering over a state shows the current pollution impact percentage, target percentage, daily change rate, total generated pollution, arable land, effects, and reduction percentage from the health system institution.

### Population

This map shades states from white to green according to their population with more populous states resulting in a darker green color. Hovering over a state shows the population, projected growth, [migration](Migration.md), [birth rate](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Birthrate), mortality, unemployed, peasants, and qualifications.

The population at the start of the game

### Radicals

This map shows how many Pops in your states **only** are [radicals](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Radicals). Hovering over a state will show how many Pops are radical.

### Standard of Living

Main article:
Standard of living
This map shades states red to green according to their average standard of living with higher averages resulting in a darker green color. Hovering over a state shows the average standard of living, average minimum expected, and percentage of pops below the minimum expected.

The standard of living in each state at the start of the game

### States

This map shades each state individually according to a color scheme based on the colors from the countries - at peace map mode. Split states are shaded according to the owner of the specific provinces (i.e. Pondicherry is a shade of blue to match France but Madras is a shade of red to match Great Britain, the overlord of the British East India Company).

The states at the start of the game

### Strategic Regions

Main article:
Strategic Region
This map colors states according to the [strategic region](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Strategic_regions) they belong to. Zooming in shows all countries with an active interest in each strategic reason.

The regions at the start of the game

## Context specific map modes

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.5. |
|---|---|

Some map modes are context specific and open automatically when taking certain actions such as using the [lens](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Lens) button or viewing details about a good. Many of these map modes use the same color scheme for the selected interaction; orange indicates the area cannot be interacted with, typically because it already meets the condition (i.e. automatic interests), green indicates it can be interacted with, blue are areas in the process of implementing the selected interaction, red are currently ineligible areas but that can typically meet the condition with limited changes, and white are areas that are ineligible and cannot easily meet the condition. Hovering over states will provide additional details about the effect of the selected interaction or details on why a state is ineligible. When a description below says states are colored according to the interaction or possible interactions it is using this color scheme.

### Production lens

Clicking the production lens opens the production in states map mode. This map shades states from white to green according to GDP with a higher GDP resulting in a darker green color. Additionally states with state traits will have the icon appear on the map. Hovering over the trait icon provides details of the effects. Hovering over a state shows it's incorporation status, unused arable land, peasants, unemployed, GDP, and percentage of GDP relative to the country total. Selecting a building for construction will open the construction interaction map mode detailed below.

### Political lens

Clicking the political lens opens the political map mode. This map shades your owned states white to green according to the total political strength with higher political strength resulting in a darker green color. Zooming in shows active decrees as well as interest group icons and their clout, Hovering over a state shows the total of each interest groups political strength in the state. Selecting a building for construction will open the construction interaction map mode detailed below.

#### Issue decree

Selecting a decree for issue will show icons on states with active decrees and color your owned states according to possible interactions using the selected decree.

#### Incorporate state

Clicking incorporate state will color your states according to the incorporation interaction.

#### Move capital

Clicking change capital will color your states according to the move capital interaction.

#### Move market capital

Clicking move market capital colors states according to the move market capital interaction.

#### Reset production methods

Clicking reset production methods will highlight states green with production methods not matching your most commonly selected. Hovering over a state will detail which production methods will be changed and to what.

### Diplomatic lens

Clicking the diplomatic lens opens the diplomatic map mode except when using the diplomatic demands tab which opens the strategic regions map mode detailed above. The diplomatic map mode shows other countries attitudes to you as well as current wars and diplomatic plays. Hovering over a state will show more information about the state's incorporation status, owning country's attitude towards you, current relations value, AI strategies, and which cultures consider the state a homeland.

#### Declare interests

Clicking declare interests shows each strategic region and colors them according to the declare interest interaction. Hovering over a region provides information on how many interests you have declared out of your maximum.

#### Establish colony

Clicking establish colony shows all potentially colonizable states and colors them according to the establish colony interaction. Zooming in will show all currently growing colonies as well as the flag of the owning country and the remaining days to finish colonization of the province. Hovering over a growing colony shows details about the tension and growth rate.

#### Demand on a country

Selecting a diplomatic demand targeting a country colors countries according to possible interactions relating to the selected diplomatic demand. Hovering over a country provides details related to the ensuing diplomatic play.

#### Demand on a state

Selecting diplomatic demands targeting a state colors states according to possible interactions relating to the selected diplomatic demand. Hovering over a state provides details related to the ensuing diplomatic play.

#### Diplomatic action

Selecting a diplomatic action colors states according to the interaction. Hovering over a country shows the influence cost and provides details about a country's acceptance score (when applicable).

### Military lens

Clicking the military lens opens the military map mode detailed above.

#### Designate strategic objective

Clicking designate strategic objective colors states green that can be designated a strategic objective.

#### Create military formation

Clicking create army colors green HQs where you can build an army.

### Budget

Clicking the budget window shows the taxation revenue and government expenses on each of your owned states. Hovering over either number provides a more detailed breakdown.

### Local goods prices

Clicking on a good from the market tab shows the local price of that good in each state. States are colored from blue to orange with higher local prices resulting a darker orange color. Hovering over a state shows more details about the price relative to the market, production, consumption, and the base price.

### National cultures

Clicking on a culture from the cultures window shades owned states from white to green according to the population of the selected culture with higher population resulting in a darker green. Additionally each state the selected culture considers a homeland will show the flag of the country that currently owns it. Hovering over a state shows the current population of the selected culture.

### Religion

Selecting a religion shades states from white to green according to the population of the selected religion with higher population resulting in a darker green. Hovering over a state shows the current population of the selected religion.

### National Religion

This map performs the same as religion except it shades only your owned states.

### Interest groups

Selecting an interest group shades owned states from white to the interest group color (i.e. purple for landowners or orange for industrialists) according to political strength with more political strength resulting in a darker color. Hovering over a state shows the political strength and population of the selected interest group.

### Construction Interaction

Selecting a building for construction will show the current and max level (when applicable) of the building in each state with selected building type. The map will also shade states where the selected building can be constructed green with higher productivity resulting in a darker green color.
