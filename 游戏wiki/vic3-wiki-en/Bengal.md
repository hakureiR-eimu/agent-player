**Bengal** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in northeast India. It represents a restored Bengal Sultanate or Bengal Subah.

Bengal can form [India](India.md) (BHT).

It can be formed either the conventional way by a Bengali country, or by the [British East Indian Company](East India.md) via the Journal Entry [The End of British Rule?](Colonial Indies content.md).

## Historical context

The Bengal emerged as an independent state from the decline of the Mughal Empire at the beginning of the 18th century. In the mid-1700s, as the [British](Great Britain.md) and [French](France.md) East India Companies prepared to fight, the Nawab of Bengal ordered them to stop then occupied the British colony of Calcutta. In response, the British sent an expedition to exert their power and supported a coup against the Nawab. After defeating the Nawab, the British installed their own ruler. A few decades later, the East India Company assumed direct rule of Bengal.

Prior to the British conquest, Bengal was among the richest parts of India, with a proto-industrial base for many goods. Under the East India Company, the economy shifted to raw material production, with finished goods mostly imported from Britain.

## Formation

Two of the three core states are needed in order to form Bengal
Bengal can be formed by the following country when it fully controls two core states.

| Country | Capital | Primary Cultures | State Religion | Tier | Type | Core states |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cooch Behar** | West Bengal | Bengali | Hindu | Principality | Unrecognized | Split state in West Bengal |

If Bengal is formed by Cooch Behar, its state religion remains Hindu instead of becoming the default Sunni.

Bengal may also appear as part of a rebellion against the [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company). If Bengal forms this way, it is recognized.

Bengal may also be [released](Releasable countries.md), in which case Assam is not used as a core state.

Cooch Behar is the only country that can form Bengal. It starts with a split state in West Bengal. It is an unrecognized power. Cooch Behar generally starts with the Zamindars in government with the Hindu Priesthood, Armed Forces, Ryots, and Intelligentsia in opposition, and the remaining interest groups marginalized.

The Hindu Priesthood has the Hindu Moralist ideology instead of the default Moralist. The Petite Bourgeoisie has the Modernizer ideology instead of the default Reactionary. The Intelligentsia has the Constitutionalist ideology instead of the default Republican.
