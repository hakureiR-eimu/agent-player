**Turtle Island** is a special [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in North America.

## Journal

Turtle Island can only be formed through the journal entry *The Nations of Turtle Island*. Thus, unlike other formable nations, it does not require particular states to be controlled, instead allowing any indigenous North American nation to form it. Additionally, it keeps the primary cultures of the nation that forms it, rather than changing to the defined primary cultures. Like regular formations, its state religion remains the same the as the country that forms it, rather than changing to Protestant by default.

For ourselves and our brothers to survive, we must band together. Only united will we be able to resist the advances, both diplomatic and forceful, of the colonizing powers on our doorsteps.
