## Struggle for the Highveld

There are four different groups of countries involved in the Struggle for the Highveld: the ![Flag GBR uk.png](/images/thumb/6/6e/Flag_GBR_uk.png/24px-Flag_GBR_uk.png) [Cape Colony](South Africa.md), the Boer countries ( [Potchefstroom](Transvaal.md), [Winburg](Oranje.md) and [Natalia](Natalia.md), when it [appears](Southern Africa content.md)), the Griqua countries ( [Waterboersland](Griqualand.md) and [Philippolis](Philippolis.md)), and the natives ( [Zulu](Zulu.md), [Swaziland](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Swaziland) and [Basutoland](Basutoland.md)). There are slight differences depending on the specific group, but the rewards are all the same.

Flavor text:
The warriors beat on their shields, soothing their rattled nerves, before they charged once more at the laager, crying the name of their [ruler]. As they did, the fire and thunder from the laager intensified. Muskets emerged from behind the wagons firing bullets, loose buckshot, anything that could be frantically loaded and flung at the charging enemy. Any warrior who cleared this hail of fire and scaled the thornbushes to reach the wagons was met with the swing of hatchets. The smoke of the guns mixed with spilled blood to create a thick haze, making attackers and defenders alike breath death.
When the cries of battle ended and the smoke cleared, among the mangled bodies strewn on the battlefield laid the ambitions of [Mzilikazi], as dead as the warriors who had charged the laager that day.
