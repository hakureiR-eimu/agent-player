*"Now independent from both Spain and Gran Colombia, Venezuela must carve out its own destiny. Can a stable government be formed, or is Venezuela doomed to be ruled by a succession of caudillos?"*

**Venezuela** is a minor power in South America. It borders [Colombia](Colombia.md) to the west, [Grão-Pará](Grão-Pará.md) to the south and the [British](Great Britain.md) colony in Guyana to the east.

Venezuela can form [Gran Colombia](Gran Colombia.md) after Nationalism has been researched, and [Federation of the Andes](Federation of the Andes.md) after Pan-nationalism has been researched.

## Historical background

Venezuela is one of the three successors to Gran Colombia, along with Colombia and Ecuador. Gran Colombia was proclaimed in 1819 after Simón Bolívar's victory over [Spanish](Spain.md) forces near Bogotá and officially formed two years later when its constitution was adopted. After the war with Spain concluded, regional frictions resurfaced and eventually led to the break-up of Gran Colombia in 1830.

## Formation

If Venezuela ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country controlling its capital state of Miranda. Its core states include Miranda, Bolívar, and Zulia.

Venezuela is a Presidential Oligarchy, with President José María Vargas supporting the Intelligentsia. Venezuela starts with the *Age of Caudillos Age of Caudillos Country modifier +250% Political Strength of Officers +50% Armed Forces Political Strength +10% Armed Forces Pop Attraction* modifier, increasing the political strength of the Armed Forces greatly.

If Venezuela gets a law that allows for voting in Elections (except for Single-Party State), the law will automatically be amended with Electoral Clientelism (), which increases authority but greatly reduces electoral confidence, representing the deeply entrenched electoral rigging system present in Venezuelan politics.

### Interest groups

Venezuela begins with the Intelligentsia in government. The Armed Forces, Catholic Church, Landowners, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Rural Folk are in opposition, while the Industrialists and Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Armed Forces have the Caudillismo ideology in addition to their standard ideologies. The Landowners have the Elitist ideology instead of the default Paternalistic. The Landowners also have the Pro-Slavery ideology due to the starting Legacy Slavery.
