*"South Australia was granted its own administration only two years ago, separating from New South Wales. What does the future hold for Australia?"*

**South Australia** is an insignificant power located in Oceania.

South Australia is able to form [Australia](Australia.md).

## Scripted content

As an Australian country, South Australia receives the journal entry and decision both named *Federate Australia*.

### Interest groups

The Armed Forces are in government. The Squattocracy, Anglican Church, Rural Folk, and Intelligentsia are in opposition. The Petite Bourgeoisie, Trade Unions, and Industrialists are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

South Australia begins as a colony of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) and thus part of the *British Empire* power bloc.

South Australia starts with claims on the state of South Australia.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.12. |

South Australia has the Peasant Levies law.

South Australia starts with one Battalions and can call on no Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st South Australian Army | 1 Line Infantry | — | Oceania |

South Australia starts with no flotillas.
