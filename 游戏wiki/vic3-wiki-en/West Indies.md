**West Indies** is a small [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in the Caribbean. It may be formed by any Afro-Caribbean country below Kingdom tier.

## Scripted content

This event only fires if West Indies is released from an applicable country.

id

### A Constitution of Freedom

For centuries the [African Diaspora culture] people have toiled under the yoke of the whip, even while yearning for freedom. With the establishment of an independent [Country], the faint hope is now a mere pen-stroke away from becoming reality, as a proposal for an emancipatory constitution have been presented to the President.
---

Flavor text:
1) The people inhabiting [Country] hereby agree to form themselves into a free state sovereign and independent of any other power in the universe.
2) Every resident person in the territory of [Country] is free in full right.
3) Slavery in [Country] is forever abolished.
---

|---|
| Trigger conditions: None |
| Always enabled: Freedom from slavery shall be enshrined in the constitution. [Country] enables Slavery Banned 2% of [African Diaspora culture] pops become more loyal Enabled if: Has Autocracy or Oligarchy enacted Who then shall work the fields? I think not. ## Formation

All of the two core states are needed in order to form West Indies; dashed states are additional claimed homelands
The following countries can form West Indies when they fully control both core states.

| Country | Capital | Primary Cultures | State Religion | Tier | Type | Core States | Exists at start |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bahamas** | Bahamas | Afro-Caribbean | Protestant | Principality | Colonial | Bahamas | ![No](/images/thumb/0/04/No.png/24px-No.png) |
| **Jamaica** | Jamaica | Afro-Caribbean | Protestant | Principality | Colonial | Jamaica | ![No](/images/thumb/0/04/No.png/24px-No.png) |
| **Miskitia** | Nicaragua | Miskito, Afro-Caribbean | Animist | Principality | Unrecognized | Split state in Nicaragua | ![Yes](/images/thumb/7/77/Yes.png/24px-Yes.png) |

West Indies may also be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country that controls both of its core states. If Miskitia forms West Indies, its religion remains Animist.
