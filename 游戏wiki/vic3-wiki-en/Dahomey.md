**Dahomey** is an unrecognized power in West Africa. It directly borders [Borgu](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Borgu) to the north, [Ashanti](Ashanti.md) and the decentralized [Ewe](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Ewe) to the to the west, and [Oyo](Oyo.md) to the west. The [Danish](Denmark.md) African colony in Togo is also close by.

## Formation

If Dahomey ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country that controls its sole core state, Dahomey.

### Interest groups

Due to Dahomey's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Dahomey begins with the Landowners and Devout in government. The Armed Forces, Petite Bourgeoisie and Rural Folk are in opposition while the rest are marginalized.

## Military

Dahomey has the Peasant Levies law.

Dahomey starts with 7 Battalions and can call upon 4 Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions comprise a single army.
