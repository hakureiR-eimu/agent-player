## The Carlist War

This journal entry represents the historical [First Carlist War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Carlist_War), between the [Carlists](Carlist Spain.md) and the [liberals](Spain.md). It is available to both countries at game start.

Uniquely, the war between the two factions is represented in game as a guerilla war. Guerilla wars are a special kind of war subject to a set of rules distinct from regular warfare. The following general rules are applied during a guerilla war:

- Battles are much smaller in size and created at a lower pace.
- The Skirmish, Ambush and In Disarray [battle conditions](Land warfare.md) can occur, all of which greatly reduce casualties taken in battle.
- Victories do not grant Occupation Score.

The war can be escalated into a regular war through the buttons in the journal below.

Since the War of Independence, Spain has been trapped in an oft-violent struggle between "the two Spains", the first loyal to Church and Crown, and the second to Enlightenment and Constitution.
