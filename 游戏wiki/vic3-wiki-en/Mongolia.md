**Mongolia** is an unrecognized [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in East Asia. It may be released by a country that controls its capital state or revolt as the cultural secession country of Mongols. At the start date, both of its core states are controlled by [Great Qing](China.md).

Mongolia may also be released if China [shatters into warlord states](Chinese content.md).
