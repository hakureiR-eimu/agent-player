*"Disputing the young Queen Isabella's right to the throne, Carlos, the late King Ferdinand's brother, has rallied conservatives across the nation with the intent to march on Madrid. Will this grand expedition succeed, or is Spain to be given over to liberalism?"*

**Carlist Spain** is a minor power in Southwest Europe. Championing traditionalist and conservative values, the Carlists find themselves facing the Isabelline government of liberal [Spain](Spain.md) in Madrid, through [guerrilla warfare](Spanish content.md).

## Scripted content

Main article:
Spanish content
Carlist Spain begins with [the Carlist War](Spanish content.md) journal entry, shared with liberal [Spain](Spain.md). Should they win the conflict, the rest of Spanish content will be available to the Carlists as well.

Carlist Spain is an Absolute Monarchy, championing traditional-conservative laws such as Autocracy and Subjecthood. However, unlike liberal [Spain](Spain.md), Carlist Spain does start with Slavery Banned. It is ruled by King Carlos de Borbón-Anjou, who supports the Landowners.

### Interest groups

Carlist Spain begins with the Landowners, the Armed Forces and the Catholic Church in government, with the Industrialists, the Petite Bourgeoisie, the Intelligentsia and the Rural Folk in opposition. The Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Landowners have the Carlist ideology instead of the default Paternalistic, and indeed most characters in Carlist Spain are Carlists, making them all more conservative than normal, supporting Subjecthood, Traditionalism and Autocracy. Additionally, the Armed Forces have the Conservative Praetorian alongside their default ideologies, making them very reactionary as well. The Petite Bourgeoisie have the Mercantile ideology due to the starting Traditionalism.

### Diplomacy

Carlist Spain begins in a [guerrilla war](Spanish content.md) with [liberal Spain](Spain.md). Several of their starting states are surrounded by Spanish territory. Aside from that, they also border [France](France.md).

Carlist Spain has pro-country lobbies towards the [Papal States](Papal States.md) () and [Austria](Austria.md) ().

In order to prevent Carlist Spain from suffering from famines and constant resource shortages in the early game, its states begin with the *Raided Grain Storages Raided Grain Storages Country modifier +10 Grain Sell Orders* modifier. Aragon also begins with *Ad Hoc Manufacturing Ad Hoc Manufacturing Country modifier +15 Small Arms Sell Orders*, and País Vasco has *Ad Hoc Manufacturing Ad Hoc Manufacturing Country modifier +15 Small Arms Buy Orders* and *Pyrenean Smuggling Pyrenean Smuggling Country modifier +200 Blockade Resistance +5 Infrastructure +20 Trade Capacity +2 Weekly Trades*. These modifiers are removed upon winning the Carlist War.

### Industry and Resources

**Industry**

Carlist Spain starts off with almost all its buildings in País Vasco, with only a few Wheat Farms in Aragon and Catalonia. Naturally, however, after winning their war against Spain, they will inherit all of the Spanish starting buildings - which aren't many either, and don't include any heavy industry.

**Resources**

País Vasco has a lot of iron, and its state trait makes its Iron Mines some of the best in the world. Aside from that, the rest of Spain is rich in other mineral resources, though they're scarcely developed, and lacks crucial late-game resources such as oil and rubber.

## Military

Carlist Spain has the Professional Army law.

Carlist Spain starts with 60 Battalions and can call upon 1 Conscript. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions are divided into three armies.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ejército del Norte | 26 Line infantry 3 Mobile Artillery 1 Hussars 1 Cuirassiers | Vicente González Moreno Rafael Maroto | Iberia |
| Ejército del Maestrazgo | 15 Line Infantry 1 Dragoons 1 Cuirassiers | Ramón Cabrera | Iberia |
| Ejército de Cataluña | 8 Irregular Infantry 4 Line Infantry | Juan Antonio de Urbiztondo | Iberia |

Carlist Spain starts with no Flotillas, being landlocked.

### General aim for the civil war:

Aim to expand the war as soon as possible and begin the Royal Expedition- The longer the war lasts the more of your much smaller population will be attrition'd, with a hard time limit of 5 years due the expiry of modifiers such as Ad-hoc manufacturing.

### Details:

Set Military Wages to Maximum. Carlist Spain does however have more money than it can use, and will have about 2k positive income at minimum taxes and maximum Government and Military Wages.

Disband the 8 irregulars from Catalonia (In Army Ejército de Cataluña) as they will lower your average stats (And Catalonia does not have Ad-hoc manufacturing for local small arms to upgrade these units to Line Infantry.)

Upgrade the Hussar in Ejército del Norte to Cuirassiers. It may be possible to upgrade to Lancers but as the war drags on an iron shortage is very possible.

Make sure all Cavalry and Artillery are in 1 army, probably Ejército del Norte- this makes an Army with as much Offense stat as possible.

Enact Conscription decree and hire 6 artillery in País Vasco to increase the offensive power of your attack army.

Transfer Rafel Maroto to Army Ejército del Maestrazgo for use as a defensive army comprised entirely of Line Infantry, or at the very least do NOT merge all units as, if you give mobilization goods, the barracks in the isolated state regions will suffer goods shortages. This makes use of the fact that Carlist Aragon, although isolated, has Ad-hoc manufacturing and so can supply the line infantry in the state.

Assign Rafel Maroto (Tactful) to defense Order and Ramon Cabrera (Wrathful, Experienced Offensive planner) to advance.

Begin passing a law that the Armed Forces likes, such as Dedicated Police Force, to activate the Armed Forces trait Patriotic Fervor for more unit stats. After the first few engagements, set the Maize farm Harvesting PM method in País Vasco (Basque) to use Tools in order to free up additional manpower for the army.

You can set the PMs for the Wheat Farms in your isolated state region to Orchards for increased profitability.

### WITHOUT [Iberian Twilight] DLC:

Carlists has no advantages, as with no unique JEs after being victorious Carlist Spain is simply Spain with an even higher barrier to industrialization due to the Carlist Character Ideology favoring medieval laws such as Guilds, Traditionalism and Serfdom. However, this was the historical beliefs of the Carlists of this time period, and as the rebellion was crushed there is no precedents that could indicate otherwise.

### WITH DLC:

Modern Industrialization is still hard, but income can be supplemented if one chooses Reconquista and begins successfully vassalizing the former Spanish Colonies. It may be possible to provoke a liberal rebellion to marginalize the IG to complete the "The Two Spains" JE for a conservative victory.
