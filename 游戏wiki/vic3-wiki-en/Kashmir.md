**Kashmir** known as **Jammu** at the start of the game is an unrecognized power in northwestern India. It is a neighbour and vassal of [Punjab](Punjab.md) and is at war with its only other neighbour [Ladakh](Ladakh.md).

Kashmir can potentially form [Pakistan](Pakistan.md). While Kashmir can in itself not form [India](India.md), if they manage to form Pakistan, they gain enough primary cultures to then go on to form India.

### Interest groups

Due to Kashmir's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Kashmir starts with the Zamindars in government. The Ryots, Hindu Priesthood, Intelligentsia, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Armed Forces are in opposition, while the Trade Unions, and Industrialists are marginalized.

The Hindu Priesthood has the Hindu Moralist ideology instead of the default Moralist. The Petite Bourgeoisie has the Modernizer ideology instead of the default Reactionary. The Intelligentsia has the Constitutionalist ideology instead of the default Republican.

### Diplomacy

Kashmir starts is a vassal of [Punjab](Punjab.md) and is at war with [Ladakh](Ladakh.md) with the wargoal of returning the state of Kashmir. Kashmir's overlord Punjab is not involved in the war.

Its only active interest is in the Himilaya region itself.

Kashmir has claims on both [Ladakh](Ladakh.md)'s and [Chitral](Chitral.md)'s parts of the state of Kashmir.

Kashmir has **2.2** infamy at the start of the game.

[British East India](East India.md) also has claims on Kashmir.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Kashmir has a few industries of level 1: Arms Industry, Artillery Foundry, Paper Mills, and Textile Mills.

**Resources**

Kashmir has potential for wood and a tiny bit of iron. They start with 2 Logging Camps, but no Iron Mines.

### Initial war(s)

The starting war against [Ladakh](Ladakh.md) should be a walkover. The Kashmiri army is over 3 times as big and has better units, and the war has already started so there is not risk of any outside influences preventing Kashmir from winning.

On paper [Chitral](Chitral.md) should also be fairly manageable, assuming no outside influence, as they also have a measly 4 battalions. The problem is Kashmir's status as a vassal, preventing war declarations.
