**Puerto Rico** is an insignificant power in the Caribbean. At the start of the game, it is a colony of [Spain](Spain.md).

Puerto Rico may form the [Antillean Confederation](Antillean Confederation.md).

## Formation

If it ceases to exist, Puerto Rico can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country controlling its capital and only core state of Puerto Rico.

Landed Voting has the Electoral Clientelism amendment (), increasing Authority by **+10%** and reducing the impact of electoral confidence by **−25%**.

Subjecthood has the Colonial Racialization amendment (), increasing acceptance from common heritage traits and slightly decreasing acceptance for cultures with no shared heritage.
