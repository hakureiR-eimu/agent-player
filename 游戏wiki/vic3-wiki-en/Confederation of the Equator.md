**Confederation of the Equator** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in Brazil. It represents a restoration of the [brief rebellion of 1824](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/en:Confederation_of_the_Equator)[wp 1].

It cannot be released if Brazil has [integrated the Nordestino culture](Brazilian content.md) unless its core states are owned by country other than Brazil.

## Core states

Confederation of the Equator's core states represent most of the Nordestino homelands, all in the Northeast of Brazil.
