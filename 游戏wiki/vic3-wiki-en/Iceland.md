**Iceland** is a small releasable country in the North Atlantic. They start the game under [Denmark](Denmark.md)'s rule. When released they control only a single state – Iceland itself, with its tiny population. Iceland can potentially form [Scandinavia](Scandinavia.md). Except for [Greenland](Greenland.md) it is the smallest and probably hardest country to do so with.

## Core states

The state of Iceland starts underdeveloped with no real agriculture, no industries and no mining. There is also no construction sectors, although if there were it would be difficult to finance them.

Iceland has potential to build sulfur mines and whaling stations, the only real prospects of any exports. The state of Iceland has the *Arctic Whaling* trait, increasing the throughput of whaling stations. In the early game, it might however be difficult to actually sell the produced oil. A potential major issue for Iceland is the complete lack of potential logging camps, so getting wood might be difficult.
