*The Islands have passed from Ottoman to Russian, French and finally British control within the past 30 years. Can the Islanders achieve Enosis with the new Greek state or will they seek a new path forward under British guidance?"*

**Ionian Islands** are an insignificant power in the Balkans. They start as a [British](Great Britain.md) protectorate. Typically, they are annexed by [Greece](Greece.md) when it takes the decision [Annex the Ionian Islands](Greece.md) though this is only possible when both Britain and the Ionian Islands are controlled by AI.

## Formation

If it ceases to exist, the Ionian Islands can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country controlling its capital and only core state of Ionian Islands.

### Interest groups

Due to Ionian Islands' general lack of historical IG leaders and its
Wealth Voting law, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Ionian Islands typically begins with the Armed Forces in government. They may be joined by the Landowners, Orthodox Church, and Petite Bourgeoisie as the Conservative Party. The Rural Folk, the are typically in opposition; each may be affiliated with a party or not. The Industrialists and Trade Unions are marginalized. The Intelligentsia may be marginalized or in opposition.

The Orthodox Church has the Orthodox Patriarchy ideology instead of the default Patriarchal.

### Diplomacy

Ionian Islands is a protectorate of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), and thus part of the *British Empire* power bloc.

[Greece](Greece.md) starts with a claim on the state of Ionian Islands.
