*Calls for Romanian unification and political independence complicate regional politics, as both Russia and the Ottomans have an interest in enforcing the status quo. Can Romania win its freedom?*

**Romania** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in Central Europe. It can be formed by any country below kingdom tier with Romanian primary culture: [Wallachia](Wallachia.md), [Moldavia](Moldavia.md) or [Transylvania](Transylvania.md) if re-released.

Romania can also form [Danubian State](Danubian State.md).

## Historical background

The regions of [Wallachia](Wallachia.md), [Moldavia](Moldavia.md) and [Transylvania](Transylvania.md) that would eventually form Romania have a long history of being contested by [Austria](Austria.md), [Hungary](Hungary.md), [Russia](Russia.md) and [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md). Although they occasionally enjoyed independence, they were more often dominated or fought over by larger empires. However, the 19th century proved a golden era for the Romanian nation; the 1848 Romanian uprisings demanded union of the 3 principalities. Although the revolts in all 3 regions failed, Wallachia and Moldavia created a customs union after 1848. After the Peace of Paris ended the Crimean War in 1856, unionists brought their case to the great powers. In 1858, the Great Powers decided to allow a union and created a constitution known as *"The Convention from Paris"*. This was a weak union mostly in name only, and designed to fail; it was made only to appease the unionists. They will be known as *"The United Principalities of Moldavia and Wallachia"*, but will have separate thrones, governments and institutions. Only the army was unified, but Moldavian and Wallachian regiments will keep their old flags.

However, the Moldavians elected Colonel Alexander Ioan Cuza, who took part in the 1848 uprising in Moldavia, as prince on 5 January 1859. Then, on 24 January, the Wallachians elected the same man as prince. This went against the spirit of the Convention from Paris, but not against the letter, as when the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md) protested this in front of the great powers, the Romanians argued that the Convention from Paris specifically mentioned that the 2 thrones of Moldavia and Wallachia have to remain separate, but there was no clause preventing the same man from sitting on the 2 separate thrones. This loophole in the Convention from Paris was eventually accepted by the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md). However, Cuza further pushed the Convention from Paris to its limit. The governments and institutions will remain separate as stipulated by the treaty but will have to meet and make decisions in the same room. This effectively unified the Moldavian and Wallachian governments and institutions in all but name. In 1862, the name of the country was officially changed to *"United Principalities of Romania"*.

By the time Cuza was overthrown by the so-called Monstrous Coalition and succeeded by the German prince Karl Eitel Friedrich von Hohenzollern-Sigmaringen in 1866, Prince Carol I inherited an already unified country. With support from [France](France.md) and [Prussia](Prussia.md), he formally unified the Moldavian and Wallachian governments and institutions and officially changed the name of the country to *"Romania"*.

Economically and culturally, the period between 1866-1914 represented an age of growth and rebirth for the Romanian nation, being somewhat of a rising star. Many intellectuals emerged: writers, poets, scientists and artists such as Mihai Eminescu, Constantin Brancusi, Ion Creanga, Nicolae Iorga, George Enescu and Titu Maiorescu. They often came from boyar families who had enough money to give them a foreign education in [France](France.md), only for them to return and implement western ideas in Romania. The literary society *"Junimea"* was formed; through scientific papers and essays, the society helped established the basis of modern Romanian culture. The literacy rate increased significantly and the upstart of modern industry in Romania began, especially driven by extractive industries of oil, coal and non-ferrous ores. Coupled with a growing manufacturing sector and the expansion of the railway system, these dragged Romania into the modern era, with a constant GDP increase of on average 12% per year until World War I.

Following the Russo-Turkish War of 1877-1878, in which Romania allied with [Russia](Russia.md), the country's independence was acknowledged at the 1878 Congress of Berlin. As part of the settlement, Southern Bessarabia was transferred to Russia, while Romania was awarded the region of Dobrogea. In 1881, the Romanian Prince adopted the title of King Carol I. His reign solidified Romania’s alignment with [France](France.md) and [Prussia](Prussia.md), diminishing the influence of [Russia](Russia.md). Under King Carol's leadership, Romania played a key role in the Second Balkan War of 1913, capturing Southern Dobruja from [Bulgaria](Bulgaria.md). Carol was hailed as a national hero, but passed away in 1914 at the age of 75. His nephew, Ferdinand, succeeded him during a turbulent period in Europe. With World War I raging, many assumed Ferdinand would align with his German relatives and join the Central Powers. However, people were taken aback when he declared at his inauguration before the Romanian Parliament: *"I will reign as a good Romanian"*.

In 1916, Ferdinand turned his words into action by joining the war on the side of the Entente. Initially, the Romanian army achieved some victories in [Transylvania](Transylvania.md) capturing one third of the land, and the navy successfully raided [Austria](Austria.md) on the Danube. However, things quickly took a turn for the worse as a coalition of forces from [Germany](Germany.md), [Bulgaria](Bulgaria.md), [Austria](Austria.md) and [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md) invaded Dobruja and launched a counter-offensive in [Transylvania](Transylvania.md), overwhelming the small country. Within just three months of entering the war, Bucharest was occupied by foreign troops, forcing the government to flee to Iași. Despite the grim situation, Romania received assistance from [Russia](Russia.md) and a military mission from [France](France.md) led by General Henri Berthelot. During this time, the Royal Family remained steadfast, doing everything they could to maintain national morale.

In 1917–1918, Romania faced a major setback as [Russia](Russia.md) was engulfed in a Civil War and subsequently withdrew from the conflict, effectively ending the war on the Eastern Front and leaving the small Kingdom to fend for itself. Faced with this dire situation, Romania opted to sign an armistice, and in May 1918, the Treaty of Bucharest was signed. As part of the treaty, Romania was compelled to give up Dobruja to [Bulgaria](Bulgaria.md) and a stretch of land around the Carpathians to [Austria](Austria.md). Although Romania retained control over Bessarabia, which had been part of the fallout from the Russian Revolution, the oil fields in Ploiești were placed under the control of a joint Romanian-German-Austrian consortium.

After the Treaty of Bucharest in 1918, Romania faced a difficult and uncertain future, as its position seemed weak after the losses it had suffered during the war. However, despite the formal armistice, Romania secretly rearmed and rejoined the war on the side of the Entente before the conflict officially ended. This decision was driven by Romania’s desire to regain lost territories and bolster its standing in the post-war settlement. The most important consequence of Romania’s participation in the final stages of World War I was the Great Union of 1918. After the war, Romania’s borders expanded significantly. Transylvania, Bukovina, and Bessarabia were all added to Romania, territories where ethnic Romanians were in the majority. These regions had long been a part of Romanian national aspirations, and their inclusion was a historic moment in the country’s history. The unification was celebrated as a major triumph, but it also created new challenges, particularly in dealing with tensions with neighboring [Hungary](Hungary.md), which after the Hungarian-Romanian War of 1919 had lost significant territories at the Treaty of Trianon.

Three of the nine core states are needed in order to form Romania

[Transylvania](Transylvania.md) does not have the Romanian culture as primary at the start, but will gain it if it ceases to exist and is then released.

## Strategies and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for

. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
Out of the two starting options that can possibly form Romania, [Wallachia](Wallachia.md) has a slight advantage because it starts with a Lead Mine, Food Industries and potential for a Coal Mine, larger population, a unique technology boosting its silk output, and the fact that the Wallachian prince is an Abolitionist as opposed to Moldavia's Moderate ruler. Although the same strategy is just as applicable for [Moldavia](Moldavia.md) as well.

### Unification via journal entries

The two principalities can first puppet and later annex the other via a [journal entry](Romania.md), if relations are good enough with all great powers with an interest in the area (including the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md)). The other half will become a personal union/ puppet,[2] and if they remain so for 10 years, they will be annexed and Romania will be formed. This can be done even while a protectorate of the Ottomans. The problem is that if the Ottomans turn Wallachia/Moldova from a protectorate to a puppet, all progress can be lost, as Ottomans will inherit all puppets, if they do so. So it is a fine balance to strike. Keeping the Ottomans around 0 relations are best.

This however assumes that the Ottomans will be friendly. If they are not, it will be very difficult to get the other half via event, if Ottomans are not initially friendly, since relations starts cold. Then one would need to build up an army and the backing of a major power. Luckily [Russia](Russia.md) is already committed to help either principality, and that should be enough assuming no one else (like the British) intervenes. Here it is important to add the [war goal](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/War_goal) *transfer subject* for the other Romanian state and secondarily annexing the Ottoman owned parts of Romania, since this will allow Romania to continue with the journal entries.

It is very likely that the Ottomans will be allied to [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), or at least has friendly enough relations that Great Britain will come to their aid in a diplomatic play. Therefore it is important to time the attack, while GB is occupied elsewhere for example during the Opium Wars.

#### The Turkish Tanzimat reforms and Independence

The Ottoman [Tanzimat reforms](Turkish content.md) will grant any puppets independence after 30 years, if The Ottomans fail to make the reforms. There is however not much, if anything, that can be done to provoke it, but it is important to prepare for. Therefore industrial construction has to strike a balance between finding a lucrative niche in the Ottoman market, while also prepare a well rounded industry for eventual and sudden independence. When independent, Romania will not have a coast (not even unified), so trade is not reliable to fix the shortcomings in the short term.

A [journal entry](Romania.md) will make it possible to get hold of some of the Russian parts of Romania, if you have good relations with them and at least one other [Great Power](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Great_Power) with an [interest](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Interest) in the region, which crucially gives Romania access to some coastline. Russia will keep the most of it though.

The economy will likely take a hit post independence, as the bigger market of the Ottomans is gone, and likely also some trade centers, that Romania had been using until now. A consideration could be to join the Russian power bloc and market, since it is in Romania's interest to be friendly with the Russians anyway, at least for the time being. This is dependent on which principles Russia has chosen for their power bloc, so it is important to keep track of.

Later, an alliance with [Germany](Germany.md) can be useful to turn on Russia.

### Make Greater Romania

With the National Awakening patch Wallachia and Moldavia have seen some unique flavor added to them, it is better to start with [Wallachia](Wallachia.md) as it has a better economy than [Moldavia](Moldavia.md). Both start with the Treaty of Adrianopolis which means that you are forced to use Organic Regulation Law unless you want to upset Russia and that Russia will support Wallachia and Moldavia's independence. From day 1 you can get a Treaty of alliance & support independence with Moldavia and Serbia, can also ask for some extra money in the treaty, make it for 25 years. As part of the Ottoman Market build a: Cotton Plantation, Iron Mine, Tooling Workshop; they are scarce on the Ottoman Market and give you a good economic boost before you become independent. In technology start with Empiricism and then push for Nationalism, then do only society researches: Egalitaism, Banking, Central Archives, Stock Echange. Get decrees for Wallachia: Road Maintenance, Promote Social Mobility, Encourage Resource Industry, Greener Grass Campaign. For Laws, Wallachia and Moldavia start with some of the worst laws in the game. The distibution of power Organic Regulation is good for 2 reasons: you can change your government at will and you will have good relations with Russia as long as you keep it. First get Dedicated Police Force legislation since it will pass easily and suppress the pro-Slavery movement. Diplomatically: declare interest in the Balkans and declare as rivals: Greece, Ionian Islands, Montenegro. Easy to defeat nations that you don't need to worry about. Use the extra influence from rivals to improve relations with: England, France, Prussia and Austria.

After Dedicated Police Force is passed, reform the government by placing in power: Landowners + Devout/Clergy + Rural Folk. This will allow you to pass more laws including the Education System law Religious Schools that is good for literacy. As well as give you some support to change the Land Reform law Manoralism (Serfdom) law to Homesteading, this not only will decrease Landowner's power but will make the peasants eventually be able to become rich. Then change the: Economic System law Traditionalist -> at least Agrarianism, then you can change later to Inteventionism, Industrialism, Laissez-Faire or however you wish to form your state but Agrarianism is an important stepping stone. Then change the Taxation law from Land Based Taxation -> Per-capita Taxation (or Proportional Taxation if you can, it's better) so you can have a running economy where you actually tax people. After that is one you may want to replace the Rural Folk in government with the Petite Bourgeoisie, Industrialists, Intelligentsia depending on what laws you want to pass next. Then change the: Slavery law Legacy Slavery -> Banned Slavery. This will be very difficult as only the Inteligentia and Trade Unions wish for it but the ruler Alexandru Ghica is an Abolitionist so you will have at least 10% support, however it's worth it because having a lot of population trapped in slavery is not good for the economy.

When you have good relations with the Great Powers (at least +40), you can go to Diplomacy and ask the Ottomans for your own market and to publicly praise the current Wallachian government. They will refuse and you can demand independence. Demand Independence + Dobruja + a lot of money from the Ottomans. Russia will support you, if Moldavia and Serbia broke their treat of mutual support you can request pupeting them in part of the Diplomatic Play. France and Prussia don't necessarily need to help you, they only need to stay neutral. After you become independent Russia will ask you to join their Bloc, accept it. You are not a pupet and you don't wish to upset Russia. After you become independent you need to build the following factories yourself: Paper, Furniture, Arts, Small Weapons, Glass, etc; so build at least one of each of these. And Trade Centers so you can do trade with the rest of the world.

To form Romania you need at least 3/9 cores: Crisana, North Transylvania, South Transylvania, Banat, Wallachia, Moldavia, Dobruja, Bessarabia, Bukovina. With Wallachia + Dobruja + Moldavia as either independent on friendly relations of puppet you can form Romania as soon as Nationalism is researched. Afterwards you can go 2 ways: Anti-Austria for Transylvania or Anti-Russia for Bessarabia. While Austria isn't that strong usually Austria and Prussia have very good relations. When the 1848 revolutions come it will be a clash between radicals and loyalists, it's up to you whether you want to listen to the radicals, the liberals or the loyalists. The Universal Suffrage will keep the radicals in check if you want to go that route. Do not forget Egypt and Sicily, they are secondary powers who can help you a lot. If Hungary revolts against Austria generally you want Hungary to win because it's a lot easier to take Transylvania from them than from Austria.

Before you build any Construction Sector building new buildings will be free but very slow. However, after you build them they will cost both materials and labor, they can be seen in the budget under Construction Goods and Government Wages. Construction Sectors use wood and fabric for the basic production method, and iron, steel, glass for the more advanced production methods. Decreasing the cost of wood and fabric by having a lot of factories, more supply than demand, will make Construction Sectors' costs cheaper. Generally, you want to build enough Construction Sectors that your balance is in the green (positive) while constructing. You need to balance your number of Construction Sectors with your budget to have as much Construction Sectors as possible while still being in the green. Due to the private sector I recommend right with at least 4 Construction Sectors right away, 18 construction points total, but only after building at least one of the previously mentioned buildings: Cotton Plantation, Iron Mine, Tooling Workshop, Paper, Furniture, Arts, Small Weapons, Glass, Trade Centers, etc; remember that the economy boost you get from defeating the Ottomans and requesting money only lasts 5 years, use that period to build your economy. Construction Sectors like all other factories have different Production Methods: Wooden Buildings, Iron-Frame Buildings, Steel-Frame Buildings, you can start using a more quality/expensive/fast one like Iron-Frame or Steel-Frame if you can afford, and if you notice your economy gets red switch to Wooden Buildings production method. This way you can still have an economic increase without hurting your construction.

If your Power status increases and you can no longer rival Greece, Ionian Islands and Monetengro. Rival the small German states. Generally Austria and Russia will have an alliance, this is bad for you because you want to use the Austrians to fight the Russians or Russians to fight the Austrians, otherwise it's going to be very difficult and you need a lot of Great Powers on your side. If you want Prestige you can get it from the military. Generally: full infantry for defense. 50% infantry, 50% artillery for attack. Generally being on the defensive is more advantageous. Try to pass the Professional Army law as it helps a lot when building your army. Culturally, your most valueable resources is people, no matter how many factories you can build they don't mean anything unless you have the population to work in them. For non-primary cultures their ability to work is affected by their level of acceptance, in the Society tab you will see every Population's acceptance score, it is determined by adding their Culture and their Religion score. These scores come from your Citizenship laws, and Church and State laws. At level 1 of acceptance 0-19 they can hardly work anywhere, they will also not be able to assimilate to a different culture. Only from level 2 upwards they can assimilate to a different culture. Populations won't change their culture if they live in one of their homelands. For religion, populations whose religion has a low acceptance score will convert to the state religion. With Multicultralism law pretty much everyone will be accepted.
