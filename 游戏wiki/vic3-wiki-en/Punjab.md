**Punjab** (also known as the *Khalsa Raj*) is an unrecognized major power in northwestern India. As a neighbor of both the [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company) and [Great Qing](China.md), eastward expansion is difficult.

Punjab is able to form [India](India.md).

## Formation

Both core states are needed to form Punjab
If Punjab ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) or [reformed](Formable countries.md). Its core states are the Punjabi homelands which are the states Punjab and Punjab Hills.

### Interest groups

Punjab starts with the Petite Bourgeoisie and Khalsaji in government. The Zamindars, Jats, and Intelligentsia are in opposition. The Trade Unions, Granthis, and Industrialists are marginalized.

The Granthis have the Sikh Moralist ideology instead of the default Moralist, which leads them to favor National Militia, Professional Army, and Freedom of Conscience. The Khalsaji have the Sikh Militancy ideology instead of the default Jingoist, which leads them to favor Freedom of Conscience and Multiculturalism. The Petite Bourgeoisie has the Modernizer ideology instead of the default Reactionary, and they also have the Mercantile ideology, leading them to favor Interventionism. They also start with the modifier *Favorites of the Wazir Favorites of the Wazir Interest group modifier +25% Political strength −5 Approval in opposition*, which decays over 20 years. The Intelligentsia has the Constitutionalist ideology instead of the default Republican.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Punjab starts with a small industrial base, including Textile Mills, Glassworks, and Arms Industries, though the last are hampered by a lack of iron.

**Resources**

Punjab has access to a small amount of coal and iron, but its main wealth in is agriculture, as the state of Punjab has 505 arable land, a **+20%** agriculture and plantation throughput boost, and the ability to grow all cash crops.

## Military

Punjab has the Professional Army law.

Punjab starts with 86 Battalions and can raise up to 22 Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions comprise three armies.

player suggested strategies for
Punjab
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

### Grand strategy

Punjab is a mix of good and bad. On the bad side, Punjab is landlocked, its official religion is very rare (get ready for radicals from [Discrimination](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Discrimination)), and its initial [Taxation capacity](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Taxation_capacity) is inadequate. On the plus side, its army is passable and most of its neighbors are on the weak side. The in-game territory of Punjab has a massive population, a **+20%** boost to agriculture, and is very versatile in what crops it can grow.

Punjab has a lot of flexibility in how to play the game. Obviously, securing access to the sea should be a major priority, and it usually isn't particularly difficult to do so with its sheer military strength. [Sindh](Sindh.md) is an important first target due to its value, sea access, and the fact that taking it denies East India the ability to expand eastward. After that, it can go west to Persia without much trouble. Cutting into the [East India Company](East India.md) is more difficult, between the British support and all of its vassals, but is more immediately rewarding, as the player can seize the rest of the Punjab territory and the similarly wealthy Delhi territory. Long term, India is likely to drift out of Great Britain's orbit due to successive waves of revolts (and is likely to be weakened enough to steal states from during said revolts). A more colony-minded Punjab player could also go to Africa, some of the Arab states, or Southeast Asia, but will have trouble with the relative naval power of the European majors. A player using Punjab could also attack Western [China](China.md) for Tibet or other provinces, but these are relatively poor and underpopulated.

A Punjab player will have some issues with [Russia](Russia.md) expanding to its north. To counter this, the player can either grab the Central Asian states themselves, or look into ways to weaken Russia.

Due to the rarity of its official religion, a player should either go for Religious Schools (for high [Conversion](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Conversion)) or Total Separation (for high tolerance) as soon as possible. Otherwise, the player is setting themselves up for a lot of trouble if they expand. There simply aren't that many Sikhs around. Alternatively, a player could also go simply go for Freedom of Conscience since Hindus are in the same religious group as Sikhism. While it may be tempting to form a Religious Convocation power bloc, both to allow for imposing the state religion and the increased conversion and birth rate, the combination of the Granthis' marginalized starting position, and the Sikh IG ideological opposition to state religion are such that even after intentionally investing in clerical infrastructure and maximizing education investment with religious schools, the Granthis will not attract enough pops to establish the power bloc's cohesion.

### Politics

Punjab starts the game with an unusual government position, with an autocratic monarchy led by the petite bourgeoisie and Khalsaji, a unique military IG with the Sikh Militancy ideology, which supports Freedom of Conscience and Multiculturalism in addition to the more typical Professional Army and preference for colonization. While reforming government to kick out the petit bourgeoisie and bring in the Zamindars (Landlords) and/or Jats (Peasants) increases legitimacy, this will hinder rather than help the player reform Punjab's laws. At the outset, the player has the option of adopting Wealth Voting , Census Suffrage or Protectionism, which start off at 11%/86% odds but can be immediately improved to 22%/77% by swaying the Khalsaji.

### Economy and Industry

Punjab's economy at game start has two Construction Sectors, but these are liable to be more of a drain than a benefit at the outset, and thus it may be advisable to downsize them.

The overwhelming majority of Punjab's POPs are concentrated in the capital region of Punjab, making it the nearly only place worth constructing in. Unfortunately, it does not contain many of the resources necessary for a proper industry in anywhere near adequate numbers including a measly 6 iron and coal potentials. But due to the overwhelming population advantage of the capital, it is still best to place industry there.

### Diplomacy

It would benefit a Punjab player to get the British out of India. That is however difficult to achieve, at least initially. Punjab is strong enough to not be overrun by the Europeans, but probably too weak to get a good alliance with them. Still, partnerships with Europeans (even if it is just a [Trade agreement](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Trade_agreement)) are a good thing to get Punjab the goods it needs while it builds itself up economically.

### Potential

Punjab is all but guaranteed to be able to generate enormous agricultural wealth. The Punjab territory is arguably one of the best in the game, due to its **20%** agricultural bonus with a broad range of crops and a sizable labor pool. Taxation capacity is a problem and will be a problem for some time, but once its sorted the income is substantial for such a small bit of land. If the player is able to secure Punjab and Delhi, it isn't hard for the economic snowball to get moving.

### Weaknesses

Punjab is the one of two countries in the world with the [Sikh](Religion.md) state religion. Depending on how the player handles religion, this could generate a lot of radicalism for a long time. This will only become a bigger problem, if Punjab begins to expand. A lot of Indian region has [Bureaucracy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Bureaucracy) issues at game start, and Punjab is not exempt from this. While the Punjab province has enormous wealth capacity, a player could be hamstrung by [Infrastructure](Infrastructure.md). This includes the costs of building and supplying [Railways](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Railways), and it can even take a while, before they are available. Punjab starts behind the European majors in [Technology](Technology.md), but its larger population means covering for that is not as painful. Note that due to the overwhelming importance of the capital, Punjab, it might be worthwhile to overbuild there, knowing that it will be isolated, since even when isolated it still contains way more than 50% of the total economy.
