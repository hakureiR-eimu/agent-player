**Chile** is a Minor Power in South America. It borders [Argentina](Argentina.md) and [Mapuche](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Mapuche) to the east, [Bolivia](Bolivia.md) to the north, and [Tehuelche](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Tehuelche) to the south.

Chile begins colonizing the Araucania state owned by the [Tehuelche](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Tehuelche). Chile has the largest army of La Plata region at the start of the game.

Chile can form the [Federation of the Andes](Federation of the Andes.md), as long as it does not nationalize its culture.

## Historical background

Chile's independence began with the First Junta in 1810, during the Peninsular War when Ferdinand VII was overthrown by Napoleon's forces. The Junta was largely controlled by the *criollos*, Spaniards born in the colonies. Although they initially declared loyalty to Ferdinand and [Spain](Spain.md), they also sought greater autonomy, and eventually independence. Soon after, Spanish forces from Peru launched an invasion known as the *Reconquista*, successfully bringing Chile back under Spanish control from 1813 to 1818 and exiling leaders of the Junta. These exiles found support in [Argentina](Argentina.md), raising an army and crossing the Andes in 1817, and by 1818 they took back Santiago, declaring full independence from Spain. The [Mapuche](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Mapuche) fought on both sides as they had a treaty of recognition with Spain but some saw opportunities with the new local government. Chile helped in the expedition to liberate [Peru](Peru.md) from Spain and even went to [Mexico](Mexico.md) but the fleet got there too late to be effective.

Following independence, Chile underwent a period of internal instability, as the form of the new government was debated and changed several times, culminating in the civil war of 1829 where conservative forces defeated the liberal government. The victorious conservatives established a lasting constitution and remained in power for the next 30 years. Though mostly suppressed, the liberals eventually revolted, unsuccessfully in 1851, then semi-successfully in 1859. Actual change of power came with the election of 1861. The liberals remained in power until the civil war of 1891 when the Chilean congress and navy defeated the president and army. Following this time, Chile was dominated by the congress with the president having very little power.

The modern borders of Chile were forged across the 19th century in several conflicts, primarily the the Occupation of the Araucania from 1861 to 1883 against the Mapuche, in which the government arrested and pursued a French man called Orélie-Antoine de Tounens who was proclaimed King of the Araucania and Patagonia with the support of some powerful Mapuche families (1860-1862), the War of the Pacific with Chile against a secret alliance between Peru and [Bolivia](Bolivia.md) from 1879 to 1883, and the Annexation of Rapa Nui in 1888 by a treaty with the local chiefs.

Chile also fought in the War of the Confederation against [Peru-Bolivia](Peru-Bolivia.md) with the support of [North Peru](North Peru.md) dissidents and Argentina and the Chincha Islands War from 1865 to 1871 with Chile, Peru, [Ecuador](Ecuador.md) and Bolivia against Spain.

## Formation

If Chile ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country that controls its capital. In addition to its capital, Los Ríos is also a core state. When released, Chile reverts to South Andean, even if it nationalized its culture.

### Interest groups

Chile begins with the Armed Forces in government. The Landowners, Catholic Church, Intelligentsia, and Petite Bourgeoisie are in opposition, while the Industrialists[1], the Rural Folk and the Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Armed Forces have the Caudillismo ideology in addition to their standard ideologies. The Landowners have the Elitist ideology instead of the default Paternalistic.

### Diplomacy

Chile has claims on Antofagasta, owned by [Bolivia](Bolivia.md), as well as on Araucania and Patagonia, which are decentralized. Araucania is under active colonization. It also begins with a declared declared interest in the Andes strategic region.

Chile has cold relations with [Bolivia](Bolivia.md) and poor relations with [Spain](Spain.md). Chile also starts with the modifier *Independence Not Recognized by Spain Independence Not Recognized by Spain Country modifier −10% Influence*, until [Spain](Spain.md) recognizes its independence, or Chile becomes strong enough to disregard Spain.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Chile starts off with only a single level of Tooling Workshops, in its capital state of Santiago.

**Resources**

Chile has great potential for a tall playthrough, as its states of Santiago and Los Ríos hold very respectable amounts of all crucial early industrial goods, with Santiago even having access to gold (and starting with 3 Gold Mines.) Later on, Araucania even has the potential for oil.

The states of Santiago and Los Ríos additionally have the *Valle Central* trait, making them great agricultural producers as well.

However, Chile's progress is bottlenecked by the *Andes* trait shared by all its states, making development slow. Generous use of the Road Maintenance decree is necessary for early industrialization, but only Railways will unlock Chile's potential.

### The Tehuelche question

The [Tehuelche](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Tehuelche) or [Mapuche](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Mapuche) may approach Chile and ask to become a vassal. Accept this offer, as it turns them into an unrecognized power, allowing Chile to annex its territory in a diplomatic play.

### The Federation of the Andes

Chile has a unique challenge in its attempt to form the Federation as it has to incorporate 10 states in South America. After subjugating Argentina, Paraguay and Uruguay, Chile is still not really strong enough to challenge Peru-Bolivia or Brazil without calling in allies, while invading the North Andean countries (Venezuela, Colombia and Ecuador) requires a sizeable navy as support.
