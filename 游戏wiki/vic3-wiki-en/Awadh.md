**Awadh** is an unrecognized regional power in South Asia. It is one of the "princely states", a puppet of the [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company), and thus a part of the [British Empire](Great Britain.md). It is called **Oudh** at the start of the game, and uses that name while it is a subject of an English or Scottish country.

As an Avadhi country, Awadh is able to form [India](India.md). It is the only Shiite country capable of doing so.

### Interest groups

Oudh starts with the Zamindars in government. The Ryots, Intelligentsia, Armed Forces, and Petite Bourgeoisie are in opposition. The remaining interest groups are marginalized.

The Petite Bourgeoisie has the Modernizer ideology instead of the default Reactionary. The Intelligentsia has the Constitutionalist ideology instead of the default Republican.

## Military

Oudh has the Professional Army law.

Oudh starts with 49 Battalions and can call on 11 Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals |
|---|---|---|

Oudh starts with no Flotillas, as it is landlocked.
