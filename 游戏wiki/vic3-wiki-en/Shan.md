**Shan** is an unrecognized power in northwest Indochina. As a neighbor of both the [Chiang Mai](Chiang Mai.md), [Luang Prabang](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Luang_Prabang), [Burma](Burma.md) and [China](China.md).

It starts the game as a vassal of [Burma](Burma.md).

### Interest groups

Due to Shan's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Shan starts with the Landowners in government. The Rural Folk, Theravada Monks, Armed Forces, and Intelligentsia are in opposition, while the Trade Unions, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Industrialists are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Shan starts as a vassal of [Burma](Burma.md). Otherwise, they start with no diplomatic relations of note, and its only active interest is in the Indochina region itself.
