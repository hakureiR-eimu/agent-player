*"In the wake of recent revolutions across Europe, Bavaria has turned to reaction. But the embers of rebellion still burn; will Bavaria be consumed by the flames?"*

Bavaria is a minor power and the third largest German country after [Austria](Austria.md) and [Prussia](Prussia.md). Its starting borders approximately match the modern German state, with the addition of an enclave in Rhineland.

Bavaria neighbors Austria to the south and east and has several smaller neighbors: [Saxony](Saxony.md) and the Saxon minors to the northeast, [Hesse-Kassel](Hesse-Kassel.md), [Frankfurt](Frankfurt.md), and [Hesse](Hesse.md) to the northwest, and [Baden](Baden.md) and [Württemberg](Württemberg.md) to the west. Through its enclave in Rhineland, Bavaria also neighbors Prussia and [France](France.md)

As a South German nation, Bavaria is able to form the [South German Federation](South German Federation.md) and [Germany](Germany.md). As an alternative, Bavaria can also form the [Confederation of the Rhine](Confederation of the Rhine.md). Behind the two great powers, Prussia and Austria, Bavaria is the third best candidate to form Germany.

## Historical background

Bavaria has its origin as a stem duchy of the Holy Roman Empire. During the Thirty Years' War, the duke of Bavaria was elevated to be an Elector of the HRE – an honor which was kept until the empire was dissolved in 1806. In compensation for the loss of electorship, Bavaria was elevated to a kingdom.

## Formation

If Bavaria ceases to exist, it can be released by any country that controls the state of Bavaria. Its core states are Bavaria and Franconia.

### Diplomacy

Bavaria is a member of the *Zollverein* [power bloc](Power bloc.md), led by [Prussia](Prussia.md) and has a [treaty](Treaty.md) of mutual military access, which is bound for 10 years. It also starts with an anti-country lobby towards [Prussia](Prussia.md) ().

Bavaria starts with active interests in South Germany and Rhine, and a declared interest in North Germany.

## Strategy and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.9. |

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Bavaria
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
While it takes quite a bit of work, Bavaria can contest against Prussia and Austria for leadership of German unification. Doing so requires making Bavaria into a great power so it can become a unification candidate. Carefully conquering Bavaria's neighbors is typically the first step in the process, though beware of interventions by France, Prussia and Austria, which can quickly sink any chance of expansion.

### The German wars and rivalries

There will be many wars. Prussia and Austria directly against each other, against either France or Denmark, or on each side in a revolution in a minor German nation. Bavaria can often be the deciding factor in those wars by choosing a side, and both side will most likely happily pay [obligations](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Obligations) for Bavaria's favour. Alternatively, one can stay out and become a safe haven in a turbulent world.

Note that it is in Bavaria's interest that *neither* German Great Power wins German leadership (at least until Bavaria becomes a great power). If Austria becomes sole unifier, Bavaria is in danger of being annexed once the Schleswig-Holstein question is resolved. If Prussia becomes sole unifier and forms the [North German Federation](North German Federation.md), it becomes very difficult for Bavaria to reach a coast.

Bavaria is also first among equals among the German minors; rival Hanover and [Saxony](Saxony.md) (and any other German minor) and use the extra influence to increase relations with the great powers. Note that Prussia is contiguous from the start. This poses a problem as Bavaria would then have to fight Prussia if it wishes to expand northward.

Note that if Bavaria forms the SGF, it has likely defeated both Prussia and Austria for German leadership. As such, both countries will be left out of future unification plays unless revolutions break out in both countries.

#### Protecting the Bavarian market

Without prior preparation, Bavaria should not attempt to leave the *Zollverein* as the Bavarian market is split into two. Bavaria has to annex either [Hesse](Hesse.md) or [Baden](Baden.md) to be contiguous.

### Expanding southwards

Consider striking at Baden immediately so as to gain access to East [Switzerland](Switzerland.md) and (after Switzerland) Piedmont. Alternatively, attack [Austria](Austria.md), draw [Prussia](Prussia.md) onto your side, and conquer Tyrol, South Tyrol, and Venetia in order to gain access to the coast.
