Luxembourg is a minor power and one of the smallest countries in continental Europe and in the entire game. It is the junior member of a personal union with [Netherlands](Netherlands.md), but at the same time it has a separate market, as it is separated from the Netherlands by [Belgium](Belgium.md) and [Prussia](Prussia.md).

As a North German country, Luxembourg can potentially form the [North German Federation](North German Federation.md) and [Germany](Germany.md), but the state of Wallonia does not count towards either formation as it is not a North or South German homeland. Alternatively, as a Wallonian country, Luxembourg may form the [United Netherlands](United Netherlands.md). Thirdly, France has a [decision](Decision.md) to [Purchase Luxembourg](French content.md) as long as they are a subject. This can however not happen to a player country.

### Interest groups

Luxembourg typically starts with the Landowners and Kleinbürger in government. The Catholic Church, Armed Forces, Landvolk and Intelligentsia are in opposition. The Trade Unions and Industrialists are marginalized.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Luxembourg begins with only a single Wheat Farm and single Livestock Ranch and no other industry.

**Resources**

Luxembourg has access to iron, coal, and lead, though in reduced amounts due to owning only a small portion of its state region.

## Strategy

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.12.4. |

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Luxembourg
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

#### A single province nation

As rebellions can not occur in capital provinces and Luxembourg has only one single province, it means that revolutions are impossible. One can change as many laws as you want and have as illegitimate a government as you like, and the only potential consequence is [turmoil](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turmoil) until another province is gained. Another province is likely to be gained as a result of the London Conference journal entry, as most outcomes increase the size of Luxembourg. The only other outcome results in Luxembourg's annexation.

### Declaration of Independence

Luxembourg starts in a frankly very strange place. Completely isolated from the Dutch overlords, it essentially functions as its own market. Furthermore, if you declare war on the Netherlands early on it is very likely you can win a war against the Netherlands without ever fighting a battle or even raising troops. The Netherlands AI will be cocky due to their in paper overwhelming force and will therefore likely not call in any allies. But they cannot actually get any troops to Luxembourg. As long as you do not demand any other war goal or try to recruit any allies, the war will come and go without any events at all, and the Dutch will eventually capitulate giving you independence.

### Next steps

Once either Austria or Prussia has researched [Nationalism](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Nationalism), the German Major unification starts. Since Luxembourg can actually vote in the matter, Prussia has an interest in your favour, however small you are.

If you improve relations to **+50** and officially support Prussia, it is very likely that they will either accept you as a protectorate or for an [obligation](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Obligation) let you into their power bloc. This vastly improves the [standard of living](Standard of living.md) and economy, as well as give you a bona-fide world class protector. Once you are in the power bloc, you can safely remove your support of Prussia.

Which one you should choose is a dilemma. Not being a protectorate gives you of course some independence, but accepting protectorate status makes it very likely that Prussia will fight offensive wars for you to annex the rest of Wallonia, Flanders and possibly Dutch provinces. Gaining access to the Prussian market will also provide a steady flow of immigrants, and will make building specialized industry for the *Luxurious Luxembourg* achivement much more profitable.

Alternatively, the player can orient to one of the other Great Powers' power blocs by dedicating their diplomatic resources in that direction. These alternatives come at a significant drawback, that none of the other major power blocs at the beginning of the game are Trade Leagues nor include perks that provide Customs Union membership to their blocs (additionally, accessing the power bloc's market if available will also require either a coastline or a land border, which is only likely to be shared with France for much of the game).

If Luxembourg lacks a broader Customs Union, it will soon run out of population to work in its industries. In order to attract mass migrations as an independent state, Luxembourg will need to conquer a complete state, and ideally liberalize its citizenship law (although it will already be able to attract German immigrants even with the National Supremacy it starts with).

### To the future

Annexing the rest of Wallonia and Flanders is the obvious next choice. You are not going to win any wars against your other neighbours, and you do need some coastline before you can expand anywhere else. After Belgium is annexed, you can establish colonies in either Asia or Africa as a good way to gain resources, which your home provinces can use in industry. Alternatively, try to puppet the Netherlands after reducing them to a Minor Power; this will make you the overlord of the [Dutch East Indies](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Dutch_East_Indies). Forming the [United Netherlands](United Netherlands.md) will annex the Dutch East Indies and transfer its puppets to you.

You can tailor your production to the larger markets and possibly be one of the richest places on the planet.

### France's natural borders

|  | Available only with the [Voice of the People](Voice of the People.md) DLC enabled. |
|---|---|

If France activates the [journal entry](French content.md), it will receive claims on Flanders and Wallonia. Needless to say, this makes France extremely dangerous. It is advised to seek an ally or defensive pact with a GP rival of France (usually [Great Britain](Great Britain.md)). You can also reset France's diplomatic attitude towards you by siding with them on a Diplomatic Play (for example, when they try to invade an Algerian minor).
