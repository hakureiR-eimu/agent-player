**Chiang Mai** is an unrecognized power in north Indochina. As a neighbor of both the [Shan](Shan.md), [Luang Prabang](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Luang_Prabang), [Burma](Burma.md), [Siam](Siam.md) and [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company).

It starts the game as a vassal of [Siam](Siam.md) together with [Champasak](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Champasak).

### Interest groups

Due to Chiang Mai's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Chiang Mai starts with the Landowners in government. The Rural Folk, Intelligentsia, Theravada Monks, and Armed Forces are in opposition, while the Trade Unions, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Industrialists are marginalized.

The Vajrayana Monks have the Buddhist Moralist ideology instead of the default Moralist.

### Diplomacy

Chiang Mai starts the game as a vassal of [Siam](Siam.md) together with [Champasak](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Champasak). Otherwise, it starts with no diplomatic relations of note, and its only active interest is in Indochina.
