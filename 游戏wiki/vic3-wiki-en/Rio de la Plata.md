**Rio de la Plata** is a formable country in South America. It can be formed by any Platinean country below empire tier that is at least a minor power with at least 500 army power projection.

Rio de la Plata can gain South Andean as a primary culture and thus be eligible to form the [Federation of the Andes](Federation of the Andes.md). If it does form the federation, it is known as the *United Provinces of South America*.

## Historical background

The United Provinces of Rio de la Plata declared independence from [Spain](Spain.md) in 1816 as part of the Spanish American wars of independence. The initial United Provinces consisted of most parts of the earlier Spanish Viceroyalty of Rio de la Plata, now [Argentina](Argentina.md), [Bolivia](Bolivia.md), [Paraguay](Paraguay.md), and [Uruguay](Uruguay.md). During the fight for independence, Rio de la Plata gained and lost control of several regions, eventually being reduced to effectively just Argentina, which retained the official name of United Provinces of Rio de la Plata.

## Formation

9 of the 11 core states are needed to form Rio de la Plata
The following countries can form Rio de la Plata when they control 9 of the 11 core states, and are at least a minor power with at least 500 army power projection. If any of these have changed their primary culture from the region Platinean to their national primary culture, they can no longer form Rio de la Plata.

If any of the above countries cease to exist, they are all [releasable](Releasable countries.md) by any country that controls their capital.

If any of Antofagasta, Santiago, Los Rios, or Araucania still have a South Andean homeland, forming Rio de la Plata gives the option to gain South Andean as a primary culture.

If Rio de la Plata is formed by [Paraguay](Paraguay.md) after it continued Francia's legacy, it gains Guarani as a primary culture.

## Strategy and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Rio de la Plata
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
To form Rio de la Plata, you need 9 out of 11 specific south American states, you need to be at least a minor power with at least 500 army power projection, and you need Platinean primary culture (which will go away if you take too long). The easiest nation to do it as is [Argentina](Argentina.md). Until you've won your first three wars, focus on building units and supply those units with armaments. Argentina starts with full control of three states; to accomplish seven more, you can take three in wars: one from [Uruguay](Uruguay.md), one from [Paraguay](Paraguay.md), one from [Bolivia](Bolivia.md). You get two from agreeing to [Mapuche](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Mapuche) becoming your puppet, and you get the two southern states in the decentralized state of [Guarani](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Guarani_(GNI)). When you form Rio de la Plata, you have an option to gain South Andean primary culture and to form the [Federation of the Andes](Federation of the Andes.md). If you take too long, you'll fail to make Rio de la Plata. Conquest is a prerequisite to this formable.

Forming Rio de la Plata as the Argentine Confederation is somewhat straightforward. Beeline the Nationalism technology: research Mass Communication, then Nationalism. First declare war and conquer Rio Grande do Sul (for leverage against [Brazil](Brazil.md)), if [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) or anyone else intervenes, restart. Next, conquer Uruguay and then Paraguay (you only need Bajo Paraguay). Then make a return state claim on Bolivia, offer [Chile](Chile.md) the war goal of Antofagasta and sway Brazil with Rio Grande do Sul, [Ecuador](Ecuador.md) might join in exchange for liberating [North Peru](North Peru.md). With enough people on your side, beating Bolivia should be easy, but Brazil is usually on the fence, so timing is key.

**Colonization**

You don't need to colonize the south as an event can fire to centralize Mapuche and make them your puppet, which gives you effective control of those their states. Then, you just need the two states from Guarani. This is ideal for forming Rio de la Plata, as otherwise it takes a long time to finish colonizing the required states.

Don't build government administration buildings unless it helps you colonize faster as it will cause the South American nationalism event that prevents you from forming Rio de la Plata to fire faster. After researching Nationalism, Quinine ( Pharmaceuticals->Quinine) will help you colonize 50% faster by increasing the maximum level for colonial affairs institution. Economic and military technologies will spread to you, society technologies such as Nationalism and antibiotics (Pharmaceuticals, then Quinine) will not.

**The New Nation**

[The [New Culture] Nation](South America content.md) changes your culture from Platinean to a 'national' culture, such as Argentine. This journal is activated by researching Nationalism, so delaying Nationalism until you have achieved the required states can be beneficial to prevent losing Platinean primary culture and thus your chance at forming Rio de la Plata. Alternatively, the progress bar on the journal entry can be slowed or stalled indefinitely by controlling enough non-cultural homelands.
