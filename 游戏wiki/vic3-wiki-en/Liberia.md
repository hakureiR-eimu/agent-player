**Liberia** is an insignificant power in the Senegal region. It starts as a protectorate of the [USA](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/USA).

## Formation

If it ceases to exist, Liberia can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country controlling its capital and only core state of Grain Coast.

### Interest groups

Liberia starts with the Intelligentsia in government. The Landowners, Armed Forces, Rural Folk, and Evangelicals are in opposition, while the Petite Bourgeoisie, Trade Unions, and Industrialists are marginalized.

### Industry and Resources

**Resources**

Liberia has access to iron and eventually rubber. It also has a decent amount of arable land and can grow most cash crops.

## Military

Liberia has the Peasant Levies and Merchant Navy laws.

Liberia starts with with no Battalions and can raise up to five Conscripts.

Liberia starts with no flotillas.

## Strategies and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Liberia
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
A major issue for Liberia is that the primary culture Afro-American and state religion Protestantism is a minority in its own country, meaning problems with radicalism and government buildings unable to hire unless the player passes at least Racial Segregation and Freedom of Conscience. Its primary culture's other homelands are also far away in North America, meaning that Liberia will have just a hard time colonizing as the Europeans do.

- Default flag
