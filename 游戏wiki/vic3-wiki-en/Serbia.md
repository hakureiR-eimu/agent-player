*"Serbia has fought hard for independence, and will surely have to continue its struggle to retain it. Wedged between great powers, will Serbia become the powder keg of Europe?"*

**Serbia** is a minor power in Eastern Europe, starting as a protectorate of the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md). It can form [Yugoslavia](Yugoslavia.md). It can also become Serbia-Montenegro through scripted content.

## Formation

If Serbia ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by a country controlling its capital state of Western Serbia. Its core states are the states of Western Serbia, Eastern Serbia, Montenegro, and Kosovo.

## Scripted Content

Serbia has scripted content regarding the relations and union with [Montenegro](Montenegro.md) and the rightful ruler of Serbia.

### Interest groups

Serbia starts with powerful Landowners in government. The Armed Forces, Orthodox Church, and Rural Folk are in opposition. The Intelligentsia, Petite Bourgeoisie, Industrialists, and Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Orthodox Church has the Orthodox Patriarchy ideology instead of the default Patriarchal.

### Diplomacy

Serbia starts as a protectorate of the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md) and is thus in the *Devlet-i ‘Aliye* power bloc. It has a claim on the unowned parts of Eastern and Western Serbia states, as well as on Vojvodina. Other Serbian homelands are divided between [Hungary](Hungary.md), the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md), [Montenegro](Montenegro.md), and [Austria](Austria.md), but Serbia does not start with a claim on these states.

Serbia has a pro-country lobby towards [Russia](Russia.md) () and one towards [Austria](Austria.md) (). It also starts with an anti-overlord lobby towards the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md) ().

## Strategy and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Serbia
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

#### The Turkish Tanzimat reforms and Independence

If the Ottomans fail to complete the [Tanzimat reforms](Turkish content.md) with 30 years, Serbia becomes independent. There is however not much, if anything, Serbia can do to provoke it (or prevent it for that matter), but it is important to prepare for. Therefore, industrial construction has to strike a balance between finding a lucrative niche in the Ottoman market, while also preparing a well-rounded industry for eventual and sudden independence.

### Conquest

Taking the rest of the Serbian states are difficult, but not impossible. They are own partly by the Ottoman Empire and by [Austria](Austria.md). Therefore, Serbia probably needs outside help. [Russia](Russia.md) is an obvious choice for this.

### Montenegro

If Serbia gets independence and share a border with [Montenegro](Montenegro.md) (while Montenegro also remains independent), it is possible to unify the two countries and continue playing as either 'Serbia', 'Montenegro' or 'Serbia-Montenegro'. After getting independence, the only thing standing in the way is the Ottoman parts of Western Serbia and Montenegro. Serbia has a claim on western Serbia and Montenegro has a claim on the rest of Montenegro, and can potentially get parts of it through [journal entries](Montenegro.md).

Note that choosing which country to continue as affects which [achievements](Achievements.md) can be earned.
