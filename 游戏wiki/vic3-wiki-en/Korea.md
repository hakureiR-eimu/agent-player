**Korea** – called *Joseon* at the start of the game – is an unrecognized regional power in East Asia. It is a tributary of [China](China.md), its northern neighbor.

## Formation

If Korea ceases to exist, it can be released by any country that controls its capital, Gyeonggi. Its core states are the Korean homelands.

## Scripted content

Main article:
Korean content
Korea has a decision to proclaim the Empire of Korea. With Sphere of Influence, Korea also has journal entries and events related to the [Donghak movement](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/en:Tonghak_Peasant_Revolution)[wp 1].

### Interest groups

Korea begins with the Yangban Officials in government. The Rural Folk, Armed Forces, Confucian Scholars, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Intelligentsia are in opposition, while the Trade Unions and Industrialists are marginalized.

The Confucian Scholars have the Confucian ideology instead of the default Moralist and Pious ideologies. The Petite Bourgeoisie have the Mercantile ideology in addition to the rest.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Korea starts with a small number of Glassworks, and a single Shipyards and Iron Mine. Agriculture is slightly more developed with a number of Tobacco Plantations and Rice Farms.

**Resources**

Korea has access to a decent amount of coal, iron, lead, and sulfur. Korea can also grow silk and tobacco.

### Getting independence

It is possible for Korea to demand independence from [China](China.md) relatively soon, as [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) is likely to support Korea. This is however dependent on their [attitude](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Attitude). Generally, it is best wait until after Line Infantry has been researched. On top of independence, it is possible to demand some of bordering Chinese states or some money on top. Adding one of the states, as a primary war goal can be a good idea, as Qing may be willing to let Korea go without a fight, but will rarely surrender, if they would lose land by it.

Do note however, that [Russia](Russia.md) can get [claims](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Claims) on some Chinese states via a [journal entry](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Journal_entry) [Ruler of the east](Russian content.md). Good relations with them is paramount. It is also important to monitor [Japan](Japan.md); if they complete [the restoration](Japanese content.md), they can gain claims on all of Korea's core states; needless to say this makes them a very dangerous foe.

### Politics

The Confucian Devout IG opposes neither Freedom of Conscience nor Public Schools. (When it's not lead by a Traditionalist).

The Traditionalist movement can be very problematic when modernizing, not least because Korea's ruler and heir also have this ideology. It is best to suppress it as, to minimize the risk of a new devout IG leader adopting this ideology.

If you have taken states from [China](China.md), consider not integrating them immediately, as this will greatly strengthen the landowners.

### Economy

At game start, Korea has a fairly substantial budget surplus, and can immediately begin to expand construction sectors and upgrade to iron-frame construction. However, this is combined with a significant deficit in taxation capacity that will need to be addressed with government administrations and paper infrastructure in order for Korea to reap the benefits of growth.

Korea has a lot of whaling stations, so consider giving investments rights to [Russia](Russia.md), as they have the [flavored company Russian-American Company](List of flavored companies.md) which has meat as a [prestige good](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Prestige_good).

### Moving on

Getting back to attacking China is a good idea, as long as they are still suffering from the negative consequences of Opium. Especially taking Beijing, which due to the [unique building](Unique building.md) *The Forbidden Palace*, a large population, and generally good resources is one of the best states in the entire game.
