**Algeria** is a [formable nation](Formable countries.md) in North Africa. Its homeland is divided between four splinter states, in addition to some ports controlled by [France](France.md) at the start.

Algeria can form [Maghreb](Maghreb.md).

## Historical background

Shortly before the start of the game in 1830, France annexed Algiers and in 1834 made it a military colony. Over the course of the game period, the entirety of Algeria came under French control, which lasted until the end of the Algerian War of Independence in 1962.

The Algerian minors are all unrecognized powers, except for Mascara which is an unrecognized regional power, and have similar basic stats, starting technology, and politics. Each starts with states or split states in Algeria's core states. They are all Principalities and Sunni. They generally start with the Landowners in government with a mix of the Armed Forces, Sunni Ulema, and either Intelligentsia or Rural Folk in opposition, and the remaining interest groups marginalized.

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.10. |
|---|---|

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Algeria
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
[Constantine](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Constantine) has a very unique ability to re-swear fealty to the Ottomans when they form Algeria, gaining Turkish as a primary culture and becoming a subject – preferably, while the Ottomans are still recognized. (Note: Forming [Maghreb](Maghreb.md) removes Turkish as a primary culture)

[Mascara](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Mascara) is by far the strongest nation with an army of 23 divisions, double the total combined units of the rest.

If not Mascara, build up army to at least 25 or research skirmish infantry and shrapnel artillery. Uniting the Algerian minors into a single state should be a top priority, as the longer the player waits, the greater likelihood there is of one of them winning the patronage of a much more powerful foreign power. They are also easily integrated given the compatibility of the two main cultures throughout the North Africa region, Amazigh and Maghrebi both of which are fully accepted by [Mascara](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Mascara) and [Constantine](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Constantine), and which still have multiple traits in common beyond that. States in [Morocco](Morocco.md), [Tunis](Tunis.md) and [Tripolitania](Tripolitania.md) are similarly easy to integrate, although it may be difficult to conquer these regions without attracting unwanted interventions from foreign powers. Stealing Inner Morocco from Morocco in particular, if combined with demanding Morocco renounce claims on West Sahara, will allow for colonization of the Sahrawi territory.

At the start of the game, the Barbary minors are in no position to take on [France](France.md) directly, but France may be persuaded to hold off by improving relations and cutting trade and investment deals. Maximizing relations with the other major powers is also advisable, although they are unlikely to make any commitments more significant than economic investment or a guarantee of independence until the player is diplomatically recognized. The Ottoman Empire is likely to be the first major power to want to strike a defensive pact or alliance with the player, although its positive geopolitical predisposition to France may make an Ottoman alliance a liability in the long term. It may be worth becoming a protectorate of a great power such as [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) until the player is sufficiently strong, however one make sure their liberty desire stays relatively high, otherwise they are at risk of being reduced to a puppet.

Economically, Algeria and North Africa more broadly have ample supplies of iron, and lead, but only meager reservoirs of and . Agriculturally, it can support many livestock ranches for , meat and fertilizer, but has more limited room for wheat or cotton, and no capacity for growing dye, silk, tobacco, or opium domestically. Asking [Russia](Russia.md) or another power for trade deals of surplus of wood and other basic economic goods will be necessary as Algeria industrializes beyond its ability to harvest basic goods domestically. If wood can be sourced, Algeria can relatively quickly industrialize its economy and build up towards production on the strength of its mining industry. Colonizing the Sahara will provide additional access to coal, and bordering African colonial regions contain additional coal, iron and . Algeria's resources are well-suited to military industry production long term.

Modernizing the economy, building universities, and liberalizing laws will put Algeria on a trajectory to recognition. Once recognition is secured, Major Powers will be much more willing to enter into alliances and other treaties, and infamy for claiming the French colonial outposts on the coast is reduced, paving the way to expelling France. It is nevertheless advisable to heavily invest in expanding the military and associated industries, both to dissuade foreign intervention and to make Algeria a more appealing ally quicker. Annexing Morocco or Tunis and researching Pan-nationalism will unlock formation of the [Maghreb](Maghreb.md).

Disabling Voice of the People makes it less restrictive on timing of conquering neighbors by France.
