**Transvaal** – called *Potchefstroom* at the start of the game – is a [republic](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Republic) and unrecognized power in the South Africa region. Its capital is the city of Pretoria in the state of Transvaal, next to its twin state [Oranje](Oranje.md) (Winburg).

## Historical background

Transvaal was founded by Boers, descendants of Dutch settlers in Cape Colony, who left after the British took formal ownership of the colony in 1815.

Historically, the Boers left after 1836, and Transvaal was not officially founded until 1852, though there were several short-lived Boer republics established before then.

## Scripted content

Main article:
Southern Africa content
Transvaal shares a journal entry with nearby countries related to the struggle for dominance over the *South Africa* [geographic region](Geographic region.md). Specifically, Transvaal must ensure diplomatic recognition and coastal access to gain dominance over the other minor states in the region. Through this journal entry, Transvaal may seek confederation with other Boer states, such as [Winburg](Oranje.md) and [Natalia](Natalia.md).

### Diplomacy

Transvaal's sole interest in its home Southern Africa region. It neighbors the decentralized [Ndebele](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Ndebele) and [Tswana](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Tswana), as well as the centralized [Griqualand](Griqualand.md) and [Winburg](Oranje.md).

Transvaal starts with a treaty with three treaties giving them transit rights to allow for trade with the World Market, as Transvaal starts off landlocked: they have one with [Winburg](Oranje.md), one with [Waterboersland](Griqualand.md) and one with [Great Britain](Great Britain.md). The last one also guarantees the independence of Transvaal. All treaties last for 10 years.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Transvaal has no starting industry and only two agricultural buildings, a Millet Farm and a Livestock Ranch.

**Resources**

Transvaal starts with access to a large amount of coal and iron, though almost no wood. This makes starting industrialization difficult initially, but easier once started.

### Culture and religion

Transvaal's only accepted primary culture is Boer, who are the descendants of Dutch settlers in the cape colony that were living there for over a hundred years before the British seized the colony during the Napoleonic wars. Dissatisfied with British rule, these settlers ventured out of Cape Colony and established the Independent Republics of Transvaal and Oranje.

As such, despite being the sole primary culture, Boers represent only 1.81% of Transvaal's starting population. The remaining 98.19% are comprised of the state's indigenous black African population, which include: Sotho (48.3%), Nguni (29.2%), Tswana (12%), Zulu (4.97%). There's also the Khoisan (3.55%) who, unlike the rest who are Bantu peoples that migrated from the north during the early medieval period, have inhabited the area since the Stone Age.

Like their cultural make-up, the Transvaal's religious order is also extremely lopsided. Though [Protestant](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Protestant) Christianity is the [state religion](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/State_religion), it is only adhered to by 1.81% of the population at the start of the game, whilst the vast African majority follows their traditional animist beliefs.

As hinted at earlier, Transvaal's demographic woes are going to be the major source of political strife. Though a republic with a (limited) democratically elected government, it is a very conservative and exclusionary country. Not only is over 90% of their population unaccepted on both religious and cultural grounds, but a portion of the Khoisans are also enslaved.

Though it's definitely possible to end the game with a sheer Protestant Christian majority, you are very unlikely to displace the indigenous Africans as the majority cultural group even with the help of later European immigrants (who themselves may also not be of an accepted cultural-religious group), and especially so if you intend to expand you reach across the South African region. Therefore, it's important to pass progressive reforms in order to placate the massive non-Boer population. In particular, Cultural Exclusion is a must, since in addition to increasing acceptance, it will allow pops in the Cultural Erasure acceptance status to work in government buildings, otherwise the player may see their construction sectors and government administrations largely empty.

### Economy and Trade

In this time period, gold isn't only worth a lot of money, it is money. Therefore, a country that's lucky enough to possess gold as an extractable resource is bound to be a ridiculously wealthy one if it works to develop the mines for it. The Transvaal Republic is one of those nations, as the Transvaal state is loaded with gold, though it is not immediately discovered at the start of the game. Additionally, gold is also abundant in the bordering states of Vrystaat and the Northern Cape, which can provide the Transvaal with even greater wealth if these states are annexed. A fully developed and staffed goldmine is a virtual money printer, and so the Transvaal can generate enough income from minting to afford the highest spending commitments whilst keeping taxes low, greatly encouraging a high standard of living.

Besides gold, the Transvaal state also has abundant coal reserves and access to iron and wood, useful for feeding their gold mines the tools and fuel they need to rack in cash. Another resource that becomes very important later on in the game, rubber, can also be discovered in the neighboring Portuguese colony and in Zambezi to the north.

In regards to trade, Transvaal starts the game isolated to its own internal market, and so it can be very beneficial to enter the market of a major European power, even perhaps that of the hated British Empire.

### Military and Expansion

Transvaal starts the game with a rather small force of three professional battalions and nine potential conscripts under Peasant Levies law, but fortunately your fellow Boers in [Oranje](Oranje.md) (your likely first target for expansion) have an even smaller starting army. Also, though the armies of neighboring [Zulu](Zulu.md) and [Gaza](Gaza.md) (easy access to the sea) are about 3x larger, they are also much more primitive, and so your European firearms and tactics will prove to be quite the equalizer in the early game. However, the combined military manpower of the three previously mentioned states are unlikely to be enough to face the British Empire (which also starts the game in a defensive pact with the neighboring Portuguese), so it's important to bide your time and build up your manpower reserves if your intention is to unite the entire Boer homeland. It may be necessary to become a client of the British Empire in order to avoid a disastrous confrontation early on.

Later on in the game, when your most likely enemies will be the other European powers blobbing across the continent in addition to the British themselves, you'll find that the by then extremely wealthy Transvaal can punch well above its weight militarily, provided you have passed the right reforms to boost your manpower reserves as much as possible. This is because you can easily afford to equip all your troops with cutting edge hardware and pay them well enough that the Patriotic Fervor buff provided by the military interest group triggers, adding a +10% or even +20% boost to your troops' attack AND defense.
