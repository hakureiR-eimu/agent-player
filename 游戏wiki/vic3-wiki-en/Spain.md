*"Ruling in the name of the young Queen Isabella, Queen Mother and regent Maria Christina has been forced to cede power to Spanish liberals to secure the throne against the Carlist threat. Can this threat be curtailed, or will another Ominous Decade come to pass?"*

Spain is a major power in southwest Europe. The dominant force on the Iberian peninsula, it has two long borders with [France](France.md) and [Portugal](Portugal.md), as well as a short one with [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) through Gibraltar. Through the cities of Ceuta and Melilla in al Rif, it also borders [Morocco](Morocco.md). It controls [Cuba](Cuba.md), [Puerto Rico](Puerto Rico.md) and the [Philippines](Philippines.md) as colonies, a remnant of its once-vast colonial empire.

As an Iberian nation, Spain can form [Iberia](Iberia.md). However, the country starts in a civil war against the [Carlists](Carlist Spain.md), fought through [guerilla warfare](Spanish content.md).

Spain is one of four recommended countries for the *Hegemon* objective, along with [Prussia](Prussia.md), [Japan](Japan.md), and [Egypt](Egypt.md).

## Historical background

Spain was formed in 1469 by the marriage of Ferdinand of Aragon and Isabella of Castile. These two would oversee Christopher Columbus as he landed in the New World and claimed the lands for them. Between that time and nearly 20 years before the game start, Spain was the most powerful and wealthiest empire in the world, controlling much of what is now Central, South and some of North America.

However in 1808, Napoleon invaded and occupied Spain, deposed the royal family and chose to put his brother in charge. The Spanish people revolted at this, and the Peninsular wars were expanded, the war arguably started in 1807, after the war of the 4th Coalition, when Napoleon went through Spain to occupy Portugal for refusing to join the Continental system. The Spanish Empire arguably was broken by the occupation; without acceptable rulers (Ferdinand VII and his father Charles IV, or Carlos in Spanish) and the mainland occupied with a pro-revolution authority, Spanish fighters fashioned the term "Guerrilla warfare" as their resistance against the French; unable to defeat he *Grande Armée* in a direct confrontation, but nevertheless hounding, killing and opposing the occupiers at any opportunity, at great cost. The reprisals and attempts to defeat the resistance devastated much of mainland Spain, and the Peninsular war lasted until Napoleon abdicated in 1814, to be sent to his first exile on Elba. The power vacuum in the Spanish Empire during this period may have been the first step in sowing the seeds of ideas of self-rule in their American colonies. After the wars of independence, only [Cuba](Cuba.md), [Puerto Rico](Puerto Rico.md) and the [Philippines](Philippines.md) remained, and Spain underwent a series of civil wars, called the Carlist Wars, over who was the rightful heir of Spain.

At game start, Spain is experiencing the 1st Carlist war (1833-1840), a conflict mainly centered on the [Carlist](Carlist Spain.md) homelands of the Basque Country and Aragon, Catalonia and Valencia, and about the legitimacy of Isabella II, daughter of Maria Christina of Bourbon-Two Sicilies and Ferdinand VII, with the Carlists contesting that his brother Carlos (hence *Carl*ist) would be heir had Ferdinand not repealed the Salic Law just before his death (Salic law forbids inheritance by women).

## Formation

If Spain ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country which controls its capital, Madrid, and at least one other Spanish homeland state. Its core states are the Spanish homelands, which comprise its starting states in the Iberia region. Spain can also be formed by any country below Kingdom tier with Spanish as a primary culture[1] that controls 75% of the Spanish homelands.

## Politics

Spain is a [Constitutional Monarchy](Government type.md) in a Regency, with Queen Regent María Cristina de Borbón supporting the Landowners, while the child Queen Isabel de Borbón will support the Petite Bourgeoisie. This provides the country with the modifier *Regency Period Regency Period Country modifier −25% Enactment Speed −10 Legitimacy +25% Petite Bourgeoisie Political Strength*.

### Interest groups

Spain begins with the Landowners, the Catholic Church and Armed Forces in government as the Moderate Party. The Intelligentsia, the Industrialists, and the Petite Bourgeoisie are in opposition as the Progressive Party, while the Rural Folk don't participate in elections due to their starting Carlist leader. The Trade Unions are the only marginalized interest group at the start.

The Landowners have the Moderantist ideology instead of the default Paternalistic, making them more receptive of electoral systems as well as making them endorse Free Trade. They also have Pro-Slavery (colonial) in addition to the rest. Meanwhile, the Armed Forces have the ideology Liberal Praetorian, making them proponents of Census Suffrage and National Supremacy.

## Strategies and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Spain
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

### Iberian Twilight opening

With the Iberian Twilight DLC, Spain starts off the game facing a challenging Journal Entry, *The Carlist War*, putting the player's relatively liberal Cristino forces against [Carlist Spain](Carlist Spain.md). Despite Carlist Spain's deceptively small territory, they have a formidable army, and defeating them will require investing in the military. Luckily, although Spain starts in a deficit, its subjects provide plentiful tobacco and sugar to boost military morale, although this alone is unlikely to be sufficient. 5 infantry and 5 cavalry are likely enough to reliably win battles against the Carlists. Putting the army into fighting shape is also going to require increasing the availability of iron in the Spanish market, or else armies will face organization penalties from shortages. The military emergency is urgent enough that it is advisable to build Construction Sectors despite the deficit, and plan to rely on the private sector to build the economy while the government pays off debt. Particularly given that the objectives of the difficult mid-game *Economic Regeneration* Journal Entry primarily relate to significantly increasing both industry and SoL, trading away foreign investment rights can also help build towards these goals while facing debt. Spain itself can later easily get further investment rights in its former colonies in Latin America if there is ever a shortage of investment opportunities in Spain. Offering up investment rights can go a long way to getting a non-player Major Power to permanently cede colonies and other far-flung dependencies. Maxing out relations with Great Britain will also allow for a Decision that is likely to result in Britain ceding Fernando Poo back to Spain.

Once *The Carlist War* is complete, *The Spanish Main* will fire as soon as Spain is at peace, and allows the player to choose between pursuing the *Hispanoamerica* Journal Entry to seek peaceful relations with the former Spanish colonies of Latin America, or *Reconquista* to attempt to reconquer them (and immediately incur the wrath of Britain, USA, and all historical Spanish colonies). Spain will also encounter *The Two Spains*, which creates a tug of war between Conservative (Catholic Church and Landowners) and Liberal (Intelligentsia and Trade Unions) interest groups, as well as an accompanying Tension bar, which further increases Movement Activism and Coup chances in proportion to Interest Groups' overall discontent. Completing the event in favor of either side will unlock an event that further cements the power of the victorious faction.

Researching Nationalism will fire an event that gives Spain a claim on al Rif and a 10 year timer to take it or face consequences. Unless the player has gone out of their way to anger Britain or France, winning this war after the Carlist war is complete should be trivial.

Researching Nationalism, Railways *and* Improved Fertilizer will fire the *Economic Regeneration* Journal Entry. While the Journal Entry's rewards are significant, completing it requires a rather robust economy. Depending on the state of Spain, it may be advisable to delay researching the final technology (likely Improved Fertilizer) until the objectives of *Economic Regeneration* are in reach.

### Initial construction and economy

Construction Sectors are a must, since Spain has a measly 4 of them, in the states of Castile la Nueva, Cataluña and País Vasco. The investment pool will hoard very quickly. Initially the construction runs on wood, but it should be easy to switch to iron quickly, as there is plenty of potential Iron Mines in northern Spain. Incorporating País Vasco should also be a priority, as it is a prime location for mines and heavy industry, but only if construction efficiency penalties are removed.

A concern long term is the lack of wood production available in Iberia. The best states to build in by far are Asturias and País Vasco in the north, due to the coal and iron [state traits](State traits.md) respectively and their role in establishing either of the two [flavored companies Altos Hornos de Vizcaya or Duro y Compañía](List of flavored companies.md), both of which are really suitable to kickstart Spain's industry. It is probably best to pick just one of them, as they have a big overlap.

There is however an issue with just investing in those two states; namely that there is a limit to how much can be invested in each state before Railways are unlocked. Researching this is therefore a priority for Spain. Until then, Baja Andalusia, Valencia and Castile la Vieja are also not bad due to their iron deposits, once infrastructure in the north runs out. Alta Andalucía and Murcia are the best locations for Glassworks due to being the states with the most lead lead.

### Gibraltar

Before the introduction of tolls, it is generally not worth it to attack [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) solely for the purpose of retaking Gibraltar, but if they take the other side in a [diplomatic play](Diplomatic play.md), one can add it as a demand. Just make sure not to demand anything else from the UK, so you don't have to venture anywhere else and risk being successfully invaded by sending armies away on an expedition; the British army almost always has perfect naval invasion support for even their largest army group, so do not underestimate their capacity to naval invade. Alternatively, a friendly Great Britain is liable to part with it to pull a powerful Spain into a Diplomatic Play, or to trade it for temporary concessions like market access.

With the introduction of tolls, the lucrative Gibraltar toll makes Gibraltar a worthy target; be sure to break the starting defensive pact (*Treaty of the Quadruple Alliance*) first. Unlike other tolls, the Gibraltar toll can only be collected when one power controls *both* ends.

### Expanding the Navy

Spain starts with a very weak navy, at least compared to its size, so naval invasions are initially out of the question. While expanding the navy, consider using the [Philippines](Philippines.md) as a staging ground to annex that part of South-east Asia or the Spanish part of Al Rif to attack [Morocco](Morocco.md) and potentially later [Algeria](Algeria.md). A third possibility is to establish a colony in Togo, and hope that it will border either [Danish](Denmark.md) Togo or [Dahomey](Dahomey.md) and expand from there.

### Iberia

Spain needs to have Portugal as a puppet in order to form Iberia. Getting a puppet is preferable over outright conquering them, due to both the lower [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy) cost, and the fact that Portugal has so many colonies spread around the world, that has to be either taken or released. This is easier said than done however, as Portugal starts with a defensive pact and trade agreement with Great Britain, and sometimes Portugal may join the British Empire.

Starting a Sovereign Empire yourself could be a solution, as Spain starts with good relations with Portugal, a defensive pact, as well as an obligation from them. Getting Portugal to join and then using the bloc leader action will do the job.

### Reclaiming the old empire

Many countries in Central and South America (nearly all of them except [Haiti](Haiti.md) and [Brazil](Brazil.md)) were formerly parts of the Spanish empire. As such, many states have primary cultures which can be accepted by Spain even with National Supremacy (Hispanophone and European heritage). Be sure to expand the navy as Spain starts with a weak navy.

### Bourbon for everyone!

When going for the achievement, it will be a very economy focused game, with only minimal expansion. Spain's relatively isolated position and ample potential for mines and agriculture also mean that it is fairly viable to neglect Spain's military altogether and still grab the achievement by the 1920s, even without additional colonization.

Create good relations with both [France](France.md) and Great Britain, because you generally want to focus solely on economy, and thus want as few problems from European geopolitics as possible. It is not advised to expand into territories in Europe anyway, since it means more mouths to feed, which likely lowers [SoL](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/SoL). Likewise, limiting [immigration](Migration.md) can be helpful. İf you focus early on colonization, Spain is in a good position for that. So expanding in East Africa and eastern İndonesia is very helpful for the late game cause it has significant amounts of rubber. Once they are conquered they can be consolidated into a colonial puppet.

Ultimately, to increase SoL, the number of unemployed, peasants, laborers and unaccepted pops should be reduced as much as possible, and it is certainly possible to achieve 0% unemployment in mainland Spain. Citizen laws matter too. Especially implementing a law that increases the acceptance of Basque POPs. Cultural Exclusion and Multiculturalism will on the other hand lower wage for primary culture POPs, so it is a balance.

Unlocking the highest levels of institutions that increase SOL is vital. Enacting [welfare payments](Human rights laws.md) helps a lot, as does Workers' Protections/ Regulatory Bodies. Women's Suffrage/ Women in the Workplace can also help, assuming that the increase in employed POPs does not result in unemployment. Giving too much power to [capitalists](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Capitalists) is a risk since they do not typically like passing the laws for institutions that increase SoL. After you got enough colonies you can decrease the colonial institution and use that Bureaucracy for social security. A power bloc with the *food security* principle can get a flat **+1** to SoL and Public Health Insurance can give up to **+2.5** flat.

If running short of the mark of 20 SOL, you can release states with low SoL as puppets and colonies that are pumping resources into your market but not changing your SoL with that. Unfortunately, however, the Basque, Catalan and Andalusian states are also typically Spain's best states. After you reach 18 SOL with your new technologies, and profitable companies you should subsidize all the factories and keep taxes as low as possible. Subventions on clothing, furniture or groceries can also help getting over the line. Building a single level of Textile Mills, Furniture Manufacturies, Food Industries and Wheat Farms in every single state can also help to mitigate the effects of [MAPI](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/MAPI). Lastly, increasing government and military wages to maximum, will also increase SoL. Then, reaching SoL 20 is easily possible around 1910s - 1920s.
