*"In its transition from penal colony to Crown Colony, New South Wales has seen its territory reduced as the other provinces gained their own administrations. What does the future hold for Australia?"*

**New South Wales** is an insignificant power located in Oceania. Starting as the most populated and richest Australian country in the game, it is a natural candidate to form [Australia](Australia.md).

## Journal and decisions

As an Australian country, New South Wales receives the journal entry and decision both named *Federate Australia*.

### Interest groups

The Armed Forces and Rural Folk are in government. The Squattocracy, Anglican Church, Petite Bourgeoisie and Intelligentsia are in opposition. The Trade Unions and Industrialists are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

New South Wales begins as a colony of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) and thus part of the *British Empire* power bloc.

New South Wales starts with colonies in [Wati](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Wati) and [Ngāti Toa](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Ng%C4%81ti_Toa).

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.12. |

New South Wales has the Peasant Levies law.

New South Wales starts with three Battalions and can raise up to four Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st New South Welsh Army | 3 Line Infantry | — | Oceania |

New South Wales starts with no flotillas.
