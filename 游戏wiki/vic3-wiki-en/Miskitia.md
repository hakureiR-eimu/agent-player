The **Miskito Kingdom** is an unrecognized power in Central America. It has a single border with [Central America](Central America.md) who is likely to dissolve within a few years. With decent starting technology and many weak neighbors, there is a lot of opportunity to become an influential power in its region, and potentially the wider world. It is one of only three unrecognized nations in the Americas playable at the start, alongside the [Indian Territory](Indian Territory.md) and [Iquicha](Iquicha.md).

## Historical background

The Miskito Kingdom is a tiny nation in 1836, made up of the local Miskito people who have intermarried with descendants of a shipwrecked slave ship in the mid-seventeenth century. Placed under British protection as a protectorate from 1638-1787 and again in 1844-1860, the Miskito Kingdom has long been a historical ally of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) against incursions from [Spain](Spain.md). The Treaty of Manugua in 1860 would cause the British to recognize Nicaraguan sovereignty over the Miskito Kingdom, though promising rights to autonomy and self-governance to the Kingdom. By 1894, however, British interest in the region had waned and Nicaragua moved to permanently annex the Miskito Kingdom.

### Interest groups

Due to Miskitia's complete lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Miskitia typically starts with the Landowners and Petite Bourgeoisie in government. The Armed Forces, Devout, and Rural Folk are in opposition. The Intelligentsia, Trade Unions, and Industrialists are marginalized.

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.10. |

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Miskitia
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
The Miskito Kingdom seems like a difficult starting position, but it is surrounded by weak nations. Once Central America implodes, building a military larger than your neighbors can lead to quick military expansion. After conquering [Nicaragua](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Nicaragua), it can be advantageous to wait a couple years to allow your neighbors to make more buildings as you will be handicapped by your construction industry. But, don't take too long as larger countries such as [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) may try to annex or puppet them. Conquering [Panama](Panama.md) can also be an excellent economic boon, as you can later sell the Panama Canal Treaty Port to gain a sizable 20K income for 10 years.

#### Managing Turmoil

A unique problem that the Miskito Kingdom can run into is its rampant turmoil from discrimination, as its accepted cultures are pops with Native American and African heritage, which most of the Central American population is not. The law enforcement [institution](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Institution) should be established and upgraded as soon as possible. Nations such as [Guatemala](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Guatemala) have pops of Native American heritage that can be integrated with less problems for your empire.
