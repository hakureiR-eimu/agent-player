*"Ryukyu holds a highly strategic position in East Asia, existing in a state of dual subordination between the Qing Dynasty and the Japanese Shogunate. Will external pressure compel Ryukyu to play its hand?"*

**Ryukyu** is an unrecognized regional power on the island chain of the same name in the East China Sea. As a tiny country caught between [Great Qing](China.md) and their overlord [Japan](Japan.md) it will have to balance between the two. To make matters even more difficult Ryukyu is a Confucian country with an almost entirely Mahayana population.

## Military

Ryukyu has the Peasant Levies law.

Ryukyu begins with no Battalions and can call upon one Conscripts.

Ryukyu begins with no Flotillas.

## Strategy

Ryukyu begins with a budget deficit that is not immediately surmountable, leading almost inevitably to recurrent bankruptcy. Ryukyu's economic expansion is further hampered by a state religion that is at odds with most of its population. Despite this deficit, it is still advisable to recruit a few troops immediately, as otherwise Ryukyu suffers from a severe liberty desire penalty
