This page is intended to advise the player on how to play the [United States of America](America.md) in a "standard" fashion, with the goals of acquiring all potential homelands and generally becoming as powerful as possible. This is not intended for "challenge" or "alternate history" runs though some advice for pursuing the Star-Swarmed Banner achievement is included.

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
America
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

## Overview

America starts as a Great Power but only barely qualifies for that status and is nowhere near as powerful as the likes of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) or [France](France.md). That said the country has immense potential and can rise to become the undisputed #1 Greatest Power.

America starts off as a relatively liberal country. All pops of European Heritage are accepted regardless of their religion and those who have at least 10 wealth can vote; its [acceptance](Acceptance.md) of European heritage pops is at a base **+65**, although Afro-American base acceptance is only at **+25**. This does disenfranchise the poorest pops, especially Peasants and Laborers early on. Laborers may be able to eventually become wealthy enough to vote if there is a significant increase in Workforce Ratio and/or wages.

Government legitimacy under Census Suffrage heavily relies on including the winning party in the government. American politics are also unique for favoring a two-party system between the Democratic Party and the Whig or Republican Party. Most other parties are much harder to form in America with the notable exceptions of the Free Trade Party and Socialist Party of America; this can lead to groups like the Industrialists and Trade Unions forming their own parties and losing heavily to the mainstream party while the members of the latter gain vast amounts of political strength from votes. America also has the option to switch to Universal Suffrage in the early stages of the game, though this will make it even harder to get the Industrialists into government if the player desires any of their laws.

The Jacksonian Democrat ideology is unique to America and will be very common among newly generated characters for as long as Andrew Jackson is active. Apart from the support for Universal Suffrage, this ideology can be troublesome as it desires to maintain the status quo in several categories, most notably to preserve Legacy Slavery.

Early American politics are dominated by the Democratic Party which is primarily a coalition of the Southern Planters and the Yeoman Farmers. Though this is a strong conservative bloc, America's starting laws make this status quo much more tolerable compared to many other countries, with the notable exception of the issue of slavery. Any move to abolish or expand slavery will almost certainly threaten a civil war. The Southern Planters will be Powerful for several decades which makes it extremely difficult and dangerous to try to abolish slavery, but their power should naturally wane as America industrializes and the benefits of *The Missouri Compromise* decay.

America's primary quest in the early game is to expand west towards the Pacific Coast. If successful this will double America's already sizeable core territory to the point it rivals countries like [Russia](Russia.md). The only real military obstacle in the way is [Mexico](Mexico.md) which America significantly outclasses even at the start of the game.

This vast territory comes with an abundance of resources including a large domestic potential for Oil. America can support a large variety and amount of industries off of its domestic resources alone. Some regions of the country also have bonuses to Coal, logging and agriculture. That said, it lacks domestic Silk which hampers the textile industry and also lacks Rubber for electronics and Automobiles production later in the game. America will have a large number of coastal states to facilitate a large navy and Convoy fleet. In addition its access to both the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans allows for efficient trade with both Europe and Asia even without the aid of canals. America has a head start in industrialization with its high starting technology level, relatively high literacy (the second highest among the great powers) and already-decent economic laws.

One issue that holds America back is low population density; there simply aren't enough workers to make use of all of that land and resources. Attracting immigrants is one way to solve this problem, and America has a secret weapon in the Statue of Liberty to help achieve this along with naturally high Migration Attraction from all of its Arable Land.

America is a good country to play if:

- You want to become the undisputed #1 Greatest Power
- You want the benefits of a Great Power without dominating the game from the start
- You want clearly defined expansion goals
- You want to build a large and mostly self-sufficient economy
- You want to be relatively far from the diplomatic chaos of Europe
- You want to industrialize right away without having to deal with low literacy and/or deeply entrenched Landowners

America is a recommended country for the Economic Dominance objective.

## Politics

Before continuing, it is important to understand the roles of America's interest groups, how their balance of power is likely to shift over the course of the game, and how leader ideologies can help or hinder the player's goals.

### Interest Groups

#### Southern Planters

*"Many of the Founding Fathers saw the independent yeoman farmers of the North as the basic building block of the citizenry, but the vast wealth of the Southern tobacco and cotton plantations cannot be denied. That wealth gives the Southern Planters a just claim to participate in the administration of the republic."*

The Southern Planters largely represent the interests of Aristocrats, but a small number of Officers will also align with them. Under Homesteading a significant number of Farmers will also align with the Southern Planters.

The Southern Planters start as a leading force in the ruling Democratic Party. They get bonus political strength from *The Missouri Compromise* (which decays slowly over 50 years) and from Local Police Force. Their most notable quality is their vehement opposition to the abolition of slavery which puts them at the center of the Slavery Debate and American Civil War. Even putting aside the issue of slavery the Southern Planters are generally going to be an obstacle to the player's goals. Fortunately the process of industrialization will cause the Southern Planters' influence to wane as other interest groups gain wealth and rise to prominence in elections. Be aware that your Manor Houses will seek to increase their wealth - and that of the Southern Planters - by using some of the Investment Pool to build and/or buy new profitable farms and plantations, and all you can do is outpace them with buildings favored by Financial Districts (which will instead benefit Capitalists and Shopkeepers and their respective interest groups). Winning the American Civil War will immediately eliminate their influence over the country and as long as the player continues industrializing they are likely to remain marginalized for the rest of the run. Having the Southern Planters marginalized will also prevent the Democratic Party from running in elections, which will leave the Republican Party uncontested control over the nation's politics following the Civil War.

#### Yeoman Farmers

*"The smallholding American rural class, regarded by many of the Founding Fathers as the bedrock of the nation. The yeoman class has long established itself as the vanguard of both American rural populism and Westwards expansion."*

The Yeoman Farmers primarily represent the majority of Farmers and politically active Peasants. Some Clergymen and Laborers working in rural buildings other than mines will also join them (this includes logging, fishing and whaling buildings).

The Yeoman Farmers are the other leading force of the Democratic Party. They gain a significant amount of power from the Homesteading law which increases the political strength of Farmers and allows them to gain additional wealth from dividends of agricultural buildings which also helps them meet the wealth threshold to vote under Census Suffrage. Similar to the Southern Planters, the Farmers will try to increase their collective wealth by using Investment Pool funds to expand their farms and plantations but their power will wane with industrialization. Andrew Jackson's Jacksonian Democrat ideology causes them to oppose any change to the slavery law, either abolition or expansion, and risks their joining the [Confederate States of America](Confederate States of America.md) in the Civil War. This same ideology is also largely responsible for their alignment with the Democratic Party. When they are not run by a Jacksonian Democrat or Slaver the Yeoman Farmers won't care about slavery and may even join the liberal party. The Yeoman Farmers support Universal Suffrage and stand to gain from it as even Peasants will be eligible to vote and tend to align with the Yeoman Farmers. Their support for Universal Suffrage will persist even after Jackson's departure because it is also part of their inherent Jeffersonian ideology. Apart from that the Yeoman Farmers, like the Southern Planters, are most likely going to be an obstacle to the player's legislative efforts. Their most notable oppositions are toward Colonial Exploitation or Colonial Resettlement, which are required to colonize beyond the American West and particularly Alaska and Africa, and toward Multiculturalism which is necessary to pass during Reconstruction to gain Afro-American as a primary culture. Other notable oppositions are toward Laissez-Faire and Free Trade. They will also vehemently oppose changing out of Homesteading, as doing so will greatly reduce their political influence. The Yeoman Farmers can be appeased by passing Dedicated Police Force which the player may already want for reducing the power of the Southern Planters. Migration Controls will also appease the Yeoman Farmers and in practice will do little to hinder immigration to America because all religions and many cultures are accepted. Passing both Laissez-Faire and Free Trade can cause the Yeoman Farmers to join forces with the Trade Unions in the Socialist Party of America

#### Evangelicals

*"Ardent Christians from a surprisingly wide variety of Protestant denominations, the Evangelicals all agree that the nation needs to find its own spiritual awakening in order to save itself."*

The Evangelicals are primarily comprised of Clergymen and some Peasants. Only Protestant pops will join the Evangelicals.

The Evangelicals' influence is severely limited by Total Separation which accepts all religions and thus prevents religious conversion and denies the Evangelicals additional members from immigration and/or conquest. Their power also tends to naturally wane with industrialization and with rising Literacy. If they become politically relevant it will likely be as a secondary member of one of the two major parties. The Evangelicals are your earliest means to pass Public Health Insurance which is essential for population growth that the USA desperately needs. They will also support Regulatory Bodies after Corporatism is researched which is another essential boost to population growth. The evangelicals notably oppose Cultural Exclusion and expanding the rights of women. They are initially aligned with the Whig Party due to the Abolitionist ideology of their leader Charles Finney.

#### Intelligentsia

*"The intellectuals leading the nation's trends in literature, art, music and theater."*

The Intelligentsia primarily represent Academics and Bureaucrats, along with a smaller amount of Clerks.

The Intelligentsia are the initial leaders of the Whig Party and the primary advocates for the abolition of slavery, by sponsoring Slavery Banned and Cultural Exclusion. They will also support expanding the rights of women after Feminism is researched. They do not oppose any notably useful laws. Most members of the Intelligentsia are government employees and so the group's political strength can be increased by increasing Government Wages in the Budget.

#### Petite Bourgeoisie

*"Proprietors of small shops, landlords with a half dozen tenants, frugal clerks with tiny pensions and others who have more than the poor, but less than the rich."*

The Petite Bourgeoisie represent a surprisingly diverse number of professions. They are favored by Shopkeepers, Engineers and Clerks and will also attract some Bureaucrats and Academics. Under Homesteading some Farmers will also join. Only primary cultures and those that share both the European Heritage (or African Heritage if Afro-American becomes a primary culture) and the Anglophone traits will join the Petite Bourgeoisie.

The Petite Bourgeoisie are likely to rise to prominence after about two decades of industrialization, as the population of Shopkeepers grows and some gain a share of ownership over the country's industries. Their default stances aren't particularly useful and in fact they oppose the passage of Cultural Exclusion. However there are a number of leader ideologies that they can acquire that will allow the player to take advantage of their clout. One quirk of the Petite Bourgeoisie is that they can sponsor Proportional Taxation if Consumption-Based Taxation is passed beforehand, which can be done with the support of the Southern Planters and/or Yeoman Farmers. The Petite Bourgeoisie are initially aligned with the Whig Party due to the Republican ideology of their leader Daniel Webster, but this allegiance can change depending on the ideology of future leaders. Overall the Petite Bourgeoisie are a "wild card" in American politics; troublesome by default but capable of being helpful with the right leaders.

#### Industrialists

*"Investors and shareholders with an interest in technological advancement and industrial growth."*

The Industrialists primarily represent Capitalists along with a small number of Engineers and Shopkeepers.

The Industrialists will rise alongside the Petite Bourgeoisie as the country industrializes, with Capitalists reaping a share of the profits from industries. The Industrialists can be further strengthened - at the expense of the Petite Bourgeoisie - by researching Mutual Funds and switching Financial Districts to Publicly Traded which will eliminate Shopkeeper ownership of industries and leave all of the profits in the hands of Capitalists. The Industrialists primarily desire free-market economic reforms including Laissez-Faire, Free Trade and Commercialized Agriculture. They also endorse the expansion of colonization through Colonial Exploitation which will allow colonization of Africa and Alaska. However they will oppose liberal tax reforms such as Proportional Taxation, the abolition of child labor (which is necessary to maximize literacy and thus research rate) and expansion of worker's rights (which can help improve population growth by reducing mortality from dangerous jobs via the Workplace Safety Office). They endorse Private Health Insurance but vehemently oppose Public Health Insurance. The Industrialists sometimes join the liberal party but they are also very likely to go off on their own and form the Constitutional Union Party, even with the **-10** attraction penalty from the American Two Party System. The latter case can make it difficult to include the Industrialists in a legitimate government, especially under Universal Suffrage. Abolishing child labor and passing more liberal tax laws will further increase their attraction to this party, while passing Free Trade will reduce attraction. As long as industrialization continues and profitable buildings continue to be constructed and purchased by Capitalists the Industrialists are likely to maintain high approval from Loyalists which can keep them **Happy** even when trying to pass laws they oppose. Notably if they are not in government and their approval stays at least **+5** while trying to pass a law they oppose they will not form a counter-movement and thus will not contribute to the Stall chance. This can make it much easier to pass laws such as Proportional Taxation, Public Health Insurance and Regulatory Bodies.

#### Trade Unions

*"Organized labor seeking to organize workers in the mines, factories and fields and in so doing pool the strengths of the disparate interests into one voice."*

The Trade Unions will represent Laborers and Machinists, particularly those who don't work in agriculture. The Trade Unions' popularity will be reduced until Labor Movement is researched.

The Trade Unions will typically be the last interest group to rise to prominence. Their power will grow as the number and wages of Laborers and Machinists rise, which notably eats into the profits and thus the strength of the Industrialists. Passing Commercialized Agriculture will also bolster the Trade Unions by allowing Laborers working in agriculture to join the Trade Unions rather than the Yeoman Farmers. Universal Suffrage will also expedite their rise by removing the wealth barrier to vote. The Trade Unions can act as a "catch-up mechanic", sponsoring a number of useful laws the player may have missed out on earlier. These include Proportional Taxation, Compulsory Primary School, Regulatory Bodies and Public Health Insurance. On the other hand they can make it difficult to pass Laissez-Faire or Free Trade if they haven't already. The Trade Unions may join the liberal party but are more likely to form the Socialist Party of America especially if they are Powerful (socialist parties are exempt from the attraction penalties of the American Two-Party System).

#### Armed Forces

*"Professional soldiers and others whose interest are tied strongly to the country's military."*

The Armed Forces primarily represent Servicemen and Officers along with a small number of Aristocrats.

The Armed Forces are heavily restricted by National Militia which severely limits the number of Servicemen and Officers that the country can employ in peacetime and further reduces the group's political strength by 25%. They are the only interest group that naturally supports changing out of National Militia while the Yeoman Farmers and Trade Unions oppose. It is very unlikely the Armed Forces will be a significant player in American politics unless the player goes out of their way to support them. If the player wants to try to increase their power the best bet is to invest in the navy; America has many coastal states and the navy is not limited by the army model law. Increasing Military Wages in the Budget will also increase the Armed Forces' political strength. Apart from the army model the only other potential contribution of the Armed Forces is to sponsor expansion of colonialism.

### Notable Leaders and Ideologies

Abolitionist, Slaver and Jacksonian Democrat leaders can drastically change the balance of power in the fight over slavery. For example Abraham Lincoln can potentially become leader of the Petite Bourgeoisie and turn them into powerful advocates for abolishing slavery. Meanwhile Slavers and Jacksonian Democrats will oppose the abolition of slavery and potentially even join the Confederate States in the Civil War, such as the case with Andrew Jackson and the Yeoman Farmers. Abolitionists are also virtually guaranteed to join the Whig Party while Slavers and Jacksonian Democrats will join the Democratic Party. America has multiple historical Abolitionist Agitators, including the Evangelical John Brown. Note that Jacksonian Democrat can appear on generated characters as long as Andrew Jackson is active.

A Reformer can greatly ease the passage of Cultural Exclusion, especially if they are the leader of an interest group that normally opposes it such as the Petite Bourgeoisie. Reformers also endorse children's rights and appear long before the Trade Unions rise to power, but remember that Human Rights must be researched before Compulsory Primary School can be enacted. In particular getting a Reformer such as Theodore Roosevelt in charge of the Industrialists will flip their stance on children's rights from opposition to endorsement. That said Andrew Carnegie is likely to beat Roosevelt to the position of leader of the Industrialists if Carnegie Steel Co. is formed, and even putting Carnegie aside there is also the possibility that Roosevelt appears as leader of the Intelligentsia or Yeoman Farmers instead.

A Radical will endorse Universal Suffrage, Guaranteed Liberties and Protected Speech. A Radical that leads the Petite Bourgeoisie will flip their opposition to the latter two laws into endorsement. Ulysses Grant is a historical example, but he starts with -10 popularity and so will need to either gain popularity through winning many battles or get lucky with the Commander Commends Civic Coalition event in order to become their leader.

A Market Liberal can ease the passage of laws such as Laissez-Faire, Free Trade and Commercialized Agriculture especially when you can't include the Industrialists in government. Having the Yeoman Farmers led by a Market Liberal presents a golden opportunity to get the above laws especially Commercialized Agriculture as their oppositions will flip into support. Having a Market Liberal such as Andrew Carnegie in charge of the Industrialists may seem redundant at first glance but in fact it helps sway the Industrialists towards the liberal party where they are more likely to gain votes and legitimacy for government. Carnegie is almost guaranteed to rise to leadership of the Industrialists if the Carnegie Steel Co. is established. Beware that Market Liberals oppose children's and workers' rights just like the Industrialists.

A Jingoist leading any interest group other than the Armed Forces will greatly ease the process of reforming the army and/or expanding colonialism. A Fascist will also endorse army reform.

A Humanitarian leader allows for the passage of Multiculturalism which will make every culture accepted and thus, combined with accepting all religions, will make everyone in the world not blocked by other countries' migration laws eligible for immigration to America. This is especially handy for runs that don't trigger the American Civil War or otherwise miss out on gaining Afro-American as a primary culture, or if the player just really wants to run away with immigration. Note that Multiculturalism also satisfies the requirement to gain Afro-American as a primary culture if passed during Reconstruction, and that, however unlikely it is, managing to pass it during the Indian Removal will also immediately end that journal entry as well.

A Social Democrat will endorse Universal Suffrage, Proportional Taxation and Regulatory Bodies. They will also likely form a coalition with the Trade Unions in the Socialist Party of America; this quality can help get the Trade Unions into government to push for Public Health Insurance.

Joshua Norton will appear via event after 1860 and the incorporation of California. He is an extremely popular Enlightened Royalist Agitator aligned with the Petite Bourgeoisie and if allowed to stay in the country will attempt to transform it into a Monarchy with himself as emperor. Do not underestimate his ability to accomplish this; if the player doesn't want to become a Monarchy then it is best to just immediately exile him with the appropriate event response. On the other hand, he also supports Multiculturalism, which makes completing "Equality for All" much easier. Additionally, becoming a Monarchy opens up the Sovereign Empire Power Bloc type which may be of interest. There is also an achievement that requires pursuing this alternate history path.

## First Steps

The following should be done before unpausing at the start of the game:

- Begin Improving Relations with [Texas](Texas.md). Also recommend improving relations with [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) and [Russia](Russia.md). This is to facilitate the various expansions possible via [journal entries](American content.md).
- Switch Universities to the Philosophy Department production method.
- Switch some of the logging camps to [Priotize Softwood Production](List of production methods.md), preferably in the states that do not have a hardwood [state trait](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/State_trait).
- Switch Food industries to Sweeteners
- Institute consumption taxes. Services, Luxury Furniture, Tobacco, Liquor and Wine will provide the most revenue for Authority spent.
- Incorporate the states of Arkansas, Iowa, Michigan and Wisconsin, as America has plenty of administrative capacity left, and doesn't even need to use the best available government administration production method

The following are good choices for early research efforts, in no particular order:

- Nationalism will unlock the Manifest Destiny decision and is the first step towards gaining homelands in the west.
- Pharmaceuticals followed by Quinine will allow expeditions to Map the American Western Frontier. Quinine also unlocks the third level of Colonial Affairs which will accelerate colonization of the Native Americans. There's however a risk of ending up with a too large institution, once America has colonized all of the plains and does not have more states to go for, and then it will take a while before the institution can be scaled back.
- Modern Sewerage for additional Infrastructure to delay the need for Railways.
- Water-tube Boiler to increase the efficiency of mines.

Increasing construction capacity is the top priority. Build more Construction Sectors; with the above consumption taxes in place America is able to support up to 80 construction capacity before entering deficit. It is recommended to build these first Construction Sectors in either New York, Kentucky or Pennsylvania due to their large labor pools and Iron potentials. Speaking of Iron, the next step is to build up Iron Mines followed by Logging Camps, Coal Mines and Tooling Workshops in the same state(s) to make construction more affordable.

America starts well below its [innovation](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Innovation) cap. Build more Universities when the budget and construction capacity allow until at least the innovation cap is reached to maximize research rate. Additional Paper Mills will be needed to keep the price of Paper affordable. District of Columbia and New York are best for these, as it is here that Paper is used the most.

America starts with no Artillery Foundries; securing a steady supply is vital for the military.

## Going West

*"It is our destiny to stretch our territory from the Atlantic to the Pacific. From sea to shining sea!"*

America has multiple [journal entries](American content.md) that heavily encourage expanding west towards the Pacific Coast. Annexing these states and completing the relevant journal entries will add Yankee or Dixie homelands to these states, which will dramatically decrease the time needed to incorporate them and also encourage primary cultures to migrate to these states. A lot of the expansion will even be [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy) free.

### Texas

*"We observe the conflict between Mexico and Texas with great interest. Should the Texan Republic emerge triumphant, we should try to sway them towards joining our righteous union."*

[Texas](Texas.md) starts the game already at war with [Mexico](Mexico.md). All the player can do is hope the numbers roll in Texas' favor. If Mexico wins the first battle then chances are they will continue winning and annex Texas. In this case, it's best to just cut all diplomatic ties with Texas and call it a lost cause; war with Mexico for their portion of Texas is inevitable anyway. However if Texas manages to win a battle they will [capture Santa Anna](Texas.md) and immediately secure their independence. In this case you will want to increase relations with Texas to **Cordial** as soon as possible. Improving Relations is the minimum Influence that should be spent on this. You can also Bankroll and negotiate for [Foreign Investment Rights or Guaranteeing Independence](Treaty.md) to raise relations even faster, although once Texas is at peace you are not in a hurry. Once the necessary relations threshold is reached an [event](American content.md) will trigger that will allow the player to immediately annex Texas.

### The Mexican-American War

The player will need to research Nationalism and activate the [Manifest Destiny decision](American content.md). This will add claims on many western states, but will also cause a diplomatic incident in the Mexico strategic region that will add **24** [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy). It is advised to wait for this infamy to decay to at least **2** before starting the Mexican-American War, to avoid becoming **Infamous** and minimize the risk of Great Power intervention and radicals in the conquered territory. You may also want to build at least one Artillery Foundry and Munitions Plant to avoid equipment shortages.

It is advised to start the Diplomatic Play by demanding Return Mexican Texas; this puts the play in the Dixie region where [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) is typically the only other significant power with an interest and they are unlikely to intervene. It is also recommended to force the demand into a full Diplomatic Play since multiple states will need to be demanded in one war. Add Return California, Nevada, Arizona, Utah, New Mexico and Mexican Colorado and elevate all of them to Primary Demands. Use your remaining maneuvers to add War Reparations and possibly another infamy-free demand such as Revoke Claim if you have enough maneuvers left. These extra demands are primarily there to give Mexico an "out" to accept a peace deal as soon as possible. Even with this "out" and Mexico being **Fearful** it is very unlikely they will simply give in to your demands without going to war, but it is not unheard of either.

Mobilize your regular troops and at least 45 conscripts, an even mix of Skirmish Infantry and Mobile Artillery. Make sure to switch the Conscription Centers to General Training so your manpower mobilizes as quickly as possible. At least two armies is advised due to the nature of the front being split by Native territories which can cause armies to move around unexpectedly and potentially leave the front vulnerable. These forces should be sufficient to push all the way west to California; the Mexican army is slow to recover losses and will quickly find itself consistently short on manpower for successive battles. Alternatively sending about 15 battalions on a naval invasion to Veracruz can quickly capture Mexico's capital while the bulk of the Mexican army is busy fighting on the main front. Your navy can also Convoy Raid in the Gulf of Mexico. It will be necessary to either occupy all of the demanded states or the enemy capital to get Mexico to accept a peace deal. It is recommended to leave at least one of the "extra" wargoals unpressed so Mexico doesn't consider it a capitulation.

### Colonizing the Frontier

*"We intend for the Great Plains to become a new homeland for the American people. Its current inhabitants must be brought firmly under control."*

America must fully colonize all of the various Native American lands in order to complete Manifest Destiny and gain its western homelands. This is also necessary to resolve the [Oregon Border Dispute](American content.md). The process can be accelerated by researching Quinine and expanding the Colonial Affairs institution. There will be random events that can further accelerate colonization rate in exchange for increasing tension with the natives. In general tension is a good thing as it can spark Native Uprisings which are easily crushed and grant America Colonization Rights with the defeated tribe for even faster colonization.

Note that Colorado must be fully controlled by the United States in order to complete this journal entry, meaning the US will still need to attack from Mexico to complete this journal entry.

### The Oregon Border Dispute

*"The proper location of the Oregon border has long been an undecided matter between Great Britain and the United States. It is high time that this dispute is settled."*

Researching Quinine also unlocks expeditions to Map the American Western Frontier. Completing this expedition three times is another necessary step toward resolving the Oregon Border Dispute. Technically the expedition must be completed three times collectively by all nations, but other countries cannot be counted on to complete these in a timely manner so it is best to do them yourself. It is advised to select commanders with Explorer or Surveyor traits as they will have increased chances of events during the expedition. Generally if the odds of gaining progress are in your favor, go for the risky option, otherwise avoid likely peril. The following are some specific tips for completing the expeditions:

- Building a fort during "A Stop on the Way" will not only reduce the peril of the current expedition, it will unlock an option for the same event on future expeditions that will not only reduce peril but also add progress.
- During "The Miracle of Life" always choose "It's best for them to go to a village". This not only adds progress, but unlocks an option during "Peaceful Intentions" that will add progress and reduce peril.
- During "Peaceful Intentions" picking "Is there anyone who can guide us?" will not only add progress but also unlock an option during "When the Earth opened its mouth" that is guaranteed to add progress.
- If you successfully claim a mountain during "Mountain Sighted", always pick "We don't have the time" on all subsequent instances of the event; chances are even if you reach the summit it will already be claimed.

The last requirements for resolving the Oregon Border Dispute are that [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) is the overlord of [Columbia District](Oregon.md) and that American relations with Britain are at least **Cordial**. When all of the requirements are met Great Britain will receive an event that allows them to peacefully cede the states of Washington, Oregon and Idaho to America. The AI will always choose this option. Be on the lookout for Diplomatic Plays to transfer the Columbia District to another overlord such as [Russia](Russia.md) as this could make the Oregon Treaty impossible. In such a case the player will need to go to war and conquer these states. Regardless of how it is achieved once Washington, Oregon and Idaho are owned by America they will all become Yankee homelands and America will also gain a Claim on Idaho. Likewise the rest of the western states will gain Yankee or Dixie homelands once the Native American lands have been fully colonized and the states are fully owned by America.

An alternate way to resolve the Oregon situation is to go to war with Great Britain and transfer Columbia District; this is also another way to gain an interest in the Pacific Coast for *Manifest Destiny*. However, this method will not add Yankee homelands if the journal entry is not active.

## Abolishing Slavery

*"Slaveholders and abolitionists are engaged in increasingly rancorous debate across the nation. Sooner or later, the arguments will stop being made with words and start being made with bullets."*

Before abolishing slavery the player should consider how important it would be to gain Afro-American as a primary culture and/or whether it is worth the expense of the American Civil War. If the player is pursuing the Star Swarmed Banner achievement and/or plans to expand significantly into Africa then the Civil War route is recommended to gain African Heritage as a primary culture trait and greatly speed the process of incorporating African states. Otherwise the player may instead prefer to abolish slavery without triggering the Civil War. Regardless of the pursuit solution, one should decide early if America goes the Civil War or tries to prevent it. Both routes will be discussed here.

Regardless of which route is pursued it is highly recommended that the player not attempt to ban slavery while Andrew Jackson leads the Yeoman Farmers. The Jacksonian Democrat ideology will cause them to side with the Southern Planters which will all but guarantee that the Civil War will start and the Yeoman Farmers will greatly increase the rebels' power base. Beware of other interest groups that are also led by Jacksonian Democrats or Slavers.

### The American Civil War

*"War between the states is upon us. Now is the time to preserve the Union, or die trying."*

Going through the Civil War is necessary for America to gain Afro-American as a primary culture and with it, African Heritage as a primary culture trait. Winning the Civil War will also eliminate the Southern Planters' Clout and end the Democratic Party, leaving the Republican Party to govern the country uncontested and opening the floodgates for many reforms. However the war is certain to be extremely expensive and the Union will inherit the Confederacy's debt when the war ends, which can leave the player deeply in debt and saddled with substantial interest payments for many years.

There is a high chance that your President will be assassinated shortly after the conclusion of the Civil War. With this knowledge it is advised to prepare a desirable successor ahead of time. Look for Generals and/or Admirals who are aligned with the President's interest group, have a desirable ideology and at least 0 Popularity. This can be a way to get a Reformer into power at the critical time to pass Cultural Exclusion.

Attempting to pass Slavery Banned will greatly anger the Southern Planters and any other interest groups that oppose banning slavery, such as those led by a Jacksonian Democrat or Slaver. Angry opposition interest groups will start a highly radicalized movement to Preserve Legacy Slavery, and those interest groups which are in the government but not powerful will leave the government and join the movement as well. If this movement is backed by the Southern Planters and boils over into full-on civil war the Slavery Debate will fail and the American Civil War will begin. The revolutionaries will become the [Confederate States of America](Confederate States of America.md) and change their wargoal from annexing America to revoking America's claims, transforming the revolution into a secession. Note that it is not necessary for Slavery Banned to have enough support to actually pass, because it can be enacted instantly via the Emancipation Proclamation once the civil war starts. Also note that if the Southern Planters are powerful and in the government they will instead attempt a coup which will not start the Civil War.

As a revolutionary country, the CSA will have a very high conscription rate and can potentially have a larger army than the Union despite lower population. If too many states secede then the war could be very difficult. On the other hand, if the Southern Planters are too weak (from years of active industrialization), then the war may not be able to trigger in the first place due to insufficient radicalism. A careful balance must be struck to ensure the war can happen, while at the same time not making the Confederacy too powerful. The movement's radicalism will come directly from the clout of the Southern Planters, so bolstering or suppressing the group in the lead-up to the war can help modify their power as needed. Also, pay attention to the revolution events. Generally speaking, if the player has been actively industrializing since the start of the game, the Civil War can be won *after* Andrew Jackson dies, and the Jackson Democrat ideology disappears from US politicians.

The player will likely need to utilize all available Conscription Centers in order to match the Confederacy's manpower. The player should plan ahead for this and build enough spare Arms Industries and Munition Plants in the north to ensure these conscripts are adequately supplied and at a relatively affordable cost (remember that mobilized battalions consume 60% more goods than non-mobilized regulars). Under National Militia the player will not be able to keep all of these industries profitable in peacetime; one method to deal with is is to subsidize just enough buildings to cover the needs of peacetime regulars while leaving spare buildings unsubsidized and on basic production methods, then activating the more advanced production methods on the spare buildings upon mobilizing. Conversely if military industries in the south are not expanded before the war then the Confederacy will suffer severe shortages which will reduce their army to half-strength. Convoy raiding can furthermore ensure they cannot make up this deficit through imports. The shortages will also cause the Confederacy to spend lots of money it simply doesn't have and drive them towards bankruptcy, the Confederacy typically capitulates upon running out of Credit and going into Default, though unfortunately the player will take on the Confederacy's debt afterward.

### Reconstruction

*"While the military fight has finished, the fight for the nation's soul has not. The United States of America must rebuild itself after being split in two."*

Once the Civil War is won the player will have 12 years to complete three Reconstruction journal entries. Completing all three entries will end the Reconstruction period and add Afro-American as a new primary culture of America. Otherwise any changes to primary culture will occur once the 12 years have passed, depending on which objectives were or were not completed.

#### Equality For All

*"Create a system that recognizes all men, from the North and South, and Black and White, as true equals in this nation of ours."*

Passing Multiculturalism will secure Afro-American as a new primary culture when reconstruction ends. This is also very helpful if the United States wants to colonize Africa, as African states (once they have finished colonizing) will only take five years to incorporate due to the shared heritage trait. However, it is a very difficult law to pass as it requires specific leader ideologies such as Humanitarian or Anarchist. Joshua Norton, as an Enlightened Royalist, can be a large boon in this goal.

After [Patch 1.10](Patch 1.10.md), however, Afro-American average cultural acceptance must be at least **60** in order to complete the journal entry, which is quite difficult as Afro-American acceptance will be heavily penalized by timed modifiers. Always take the options increasing Afro-American acceptance from pulse events, if the goal is to complete the JE. Since Joshua Norton will spawn in the 1860's, it is best to wait until then to trigger the American Civil War, as Norton will make passing Multiculturalism relatively easy. Temporarily passing State Religion (increasing acceptance of Protestant pops, which nearly all Afro-Americans will be) or No Colonial Affairs (increasing acceptance from homelands) might be necessary if acceptance is still not high enough.

#### Readmit the Secessionist States

*"Readmitting the rebellious states back into the Union is essential for restoring the shaken spirit of the nation, and may help establish new powerbases for the government."*

Completing this journal entry is necessary to keep Dixie as a primary culture after the end of Reconstruction. However losing Dixie as a primary culture means little as Yankee has the exact same traits and so Dixie would remain eligible to join the Petite Bourgeoisie and would only be discriminated under Ethnostate, which is extremely unlikely given that the citizenship law is going in the other direction. Overall trading Dixie for Afro-American is a net gain due to the latter adding African Heritage as a primary culture trait. Therefore Readmitting the Secessionist States can be seen as an optional objective. If you accomplish this then Reconstruction can end early and apply the new Afro-American primary culture without having to wait out the remainder of the normal Reconstruction period.

After the conclusion of the Civil War all states that are Dixie and not also Yankee homelands will become unincorporated (notably this includes your capital the District of Columbia). Whether you intend to keep Dixie as a primary culture or not, it is advised to re-incorporate all of these states during the 12-year period of Reconstruction while Dixie is still a primary culture so the incorporation takes only 2 years to complete.

The real challenge of this journal entry is raising the percentage of Loyalists among Dixie populations in all of the Dixie homeland states (including those that are also Yankee homelands) to at least 20%. This task can be made easier by passing Guaranteed Liberties and then investing heavily into the Home Affairs institution which will increase the Loyalists gained from increasing Standard of Living. Start with nationwide SoL boosts like lowering taxes. Passing Proportional Taxation can help by shifting some of the tax burden away from the lower classes. Passing Charity Hospitals or Public Health Insurance and investing in the Health System institution will also significantly increase Standard of Living nationwide. These measures will likely generate enough loyalists in at least some of the Dixie states to meet the 20% requirement. Next consult the Loyalists map mode and identify which Dixie homeland states still need more Loyalists, and concentrate construction in these states. Employ all of the Peasants and create labor competition to generate higher wages.

As long as this entry is active there will be random events that can potentially make Dixie pops in a state more loyal. In general if you are trying to maintain Dixie as a primary culture then it is advised to always pick the option that is more favorable to Dixie. Note that several states particularly those out west especially Arizona, New Mexico and Oklahoma rarely if ever are subject to these events and so one cannot rely solely on these events to raise the necessary loyalists.

#### The Wild, Wild West

*"The western frontier has been set alight by the unrest in the rest of the nation. We must find a way to calm it down."*

This is another optional objective that primarily serves to ensure that Reconstruction takes a minimum of 5 years to complete. Simply maintaining low Devastation and Turmoil in the country will allow this entry to tick towards completion. As long as this entry is active there will be random events that can potentially add Devastation to a state, it is advised to always choose the option that does not add Devastation or adds less Devastation than the alternative.

### Avoiding the Civil War

The Civil War can be avoided by passing Slavery Banned and keeping it active for 10 years total, which will complete The Slavery Debate and trigger the Peculiar Reconciliation event which will remove the Southern Planters' Pro-Slavery ideology. (The Slavery Debate suppresses the normal trigger for this event.) If the civil war is avoided, America will avoid the expense of the war and the following reintegration of southern states, but lose the opportunity to gain African Heritage as a primary culture trait by adding African-American as a primary culture. It is also a much longer process, keeping the planters in power for longer, and likely halting the implementation of other desired laws, compared to just beating the southern states in a war. This option may be preferable if the player does not plan significant expansion into Africa or plans to pass Multiculturalism without the pressure of having to rush it before Reconstruction ends.

Preventing the civil war is a matter preparation and deliberate thought in where the construction of the country is invested. The key to avoiding the civil war is to minimize the clout of the Southern Planters and prevent any other interest groups from joining their movement to Preserve Legacy Slavery, and at the same time making Dixie POPs loyalists. In doing so, the movement's radicalism can be kept below 85-100 which will cap the revolution progress below the 100% threshold needed to start the war. The Southern Planters' clout should naturally fall over time as the country is developed with mines and industries.

There are various events regarding the extension of slavery in the west. Here it is possible to temporarily anger the slavery movement, assuming that one does not intend to press the matter for some time. This can weaken the slavers by decreasing the amount of POPs involved in the movement.

Note that even if the Southern Planters are not powerful, even a very small slavery movement can still start a civil war involving all Dixie states. It is also possible that the Southern Planters are overall not very powerful, but they still have a significant power base in a single underdeveloped state, giving them a platform to launch a civil war involving all southern states. In order to prevent the movement from radicalizing too much, one should invest in the Dixie states to increase loyalists through [standard of living](Standard of living.md) increases.

#### Mechanics

The base radicalization for trying to abolish slavery is **+75%**. America has a unique added *Slavery debate* modifier of **+20%**, meaning a base level of **95%**; almost but not quite starting the civil war by default. Keeping Local Police Force adds **-5%** on its own, setting the base level at **90%**. Note that changing the police law can in fact also trigger the civil war on its own. Additionally, there are effects that are more fluctuating in their nature. An imbalance in barracks and/or naval bases between Yankee and Dixie states also drives up radicalization on its own, so counter-intuitively, to prevent the civil war completely, one should place investments in the military of the southern states, not the northern. Lastly the amounts of loyalists and radicals among the POPs that support the movement, affects radicalization. Adding all of these factors together, one should be able to estimate the radicalization before starting to implement the law.

The very act of changing the law will likely drive up radicals, pushing the movement over the edge, so it is best to aim for a presumed radicalization of around **75%**, when starting to implement the law.

#### Implementation

The decision to make the law commitment should come at a time where there is not a war ongoing or on the horizon, and there is not any other reason to expect a sudden increase in loyalists/radicals for example due to an increase in unemployment, a sudden embargo or sudden decrease in the profitability of key industries. So don't change the production method of the entire construction sector at the same time, or something like that.

When implementing the law, it is best to keep as much surplus [authority](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Authority) as possible to speed up the process, as well as choosing any event options that increases implementation chance, decreases time or lowers the amount of Dixie radicals at the expense of various other enticing benefits. Suppressing the Pro-Slavery movement can also work, as they can't start a civil war if under 5% support, even at 100% activism. Passing Censorship and Secret Police helps significantly in this regard.

If the radicalization unexpectedly jumps to 100 anyway, there is nothing lost in abandoning the process and trying again two years later.

## At war with Great Britain

With changes to logistics in patch 1.13, Britain will have difficulty transporting large armies to reinforce Canada. However, America has a weakness in [Liberia](Liberia.md). Consider annexing Liberia and leaving an army there as defence. As [Hudson's Bay Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Hudson%27s_Bay_Company) and [Oregon](Oregon.md) have claims blocking colonization, it's probably better to use the "Transfer Subject" war goal. Note that the *Oregon Border Dispute* journal entry, which grants Yankee homelands to Oregon, Washington and Idaho, will become difficult to complete normally; however the player may simply transfer the Columbia District, then later annex them, since directly owning the three states completes the journal entry.

## Raising Population

The number one problem that holds America back is low population density. The homelands are vast and hold abundant resources but there simply aren't enough bodies to make use of it all. America will need to attract many immigrants and maximize its natural pop growth rate in order to raise enough population to reach its full potential. The remainder of the run will likely be focused on increasing the domestic population.

### Domestic Development

America is a "wide" country by nature, consisting of many states with relatively low individual populations, and many states that lends itself well to a specific specialization that together covers close to all bases. Each state must be developed with the concepts of Market Access Price Impact (MAPI), Economy of Scale and Pollution in mind. On one hand the presence of local supply and demand in each state will make certain industries more profitable to build in some states than others, such as Construction being cheaper in states with local Iron Mines while the Construction also makes those same Iron Mines more profitable. On the other hand each industry in a state should be able to eventually grow to at least level 50 (or its hard cap in the case of resource industries) to maximize the throughput bonus from Economy of Scale, the ability to do this being limited by the state's available workforce. Some states also have special traits that can further benefit certain industries. While it likely won't become a problem until later in the game the impact of Pollution also cannot be ignored. Pollution acts primarily as a punishment mechanic for stacking too many industries in a single state relative to its Arable Land, causing a double-whammy of reduced Standard of Living (SoL) as well as further increased Mortality throughout the state. Mines are notoriously polluting especially with more advanced production methods. Pennsylvania and Kentucky are particularly vulnerable to being choked by Pollution if their huge potential Coal Mines are fully developed; consider developing these particular mines no higher than level 50 so they get maximum Economy of Scale while limiting their Pollution. On the other hand states like California and Texas have huge amounts of Arable Land and thus far more capacity to handle Pollution. The following are some general tips on choosing which states to build which industries:

- States with Iron (and Coal) are ideal for hosting Construction Sectors and for making Steel and its derivative products. Especially Pennsylvania, since it already starts with a steel mill.
- States with Sulfur are ideal for making Fertilizer, Explosives and synthetic Dye. Paper is also a good choice if Wood is also present.
- States with Lead are ideal for making Glass. If Oil is also present then the state is ideal for running Houseware Plastics.
- States that can grow Cotton are ideal for making Clothes. A few states that can also grow Dye like Georgia are even better. States with both Cotton and Sulfur potential should also be considered once synthetic Dye can be made.
- North Dakota, South Dakota, Nebraska and Kansas enjoy **+20%** Agriculture Throughput but only Wheat/Maize Farms can take advantage of this, therefore production of Grain should be concentrated in these states. These farms can also produce some Fruit and Sugar. Grain, Sugar and Meat can all be used to make Groceries. Other states with Agriculture bonuses like California should focus on other products.
- Some states enjoy **+25%** output of Hardwood making them ideal for hosting Furniture Manufacturies that produce Luxury Furniture. California is also a good choice with its **+20%** Logging throughput bonus. If Iron is also present then various military goods can also be efficiently produced, especially.
- New York, New Jersey and Massachusetts enjoy **+10%** Shipyards and Military Shipyards throughput making them ideal for ship construction.
- At least one large Vineyard and Tobacco Plantation should be considered to help satisfy Pop needs for Intoxicants and Luxury Drinks.

Early industrialization will likely be focused on gainfully employing Peasants and generating raw profit which translates into more funding for the Investment Pool. Gainfully employed workers will also generate more tax income through Income Taxes, especially under Proportional Taxation, which will further increase the amount of Construction the country can afford and thus how quickly its economy can grow. When Peasants in a state are exhausted activating automation methods will lay-off a significant amount of workers but will allow the industries to grow further, increasing the Economy of Scale bonus and eventually re-hiring those laid-off workers and even promoting some of them to higher paid positions such as Machinists. Continuing to build in a state that has exhausted its available workforce will create competition between industries for the workforce, which forces them to offer higher wages in order to reach full employment. Productivity is the key to winning this competition and this is where industries that benefit from State Traits, Companies, MAPI and high Economy of Scale have a distinct advantage.

America is notable for having [flavored companies](List of flavored companies.md) that increase the efficiency of Railways. Standard Oil is one of the few companies in the game that directly benefits Railways with a construction and throughput bonus, and its prosperity bonus increases the Railways' throughput by another **+20%**. The prosperity bonus from Carnegie Steel Co. further increases the throughput of Railways by another **+10%**. Remember that Railways do not benefit from Economy of Scale and usually don't have access to company bonuses either, so these bonuses will make American Railways significantly more efficient than those of most other nations. This means less money spent on subsidies and fewer workers needed to generate Infrastructure.

In general gainfully employing the population and especially increasing their wages will result in increasing their SoL and that of the state as a whole. For the pops higher Standard of Living means higher growth rate, while increasing the SoL of a state increases its migration attraction. Raising SoL through increasing Wealth also stimulates the economy by creating more demand for consumer goods. Other methods of raising SoL include lowering taxes and the prices of consumer goods. In addition to lowering the tax rate and eliminating consumption taxes passing more progressive tax laws will also reduce the tax burden on those who can least afford to pay it. Proportional Taxation notably eliminates the Poll Tax and Land Tax in favor of primarily taxing Income and to a lesser extent Dividends; as a result it is usually the taxation model that brings in the most tax revenue overall. Graduated Taxation may not bring in as much overall tax income but it is the least burdensome on the lower classes with its lower Income Tax and higher Dividends Tax rates. Increasing the Workforce Ratio not only increases available labor but also generally has a significant impact on SoL as more income is brought into each household. Keeping the Trade Unions Influential and **Loyal** is one way to increase your Workforce Ratio.

Expanding the rights of women will greatly increase the Workforce Ratio at the cost of reducing Birth Rate; this is a net loss for overall population growth but a net gain for workforce growth. You may want to keep Legal Guardianship for the higher Birth Rate in the middle stage of the game and especially while there are still Peasants available to employ, but Women's Suffrage will be necessary eventually to maximize the available workforce. Also bear in mind that while the reduced Birth Rate is applied immediately upon passage of the law the workforce will need about a decade in order to adjust to the new Workforce Ratio.

Public Health Insurance is essential to maximizing population growth; it has the highest general effect on reducing overall mortality and the effects of pollution and also universally increases all pops' SoL. The Workplace Safety Office also contributes to population growth by reducing and eventually eliminating the increased mortality of certain professions; Regulatory Bodies will allow the establishment of this institution without any extra bells and whistles such as a minimum wage. Researching Socialism will unlock the final level of the Workplace Safety Office. Compulsory Primary School will further eliminate some additional mortality from certain professions and also unlock the last two levels of the Education institution allowing for maximum Literacy and research rate.

Lastly the [Statue of Liberty](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Statue_of_Liberty) is America's "secret weapon" in the competition for migration attraction. Steel-Frame Buildings is the required technology to build the Statue of Liberty and thus should be a priority when entering Era III Society Research. America will already have high base migration attraction from its vast amount of Arable Land and increasing SoL and available employment will add even more. Then adding an extra 25% to every state with the Statue of Liberty all but guarantees America will have the highest migration attraction in the world, which will lead to many automatic mass migrations. These will initially be of cultures of European Heritage, but gaining African Heritage as a primary culture trait through Reconstruction will open up many more mass migrations as well. Keeping the Intelligentsia **Loyal** will further increase nationwide migration attraction.

### Market Expansion

Expanding the American Market will open up additional avenues of immigration. This can be accomplished by either forcing countries to become Protectorates or convincing them to join your Power Bloc. A Trade League is the most suitable Power Bloc type for this purpose because all members will automatically be brought into the American Market. Other Power Bloc types must spend three mandates on Market Unification principles to achieve the same functionality. A Sovereign Empire would also be suitable for its ability to peacefully subjugate its members but this type is only available to a Monarchy. Freedom of Movement principles will help further stimulate immigration while Food Standardization principles will help with your domestic pop growth. Level 3 Freedom of Movement also allows migration between bloc members without actually bringing them into the same market.

Expanding the Power Bloc through diplomacy means not only generating Leverage but also improving countries' attitude towards you so they actually accept your invitation to join your Bloc. Maintaining a Power Bloc uses up 500 Influence which is several diplomatic actions' worth. It is best to begin cultivating relationships with desired Power Bloc members early on and well before you actually establish your Bloc while you have the extra Influence available. Simply having an interest in the region where the country's capital is located will generate some Leverage. Guarantee Independence, Trade Agreement and gaining Investment Rights in a country will all also add Leverage. More advanced pacts like Defensive Pact and Alliance will generate even more Leverage at the cost of more Influence. If that is still not sufficient Leverage you can close the gap by Bankrolling, exporting goods to the country and constructing buildings through Foreign Investment.

There are several things to consider when choosing countries to add to the American Market. First of all pops who would be discriminated wont be able to migrate if Migration Controls is active and even without it they are far less likely to do so. Countries whose primary cultures share traits with your own are also significantly easier to add to your Power Bloc through diplomacy. For both of the previous points you will want to initially look for countries with primary cultures of European Heritage; if you gain Afro-American as a primary culture through Reconstruction you can add African Heritage to that list as well. Passing Multiculturalism will make every culture likely to immigrate provided their countries of origin allow them to do so but won't make it easier to add them to the Power Bloc. Immediate neighbors are also easier to add to the Power Bloc. In addition to immigration candidates you will also want countries that can provide the few resources that America lacks domestically, most importantly Rubber but also Coffee and/or Tea to satisfy your pops' Luxury Drinks variety. Lastly you will want countries that are at least Minor Powers to gain Mandates more rapidly. With these requirements in mind there are several candidates that come to the forefront:

- [Mexico](Mexico.md) is your immediate neighbor to the south, is a Minor Power, shares your European Heritage and possesses potential for Rubber and Coffee. The obvious hurdle here is that completing Manifest Destiny will drop relations with Mexico to rock-bottom and even after years of repairing relations they are likely to just expel your diplomats before you can become **Cordial**. But if you are persistent in your diplomatic outreach Mexico may eventually change their strategy and become open to diplomatic relations. This can be preferable to just outright subjugating them as doing so will incur a lot of Infamy and will almost certainly bring at least one Great Power to their defense. However there is also a strong possibility of Mexico having a strong Anti-American Lobby which can make it far more difficult to add them to a Power Bloc through peaceful means. Funding Lobbies can potentially generate a Pro-American Lobby to counter the Anti-American Lobby but it is costly and will likely take a very long time to generate a Lobby. Alternatively, the player could simply protectorate Mexico, though this will cost a lot of infamy and they are likely to be rebellious afterwards.
- [Brazil](Brazil.md) is the largest prize in South America. Like Mexico they start as a Minor Power, share European Heritage and possess potential Rubber and Coffee. While Brazil doesn't have the same diplomatic hurdles as Mexico they can end up becoming too powerful to bring into the Power Bloc. If they rise to Major Power it can take a lot of foreign investment to generate enough leverage to invite them into the Bloc. In this case a Pro-American Lobby in Brazil can help close the gap and Funding Lobbies can help generate one. Brazil can even rise to Great Power and become near-impossible to add to your Bloc; in this case you may want to consider a war to break up Brazil by forcing it to release countries who will likely be more than happy to join your Bloc. Lastly taking a state in Guyana from either [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), [France](France.md) or the [Netherlands](Netherlands.md) will make Brazil your immediate neighbor and increase Leverage.
- [Colombia](Colombia.md) also known as New Granada will become your immediate neighbor if you acquire the land for the Panama Canal and fulfills all of the other desirable criteria.
- Other desirable candidates in South America include [Venezuela](Venezuela.md), [Peru-Bolivia](Peru-Bolivia.md), [Chile](Chile.md) and [Argentina](Argentina.md). Be aware that the various South American countries tend to form rivalries with one another and courting a country's rivals can cause anti-lobbies towards your country to form. In general you will want to prioritize Brazil and avoid courting countries that Brazil is hostile to.
- [Cuba](Cuba.md) starts as a subject of [Spain](Spain.md) but would be a desirable addition to the Power Bloc. Keep an eye on Cuba's Liberty Desire and attitude towards Spain and look for an opportunity to help them win their independence. The [Philippines](Philippines.md) are another Spanish subject that would be a good addition to the Power Bloc. As Spain is usually hostile towards America (no matter which side wins the initial civil war), simply attacking them to transfer their subjects (or liberate them, then pull them into the power bloc later) is the easiest way to pull them into your bloc.
- [Canada](Canada.md) is likely to remain a loyal subject of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) but would be a very valuable addition to the Power Bloc for its large population of European Heritage pops and the fact that it is your immediate neighbor to the north. If Canada and Great Britain should ever have a falling-out do not hesitate to capitalize on the opportunity.

Although Minor Powers are recommended for getting more Mandates, do not limit yourself to them. Even an Insignificant Power can provide a little bit of extra immigration and count towards unlocking the third and fourth Principle slots; at least 10 members are required to unlock the fourth slot. America's sheer power should make it easy to maintain Cohesion in a Trade League or Sovereign Empire so there is little downside to adding an easy Insignificant Power to the Bloc.
