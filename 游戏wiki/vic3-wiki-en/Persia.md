**Persia** is an unrecognized major power in the Middle East. It borders [Turkey](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey) to the west, [Russia](Russia.md) and [Turkmenia](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkmenia) to the north, [Herat](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Herat), [Kandahar](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kandahar) and [Makran](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Makran) to the east, and [Oman](Oman.md) to the south.

If Persia becomes recognized, its name changes to *Iran*.

Persia is one of six eligible countries for the *Great Game* [objective](Objective.md), the others being [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), [Russia](Russia.md), and the Afghan minors of [Kabul](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kabul), [Herat](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Herat), and [Kandahar](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kandahar).

## Scripted content

With Sphere of Influence, Persia starts with a journal entry to expand eastward. Persia is also one of the countries that can be played for the [Great Game objective](Objective.md).

### Interest groups

Persia begins with the Landowners and Shi'a Ulema in government. The Armed Forces, Intelligentsia, and Rural Folk are in opposition, while the Petite Bourgeoisie, Industrialists, and Trade Unions are marginalized.

Persia rules over [Muhammara](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Arabistan) as a vassal.

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.11. |
|---|---|

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Persia
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

### General overview

Persia starts out the game with little technology, a low literacy rate, and as a direct target of conquest for two Great Powers that are more than willing to use it as fodder for its imperialist ambitions. It has very conservative laws and a very high number of peasants working in subsistence farms.

However, it has a steady supply of every single resource needed in the early game, and its geography can be turned into an advantage as it has an open door to both the Middle East to its west and India to its east. A powerful Persia can have a continent-spanning empire, exerting heavy influence on in Central Asia, India, the Middle East, and the Horn of Africa.

### Industrializing

Persia has iron in every state at game start, coal in most states, lead in most states, and sulfur in its southern states. It does have wood in all of its states, but only small amounts. A Goods Transfer treaty with Russia can supply you with the wood you need until you conquer a more sustainable source of it. Mazandaran, Irak-i-Ajimi, and Khorasan are all great places to make your industrial bases, as they have supplies of all three of iron, coal, and wood and a large number of peasants.

Given the overwhelming power of the Landowners, it is highly recommended that you use Law Commitment treaties to help kickstart the process of modernizing Persia's economy, as it will take a long time to build up the clout of other IGs to the point you can pass laws with their support alone.

### Claiming Greater Khorasan

If you are attempting to complete the *Eastern Frontier* journal entry, the first objective will be to Claim Greater Khorasan. This only requires annexing [Herat](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Herat), and grants you a strong military buff as well alongside claims over various states in Afghanistan and Central Asia. Defeating Herat is not difficult, as countries will rarely come to their aid and their military is much smaller and weaker than yours.

The biggest threats to you in the early game are [Russia](Russia.md) and [Great Britain](Great Britain.md). Although you may eventually want to declare war on them, it is better to be cordial with them at the start and to get defensive pacts or alliances with one or both of them. You start with a lobby that supports Russia and a lobby that hates Russia, and the pro-Russia lobby may give you useful opportunities. [France](France.md) is a good fallback third option to try get a defensive pact with in case both Russia and Great Britain are irrevocably belligerent towards you.

After conquering Herat, you should wait until you have cordial [relations](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Relations) or better with Britain before claiming Greater Khorasan. Britain will receive an event after you claim Greater Khorasan, and if you have less than Cordial relations they may react negatively and roll a Belligerent attitude towards you, ending with them almost certainly starting a diplomatic play to take a few states.

### Further expansion

The claims you receive from the Greater Khorasan decision will give you a blueprint for the medium-term. As the foremost regional power with the largest economy and military it isn't too difficult to solidify your position in Afghanistan, Pakistan, and the rest of Central Asia. To expand into the Middle East, you may want to invade [Bahrain](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Bahrain) and/or [Mahra](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Mahra), as they both typically have extremely tiny armies and thus only require a token naval force to invade. Mahra's piece of Yemen will also give you a front with [Majerteen](Majerteen.md), which gives you an opening into East Africa.

The *Pacify the Turkmens* decision will cost a large amount of money for slightly faster colonization speeds. This is usually not a good decision to take as the expenditure scales with your GDP rather than your tax base, and you will only be able to colonize Merv after you defeat Russia in a war to revoke their claims on the rest of Turkmenia.

Once you have a border with them, you can enter India by invading [Punjab](Punjab.md). The states of Punjab and Pahari Punjab have a relatively large population and they produce a tremendous amount of dyes and silk, which can turn you into a powerful textile empire.

The [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md) will likely be the first Major Power(ish) that you want to fight. In most games, by the time you've established a strong position in Iran and Afghanistan, they'll be extremely weak and will have lost [Egypt](Egypt.md) as a subject. This makes them a great potential target for expansion. The Iraqi states of Basra or Baghdad are useful to have as they will give you a border with [Jabal Shammar](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Jabal_Shammar) and [Nejd](Nejd.md), allowing you to expand into the Middle East more easily. With **+50** relations with a Great Power, declare war on the Ottomans before they become the *Dead Man of Europe*. With enough war goals, Persia can get recognized in a single war. Preferably, this war should be fought soon or during Egypt's war with the Ottomans.

### Defeating Russia

At some point you will have to fight Russia. This is necessary for completing the *Eastern Frontier* journal entry as they have claims on Turkmenia and Chorasmia, subjects that control parts of Chorasmia, and will likely have expanded into other areas such as Tajikistan as well. This will also usually be the path of least resistance when it comes to earning recognition.

Russia's military is generally a paper tiger. Although it has an extremely large manpower pool, Russia often lags behind when it comes to military technology and rarely upgrades its units proactively. This means that the best way to beat them is to focus on modernizing your army and striking as fast as possible after you've researched General Staff and upgraded your army to Skirmish Infantry. Take note that even with your quality advantage Russia will still throw mountains and mountains of troops at you, so it is recommended to pick easy wargoals near the front to not prolong your wars. Besides the states needed for the journal entry, your claims on Armenia and the Azerbaijani states are good states to conquer as well - especially Baku, which will eventually spawn 240 oil fields.

Once you've beaten Russia enough, you will have all the states needed to complete the Eastern Frontier journal entry and will be recognized as Iran. As mentioned in the previous section, do consider the Ottomans as a target to gain recognization.

### Strait of Hormuz

With The Great Wave DLC, another viable strategy is to seize control of the Strait of Hormuz from Oman, in order to use strait tolls to fund further economic development.
