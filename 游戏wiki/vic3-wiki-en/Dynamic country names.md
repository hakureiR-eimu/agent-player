A number of [countries](Countries.md) have **dynamic names** when certain conditions are fulfilled. The usual determiners include the [states](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/States) that the country controls, the [laws](Laws.md) of the country, or whether it is a [subject](Subject.md) of another country. Dynamic names often overlap with [dynamic map colors](Dynamic map colors.md) and [dynamic flags](List of flags.md), though not always at a one-to-one correspondence.

## Revolutionary country names

Countries that appear through [revolutions](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Revolution) also take dynamic names, depending on the [political movement](Political movement.md) that started the revolution or [interest group](Interest group.md) leading the revolution.

A revolution from the [USA](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/USA) while the [journal](Journal.md) entry *The American Civil War* becomes and always uses the name [Confederate States of America](Confederate States of America.md) or [Free States of America](Free States of America.md), overriding any other name.

A revolution from [Sweden](Sweden.md) whose capital is in the state region of Gotland always uses the name **Gotland Revolt**.

In the following tables:

- "*Name*" refers to the name of the base country (e.g., [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), [France](France.md)),
- "*Adjective*" refers to the adjective form of the base country (e.g., British, French).

## Colonial administration names

Colonial administrations are named after the [strategic region](Strategic region.md) where the colony was initially founded. They all have a priority of 100 and are prefixed by the overlord's adjective.

| Name | Strategic region | Other conditions |
|---|---|---|

## Company country names

Company countries, that is countries "owned" by a [company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Company), always use the same name as the company. This overrides any other specific names.

## Country-specific names

In the following tables:

- A blank cell for "Priority" indicates a weight of 0 (i.e., least prioritized); if two or more names are valid at the same time, the highest priority one is used.
- "Primary" indicates that this name will only be used for the primary country (i.e., not by a revolutionary country).
- "Is any kind of republic" means a Corporate State, Presidential Republic, Parliamentary Republic or Council Republic.
- "Is a dictatorship" means a Corporate State, Presidential Republic, Parliamentary Republic or Council Republic with Autocracy or Single-Party State.
