**Ashanti** is an unrecognized regional power in West Africa. It directly borders [Borgu](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Borgu) and [Dahomey](Dahomey.md) to the east. Additionally it borders a list of decentralized countries: [Mossi](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Mossi) to the north, [Kong](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kong) and [Anyi](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Anyi) to the west, and [Ewe](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Ewe) to the south. Lastly, it borders [Danish](Denmark.md), [French](France.md), [British](Great Britain.md), and [Dutch](Netherlands.md) African coastal colonies.

## Formation

If Ashanti ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country that controls its sole core state, Ghana.

Ashanti begins with the Landowners in government. The Devout, Armed Forces, and Rural Folk are in opposition while the rest are marginalized.

Ashanti starts with an abysmal [literacy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Literacy) rate; one of the worst in the entire world. Luckily, it is better in the nearby states of [Borgu](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Borgu), [Dahomey](Dahomey.md), [Oyo](Oyo.md) and [Benin](Benin.md), so conquering them will improve literacy, once they have been integrated.
