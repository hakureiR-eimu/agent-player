Victoria 3, and communities surrounding it have their own **jargon**, including unique slang terms and acronyms. These sometimes stand in contrast to official [game terms](Vickypedia.md).

## Acronyms

The following are some common acronyms used based on game terms and discussions around the game:

**AAR**
  - After Action Report – A Player's recounting of their Victoria 3 game in the form of a Fan Fiction. For more detail, see the AAR section.
**ACW**
  - [American Civil War](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/American_journal_entries)
**AI**
  - Artificial Intelligence – The governance of the "computer players". The game AI controls all nations that are not being played by humans.
**AF**
  - [Armed Forces](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Armed_Forces) – One of the game's interest groups, generally representing military interests.
**AH**
  - Short for [Austria-Hungary](Austria.md). Alternatively, "alternate history".
**BYZ**
  - Short for [Byzantium](Byzantium.md)
**CB**
  - [Casus Belli](Diplomatic play.md) – Reason for war, also referred to as *War Goal*, a term from Paradox's history-based games.
**CE**
  - [Command Economy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Command_Economy)
**CP**
  - [Construction](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Construction) Points – The currency used to construct new buildings in game.
**CTD**
  - Crash To Desktop – Frequently used in patch notes, occurs when the game crashes and instantly shuts down, returning the player to the desktop.
**DD**
  - [Developer Diary](Developer diaries.md)
**DEI**
  - Short for [Dutch East Indies](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Dutch_East_Indies)
**DLC**
  - [Downloadable content](Downloadable content.md) – For-sale content made available by Paradox that adds more game mechanics, music, and other features to the game. Compare/Contrast with Mod.
**DOW**
  - Declaration of War – Can be used as a transitive verb ("I shall DOW you, Denmark!"). Alternatively, the cosmetic pack [Dawn of Wonder](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Dawn_of_Wonder).
**DP**
  - [Diplomatic play](Diplomatic play.md)
**EP**
  - Expansion Pack – Before digital downloads and DLC largely replaced physical discs, an "expansion pack" was the principal method of adding content to a PC video game after it came out. Expansion packs added enough content to be worth fitting onto a CD and selling at a store. Now, it refers to a DLC containing enough content to befit a classical Expansion Pack. It can also be used as a term in place of DLC.
**GB**
  - Short for [Great Britain](Great Britain.md)
**GDP**
  - Gross Domestic Product – A measurement for the total volume of the economy in a country, both in game and in real life. [See: Gross domestic product on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_domestic_product)
**GM**
  - Game Master – Leader or organizer of a multiplayer game.
**GP**
  - [Great power](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Great_power) – A very powerful country generally and specific rank in Victoria 3; often a player's goal is to raise a country into a great power
**IG**
  - [Interest Group](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Interest_Group) – A representation of shared interests and ideology of a group of pops and individuals.
**JE**
  - [Journal](Journal.md) entry – One of the types of scripted content in Victoria 3; similar to a quest from an RPG.
**LF**
  - [Laissez-Faire](Laissez-Faire.md) – From French for 'let do', an economic philosophy of reducing or removing government influence on business. In game, this is an economy law and an interest group ideology.
**LO**
  - [Landowners](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Landowners) – One of the game's interest groups, generally representing aristocratic interests.
**MAPI**
  - [Market Access Price Impact](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Market_Access_Price_Impact) – A modifier for the local price of goods compared to the national price.
**MP**
  - Multiplayer, alternatively [major power](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Major_power)
**NGF**
  - [North German (Con)federation](North German Federation.md) – A historical federation of German states, and a formable country in Victoria 3; often a stepping stone to form Germany.
**OE**
  - Short for the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md).
**OOS**
  - Out of Sync – Notorious desynchronization error that occurs in multiplayer, requires the game to be loaded again.
**OP**
  - Overpowered – In-game, this label can be applied to any item, build, strategy or tactic that is much stronger compared to all other equivalents with seemingly no balancing factor to counter its power (see also Meta). On the forums, it can also refer to the Original Poster of a thread.
**OPM/OSM**
  - One Province/State Minor – A country that controls only one province or one state. Originally from EU4 Term "One Province Minor".
**PB**
  - [Petite Bourgeoisie](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Petite_Bourgeoisie) – One of the game's interest groups, generally representing conservative middle class interests.
**PC**
  - Personal Computer – often includes laptops or Macs, typically in contrast to a gaming console (e.g. Xbox, Playstation)
**PDS**
  - Paradox Development Studio – The company that develops Victoria 3 and several other Paradox games.
**PDX**
  - [Paradox Interactive](Paradox.md) – The parent company of PDS; PDX publishes the games developed by PDS and other game studios. Sometimes also known as PI
**PLC**
  - Short for [Poland-Lithuania](Poland-Lithuania.md)
**PM**
  - [Production Method](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Production_Method) – A representation of what and how a building produces in game. Can alternatively also mean private/personal message
**PMG**
  - [Production Method Group](Production method.md) – A set of production methods that are mutually exclusive.
**PU**
  - Personal Union, a special type of [puppet](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Puppet)
**RF**
  - [Rural Folk](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Rural_Folk) – One of the game's interest groups, generally representing farming interests.
**RNG**
  - Random Number Generator – Mathematical sub-system that generates a random number. Used to determine chances for events, battle results, and other game features that use randomization of results to any degree.
**RP**
  - Roleplaying – where a player plays according to how a country would likely act rather than how a player would act. Often (but not always) runs in direct opposition to meta. Also can refer to Region Pack, a type of DLC that focuses on several countries in a geographic region.
**SGF**
  - [South German Federation](South German Federation.md) – An ahistorical counterpart to the North German Federation.
**SoL**
  - [Standard of Living](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Standard_of_Living) – A measurement of a pop's wealth and well-being; maximizing SoL is a common goal.
**S-P**
  - Short for [Sardinia-Piedmont](Sardinia-Piedmont.md)
**TS**
  - Short for [Two Sicilies](Two Sicilies.md)
**TU**
  - [Trade Unions](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Trade_Unions) – One of the game's interest groups, generally representing industrial labor interests.
**UC**
  - [Urban Center](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Urban_Center) – A building in game that represents the various small businesses that make up a city.
**UHP**
  - Short for [United Baltic Provinces](United Baltic Provinces.md)
**UI**
  - [User Interface](User interface.md) – The graphical and non-graphical presentation of information by the game; most often refers to the layout and design of in-game screens, as a shorthand for GUI or Graphical User Interface.
**UX**
  - User Experience – The player's interaction with the game's controls, interface, and mechanics.
**WAD/WAI**
  - Works/Working As Designed/Intended – Means that the thing in question is wrongly considered a bug and is actually intentional, used mostly with bug reports.
**WC**
  - World Conquest – Taking over every single state in the world, uniting the whole world under one single country; often used as the ultimate end goal for ambitious players.
**WP**
  - White Peace – a peace deal where no war goals is pressed on either side

All DLC's have a commonly used acronym.

**VotP**
  - [Voice of the People](Voice of the People.md), the 1st DLC for Victoria 3
**CotS**
  - [Colossus of the South](Colossus of the South.md), the 2nd DLC for Victoria 3
**SoI**
  - [Sphere of Influence](Sphere of Influence.md), the 3rd DLC for Victoria 3. Also a game concept in Victoria 2, analogous to [power blocs](Power bloc.md)
**PoE**
  - [Pivot of Empire](Pivot of Empire.md), the 4th DLC for Victoria 3
**CoC / ChoCo**
  - [Charters of Commerce](Charters of Commerce.md), the 5th DLC for Victoria 3
**NAw**
  - [National Awakening](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/National_Awakening_(DLC)), the 6th DLC for Victoria 3
**ITw**
  - [Iberian Twilight](Iberian Twilight.md), the 7th DLC for Victoria 3

## Slang

The following are some common slang words used based on game terms and discussions around the game:

**Art/Arty**
  - Slang for [artillery](Artillery.md)
**Badboy**
  - Slang for [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy). In Victoria 2 and 1, it was the term used in game files and [modding](Modding.md).
**Balkanizing**
  - Trying to release as many countries from a rival as possible to weaken them
**Beelining**
  - Going for one specific advanced tech immediately, prioritizing only its prerequisites and ignoring the added innovation cost.
**Blobbing**
  - Trying to conquer as much territory as possible, alternatively measuring the success of your game by the size/population of your country
**A blob**
  - a country that has grown to an unusual size and power. Often combined with the colour of the country, e.g. Blue blob for an unusually large France
**Bordergore**
  - Aesthetically displeasing borders, usually because multiple countries have exclaves in places you don't expect them to
**Cav**
  - Slang for [cavalry](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Cavalry)
**Checksum**
  - A string of four letters that are used to verify whether two or more parties have exactly the same copy of the game. Checksums can change by one letter by playing and resigning, and differ completely if the two compared copies are not the same. Can be found on the lower left of the main menu to the right of the version number you are using.
**Cheese**
  - Using strategies that are technically possible in the game, but seen as cheap or not as the game is intended to be played
**Clay**
  - Provinces or Rightful land. From the Polandball comics. Often used in memes
**Construction Loop**
  - The phenomenon that in the early and mid game, almost all goods used for construction (iron, wood, tools directly, and steel and coal by proxy) also are the only goods used in production of each other, and they will thus dominate early- to mid-game construction. See [Beginner's_guide#The_construction_loop](Beginner's guide.md).
**Core**
  - States which are defined as part of a country when [released](Releasable countries.md) or [formed](Formable countries.md).
**(to) Curbstomp / roflstomp**
  - Used as either a verb, a noun or an adjective. Describes a war or a battle in which the odds are overwhelmingly unbalanced, often leading to the annihilation of the losing side.
**Dev**
  - Shorthand for developer, one of Paradox' employees working on the game, or a community member working on a mod.
  - Can also mean development in a province, a term borrowed from EU4.
**Gamey**
  - Used as an adjective to signify an action or tactic that, while not a cheat, significantly destroys game balance and gives tremendous bonuses to the player, especially when it involves actions that wouldn't make sense in historical context.
**Gross Germany / Großdeutschland**
  - (from German: Greater Germany) Germany which has included Austria, or alternatively included all of the starting provinces of Austria, possibly by being formed by Austria. See also Super-Germany. Is in opposition to Klein Germany / Kleindeutschland (Lesser Germany).
**Hugbox / hug fest**
  - Large or unbreaking multiplayer alliances that tend to stagnate games. Can also be used as a verb "Hugboxing"
**John Paradox and Victoria Clausewitz**
  - Fan given names to the two people on the loading screen
**Let's Play**
  - An alternative to the AAR, a video narrative of a game in progress with the action ongoing rather than already complete.
**Meta**
  - Shortened from "meta-game"; using or seeking the most optimal way to run your country, economy, etc. Often used as the opposite to roleplaying.
**Min/Maxing**
  - trying to optimize every aspect of a playthrough.
**Minor**
  - A small country which has a larger country with the same [primary culture](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Primary_culture). For example [Texas](Texas.md) is an American minor to [USA](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/USA). Alternatively a small country which can technically form a nation, but under normal circumstances will never be able to do so, and is just waiting to be annexed. For example [Hamburg](Hamburg.md).
**Mod**
  - A software package that modifies the game, written by the player community. Compare/contrast with DLC. On the forums or Discord, this can also refer to a moderator.
**One-tag World Conquest**
  - A world Conquest where not even [puppets](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Puppets) count towards, if world conquest is achieved or not.
**Otto(s)**
  - Slang for the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md)
**Ragequit**
  - Used as either a verb or a noun; the verb describes the rageful quitting of a game, most often without saving, due to unforeseen circumstances which have ruined the game, the noun describes one iteration of the verb.
**Savescumming**
  - Reloading earlier saves when something goes wrong in order to set things right. Often considered a negative trait; impossible to do if Ironman is activated.
**Slingshot method**
  - A strategy regarding technology, where you avoid researching a desirable technology that is currently spreading, to maximize the amount of weeks with a desirable tech spread. See [Technology#Slingshot_method](Technology.md)
**Super-Germany**
  - Alternatively Mega-Germany or Ultra-Germany. A [Germany](Germany.md) that includes all of [Austria](Austria.md)'s starting states, possibly by being formed by Austria.
**Tall**
  - Playing without looking to conquer or colonize more states, instead trying to make a few states as good as possible. Opposite of playing wide.
**Vanilla**
  - The game played unmodded. Alternatively without expansions, sometimes without even updates.
**Vassal feeding**
  - Alternatively just called 'feeding'. Conquering states and giving them to your vassal or setting your vassal up to conquer provinces.
**Vassal swarm**
  - A large number of subjects of one country that must be fought through in addition to their overlord. Allows waging wars without doing anything by oneself.
**Vicky**
  - Slang for the Victoria game series, that is Victoria 3, [Victoria 2](https://vic2.paradoxwikis.com/Victoria_2_Wiki), or [Victoria: An Empire Under the Sun](https://vic1.paradoxwikis.com/Main_Page). Sometimes used specifically for the first game.
**Wardec**
  - The act of declaring war, or being declared war upon.
**Warmonger**
  - Describes a player (and certain AI-controlled countries) who constantly declare wars whenever possible and feasible, sometimes even several at once, for the sole purpose of conquering even more states. These countries are rarely ever at peace.
**Wide**
  - Looking to conquer or colonize more and more states, instead of making each state as good as possible. Opposite of playing tall.

### Modding

These are terms that are often used in [modding](Modding.md) or to describe a mod.

**Sub-mod**
  - A mod which depends on and modifies a different mod.
**Tag**
  - Another term for a country, based on the three-letter identifiers used in the game's scripting.
**Total Conversion**
  - A mod that changes the setting of the game and often changes or introduces mechanics. Similar to a "total overhaul", which keeps the setting but changes most mechanics.

## AAR

**After Action Reports** (**AARs**) are threads posted to the [Victoria 3 AAR-forums](https://forum.paradoxplaza.com/forum/forums/victoria-3-after-action-reports-aars.1126/) or a personal web site or blog written by players about their ongoing games. They can be written from the game player's personal point of view (gameplay AAR), or can be written from a fictitious narrative perspective (narrative AAR), like a serialized novel. Some writers also blend the two elements, going from a metagame discussion to a fictitious narrative set in their game's alternate history world.

Players can include screenshots from their games, maps, period art, and other elements to add depth to their reports.
