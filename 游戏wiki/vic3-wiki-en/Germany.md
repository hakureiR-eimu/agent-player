*The Congress of Vienna established the German Confederation, a loose assortment of German states intended to keep the peace in Central Europe. The Confederation in practice is dominated by the rivalry between Austria and Prussia. Can the smaller nations survive the coming conflict?*

**Germany** is a [formable nation](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_nation) in central Europe. It can be formed by any country with North German or South German as a primary culture.

Germany can form [Central Europe](Central Europe.md).[1]

## Historical background

Through the Middle Ages and early modern era, Germany was semi-united under the Holy Roman Empire. While nominally all subjects of the Emperor, always a Habsburg since the mid-1400s, most German states enjoyed a great deal of autonomy. By the end of the 1700s, Austria and Prussia had emerged as the two predominant German states, with no others coming even close.

During the Napoleonic Wars, Napoleon abolished Holy Roman Empire and controlled much of Germany, either directly administered or though the Confederation of the Rhine; only Austria and Prussia remained independent. After Napoleon was defeated, the Congress of Vienna redrew the borders of Germany, consolidating many of the smaller states into fewer, larger ones. These formed the German Confederation, which included the German portions of both Austria and Prussia as well as the smaller German states.

This Confederation lasted until 1866 when it was replaced by the North German Confederation following the Austro-Prussian war.

Full unification of Germany was considered first during the 1848 revolutions; however, the Prussian king refused the crown "from the gutter", and full unification was only achieved later in 1871.

23 of the 31 core states are needed in order to form Germany
Germany can be formed by any country with North German or South German as a primary culture that controls at least 23 of the North and South German Homelands after either researching Pan-nationalism or completing the journal entry *German National Identity*. However, as Germany is a [major unification](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Major_unification), it is possible to form Germany without holding these states directly. Typically, [Prussia](Prussia.md) and [Austria](Austria.md) are the leading candidates to form Germany.

Once a Great Power with North or South German as its primary culture has researched Pan-nationalism or completed the journal entry *German National Identity*, it can become a unification candidate, if there are no such great powers, then Major Powers are eligible instead. Other countries can choose to support any of the unification candidates, and while there is more than one candidate, each candidate can use the *German Leadership* diplomatic play to force other candidates to withdraw their candidacy. Once there is only one unification candidate, it can form Germany if it has sufficient support to control 23 of the 31 German core states. Alternatively, the candidate can use the *Unify Germany* wargoal, which immediately annexes all of its supporters and targets all German core states not held by allies or subjects[2] for conquest.

If Germany is formed by a Catholic country, its state religion remains Catholic instead of becoming the default Protestant.

Germany **cannot** be formed if [Confederation of the Rhine](Confederation of the Rhine.md) exists; even if the Confederation is trying to unify Germany itself or is a subject of the country unifying Germany.

[Austria](Austria.md) cannot form Germany if it has the Dual or Triple Monarchy government type; Austria also cannot be targeted for a unification play if it has either of those government types. [Danubian State](Danubian State.md) also cannot form Germany.

When Germany is formed while holding all Austrian lands, the new Greater Germany will get an [event](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Event), where they have to choose between releasing all non-German countries, keeping them and receiving **70** [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy), or releasing them as puppets and getting **40** infamy. Therefore it is important to time the formation, so that Prussia has no more than 29.9 infamy, as well as at least decent relations with most other [great powers](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Great_power). Within a few years of stabilization, Super-Germany should be powerful enough to take on the entire world, but a *cut down to size* war immediately will be a problem. As long as Germany does not have more than 30 infamy, they should be fine though.
