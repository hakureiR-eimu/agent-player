The **Corn Laws** is a journal entry and a chain of events that involve the political consequences of high grain prices and import tariffs. Historically, the [Corn Laws](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/en:Corn_Laws)[wp 1] were a series of British tariffs and trade restrictions on imported food and corn between 1815 and 1846 that aimed to protect the interests of domestic grain producers. In the game, any country that has enacted Protectionism or Mercantilism and has a powerful Landowners interest group can trigger the Corn Laws journal entry.

## Strategies and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Corn Laws
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
Until 1.9, Corn Laws were especially powerful if the Voice of the People DLC was enabled, as the agitator created was affiliated with the Landowners and could be granted leadership of the interest group. Since 1.9, they're now affiliated with the Petite Bourgeoisie.

Even so, if you have powerful Landowners and quite influential Petite Bourgeoisie while still having a backwards set of laws and either Protectionism or Mercantilism, deliberately triggering the Corn Laws to receive a Market Liberal leader can be a strategy to help liberalize your laws and industrialize more efficiently. This may be helpful for countries such as [Egypt](Egypt.md), or the [Two Sicilies](Two Sicilies.md). This will involve increasing grain prices so that they are above **+25%** of base price, boosting the movement that the Market Liberal agitator joins, and then granting them leadership.

The first step is to increase grain prices to +25% of base price. There are various ways to increase the prices of grain, including:

- Subventions on grain
- Switching away from the Maintain Single Crop production method in Wheat Farms and other farms
- Turning on the Harvesting Tools production method in farms and then turning it off to rapidly fire a number of workers
- Starting a war and conscripting a heavy amount of units (especially cavalry units)

Grain prices will only need to be +25% for one tick to trigger the Corn Laws journal entry (as long as all the other requirements are satisfied), and it is recommended to undo any steps you may have taken after triggering the entry to not reduce Standard of Living too much.

The event *The Anti-Corn Laws* will eventually fire after a few months. One option in the event will give you a new Character, who is a petite bourgeoisie agitator with the Market Liberal ideology. They will generally join or create a Liberal, Reformer, or Modernizer Movement. The next step is to ensure that the movement they join pressures the petite bourgeoisie interest group. Some steps that can increase the amount of pressure include:

- [Bolstering](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Bolster) the movement
- Suppressing any other movements that pressure the petite bourgeoisie
- Selecting options in events that increase the movement's attraction
- Angering the movement to raise their radicalism, raising their attraction

When the movement pressures the petite bourgeoisie, the agitator can be *granted leadership* (only available with the DLC). The petite bourgeoisie will now be led by a leader that strongly dislikes laws such as Serfdom, Traditionalism, and Protectionism. It will now be easier to move away from these laws and to other, more liberal laws like Laissez-Faire and Free Trade.

It is also possible to get a Market Liberal Landowner leader randomly if the Liberal movement pressures the Landowners. The same actions as above can be done to aid in this.
