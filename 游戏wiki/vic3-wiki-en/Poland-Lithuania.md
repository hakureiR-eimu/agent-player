**Poland-Lithuania** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in the Eastern Europe strategic region. Seven of its core states start the game controlled by [Russia](Russia.md), two by [Prussia](Prussia.md), and two by [Austria](Austria.md).

## Formation

All of the 11 core states are needed in order to form Poland-Lithuania
The following countries can form Poland-Lithuania when they control all 11 core states.

Krakow starts as a puppet of Austria; Galicia-Lodomeria or Lithuania can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country which controls its capital state; Poland or United Baltic Provinces can be released by any country which controls its capital state and two other core states; Poland and United Baltic Provinces are also formable themselves.

A Poland-Lithuania formed by Galicia-Lodomeria or United Baltic Provinces retains their state religion, rather than becoming Catholic.

Note that since United Baltic Provinces starts as the Baltic Governorates, with North German and Russian primary culture, it is not eligible to form Poland-Lithuania unless it itself is reformed or re-released to its default cultures which include Lithuanian.

### Core states

Poland-Lithuania has eleven core states, seven are Polish homelands, with the other four comprising the old core of the Grand Duchy of Lithuania. All states are part of the Eastern Europe strategic region, as well as the claimed state of Warmia (East Prussia), while the claimed state of Górny Śląsk (Upper Silesia) is in the Central Europe region.

An independent Poland-Lithuania is likely to be at least a major power, with around 15M starting population in its core states and ready access to industrial resources, more so if able to control the [Polish](Poland.md) core state of Silesia. While coal is abundant, the other mineable resources are limited in supply, particularly without control of Upper Silesia.

## Strategy and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Poland-Lithuania
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
Poland-Lithuania requires states from Russia, Prussia, and Austria, weakening them somewhat. However, each of these countries remain powerful without those states, and while Poland-Lithuania is powerful, careful diplomacy is still required to avoid another partition.
