**Philippolis** is a [captaincy](Government type.md) and unrecognized power in the Southern Africa strategic region. Its capital is the city of Philippolis in the state of Transorangia, next to its twin state [Griqualand](Griqualand.md).

## Scripted content

Main article:
Southern Africa content
Philippolis shares a journal entry with nearby countries related to the struggle for dominance over the *South Africa* [geographic region](Geographic region.md). Specifically, Philippolis must ensure diplomatic recognition and coastal access to gain dominance over the other minor states in the region. Through this journal entry, Philippolis may seek confederation with other Griqua states, such as [Griqualand](Griqualand.md).

### Interest Groups

Griqualand starts with the Landowners and the Rural Folk in government, while the Armed Forces and the London Missionary Society are in opposition. The Trade Unions, Petite Bourgeoisie, Intelligentsia and Industrialists start marginalized.

The London Missionary Society have the Laissez-Faire ideology in addition to their starting ideologies. Additionally, they will be lead by characters of English and Scottish culture.

### Diplomacy

Philippolis' sole interest in its home Southern Africa region. It neighbors its twin Griqua state [Griqualand](Griqualand.md), the Boer republic of [Winburg](Oranje.md), the native African state of [Basutoland](Basutoland.md), and the much larger ![Flag GBR uk.png](/images/thumb/6/6e/Flag_GBR_uk.png/24px-Flag_GBR_uk.png) [Cape Colony](South Africa.md).

Philippolis starts with a treaty with [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) that guarantees the independence of the former and also gives them transit rights to allow for trade with the World Market, as Philippolis starts off landlocked. It also starts with a treaty with [Winburg](Oranje.md) that gives the latter transit rights for the same reason. Both treaties last for 10 years.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Philippolis has no starting industry and a single agricultural building, a Livestock Ranch.

**Resources**

Transorangia has access to a decent amount of iron and coal, but very little wood. Furthermore, without Prospecting, Philippolis won't be able to make use of the state's Gold Fields.
