*“Argentina has won its independence from Spain, but now faces internal divisions. Can Argentina form a stable government?”*

**Argentina** is a minor power located in lower South America, in the resource-rich *La Plata* strategic region. It borders [Bolivia](Bolivia.md), [Guarani](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Guarani_(GNI)), [Paraguay](Paraguay.md), and [Brazil](Brazil.md) to the north, [Piratini](Piratini.md) and [Uruguay](Uruguay.md) to the east, [Mapuche](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Mapuche) to the south, and [Chile](Chile.md) to the west.

Argentina begins the game with a relatively small geographic territory of five incorporated and three unincorporated states, and is one of a few countries with incorporated states that are also colonies, namely Buenos Aires and Santa Fe. It shares claims with Chile over Araucanía and Patagonia, and is the only colonizing power with access to Alto Paraguay.

Argentina can create the formable country [Rio de la Plata](Rio de la Plata.md), which can later form the [Federation of the Andes](Federation of the Andes.md).

## Historical background

[Argentina declared independence on July 9th, 1816](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/en:Argentine_Declaration_of_Independence)[wp 1] during the [Spanish American Wars of Independence](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/en:Spanish_American_Wars_of_Independence)[wp 1], a series of conflicts in the early 1800s in which South American colonies revolted against [Spain](Spain.md). In its early years, the Argentine Republic suffered internal instability and recurrent civil wars between the supporters of a centralized, unitary government and the supporters of a federal system, culminating in a state of near anarchy during which its provinces acted mostly independently. By 1831, the federalists, led by Juan Manuel de Rosas, decisively won the national election and reorganized the country as a confederation. By 1835, de Rosas had secured enough support from the legislature to grant him near-autocratic authority over the country. A disastrous war against [Brazil](Brazil.md) in 1851-1852 resulted in de Rosas being deposed; he then fled the the country to seek asylum in [Great Britain](Great Britain.md). Following the exile of de Rosas, Argentina reformed into a constitutional republic.

During the Victorian era, Argentina rose from being just one of many Spanish colonies to becoming one of the first modern constitutional republics. Its thriving agricultural industry led Argentina to grow to become one of the wealthiest countries in GDP per capita of the 20th century. Argentina was one of the first countries to support movements to abolish slavery, following major powers such as [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) and [France](France.md). Perceived as an up-and-coming major power themselves, Argentina was truly a dominant economic force in its prime.

## Formation

If Argentina ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country that controls its capital state of Buenos Aires. Its core states are the Platinean homelands; these comprise Argentina's starting states, plus the states of Uruguay, Alto Paraguay, and Bajo Paraguay. Additionally, one of either Araucanía or Patagonia can gain a Platinean homeland through an event. If the Platinean homelands are replaced by national homelands, the number of core states is reduced. If no Platinean homelands remain, Argentina cannot be released.

### Interest groups

Argentina begins with the Landowners and Armed Forces in government. The Petite Bourgeoisie, Intelligentsia, Catholic Church, and Rural Folk are in opposition, while the Industrialists and Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Armed Forces have the Caudillismo ideology in addition to their standard ideologies. The Landowners have the Elitist ideology instead of the default Paternalistic. The Landowners also have the Pro-Slavery ideology due to the starting Legacy Slavery.

### Diplomacy

Argentina has claims on the states of Buenos Aires and Mendoza, and shares claims with [Chile](Chile.md) on the states of Patagonia and Araucanía. These claims prevent other countries from colonizing these states. Argentina starts with colonies in [Guarani](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Guarani_(GNI)) (Chaco and Santa Fe) and [Mapuche](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Mapuche) (Buenos Aires and Mendoza). They also have claims on [Bolivia](Bolivia.md)n owned Jujuy.

Argentina has poor relations with [Brazil](Brazil.md) (with whom it has a mutual Rivalry), [Bolivia](Bolivia.md) and [Spain](Spain.md). Additionally, the latter doesn't recognize Argentina's independence, and until it does so, Argentina will suffer the modifier *Independence Not Recognized by Spain Independence Not Recognized by Spain Country modifier −10% Influence*.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Argentina has a very small industrial base at the start of the game, producing tools, clothes and furniture.

**Resources**

Argentina has substantial access to iron and sulfur, modest access to coal and lead, and begins with two gold mines and the potential to build two more. Conquering the neighboring countries of [Chile](Chile.md) and [Bolivia](Bolivia.md) secures even greater access to resources including an absurd amount of gold, and it is crucial to take out these nations early before they become too powerful themselves.

With access to several state modifiers, Argentina has the potential to become a top producer of agricultural goods. The *Pampas* trait gives **+15%** agricultural and plantation throughput to several states, and the *Paraná River* and *Paraguay River* traits grant **+20** Infrastructure each. One state in the region, Corrientes, has all three modifiers and is already an incorporated Argentine state.

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.9. |

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Argentina
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
Argentina begins the game surrounded by several neighbors that can be conquered rather easily when pursued in the right order. This guide serves as a general roadmap for the player to follow should they wish to conquer most of South America under the might of Argentina. Before beginning your adventure across South America, however, there are two elephants in the room that must be addressed: Brazil and Great Britain.

- [Brazil](Brazil.md) is the powerhouse of the continent, and can quickly become a major threat to the player's ambitions. Strive towards improving relations with them. An alliance with Brazil secures a serious advantage for the player, as they have a healthy economy and a military that will rapidly grow in size. Because Brazil shares a land border with Argentina, trade routes between the two countries cost no convoys.

- [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) has an active interest in the *La Plata* strategic region thanks to its holdings in the South Atlantic Islands state, primarily comprising of the modern-day Malvinas (or Falklands) Islands. As the number one global power, befriending Great Britain will ensure that it does not decide to act against you during your campaigns.

### Conquer your Neighbors

Now to the fun part: Conquering your neighbors.

1. At the start of a new game, the neighboring [Riograndense Republic](Piratini.md) is at war with [Brazil](Brazil.md), who is pre-occupied with a larger conflict to its north against [Grão-Pará](Grão-Pará.md). Without outside influence, Brazil will easily win these conflicts and annex both nations by the end of 1836. This presents a very small window of time for Argentina to annex Riograndense Republic instead. Conquering the one-state nation voids Brazil's war with them and does not appear to result in any negative repercussions for the player. Note that Brazil has a claim on the state of Rio Grande do Sul. The player will be able to use that claim to sway Brazil to their side later in a war against [Bolivia](Bolivia.md).
2. Our second target is [Paraguay](Paraguay.md), which is a priority target because one of its two states exists within Brazil strategic region. This typically causes Brazil to eventually either conquer or befriend Paraguay themselves, usually guaranteeing its independence or forming a defensive alliance. Paraguay is defenseless on their own, and it is a relatively quick war for the player to conquer them before Brazil finishes their own war with Grão-Pará. With Paraguay annexed, you now hold part of the state of Mato Grasso, which is the second state that can be used to sway Brazil to your side in future conflicts.
3. [Uruguay](Uruguay.md) is the next most enticing target. Like the Riograndense Republic, they are a weak and defenseless one-state target for the player to conquer. Should Uruguay be left alone, Brazil is likely to annex it themselves within a few years. By annexing the state of Uruguay, which Brazil does not have a claim over, the player gains an active interest in the Brazil strategic region which is a crucial step in developing a positive relationship and future alliance with the powerful neighbor to the north.
4. By this point, the player should own two states that [Brazil](Brazil.md) has a claim over, Rio Grande do Sul and a small portion of Mato Grasso. The player should now begin a play against [Bolivia](Bolivia.md) using the Transfer Subject war goal for one of their three puppet states. During the diplomatic play, sway Brazil to your side in exchange for either Rio Grande do Sul or Mato Grasso, and sway [Chile](Chile.md) to your side in exchange for the Return State war goal for their claim on Antofagasta. Chile is the player's next target for annexation after this war, so Antofagasta will soon be under the player's control. With both Brazil and Chile on the player's side, add the war goals to transfer Bolivia's two remaining puppets to the players control. The player should have just enough diplomatic maneuvers to accomplish these tasks. This leaves Bolivia as an independent country without puppets and removes its ability to form the [Peru-Bolivia](Peru-Bolivia.md)n Confederation. Furthermore, Bolivia no longer has sea access now that they no longer control Antofagasta, forcing the country to become dependent on the Argentine economy.
5. The final target for this [Chile](Chile.md). Although more formidable than any other nation in the La Plata Strategic region, the player should now be able to conquer Chile with relative ease and can still use a transfer state sway to bring Brazil to make the fight even less of a hassle. Chile's states have vast iron resources which can greatly benefit the Argentine economy in its early years.

Argentina now owns every state that it can in the La Plata strategic region, has puppeted three states in The Andes strategic region, crippled Bolivia from becoming a future threat, and made a friend of Brazil by returning two of their claimed states to their control. [Ecuador](Ecuador.md) is the next easy target for future expansion, leaving only [Venezuela](Venezuela.md) and [New Granada](Colombia.md) to the far north of the continent.

### Alternate strategy

If forming a large [Federation of the Andes](Federation of the Andes.md) is a target, consider allowing [Peru-Bolivia](Peru-Bolivia.md) to form and instead declare war on Brazil on day 1, with liberating countries as wargoals; [Minas Gerais](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Minas_Gerais), [Confederation of the Equator](Confederation of the Equator.md), and [Paulistânia](Paulistânia.md) are all good options. This also adds to the possibility of [Grão-Pará](Grão-Pará.md) and [Piratini](Piratini.md) succeeding in their rebellions. This strategy is to preserve states with Andean culture for future annexation by the federation. Hence, Argentina's goal is to weaken Brazil and then annex its former territories.
