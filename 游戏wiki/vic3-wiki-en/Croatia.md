The Illyrian movement is sweeping the country, with academics calling for resistance to Magyarization and linguistic reforms. Will Croatia find a place of its own within the Empire, or will it be drowned beneath the Hungarian yoke?"*

**Croatia** is a minor power in Eastern Europe, starting as the crown land of **Croatia-Slavonia** under [Austria](Austria.md). It can form [Yugoslavia](Yugoslavia.md) or the [Danubian State](Danubian State.md).

## Politics

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.10. |

Croatia starts as a Crown Land, and thus adopts a dictatorial transfer of power despite its Monarchy law. Ban Franjo Vlašić is supporting the Armed Forces, but the power is likely to move the the leader of the Landowners or the Catholic Church once he dies.

The Croatian culture is experiencing a Cultural Awakening centered in the state of Croatia, which will go on for another 9 years.

### Interest groups

Croatia starts with Landowners and Armed Forces in government. The Catholic Church, Intelligentsia, Rural Folk, and Petite Bourgeoisie are in opposition. The Industrialists and Trade Unions are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Croatia starts as a crown land of [Austria](Austria.md) and is thus in the *Metternich System* power bloc.

### Industry and Resources

**Industry**

Croatia starts with a Paper Mill, some agricultural buildings, two Fishing Wharves and three Logging Camps.

**Resources**

By itself, Croatia only has access to coal.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.10. |
|---|---|

Croatia has the Professional Army law.

Croatia starts with 24 Battalions, and can raise 4 Conscripts. All barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions form a single army.

Croatia does not start with a fleet.
