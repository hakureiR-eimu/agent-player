A **political lobby** or *lobby* is a collection of one or more [interest groups](Interest group.md) within one country that oppose or endorse another specific country. Each political lobby has an appeasement value between **−10** and **+10**, which always decays towards **0**.

## Lobby types

There are four types of lobbies: two which endorse a target country and two which oppose it. Alternatively, two types of lobbies target a country's overlord, and two target

|  | Non-overlord target | Overlord target |
|---|---|---|
| Pro- | Pro-Country Lobby Seeks closer diplomatic ties with its target | Loyalist Lobby Seek closer ties with the country's overlord |
| Anti- | Anti-Country Lobby Seeks to antagonize its target | Independence Lobby Seeks more autonomy or even independence from the country's overlord |

Each lobby has a semi-random name selected from a list depending on its type. Such as *British-French Society* or *Anti-Prussian League*.

Overlord and non-overlord lobbies convert to the other type if the target country becomes or stops being the overlord of the lobby's country.

## Lobby formation

Lobbies have a chance to form when certain diplomatic catalysts occur. Not every catalyst has a chance for a lobby to form and most catalysts can only allow certain types of lobbies to form.

For non-overlord lobbies, the target country must be of the same rank or higher than the lobby's country. Lobbies can only target countries that are neighbors or have [interests](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Interests) in the same strategic regions. Lobbies disband if these requirements are not met. Additionally, a lobby disbands if it has no non-marginalized interest group members.

Newly formed lobbies can have one to three interest groups, most often having two.

### Interest group membership

Interest groups join or leave lobbies based on scripted weights. When a new lobby is formed, interest groups with at least 50 join weight may join the new lobby. If an interest group has 100 or more join weight, it is guaranteed to join the lobby.

Interest groups can only be in one lobby of each type and only lobby for a given target country. If an interest group is already part of a lobby of the same type, it must have 25% more join weight towards the new lobby to join it instead.

If an interest group in a lobby has a join weight of 0 or less, it may leave the lobby.

Interest groups may also join or leave lobbies by event.

### Lobby clout effects

Lobby clout affects a number of mechanics, primarily related to diplomacy. The each percentage of clout in a lobby has the following effects, with direction depending on the lobbies views; if multiple lobbies are concerned with the same country, their clout cancels out.

- **±1%** Influence cost of diplomatic actions and treaty articles, capped at **±50%**
- **±1%** Influence gain from rivalries, capped at **±50%**
- **±0.01** War exhaustion gain
- **±5** Leverage

For example, if [Spain](Spain.md) has a pro-country lobby towards [France](France.md) with 30% clout, then friendly actions and articles with France cost **−30%** less and, Spain would gain **+0.3** more weekly war exhaustion in a war against France.

## Appeasement

Appeasement represents how happy the lobby is, from **−10** and **+10**. Appeasement always decays towards **0** over time, changing by **±1** every 2.8 years1025 days. Appeasement directly affects the approval of all member interest groups by an equal amount.

Depending on the type of lobby, different diplomatic actions increase or decrease that lobby's appeasement. Generally, actions that increase the appeasement for a pro-lobby decrease appeasement for an anti-lobby and vice-versa.

## Lobby interactions

|  | Available only with the [Sphere of Influence](Sphere of Influence.md) DLC enabled. |
|---|---|

Occasionally, a lobby can present a demand or opportunity to the country in the form of a journal entry.

### Fund lobbies

Fund Lobbies is an ongoing diplomatic action. While a country is funding lobbies, and the recipient has no pro-lobby towards the funder, there is a 0.25% to 1% chance each week for a new pro-country lobby to form, or 2% for a pro-overlord lobby to form. This also increases the weight for interest groups to join lobbies that benefit the funder and less likely to join lobbies that are detrimental for the funder.

### Lobby demands

Lobby demands are always various diplomatic actions related to the lobby's target country – sometimes the target country itself, and sometimes a rival or opponent of the target country. Completing the demand increases appeasement by **+3**, while allowing it to timeout reduces it by **−5**.

### Lobby opportunities

Lobby opportunities make a given diplomatic action related to the lobby's target country easier to accomplish than normal. There is no direct effect on appeasement for completing the opportunity or letting it timeout.
