**Ladakh** is an unrecognized power in northwestern India. As a neighbor of both the [Jammu](Kashmir.md), [Chitral](Chitral.md), [Tibet](Tibet.md), [Punjab](Punjab.md) and [China](China.md).

It starts the game at war with [Jammu](Kashmir.md). The Kashmiri armies are much stronger, and since defeat would result in the annexation of Ladakh, they are truly one of the most difficult countries in the entire game.

### Interest groups

Due to Ladakh's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Ladakh starts with the Landowners in government. The Rural Folk, Vajrayana Monks, Intelligentsia, and Armed Forces are in opposition, while the Trade Unions, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Industrialists are marginalized.

The Vajrayana Monks have the Buddhist Moralist ideology instead of the default Moralist.

### Diplomacy

Ladakh starts is at war with it's neighbour [Kashmir](Kashmir.md) with the wargoal of returning the state of Kashmir to Kashmir, resulting in the complete annexation of Ladakh. Ladakh's war goal is to renounce the claims. Kashmir's overlord Punjab is not involved in the war. Its only active interest is in the Himilaya region itself.

To make matters worse [British East India](East India.md) also has claims on Kashmir.
