*Almost all of India is now under the direct or indirect control of the British East India Company. The Nawabs of the princely states still exert a strong degree of rule over their territories, but ultimately answer to the Company. Will India ever be free from British rule?*

**India** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in Central Asia. It can be formed by any country with an "Indian" primary cultureAssamese, Avadhi, Baluchi, Bengali, Bihari, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Oria, Punjabi, Pathan, Rajput, Sindi, Tamil, Telegu, Hindustani, Bundeli, Pahari, Gondi, Bageli, Chhattisgarhi, Deccani.

It is one of the few Hegemony-tier countries, the others being [Central Europe](Central Europe.md) and [Federation of the Andes](Federation of the Andes.md).

## Historical background

Prior to the modern era, the Indian subcontinent had been nearly unified several times, but more often was divided into a number of lesser empires and kingdoms. The most recent greater empires were the Muslim [Mughal](Hindustan.md) and Hindu [Maratha](Maharashtra.md) empires, both of which ended under [British](Great Britain.md) rule as the [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company) expanded its control of India in the early 1800s. In 1858, the East India Company was dissolved and direct British rule was established. Following the Second World War, India gained independence from Britain; however, majority Muslim regions – namely Bengal and north-west India – were partitioned as [Pakistan](Pakistan.md). Bengal would later go independent as Bangladesh in 1971.

### Formation by East India

See also:
East India#Strategies and guides
India is most easily formed by [East India](East India.md), who must annex all Indian subjects under its charge and seek independence from [Great Britain](Great Britain.md). If it has researched Pan-nationalism, gaining independence allows it to become India directly. Otherwise, East India must choose the "divide India" option. This changes East India into [Bengal](Bengal.md), while dividing the rest of East India's territory among its Indian subjects. But, if there are no Indian subjects to divide the land with, the newly formed Bengal retains all territory.

Note that while mega-Bengal theoretically holds enough states to form India, there are some states that are split with other powers:

- Punjab, which is split with the country of [Punjab](Punjab.md) (Punjab also holds two other states which count towards India's formation);
- Bombay and Gujarat, with [Portugal](Portugal.md)'s treaty port Nova Goa;
- Carnatic, with [France](France.md)'s treaty port Pondicherry, and Danish Carnatic

The state of Baluchistan is also split among four forces: [Kalat](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kalat), [Makran](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Makran), [Kandahar](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kandahar) and [Oman](Oman.md)'s treaty port of Gwadar. East India/Bengal should consider annexing them early.

As annexing subjects is dependent on their low Liberty Desire (for low infamy), expect a slower pace of puppet annexation and greater use of the ["doctrine of lapse" interaction](Colonial Indies content.md). Also, expect more revolts from puppets as they join each other to resist annexation. If East India remains a Chartered Company, it cannot agitate for independence until it has researched Civilizing Mission. On the other hand, as the *British Raj*, East India is no longer a *Chartered Company* and so does not require *Civilizing Mission*.

### Expansion Plans

With Racial Segregation, India has good acceptance for all cultures with South Asian and Central Asian heritages. With Cultural Exclusion, it can have good acceptance for Persian and Kurdish. Thus, should there be a need to expand, consider Central Asia, [Persia](Persia.md) and the eastern parts of the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md). As such, it is very likely for India to play the "Great Game" with [Russia](Russia.md).

To the northeast, [Nepal](Nepal.md) has pops with cultural affinity and Lead.

For Pashtun and Baluchi homelands, India can consider releasing [Afghanistan](Afghanistan.md) and [Baluchistan](Baluchistan.md) as puppets. Note that Baluchistan may ask to join the union when *The Union of India* journal entry is active. Also, it takes 20+ years to incorporate Baluchi homelands (if India is formed from EIC independence).

Any potential Indian minors can be annexed either aggressively or through a [journal entry](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Journal_entry): *The Union of India*. It allows the annexation of princely states that have **50** or higher relations and less than 25 liberty desire. This will happen automatically to any states that fulfil the requirements, and the journal entry has a button which invites all eligible states; the button can be used once a year. However, annexing them this way creates permanent national expenses equal to your GDP times 0.01 times number of princes annexed using the journal entry, so it's better to annex them using regular means. For puppets, consider increasing autonomy so that they become protectorates, then annex them using the "Unify India" wargoal after the truce period. While *The Union of India* is active, a princely state might ask to join the union; consider granting independence as no truce will be reinforced, allowing the immediate usage of the "Unify India" wargoal. Note that princely states which become independent due to this event become recognized; as such, the infamy generated with the *Unify India* wargoal would most likely be higher.
