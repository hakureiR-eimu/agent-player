Some countries which have ceased to exist entirely, whether before or during the game, can be *released* and returned to existence. Countries can be released either from owned states through the Diplomacy screen or from other countries' states through a *Liberate Country* diplomatic play. The conditions to release a country are often similar to [country formation](Formable countries.md), and some countries can be either formed or released when they do not exist.

## Diplomatic release

To release a country diplomatically, the requirements are:

- The released country does not currently exist
- Controls the released country's capital state, even indirectly through a subject
- The releasing country's capital is not the same state as the released country's capital
- Controls part or all of a sufficient number of the released country's *core* states
- Some countries have additional requirements to be released

*Note:* Being at peace is **not** required to release a country.

As soon as the requirements are fulfilled, a button to release the nation can be pressed in the "Diplomacy" tab. The country can be released as independent or a [subject](Subject.md) of the releasing country, and the player can also choose to switch to playing as the released country in either case. The releasing country can also choose to withhold their own homeland states or not. The released country gains all core states from the releasing country, but not from any of the releasing country's subjects and not the releasing country's capital state. All of the released states will be incorporated for the released country.

The released country starts with the same laws as the releasing country.

When releasing as a subject, the type of subject the newly released country becomes depends on the releasing country's recognition status and the released country's type. Additionally,insignificant and unrecognized powers cannot release countries as subjects.[1]

## Liberate country war goal

The target of a Liberate Country war goal must directly hold the liberated country's capital state and the capital state must in a region the acting country has an interest in. Additionally, the targeted country must be capable of releasing the country itself. It costs 30 maneuvers to add this war goal. If the war goal is pressed, the liberated country gains **+50** relations with its liberator and **−50** with the country from which it was released.

## Event release

Certain [events](Events.md) can release countries, and a handful of countries can only appear this way.

## On release

When a country is released through any means, the following occurs:

- If [Bulgaria](Bulgaria.md) was released as an independent country, it activates the JE [Prussia of the Balkans](Bulgaria.md) and triggers the event [Free at Last](Bulgaria.md)
  - Regardless of who released Bulgaria, if [Russia](Russia.md) does not have the JE Crisis and Opportunity, triggers the event Protector of the South Slavs for [Russia](Russia.md) [2]
- If [Yugoslavia](Yugoslavia.md) was released, it loses Bulgarian primary culture
- If [New England](New England.md) was released, a generated character of Luddite ideology takes over its Rural Folk
- If the released country has Ashkenazi primary culture (currently only [Israel](Israel.md)) while Palestine (state) is not a homeland, Palestine (state) gains Sephardic and Ashkenazi homeland cultures
- If the released country is independent, has an African Diaspora culture Afro-Caribbean, Afro-Antillean, Afro-American, Afro-Caribeño, or Afro-Brazilian and either Legacy Slavery, Colonial Slavery, or Slave Trade, it triggers the event [A Constitution of Freedom](New Africa.md)
- If a country in the Europe region is released as a subject of [Austria](Austria.md) with Monarchy, its subject type is changed to crown land
- If releasing a chartered company, it gains the following laws:

- Colonial Administration
- Oligarchy
- Subjecthood
- Extraction Economy
- Colonial Exploitation
- No Schools
- No Health System
- Censorship
- No Workers' Rights
- Child Labor Allowed
- No Social Security
- Migration Controls

## List of releasable countries

Releasable countries can be categorized in a few ways:

1. By core states:
  1. Countries with a defined list of core states
  2. Countries with cultural homelands as core states
  3. Countries with a defined split state
2. By required states:
  1. Only the capital state
  2. Capital and a certain minimum number

The AI never releases countries diplomatically, but may use the liberate country war goal in diplomatic plays.
