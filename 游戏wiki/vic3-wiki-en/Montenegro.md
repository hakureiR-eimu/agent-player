**Montenegro** is a insignificant power in Eastern Europe. It can form [Yugoslavia](Yugoslavia.md). It can also become Serbia-Montenegro or Serbia through [Journal](Journal.md) Entries.

## Historical Context

From 1392, numerous parts of Montenegro were controlled by Republic of Venice, centered on the Bay of Kotor. Large portions fell under the control of the Ottoman Empire from 1496 to 1878.

As far as the Ottoman Empire was concerned, Montenegro was not an independent state in 1836 but a Christian group practicing religious freedom on top of the mountain, with a habit of killing tax collectors to the point where it was simply easier for everyone involved to stop sending them. This was why Montenegrin rulers were given the title Prince-Bishop and (since Bishops cannot marry) rule tended to be handed down to a convenient nephew rather than a son.

The Montenegrins, however, saw themselves as an independent Serbian state (a view supported by the Russians). Because they had little arable land, the Montenegrins were forced to raid local ottoman lands to survive. There was a strong tradition of headhunting, which entailed literally collecting the heads of Ottomans they killed in battle as trophies. To be fair, the Ottoman Pasha on the other side instructed his men to do the same-though the morality of all this is up to you to decide.

## Formation

If Montenegro ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country controlling its capital state of Montenegro, which is its only core state.

## Politics

Montenegro, uniquely, starts as a hereditary Theocracy. Montenegro is also one of just two countries (the other being [Grão-Pará](Grão-Pará.md)) to start with Consumption-Based Taxation. They have two unique laws, Šef Paternalism and Women in the Fields. Montenegro, along with [France](France.md) and [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), are the only countries to start the game with the Social Security institution enabled.

### Interest groups

Montenegro starts with quite powerful Landowners in government. The Rural Folk, Petite Bourgeoisie, Orthodox Church, and Armed Forces are in opposition. The Industrialists, Intelligentsia, and Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Orthodox Church have the Montenegrin Moralist and Orthodox Patriarchy ideologies instead of the default Moralist and Patriarchal. The Petite Bourgeoisie have the Mercantile ideology due to the starting Traditionalism law.

### Diplomacy

Montenegro starts with a claim on the rest of the state of Montenegro, which is split between the [Ottoman Empire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey) and [Austria](Austria.md).

Montenegro starts with two [treaties](Treaty.md):

- *Protection of Orthodox Christians*, where [Russia](Russia.md) guarantees Montenegrin independence, bound for 27 years.
- *Convention of Verona* with [Austria](Austria.md), where Austria transfers 18 grain, 4 fruit and Montenegro gets transit rights through Austria.[1]

Montenegro starts with cordial relations with [Serbia](Serbia.md) and [Russia](Russia.md) (**+30**), and poor relations with the [Ottoman Empire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey) (**-30**). They also have **-15** relations with [Austria](Austria.md).

Montenegro has a pro-country lobby towards [Russia](Russia.md) ().

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.10. |

Montenegro plays very uniquely, the majority of your money comes from raiding the [Ottoman Empire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey), and most of the rest comes from donations from [Russia](Russia.md), who guarantees your independence from the former. Relations with Russia are paramount and Montenegro should be constantly improving them. Relations with Russia will go down over time with **-1** pr 50 days due to the raiding. With constantly improving relations, this will "only" **-1** every 200 days, meaning the **+30** you start with, will last about 16.5 years. It is however **not** a good idea to join their power bloc, due to the relations with Austria and the Journal Entry regarding them. Improving relations with [Austria](Austria.md) is also important, since they are the way to access the world market, and it is possible to get the Austrian parts of Montenegro, the port of Kotor (Cattaro) via JE.

### The unusual economy of Montenegro

Raiding income depends on army power projection, so uniquely expanding the army is not an economic drain, but means state income is expanded, and furthermore Monenegro has a unique modifiers that lowers military wages and goods cost by **35%** and **25%** respectively, making this a good way to get rid of unemployment.

Your population is far higher than your meager arable land can support, so building non-agricultural buildings should be your first priority, probably a Logging Camp followed by Trade Centers.

Montenegro has the [state trait](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/State_trait) *Terra Rossa* boosting farms, but Wheat Farms cannot be built immediately, as all the secondary wares of subsistence farms would be lost. Normally, they are a complete non-factor, but to Montenegro they are crucial to uphold the already terrible [standard of living](Standard of living.md) (and would by the way do nothing to solve the rampant unemployment). So before building them, Montenegro has to build factories that supplies those goods: furniture, clothes, wood and liquor. services are also needed as Montenegro does not have an urban center. tools are also important also to improve the production methods of various buildings. Montenegro has the unique law Women in the Fields as well as a positive modifier to [infrastructure](Infrastructure.md), so they can support many more buildings, than their population and state suggest at a glance. If a building are built, and afterwards proves detrimental, don't be afraid to delete them again.

Note that Montenegro pays for [subventions](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Subventions) for artillery imports, but has no actual need of them yet, so getting rid of them as soon as possible is advised.

It might get to a point, where constructing any more buildings is counter-productive before getting the rest of Montenegro, as there are no more workers. At that point, it is best to begin constructing buildings, but leave them unfinished and put them back in the queue, so they are ready, once Montenegro finally gets more of the state.

Prior to completing From Rebels to Nation, the player should be able to safely maintain raiding at 75% without incurring the wrath of the Ottomans or unduly upsetting relations with Russia.

### From Rebels to Nation

The 'From Rebels to Nation' Journal Entry is key to earning recognition. When this happens, Montenegro will get some more states from the Ottomans, and failing to do so will result in the event *Barbarians!*, which is just bad across the board. To do so, Montenegro must implement the following laws:

- Monarchy
- Land-Based Taxation (or better)
- Some kind of Law Enforcement
- legitimacy better than **50**

While an Education institution is not on the list, it is highly needed and also gives Montenegro additional positive modifiers such as better armies, when implemented. A Health system institution should also be implemented. Šef Paternalism actually gives Montenegro Bureaucracy rather than using it, so it is possible to have many [institutions](Institutions.md).

Due to the unique ideology of the Orthodox Church, Montenegrin Moralist, they are opposed to any tax system as well as Dedicated Police Force, and in favor of maintaining the Theocracy, so it is a balancing act implementing laws that they specifically dislike. It is a good idea therefore to alternate between taxes and monarchy and implementing Religious Schools and Charity Hospitals to make sure that they are never too angry.

Monarchy is by far the most problematic, as it is the law that makes the Orthodox Church the most angry while also having the least support. In fact it only the landowners that support the law, so it is important to prioritize this law sandwiched between Religious Schools and Charity Hospitals. It is also important to implement the law before too much industry deludes the power of the landowners too much.

The rural folk can also prove problematic. Changing from Peasant Levies to Professional Army can be used to please them. Enacting Homesteading is counterproductive, as it strengthens them too much, and weakens the landowners.

Montenegro has two unique decision *Build the Biljarda* and *Canonize Saint*, which cost 15000 and 20000 ducats up front to take, and they are worth going for as soon as possible. The first is required to complete the *From Rebels to Nation* Journal Entry, and the second makes it much easier to complete it due to an increase in law implementation chance and base legitimacy. As it modernizes, Montenegro gets several Journal Entries that give the option of significant 20-50 year bonuses to military stats. While other options may also be powerful in their own right, combining several of Montenegro's unique military bonuses can allow it to conscript an army that can go toe-to-toe with the Ottomans even before the Great Eastern Crisis fires.

Land-Based Taxation should be saved for last as it can wreck havoc on Montenegro's already fickle SoL.

Montenegro can also choose to *visit Moscow* which makes it impossible to implement [laws](Laws.md) for 2 years, but gives them an option to improve relations with Russia by **40** every 10 years. This is enough to offset the diminishing relations and then some. In general Montenegro should always either implement a law or be visiting Moscow.

### Austria and Change of Tack

It is possible to get the Austrian parts of Montenegro, Kotor, through a journal entry *Change of Tack*. This is crucial as it means access to the world market is not contingent on Austria's graces. Note that this is possible to fail even before it comes up, because it requires that Montenegro does *not* join the Russian power bloc, but the JE does not inform you of this before completing *From Rebels to Nation.*

For this reason and for the fact that Austria is the only country which you can import wares via [treaties](Treaty.md), it is important to improve relations with them from day one. Succeeding the JE will also allow the player to select another round of military bonuses.

If the journal entry fails, it is possible to get sea access by annexing either the rest of Ottoman Montenegro or Albania.

Montenegro can of course also take Kotor the conventional way, but it is unlikely that the Russians will help Montenegro here, as they have a defensive pact with Austria.

### The World Around Montenegro

It is likely that a lot will happen around Montenegro due to both the [Ottoman Empire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey) attacking [Egypt](Egypt.md), the Ottomans trying to annex any of their Balkan subjects or a rebellion from any of the various minorities in the Ottoman Empire, followed by Austria and/or Russia going against the Ottomans.

They can have various outcomes from releasing countries to [Serbia](Serbia.md) becoming independent to Austria or Russia annexing provinces. In fact it is not unlikely that Austria decides to annex the state of Montenegro from the Ottomans. This is **not** a problem, as the journal entries will work just fine regardless. In fact this guarantees that Montenegro will get the entire state if they manage to complete both *From Rebels to Nation* and *Change of Tack*.

### Serbia

If [Serbia](Serbia.md) gets independence and share a border with Montenegro, it is possible to unify the two countries and continue playing as either 'Serbia', 'Montenegro' or 'Serbia-Montenegro'. After completing *From Rebels to Nation*, the only thing standing in the way is the Ottoman parts of Western Serbia, which Serbia has a claim on and Montenegro can get a claim on through events.

Note that choosing which country to continue as affects which [achievements](Achievements.md) can be earned. In isolation picking either Montenegro or Serbia and Montenegro is probably better, as they have better modifiers and likely also better laws.

### Unifying the Balkans

After completing the journal entries, it is time to look at the state of the Ottoman Empire. If [Bulgaria](Bulgaria.md), Serbia and/or the [Romania](Romania.md)n minors are independent, getting them to team up to beat the Ottomans, should be enough to take them on, assuming [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) is not willing to help them. Russia or Austria should be willing to lend a hand as well. Getting Kosovo, Skopia, Bosnia and the rest of Serbia is important to move towards forming [Yugoslavia](Yugoslavia.md).

While it may be tempting to liberalize quickly after completing Montenegro's journal entries, doing so may alienate Russia and Austria enough that they will not be willing to support Montenegro in wars against the Ottomans.

Afterwards, you have to turn towards Austria, to get the Slovene and Croatian parts. With a bit of luck, [Croatia](Croatia.md) has become independent.

### Slaying Goliath

Arguably the most difficult part of defeating the Ottoman Empire once and for all is maintaining a land border with them in order to launch continued invasions as they are pushed deeper into the Anatolian hinterland. Consider prioritizing the conquest of Thrace and Anatolia before liberating or annexing Albania and Bosnia, in order to guarantee that Bulgaria, Egypt and Russia will not cut off land-based routes to the Ottomans. There are no releasable countries in western Anatolia (although Greece or Egypt may be willing to accept some territories), forcing Montenegro to contend with relatively quickly annexing large amounts of heavily discriminated pops. While the wars against the Ottomans will get easier, repeat Turkish separatist revolts and the increasingly unfriendly attention of other world powers present significant obstacles. Researching Human Rights in order to adopt Multiculturalism is advisable, as the annexed Turkish pops will lend enough support to make the law passable without need for Anarchist/Utopian/Humanitarian IG leaders.

Once the player can reliably overrun all of Ottoman territory (or can count on Russia or another ally to do the same), releasing Iraq will contribute significant progress towards the achievement without costing infamy. Note that this will block the release of Kurdistan or Assyria, as all three countries require the inclusion of the state of Mosul; unless the Ottomans have already lost southern Iraq, it is not advisable to release Kurdistan or Assyria as this will create an Ottoman rump state out of immediate reach of the player.

If solely hunting for the achievement, Montenegro is free to concede most of its western territories to avoid wars and revolts once the Ottomans have been cornered in Anatolia. Removing the Ottomans from the map will result in a further Journal Entry reward to help consolidate the new Montonegrin empire.
