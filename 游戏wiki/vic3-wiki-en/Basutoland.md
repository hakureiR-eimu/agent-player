**Basutoland** is an unrecognized power in the Southern Africa strategic region. It neighbors [Zulu](Zulu.md), [Winburg](Oranje.md), [Philippolis](Philippolis.md), and the decentralized [Xhosa](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Xhosa). Historically, Basutoland become a subject of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), and eventually gained independence as the modern country of [Lesotho](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesotho)[1].

## Scripted content

Main article:
Southern Africa content
Basutoland shares a journal entry with nearby countries related to the struggle for dominance over the *South Africa* [geographic region](Geographic region.md). Specifically, Basutoland must ensure diplomatic recognition and coastal access to gain dominance over the other minor states in the region.

Basutoland is an Absolute Principality organized as an Autocracy, ruled by Prince Moshoeshoe Bamokoteli, who supports the Landowners. Basutoland is one of only two countries in its Home Region (and the only Animist country) to start with Freedom of Conscience.

### Interest groups

Basutoland begins with the Landowners in government, with the Armed Forces and Devout in opposition. The Rural Folk, Petite Bourgeoisie, Trade Unions, Intelligentsia, and Industrialists are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Basutoland borders the decentralized [Xhosa](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Xhosa) to the south, the powerful [Zulu](Zulu.md) kingdom to the east, [Winburg](Oranje.md) to the north, [Philippolis](Philippolis.md) to the west, and the ![Flag GBR uk.png](/images/thumb/6/6e/Flag_GBR_uk.png/24px-Flag_GBR_uk.png) [Cape Colony](South Africa.md) to the southwest - by far the largest country in the region.

Basutoland starts with no diplomatic pacts, and its only interest is in the Southern Africa region.

## Military

Basutoland has the Peasant Levies law.

Basutoland starts with 5 Battalions and can call upon 1 Conscript. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st Basuto Army | 5 Irregular Infantry | — | Southern Africa |

Basutoland starts with no Flotillas, as it is landlocked.
