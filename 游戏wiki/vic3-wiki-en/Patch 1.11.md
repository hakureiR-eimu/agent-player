**Patch 1.11**, aka "Chamomile", was released on 2025-10-16[1] with the checksum *3360*. It was released alongside the [Songs of the Homeland](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Songs_of_the_Homeland) music pack.

## Songs of the Homeland features

11 new music tracks:

1. *A New Day Awakens*
2. *Walking Through Mist*
3. *Approaching Tempest*
4. *The Secret Glade*
5. *Old Ruins in the Forest*
6. *Sicilienne*
7. *Dance of the Shepherds*
8. *A Nap in the Fields*
9. *The Pride of People*
10. *Ode to the Night*
11. *Evening Lights*
