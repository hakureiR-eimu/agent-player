**Carolina** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) on the Atlantic coast of North America. It represents a unification of the Carolinas or restoration of the original colonial Province of Carolina.

Carolina can (re)form the [United States of America](America.md).

To be released, it requires that the [game rules](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Game_rules) are set to include historically implausible nations.

## Historical background

Carolina was established as a British colony in the mid 1600s. The original charter was granted by King Charles I, then re-issued by Charles II. The colony was named in honor of Charles I. As the colony developed, difficulties of communication between the northern and southern regions of the colony created a de facto division, which was formalized in 1712, though both colonies remained under the control of the same proprietors for the rest of the decade.

## Formation

Both core states are needed to form Carolina; Tennessee can be claimed by the formation event.
Carolina can be formed only by North Carolina or South Carolina when they control both states and requires the [game rule](Game rule.md) *Formable Nations: All*. All of the Carolinas are [releasable](Releasable countries.md) with the game rule *Releasable Nations: All*.
