**Athabaska** is a decentralized nation in North America. As a North American native nation, it may form the [Turtle Island](Turtle Island.md).

Athabaska is playable if [Alaska](Alaska.md) becomes independent, has enacted No Colonial Affairs, has researched Nationalism, and has either enacted Multiculturalism or Council Republic. Under these conditions, the Alaskan independence event *Alone in the Cold* has an option to switch to play as Athabaska.

If Athabaska exists, the player switches to play as them, annexing Alaska. If not, Alaska becomes Athabaska, resulting in them keeping the Orthodox religion and Russian culture alongside Athabaskan.
