*"The strength of the ruling Tokugawa shogunate is failing, with famine and unrest spreading all across the country. The Edo period has lasted in Japan for over 200 years, will it be able to last for 200 more?"*

**Japan** is an unrecognized major power in East Asia. As an island nation, Japan only directly borders its Vassal [Ezochi](Ezochi.md) in the north, and through a Split State, its Tributary [Ryukyu](Ryukyu.md) in the South. [China](China.md) and [Korea](Korea.md) are across the Sea of Japan, while [Russia](Russia.md) looms over the Sea of Okhotsk.

Japan is one of four recommended countries for the *Hegemon* [objective](Objective.md), along with [Prussia](Prussia.md), [Spain](Spain.md), and [Egypt](Egypt.md).

## Historical background

The Tokugawa shogunate was established in 1600 after Tokugawa Ieyasu's victory at the Battle of Sekigahara, ending the *Sengoku* – or Warring States – period. Although the Emperors of Japan retained *de jure* control of the country, the Shogun ran the government *de facto* and made all state decisions. Japan also remained mostly isolated from other countries due to the *Sakoku* policy, though trade continued with [China](China.md), [Korea](Korea.md), the [Netherlands](Netherlands.md), and its subjects in Hokkaido.

*Sakoku* ended in 1854, after [America](America.md)n admiral Matthew Perry forced the Shogun to sign diplomatic and trade agreements between the United States and Japan. Soon after, the power of the Shogun ended in 1868 with the Meiji Restoration, which was followed by a period of rapid change throughout Japan.

## Formation

If Japan ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country that controls Kansai and two other Japanese homelands, which make up Japan's core states. All nine Japanese homelands are fully owned by Japan at the start of the game, except for Hokkaido which is split with [Ezochi](Ezochi.md).

### Interest groups

Japan starts with the Daimyō in government. The Samurai, Heimin, Jisha, Rangakusha, and Chōnin are in opposition. The Trade Unions and Gōshō are marginalized.

The Daimyō have the Bakuhan ideology instead of the default Hierarchic.

The Samurai gain the Bakufu ideology in addition their regular ideologies, and the Jisha have Buddhist Moralist ideology instead of the default Moralist. The Rangakusha have Rangaku ideology instead of the default Republican. The Gōshō have the Interventionist ideology instead of the default Laissez-Faire. The Chōnin have the Mercantile ideology due to the starting economic and trade laws.

### Diplomacy

Japan has [Ezochi](Ezochi.md) as a vassal and [Ryukyu](Ryukyu.md) as a tributary. [Great Qing](China.md) and [Joseon](Korea.md) are across the Sea of Japan.

Japan starts with **+30** relations with the [Netherlands](Netherlands.md), making these countries cordial, while relations with [Great Qing](China.md) and [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company) are poor, at **−30**. It starts the game with two [treaties](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Diplomatic_Treaty): "Dejima Trade" with the [Netherlands](Netherlands.md), which grants them Trade Privileges and is binding for 23 years; and the "Nagasaki China Trade" with [Great Qing](China.md), transferring 20 units of lead to them and £700 in exchange for 50 units of sugar , binding for 18 years.

Japan has claims on Sakhalin, and as [Russia](Russia.md) and [Great Qing](China.md) also starts with claims on Sakhalin, it is likely to be a source of tension between the three countries.

Japan starts with a Pervasive level of interest in its capital strategic region of Northeast Asia, and has an Engaged level of interest in the strategic regions of South China, North China, and Siberia.

### Industry and resources

Japan has most of the resources required for industrialization in its starting states. This allows Japan to ignore international trade and expansion somewhat in the early part of the game. Japan starts with small consumer industry and a well developed agriculture sector. It also starts with access to all resources except rubber. Japan can also grow several cash crops.
