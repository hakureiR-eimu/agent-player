Some [states](State.md) have **state traits** which provide bonuses or penalties. The most common effects are bonuses to certain to [throughput](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Throughput) on certain goods or industries and bonuses or penalties to [infrastructure](Infrastructure.md) and/or construction efficiency.

In split states, the state trait applies to all parts of the state, even when there is not an applicable resource. State traits are mostly fixed and generally do not change throughout the game, but certain events can add or modify them. Notably, (severe) malaria can be disabled by certain technologies.
