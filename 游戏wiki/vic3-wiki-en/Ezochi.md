*"Ezochi, or the 'Ainu Lands', has long been ruled by the Japanese Matsumae clan on the basis of the contract fishery system. While Japanese settlement north of the Wajinchi is forbidden by law, many fishermen overstay, putting increased pressure on the native population. Will the Ainu persevere, or will they be absorbed by their more powerful southern neighbors?"*

**Ezochi** is an unrecognized power in Northern Asia. It is a vassal of [Japan](Japan.md). Its capital state is Yezo, which it splits with its overlord, and it shares the archipelago state of Sakhalin with [Evenki](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Evenki), [Ainu Mosir](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Ainu_Mosir), [Ulta](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Ulta), and [Russian-American Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Russian-American_Company).

## Formation

If it ceases to exist, Ezochi can be released by any country controlling its capital and homeland of Yezo.

## Scripted content

Ezochi participates in some of [Japan](Japan.md)'s [content](Japanese content.md).

### Interest groups

Ezochi begins with the Daimyō and Chōnin in government, with the Devout, Heimin, and Samurai in opposition. The Trade Unions, Industrialists, and Rangakusha are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Ezochi starts as a vassal of [Japan](Japan.md).

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Ezochi has no starting industry.

**Resources**

Ezochi has exactly 1 fishing wharf in each state, both owned by Japanese financial districts.

## Military

Ezochi has the Warrior Caste law.

Due to its low population, Ezochi starts with with no battalions and can raise no conscripts.

Ezochi starts with no flotillas.
