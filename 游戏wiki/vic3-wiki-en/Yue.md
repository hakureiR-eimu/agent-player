**Yue** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in South China. It may be released by a country that controls its capital. At the start date, all of its states are controlled by [China](China.md), with the exception of a treaty port in Western Guangdong owned by [Portugal](Portugal.md); both countries may release Yue at the start.

If [China](China.md) fails the [Fragile Unity](Chinese content.md) journal entry, Yue is released in Western Guangdong as a warlord state. In that case, they are given Han as an additional primary culture, and can thus potentially reform China.
