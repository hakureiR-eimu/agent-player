**Paulistânia** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in Brazil. It can be released by any country which controls its capital, which in 1836 is owned by [Brazil](Brazil.md).

It cannot be released if Brazil has [integrated the Paulista culture](Brazilian content.md) unless its core states are owned by country other than Brazil.
