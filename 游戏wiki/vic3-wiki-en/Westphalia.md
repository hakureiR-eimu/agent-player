**Westphalia** (German: Westfalen) is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in the Rhine region. Its core states comprise the [Prussia](Prussia.md)n Rhine area. When released, it likely borders [Hanover](Hanover.md), [Belgium](Belgium.md), [Netherlands](Netherlands.md), [Luxembourg](Luxembourg.md), [France](France.md), and a number of small German states.

Westphalia can be released by any country which controls the state of Westphalia and another of its core states.

As a German state, it can form, [Confederation of the Rhine](Confederation of the Rhine.md), [North German Federation](North German Federation.md) and [Germany](Germany.md) proper.

## Historical background

Westphalia is one of the richest parts of Germany and the whole of Europe. It was divided between numerous states as part of the Holy Roman Empire until the Napoleonic Wars, after which it was a province within the Kingdom of Prussia.

## Core states

Westphalia has three core states. They start with sizeable Arms Industries and Steel Mills as well as coal and iron. All three states have positive [state traits](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/State_trait): Rhine River and/or Ruhr Coalfield, with the first improving [infrastructure](Infrastructure.md) and [market access price impact](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Market_access_price_impact) and the second improving coal mine [throughput](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Throughput). Westphalia does however lack agricultural products and, when independent, may have a hard time selling the weapons they produce if they do not join another market.
