### The Age of Metternich

*The aftermath of the Napoleonic Wars have established Austria as a leading conservative power in Europe. The leading architect of the post-Napoleonic order, Klemens von Metternich, has served as Chancellor of Austria since the tumultuous year of 1821. His agenda calls for upholding the Austrian Autocracy, the maintenance of the conservative peace in Europe, and the promotion of the arts and sciences.* The Metternich Agenda: Realizing various items on Chancellor von Metternich's agenda will improve the effects of this Journal Entry. These achievements will be scored upon this journal entry successfully completing.

- Prevented Italian Unification
- Germany has not been unified by any other country
- Austria is the leading producer of fine art
- Austria owns the entire state region of West Galicia
- Austria has researched 52 technologies
- Metternich System has more than 12 members
- Cohesion of Metternich System is greater than 75
- All Liberal or Socialist [political movements](Political movement.md) have marginal support
- Austria has Autocracy, Censorship, and Secret Police
- Home Affairs and Law Enforcement institutions are at level 2 or higher
