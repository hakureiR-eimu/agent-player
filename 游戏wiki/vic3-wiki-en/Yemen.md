**Yemen** is a small [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in the Arabian peninsula. Yemen represents a union of the four Yemenite and Bedouin states at the start of the game: [Qasimid State](Azal.md), [Lahej](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Lahej), [Kathiri](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kathiri), [Mahra](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Mahra). All four states are independent at game start, and [Hedjaz](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Hedjaz) also controls a part of Yemen at the start of the game. Yemen is also releasable.

Yemen is able to form [Arabia](Arabia.md).

## Historical context

Yemen's long and complex history includes the ancient kingdoms of Saba, Hadramout, Ma'in, and Qataban. It was an important center of trade and culture in ancient times, with the Sabean kingdom being one of the most powerful. Yemen played a crucial role in the early spread of Islam and was ruled by a series of Islamic dynasties. It was under Ottoman and British control before North and South Yemen were unified in 1990. However, the country has faced political instability, economic challenges, and conflict, including a civil war that began in 2015. Overall, Yemen's history is characterized by its strategic location and the influence of various empires, religions, and cultures over the centuries.

## Yemeni minors

The Yemeni minors are all unrecognized powers and have similar basic stats, starting technology, and politics. Each starts with only a split state in Yemen. They are all Principalities and Sunni, except Azal (Qasimid State) which is Shiite. They generally start with the Landowners in government with a mix of the Rural Folk and Sunni Ulema in opposition, and the remaining interest groups marginalized.

They all start with the following technology and laws, except Qasimid State which has Theocracy:
