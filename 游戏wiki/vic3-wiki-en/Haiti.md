**Haiti** is an insignificant power power in the Caribbean region. Haiti is an island nation next to [Cuba](Cuba.md) and [Puerto Rico](Puerto Rico.md). It is called *Saint-Domingue* as a subject of [France](France.md).

## Historical Context

Haiti won its independence from France in 1804 and united the island of Hispaniola in 1822 when it occupied the newly independent Spanish Haiti, previously the colony of Santo Domingo. In 1825, King Charles X of France imposed an indemnity on Haiti by force in return for diplomatic recognition.

## Formation

If Haiti ceases to exist, it can be released by any country that controls the state of Haiti.

## Scripted content

Haiti starts with the journal entry *An Odious Debt* which represents the indemnity it negotiated with France as part of its independence.

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Haiti
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

### Paying off the Odious Debt

Overall Haiti should reduce its government [expenditures](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Budget) and increase tax income. Reducing the number of battalions is a good place to start since none of Haiti's neighbors are likely to invade. Developing cash crops is an option to increase income since those buildings are quick and often profitable at even low prices. Then, exporting those cash crops to the Major Powers to keep those buildings profitable. Early on Haiti may want to invest in a Port in order to support the export economy. Building a shipyard pairs well with Haiti's fishing and wood resources and in addition, make the clippers used by the port cheaper. It is recommended to not invest in Universities or Construction sectors early on as Haiti cannot afford it until the debt is paid off.

Alternatively, Haiti may ask great and major powers for monetary support.in exchange for obligations, power block embassies, trade rights. Depending on the amount of the money received, Haiti may even afford several Construction sectors. Giving Investment rights is rather questionable in the long run, because Haiti has limited population and Migration quotas. Early colonisation in Senegal is also a viable strategy to majorly boost the extraction of resources, mainly iron, sulfur and fabric, and convert those into viable trading goods.

### Unilaterally Cancel Debt

[The Great Wave](The Great Wave.md) has complicated the naval transportation of troops for both, a player and the AI. Any island/isolated coastal country, including Haiti, now has a much better chance to defend their homelands from the naval invasions. On the other hand, it heavily cripples the expansion, because Naval Invasion with mere 8 battalions requires heavy investment into the ships and enormous time to build them for a small nation.

Nevertheless, **Unilaterally Cancel Debt** is now a *consistent* strategy which is rather straightforward to perform, although with the two main downsides: *-50% prestige and -50 treaty acceptance with all countries for 20 years*. The latter is very annoying as it restrains any Good Transfer deals, maiming the initial acceleration of Haitian economy growth on the island with scarce resources.

For the best possible result, it needs some preparations:

- get rid of all Eastern-Haiti troops (Afro-Caribeno and Caribeno battalions), two regiments of resource-eating cavalry and the canon;
- build Logging Camps and Arms Industries to get own supply of small arms,
- upgrade Irregular Infantry,
- delete the port in Eastern Haiti and release [Santo Domingo](Santo Domingo.md); exempt them from services. This means [France](France.md) doesn't get a claim on the other part of the island, nor are they able to invade the Eastern Haiti. Also reducing payments and giving investment rights may help to rapidly integrate your puppet after the inevitable war with France,

*As of 1.13.9, such a sequence of actions seems to break the scripted scenario for Haiti about rebellious Afro-Caribeno people. So, if you want to play through One and Indivisible , start the game with integration of the Eastern Haiti, but beware of potential separatism penalties and civil war during the ongoing war with France.*

- ideally, find a trade partner who gives you iron; otherwise, set iron to the highest subventions,
- arrange economy sliders and assign taxation on goods to get some profit,
- consider building Naval Fortification, provided you are able to supply [Basic Fortification](List of production methods.md)

The more Haiti waits, the more ships [France](France.md) will build and the more battalions they will be able to fetch. Therefore, cancelling the debt on January 1 is perfectly doable, because France usually needs some time to process Haiti's impudence and delays the war declaration until the start of 1837, already after your small arms industry will have been established.

As soon as the Diplomatic Play is initiated, assign the army on *Defence* (ideally, find a general with *Adamant Defence*), and increase the military spending to the maximum. Just in case, recruit at least three Line Infantry as Conscripts to reach 20 battalions in your army; you may safely remove the conscripts after the first invasion if the impact on your budget seems too harsh. Don't forget to revoke the French claim, but don't bother adding any additional wargoal, as it will be next to impossible to achieve it.

[France](France.md) will send 15 to 20 battalions, and the game may even scare you with the negative advantage. However, the first battle is usually decisive, and if Haiti wins, the rest of the war is determined. If Naval Fortification was built and supplied, **Haiti's** Line Infantry is even able to withstand the next-level Skirmish Infantry sent by [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) who may also try to establish a protectorate over Haiti.

After the war with France is won, integrate [Santo Domingo](Santo Domingo.md).

The next steps are rather subjective, but most of them require at least some ships to get out of the imprisonment on the island and promote glorious Afro-Antillean culture.

### The Black Girondins

Haiti's immediate problems are almost effortlessly solvable, requiring no luck, and can consistently result in having the debt paid by 1853 and the Dominicans pacified by 1869 at the absolute latest. Before unpausing, begin incorporating Est D'Hayti, raise taxes to maximum, impose a consumption tax on tobacco, stop subsidizing your port buildings, and disband the entire military. Issue the [Promote Social Mobility](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Promote_social_mobility) and [Road Maintenance](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Road_Maintenance) [Decrees](Decrees.md) on both sides of the island. Bring every interest group into the government, (crashing legitimacy to zero). For research, queue up [Empiricism](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Empiricism), [Pharmaceuticals](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Pharmaceuticals), [Intensive Agriculture](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Intensive_Agriculture), [Mechanized Workshops](Mechanized Workshops.md) (prerequisites will be queued up automatically), and [Dialectics](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Dialectics). [Central Archives](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Central_Archives) may also be useful after Pharmaceuticals, if you want to get [Technocracy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Technocracy) early (which will require just a bit more finagling). Declare an interest in Gran Colombia, and boost relations with every country from Ecuador to French Guyana, plus Spain and the United States.

Haiti faces two problems: Debt and Dominicans.

First, the Debt: Haiti does not have [Coal](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Coal) or [Iron](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Iron), it has [Wood](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Wood), [Fish](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Fish), and arable land for [Dye Plantations](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Dye_Plantations), [Sugar Plantations](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Sugar_Plantations), [Rice Farms](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Rice_Farms) and [Livestock Ranches](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Livestock_Ranches). Focus on building Light Industries that use Haiti's existing resources: [Food Industries](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Food_Industries), [Furniture Manufacturies](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Furniture_Manufacturies), [Shipyards](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Shipyards), [Textile Mills](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Textile_Mills), [Tooling Workshops](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Tooling_Workshops), [Glassworks](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Glassworks), and [Paper Mills](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Paper_Mills). Be mindful of avoiding [Production Methods](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Production_methods) that may add demand for Coal, Iron, [Sulfur](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Sulfur), or [Lead](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Lead). Build [Logging Camps](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Logging_Camps) and [Fishing Wharves](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Fishing_Wharves) to cheaply build up financial districts. New players should set the aforementioned buildings to auto-build, and focus primarily on advancing a factory to the top of the build queue if there is a shortage of its output or the good is otherwise expensive in your [Market](Market.md). The easiest way to tell which goods are expensive in your market is to look at the "Haitian Market" bar on the right side of the screen. The three goods on the left are the most expensive, the three goods on the right are the least expensive. Note that [Groceries](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Groceries) will almost never show up as "most expensive" because pops can buy [Meat](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Meat) and [Grain](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Grain) directly, so you should remember to build a Food Industries regardless. In fact, you should build five food industries in order to create the Basic Food [Company](Companies.md) to boost your birth rate, as Haiti's population is quite small. Once Haiti's [Treasury](Treasury.md) is no longer in the red, having gold reserves instead of loans, lower [Taxes](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Taxes) from "Very High" to "High" to slow down the development of Radicals from Political Movements. Don't forget to re-subsidize ports as well, as your infrastructure may otherwise be strained, particularly in the less populated Est D'Hayti. From there, paying off the Odious Debt is only a matter of time, and you can easily generate budget surpluses in the 4k-6k range. Each time you pay a portion of the debt, construct one (1) additional [Construction Sector](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Construction_Sector), to a total of four after the last portion is paid, two on each side of the island. Once the debt is completely paid, lower taxes to Normal in order to stop creating radicals. Note that the Odious Debt itself also creates radicals. Furthermore, when the debt is paid, you can either get a 10 year minor boost to prestige or a 20 year major boost to suppressing of radicals. Take the boost to suppressing radicals. This leads us to Haiti's next problem.

Second, the Dominicans: Even though your legitimacy is zero at game start, the Dominican National Movement's activism will allow you to pass the laws which they want you to pass. Before unpausing, start by passing [National Guard](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/National_Guard), [Private Schools](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Private_Schools), or wait a year to pass [Right of Assembly](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Right_of_Assembly) depending on your preference. Do not attempt to pass Right of Assembly without first retiring your three generals, which you will have to wait until 1 Jan 1837 to do, or else they will attempt a [coup](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Coup!). Researching Empiricism allows passing [Public Schools](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Public_Schools), replacing Private Schools if you've already passed it, deradicalizing the Dominicans even more. However, passing Right of Assembly allows passing Guaranteed Liberties, but you will not be able to replace National Guard during this period if you've already passed it, As both the Liberal and Dominican movements are indifferent between the two. Once you're satisfied with your Internal Security, Education, and Free Speech laws, you can easily abolish the Junta in favor of [Oligarchy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Oligarchy), [Landed Voting](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Landed_voting), [Wealth Voting](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Wealth_Voting), or [Census Suffrage](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Census_Suffrage). Go for Wealth Voting for now, and switch to Census Suffrage only after Haiti's midgame problems are solved. A [Conservative Party](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Conservative_Party) will likely win the first [Election](Elections.md), allowing the passing of both [Dedicated Police Force](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Dedicated_Police_Force) (which reduces radicals) and [Public Health Insurance](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Public_Health_Insurance) (if you want to go that way). If you make sure to propose laws the exact same day that the previous one passes so that the Dominicans don't have time to reduce their activism because they got a law they liked, you won't have to wait for Dominican or Liberal activism to come back up to 25%, and you can disband the Junta as early as 1842, rendering the Dominicans almost completely inert once the debt is paid off and taxes are back to normal.

The Liberals support [Cultural Exclusion](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Cultural_Exclusion), and this is a law that you will likely want to pass early. Be warned: despite not being a Primary Culture of Haiti, the Dominicans have the National Liberal ideology. This means that they actually *support* Haiti's [National Supremacy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/National_Supremacy) Law (to the point that the Polish Nationalization Amendment contributes slightly to their activism), and become *more upset* if you try to pass Cultural Exclusion. Presumably, this is because they support National Supremacy *for Dominicans*, and will indeed have that law if they run a secession play. Do *not* try to pass Cultural Exclusion until *after* the Dominican National Movement *no longer exists at all.* This will not happen automatically once the One and Indivisible Journal Entry is cleared. You will need to [suppress](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Suppress) the movement afterwards until it is gone for good.

If you want to make an early pivot to Technocracy, this can be done but requires a bit more careful planning. Because Haiti's starting power distribution law is [Autocracy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Autocracy), there is a strong consensus among most [Interest Groups](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Interest_groups) and both the Liberal and Dominican movements in favor of shifting to Technocracy if you have Central Archives. The problem is that Central Archives is a Tier 2 technology that may take as late as 1856 to research. The Dominicans may not be so patient. In that case, instead of rushing the laws they want, stretch out their implementation. Don't propose National Guard, Private Schools, or Right of Assembly until their activism reaches 74%. Then, wait for their activism to come all the way back up to 74% before proposing the next law. Remember that you can pass Private Schools even if you plan to pass Public Schools without issue, and this is a good way to stall for time while you research Central Archives, but you cannot do this with National Guard if you plan to pass Guaranteed Liberties. As the debt is paid down and taxes get lower, it will take them longer and longer to get angry again, which should give you the time to pass Technocracy. As with Wealth Voting, your first government will likely be Conservative one made up of Landowners and the Catholic Church, allowing the passage of Dedicated Police Force and Public Health Insurance.

In either case, once you have Normal taxes, Dedicated Police, no Odious Debt, and a Legitimate Government (whether under Wealth Voting or Technocracy), the Dominicans should eventually go below 25% activism and Est D'Hayti should go below 10% turmoil, completing the One and Indivisible Journal Entry and allowing you to suppress their movement into oblivion. From there, if you desire, you can then pass Cultural Exclusion.

This can all be done before 1869, allowing Haiti to enter the midgame with a respectable industrial base and some of the best laws in the game. While players may be tempted to build out more than four Construction Sectors at first, this is a mistake. Instead, players wishing to improve Haiti's construction speed should focus on alternating between wood, iron, and steel construction over building new construction buildings. After Steel-Frame construction is researched and sustainable, only then should players begin building new Construction sectors. In the meantime, Players should focus on constructing universities. Two on each side of the island is a good start. Haiti's small population allows it to become literate quickly with any educational system paired with the Promote Social Mobility decree and, without native iron or coal reserves, therefore has a comparative advantage in tech rushing.

In the midgame, Haiti's other problems reveal themselves. Namely, Haiti has one of the most Jeffersonian constitutions ever written. Agrarianism as an Economic System, Mercantilism as a Trade Policy, and Homesteading as a Land Reform means that if you pass Census Suffrage too early, the Rural Folk will dominate, and industrialization will be that much more difficult. With Wealth Voting still in play, prop up your Industrialists until you can switch from Agrarianism to Interventionism or Laissez-Faire (depending on your preference). Likewise from Mercantilism to Protectionism or Free Trade (again, preference). Keeping Homesteading or switching to Commercialized Agriculture comes down to what game you are trying to run. Switching from Per-Capita Taxation to Proportional Taxation may prove difficult with both Industrialists and Landowners resisting the change and only Trade Unions supporting it, but it is necessary to maintain a respectable level of tax revenue, and may require switching to Census Suffrage.

Even though Haiti does not have native mineral resources of any sort, players should build one of every type of factory on each side of the island, particularly for those industries which feed Railways and Steel Frame Construction: Steel Mills, Motor Industries, Fertilizer Plants, and Explosives Factories. Do *not* subsidize this factories. This ensures that players will be able to receive the "best price" for the relevant outputs, between importing Steel and Explosives directly, versus workers being able to fill up the steel plant and produce it from imported coal and iron as steel's price increases relative to its inputs. This is particularly powerful if you have Free Trade.
