The **Indian Territory** is an unrecognized power in North America. It starts as a puppet of [America](America.md). If Indian Territory becomes independent, it is called *Sequoyah* and it can form [Turtle Island](Turtle Island.md). It will be annexed by America if they complete the *Manifest Destiny: Frontier Wars* journal entry.

## Formation

If it ceases to exist, the Indian Territory can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country controlling its capital and only core state of Oklahoma.

### Interest groups

The Indian Territory begins with the Landowners in government. The Evangelicals, Rural Folk, Armed Forces, and Intelligentsia are in opposition, while the Petite Bourgeoisie, Trade Unions, and Industrialists are marginalized.

## Strategies and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Indian Territory
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
Following changes to the Indian Territory's ability to court great powers into war against the United States in patch 1.2, gaining independence is extremely difficult and will most certainly require the support of another great power, most likely [Great Britain](Great Britain.md). Any successful run as Indian Territory will require nearly perfect RNG.

Building a small army is an early priority, otherwise the Indian Territory will quickly lose Liberty Desire and become annexed. The Indian Territory will have difficulty filling vacancies at the outset of the game, between Serfdom and low [literacy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Literacy). Researching Academia and building a University or two is an important early goal in between modestly increasing the size of lumber mills and agricultural buildings. Railways and Colonization are further important technologies to prioritize, otherwise the Indian Territory will have no way to expand its economy.

Bringing more IGs into the government will allow the player to liberalize laws and increase social and economic mobility. At game start, the Armed Forces and Evangelicals can join the government, and the Rural Folk will soon be able to join as well given a small investment in agriculture
