**Israel** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in the Middle East. Israel's sole core state, Palestine, is owned by [Egypt](Egypt.md). The state is also claimed by the [Ottoman Empire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey) at the start of the game.

Israel can be released by any country that controls its capital and has researched the Pan-nationalism technology. Israel is the only country in Victoria 3 to have Jewish state religion.

## Historical background

The history of Israel is a complex and multifaceted one, spanning thousands of years. The ancient Israelite kingdoms of Judea and Israel were subjects or clients of external empires for much of antiquity, from the Neo-Assyrians, to the Babylonians, Persians, Greeks, and finally the Romans. Under the Romans, the Jewish Israelites revolted three times in the first and second centuries AD, with severe reprisals after each. The primary punishment was enslavement and banishment from Judea and a prohibition on entering Jerusalem. This was the beginning of the diaspora; though a number of Jewish Israelites remained in Judea and nearby regions, the majority lived in more distant parts of the Roman Empire.

After the Roman Empire fell, Palestine came under first Arab rule, then Turkish. The Jewish populations in Europe began to develop into two distinct groups: the Germanic-influenced Ashkenazi and the Iberian-influenced Sephardim. European attitudes towards Jews varied from cautious acceptance to outright persecution, often changing with each ruler or when disasters happened.

In the 19th century as nationalism rose to prominence, Jewish nationalism – called Zionism – took shape, primarily seeking a return of a Jewish Israel in Palestine, then controlled by the Ottoman Empire. Jewish immigrants to Palestine also increased in this period. The 19th century also saw the revival of the Hebrew language as vernacular language.

In the 20th century, Britain issued the Balfour Declaration in 1917 promising support for a Jewish homeland and was granted a mandate by the League of Nations to govern Palestine after World War I. Jewish immigrants increased greatly during that time, and following World War II, the modern state of Israel was established in 1948. The establishment of Israel was a controversial and divisive event, with the displacement of Palestinian Arabs and ongoing conflicts between Israelis and Palestinians to this day.

## Core states

Palestine is Israel's only core state. Palestine is also a core state for [Palestine](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Palestine), [Syria](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Syria), and [Arabia](Arabia.md). If Israel is released, Palestine gains homelands for both Ashkenazi and Sephardim.

Israel has a low population, and an even lower ethnic and religious Jewish population. Despite Ashkenazi being a primary culture of Israel, no Ashkenazi pops actually live in the state at the start of the game.

Palestine has few resources, with some iron and a smaller amount of Logging Camps for wood. Although several types of plantations can be built, including silk, the total amount of arable land is also low. Without many resources, it's hard to be successful as Israel without joining another market.

**Starting Situation**

Israel does not exist at the start of the game and is not releasable until [Pan-nationalism](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Pan-nationalism) is researched, which is a mid to late game technology. If a player wants to play as Israel, it is best to release them via console command and go from there.

**Grand Strategy**

Israel's position is arguably disadvantageous, between two significant powers ([Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md) and [Egypt](Egypt.md)) that both have claims on its land. It could be advantageous to either gut them as powers or force them to give up their claims.

A good way forward is to become a [Protectorate](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Protectorate) of [Russia](Russia.md) or join their [power bloc](Power bloc.md). Russia's military should scare off most local threats, and Russia has lots of Jews that can immigrate to Israel. This is important, given the territory's low starting population, and it will not be hard to beat Russia's standard of living. Britain and France are also good choices.

Israel does not have much in the way of either resources or arable land, so looking into other areas can become paramount (unless the player's goals are to product dump on the Russian market). Suez is nearby, and there is a lot of relatively weak lands in Africa, Southeast Asia, and Arabia.

**Diplomacy**

Israel does not necessarily need a benefactor, but is much better off with one. The player should actively seek either to become a protectorate or have at least a defensive alliance, given their own weakness.

**Potential**

Israel in Victoria 3 is mostly a nation that is played for the meme value. There are other nations that start nearby that have significantly higher growth potential, or could otherwise make better use of the land.

**Weaknesses**

The land Israel gets starts out weak, poor, underdeveloped, and underpopulated. As such, the nation starts out incredibly weak.
