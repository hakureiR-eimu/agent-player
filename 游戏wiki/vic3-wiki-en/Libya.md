**Libya** is a [formable country](Formable countries.md) in North Africa. Its core states are divided between [Tripolitania](Tripolitania.md), [Fezzan](Fezzan.md), and a few decentralized nations at game start.

Libya can go on to form [Maghreb](Maghreb.md).

## Formation

Libya can be formed by the following countries when they have researched Nationalism, control all three states of Tripoli, Cyrenaica, and Fezzan, and their capital is located in one of these states.

| Country | Capital | Primary Cultures | State Religion | Tier | Type | Core States | Exists at start |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cyrenaica | Cyrenaica | Maghrebi | Sunni | Principality | Unrecognized | Cyrenaica | ![No](/images/thumb/0/04/No.png/24px-No.png) |
| Fezzan | Fezzan | Maghrebi | Sunni | Principality | Unrecognized | Fezzan | ![Yes](/images/thumb/7/77/Yes.png/24px-Yes.png) |
| Tripolitania | Tripoli | Maghrebi | Sunni | Principality | Unrecognized | Tripoli Cyrenaica Fezzan | ![Yes](/images/thumb/7/77/Yes.png/24px-Yes.png) |

All three countries can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country controlling their capital. Libya can also be released by any country controlling its capital.

### Core states

Libya has three core states – Tripoli, Cyrenaica, and Fezzan. Its core states are very resource poor except for oil, lacking even any wood.
