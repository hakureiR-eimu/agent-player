*"Following a long period of de facto independence, Tripolitania has once again fallen under Ottoman rule. Will the Barbary Pirates sail again?"*

**Tripolitania** is an unrecognized power in Northern Africa. It starts the game as a puppet of the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md) and in a war against [Fezzan](Fezzan.md).

Tripolitania can form [Algeria](Algeria.md) and [Maghreb](Maghreb.md).

## Formation

If Tripolitania ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country controlling its capital state of Tripoli. Its core states include Tripoli, Libya, and Libyan Desert.

### Interest groups

Tripolitania starts with extremely powerful Landowners in government. The Armed Forces and Sunni Ulema are in opposition, while the Petite Bourgeoisie, Trade Unions, and Industrialists are marginalized. The Intelligentsia and Rural Folk may each be in opposition or marginalized.

### Industry and Resources

While Tripolitania will have access to oil later in the game, there are no other resources to be used, not even wood. The neighbouring states of Tunis and Constantine, the most likely candidates for expansion, luckily contain a multitude of resources.

Tripolitania has the Peasant Levies law.

Tripolitania starts with 15 Battalions and can call upon 8 Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.

### The Turkish Tanzimat reforms and Independence

The Ottoman [Tanzimat reforms](Turkish content.md) will grant Tripolitania independence after 30 years, if [The Ottomans](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey) fail to make the reforms. There is however not much, if anything, Tripolitania can do to provoke nor prevent it, but it is important to prepare for. Therefore industrial construction has to strike a balance between finding a lucrative niche in the Ottoman market, while also prepare a well rounded industry for eventual and sudden independence.
