**Wales** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in England consisting of a single state – Wales – which, at the start of the game, is owned by [Great Britain](Great Britain.md).

## Core Sates

Wales has a single core state – also named Wales. The state of Wales has access to coal, wood, fish, and the South Wales Valleys state trait, increasing Coal Mines throughput by **10%**.
