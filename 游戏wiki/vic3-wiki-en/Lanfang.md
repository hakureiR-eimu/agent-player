*"The Republic of Lanfang was established by a democratic syndicate of Chinese miners hoping to exploit the gold reserves of Borneo. Can Lanfang resist the advances of the Dutch and achieve the prosperity it seeks?"*

**Lanfang** is a small Chinese unrecognized power located on the coast of West Borneo. It is a tributary of [Great Qing](China.md). It borders the [Dutch East Indies](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Dutch_East_Indies) protectorates of [Pontianak](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Pontianak) and [Sambas](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Sambas) as well as the independent nations [Brunei](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Brunei) and [Sintang](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Sintang).

If Lanfang owns all of Borneo, it is called *Bo-Ni*.

Lanfang is one of the few republics in Asia, and one of very few countries that start with a non-marginalized Trade Unions.

### Interest groups

Due to Lanfang's general lack of historical IG leaders and its
Census Suffrage law, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Lanfang typically starts with the Petite Bourgeoisie as the sole interest group in government. In opposition, the Landowners lead the Conservative Party, they may be joined by the Confucian Scholars, the Armed Forces, or both; the Literati and Industrialists are typically all members of the Liberal Party. The Rural Folk or Trade Unions may be marginalized.

The Confucian Scholars have the Confucian ideology instead of the default Moralist and Pious ideologies. The Literati also lose their default Anti-Clerical ideology.

### Industry and Resources

Lanfang has only three buildings: a port, some barracks, and a single gold fields. The entire productive economy is based on that gold field. With time, more gold fields will be discovered.

The [state trait](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/State_trait) Borneo Rainforest gives a throughput bonus to hardwood.

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.10. |

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Lanfang
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

### Early politics

Lanfang starts with a completely illegitimate government, and a Trade Union-Literati-Industrialist Liberal Party that is happy to swoop into government and start passing reforms. However, expanding past Sintang will mean annexing a significant amount of Manor Houses, which will swing power back to the Landowners and likely destabilize the government

### Gold mining

A lot of Lanfang's early success is based on pure luck, unfortunately: how often more gold is discovered, and how often gold fields run dry. Especially as long as Lanfang only has one or two gold fields. Whenever a gold field runs dry, it should be an immediate priority to build a gold mine to replace it. Prioritizing [production technology](Production technology.md) which improves mining [production methods](Production method.md) is a good idea.

Lanfang has a solid source of income due to the [minting](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Minting) from gold fields and gold mines. In all honesty, it actually has a hard time spending all the money, if it does not create a [construction sector](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Construction_sector). If a construction sector is built, it will however be too expensive to maintain. It can be a good idea to build one temporarily and let it run as long as possible, and then demolish it. Using iron-frame buildings, the turning point where it is useful to build temporary construction sectors is, if you can run it for more than 21 weeks. Once the budget has recovered, which it eventually will do, the construction sector can be rebuilt. This can be done again and again, until Lanfang can actually finance it permanently.

### Diplomacy and conquest

The [Dutch East Indies](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Dutch_East_Indies) especially, and the rest of the European powers, are an existential threat. Being a Chinese protectorate is not an adequate safety measure. Firstly, they might not prioritize coming to your defense at all, secondly they are geographically much farther away than the DEI, and thirdly they have plenty of issues on their own with the [Opium Wars](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Opium_Wars). Immediately, Lanfang should improve relations with Dutch East Indies and [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), followed by [Portugal](Portugal.md), [Netherlands](Netherlands.md) proper, [Spain](Spain.md), and [France](France.md). This is the top priority and all available [influence](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Influence) should go towards this.

In terms of conquest, the two first priorities are [Sintang](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Sintang), immediately followed by [Brunei](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Brunei). Sintang is the same [state](State.md) as Lanfang, and very soon more gold will be discovered in Brunei. They do usually not have anyone to come to their defense, and Lanfang can beat them one-on-one with a little preparation. The risk is that one of them might sell out their independence with the backing of the DEI. Rushing to attack [Sulu](Sulu.md) and [Bulungan](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Bulungan), and [Kutai](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kutai) is advisable, as Kutai will be in a race with the DEI (and to a lesser extent, every Major Power)to conquer these territories. The player can even start the next diplomatic play before one war has ended. Note that Sulu has its capital state on the island of Sulu, and thus will require a navy to annex completely.

Later, one can attack South East Asian nations such as [Siam](Siam.md) or [Aceh](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Aceh). Later, it is possible to match DEI or Spanish [Philippines](Philippines.md), since Lanfang will grow much quicker than them.

### The Chinese market

Unfortunately, [Great Qing](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Great_Qing) has many problems to see to. It is not uncommon to experience long periods, where Lanfang is completely isolated either due to enemies [raiding convoys](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Raiding_convoys) or because China cannot or choose not to finance enough ports. Lanfang are completely insignificant to China (along with all other of China's overseas possessions for that matter), but China is crucial for Lanfang. It is advised to suffer through these periods, simply because the flow of Chinese immigrants (and wares) is simply too important.

When developing the economy, Lanfang should strike a difficult balance between catering to the Chinese [market](Market.md) while securing state-by-state self-sufficiency. Building logging camps and some kind of agriculture early is a priority.
