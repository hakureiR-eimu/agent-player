*"Ruling over both Oman and Zanzibar, the Sultanate is establishing an intercontinental empire. Can Oman remain united?"*

**Oman** is an unrecognized power in the Arabian Peninsula. It starts with ownership of Zanzibar, split state in Kenya, and portions of the Persian coast. It is one of only three playable Ibadi nations, with the others being [Mombasa](Mombasa.md) and [Fezzan](Fezzan.md) (which can convert to Ibadi through a decision.)

As a Bedouin country, Oman can form [Arabia](Arabia.md).

### Interest groups

Due to Oman's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups
in government can vary.
Oman begins with the Landowners in government. The Armed Forces, Petite Bourgeoisie, Rural Folk, Intelligentsia, and Ibadi Ulema are in opposition. The Industrialists and Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Ibadi Ulema have the Imamic and Shura ideologies instead of the default Moralist and Patriarchal.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Oman starts with almost no industry; only a single Shipyards.

**Resources**

Oman lacks most resources. Its only states with significant resources are split states, and so Oman has only a small share of the resources. Zanzibar, however, is able to grow most cash crops and has a large amount of arable land. Hormuz is able to grow both opium and silk, with a small amount of arable land available to Oman.

Oman has the Professional Army law.

Oman starts with 10 Battalions and can call upon three Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.
