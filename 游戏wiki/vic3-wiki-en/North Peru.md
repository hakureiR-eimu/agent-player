**North Peru** is an insignificant power in South America. It starts the game as a puppet of [Bolivia](Bolivia.md). It can form [Peru](Peru.md), and the [Federation of the Andes](Federation of the Andes.md) after Pan-nationalism has been researched.

North Peru is a Confederal State, with President Luís José de Orbegoso supporting the Intelligentsia. North Peru starts with the *Age of Caudillos Age of Caudillos Country modifier +250% Political Strength of Officers +50% Armed Forces Political Strength +10% Armed Forces Pop Attraction* modifier, increasing the political strength of the Armed Forces greatly.

North Peru starts with **20%** of its population as radicals.

If North Peru gets a law that allows for voting in Elections (except for Single-Party State), the law will automatically be amended with Electoral Clientelism (), which increases authority but greatly reduces electoral confidence, representing the deeply entrenched electoral rigging system present in Peruvian politics.

### Interest groups

North Peru begins with the Armed Forces and Intelligentsia in government. The Catholic Church, Landowners, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Rural Folk are in opposition, while the Industrialists and Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Armed Forces have the Caudillismo ideology in addition to their standard ideologies. The Landowners have the Elitist ideology instead of the default Paternalistic. The Landowners also have the Pro-Slavery ideology due to the starting Legacy Slavery.

### Diplomacy

North Peru starts as a puppet of [Bolivia](Bolivia.md). It is bordered by said overlord to the east, by fellow subjects [South Peru](South Peru.md) and [Iquicha](Iquicha.md) to the south, and [Ecuador](Ecuador.md) to the north - with whom it shares the split state of Pastaza.

North Peru has poor relations with [Spain](Spain.md), [New Granada](Colombia.md) and its overlord [Bolivia](Bolivia.md). North Peru also starts with the modifier *Independence Not Recognized by Spain Independence Not Recognized by Spain Country modifier −10% Influence*, until [Spain](Spain.md) recognizes its independence, or North Peru becomes strong enough to disregard Spain.

North Peru has a anti-overlord lobby towards [Bolivia](Bolivia.md) ().
