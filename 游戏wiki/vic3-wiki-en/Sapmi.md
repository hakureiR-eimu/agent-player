**Sámi** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in northern Europe. It can be released by any country which controls its capital. At the start of the game, its core states are split between [Norway](Norway.md), [Sweden](Sweden.md), [Finland](Finland.md), and [Russia](Russia.md).
