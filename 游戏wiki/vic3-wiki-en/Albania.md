**Albania** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in the Balkans consisting of a single state – Albania – which, at the start of the game, is owned by the [Ottoman Empire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey). Albania borders the Adriatic sea to the west and the Ottoman Empire to the east, north, and south.

## Historical background

After Skanderbeg of Albania died and the Ottomans finally completed their conquest, Albania was under Ottoman rule for 400 years, from the late 15th century up until 1912.

## Core states

Albania has a single core state – also named Albania. The state of Albania has access to iron and the Terra Rossa state trait, increasing the agricultural throughput by **15%**.
