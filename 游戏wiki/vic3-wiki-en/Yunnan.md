**Yunnan** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in South China. It may be released by a country that controls its capital. At the start date, all of its states are controlled by [China](China.md).

If [China](China.md) fails the [Fragile Unity](Chinese content.md) journal entry, Yunnan is released in Yunnan as a warlord state. In that case, they are given Han as an additional primary culture, and can thus potentially reform China.
