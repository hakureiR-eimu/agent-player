**Occitania** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in Europe. It may be released by any country that controls its capital or revolt as the cultural seccesion country of the Occitans. The vast majority is controlled by [France](France.md), while the province of Nice (state of Provence) is owned by [Sardinia-Piedmont](Sardinia-Piedmont.md).

Occitania can unlike [France](France.md) **not** use the [Treaty of Turin](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Treaty_of_Turin) to get Nice from [Sardinia-Piedmont](Sardinia-Piedmont.md) and must instead take it in war.
