*"Great Britain has roared into the 19th Century, rapidly becoming the world's first industrial nation. The massive empire is kept afloat by the labor of colonies across the globe. However, King William IV is close to death and who knows what sort of era the next monarch will usher in."*

**Great Britain**, formally *the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Ireland*, is a great power in northern Europe. Great Britain begins as the most industrialized nation in 1836, and has an extensive colonial empire, with subjects and colonies on every continent.

Near the start of the game, the *Hanoverian Succession*, *South Atlantic Slave Trade* and the *Opium Wars* are issues which confront Britain.

Great Britain is one of four recommended countries for the *Economic Dominance* objective, along with the [USA](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/USA), [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md), and [Brazil](Brazil.md); and one of six eligible countries for the *Great Game* objective, the others being [Russia](Russia.md), [Persia](Persia.md), and the Afghan minors of [Kabul](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kabul), [Herat](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Herat), and [Kandahar](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kandahar).

## Historical background

Having led the victorious coalition against Napoleon 20 years prior and being the premier industrial economy on the planet, Great Britain stands as the greatest power in 1836. Great Britain's overseas empire is extensive, owning Malta, much of the Caribbean, Belize, Guyana, the South Atlantic Islands, Newfoundland and all of Ireland. In addition to this, Great Britain has numerous subjects. In Africa, ![Flag GBR uk.png](/images/thumb/6/6e/Flag_GBR_uk.png/24px-Flag_GBR_uk.png) [Sierra Leone](Sierra Leone.md) and the ![Flag GBR uk.png](/images/thumb/6/6e/Flag_GBR_uk.png/24px-Flag_GBR_uk.png) [Cape Colony](South Africa.md) are colonies of Britain and [Canada](Canada.md) and [Australia](Australia.md) are owned by various British subjects.

In Germany, [Hanover](Hanover.md) has been under a personal union with Great Britain since 1714, when the Prince-Electors of Hanover inherited the British throne as distant cousins of Queen Anne. However, this union is due to end due to the differences in laws of succession, with Princess Alexandrina Victoria of Kent first in line for the British throne, while the Duke of Cumberland is due to become the next King of Hanover.

The jewel in the British crown, however, is the ![Flag GBR uk.png](/images/thumb/6/6e/Flag_GBR_uk.png/24px-Flag_GBR_uk.png) [British East India Company](East India.md), which in turn has numerous princely states vassals, keeping most of the Indian subcontinent in the British market, though the [Sikh Empire](Punjab.md), [Kalat](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kalat), [Makran](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Makran), and [Sindh](Sindh.md) remain fully independent at game start.

## Formation

Eight of the 11 core states are needed in order to form Great Britain.
Great Britain already exists at the start of the game. If it ceases to exist, it can be [formed](Formable countries.md) by a country with English or Scottish as a primary culture when it controls 8 of the 11 core states. Alternatively, Great Britain can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country which controls Home Counties and five other core states.

## Politics

Great Britain starts with HM's Government, a semi-constitutional monarchy with King William (IV) of Hanover supporting the Petite Bourgeoisie. Great Britain starts with the modifier *Unfulfilled Treaty Obligations Unfulfilled Treaty Obligations Country modifier −10% Influence −100 Prestige*, which lasts for 50 years. It is one of only four countries, together with [France](France.md), [Montenegro](Montenegro.md) and [Portugal](Portugal.md) to start with any kind of Social Security.

### Interest groups

Due to Great Britain's
Wealth Voting law, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Great Britain typically starts with the Petite Bourgeoisie as well as Intelligentsia and the Industrialists in government as the Whig Party. In opposition, the Landed Gentry, Anglican Church, and Armed Forces make up the Tory Party. If the Industrialists are in opposition, they typically form the Peelite Party. The Rural Folk and Trade Unions may each be marginalized or in opposition. Great Britain is one of very few countries that can start with a non-marginalized Trade Unions.

The Intelligentsia have the Constitutionalist ideology instead of the default Republican, which makes them endorse Monarchy, and the Landed Gentry have Magnatial instead of the default Paternalistic, which makes them less reactionary overall and notably has them support Protectionism.

### Diplomacy

Great Britain starts with a mutual rivalry with [Russia](Russia.md). It is owed an obligation by [Greece](Greece.md), and has a truce with [Ashanti](Ashanti.md) in West Africa until 1 July 1836.

Britain starts with the *Treaty of the Quadruple Alliance* with [Spain](Spain.md) and [Portugal](Portugal.md), binding until 1844.4.22. With Portugal, the treaty gives a Defensive Pact, Trade Privileges for both parties, one-sided Investment Rights Investment Rights for Britain, Military Assistance to Portugal and has the Portuguese transfer 20 units of wine. With Spain, it provides Military Assistance to the Spanish and also establishes a mutual Defensive Pact.

Britain is guaranteeing the independence of the [Maldives](Maldives.md) through the *Treaty of Canterbury*, which is binding until 1841.5.3. It also has Investment Rights in and mutual Trade Privileges with [Belgium](Belgium.md) through the *Treaty of Reciprocity*, binding until 1846.8.12. Lastly, there are identical, 10-year long treaties with each of [Potchefstroom](Transvaal.md), [Winburg](Oranje.md), [Waterboersland](Griqualand.md), and [Philippolis](Philippolis.md) in South Africa, which guarantee each country's independence and also give them all Transit Rights, to allow them access to the world market.

Great Britain has many subjects - most of which are Colonies, - including the [British East India Company](East India.md), the ![Flag GBR uk.png](/images/thumb/6/6e/Flag_GBR_uk.png/24px-Flag_GBR_uk.png) [Cape Colony](South Africa.md), [Hanover](Hanover.md) (which is in Personal Union), the [Ionian Islands](Ionian Islands.md), the [Trucial States](Trucial States.md), the [Miskito Kingdom](Miskitia.md), ![Flag GBR uk.png](/images/thumb/6/6e/Flag_GBR_uk.png/24px-Flag_GBR_uk.png) [British West Africa](Sierra Leone.md), ![Flag GBR uk.png](/images/thumb/6/6e/Flag_GBR_uk.png/24px-Flag_GBR_uk.png) [Ceylon](Ceylon.md), and the colonies of ![Flag GBR uk.png](/images/thumb/6/6e/Flag_GBR_uk.png/24px-Flag_GBR_uk.png) [Canada](Canada.md) and ![Flag GBR uk.png](/images/thumb/6/6e/Flag_GBR_uk.png/24px-Flag_GBR_uk.png) [Australia](Australia.md), which are split into many smaller autonomous regions. Britain also has directly owned colonies and overseas territories in Malta, Gibraltar, Guyana, Newfoundland, the Caribbean, West Africa, and a few other regions.

Great Britain's starting interests are hegemonic (level 5) in Western Europe; pervasive (level 4) in Canada, North India, South India, and Southern Europe; influential (level 3) in Atlantic Coast and Oceania; engaged (level 2) in Central America, Central Europe, East Africa, Gran Colombia, Indochina, La Plata, Northern Europe, Pacific Coast, and Southern Africa; and observant (level 1) in Arabia, Balkans, Eastern Europe, Russia, Siberia, and West Africa.

Great Britain has both an anti-country lobby () and a pro-country lobby () towards [France](France.md), as well as an anti-country lobby towards its rival [Russia](Russia.md) ().

Great Britain starts as the leader of the *British Empire* power bloc. All members are subjects of Great Britain. It starts with the principles Vassalization I and Colonial Offices II.

## Strategy and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Great Britain
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
Britain starts the game in an excellent position, and is able to further entrench itself as the number one great power.

### Cash flow and tolls

With its armies and navies, and the Military Assistance clauses in its treaties with [Portugal](Portugal.md) and [Spain](Spain.md), Great Britain faces a large maintenance bill. Consider removing these clauses via negotiations first.

With the introduction of tolls, the lucrative Gibraltar toll makes Spanish Al-Rif a worthy target; be sure to break or otherwise remove the starting defensive pact (*Treaty of the Quadruple Alliance*) via negotiations first. Unlike other tolls, the Gibraltar toll can only be collected when one power controls *both* ends. Be sure to have enough troops and supply ships on hand to defend Gibraltar and capture Al-Rif. The Sound toll controlled by [Denmark](Denmark.md) and [Sweden](Sweden.md) is also worth considering.

### Opium Wars

This journal entry will complete once Great Britain has a treaty port in Qing territory. Naval invasions targeting Beijing and its surrounding states, and having a border with China (e.g. by annexing Sagaing state from [Burma](Burma.md)) so that [East India](East India.md) will be able to contribute troops, should bring Qing to its knees. Qing's military will get a lot of negative modifiers due to Opium addiction.

### The Brazilian slave trade

|  | Available only with the [Voice of the People](Voice of the People.md) DLC enabled. |
|---|---|

Despite the generous timer, it is probably best to strike at [Brazil](Brazil.md) while it's tied down with its civil wars. Besides banning slavery, securing other concessions can be considered. Liberating nations from Brazil to weaken it permanently can also be considered.

### Consolidating subjects

Great Britain starts with many subjects and consolidation is necessary so that influence usage can be reduced. Form [Australia](Australia.md) and [Canada](Canada.md) when the opportunity arises.

Consider annexing [Cape Colony](South Africa.md) to colonize Northern Cape and gain access to the Boer republics.

Note that with so many subjects, it is easy for subjects' liberty desire to grow out of control, especially after annexing multiple subjects. Be prepared for wars against multiple nations when trying to annex subjects, and other powers (even great powers) jumping in to support subjects' independence.

### The Great Game

|  | Available only with the [Sphere of Influence](Sphere of Influence.md) DLC enabled. |
|---|---|

Britain should enlist [East India](East India.md)'s help for the Great Game, by establishing a land connection from East India to the Central Asian and Persian regions. Britain should also prevent Russia from occupying states around the Persian Gulf and Arabian Sea.

Also, consider helping [Circassia](Circassia.md) against Russia; this means creating armies which can beat Russia on land. If Britain can establish a land connection between Circassia and Dagestan, it can become a thorn in Russia's side; causing Russia to fail its journal entry in the Caucasus helps Britain to secure victory in the Great Game.
