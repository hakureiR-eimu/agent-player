**Grão-Pará** is an insignificant power in South America. It borders [Colombia](Colombia.md) and [Venezuela](Venezuela.md) to the northwest, [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), the [Netherlands](Netherlands.md) and [France](France.md) through their colonial possessions to the north, [Peru](Peru.md) and [Bolivia](Bolivia.md) to the southwest, and [Brazil](Brazil.md) to the southeast.

Grão-Pará starts at war with Brazil, where the Brazilian war goal is to annex Grão-Pará. This makes them one of the most difficult starts in the game.

## Formation

When Grão-Pará ceases to exist, as it usually will shortly into the game, it can be released by a country controlling its capital state of Pará. In addition to Pará, Grão-Pará's cores include Amazonas and Acre.

### Interest groups

Due to Grão-Pará's general lack of historical IG leaders and its
Wealth Voting law, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Grão-Pará typically begins with the Intelligentsia and Catholic Church in government. The Landowners, Armed Forces, and Rural Folk are typically in opposition, while the Industrialists, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Trade Unions are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Grão-Pará starts at war with [Brazil](Brazil.md). Brazil annexes Grão-Pará if it wins the war; while Grão-Pará conquers the state Maranhão and 25% of pops become more loyal if it wins.

## Military

Grão-Pará has the Peasant Levies law.

Grão-Pará starts with 12 Battalions and can raise up to 4 Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.

|---|---|---|---|
