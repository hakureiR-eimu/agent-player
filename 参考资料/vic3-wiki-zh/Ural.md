**Ural** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in Eastern Europe. It may be released by any country that controls its capital. At the start date, [Russia](Russia.md) controls most of Ural's core states, except for a split state in Chelyabinsk controlled by [Orta Zhuz](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Orta_Zhuz).

## 历史背景

Ural regionalism, the idea of an Ural autonomy, emerged in the second half of the XIX century. As most of the region's population and the intelligentsia considering the idea were Russian, it was driven more by geographic and economic rather than ethnic factors.

During World War I, the [Provisional Regional Government of the Urals](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provisional_Regional_Government_of_the_Urals) was briefly established in the region as the Russian Republic collapsed. After the fall of the Soviet Union, the idea was once again revived, and an attempt to establish the [Ural Republic](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ural_Republic) within the boundaries of the Sverdlovsk Oblast was made. In-game, the default flag is the proposed white-green-black of this Ural Republic, while the red and green flag of the World War I provisional government is used if the country is a Monarchy or a dictatorial/oligarchic republic.
