**俾路支斯坦** 是  small [可成立国家](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) 位于 中亚. It may be 成立 by  俾路支 国家 低于 王国 等级 that fully controls  州  俾路支斯坦.

它可以继续成立[巴基斯坦](Pakistan.md)和[印度](India.md)。

## 历史背景

In 1836, the region of Baluchistan was ruled by various tribes which had often been tributaries to neighboring empires. Kalat was the most powerful of these, having gained influence over the other Baluchi tribes. Kalat had been subject to the Afghan [Durrani Empire](Afghanistan.md), but gained its independence as the empire declined.

In 1839, Baluchistan came under [British](Great Britain.md) influence during the First Anglo-Afghan War, with Kalat and the other Baluchi states becoming princely states. British influence increased over the following century, lasting until the partition of the British Raj, when the Baluchi princely states chose to accede to [Pakistan](Pakistan.md).

[British East India](East India.md) has claims on Baluchistan, which could potentially end the game early. Especially for Kalat, getting a second state without EIC claims is therefore paramount as the very first thing to do. Somewhere in [Afghanistan](Afghanistan.md) is the most obvious. As EIC has a lot of claims, and they don't immediately share a border, hopefully they start by attacking someone else to give a little time.

### Forming 俾路支斯坦

Baluchistan can be formed by controlling just 1 state out of Baluchistan, Northern Baluchistan or Bambur and researching Nationalism. Baluchistan is a split state between Makran, Kalat, [Kandahar](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kandahar) and [Oman](Oman.md), while Bambur is split between Makran, Kandahar and Oman. As Oman is a dangerous opponent, the most efficient way is to annex Northern Baluchistan from [Kandahar](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kandahar). Neither Kalat or Makran has claims on the unowned parts of the states, but forming Baluchistan, will provide them with such. It will also annex the other half.

Initially Kandahar has a more powerful military, but there should be enough countries willing to help. Especially [Kabul](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kabul) is helpful.
