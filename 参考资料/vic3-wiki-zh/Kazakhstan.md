**哈萨克斯坦**是中亚的一个[可成立国家](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country)。任何以哈萨克为主文化的国家均可成立它。

哈萨克斯坦可以继续成立[突厥斯坦](Turkestan.md)。

All three of the Zhuz countries begin as protectorates of [Russia](Russia.md) and none of them are releasable. It is possible for one of them to remain a subject and still acquire Semipalatinsk, Aktobe, and Syrdarya from the other two Zhuzes and [Kokand](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kokand) and form Kazakhstan. It is also possible to acquire Ili from [China](China.md), or even conquer the Russian parts of Akmolinsk and Uralsk during a successful independence war.

哈萨克斯坦也可被释放，此时其全部八个哈萨克家园均视为其核心州。

### 核心州

哈萨克斯坦拥有六个核心州，涵盖其主流文化的大部分家园。其核心州大致对应于现代的哈萨克斯坦。

Kazakhstan's core states are very resource poor, with a small amount of coal and iron, and only eventually gaining a sizable amount of oil in Uralsk. It also has no agriculture; its arable land is only suitable for ranching.

Forming Kazakhstan grants claims on all unowned Kazakh homelands, which include Chorasmia and Uzbekia, which each have more agricultural potential, including opium.

## 脚本内容

Kazakhstan can be formed by the [Russian](Russia.md) content [Pacification of the Steppes](Russian content.md), which is also likely to result in the annexation of all three Kazakh states into Russia. Note that if it exists, Uly Zhuz will always form Kazakhstan, meaning that if the player plays as Kishi Zhuz or Orta Zhuz, they will switch to Uly Zhuz (Kazakhstan) if Russia fails the *Pacification of the Steppes* journal entry and Kishi Zhuz or Orta Zhuz previously had at least 60 liberty desire (resulting in them being annexed by Kazakhstan).

The Kazakh minors are all unrecognized powers and have similar basic stats, starting technology, and politics. Each starts with only one or more states in Central Asia. They are all Principalities and Sunni. Most often they all start with the Landowners in government with a mix of the Armed Forces, Rural Folk, and Sunni Ulema in opposition, and the remaining interest groups marginalized.
