**补丁 1.11**，别名"Chamomile"（洋甘菊），于 2025-10-16 发布[1]，校验和为 *3360*。它与音乐包[Songs of the Homeland](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Songs_of_the_Homeland)（故乡之歌）同步推出。

## Songs of the Homeland 特色内容

11 首新音乐曲目：

1. *A New Day Awakens*
2. *Walking Through Mist*
3. *Approaching Tempest*
4. *The Secret Glade*
5. *Old Ruins in the Forest*
6. *Sicilienne*
7. *Dance of the Shepherds*
8. *A Nap in the Fields*
9. *The Pride of People*
10. *Ode to the Night*
11. *Evening Lights*
