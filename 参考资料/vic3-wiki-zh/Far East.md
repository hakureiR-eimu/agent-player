**Far East** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in Northeastern Asia. It may be released by a country controlling its capital. At the start date, its core states are split between [Russia](Russia.md), [China](China.md) and a few decentralized nations in Sakhalin.

As an Inuit nation, Far East can potentially form [Turtle Island](Turtle Island.md), although it would need to move its capital to North America.
