**United Netherlands** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) on the coast of the Rhine region. It represents a renewed union of the [Netherlands](Netherlands.md) and [Belgium](Belgium.md), which separated in 1830 as a result of the Belgian Revolution.

United Netherlands can form [Central Europe](Central Europe.md).[1]

## Historical background

The Netherlands – literally meaning *the low countries* – have been united and divided several times through history. In the Renaissance era, the region was mostly united under the Duchy of Burgundy, with the main exception of the Prince-Bishopric of Liège. After the line of Burgundy died out, the low countries were inherited by the Habsburgs, who ruled it first from [Austria](Austria.md), then from [Spain](Spain.md).

Tensions between the Netherlanders and the Spanish crown resulted in rebellion in the late 1500s. For a time, the low countries were united in rebellion, but dissent between the mostly Calvinist north and mostly Catholic south resulted in only the northern low countries declaring independence as the Dutch Republic. From then, the low countries were divided into Spanish (eventually Austrian again) and Dutch realms.

During the Napoleonic Wars, the low countries came under the control of [France](France.md). Following Napoleon's final defeat in 1815, the low countries were reconstituted as the [United Kingdom of the Netherlands](Netherlands.md). However, this united kingdom only lasted 15 years until the Belgian Revolution divided the region once more. [Luxembourg](Luxembourg.md) remained under the Kingdom of the Netherlands until 1890 when its king died without a male heir and the differing inheritance laws ended the personal union.

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
United Netherlands
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

### Starting as the Netherlands

Although Belgium might be stronger economically, the [Netherlands](Netherlands.md) has at least one major advantage to forming the United Netherlands: their claims on Flanders and Wallonia. Despite this, it will be difficult to make good on these claims. Great powers like France, Prussia and Great Britain will guarantee Belgium's independence after the Treaty of London is confirmed, and may defend them if one attacks too early. In order to avoid a potentially catastrophic early-game war, one can follow a colonial strategy. Other important things to consider are improving relations with France, Great Britain, and Prussia, as they may be helpful allies, depending on the way that diplomacy across Europe develops, and will surely be terrible enemies if one is not careful.

### Colonisation

**Indonesia**

In Indonesia, it is relatively easy to subjugate most of the small nations, and even to transfer them to the [East Indies](East Indies.md) without much infamy gain.

**West Africa**

If one makes early moves to colonize in the Senegal and Niger strategic regions, it may be possible to secure rubber production, as well as other resources like iron, which may be hard to otherwise get without trade before conquering Belgium.

**South Africa**

Like in Indonesia, it is straightforward to conquer [Zululand](Zulu.md), and even to make protectorates out of [Oranje](Oranje.md) and [Transvaal](Transvaal.md). Another option would be to conquer Oranje and Transvaal in order to get the gold which will appear in their states.

**Guyana**

If one is willing to trade colonized states with Great Britain and France, then it is straightforward to acquire the whole of Guyana, and benefit from the gold rushes there.

### Invading Belgium

After developing relationships with Great Britain and/or France, they may be less willing to aid Belgium, but Great Britain may get a customs union or defensive pact with Belgium, in which case one either needs a very strong ally or enough patience to wait until it ends. Before invading, it is wise to increase bureaucracy generation to avoid going past one's limit, as Flanders and Wallonia cost a lot of bureaucracy to integrate because of large numbers of pops. Another difficulty in integrating them will be economic. It is likely that Belgium will have a highly developed construction industry and one may need to downsize some of those buildings to avoid a debt spiral. Additionally, many adjustments may need to be made to trade and production methods, as the economic situations of the Netherlands and Belgium are quite different. However, if one can successfully integrate Flanders and Wallonia, the possibilities for industrialization are enormous.

### France's natural borders

|  | Available only with the [Voice of the People](Voice of the People.md) DLC enabled. |
|---|---|

If France activates the [journal entry](French content.md), it will receive claims on Flanders and Wallonia. Needless to say, this makes France extremely dangerous. It is advised to seek an ally or defensive pact with a GP rival of France (usually [Great Britain](Great Britain.md)).
