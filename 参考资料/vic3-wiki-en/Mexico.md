**Mexico** is an minor power in North America. It neighbors the [USA](America.md), [British Columbia](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/British_Columbia) and [Texas](Texas.md) as well as several [decentralized](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Decentralized) Native American nations to the north and [USCA](Central America.md) and [British Belize](Great Britain.md) to the south. At the beginning of the game, Mexico starts at war with Texas, which has declared independence.

It is called *Mexican Empire* if it is a Monarchy, and *New Spain* if it is a subject of [Spain](Spain.md).

Mexico is one of four recommended countries for the *Egalitarian Society* [objective](Objective.md), along with [France](France.md), [Russia](Russia.md), and [Great Qing](China.md).

## Historical background

Mexico declared independence from [Spain](Spain.md) in 1821, 15 years before the start of the game. The newly founded country immediately found it self in turmoil, and did not have actual control over all the territory it claimed. On 2nd October 1835, Texas declared independence and thus started the war that plagues Mexico at the beginning of the game.

## Formation

13 of the 17 core states are needed in order to form Mexico
If Mexico ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country which controls its capital. It can also be [formed](Formable countries.md) by any country with Mexican primary culture below kingdom tier that controls 13 of its 17 core states, which are the Mexican homelands. See the states section below for details on the core states.

The following countries are capable of forming Mexico, none of them exist at the start of the game.

Mexico's initial government form is a *Junta*; a presidential republic with the Oligarchy distribution of power. The Armed Forces and Landowners are in government, with General[1] Miguel Barragán supporting the Armed Forces. Mexico starts with the *Age of Caudillos Age of Caudillos Country modifier +250% Political Strength of Officers +50% Armed Forces Political Strength +10% Armed Forces Pop Attraction* modifier, increasing the political strength of the Armed Forces greatly.

### Interest groups

Mexico begins with the Landowners and Armed Forces in government. The Catholic Church, Rural Folk, Intelligentsia, and Petite Bourgeoisie are in opposition. The Industrialists, and Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Armed Forces have the Caudillismo ideology in addition to their standard ideologies. The Landowners have the Elitist ideology instead of the default Paternalistic.

### Diplomacy

Mexico begins at war with [Texas](Texas.md). Winning the war annexes Texas, while losing cedes Mexico's part of the state of Texas. If Mexico loses a battle to Texas while General Santa Anna is commanding, the war immediately ends in a white peace by event.

Mexico has poor relations with [Spain](Spain.md) and cold relations with [USA](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/USA). Spain however is often conciliatory. Mexico also starts with the modifier *Independence Not Recognized by Spain Independence Not Recognized by Spain Country modifier −10% Influence*, until [Spain](Spain.md) recognizes its independence, or Mexico becomes strong enough to disregard Spain.

Mexico starts with ongoing colonization of several Native American decentralized nations.

The Mexican states give automatic interests in the Pacific Coast, Mexico, Dixie and Central America. Mexico has one available interest, which it uses to maintain an interest in the Great Plains strategic region. The interest is needed to maintain its starting colonization effort.

Mexico starts with a pro-country lobby towards [France](France.md) () and an anti-country lobby towards the [USA](America.md) ().

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Mexico produces mainly agricultural products, but there are a few clothing, furniture and food industries mostly centralized in its capital state.

**Resources**

There are almost no mines at the start, but Mexico has access to iron, coal, sulfur, and lead mines in many states. In many states, a wide range of agricultural buildings are available including dye, cotton, fruit, coffee, and tobacco. In the state of Bajío, there are three gold mines, with the immediate potential of building a fourth. Many Mexican states have discoverable gold, rubber, or oil. Sinaloa is the only state in North America where opium can be grown.

Mexico

### The war with Texas

On paper, the war with Texas should be easily winnable. Texas has a meager five battalions. The issue is that an [event](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Event) can see [General Antonio López de Santa Anna](Texas.md) captured by Texan troops, [as happened in real life](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/en:Texas_Revolution#Mexican_retreat_and_surrender)[wp 1] if a battle involving him is lost. This means losing the war automatically in a white peace. One does not have to make haste, but instead should make sure to stabilize the country and prepare properly. One sure way to avoid the event is to change the commanders in the starting armies to only use Juan Alvaréz.

### First production

Mexico starts with only 7 construction produced weekly, an amount which is woefully inadequate for a country of Mexico's size. In Bajío, it is possible to build a Gold Mine, an obvious choice for a first building.

Alternatively, Mexico starts with a shortage of tools, which should be addressed shortly, either by building a tooling workshop or via trade. Soon, wood, iron, paper and small arms should also be addressed to lessen the government expenses and facilitate more construction.

### United States of Central America

To the south of Mexico is [United States of Central America](Central America.md) (USCA). Like they did historically, it is very likely that they will soon fall apart into [Guatemala](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Guatemala), [Costa Rica](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Costa_Rica), [El Salvador](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/El_Salvador), [Honduras](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Honduras) and [Nicaragua](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Nicaragua).

They provide an obvious choice of conquest, but it is in general advised to wait until they fall apart. If you as an example choose to make USCA itself a protectorate, they might then disintegrate during the diplomatic play, which can leave you with nothing. When it comes to war, either before or after disintegration, they will pose no issues. The only potential problem would be either UK or USA intervening.

### Further targets

After USCA is fully annexed, [Colombia](Colombia.md)n Panama is next on the list.

Alternatively, East Asia or [Haiti](Haiti.md) can be attacked. They do however require that Mexico invests in a functional navy; an investment that might not be worth it early in the game. However, much of the Hispanosphere is accessible only with naval help.

### The full potential of Mexico

Mexico has a large potential. Very large. The country is huge; it starts with a sizeable population, which can potentially grow to dizzying heights with immigration. There are many resources and lots of arable land. Soon, many provinces will produce large amounts of gold, and more or less every industrial resource is available.

Becoming the hegemon in North America is not out of the question. And becoming the hegemon in North America means becoming the Hegemon in the world.

### The United States of America

Without a doubt, the [United States](America.md) is the number one threat to Mexico, bar none. With *Manifest Destiny*, the US will have many claims on Mexican territory. Combined with the initial tech and manpower superiority, Mexico must be defensive, until she secures military superiority or the United States descends into civil war. Securing defensive pacts or alliances with America's GP rivals will help immensely. *Manifest Destiny* will activate if the US has an interest in the Pacific Coast or has colonized 40% of the Great Plains.
