**Chechnya** is a unrecognized power in the Caucasus mountains. It starts as the theocratic *Caucasian Imamate* in the middle of the Gazawat against [Russia](Russia.md).

## Formation

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.11. |
|---|---|

If Chechnya ceases to exist, it can be released by any country that controls its capital and sole core state – North Caucasus. When released, it has only Chechen as a primary culture.

### Interest groups

Chechnya begins with the Landowners and Sunni Ulema in government. The Armed Forces, Rural Folk, and Intelligentsia are in opposition. The Petite Bourgeoisie, Trade Unions, and Industrialists are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Chechnya starts with a mutual truce with [Russia](Russia.md) until 1856.

Chechnya has claims on Dagestan and North Caucasus.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.11. |
|---|---|

Chechnya has the National Militia law.

Chechnya starts with 10 Battalions and can call upon no Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose two armies.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| Milishia | 6 Irregular Infantry | Shamil Murid | Caucasus |
| Murtazeki | 3 Irregular Infantry 1 Hussars | Hadji Murad | Caucasus |

Chechnya starts with no Flotillas, as it is landlocked.
