**Ideologies** determine what stance an [interest group](Interest group.md) or [political movement](Political movement.md) has on various [laws](Laws.md). Each interest group has a set three or four standard ideologies. Political movements each have a single [ideology](Movement ideology.md). In certain circumstances, interest groups may also have other ideologies in addition or instead of these standard ideologies. [Characters](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Characters) also have their own [ideology](Character ideology.md), and the ideology of an interest group's leader influences that group's stances.

### Non-starting ideologies

The following ideologies – except Pro-Slavery (and its colonial variant) and Mercantile – are not present at the start of the game, but are standard for certain interest groups given certain conditions.

- Corporatist ideology replaces Pious for the Devout when Corporatism is researched.[1]
- Egalitarian () and Liberal () ideologies gain new stances on the *Rights of Women* laws when Feminism is researched.[2]
- Pro-Slavery ideology is added to the Landowners if [slavery](Human rights laws.md) of any kind is enacted, and it is removed from all interest groups some time after it is banned.[3]
- Pro-Slavery (colonial) ideology is added to the Landowners in countries that start with Colonial Slavery, instead. It is removed if any other law is enacted, immediately for other kinds of slavery, or after some time if it is banned.[3]
- Socialist ideology is added to the Trade Unions when Socialism is researched.[4]
- Mercantile is added to the Petite Bourgeoisie if either Traditionalism *or* Isolationism is enacted at game start. It remains even after these laws are changed[5]
  - The only exception is [Japan](Japan.md), whose Petite Bourgeoisie lose Mercantile through the event *The Common Citizen*.

## Leader ideologies

Main article:
Character ideology
All [characters](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Characters) have an individual ideology. When a character leads an interest group, this ideology adds to – and may override – that interest group's default stances. The ruler's ideology also provides support or opposition to law changes.

## Movement ideologies

Main article:
Movement ideology
See also:
Political movement
Each type of movement has an ideology which determines its goals. Most movement ideologies are similar or identical to a regular interest group or character ideology.

## Event ideologies

See also:
Ideology content
Event ideologies come into effect due to a journal entry or event chain. Some event chains add or remove standard ideologies from interest groups that normally would not have that ideology.

**Call for Women's Suffrage**

This [journal entry](Ideology content.md) can begin after researching Feminism and Political Agitation. When completed, it has an option to add the Feminist ideology to the Intelligentsia, or replace Patriarchal ideology with Modern Patriarchal on a random interest group, depending on which interest groups are in government at that time.
