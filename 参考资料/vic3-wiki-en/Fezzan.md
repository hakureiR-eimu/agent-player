**Fezzan** is an unrecognized power in Northern Africa. At the start of the game, it is waging an independence war against [Tripolitania](Tripolitania.md).

Fezzan can form either [Libya](Libya.md) or [Algeria](Algeria.md), as well as [Maghreb](Maghreb.md).

## Formation

If Fezzan ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country controlling its capital state of Fezzan.

### Interest groups

Tripolitania starts with extremely powerful Landowners in government. The Armed Forces and Sunni Ulema are in opposition, while the Petite Bourgeoisie, Trade Unions, Industrialists, Intelligentsia and Rural Folk are marginalized.

### Industry and Resources

Like [Tripolitania](Tripolitania.md), Fezzan will have access to oil later in the game, but there are no other resources to be used. The neighbouring states of Tunis and Constantine, the most likely candidates for expansion and coastline access, luckily contain a multitude of resources.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.12. |

Tripolitania has the Peasant Levies law.

Fezzan starts with 11 Battalions and can call upon no Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose two armies.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| Al-Nasir’s Forces | 7 Irregular Infantry | Abd al-Jalil al-Nasir | North Africa |
| Ghuma’s Forces | 4 Irregular Infantry | Ghuma al-Mahmudi | North Africa |

Fezzan has no flotillas, as it is landlocked.
