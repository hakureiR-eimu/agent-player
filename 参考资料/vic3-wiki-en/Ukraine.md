**Ukraine** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in Eastern Europe. It may be released by a country that controls its capital and two other core states. At the start date, [Russia](Russia.md) is the only eligible country, controlling most of Ukrainian core states.

If Ukraine has enacted the Council Republic and Anarchy laws, it is called **Free Territory**.
