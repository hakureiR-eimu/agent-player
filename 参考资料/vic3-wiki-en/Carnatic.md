**Carnatic** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in southern India. It represents a restored Carnatic Sultanate.

Carnatic can form [Dravida Nadu](Dravida Nadu.md) and [India](India.md) (BHT).

## Historical context

The Carnatic Sultanate emerged as an independent state from the decline of the Mughal Empire at the beginning of the 18th century. It soon came into conflict with the Maratha Confederacy and [Hyderabad](Hyderabad.md). By the mid-1700s, [France](France.md) and [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) became involved, supporting rivals to the Carnatic throne. The British candidate succeeded to the throne and Carnatic became a British ally during the later Anglo-Mysore wars.

In 1801, the East India Company compelled the Carnatic Nawab to sign over administration of the Sultanate, effectively annexing it.

Pudukottai is the only country that can form Carnatic. It is an unrecognized power and a puppet of [East India](East India.md).

Pudukottai generally starts with the Zamindars in government with the Hindu Priesthood, Armed Forces, Intelligentsia, and Ryots in opposition, and the remaining interest groups marginalized.

The Hindu Priesthood has the Hindu Moralist ideology instead of the default Moralist. The Petite Bourgeoisie has the Modernizer ideology instead of the default Reactionary. The Intelligentsia has the Constitutionalist ideology instead of the default Republican.

Pudukottai is ruled by Raja Raghunatha Tondaiman. Pudukottai starts with a split state in Carnatic.
