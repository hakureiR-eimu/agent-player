**Gran Colombia** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in South America. Historically, it existed briefly from Simón Bolívar's victory over [Spanish](Spain.md) forces near Bogotá in 1819 until 1830, when it broke up into [Ecuador](Ecuador.md), [New Granada](Colombia.md) (Colombia) and [Venezuela](Venezuela.md).

Gran Colombia can continue on to form [Federation of the Andes](Federation of the Andes.md).

## Historical background

Gran Colombia – officially the *Republic of Colombia* – was proclaimed in 1819 following Simón Bolívar's victory over Spain in the same year during the Spanish American wars of independence. While much of the claimed territory was initially controlled by Spain, Bolívar and the other revolutionaries were able to capture these territories and achieve international recognition by the mid 1820s.

Following the end of the war, politics in Colombia fell to infighting, with those seeking a stronger central government supporting, Bolívar, the president, and proponents of a federal system coalescing around Francisco de Paula Santander, the vice-president. In 1830, the divisions grew so great that Venezuela and Ecuador voted to secede from Colombia, with the remaining portion renaming itself as New Granada in 1832.

## Formation

Seven of the eight core states are needed in order to form Gran Colombia
The following countries can form Gran Colombia when they control seven of its eight core states.

| Country | Capital | Primary Cultures | State Religion | Tier | Type | Core States |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Colombia** | Cundinamarca | North Andean | Catholic | Kingdom | Recognized | Cundinamarca Antioquia Cauca Guaviare |
| **Ecuador** | Ecuador | North Andean | Catholic | Principality | Recognized | Ecuador Pastaza |
| **Venezuela** | Miranda | North Andean | Catholic | Kingdom | Recognized | Miranda Bolívar Zulia |

If any of the above countries change from North Andean to a national culture, they are not able to form Gran Colombia.
