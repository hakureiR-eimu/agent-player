**Wallachia** is a minor power in Eastern Europe. It is able to form [Romania](Romania.md) and [Danubian State](Danubian State.md).

## Historical background

The Principality of Wallachia emerged in the early 14th century as local Romanian rulers consolidated their authority south of the Carpathians. The founder of the state is Basarab I whose victory over the Hungarian king at the Battle of Posada (1330) secured Wallachia's independence. Over the next century, Wallachia's rulers worked to maintain autonomy while balancing the pressures of neighboring Hungary, the expanding Ottoman Empire, and internal boyar factions. By the 15th century, Wallachia found itself on the front line of the Ottoman advance into the Balkans. The most famous Wallachian ruler of this era, Vlad III Țepeș (the Impaler), ruled intermittently between 1448 and 1476. Vlad became renowned for his uncompromising crackdown on boyar disloyalty and for his brutal but effective campaigns against Ottoman incursions, including the celebrated Night Attack of 1462. Though his methods were ruthless, his reign became a symbol of Wallachian resistance and sovereignty. In the late 16th century, Wallachia experienced a brief moment of regional prominence under Michael the Brave. Beginning his rule in 1593, Michael launched a campaign that temporarily united Wallachia, Transylvania, and Moldavia under his personal control, though the union collapsed within a year and Michael was assassinated in 1601, it was an unprecedented moment that elevated the Romanian national memory.

In stark contrast to the heights of Michael the Brave's short lived union, the 17th and early 18th centuries were Wallachia's darkest hour, marked by increasing dependence on the Ottoman Empire, culminating in the Phanariot era (1716-1821). During this period, the sultans replaced local dynasties with Greek-appointed princes from Constantinople. Phanariot rule brought heavy taxation, corruption, and instability, but also introduced administrative reforms, early codification efforts, and exposure to Enlightenment ideas. Wallachia entered the 19th century as a principality caught between Ottoman suzerainty and Russian influence. The Wallachian uprising of 1821, led by Tudor Vladimirescu, ended Phanariot rule and restored native-born princes. Over the next decades, rising national consciousness and reformist pressures transformed Wallachian society. The pivotal moment came in 1859, when both Wallachia and Moldavia elected Alexandru Ioan Cuza as their ruler, forming the basis of the United Principalities that 3 years later would be renamed [Romania](Romania.md).

## Formation

If Wallachia ceases to exist, it can be released by any country that controls its capital, and only core state, the state of Wallachia.

## Technology

Wallachia starts with most era 1 technologies. It also has better starting technology than its overlord [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md). It also has the unique Sericulture technology, shared with only 7 other countries, which boosts its Silk Plantations throughput by 25%. Forming another country as Wallachia retains the technology. It is the only country outside Asia with this technology.

### Interest groups

Wallachia begins with the Boyars in government. The Orthodox Church, Petite Bourgeoisie, Rural Folk, and Armed Forces are in opposition. The Intelligentsia, Industrialists, and Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Orthodox Church has the Orthodox Patriarchy ideology instead of the default Patriarchal. The Landowners have the Pro-Slavery ideology due to the starting Legacy Slavery. The Petite Bourgeoisie have the Mercantile ideology due to the starting Traditionalism.

### Diplomacy

Wallachia starts as a Protectorate under the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md) and thus part of the *Devlet-i ‘Aliye*.

Wallachia starts with pro-country lobbies towards [Russia](Russia.md) () and [France](France.md) (), as well as an anti-overlord lobby against the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md) ().

It has a [treaty](Treaty.md) with [Russia](Russia.md) *Treaty of Adrianople*, where Russia supports Wallachian independence in exchange for [power bloc embassy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Power_bloc_embassy), military access, and Wallachia committing to Organic Regulation.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Wallachia starts with a Food Industries building and quite a bit of resource and agriculture buildings.

**Resources**

Wallachia has access to a lot of coal, as well as some iron and sulfur. It may grow both cotton ( fabric) and silk, with the latter's output being boosted by its unique technology.

Wallachia has the Peasant Levies law.

Wallachia begins with four Battalions. It may raise up to 25 Conscripts at the start. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.

## Strategy and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Wallachia
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

### Starting conditions

Wallachia start in a precarious situation as the protectorate of the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md), together with fellow Romanian minor [Moldavia](Moldavia.md). The laws are extremely backwards, the [literacy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Literacy) is bad, the economic situation is bad, there is high unemployment, and most Romanian homelands are owned by three great powers (Ottomans, Russia and Austria).

The initial conditions depends a lot on which [attitude](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Attitude) the Ottomans take (protective or domineering), since it determines if Wallachia can play friendly with them or not. There are both advantages and disadvantages to playing friendly with them.

#### Assuming the Ottomans are friendly

One can first puppet and later annex Moldavia via a [journal entry](Romania.md), if [relations](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Relations) are good enough with all great powers with an interest in the area (including the Ottomans). Moldavia will become a personal union/ puppet[1] of Wallachia, and if they remain so for 10 years, they will be annexed. This can be done even while a protectorate of the Ottomans, assuming that Moldova is also still a protectorate. So improving relations with all great powers is important. The relevant great powers are usually just The Ottomans, Russia and [Austria](Austria.md), but it is also possible that [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) has also declared an interest. There is not many other uses for diplomatic capacity; Wallachia is not going to produce any [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy) anytime soon. The tech Nationalism is needed to do so, but this is probably not the first priority. Stock Exchange and industrial techs are way more pressing concerns. *Note that Moldavia has the exact same possibility, so if Wallachia takes too long, they can find themselves suddenly under Moldavia.*

The problem is that if the Ottomans turn Wallachia from a protectorate to a puppet, all progress can be lost, as Ottomans will inherit all Wallachia's puppets, if they do so. So it is a fine balance to strike. Keeping the Ottomans around 0 relations are best. Expelling diplomats and various demands should be used if needed.

#### If they are not

It will be very difficult to get Moldavia via event, if Ottomans are not initially friendly, since relations starts cold. Then one would need to build up an army and the backing of a major power. [Russia](Russia.md) is already committed to help, and that should be enough assuming no one else (like the British) intervenes. Here it is important to add the [war goal](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/War_goal) *transfer subject* for Moldavia and secondarily annexing the Ottoman owned parts of Romania, since this will allow one to continue with the journal entries.

It is very likely that the Ottomans will be allied to [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), or at least has friendly enough relations that Great Britain will come to their aid in a diplomatic play. Therefore it is important to time the attack, while GB is occupied elsewhere for example during the Opium Wars.

#### The Turkish Tanzimat reforms and Independence

The Ottoman [Tanzimat reforms](Turkish content.md) will grant Wallachia independence after 30 years, if The Ottomans fail to make the reforms. There is however not much, if anything, Wallachia can do to provoke it (or prevent it for that matter), but it is important to prepare for. Therefore industrial construction has to strike a balance between finding a lucrative niche in the Ottoman market, while also prepare a well rounded industry for eventual and sudden independence.

#### Economy

The big money maker in 1836 is the Food Industries, and it is worth it to expand this further. The economic negatives are, that there is a sulfur mine in Wallachia that is actually worthless (0 sell orders) and a Vineyard which is practically worthless (turks don't drink wine). Researching Mechanical Tools to build a Paper Mill and/or getting a trade center is a priority, so that sulfur can actually be used and wine can be exported. From the start Wallachia can finance a construction sector, and has both coal and iron locally. The money earned from building something and then privatizing it to either British or Ottoman owners, will be huge relative to Wallachia's budget, and therefore one can run a sizeable deficit without any concerns. The concern is of course that ownership will be foreign. Later a [company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Company) can be used to indirectly nationalize foreign owned buildings, since they can buy up even foreign owned buildings. Otherwise the Ottoman owned buildings can be nationalized when an eventual war comes. The British owned buildings are more difficult to get back.

Initially, one can use every single [decree](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Decree), and have plenty authority left. Especially promoting education is important, as [literacy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Literacy) is abysmal. Passing a school reform is important too.

With the road maintenance decree, Wallachia has around 125 [infrastructure](Infrastructure.md), so it is important to make sure to prepare and research Railways in time, for when this runs out. If infrastructure is running out before railways comes, pleasing the Rural Folk can help temporarily.

#### Politics

Due to Wallachia's unique law Manorialism, it is not as much of a concern to get away from Serfdom, as it otherwise would be. It is possible to implement a school system, and it does not strengthen the landowners as much as serfdom.

### Post independence

If you have acquired Moldova, a [journal entry](Romania.md) will make it possible to get hold of some of the Russian parts of Romania, if you have good relations with them and at least one other [Great Power](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Great_Power) with an [interest](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Interest) in the region, which crucially gives Romania access to some coastline. Russia will keep the most of it though.

When independent, Wallachia will not have a coast (not even with Moldova annexed), so trade is not reliable to fix too many shortcomings in the very short term.

The economy will likely take a hit post independence, as the bigger market of the Ottomans is gone, and likely also some trade centers, that Wallachia had been using until now. A consideration could be to join the Russian power bloc and market, since it is in Wallachia's interest to be friendly with the Russians anyway, at least for the time being. This is dependent on which principles Russia has chosen for their power bloc, so it is important to keep track of.

With time, an ally such as [Germany](Germany.md) will be good to turn on the Russians and take back the rest of Bessarabia.

If [Bulgaria](Bulgaria.md) achieves independence, they can be a good target. Especially if they have Dobrudja.

### Late game

In the mid to late game oil will be discovered in Wallachia, and of top of that they has a good [flavored Oil Company](List of flavored companies.md), meaning oil will an essential export late game.
