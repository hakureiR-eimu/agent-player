*"India is the jewel in the crown of the British Empire. Can the East India Company maintain rule over this vast landscape while appeasing its shareholders?"*

**East India**, starting as the *British East India Company*, is a minor power in East Asia. At the beginning of the game, it is a chartered company of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), and in turn is the overlord of the many princely states. One of the many subjects of the British Empire, the East India Company has various vast opportunities at the beginning of the game to expand and further dominate Asia, through economic or militaristic means. Starting with a government dominated by repressive company rule, it requires extensive effort to reform the nation to become a haven for liberty and prosperity.

As an English nation, East India Company is able to form [England](England.md) and [Great Britain](Great Britain.md). They can also form [India](India.md) either through the [the Journal Entry " The End of British Rule?"](Colonial Indies content.md) or by using this journal entry to become [Bengal](Bengal.md) and then forming India the conventional way.

## Scripted content

Main article:
Colonial Indies content
East India has a journal entry to avoid munity by its sepoy soldiers. This can be accomplished by completing an entry to consolidate control of India and raising the standard of living. East India also has a repeatable decision that allows it to annex states from its princely state subjects.

Lastly it has a journal entry to end British rule and become an Indian nation.

### Interest groups

East India starts with the East India Company and Presidency Armies in government. The Zamindars, Ryots, Hindu Priesthood, Bhadraloks, and Civil Servants are in opposition. The Trade Unions marginalized.

The East India Company and Presidency Armies will always select British characters as leaders, while the remaining groups will select leaders from Indian cultures.

As the British Raj, the Industrialists are called as the Government of India.

The East India Company has the Colonialist, Enclavist, and Malthusian ideologies instead of the default Industrialist ideologies. The Hindu Priesthood have the Hindu Moralist (British) ideology instead of the default Moralist. The Civil Servants have Constitutionalist instead of the default Republican. The Bhadraloks have the Modernizer and Sovereignist ideologies instead of the default Patriotic and Reactionary.

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.12. |
|---|---|

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
East India
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
The EIC begins the game as an undeveloped subject nation with a large amount of population and arable land, as well as a number of subjects. It can easily become the number one great power in the world without a need to conquer anything outside of its home region.

### Journal entries

EIC starts with two unique journal entries active: The Unstable Raj and Consolidate Colonial Rule.

Consolidating colonial rule requires that you, [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), or a subject of either of you two owns 80% of the required states, while keeping any secession movements down. Your targets are [Burma](Burma.md), [Punjab](Punjab.md) and their subject, [Sindh](Sindh.md), [Kalat](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kalat), half of [Makran](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Makran), [Chitral](Chitral.md), and parts of smaller states in modern day [Pakistan](Pakistan.md) to unify split states, including a tiny bit held by [Oman](Oman.md). EIC starts with claims on all of those states except those held by [Burma](Burma.md), so it may be a good idea to Protectorate them.

Your military is vastly stronger than that of your targets. They will often back down if the situation allows it, so many sure to promote secondary demands to primary as needed. Otherwise make sure to target the biggest and most important states as the first demand, especially against [Punjab](Punjab.md). Watch the [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy) and try to keep it under 25 at all times, as going over will increase [liberty desire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Liberty_desire) of all your subjects, but otherwise EIC can move fast and conquer often.

The *Unstable Raj* is very difficult to complete. It has three stability bars for three different areas of India. Lower taxes, happy Landowners, and loyal subjects will increase stability, while the opposite will lower it. The entry also starts with a timer for 24 years and 8 months (expiring in 1860). If the stability is too low for a long period of time or if the timer runs out, Sepoy Mutiny will happen, with its strength determined by the stability at the moment when it triggers.

Successfully completing the journal entry requires you to complete Consolidate Colonial Rule, have less than 10% radicals, and raise your average SoL to 10. Due to EIC's large population it can be very hard to raise the SoL. Also, completing the journal entry may not necessarily be to your advantage. Note that e EIC starts with High Taxes, which has a radicalizing effect on the population.

Upon failing the entry and dealing with the resulting rebellion, EIC will be reorganized into British Raj, with the following effects:

- Autocracy will be enacted and locked in.
- Caste System Codified will be enacted.
- It will be turned into a Colony. This removes your diplomatic autonomy, but also allows you to create your own [Companies](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Company), and you can potentially ask to be turned into a Dominion.
- Your own subjects will remain your subjects, but you can no longer invoke Doctrine of Lapse. Until you are a Dominion, you won't be able to annex them at all.

Even if planning to fail the entry, it's still worth it to drag it out for as long as possible to annex as many of your subjects as possible using Doctrine of Lapse.

### Princely states and Doctrine of Lapse

East India starts with many subjects, and has a unique vassal interaction [Doctrine of Lapse](Colonial Indies content.md). Using it will annex any Indian vassal for **10-15** [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy), depending on size, while increasing liberty desire and lowering relations with other princely states. Using it has a 2-year cooldown. EIC cannot annex subjects normally, using a diplomatic demand. It is important to make use of this interaction while keeping the subjects loyal enough to keep the stability manageable. Prioritise states that you partially own and have incorporated.

Note that this decision cannot be taken during a war, and Britain will drag EIC through a lot of wars.

Maintaining high [relations](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Relations) with your subjects is very important to keep their liberty desire down. EIC can freely declare rivalry on [Portugal](Portugal.md) and [Denmark](Denmark.md) to get more influence. You may want to move your interests to Europe or South America to rival minor nations there.

Note that [Ceylon](Ceylon.md) is NOT your subject, they are an entirely separate British subject.

### Releasable states

Conquering certain states as EIC will prompt you to create subjects or face penalties when you fully control them. The following states trigger it.

- Sindh: "Allow..." releases Khairpur in a small part of the state and leaves the rest to you. "Establish..." releases the entire state as Sindh.
- Baluchistan: "Distribute..." splits the region between 4 subjects while keeping a small part of the state. "Partition..." will create 3 subjects, giving most to Kalat, while keeping a small part of the state. "Establish..." will release the entire state as Kalat.
- Pashtunistan: "Grant..." creates 3 subjects while keeping around half of the state. "Expand..." will create one subject while keeping around half of the state.
- Kashmir: the entire state will be released as Kashmir.
- Shan: the entire state will be released as Shan States.

You always have the option of keeping the state, at the cost of increasing [liberty desire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Liberty_desire) of all princely states by 15 and lowering relations by 10. It's recommended to not take that option and instead release these states into as few subjects as possible, since the penalties are harsh, and the states will be unincorporated and full of radicals.

Consider keeping Baluchistan and Pashtunistan as Baluchi and Pashtun homelands take 20+ years to incorporate. When EIC forms India from independence and Pan-nationalism, it will not have Baluchi as a primary culture.

### Early game economy

The East India Company begins the game as a Chartered Company of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), and furthermore, it is a [company country](Companies.md). It has a large amount of population and a decent number of buildings; mostly plantations, but some industry as well. Britain will usually build in your country, but they will mostly build [plantations](List of buildings.md). These are a double-edged sword, as while they will increase your GDP, each level will only employ 5000 employees, while most of your states use subsistence rice paddies, with each level employing 10000. Thus, every plantation will create 5000 unemployed people. This is exacerbated by EIC being a company country, making it very likely that the company will be constructing its buildings, which are all plantations. This issue will persist for a very long time (becoming British Raj will somewhat diminish it), so therefore it is very important to focus on economic and industrial buildings, and build fast to reduce unemployement.

Many of EIC's buildings use early-game production methods at the start of the game despite having the technologies that unlock better ones. You should immediately switch them over. For obvious reasons the labor saving PMs are largely useless to you.

Your first priority should be getting out of Extraction Economy. It prevents you from building heavy industry, reduces your liberty desire, and significantly reduces investment pool contribution. EIC is in an unusual situation of having Landowners support a switch to Interventionism, which you should try to take advantage of.

As far as construction goes, industrial and government goods like wood, iron, tools and paper should be a priority. [Logging Camps](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Logging_Camps) are of a particular note due to employing a lot of people per construction cost, and paper will be a significant part of your expenses, so it's worth trying to keep the prices down. Secondary priority should be to try to lower your liberty desire by checking which buy orders are fulfilled by Britain in the subject menu and building those up. When preparing for independence, make sure to have plenty of [Trade Centers](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Trade_Center).

Depending on Great Britain's diplomacy and state of affairs, EIC might be isolated from time to time, due to wars leaving the [supply network](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Supply_network) inefficient. This is unfortunately outside of EIC's control, at least until declaring independence is feasible, so it is just something, they have to live with. It has a benefit of increasing your liberty desire, however.

It may be worth checking if there's any large nations interested in getting investment rights to build in EIC. This is highly beneficial due to EIC's large population.

EIC should avoid getting into debt except during the final independence war, due to its relatively high interest rate and Britain siphoning a portion of profits.

### State management

EIC starts with many unincorporated states. Integrating them early is generally a bad idea. It would cost a lot of Bureaucracy, which would cost a lot of paper and require construction to be spent on additional [Government Administration](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Government_Administration) buildings, while EIC has British culture and thus take 25 years to integrate those states before modifiers. It is a massive up-front cost for a very delayed benefit at a time where you should be building up your basic economy. Furthermore, you already have a massive population in your incorporated states, so simply focus on building in those. Once EIC has become either India or Bengal, it will only take two years to integrate those states, making it much more beneficial to wait until then.

### Government reforms

East India's laws are a mixed bag of progressive, regressive, and dictated by Britain. Getting rid of Debt Slavery by either abolishing it or at least changing it to Colonial Slavery is a priority. Adopting a better tax system should be considered once the increase in revenue is significant. EIC starts with Subjecthood, No Colonial Affairs, and Total Separation, giving a middling level of acceptance for pops living in their homelands. Institutions are expensive early, but Public Schools are likely worth it when affordable.

Britain might from time to time make demands to implement a certain law, which is generally a good idea to do, only when it is beneficial for EIC anyway.

### Technology

EIC starts out fairly advanced, but has no Universities at all, no schools, and low [literacy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Literacy), so actual technological development will come slowly.

The most important technologies to EIC are typically very different to basically any other country, so normal guidelines cannot be followed.

Due to EIC's status as a chartered company, getting Civilizing Mission is required for independence if planning to maintain company rule, and Pan-nationalism is required to become India and retain the entire subcontinent (see below).

Central Archives are crucial for more taxation capacity and bureaucracy.

Note that Joint-Stock Companies is **not** useful at all, since EIC as a chartered company cannot establish companies on their own. Bessemer Process is worthless as long as you have Extraction Economy.

### Independence

Independence for the EIC must be planned; as a Chartered Company, the EIC cannot agitate for independence if its Liberty Desire is lower than **90** and relations with Britain remain **Cordial** or better. Also, it's highly advised to wait until Pan-nationalism. Consider concentrating research on level 2 Society technologies so that the cost of Pan-nationalism is reduced; Nationalism is a prerequisite. Meanwhile, consider reducing relations with Britain so that when Pan-nationalism is ready, demands for concessions will be rejected, resulting in increased Liberty Desire.

When looking for nations to support independence, [Russia](Russia.md) is likely to sign the treaty. However, only do so when the EIC is ready to declare the independence war.

The actual independence war probably won't be too hard; India actually has a stronger army than Great Britain at the start of the game, and furthermore, you only need to defend your capital. However, after becoming independent, EIC will swiftly get an event ["The End of British Rule?"](Colonial Indies content.md), which will present you with the following choices:

- **Maintain English rule**: this option radicalizes the Indian pops.
- **Dividing India**: this option will allow you to retain East India (the Bengal region) and divide the remaining Indian territory among Indian subjects, who will also become independent. However, if *all* subjects are annexed before independence, all of the EIC's territory will be transferred intact to the new country ( [Bengal](Bengal.md)).
- **Forming India**: With Pan-nationalism researched, this option will form [India](India.md). It is a better option than "dividing India". However, the tech requirement may be a harsh condition.

Note that ending British rule will also remove caste-related laws from India, blocking the ability to complete the *Caste Away* achievement if it has not been reached by this point.

One possible route to independence is to allow the Mutiny event to fire, as it then gets rid of *Chartered Company*, allowing the *British Raj* to plot independence without the need for *Civilizing Mission*.

To declare an independence war, [relations](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Relations) must be below cordial. If you're unable to lower the relations sufficiently, you can expel British diplomats.

### Post-independence

Should any of the princely states still be left once you're independent, a special journal entry will activate: *The Union of India*. It allows you to annex princely states that have **50** or higher relations and less than 25 liberty desire. This will happen automatically to any states that fulfil the requirements, and you also have access to a button that invites all eligible states that can be used once a year. However, annexing them this way creates permanent national expenses equal to your GDP times 0.01 times number of princes annexed using the journal entry, so it's better to annex them using regular means. For puppets, consider increasing autonomy so that they become protectorates, then annex them using the "Unify India" wargoal after the truce period. While *The Union of India* is active, a princely state might ask to join the union; consider granting independence as no truce will be reinforced, allowing the immediate usage of the "Unify India" wargoal.

Historically, India was the crown jewel of the British Empire. An independent Bengal in control of most of the Indian subcontinent is a power to be reckoned with. It plays essentially like a smaller (but recognized) version of [China](China.md), a Great Power but in need of reform and modernization. Perhaps more crucially, mega-Bengal is in an excellent position to form India; with some consolidation pre-independence (e.g. annexing Baluchistan completely), she will be able to form India once Pan-nationalism is researched.
