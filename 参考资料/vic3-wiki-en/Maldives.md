*"Existing in relative isolation, the Maldives has thrived under British protection, shielded from the pirates that once threatened its shores. Will change ever come to this small island nation?"*

**Maldives** are an unrecognized power in South India.

## Technology

Maldives lag behind in technology at the start of the game, only having some of the era 1 technologies researched.

### Interest groups

Maldives start with the Zamindars in government. The Sunni Ulema and Ryots are in opposition. The remaining interest groups are marginalized.

The Intelligentsia has the Constitutionalist ideology instead of the default Republican.

### Industry and Resources

Maldives are a very small country, and start with no buildings. Their starting state has access to a variety of crops.

## Military

Maldives have the Professional Army and Merchant Navy laws.

Maldives start with no battalions nor flotillas.
