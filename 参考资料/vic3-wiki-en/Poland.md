**Poland** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in Eastern Europe. Poland's core states start partitioned between the Great Powers of [Austria](Austria.md), [Prussia](Prussia.md), and [Russia](Russia.md). Poland can be formed by any nation with Polish primary culture below kingdom tier: namely, [Krakow](Krakow.md) and [Galicia-Lodomeria](Galicia-Lodomeria.md). Poland has potential to become a Great Power itself, with its rich and populous core states.

Poland can also form [Poland-Lithuania](Poland-Lithuania.md).

## Historical background

The history of Poland stretches back to the 10th century when the Piast dynasty founded the country's first state. Over the years, Poland experienced both periods of prosperity and political turmoil, foreign invasion, and occupation. The Piast dynasty, under Mieszko I, ruled Poland from the 10th to the 14th century, during which Poland became a Christian country, and the territory expanded through conquest and diplomacy. The Jagiellonian dynasty, founded by Władysław Jagiełło, brought a period of cultural and artistic flourishing, territorial expansion, and ruled Poland from the 14th to the 16th century. Poland formed a union with Lithuania in the 16th century, creating the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth, which brought stability and prosperity until political corruption led to its decline.

The Swedes invaded and occupied Poland during the 17th century, known as "The Deluge," a catastrophic period of war, famine, and plague that devastated the country. The late 18th century brought the partitions of Poland by neighboring powers, Russia, Prussia, and Austria, ushering in a period of foreign domination and political oppression. This partition lasted until World War I; after the war, Poland regained independence and became a republic. However, World War II saw Poland invaded by Nazi Germany, leading to a brutal occupation, and afterward, Poland became a socialist state under Soviet influence.

In the 1980s, Poland experienced the Solidarity movement, demanding political freedom and workers' rights, leading to the collapse of socialism and the establishment of a democratic government. In 2004, Poland joined the European Union, marking a new era of political and economic integration with Europe. Throughout its history, Poland has shown resilience and vibrancy, with a rich cultural heritage and deep sense of national identity.

## Formation

Four of the eight core states are needed in order to form Poland; for releasing, East Galicia is not considered a core state.
Poland can be formed by the following countries when they control at least four of its eight core states.

| Country | Capital | Primary Cultures | State Religion | Tier | Type | Core States | Exists at start |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Krakow** | West Galicia | Polish | Catholic | City-State | Recognized | Split state in West Galicia | ![Yes](/images/thumb/7/77/Yes.png/24px-Yes.png) |
| **Galicia-Lodomeria** | East Galicia | Polish Ukrainian | Catholic | Principality | Recognized | East Galicia West Galicia | ![No](/images/thumb/0/04/No.png/24px-No.png) |

Of its eight core states, three are controlled by [Russia](Russia.md) at game start, three are controlled by [Prussia](Prussia.md), one is controlled by [Austria](Austria.md), and one (West Galicia) is split between Austria and its puppet [Krakow](Krakow.md).

Galicia-Lodomeria does **not** exist at game start, but can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country which controls its capital and one other core state.

Poland can also be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country that controls its capital, Warszawa, and two other core states. When released, East Galicia is not considered a core state. Krakow is also releasable by any country controlling its capital.

### Core states

Poland's core states mostly lie within the Eastern Europe region, except for Górny Śląsk (Upper Silesia). One of Poland's core states – Kaszuby (West Prussia) – starts as majority North German, rather than Polish.

Warmia (East Prussia) is not a core state of Poland, but is a Polish homeland and will be claimed upon formation if it is not already owned.

Poland's core states have a good amount of iron in many states as well as coal. Sulfur and lead can be found too. Upper Silesia is the most resource rich state of its cores.

## Strategy and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Poland
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

### Forming Poland as Krakow

When playing as [Krakow](Krakow.md), your priority needs to be getting [Prussia](Prussia.md) (or [Russia](Russia.md), but it is way less likely they will), to guarantee your independence and not being annexed before they do so. Don't be too aggressive with damaging relations with Austria to try and raise [liberty desire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Liberty_desire), as this will change Austria's attitude towards you to be domineering making it more likely they will annex you. Once you get guaranteed though, you can damage relations and either Austria will start a diplomatic play to try and annex you, or you'll be able to raise your liberty desire enough to start a diplomatic play to raise your autonomy (Don't try to become independent until later in the game)

Starting your diplomatic play, you'll want to add the war goal of taking West Galicia. You might also want to weaken Austria by liberating Hungary so getting other states in the future is easier (And so Prussia is able to more easily form Germany). Later, repeat the same process except now take East Galacia and you should be a protectorate now. When Prussia forms Germany, you can try and anger Russia so they will be more likely to intervene, which is useful for getting Poland's Russian cores without forging allies.

Hopefully by now you have all of the Austria provinces you need to form Poland, and if your lucky, maybe 1 or 2 of the Russian provinces. Now you are independent and might be a major power if you industrialized well and get good migration. You need Prussia/Germany to ally with you to get the rest of the cores. Then you can start going ham with them and get the rest of your provinces back. After this, you will have enough provinces to form Poland, but you still won't have the cores on the German side.

Getting the cores on the German side will be much more difficult: you still have way less population and Austria and Russia hates you, so its recommended that you just form it now and focus on getting its cores later, but its still possible by fostering rebels, then you can side with them and you might stand a chance if you go full defensive mode.
