**Indonesia** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in the Indonesia strategic region. It can be formed by any country with a Malay trait primary culture, excluding Filipino and Moro.

## Historical background

The Indonesian archipelago has been partially unified several times, first under the Srivijaya empire, then the Majapahit empire, and finally under Dutch colonial control. In most cases, the islands were unified under a system of tribute or confederation rather than direct control.

Indonesia as an independent, unified country was declared in 1945, just after Japan – which had occupied the Dutch colony – surrendered in World War II. The [Netherlands](Netherlands.md) did not recognize Indonesia's independence and fought to regain control, with support from the Allies. Following four years of fighting, the Netherlands – under United Nations pressure – finally recognized Indonesia's independence.

The Indonesian minors are all unrecognized powers and have similar basic stats, starting technology, and politics. Most start with only a split state in Indonesia. They are all Principalities and Sunni, except Bali which is Hindu. They generally start with the Landowners in government with a mix of the Armed Forces and Sunni Ulema, Intelligentsia, and Rural Folk in opposition, and the remaining interest groups marginalized. Many of them start as puppets of the [East Indies](East Indies.md).

## Strategy and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.10. |
|---|---|

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Indonesia
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

### Formation by East Indies

Ironically, Indonesia is most easily formed by the colonial [East Indies](East Indies.md). On gaining independence, East Indies can choose to divide its states among its subjects and become Java, or if it has researched Pan-nationalism it can become Indonesia directly. For the first option, however, if East Indies has no subjects, it retains all its states, making it simple to form Indonesia later after researching Pan-nationalism.

### Expansion

South-east Asian heritage cultures are accepted under most Citizenship laws. As such, countries in Indochina like [Burma](Burma.md), [Siam](Siam.md), [Dai Nam](Dai Nam.md) and their tributaries/vassals are prime targets for expansion.
