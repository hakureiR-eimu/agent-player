*"No longer under Ottoman protection, Morocco is vulnerable to French and Spanish expansionism. Can Morocco survive the imperialist onslaught?"*

**Morocco** is an unrecognized regional power in North Africa. While it is the largest and most powerful independent country in the Maghreb, it is not strong enough to stand up to the colonial ambitions of Western Europe. Unless helmed by capable hands, it will likely end up under imperialist hands.

Morocco can form [Maghreb](Maghreb.md).

## Formation

If Morocco ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by a country controlling its capital and two other states. Its core states are the four starting states; details on those can be found below.

### Diplomacy

Morocco has claims on Inner Morocco and West Sahara, both owned by decentralized nations [Tekna](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Tekna), [Delim](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Delim), and [Reguibat](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Reguibat).

Morocco has cordial relations with [Mascara](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Mascara) (**+20**), but poor relations with [Spain](Spain.md) (**-30**).

Morocco borders [Mascara](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Mascara) to the east, the decentralized [Tekna](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Tekna), [Tuat](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Tuat), and [Mzab](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Mzab) to the south, and [Spain](Spain.md) to the north through the ports of Ceuta and Melilla, in the split state of al Rif.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Morocco starts with a minimal industrial base, with only a single level each of Tooling Workshops, Textile Mills, and Glassworks. It has a decent amount of starting Livestock Ranches

**Resources**

Morocco has access to a tiny amount of coal, a modest amount of lead and sulfur (especially thanks to a state trait in Marrakech), and a decent amount of iron. A small amount of gold provides an initial minting boost.

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.10. |

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Morocco
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
Lack of coal and wood will be a serious long term problem. There are some iron deposits, but with time lack of iron will be a problem too. Especially since the best industrial state is Inner Morocco, which has the *Atlas Mountains* [state trait](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/State_trait), which hampers both construction and infrastructure. Inner Morocco's Gold Mines are worth building early, to finance more construction.

[Spain](Spain.md) owns part of Al Rif, and retaking those provinces is a good long term goal. Initially, however, keeping its initial states is more than challenging enough. Morocco does not have [claims](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Claims) on the state however.

### France

|  | Available only with the [Voice of the People](Voice of the People.md) DLC enabled. |
|---|---|

See also:
The Proclamation of Algiers
It is necessary to monitor [French](France.md) activity in [Algeria](Algeria.md); once France controls the three states directly or via subjects, it receives claims on most Moroccan states[3], making France a dangerous foe. A solution would be to take a state away from [Mascara](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Mascara) to prevent France from completing the [journal entry](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Journal_entry). They are weaker on paper, but it is close, so it can prove a difficult war if Morocco is not careful. The issue here is that Mascara starts with cordial relations with Morocco. Another possibility is to ally/get military access through Mascara to attack [Constantine](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Constantine). Taking [Ait Abbas](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Ait_Abbas) would also work, but the state would be isolated after annexation. Note that taking [Touggourt](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Touggourt) does *not* help, in fact it makes it easier for France to complete the journal entry.

### Towards Maghreb

Morocco is generally the easiest country to form [Maghreb](Maghreb.md) with, as they have the most states at the start of the game, and generally have the least problems with European great powers. It will become significant easier, if the [Ottomans](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey) fail the [Tanzimat Reforms](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Tanzimat_Reforms), since [Tunis](Tunis.md) and [Tripolitania](Tripolitania.md) will likely become independent.
