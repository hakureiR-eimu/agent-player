*"The Congress of Vienna established the German Confederation, a loose assortment of German states intended to keep the peace in Central Europe. The Confederation in practice is dominated by the rivalry between Austria and Prussia. Can the smaller nations survive the coming conflict?"*

**Hesse** is a minor power in North Germany. It borders [Waldeck](Waldeck.md), [Frankfurt](Frankfurt.md), [Baden](Baden.md), [Nassau](Nassau.md), [Hesse-Kassel](Hesse-Kassel.md), [Bavaria](Bavaria.md) and [Prussia](Prussia.md) at game start.

As a North German nation, it is able to form the [North German Federation](North German Federation.md) and [Germany](Germany.md). As an alternative, it can instead form [Confederation of the Rhine](Confederation of the Rhine.md).

## Formation

If it ceases to exist, Hesse can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country controlling its capital and only core state of Hesse.

### Interest groups

Due to Hesse's complete lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Hesse begins with the Landowners in government. The Kleinbürger, Landvolk, Intelligentsia, Armed Forces and the Evangelical Church are in opposition. The Industrialists and Trade unions are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Hesse starts in the *Zollverein* power bloc, lead by [Prussia](Prussia.md).
