**Lübeck** is a minor power in North Germany. It is is able to form the [North German Federation](North German Federation.md) and [Germany](Germany.md). As an alternative, Lübeck can also form the [Confederation of the Rhine](Confederation of the Rhine.md). Lübeck is the 5th smallest German state behind [Waldeck](Waldeck.md), [Lippe](Lippe.md), [Schaumburg-Lippe](Schaumburg-Lippe.md) and [Hohenzollern](Hohenzollern.md).

### Interest groups

Due to Lübeck's general lack of historical IG leaders and its
Wealth Voting law, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Lübeck begins with the Kleinbürger in government leading the Conservative Party together with the Landowners, Armed Forces, and the Evangelical Church. In opposition, the Intelligentsia lead the Progress Party together with the Landvolk. The Industrialists and Trade unions are marginalized.

Lübeck has the Professional Army law.

Lübeck begins with 1 Battalion and can call upon no Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training.
