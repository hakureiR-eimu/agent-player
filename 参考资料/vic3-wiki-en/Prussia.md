*"Prussia managed to gain large swathes of territory in the Napoleonic wars and has spent the last few years making startling reforms across the country. Will Prussia continue along this path and perhaps unite Germany?"*

**Prussia** is a great power in central Europe, with Friedrich Wilhelm von Hohenzollern as its king at game start. Its capital and market capital is the state of Brandenburg. It is the leading power in North Germany. It controls the nation of [Hohenzollern](Hohenzollern.md) as a puppet.

Prussia is able to form [North German Federation](North German Federation.md), [Germany](Germany.md) and [Central Europe](Central Europe.md).

Prussia is one of four recommended countries for the *Hegemon* [objective](Objective.md), the others being [Spain](Spain.md), [Japan](Japan.md), and [Egypt](Egypt.md).

## Historical background

In 1701, the Margraviate of Brandenburg and the Duchy of Prussia were unified as the Kingdom of Prussia by the House of Hohenzollern.

Prussia would begin its rise to great power status after emerging victorious, along with [Russia](Russia.md), in the Great Northern War against [Sweden](Sweden.md); Prussia effectively conquered all of Swedish Pomerania as spoils of war. Along with this, Prussia was instrumental in the partitioning of the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth in the east over the course of the 18th century.

During the Napoleonic Wars, Prussia was a member of the First, Fourth, Sixth, and Seventh Coalitions. After suffering defeat in the War of the Fourth Coalition, Prussia signed the Treaty of Tilsit, in which [France](France.md) stripped around half of the territory of Prussia away, creating French client states out of the ceded land, and severely limited the size of the Prussian army. Prussia's population, economic power, and overall influence was greatly diminished during this time.

After the defeat of Napoleon, and the subsequent Congress of Vienna, Prussia regained the land it had owned prior to 1807, save for some land in Poland and southern Germany, while also gaining control of much of Saxony, Westphalia, and the Rhineland. These gains left Prussia as the sole power composed of mostly German-speaking populations.

At the start of the game, Prussia is well positioned to continue its goal of German unification, though questions still exist over what territory a unified Germany would include. Surrounded by potential enemies – or allies – in [France](France.md), [Austria](Austria.md), and [Russia](Russia.md), Prussia must not only rely on its strong military, but tactful diplomacy if it is to reach its full potential.

## Formation

Six of the seven core states are needed in order to form Prussia
If Prussia ceases to exist, it can be [formed](Formable countries.md) by any North German country below Kingdom tier that controls six of its core states: Brandenburg, Pomerania, Lower Silesia, Upper Silesia, West Prussia, East Prussia, and Posen. Alternatively, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country which controls Brandenburg and two other core states.

### Interest groups

Prussia begins with the Junkers and Armed Forces in government. The Kleinbürger, Landvolk, Evangelical Church, Intelligentsia, and Industrialists are in opposition. The Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Junkers have the Junker ideology instead of the default Paternalistic, making them more critical of Theocracy but less critical of Appointed Bureaucrats.

### Diplomacy

Most of the German minor nations belong to the *Zollverein* [Power bloc](Power bloc.md), except for [Hanover](Hanover.md) and the [Danish](Denmark.md) subjects of [Schleswig](Schleswig.md) and [Holstein](Holstein.md) and the [Dutch](Netherlands.md) subject of [Luxembourg](Luxembourg.md).

Prussia starts with [Hohenzollern](Hohenzollern.md) as a puppet, a trade agreement with [Hamburg](Hamburg.md) and [Sweden](Sweden.md), defensive pact with [Austria](Austria.md) and [Russia](Russia.md) ("the Holy Alliance"), mutual military access with [Hanover](Hanover.md) and [Bavaria](Bavaria.md), allows transit rights for [Luxembourg](Luxembourg.md), and grants military assistance to the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md). Prussia also borders the great power of [France](France.md). To its west, it also borders [Belgium](Belgium.md) and [Netherlands](Netherlands.md).

Prussia's starting interest levels are hegemonic (level 5) in Central Europe, influential (level 3) in Western Europe and Eastern Europe, and engaged (level 2) in Balkans, Northern Europe, and Southern Europe.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Prussia starts with an advanced, though somewhat small industrial base. Most available industries have been built in some state, with the notable exception of Motor Industry and Shipyards.

**Resources**

Prussia has a strong resource base, with access to large amounts of coal, iron, lead, and sulfur. Prussia does lack most agricultural products as well as access to oil in its starting states, though some oil can be found in neighboring Hanover.
