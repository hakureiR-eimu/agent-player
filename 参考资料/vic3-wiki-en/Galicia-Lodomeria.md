**Galicia-Lodomeria** is a [formable](Formable countries.md) and [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in Eastern Europe. It can be released by any country which controls its capital or formed by the Polish city-state of [Krakow](Krakow.md). At the start of the game, its core states are controlled by [Austria](Austria.md).

As a country with Polish primary culture, Galicia-Lodomeria can form [Poland](Poland.md).

## Historical background

[Kingdom of Galicia and Lodomeria with the Grand Duchy of Kraków and the Duchies of Auschwitz and Zator](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galicia-Lodomeria) was one of the crownlands of the Austrian Empire. As its name implies, it included the territories of both Polish and Ukrainian Galicia, as well as the lands around Oświęcim and Zator.

The name "Galicia-Lodomeria" comes from Ruthenian rather than Polish: Galicia is a latinization of the name of the Ukrainian town of [Halych](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halych) (Galich), which was the capital of the [Principality of Galicia](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principality_of_Galicia), while Lodomeria is a latinization of [Volodymyr](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr,_Ukraine), which was the capital of the [Principality of Volhynia](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principality_of_Volhynia). These principalities were later unified into the [Kingdom of Galicia–Volhynia](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Galicia%E2%80%93Volhynia), which staked its claim to Ruthenia. The Kingdom managed to briefly capture Kyiv, but was defeated by Mongols, slowly going into decline before being absorbed by Poland in the 14th century. In 1772, the crownland was established by Austria from the lands it captured during the partitions of Poland. Bukovina was added to it in 1786 and was part of the crownland until 1849, when it was separated into its own [Duchy of Bukovina](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duchy_of_Bukovina). Meanwhile, [Krakow](Krakow.md) was established in 1815 as a nominally-independent free city, but was stripped of this status by Austria and made into a grand duchy under this crownland in 1846.

## Formation

Technically, Galicia-Lodomeria can be formed by any Polish or Ukrainian nation below Principality rank that controls the states of West and East Galicia. Only [Krakow](Krakow.md) satisfies these requirements, however.

### Core states

Galicia-Lodomeria has three core states: Eastern and Western Galicia in the strategic region of Eastern Europe, and Bukovina in the strategic region of Balkans; the latter only counts as a core state when releasing the country and not towards the formation requirements.
