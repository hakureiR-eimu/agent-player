*"Long subservient to its Austrian masters, the Kingdom of Hungary finds itself at a crossroads, with emerging factions arguing in favor of greater Hungarian independence. Will radical voices triumph, or will Hungary remain yet another jewel in the Austrian crown?"*

**Hungary** is a minor power in Eastern Europe, starting as a Crown Land of the [Austria](Austria.md)n Empire, it borders Austria as well as fellow Crown Lands [Croatia](Croatia.md) and [Transylvania](Transylvania.md), finally there is [Serbia](Serbia.md) to the south.

Hungary starts with its capital in Slovakia (Feldivék), rather than its defined capital state.

Hungary can form the [Danubian State](Danubian State.md).

## Formation

If Hungary ceases to exist it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by a country that controls its capital and any other core state. Its core states are the Hungarian homelands; all of which are owned by Hungary at the start – except Northern and Southern Transylvania, owned by [Transylvania](Transylvania.md).

## Scripted content

In addition to Hungarian content, Hungary is also greatly affected by [Austria's scripted content](Austrian content.md) *The Matter of Hungary*.

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.12. |
|---|---|

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Hungary
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

### General overview

Hungary starts out as a direct subject of a Great Power in [Austria](Austria.md), an overlord who will eventually come knocking in demanding annexation from their unique content. However, it starts out with a powerful Market Liberal Landowner leader in Batthyány, making it very easy to reform and industrialise in the early game and to out-compete Austria.

After independence, Hungary can be an influential power in the Balkan region and a prosperous, high-SoL Eastern European country that attracts droves of migrants from both within Eastern Europe and around the world. Due to its terrain, ease of reforming and liberalising, and the constraints from being sandwiched between two powerful nations, it is a great country to play tall.

### Pre-independence industrialisation

The states of Felvidék (Slovakia), Alföld (Great Hungarian Plain/Budapest) and Dunántúl are good choices for heavy industry early, because of the Danube [river](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/State_trait) as well as iron and coal. Great Hungarian Plain also has a potential [flavored company](List of flavored companies.md) containing Motors and [prestige tools](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Prestige_goods) (MÁVAG), as well as every resource required for Motor Industries.

The Hungarian Palatine supports the Magnates, but is actually a Reformer and the IG leader is a Market Liberal meaning they are more than happy to support getting away from Manorialism and Traditionalism. You can actually pass Laissez-Faire *very* quickly as Hungary, as the Landowners will also support all of the prerequisites necessary. Together, with the Catholic church to implement Religious Schools, it should be easy to implement good laws. It is also easy to enact Cultural Exclusion early on, which will help with migration in a tall run.

On January 1st, 1836 Hungary has a company slot open, but cannot actually establish any companies due to insufficient buildings. Prioritizing getting to such buildings quickly is paramount.

### The Hungarian Revolution

The most important thing to succeed with this Journal Entry is to increase [liberty desire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Liberty_desire). This is both to gain independence, as well as to annex [Transylvania](Transylvania.md). If you focus on industrialisation and building a moderately-sized army, your liberty desire will shoot up in no time. Afterwards, it is important to get as many powerful backers as possible. [Russia](Russia.md) and [Prussia](Prussia.md) start with defensive pacts with Austria, so it is important to keep track of their diplomacy to see if their attitude towards Austria changes. Of course, other powers like [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), [France](France.md), the [USA](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/USA), and even the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md) may be useful co-belligerents if they have negative attitudes towards Austria.

Ensure you have a robust domestic industry before starting any independence war. Even more importantly, make sure you have an indigenous defense sector supplying your army with the bullets and goods they need. The Austrian Market is large and productive, and you will find it difficult to fight a war if it turns out your economy was highly reliant on it.

### Post-independence

The most important thing you will want to do post-independence is to get Transit Rights and/or find a coastline, so that you can trade with the World Market (and perhaps expand your influence outside the Balkans). You can generally get Transit Rights with a cordial neighbour, although actually finding a coast might be difficult. After winning the independence war, Austria will typically retreat to their homelands and release a number of states, including [Croatia](Croatia.md). Striking Croatia early to get a coastline or to make them a protectorate before they get diplomatic backing can be a viable idea; alternatively, it might be worth conquering the state of Croatia in the independence war to gain immediate shoreline access, although this does make the war harder to win. If that is not possible, you may need to fight the Ottoman Empire (perhaps with German or Russian help) for a coast.

Hungary does not have any sulfur in its starting lands. Wallachia has a decent amount of it, so you may need to invade or protectorate them to gain a domestic supply of sulfur.

Hungary does, however, have a comparative advantage in agricultural products due to the Pannonian Plains states and a strong flavored company in Első Budapesti Gőzmalom Rt. As such, it can be a solid strategy to invest heavily (or at least more than usual) in wheat, groceries, and wood to become the breadbasket of Europe. This also has the added benefit of raising your SoL, which will make it easy to gain migrants and play tall. Partium, Bánát and Délvidék (Vojvodina) are best used for Food Industries as they lack crucial industrial resources but also have the *Pannonian Plain* state trait.
