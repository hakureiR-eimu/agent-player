**Ireland** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) encompassing the island of Ireland. These states start the game under the control of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md). Alternatively, Ireland can be [formed](Formable countries.md) by [Ulster](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Ulster).

## Formation

Ireland can be formed by [Ulster](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Ulster) when it controls at three of Ireland's four core states. Ireland can also be released by any country that controls Leinster and another core state.

| Country | Capital | Primary Cultures | State Religion | Tier | Type | Core States |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ulster** | Ulster | Irish Scottish | Protestant | Principality | Recognized | Ulster |

If Ireland is formed by Ulster, its state religion remains Protestant. Ulster does not exist at the start and can be released by any country that controls the state of Ulster.

## Strategy

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Ireland
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
Releasing Ireland from Great Britain directly at the start of the game grants Ireland the same [government structure](Great Britain.md) as Great Britain, but without the economy to support the [institutions](Institutions.md). This makes for an extremely challenging start as Ireland is relatively undeveloped in 1836. On the other hand, the technologies inherited will be quite good.

Industrializing will also be very challenging without gaining a steady source of coal.
