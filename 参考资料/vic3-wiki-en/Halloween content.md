### Frankenstein company

As long as the *Frankenstein et Cie., Établ. Électriques d’Ingolstadt* company is prosperous, it will create Promethean pops in a random state each month. The number is equal to 1/5000 of the country's population, with a minimum of 500 and a maximum of 100000.

Once a state reaches 10% Promethean population, it becomes a Promethean homeland.
