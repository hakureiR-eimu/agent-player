**Hindustan** is an unrecognized regional power in northern India. It starts in a much reduced state compared to its historical height. Technically it is a formable nation, but no countries can form it.

Hindustan can form [India](India.md).

## Historical background

The Mughal Empire was an early modern empire in South Asia. At its peak, the empire stretched from the outer fringes of the Indus River Basin in the west, northern Afghanistan in the northwest, and Kashmir in the north, to the highlands of present-day Assam and Bangladesh in the east, and the uplands of the Deccan Plateau in South India. In 1836, it has been reduced to the city of Delhi, and is a subject of the East Indies.

### Interest groups

Hindustan begins with the Zamindars in government, with the Ryots, Armed Forces, Intelligentsia and Petite Bourgeoisie in opposition. The Sunni Ulema, Trade Unions and Industrialists are marginalized.

The Petite Bourgeoisie has the Modernizer ideology instead of the default Reactionary. The Intelligentsia has the Constitutionalist ideology instead of the default Republican.

### Opening Moves

Switch your government bureaucracy building to not use paper, you need neither the tax capacity or the bureaucracy (yet). Improve relations with every Indian country you can. Suppress the Sunni Traditionalist Movement.

### Economy

Delhi is a very resource-poor state, only supporting 5 logging camps and 2 sulfur mines, and at the start you can only have one of each. This lends itself to one obvious industry: paper. This is actually a very good industry to specialise in as India has a huge population that will need enormous amounts of bureaucracy and taxation capacity to administer, and with the Caste System in place Universities will be needed for qualifications. Aim to have 5 paper mills ready for when you research Corporate Charters so you can establish the paper company immediately and grant it a monopoly on paper mills. Having a supply of Craft Paper in your country will be extremely useful as this will further boost the throughput of Universities and Government Administration.

### Strategy

Hindustan is a city-state that wants to grow into a subcontinent-spanning empire, this means that the early campaign is very different from the rest as you are trapped as a tiny subject waiting for the Sepoy Mutiny. Hindustan is also a Muslim country that ultimately wants to conquer tens of millions of Hindu pops, Total Separation with be essential for this campaign. As such the first technologies researched should be Academia and Empiricism. Build what you need to sustain a big University: the one Logging Camp you have capacity for and try time to time finishing a Paper Mill right as you finish researching Academia. Continuously build Universities to boost the power of the Intelligentsia, once you've built 2 levels an event will fire that gives you a big boost towards Empiricism, once this is done invite the Intelligentsia into government and get to work. With Hindustan's almost entirely Hindu population to start with your Devout are unusually weak, so passing Total Separation will be relatively easy.

Other things that you will need to build to prepare for having an independent market without sea access: a tooling workshop, an arms industry, a food industry, a banana plantation. Pay attention to what the states of British Delhi and Agra have and take these into account as they will be part of your wartime economy.

Slightly counter-intuitively National Militia is the best Army Model Law to prepare for the war against the British East India Company, due to the way the Journal Entry that will trigger the war works, suddenly granting you some of East-India's states (the amount depending on the state of the Bengal Presidency stability bar) and immediately launching a diplomatic play in the countdown to war phase. The important thing here is being able to rapidly conscript as many people as possible rather than having a large army before this. Unfortunately, only the Rural Folk and the Trade Unions will support this and both will be very hard to bring out of marginalised status early on.

As soon as the war starts Hindustan will instantly become a major unrecognised power. The war becomes far easier to win that might be imagined, all it will take is a white peace to cause British authority in India to collapse (which might result in you recieving the war goal anyway). As such, defending is much more important than attacking, ideally get a general with access to special defensive orders, such as an Defensive Strategist.
