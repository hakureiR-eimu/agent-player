**Tahiti** is an unrecognized power in the center of the Pacific Ocean which starts as protectorate of [France](France.md).

Tahiti is one of the few centralized Polynesian nations, thus able to form [Polynesia](Polynesia.md)

## Historical background

Tahiti is a broken nation in 1836. After French intervention in 1842, Tahiti became a provisional protectorate of France on September 9, 1842. In 1844, following an agreement between Du Petit-Thouars and Pomare IV Pomare, Tahiti became an official protectorate of France and was officially recognized.

### Interest groups

The Landowners begin in a strongly dominant position, with almost no opposition and many laws also strongly favoring them.

- The Landowners start in government.
- The Armed Forces, Intelligentsia Rural Folk, and Petite Bourgeoisie are in opposition.
- The Trade Unions, Evangelical Church, and Industrialists are marginalized.
