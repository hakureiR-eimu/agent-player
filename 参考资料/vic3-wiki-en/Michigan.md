**Michigan** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in the Midwest region of North America. Its capital and single core state, Michigan, starts as an unincorporated state of [America](America.md). It can be released by any country that controls the state of Michigan if the [game rule](Game rule.md) *Releasable Nations* is set to *All*.

Michigan is able to form the [Free States of America](Free States of America.md) and [United States of America](America.md).

## Core states

Michigan's single core state, Michigan, has a fair amount of iron as well as some sulfur and wood. A lack of coal hampers any industrial potential however.

## Achievements

Michigan is in a good position for the *Amish Paradise* achievement, since it does not start with any industry and therefore a very weak industrialist IG, thus making the law much easier to pass. Especially if Michigan is released as a [puppet](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Puppet) of USA, as it can then specialize within the broader American market, using a multitude of [decrees](Decrees.md) on their single state.
