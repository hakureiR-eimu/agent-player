### Interest groups

Due to Aussa's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups
in government can vary.
Aussa begins with the Landowners in government. The Armed Forces, and Sunni Ulema are in opposition. The Petite Bourgeoisie, Rural Folk, Intelligentsia, Industrialists, and Trade Unions are marginalized.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Aussa starts with only a single Fishing Wharves and no other industry.

**Resources**

Aussa has access to Iron and Wood, and eventually Rubber. The arable land is usable for a wide range of agricultures, however.
