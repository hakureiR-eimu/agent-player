**Formosa** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) that can emerge through a Min separatist revolution or be manually released. At the start date, its sole core state is controlled by [China](China.md).

## Historical background

In 1836, Taiwan (historically known as Formosa to Western powers) was administered as part of Fujian Province under the Qing Empire. The island had experienced significant Han Chinese migration from Fujian province throughout the 18th century, particularly in the western plains where rice and sugar cultivation flourished. The mountainous eastern regions remained largely under the control of various Austronesian peoples, who by 1836 were experiencing increasing pressure from Han settlement and Qing administrative control.

The island's economy was primarily agricultural, with rice farming and sugar production being major activities. The cultivation of tea was beginning to emerge as an important export crop, particularly in the northern regions. Maritime trade, both legal and illegal, played a significant role in the island's economy, with ports like Tainan and Bangka (modern-day Wanhua) serving as important commercial centers.

Given Formosa's limited starting position, achieving Great Power status requires careful strategic planning:

### Early Game Strategy

- Focus on building a small navy for regional operations
- Seeking improve relationship with Great Powers
- Joining a Customs Union to facilitate expansion into Southeast Asia
- Target smaller nations like Johore for initial expansion
- Invest in universities to advance technology and overcome initial technological disadvantages

### Late Game Development

Two primary paths to power exist:

- Southeast Asian Focus: Develop through Malaysian and Indonesian resources, particularly oil and rubber
- Continental Focus: Expand into China to acquire manpower and resources
