**Hanover** is a minor power in North Germany. Hanover starts in a personal union under [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), but typically becomes independent within a year or two when William of Hanover dies.

It is is able to form the [North German Federation](North German Federation.md) and [Germany](Germany.md). As an alternative, Hanover can also form the [Confederation of the Rhine](Confederation of the Rhine.md).

### Interest groups

Due to Hanover's complete lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Hanover typically begins with the Kleinbürger and the Landowners or Evangelical Church in government. The Armed Forces, Landvolk, and Intelligentsia are in opposition. The Trade Unions and Industrialists are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Hanover starts in a personal union under [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), and thus a member of the *British Empire* power bloc. This will however end very soon, when when William of Hanover dies. Hanover has a pro-overlord lobby () towards Great Britain. Hanover has a [treaty](Treaty.md) providing mutual Military Access with [Prussia](Prussia.md).

Hanover has an active interest in North Germany and a declared interest in South Germany.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Hanover starts with almost no industry, only a small amount of resource gathering.

**Resources**

Hanover has very limited access to resources. Its best resource state, Brunswick, is also its least populated. Hanover does eventually gain access to oil.
