**Wadai** is an unrecognized power located in the Niger [strategic region](Strategic region.md) of Africa. It borders [Bornu](Bornu.md), [Bagirmi](Bagirmi.md), and [Darfur](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Darfur), and the decentralized nations of [Tubu](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Tubu) and [Dar al Kuti](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Dar_al_Kuti).

### Interest groups

Due to Wadai's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Wadai starts with the Landowners in government. The Sunni Ulema and Armed Forces are in opposition, while the Intelligentsia, Trade Unions, Rural Folk, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Industrialists are marginalized.
