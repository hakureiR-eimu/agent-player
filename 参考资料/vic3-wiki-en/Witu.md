**Witu** is an unrecognized power located in the Zanj [strategic region](Strategic region.md) of Africa. It borders it [Geledi](Geledi.md) to the north, and [Oman](Oman.md)i Kenya to the south, as well as the decentralized nations of [Mijikenda](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Mijikenda) and [Aulihan](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Aulihan).

With a population of just 18,500, Witu is the smallest independent state at the start of the game, and the second-smallest playable country overall, behind the [Russian-American Company](Alaska.md).

## Historical context

Witu or Wituland was founded in 1810, and was a haven for slaves fleeing the Zanzibar slave trade, which led to animosity between the two states. In 1885 the Sultan of Witu formally requested to become a German protectorate to defend against Zanzibar. In the 1890 Heligoland–Zanzibar Treaty the protectorate was transferred to Great Britain, and in 1905 it was transferred to the Kenya coast protectorate.

### Interest groups

Due to Witu's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Witu starts with the Armed Forces and the Landowners in government. The Rural Folk, Trade Unions, and Sunni Ulema are in opposition, while the Intelligentsia, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Industrialists are marginalized.

Witu is one of very few countries that start with a non-marginalized Trade Unions. They are also one of the only countries that start with no unemployed POPs nor peasants
