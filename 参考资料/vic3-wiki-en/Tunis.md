*"After being forced to end its sponsorship of piratical raids, the Tunisian economy has taken a turn for the worse. Can the Bey restore prosperity?"*

**Tunis** is an unrecognized regional power in North Africa. It borders [Constantine](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Constantine) to the west, [Touggourt](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Touggourt) and [Ahaggar](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Ahaggar) to the southwest and [Tripolitania](Tripolitania.md) to the east. Tunis starts the game as a protectorate of the [Ottoman Empire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey).

Tunis can form [Maghreb](Maghreb.md).

## Formation

If it ceases to exist, Tunis can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country controlling its capital and only core state of Tunisia.

### Interest groups

Tunis begins with the Landowners in government. The Sunni Ulema, Armed Forces, Rural Folk, and Petite Bourgeoisie are in opposition, while the Intelligentsia, Industrialists, and Trade Unions are marginalized.

### France

See also:
French_content#The_Proclamation_of_Algiers
|  | Available only with the [Voice of the People](Voice of the People.md) DLC enabled. |
|---|---|

It is necessary to monitor [French](France.md) activity in [Algeria](Algeria.md); once France controls the three states directly or via subjects, it receives claims on Tunis via a [journal entry](French content.md), making France an existential threat. A solution would be to take a state away from [Constantine](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Constantine) to prevent France from completing the journal entry. They are weaker on paper, but it is close, so it can prove a difficult war if Tunis is not careful. Especially since [Egypt](Egypt.md) can be prone to help them. Note that taking [Touggourt](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Touggourt) does *not* help; in fact it makes it easier for France to complete the journal entry.

### The Turkish Tanzimat reforms and Independence

The Ottoman [Tanzimat reforms](Turkish content.md) will grant Tunis independence after 30 years, if [The Ottomans](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey) fail to make the reforms. There is however not much, if anything, Tunis can do to provoke nor prevent it, but it is important to prepare for. Therefore industrial construction has to strike a balance between finding a lucrative niche in the Ottoman market, while also prepare a well rounded industry for eventual and sudden independence.
