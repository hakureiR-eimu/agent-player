*"The Congress of Vienna established the German Confederation, a loose assortment of German intended to keep the peace in Central Europe. The Confederation in practice is dominated by the rivalry between Austria and Prussia. Can the smaller nations survive the coming conflict?"*

**Württemberg** is a minor power in South Germany. It is is able to form the [South German Federation](South German Federation.md) and [Germany](Germany.md). As an alternative, Württemberg can also form the [Confederation of the Rhine](Confederation of the Rhine.md).

### Interest groups

Due to Württemberg's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Württemberg begins with the Evangelical Church and Landowners in government. The Landvolk, Kleinbürger, Armed Forces, and Intelligentsia are in opposition, while the Industrialists and Trade Unions are marginalized.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.10. |
|---|---|

Württemberg has the Professional Army law.

Württemberg begins with 14 Battalions and can call upon 5 Conscripts. All barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions comprise a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| Königlich Württembergische Armee | 8 Line Infantry 2 Cannon Artillery 2 Dragoons 2 Lancers | Prince Friedrich von Württemberg Moriz Miller | South Germany |

Württemberg is landlocked and thus has no flotillas at the start of the game.
