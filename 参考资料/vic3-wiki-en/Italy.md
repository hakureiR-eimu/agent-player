**Italy** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in the region Italy. With all Italian Homelands, it takes up the whole Italian peninsula, as well as the island of Sardinia. It borders [France](France.md) in the west and [Austria](Austria.md) in the north, with Austria controlling some Italian states. At the start of the game, there are seven Italian nations: [Sardinia-Piedmont](Sardinia-Piedmont.md), [Parma](Parma.md), [Modena](Modena.md), [Lucca](Lucca.md), [Tuscany](Tuscany.md), [Papal States](Papal States.md), and [Two Sicilies](Two Sicilies.md).

If Italy was formed by Papal States and is a Theocracy, it is called *Kingdom of Heaven*.

As a North Italian country, Italy is able to form [Central Europe](Central Europe.md).[1]

## Historical background

Historically, Italy was unified by the Kingdom of Sardinia in 1861, two years after the Italian War of 1859 where it won Lombardy from Austria and occupied central Italy with French support.

12 of the 17 core states are needed in order to form Italy
Italy can be formed by any North or South Italian country of Empire tier or below which controls at least 12 out of the 17 North and South Italian homelands. Savoy can lose its North Italian homeland though the *Treaty of Turin* decision, in which case only 11 core states are required.

As Italy is a [major unification](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Major_unification), it can be formed without holding the required states directly. Instead, a *unification candidate* can gain the support of other Italian countries. The candidate must be at least a minor power, as long as there are no higher-rank candidates, and all nations with South Italian or North Italian culture must have researched Nationalism.

### Core states

Italy's core states are entirely within the Southern Europe strategic region. Four of Italy's core states − Istria, Lombardy, South Tyrol, and Venetia – are controlled by [Austria](Austria.md) at the start of the game, and thus are not gainable by peaceful unification unless Austria is a subject of an Italian country or the states are directly controlled by an Italian country.

Corsica and Malta are not core states of Italy, but are in the Italy region and will be claimed on formation.

## Industry and resources

Italy has a large amount of resources available. Unusually for Europe, numerous Italian states can support Silk Plantations and Sugar Plantations. Iron, coal, lead, sulfur, and even oil are also available.

The state of Sicily has both 100 levels of potential sulfur mines as well as the Sicilian Sulfur Deposits state trait giving **+20%** sulfur mine throughput. This makes it the single best sulfur producing state in the entire world.

### Vatican City

Lazio has the monument *Vatican City*. If monument effects are enabled in the [game rules](Game rule.md), Vatican City employs 500 clergymen and provides the following for Catholic countries:

- **+100** Authority
- **+100** Influence
- **+25%** Devout political strength
- **+20** [Urbanization](Urbanization.md)

Non-Catholic (including Atheist) countries gain **+25** prestige instead, with no employment required.

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.10. |
|---|---|

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Italy
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

### Traditional unification tips

Italy can be formed by either controlling or receiving the vote of 12 states (11 if Sardinia-Piedmont has given Savoy to France via the [Treaty of Turin](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Treaty_of_Turin) decision). The amount of states *not* owned by Austria at game start happens to be 13, so it is possible to form Italy completely peacefully, by getting the support of every other state except one.

Note that peacefully annexing Italian minors will still generate [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy) and lower [relations](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Relations) with countries who have an interest in the Italian region, if they are annexed diplomatically before the formation of Italy.

#### Evaluating formation candidates

Although historically formed by [Sardinia-Piedmont](Sardinia-Piedmont.md), in Victoria 3, it is arguably [Two Sicilies](Two Sicilies.md) that offers one of the easier paths to Italian unification. Two Sicilies has the largest population and strongest military among the Italian minors, giving it a strong claim to leadership among the eligible unification candidates. Sardinia-Piedmont, on the other hand, probably have the best growth potential. They have a lot of industry and available raw resources, as well as fairly powerful military, though weaker than Two Sicilies. They also have much better [technologies](Technology.md) and [literacy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Literacy), as well as a relatively easy way to receive help from [France](France.md) via the [Treaty of Turin](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Treaty_of_Turin). A good deal behind those two, [Papal States](Papal States.md) is the third best candidate. They have decent technologies and literacy, the third biggest population (although significantly behind the two big candidates) and the Vatican City [unique building](Unique building.md), but they are lacking in crucial industrial resources.

Any other nation will have to deal with the fact that Two Sicilies is unlikely to stop being a major power by the time when Nationalism is researched, so one should prepare for at least one war to take leadership from them, and possibly a unification war against them as well. Conquering land from them before researching Nationalism is also possible.

### Risorgimento

Risorgimento has a unique unification mechanic as it allows annexation of neighboring countries so long as there are no other unification candidate for Italy. This can be achieved either by becoming a major power before Risorgimento starts and keeping all other Italians at minor power or below, or defeating any other candidates in a leadership war. Additionally, countries that have a revolution by a compatible political movement Anarchist, Communist, Nihilist, Socialist, Anti-Slavery, Liberal, Positivist, Proletarian, Radical, Ethno-Nationalist, Fascist, or Modernizer may be annexed by another Italian power.

### Immediately after formation

When Italy forms, especially when done so diplomatically, it is likely that your country will grow in size and population 4-6 fold. It is therefore *very important* to pause the game and thoroughly analyze the new situation.

There are likely going to be problems with market shortages, sub-optimal production methods, [infrastructure](Infrastructure.md) shortages, insufficient administration and an amount of [construction sectors](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Construction_sectors) that is either woefully insufficient or way too expensive.

Especially the state of Emilia has to be looked at, since it has come from three separate independent states, and is thus likely to be in a very strange state.

### Austria

Austria holds four core Italian states, but has a large military, so the support of another great power, such as [Russia](Russia.md), [Prussia](Prussia.md) or [France](France.md) is almost certainly necessary, especially if Austria has a Great Power ally or two. Lombardy and Venetia should be prioritized as they are very valuable provinces with large populations and valuable resources. While supporting *Risorgimento*, activism of an Italian minority movement can be increased, potentially forcing a rebellion by [San Marco](San Marco.md). Especially while the *Springtime of the Peoples* journal entry is active, as political movements will have greatly increased activism. If Austria loses [Hungary](Hungary.md) before establishing the Dual Monarchy, it will trigger the event *Crisis on the Danube*; the AI has a 90% chance to retreat to the Erblande, releasing non-German states, including [Lombardia](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Lombardia) and [Venice](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Venice) as independent countries, who will be easy to get on your side.

Austria also guarantees the independence of the Italian minors in the state of Emilia via membership of their [power bloc](Power bloc.md). Austria's power bloc has the *Defensive Cooperation* principle, which prevents bloc members from fighting each other. Two Sicilies, Tuscany, Modena and Parma start as part of this power bloc. Of the three, [Modena](Modena.md) has the highest priority of annexation by Italy as it allows a land connection between the two halves of Italy, while [Lucca](Lucca.md) has a coastline. If one uses the *Risorgimento* mechanic of annexation by a compatible political movement Anarchist, Communist, Nihilist, Socialist, Anti-Slavery, Liberal, Positivist, Proletarian, Radical, Ethno-Nationalist, Fascist, or Modernizer, Austria can do little to stop them from joining the Italian union.

To weaken Austria further, consider denying her a coastline. Capturing Venetia and Istria and releasing [Croatia](Croatia.md) will turn Austria into a land-locked country. Releasing [Bohemia](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Bohemia) or [Hungary](Hungary.md) will also weaken Austria significantly.

### France's natural borders

|  | Available only with the [Voice of the People](Voice of the People.md) DLC enabled. |
|---|---|

If France activates the [journal entry](French content.md), it will receive a claim on Savoy. Needless to say, this makes France dangerous. It is advised to seek an ally or defensive pact with a GP rival of France (usually [Great Britain](Great Britain.md)). Alternatively, the owner can peacefully let France have the state along with Nice for an obligation via the [Treaty of Turin](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Treaty_of_Turin) [journal entry](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Journal_entry), as was done historically. The obligation is useful when going against Austria.
