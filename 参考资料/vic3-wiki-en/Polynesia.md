**Polynesia** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in the Oceania strategic region. It represents a political union of Austronesian Oceania plus Taiwan.

## Historical background

Most of the Polynesian islands were settled by Austronesian peoples from Taiwan in several waves: settling in maritime Southeast Asia and the western Pacific islands around 3000-3500 years ago, and then the eastern Pacific islands around 1000-2000 years ago. Most islands remained under local authority until the mid-1800s as European powers began colonizing the Pacific. By the 20th century, the entire Pacific was effectively divided between the Europeans, the United States, and Imperial Japan. After World War II, most of the Pacific islands became independent countries, with only the United States and France retaining any significant territories.

Polynesia retains the state religion of the nation that forms it.

## Strategy and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Polynesia
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
**Starting as United Tribes**

[United Tribes](United Tribes.md) starts as protectorate of Great Britain, and can use the *Federate Australia* decision. These are the two major advantage that makes this a strong and relatively easy country to form Polynesia. The eventual gold mines is also added bonus.

In general, the player will want to rush colonization, and at the same time build GDP higher than their Australian neighbors while maintaining amicable relations with them and with [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) to make use of the *Federate Australia* decision.

**Technologies**

Their technology is comparable to [Hawaii](Hawaii.md). The player will want to research Colonization first, to colonize the South Island, and then rush Nationalism as a second or third technology, possibly after Stock Exchange, to make use of the *Federate Australia* decision. Afterwards they might want to rush Quinine in order to colonize the New Guineas and the Pacific Islands as soon as possible.

**Colonization and Expansion**

Some of the states are owned by other countries.

Formosa is owned by [Great Qing](China.md). [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) can be swayed to help take it, preferrably early on while the Qing is still on Irregular infantry and suffering from the crippling *Widespread Opium Addiction* modifier.

[Tidore](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Tidore) is owned by the [Dutch East Indies](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Dutch_East_Indies). The player can sway Britain to take the state, or other states/subjects owned by the Dutch East if the player wants to.

[Hawaii](Hawaii.md) starts with practically no military, the player can right away conquer them with the starting army and just one ship.

[Tahiti](Tahiti.md) is also weak by itself. If [France](France.md) is preoccupied in a war elsewhere, they're unlikely to interfere. Alternatively, sway Britain.

The player will want to rush Quinine to colonize the rest of the required states as soon as possible.

**Impassable Pacific Islands**

The hardest part of forming Polynesia is arguably colonizing the Pacific Islands. In current version, exist a bug: if a state is being colonized, and other powers choose to colonize it, the state can end up with one or more provinces permanently uncolonizable. This is because some of the provinces are marked as impassable in the game files. In vanilla, the player can't fix it, but can get around it in some ways: by rushing colonization technologies to colonize as fast as possible; by colonizing the islands one at a time; or by making other powers to not colonize in the region, through treaty or through war. Short of these, the player can reload to an earlier save and hope for the RNG to be in their favor.

**Independence**

Being a protectorate means the player is at risk of becoming a puppet. It does not stop the player from colonizing, but it stops them from having an independent diplomacy, and also having to pay money to the overlord. Which is why the player might want to use the ability to do diplomacy and take the necessary states as early as possible before [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) puppets the player.

Independence can be achieved peacefully by completing the *Federate Australia* entry, by fully owning 7 of the 9 states required to form Australia. But because non-Australian country is not eligible to form it, they will become independent instead. As such, the player will have to prepare for a more independent economy, or hold off completing the entry until they're ready.

**Greater Polynesia**

The player can expand into countries or states that share similar culture or heritage, including [Madagascar](Madagascar.md), and countries in the Indonesia and Indochina region.
