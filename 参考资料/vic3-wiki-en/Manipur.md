**Manipur** is an unrecognized power in South Asia. It is one of the "princely states", a puppet of the [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company), and thus a part of the [British Empire](Great Britain.md).

Sadly, Manipur **cannot** form [India](India.md), nor any other formable.

### Interest groups

Manipur starts with the Zamindars in government. The Armed Forces, Hindu Priesthood, Intelligentsia, and Rural Folk are in opposition. The remaining interest groups are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Manipur is a puppet of the [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company) and thus a member of the *British Empire* power bloc.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.12. |

Manipur has the Professional Army law.

Manipur starts with 8 Battalions and cannot call on any Conscripts at the start of the game. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
