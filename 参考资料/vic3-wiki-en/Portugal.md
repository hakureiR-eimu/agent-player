**Portugal** is a minor power in western Europe. Portugal neighbors [Spain](Spain.md) on the Iberian peninsula and maintains a number of colonial holdings in Africa and Asia. As a Portuguese country, Portugal can form [Iberia](Iberia.md).

If Portugal can annex[1] [Brazil](Brazil.md) or bring them under a Personal Union while remaining a Monarchy, it is called *United Kingdom*.

## Historical Background

In 1807, Portugal refused Napoleon's demand to accede to the Continental System of embargo against [Great Britain](Great Britain.md); a [French](France.md) invasion under General Junot followed, and Lisbon was captured in 1807. British intervention in the Peninsular War helped maintain Portuguese independence; the last French troops were expelled in 1812.

Rio de Janeiro in Brazil was the Portuguese capital between 1808-21. In 1820, constitutionalist insurrections took place at Porto and Lisbon. Lisbon regained its status as the capital of Portugal when [Brazil](Brazil.md) declared its independence in 1822. The death of King [John VI](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/en:John_VI_of_Portugal)[wp 1] in 1826 led to a crisis of royal succession. His eldest son, [Pedro I of Brazil](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/en:Pedro_I_of_Brazil)[wp 1], briefly became Pedro IV of Portugal, but neither the Portuguese nor Brazilians wanted a unified monarchy; consequently, Pedro abdicated the Portuguese crown in favor of his 7-year-old daughter, [Maria da Glória](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/en:Maria_II_of_Portugal)[wp 1], on the condition that when she came of age she would marry his brother, Miguel. Dissatisfaction at Pedro's constitutional reforms led the "absolutist" faction of landowners and the church to proclaim Miguel king in February 1828. This led to the Liberal Wars in which Pedro forced Miguel to abdicate and go into exile in 1834 and place his daughter on throne as Queen Maria II. After 1815, the Portuguese expanded their trading ports along the African coast, moving inland to take control of Angola and Mozambique. The slave trade was abolished in 1836. In Portuguese India, trade flourished in the colony of Goa, with its subsidiary colonies of Macau, near Hong Kong, and Timor, north of Australia. The Portuguese successfully introduced Catholicism and the Portuguese language into their colonies, although most settlers continued to head to [Brazil](Brazil.md).

### Interest groups

Portugal begins with the Petite Bourgeoisie leading the Cartist Party and are joined by the Armed Forces in government. In opposition, the Intelligentsia lead the Vintist Party, and are joined by the Rural Folk and the Industrialists, while the Landowners and the Catholic Church are not affiliated with any party due to their Miguelist leaders. The Trade unions are marginalized.

The Petite Bourgeoisie have the Cartist ideology instead of the default Meritocratic, making them more conservative regarding electoral systems and supporters of Appointed Bureaucrats. They may also gain Regenerator through a journal entry. The Landowners also have Pro-Slavery (colonial), while the Intelligentsia start with Constitutionalist ideology instead of Republican. Lastly, the Armed Forces have the ideology Liberal Praetorian, making them proponents of Census Suffrage and National Supremacy.

Portugal begins with the modifier *Concession of Évora Monte Concession of Évora Monte Country modifier +25% Radicals from low Legitimacy +25% Intelligentsia Political Strength −75% Landowners Political Strength +25% Petite Bourgeoisie Political Strength −50% Miguelist Movement Pop Attraction*, which is decaying and lasts for another 10 years since game start.

### Political Movements

Portugal begins suppressing the Miguelist movement. This movement also replaces the Absolutist movement other monarchies may have.

### Diplomacy

Portugal owes [Spain](Spain.md) an obligation.

Portugal's starting interest levels are pervasive (level 4) in Southern Europe; influential (level 3) in South China, West Africa, and Western Europe; and engaged (level 2) in Balkans, Central Europe, East Africa, Equatorial Africa, Indonesia, North Africa, North India, and South India.

Portugal has a pro-country lobby towards [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) ()

Portugal starts with three nearly identical [treaties](Treaty.md) of the same name with [France](France.md), [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) and [Spain](Spain.md), the *Treaty of the Quadruple Alliance*, that are in practical terms a four-party treaty where the four countries all have a defensive pact with each other. In addition, Portugal also gives Great Britain investments rights and transfers 20 units of each week, while Portugal gets trade advantages and military assistance from Great Britain. All three treaties are bound for 10 years. It also has a treaty with [Great Qing](China.md): the *Luso-Chinese Agreement*, where Portugal transfers 150 to China each week in exchange for a treaty port in Macau. Uniquely, this agreement is bound for 99 years, practically the entire game.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Portugal starts with a mostly agrarian economy, having few industries at the start, but a good amount of agricultural buildings, plantations, logging camps, and fishing wharves.

**Resources**

Portugal has access to a modest amount of iron and sulfur, as well as a small amount of coal, lead, and even gold, which should help with its starting debt. Portugal also has the ability to build every type of plantation.

## Strategy and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Portugal
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

### Politics

Portugal starts with an unusually progressive Queen bearing the Reformer ideology, and is aligned with the Petit Bourgeoisie and the Cartist Party (which also includes the Armed Forces at game start). Inviting the Vintist Party's Intelligentsia and Rural Folk can make it possible to quickly push for liberal laws.

### Economy

With the Iberian Twilight DLC, Portugal starts the game with a significant amount of debt and a negative balance of -3.55k even before construction is queued. This can be offset by placing consumption taxes on wine and services, and raising taxes to high. Removing the country's one construction sector and relying on a constant stream of 10 free construction can also help cut costs while reorganizing the economy. Half of the deficit is due to the *Devorismo* modifier, which can be canceled by balancing the budget and paying back all of Portugal's debt. Ironically, one of the possible benefits of the Journal Entry that removes *Devorismo* reduces interest rates, incentivizing going straight back into debt after hitting zero.

A somewhat cheesy method to clear the debt can be done immediately when starting the game, which is by using the 'Take on Debt' article with [Great Britain](Great Britain.md). Then, set tax to Very High. When the game is unpaused, the Journal Entry will be completed.

Portuguese economy is mainly agricultural in the start of the game, possessing only 4 factories. Portugal does possess, however, the resources needed to build an industrial sector, and has easy access to the [Chinese](China.md) market through Macao. The Invest in Macao Journal Entry available with Iberian Twilight allows Portugal to spend a relatively small amount of funds to boost Macao's local economy.

[Great Britain](Great Britain.md) has investment rights in Portugal, which can both be a blessing and a curse, depending on what they actually choose to build.

### Conquest

Portugal early on has little chance at any domination attempts in Europe, due to its small army. However, it is enough so the player can conquer powerful gold producing states early on, such as [Oranje](Oranje.md) and [Transvaal](Transvaal.md) (via [Gaza](Gaza.md)), as well as partially colonize the Northern Cape, which lacks the malaria modifier. [Brunei](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Brunei) is also a target to keep in mind, although any rebellion here will be more difficult to suppress as you will need to do a naval invasion. You may also conquer parts of China as long as the player maintains good enough relations with the British. It should be noted that Portugal starts with a defensive pact with the British, which protects the player from being invaded by both Spain and other British colonies that might desire territories you possess.
