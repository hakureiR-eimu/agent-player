Capacities, the game's "currencies" as shown on the top-left corner near the country's flag.
**Capacities** are three of the four main "currencies" in the game (with money as the fourth). Each capacity represents one specific area of the country's ability to govern and is used solely for matters relating to that area.

## Generation and usage

Capacities are not pooled resources, instead having a constant generation and usage. The methods of generation and uses vary by capacity, as outlined in the following sections. Generally, certain [society technologies](Society technology.md) provide percentage increases to capacities, as do certain happy interest groups, certain [monuments](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Monuments), and some event modifiers. All countries have a base **+100** of each capacity.

Beyond their direct uses, each capacity provides a benefit if generation exceeds usage (a *surplus*) and a penalty if usage exceeds generation (a *deficit*). The benefit of a surplus or penalty of a deficit is scaled by the relative size of the surplus or deficit compared to total generation of that capacity, with the maximum benefit when usage is half or less of generation, or maximum penalty when usage is twice or more of generation. For example, a capacity with **100** generation would have a full surplus at **50** or less usage, or conversely full deficit with **200** or more usage.

Most actions requiring a certain amount of a capacity cannot be taken unless the full amount is currently available; however, most actions are not automatically cancelled by a deficit.

## Capacity types

### Bureaucracy

Bureaucracy represents a country's ability to collect taxes, run institutions, and employ generals and admirals. It is generated [government administration](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Government_administration) buildings. An additional **+10%** is available from the Petite Bourgeoisie's *Middle Managers* trait, or **+20%** if they are powerful.

Bureaucracy is used for the following:

- Incorporated states – Base **10** and an additional **1** per 25,000 population; this cost is applied when incorporating states, too, even before the full benefits of incorporation apply.
- [Institutions](Institutions.md) – **1** per level per 100,000 population in incorporated states, with a minimum of **10** per level.
- [Buildings](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Buildings) – **1** per nationally owned level.
- [Generals](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Generals) and [admirals](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Admirals) – Base **10** plus **5** per level they are promoted to a max of **30**.
- As well as certain [Journal entries](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Journal_entries) or events like [surveying the Suez Canal](Unique building.md).

Bureaucracy deficit is called *Administrative Overburden* and its effect scales up to the following at full deficit:

- +100% Government Dividends Waste
- +100% Tax Waste

Bureaucracy surplus is called *Efficient Bureaucracy* and its effect scales up to the following at full surplus:

- +10% State Construction Efficiency

### Authority

Authority represents the head of state's personal power and ability to enact change in the country through decrees. It is generated by the country's [laws](Laws.md) – generally, the more repressive and authoritarian the country, the more authority it generates. A country's ruler also generates or reduces authority equal to their popularity (e.g. a ruler with 50 popularity generates +50 authority while ruling).

An additional **+50%** Authority is available via [Society technologies](Society technology.md) (**+10%** each from Mass Communication, Nationalism, Pan-Nationalism, Political Agitation, and Mass Propaganda). The Devout's happy trait *Divine Right* also provides **+10%** Authority, or **+20%** if they are powerful.

Authority is used for the following:

- Issuing [decrees](Decrees.md) – **100** for most
- Bolstering or suppressing [political movements](Political movement.md) – **200**
- Enacting consumption taxes – **100** to **500** depending on good.
- Giving a [corporate charter](Companies.md), when above the charter limit - **100**
- Instituting a state monopoly on a building - **100**

Authority deficit is called *Political Dysfunction* and its effect scales up to the following at full deficit:

- −10 Opposition Interest Group Approval
- +20% Radicals from Political Movements

Authority surplus is called *Legislative Efficiency* and its effect scales up to the following at full surplus:

- +25% Enactment Speed

### Influence

Influence represents a country's ability to conduct [diplomacy](Diplomacy.md). It is generated primarily from a country's [rank](Rank.md). Rivalries and certain [monuments](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Monuments) and [journal entries](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Journal_entries) also generate influence. The Landowners's happy trait *Family Ties* also provides **+10%** influence, or **+20%** if they are powerful.

Each rank provides a certain amount of influence as well as modifying the cost of diplomatic pacts.

| Rank | Influence | Diplomatic pact cost |  |
|---|---|---|---|
|  | Great power | 1000 | **+100%** |
|  | Major power | 750 | **+50%** |
| Unrecognized major power | 750 | **+0%** |  |
|  | Minor power | 600 | **+0%** |
| Unrecognized regional power | 600 | **−25%** |  |
|  | Insignificant power | 500 | **+0%** |
| Unrecognized power | 500 | **−50%** |  |

Influence is used to establish and maintain [diplomatic pacts](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Diplomatic_pacts).

Influence deficit is called *Diplomatic Overreach* and its effect scales up to the following at full deficit:

- −50% Prestige

Influence surplus is called *Diplomatic Mitigation* and its effect scales up to the following at full surplus:

- +25% Infamy Decay
- +25% Leverage Generation
