**Angoche** is an unrecognized power located in the Zanj [strategic region](Strategic region.md) of Africa. It borders [Portuguese Mocambique](Portugal.md) to the west and east, as well as the decentralized nation of [Maravi](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Maravi) to the north.

### Interest groups

Due to Wadai's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Angoche starts with the Landowners in government. The Sunni Ulema and Armed Forces are in opposition, while the Intelligentsia, Trade Unions, Rural Folk, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Industrialists are marginalized.
