*"Currently under relatively autonomous administration, both the Hudson’s Bay Company and the US government have set their eyes on the disputed Columbia district. Will either power succeed, or will an independent Oregon emerge?"*

**Oregon** (initially called *Columbia District*) is an insignificant power located in the Pacific Coast [strategic region](Strategic region.md) of North America. It starts the game as a colony of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md).

As an Anglo-Canadian country, Oregon can form [Canada](Canada.md); as a Yankee country, it can form the [Free States of America](Free States of America.md) and [United States of America](America.md).

## Historical Background

Since the 1560s, Oregon had been reached by various European explorers and traders – including Spanish, Russian, British, and American. Lewis and Clark reached Oregon in the winter of 1805-1806 and many more Americans followed in their footsteps along along the Oregon trail, sparking tensions between the [United States](America.md), [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), and the local native tribes over land claims.

The "[Oregon Question](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/en:Oregon_boundary_dispute)[wp 1]" was a point of contentious debate between the [United States](America.md) and [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) that was supposed to be settled by the [Oregon Treaty of 1846](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/en:Oregon_Treaty)[wp 1], but poor wording of the treaty caused the treaty to only be a partial fix. Tensions nearly boiled over into all-out war between the United States and Britain in what became known as the "[Pig War](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/en:Pig_War_(1859))[wp 1]" of 1859. Further talks were put on hold due to the American Civil War, but in 1871, the [Treaty of Washington](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/en:Treaty_of_Washington_(1871))[wp 1] was signed, formally ending the dispute.

Today, the territories of British Columbia are split between the Canadian province of British Columbia and the American States of Washington, Oregon, and Idaho.

## Formation

If Oregon ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country that controls its capital, Oregon. Its core states are Oregon, Southern Columbia, Idaho, and Northern Columbia.

### Interest groups

Oregon begins with the Industrialists in government, with no party initially. In opposition. the Petite Bourgeoisie and Armed Forces typically form the Liberal-Conservative Party, while the Intelligentsia and the Trade Unions form the Liberal Party. and the Yeoman Farmers are without a party at the start. The Landowners and Anglican Churchare marginalized.

The Yeoman Farmers have the Jeffersonian ideology instead of the default Agrarian.

### Diplomacy

Oregon is a colony of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) and thus part of the *British Empire* power bloc. It borders the [Hudson’s Bay Company](Hudson’s Bay Company.md), the [Russian-American Company](Alaska.md), and [Mexico](Mexico.md)'s states in California and Nevada, as well as the decentralized nations of [Pannakwati](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Pannakwati), [Nimíipuu](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Nim%C3%ADipuu), [Séliš](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/S%C3%A9li%C5%A1), [Niitsitapi](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Niitsitapi) and [Athabaska](Athabaska.md).

Oregon has a claim on Idaho, which it is colonizing.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Oregon begins with no urban and no agricultural buildings.

**Resources**

Oregon starts out with only 2 Logging Camps, but its states are rich in both wood and coal. While they lack immediate access to iron, the state of Montana has a small amount of it - but it cannot be colonized due to being claimed by the [United States](America.md). Being part of the [British](Great Britain.md) market will likely account for any needs Ontario - and Canada - may have in other regards.

## Strategies and guides

### Achievements

Oregon is in a good position for the *Amish Paradise* achievement, since it does not start with any industry and therefore a very weak industrialist IG, thus making the law much easier to pass. Especially since Oregon is a [dominion](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Dominion) of Great Britain, as it can then specialize within the broader British market, using a multitude of [decrees](Decrees.md) on their main states.
