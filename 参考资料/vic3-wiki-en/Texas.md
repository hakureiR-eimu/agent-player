*"The newly declared Republic of Texas fights a seemingly impossible war against its Mexican overlords. Will a miracle save the Lone Star State?"*

**Texas** is an insignificant power in Dixie. Texas neighbors the [USA](America.md) and [Indian Territory](Indian Territory.md) to the east and [Mexico](Mexico.md) and the decentralized nation [Comanche](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Comanche) to the west. Texas begins at war with Mexico in the Texan Revolution.

Texas is able to form the [USA](America.md) if the latter ceases to exist.

## Historical background

Parts of the region were lightly colonized by [Spain](Spain.md) in the 1700s, primarily at San Antonio. When [Mexico](Mexico.md) achieved independence from Spain in 1821, Texas became part of the new country. Prior to and following independence, Texas was a tension point between, first Spain, then Mexico and the [United States](America.md) as many Americans migrated and settled in the region following the Louisiana purchase. Initially after independence, Mexico encouraged the immigration, but reversed course in 1830 as it grew concerned about the American settlers often bringing and keeping their slaves with them, even after Mexico abolished slavery in 1829.

The Texan Revolution began on October 2, 1835, among rising tension across Mexico over the replacement of the Mexican federal constitution with a central constitution. After a small number of battles, including the famous Alamo, the Texan army, led by Sam Houston, defeated the army of Mexican general Santa Anna at San Jacinto on April 21, 1836, and captured him shortly after the battle. A prisoner of war, he was compelled to sign a peace treaty recognizing Texas' independence.

Texas remained an independent republic for the following decade until annexation to the United States was negotiated and adopted in 1845. Mexico protested the annexation, having never completely come to terms with the peace-treaty negotiated at gun point, which lead directly to the Mexican-American war the next year.

## Formation

If it ceases to exist, Texas can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country controlling its capital and only core state of Texas.

### Interest groups

Due to Texas's
Census Suffrage law, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
The Southern Planters and Evangelicals typically comprise the Democratic Party while the Armed Forces are typically the sole members of the Patriotic Party. The Petite Bourgeoisie and Yeoman Farmers are typically unaffiliated with any parties, though the Petite Bourgeoisie sometimes join the Democratic Party. The Trade Unions, Industrialists, and Intelligentsia are marginalized.

Texas's starting government is often the Patriotic Party and Petite Bourgeoisie, but it can also be the Democratic Party.

The Southern Planters have the Elitist ideology instead of the default Paternalistic, and they also have the Pro-Slavery ideology in addition to the rest. The Yeoman Farmers have the Jeffersonian ideology instead of the default Agrarian.

### Diplomacy

Texas begins at war with [Mexico](Mexico.md), where losing will result in the annexation of Texas. Texas has claims on the states of Texas and New Mexico. If Texas wins its war with Mexico, it can be annexed by or become a Protectorate of the United States via the event [The Lone Star State](Texas.md).

Texas starts with a pro-country lobby towards the [USA](America.md) with the Southern Planters and Yeoman Farmers as members. Texas also receives military assistance from the United States.

Texas starts with a [treaty](Treaty.md) with [America](America.md): *American-Texian Military Assistance*, where USA is providing military assistance and goods transfer of 10 small arms and 1 man-o-wars.

Texas has **1.3** infamy at the start of the game.

### Winning the independence war

The event [General Santa Anna Captured](Texas.md) is of crucial importance, since Texas can win the war by just winning a single battle. In a straight fight, Texas would stand no chance, and especially not once [Mexico](Mexico.md) starts to win. Therefore the entire war is likely won or lost in the first battle. When the game starts, the entire Mexican army is not at the front, but they soon will be. If Mexico is advancing from day 1, Texas can sit back and fight defensively. Texas has a unique modifier that increases defense by **15%** as well as two defensively orientated [generals](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/General). If Mexico does not advance, you will be forced to advance in order to provoke a battle before the rest of the Mexican army arrives.

To appease the Armed Forces, it is advised to attempt to pass National Guard, Dedicated Police Force, some kind of Colonial affairs law or Professional Army. Since the Armed Forces are powerful, you can get a massive **+20%** boost to both offense and defense, if you can get them all the way to loyal. Even if it is not possible, it is good to get the ['happy' trait](Interest group.md) of reduced military spending. After the war, you can choose if you actually intend to keep going with the law. Colonization is especially good, since it allows Texas to colonize the rest of its own state.

Texas starts the game with two tremendous defensive [generals](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/General) ( Stalwart Defender and Experienced Defensive Strategist respectively), but it does not hurt to look at the options to see if there is a truly magnificent one.

Otherwise, there are not many things to do. There is not capacity to build any more barracks nor raising any conscripts, and even if there was, there wouldn't be time to staff them.

### Maintaining independence

Once relations with [USA](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/USA) gets to **+20**, the event [The Lone Star State](Texas.md) will occur, which either makes Texas a Protectorate or gives USA claims on Texas.[1] So to maintain complete independence, it is crucial to prevent this from happening by either decreasing relations or expelling diplomats as an emergency measure. Cancelling the [treaty](Treaty.md) with USA as soon as possible is also advised.

Texas should however not get too cold with USA, otherwise risking USA simply declaring a conventional war.

Even if this is managed to perfection, once USA takes the [Manifest Destiny](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Manifest_Destiny) decision, they will get claims in Texas regardless. By then Texas must have secured protection from somewhere else, and [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) is really the only serious contender.

Alternatively, Texas could just accept to be a protectorate. It is not so bad after all. The American market is a strong one, and it allows Texas an extreme degree of specialization.

### As an American protectorate

If Texas accepts to be an American protectorate, there are many advantages. Being part of a much larger market makes specialization really valuable. Late game, Texas will get oil which on its own is a useful thing for a company. It also makes car manufactures really profitable. Early game, paper could be a good first company, since USA lacks a lot of it and Texas has Sulfur.

Once the truce with [Mexico](Mexico.md) runs out, Texas can attack them and call in USA for an obligation. This is however assuming that Texas' autonomy has not been decreased. To prevent this, it is best to maintain American relations at a healthy distance, as well as weakening the American pro-country lobby if the opportunity presents itself. Taking either money from Mexico or some additional Mexican states is possible too. Taking states close to the American border can turn USA hostile in time, but at that point the states can simply be sold to USA. It can be also be a serious strategy to take some Mexican states temporarily, avoid improving living conditions there, so the POPs migrate to Texas, and then sell the states back to Mexico if USA decides they want the states for themselves.

Colonizing the rest of Texas is a good idea, but one should be wary of colonizing the other American states (uncolonized Colorado is just next door), as it can also turn USA [hostile/domineering](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Attitude). As Texas cannot sell USA a colony, that hasn't been completed yet, it is a little more problematic.

### Achievements

Texas is one of the best countries suited for the *Reading Campaign* achievement, by virtue of being a single state, so decrees are more effective, and the fact that Texas will receive hordes of European migrants with better literacy.
