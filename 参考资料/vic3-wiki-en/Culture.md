**Culture** is a characteristic of both [pops](Pops.md) and [countries](Countries.md). Each pop has a culture, and each country has one or more *primary cultures*. Culture determines how much pops are accepted depending on the [citizenship law](Power structure laws.md) enacted.

Aside from acceptance and [country formation](Formable countries.md), cultures have no inherent mechanics nor advantages or disadvantages between each other.

## Culture traits

Each culture usually has two to four culture traits:

- One heritage trait
- One language trait
- Up to 4[1] tradition traits - in practice usually one or two, if any.

Culture traits determine how closely related two cultures are; most citizenship laws have high acceptance for pops which share cultural traits (or trait groups) with any of the country's primary cultures, based on all three types of traits.

### Heritage traits

From the [Vickypedia](Vickypedia.md): *"Heritage Traits represent the ancestry or foundation of a Culture or Religion. They also belong to a Trait Group, a broader family of similar Heritages."*

Heritage culture traits are commonly associated with the ethnic group of that culture, sometimes grouping ethnicities that were historically close: for example *Nordic* for Scandinavians and Finno-Karelians.

### Tradition traits

From the [Vickypedia](Vickypedia.md): *"Tradition Traits are rites, ideas and customs that Cultures might hold or pick up on over time that can be recognizable despite very distinct Heritages or Languages. Contrary to Heritage and Language Traits, Traditions do not belong to any Group."*

Tradition traits represent similarities or shared ideas and understanding between cultures that are not related to their ethnic origins nor languages: e.g. Russosphere for loyal subjects of the Russian Empire, or Sinosphere for cultures that all use the Chinese writing system despite vast ethnic and linguistic differences. Unlike heritage and language traits, which are immutable, tradition traits can be removed or added during gameplay, and are not grouped into cultures.

### Acceptance

Main article:
Acceptance
Acceptance represents how a pop is treated and considered within a country. A pop's acceptance status is determined primarily from its culture and [religion](Religion.md) relative to the country's Citizenship and Church and State laws, and determines political power, wage, radicalism among other things.

Each pop has an acceptance score between 0 and 100 that determines its status.

An agitator of a culture with less than Open Prejudice status cannot be invited to the country.

### Obsessions

Cultures may have or gain [obsessions](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Obsessions) in the game. These represent cultural fascinations with certain goods. Mechanically, pops whose culture has an obsession give that good double weight for [needs](Needs.md) consumption: they will consume slightly more goods overall, and due to the higher priority weight given to the target of the obsession, a larger proportion of the goods they consume to meet their needs will be the good that they're obsessed with.

## Cultural homelands

Each state is the *homeland* of one or more cultures, as indicated in the state's information screen. Pops are more likely to migrate to states that have their culture's homeland and many country formations require controlling a percentage of its primary cultures' homelands. A Pop's acceptance is also higher in homelands.

## Fervor

| Law | Fervor |
|---|---|
| Ethnostate | **+20** |
| National Supremacy | **+15** |
| Racial Segregation | **+10** |
| Cultural Exclusion | **+5** |
| Subjecthood | **0** |
| Multiculturalism | **−10** |

Cultural fervor is the measure of the national consciousness of a given culture. The fervor of each culture is tracked globally on a scale from 0 to 100. Fervor has a current value and a target value; each month the current value drifts towards the target value.

Target fervor for a given culture is increased by a number of factors. Many factors are scaled by the percentage of pops of that culture.

- Primary culture pops living in independent countries with citizenship laws other than Subjecthood or Multiculturalism, scaled by percentage
- Average [literacy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Literacy) of culture (up to **+35** at 100%)
- Casualties in war (up to **+30** if casualties equal 10% or more of the culture)
- Number of academics of this culture (**+2** per 1K, up to **25**)
- Pops living in countries that have Nationalism and Pan-nationalism (**+5** each), scaled by percentage
- Primary culture pops living in countries with Public Schools (**+10**), scaled by percentage
- Having a national awakening (**+20**)

Fervor can also be decreased in a few ways.

- Primary culture pops living in independent countries with Multiculturalism, scaled by percentage
- Global average acceptance (**−0.05** per point of acceptance, **−5** at 100)

Events and other content can also increase or decrease fervor, either directly or by changing the target level.

### Effects of fervor

A culture's fervor affects both pops and countries. Fervor reduces [assimilation](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Assimilation) in direct proportion; for example, 50 fervor reduces assimilation by 50%. Pops with high cultural fervor are also more inclined to join [cultural movements](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Political_movements) – both majority and minority. Cultural minority movements for a culture with a high fervor have increased baseline activism.

A high-fervor primary culture serves as a boon to a country in times of war and nudges domestic politics in a nationalistic direction. High cultural fervor on a primary culture reduces the rate at which [war support](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/War_support) declines – each point of highest fervor of primary cultures reduces weekly war support losses by 0.01 –, and, under non-Subjecthood Citizenship laws, increases [liberty desire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Liberty_desire) gain.

Primary-culture pops with high cultural fervor are more inclined to join cultural majority movements – funneling them away from troublesome opposition political movements, pressuring their interest groups, and increasing the rate at which Ethno-Nationalist politicians appear. By maintaining discriminatory Citizenship laws, one may render the privileged sections of one’s populace politically inert.

Additionally, AI countries with high-fervor primary cultures become more bold and aggressive when seeking to capture claims, seek independence from their overlords, or humiliate rivals.

[Subjects](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Subjects) can gain [liberty desire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Liberty_desire) from their primary culture's fervor, if they do not share all of their primary cultures with their overlord.

### National awakening

This section discusses the mechanic national awakening; for the DLC, see
National Awakening (DLC)
.
A culture which reaches 25 fervor has a chance to start a national awakening, the chance is increased with higher fervor. Cultures with an active national awakening gain **+20** additional fervor while it is active, and [political movements](Political movement.md) with this culture have greater pop attraction. A national awakening lasts for 10 years.
