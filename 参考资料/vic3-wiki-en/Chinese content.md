## Fragile Unity

China has a chance of collapsing into numerous smaller states if too much goes wrong, namely failing to avoid or weather various historical events that led the historical Qing Dynasty to collapse.

Each of the following events remove a point from the progress bar that, when depleted, triggers the *China Shatters* event. Repeatable events only remove progress the first time they occur.

- Losing the Opium Wars
- The appearance of the [Heavenly Kingdom](Heavenly Kingdom.md)
- Failing to defeat the Heavenly Kingdom
- Failing to defeat the Boxer rebellion
- Taking **any** Loans
- Having a civil war
- Becoming a [subject](Subject.md)
- Abolishing the monarchy

The following can reverse the progress of this bar:

- Winning the Opium Wars
- Becoming a [recognized power](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Recognized_power)
- Finishing the *Stamp Out Monarchism* journal entry
- Defeating the Boxer rebellion

## Opium Wars

This content represents the opium crisis in the Qing Empire in the early 1800s. It can potentially trigger for any large unrecognized country with a primary culture that has an opium [obsession](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Obsession). The Han culture starts with such an obsession, so this content typically triggers for [China](China.md) early in the game.

## The Boxer Rebellion

The Boxer Rebellion is a journal entry that requires China have the Pan-Nationalism technology, that any state region that is a Han culture homeland contain a state that is not entirely owned by China (usually [Portugal](Portugal.md)'s treaty port in Western Guangdong), and that the state has at least 10% radical Han pops. China succeeds at the journal entry if it fully owns all state regions that are Han culture homelands. Success grants a large boost in Loyalist as well as a minor stability boost and meeting one of the requirements to solve the Fragile Unity JE. Failure makes failure of the Fragile Unity JE more likely, as well as causing a massive gain in radicalism and/or the loss of Manchu as a primary culture.
