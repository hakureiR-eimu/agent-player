**California** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) located in the Pacific Coast region of western North America. Its sole core state, also called California, starts as an unincorporated state of [Mexico](Mexico.md).

As a Yankee country, California can form the [Free States of America](Free States of America.md) or reform the [USA](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/USA) if it ceases to exist; as a Mexican country, it can reform [Mexico](Mexico.md) if it ceases to exist.

## Historical background

Before European settlement, California held a large population of Native Americans. Europeans first arrived in the 1500s, but colonization did not start until the late 1700s, when Spain began establishing missions and a number of forts along the coast. When Mexico gain its independence from Spain in 1821, California became part of the new country as well, though a peripheral territory with little settlement.

California was conquered by the United States as part of the Mexican-American war (1846-1848). Just after the end of the war, gold was discovered, and Americans and many other migrated to California in hopes of striking it rich. California quickly became a US state and the major locus of US power on the Pacific coast.

## Core states

California single core state – also named California – has a sizable amount of sulfur and iron, as well as 300 units of Arable land to use between Cotton Plantations, Wheat Farms, Ranches, and Vineyards. The state of California gains a Yankee homeland if the [USA](America.md) completes the *Manifest Destiny: Mexican Cession* journal entry.

## Achievements

California is in a good position for the *Amish Paradise* achievement, since it does not start with any industry and therefore a very weak industrialist IG, thus making the law much easier to pass. Especially if California is released as a [puppet](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Puppet) of USA or Mexico, as it can then specialize within the broader American or Mexican market, using a multitude of [decrees](Decrees.md) on their single state.
