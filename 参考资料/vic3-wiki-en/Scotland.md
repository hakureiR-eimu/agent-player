**Scotland** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in the northern part of the British Isles. They start the game under [British](Great Britain.md) rule. Scotland can be released by any country that controls its capital.

Scotland is able to reform [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) if the latter ceases to exist.

## Core states

Scotland's two core states represent the historical kingdom of Scotland. These are also two of the three Scottish homelands, with the third in Ulster.
