*“Brazil is in a period of upheaval following the abdication of Emperor Pedro I, which left the country in the rule of regents. Will Brazil manage to overcome these obstacles and emerge as a great power?”*

**Brazil** is a major power in South America. It is the largest and most powerful country in its region, taking up most of the eastern half of South America. It does however have many problems. First and foremost, it has two ongoing civil wars when the game starts – against [Grão-Pará](Grão-Pará.md) and the [Riograndense Republic](Piratini.md). After reconquering its revolters, Brazil borders all countries in South America with the exception of [Chile](Chile.md) and [Ecuador](Ecuador.md), though the [Peru](Peru.md)vian states are inaccessible.

Brazil is one of four recommended countries for the *Economic Dominance* [objective](Objective.md), along with the [USA](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/USA), [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), and the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md).

## Historical Background

Brazil was originally a colony of Portugal, founded in the 1500s. At the end of 1807, when France and Spain invaded Portugal, the royal court was transferred from Lisbon to Rio de Janeiro. At the end of the Peninsular War, Brazil was elevated from a colony of Portugal to a kingdom in its own right, in personal union under the Portuguese monarchy. In 1820, a revolution in Portugal forced the royal court to return to Lisbon and further tried to revert Brazil to the status of a colony.

In 1822, Brazil declared independence, with Pedro I as emperor, finally winning recognition from Portugal in 1825. Emperor Pedro was beset by crises both within Brazil and in Portugal, where his daughter was queen. These crises led him to abdicate in 1831, leaving his five-year-old son as Emperor Pedro II. During Pedro II's regency, Brazil faced several revolts and rebellions, some of which last several years. Although Pedro was declared as reaching majority in 1840, the next three decades still held more struggle and conflict for Brazil. Still, the empire was able to develop its infrastructure and introduce liberal laws, culminating with the abolition of slavery in 1888. However, shortly after this, the military – led by republican ideals – executed a coup deposing Emperor Pedro II, who did not resist, in favor of a republic.

### Interest groups

The Intelligentsia are guaranteed to be in government, with the Catholic Church and Petite Bourgeoisie likely to join it in the Exalted Liberal Party. Of the interest groups in opposition, the Landowners are members of the Moderate Party, while the Industrialists and the Rural Folk are unaffiliated with a party. The Armed Forces and Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Intelligentsia have the Constitutionalist ideology instead of the default Republican.

### Diplomacy

Brazil starts in two separate civil wars: against the [Riograndense Republic](Piratini.md) and [Grão-Pará](Grão-Pará.md). Luckily, there are no other involved parties, and as the wars have already broken out, no outside parties can directly intervene.

Brazil has claims on the state of Amazonas, which is owned in part by Grão-Pará, [Bolivia](Bolivia.md), and [Colombia](Colombia.md). Brazil has a mutual rivalry with [Argentina](Argentina.md) and [Portugal](Portugal.md) starts with cold relations.

Brazil has a pro-country lobby () and anti-country lobby () towards [Great Britain](Great Britain.md).

Brazil has **13.4** infamy at the start of the game.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Brazil has minimal industry, with a somewhat more developed agricultural sector, notably in Coffee Plantations.

**Resources**

Brazil has several states with the *Amazon Rainforest* trait, boosting hardwood production. Combined with a very large potential for Logging Camps, wood production is really Brazil's trump card. The downside is that the Amazonan also decreases [infrastructure](Infrastructure.md) and [construction efficiency](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Construction). On top of that, there are multiple states with traits increasing agricultural production. The *Pampas* modifier in soon-to-be-reconquered Rio Grande do Sul gives **+15%** agricultural throughput and so does the *Pantanal* in Mato Grosso, although the latter comes with a **−10%** infrastructure reduction.

Later, both oil and rubber become available in Brazil. The one glaring lack is sulfur, which needs to be imported or a source colonized or conquered.

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.11. |

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Brazil
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
Brazil truly has the potential to be a world power. But it is certainly not a given, when looking at Brazil's many problems.

### Opening moves

The first thing to do is to win the two civil wars. They should not be much of a problem. The two generals should be able to handle both with the standing army alone. Hold one back in defense, while you take care of the other. Afterwards, Brazil can attack either [Colombia](Colombia.md), [Uruguay](Uruguay.md), or [Paraguay](Paraguay.md).

### Local expansion

Brazil can beat any other country in South America, and unifying all of South America is not out of the question. One can simply start from one end and continue from country to country. The only thing holding Brazil back is potential European or North American interference. So one should make sure to be diplomatically on the good side of [France](France.md), [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), and the [USA](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/USA). However, France and Britain are unlikely to commit a large army to defend Guyana, and Gibraltar and Spanish Al-Rif should be conquered for the Gibraltar toll.

One resource that Brazil completely lacks, Sulfur, is luckily found in abundance in [Peru](Peru.md), so long term, getting access to these mines should probably be a priority. However, should the Peru-Bolivian Confederation endure (as [Peru-Bolivia](Peru-Bolivia.md)), Brazil should perhaps look to [Chile](Chile.md) for sulfur mines. Silk and opium are harder to find, and Brazil has to go beyond South America to find silk; opium can be found in Cundinamarca, initially held by [Colombia](Colombia.md). Note that allowing Peru-Bolivia to form may trigger a negative event for Brazil (as well as create a credible rival), while preventing the formation can be done without infamy (via liberation; [Peru](Peru.md) will form from this). If the player is willing to spend infamy early in the game, protectorating [Bolivia](Bolivia.md) will dissolve the Confederation and form Peru as an indirect subject of Brazil, as long as North Peru is not released.

### Citizenship laws

Brazil's starting law, Racial Segregation, discriminates against Afro-Brazilians, a significant portion of the population pre-expansion. Consider getting Cultural Exclusion to have them accepted; note that this requires slavery to be abolished.

### Magnanimous Monarch

|  | Available only with the [Colossus of the South](Colossus of the South.md) DLC enabled. |
|---|---|

This journal entry does restrict the player's agency in certain ways:

- Brazil must maintain its starting *Tenant Farmers* law as switching the law to anything other than Serfdom will be a massive setback.
- A balancing act should be maintained so that *The Matter of Slavery* neither completes or fails. Failure gives a modifier (*Renewed African Cycle*) which disappears if the slavery law is not Slave Trade; the slave law will be set to Slave Trade upon faliure. Also, completion is another setback. Alternatively, a GP should be allowed to enforce a slavery ban when *The Matter of Slavery* is still active, so that *Golden Law, Golden Rose [1]* is activated. Another option is to enact Slavery Banned before the regency period ends and Pedro is crowned emperor, which does not lower the progress bar.
- As secessions and revolutions count as setbacks, Brazil should ensure that these do not occur.
- It is very important to secure Princess Isabel as the heir, as doing so net increases the progress bar by 2; this can be achieved by simply passing Propertied Women and waiting for the succession progress bar to reach 120.

Note that if *The Republican Proposal* is completed (or if Brazil is no longer a monarchy), the journal entry will close and the "Magnanimous" achievement will no longer be available. Also, if Brazil abolishes the monarchy, *Subjecthood* will not be available as a citizenship law.
