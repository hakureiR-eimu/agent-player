Society technology
**Society technologies** primarily represent new ideas for organizing society. These include new concepts in politics, finance, culture and diplomacy. Several society technologies increase [capacities](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Capacities), either directly for authority and influence or indirectly for bureaucracy. Society technologies also represent new inventions in public infrastructure and construction. Many society technologies unlock new [laws](Laws.md) and [ideologies](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Ideologies) that then provide support for the new laws.
