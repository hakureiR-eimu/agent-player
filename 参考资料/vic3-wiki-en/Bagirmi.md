**Bagirmi** is an unrecognized power located in the Niger [strategic region](Strategic region.md) of Africa. It borders it [Bornu](Bornu.md), [Wadai](Wadai.md), and [Sokoto](Sokoto.md), and the decentralized nation of [Dar al Kuti](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Dar_al_Kuti).

### Interest groups

Due to Bagirmi's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Bagirmi starts with the Landowners and Sunni Ulema in government. The Rural Folk, and Armed Forces are in opposition, while the Intelligentsia, Trade Unions, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Industrialists are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Bagirmi starts with no diplomatic relations of note, and its only active interest is in Niger.
