The following is a list of character hairstyles, beards and mustache present in the character creator which can be used for [character modding](Character modding.md). Images are named after their file name. Identification in the dropdown menu should reference such names to search for the specific hairstyle in an apt category. Keep in mind that "gene strength" is used in game to list different possible hairstyles for characters that depend on the same gene (eg. characters from the same [culture](Culture modding.md)) as such certain hairstyles become "redundant" being used in multiple contexts. Certain Hairstyles, Beards or Mustache are exclusive to one character. Those may be listed as a separate category in the dropdown menu or be in "Historical_Hairstyles", "Historical_Beards" or "Historical_Mustache" as different strengths of the same gene.
