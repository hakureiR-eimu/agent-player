**Shewa** is an unrecognized power in Ethiopia. It is an excellent candidate for forming [Ethiopia](Ethiopia.md) once Centralization has been researched.

### Interest groups

Shewa starts with the Landowners in government. The Armed Forces, Oriental Orthodox Church, and Rural Folk are in opposition, while the Intelligentsia, Petite Bourgeoisie, Trade Unions, and Industrialists are marginalized.

The Oriental Orthodox Church has the Oriental Orthodox Patriarchy ideology instead of the default Patriarchal.

### General overview

As an Ethiopian minor, Shewa is a candidate to unite [Ethiopia](Ethiopia.md) via [the Age of Princes](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/The_Age_of_Princes) journal entry. Shewa starts out the game with almost no technology, one of the lowest literacy rates in the game, and no states that produce iron or coal. However, Shewa has the largest military out of all the Ethiopian princes, and is Amhara primary culture which makes it eligible to get the *Outlaw Prince* event and thus Tewodros II. With Shewa's (relatively) powerful military alongside the arrival of Tewodros II, it is the easiest nation to actually form Ethiopia with.

### Forming Ethiopia

Main article:
Ethiopia
Researching Centralization is required for the 'Age of Princes' journal entry to activate, and since it also increases tax capacity and enables road maintenance, this is a priority.

[Harar](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Harar) should be your first target: not only are they one of the stronger Ethiopian minors, it will also consolidate your capital of Oromia giving you one full state out of the two you need to form Ethiopia. You can rapidly annex Ethiopian minors left and right, as long as you pick your battles wisely and don't fight too big of a coalition. Even with Tewodros II leading your armies, it is advisable to take the princes one-on-one (or one-on-two at worst): Tewodros may be insanely powerful, but he can't stop accidental front-splitting shenanigans resulting in your capital being taken. You should also be looking to annex the Somali and Yemeni minors, as they will give you access to the coast and to iron and lead.

It's a good idea to keep track of your bureaucracy deficit. Conquering new states will give you more population, but you will find it difficult to staff your government administration buildings because of low literacy rates. Use the Promote Social Mobility decree as much as you can to raise your literacy rates.

After all of the Ethiopian and Somali minors are conquered, you will have complete control of the Horn of Africa strategic region except northern Eritrea, which is controlled by [Egypt](Egypt.md). You will need to expand or colonize further to acquire coal, sulfur and lead, and you will eventually need to find an opportune time to attack Egypt to retake Eritrea.
