*The people have risen against the devilry and corruption of Great Qing. The Qing's rule must be extinguished, and a new era of heavenly peace brought to China.*

**Heavenly Kingdom** is a special revolter from [China](China.md). It appears if China fails the journal entry *The Heavenly Kingdom*. When Heavenly Kingdom revolts, it immediately starts a special diplomatic play *Taiping Rebellion*, which annexes the opposing country if enforced.

## Formation

Failing the journal entry *The Heavenly Kingdom* causes the Heavenly Kingdom to spawn. Failure is caused by having four states with the *God Worshipping Society God Worshipping Society State modifier +25% Radicals from political movements* modifier and having any state that both contains a Protestant pop whose religion is discriminated against (state acceptance of Protestant equal to or below 10) and has at least 15% turmoil (note that turmoil usually doesn't show up in the UI until it reaches 25%, so tracking down such states takes extra effort). In turn, these conditions are caused by events from the journal entry *The Chinese Missions*, many of which unavoidably either convert pops to Protestantism or increase radicalism.

*The Chinese Missions* activates if all of the following are true:

- China lost the Opium Wars or has a Migration law other than Closed Borders
- Any neighboring state is a Treaty Port of a Christian country
- China has an Economic System law other than Isolationism or a variant thereof (i.e. Canton System)

*The Heavenly Kingdom* activates if all of the following are true:

- China exists and is not a subject of another country
- The year is 1856 or later, or, if the *Missionary Works in [PROVINCE]* event has triggered at least 5 times, 1846 or later
- Any incorporated, non-capital state in China that is a Han or Hakka homeland both contains a pop with the Protestant religion and has over 15% turmoil

If *The Chinese Missions* fires, then *The Heavenly Kingdom* is effectively certain to fire, but if you prevent the former, the latter will still probably fire eventually because as of game version 1.8.6, it only checks whether Protestants **exist** in China, not whether they are discriminated against. The JE's failure condition can still be avoided by enacting Total Separation, but even Total Separation will not prevent the JE from firing and causing trouble.

While Heavenly Kingdom is a theocracy, it uses the special [Heavenly Kingdom](Government type.md) theocracy, which is hereditary.

### Interest groups

The God-Worshipping Society is always in government, and may be joined by other interest groups

The God-Worshipping Society has the unique ideology God Worshipper instead of Moralist. The Literati lose the Anti-Clerical ideology.

Unfortunately, being immediately in a civil war with an opponent which likely will still have more valuable resources and population of (roughly? ### Military

Being a split off of China, the Heavenly Kingdom will commandeer a sizable portion of the world's largest starting army, or at least their barracks. Unfortunately, it will likely still be smaller than their opponent in the civil war. The player must fight carefully or else be overwhelmed, by sheer weight of numbers.

You will probably inherit some ships, but most notably the AI will immediately use what ships it has to harass convoys or perform blockades.

### Expansion

Likely the only goal will be to try to consume China, focusing on the most valuable provinces available, such as Shanxi, strengthening oneself whilst weakening their competitor.

## Trivia

In *Victoria 2*, China was split into multiple sub-states from the start of the game in order to better simulate the dissolution of the Qing Dynasty's central authority and the spheres of influence each European power exerted over different regions of China, given the limitations of the engine at the time. The main part of China was the most populous sector of the collection of substates and focused around the East-North part, from roughly Zhejiang (near Shanghai) to Shanxi to Beijing up north.

The Heavenly Kingdom would have a chance to spawn at any time as long as there was unrest. It was hardcoded to spawn only in its historical region and did not inherit any Westernisation progress. It would subsume the entirety of China if it won the civil war, but the substates would become independent when the war begins (including from China) and the player had to justify wars if they intend to re-unify China.
