The **Confederate States of America** (CSA) is one of two countries that can come into existence through the American Civil War; as such it does not exist at the start of the game. The Confederate States of America attempts to secede from [America](America.md) in order to preserve the institution of slavery in its states.

Historically, the American Civil War ended in a decisive Union victory, which resulted in the dissolution of the Confederate States of America and the re-integration of these states into the United States of America.

### American Civil War

The Confederate States of America typically appears through a [secession](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Secession) by the Pro-Slavery movement in [United States of America](America.md) (this is a special case where an ideological movement triggers a cultural secession instead of a revolution).

When this happens, CSA will take over some of the [characters](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Characters) from USA.

### Release

The CSA can be [released](Releasable countries.md) after the American Civil War has occurred, regardless of whether it actually formed through the war. The releasing country must fully control the state of Virginia in order to release the CSA. When released in this manner, the CSA receives all Confederate core state regions owned by the releasing country, except for the capital state of the releasing country.

### Secession

The CSA can appear through a secession by a Dixie cultural movement. The American Civil War does not have to have occurred for the CSA to appear this way.

## Flags

Aside from the default flag, there are several alternate flags for the Confederate States. This partially reflects the actual history of the Confederacy's flag changing over the war. The "stars and bars" flag was the first flag of the Confederacy and was followed by the "stainless" then the "blood-stained" banners. There are also more fanciful flags only loosely based on historical models.

- "Stars and bars" Default
- "Stainless" Has the Armed Forces in government
- "Blood-stained" Has only the Armed Forces in government
- "Battle flag" Has the Armed Forces in government Government is a 'Military Dictatorship' or 'Junta'
- Is a Theocracy
- Is a Council Republic
- Is a subject of Mexico
