**Military technologies** improve the army and navy. These consist of a mix of new weapon technologies, doctrines, and ways of organizing servicemen and officers. Some technologies establish a more reliable supply of manpower for conscription or improve military effectiveness. The naval part of the tree is primarily dedicated to the invention of new ship types, but also includes new naval strategies that unlock or improve the effectiveness of certain naval orders as well as upgrades to civilian ports to improve the country's supply network and trade capacity.
