**Ethiopia** is a [formable](Formable countries.md) nation in the eastern Africa. It can be formed by any nation with an Oriental Orthodox Oriental Orthodox Ethiopian primary culture (Amhara, Oromo, Tigray).

## Historical background

In 1836, Ethiopia was in the midst of the *Zemene Mesafint*, or "Age of Princes". While *de jure* a unified empire, the emperor of Ethiopia exerted no real influence, and the local princes held the actual authority over each region. This era came to an end with the rise of Tewodros II, who usurped the title of emperor in 1855, then reasserted its power by subduing the neighboring princes by force; however, he was not able to fully unify Ethiopia under his control. Full unification was accomplished under Emperor Menelik II, who expanded his empire from the core of Ethiopia in Gonder and Shewa to approximately the modern borders.

Ethiopia was the only part of Africa not to fall under European colonization, though it was occupied by Italy leading up to and during World War II.

Forming Ethiopia grants the achievement *Solomon's Quest*.

### Core states

Ethiopia has three core states, comprising the north of the East Africa region. Eritrea is a homeland of the Tigray culture, which is one of Ethiopia's primary cultures, but does not count towards the formation.

Ethiopia's core states are generally resource poor, having no access to mined resources, except a small amount of iron and oil. Agriculture is the main resource for Ethiopia, with a large amount of arable land and several types of plantations available.

The Ethiopian minors are all unrecognized powers and have similar basic stats, starting technology, and politics. Each starts with only split state in Ethiopia. They are all Principalities and either Oriental Orthodox or Sunni. Most often they start with the Landowners in government with a mix of the Armed Forces, Intelligentsia and Devout in opposition, and the remaining interest groups marginalized.

For the Oriental Orthodox minors, the Oriental Orthodox Church has the Oriental Orthodox Patriarchy ideology instead of the default Patriarchal.

The Sunni minors have People of the Book instead of State religion

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.12. |
|---|---|

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Ethiopia
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

### Selecting an Ethiopian minor

All of the Ethiopian minors can form Ethiopia. The best country to unite the nation for a first-time Ethiopia player is [Shewa](Shewa.md), who starts with the strongest military and have Amhara as a primary culture so that they can get Tewodros II.

[Harar](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Harar) and [Begemder](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Begemder) border Aussa directly at game start, meaning that sea and iron access is one war away. Harar also border Isaaq in addition. Through Aussa or Isaaq, the entire Somaliland allow for 25 potential Iron Mines.

### Uniting Ethiopia

If you selected an Amhara primary culture nation, you will eventually receive *The Outlaw Prince,* which gives you **Tewodros II** (baptized as **Kassa**, circa 1818 – 13 April 1868?) as your leader. He has a character modifier of **+30** offense as commander, so granting him leadership boosts your military strength and makes it easier to unite Ethiopia.

Of the three Ethiopian formation states, only one is required to form Ethiopia. There's not much of a choice though, as Amhara and Oromia are partially controlled by decentralized nations, thus requiring colonization to control completely for formation. Gonder is controlled fully by centralized minor countries and is thus much easier to control completely.

Defeating all the other Ethiopian minors is just a matter of building your economy, choosing the right battles, and being diplomatic. Don't rush into a war if there are two other minors who will support your target: even Tewodros won't be able to save you from a three-front war. The most important things you want are coastal access and access to iron, so try and annex states like Aussa and the Somaliland minors as well (even though you won't have a Unify Ethiopia CB against them). The major powers and other nations like [Egypt](Egypt.md) will almost never interact with other Ethiopian minors, so you can rest assured knowing you'll have full access of Ethiopia soon.

All of the Ethiopian minors start with horrendously low literacy rates. You should liberally use the Promote Social Mobility decree to increase your literacy rate and especially to staff your Government Administration buildings. It's very easy to get into big bureaucracy deficits as Ethiopian minors because you're conquering a lot of land very quickly and your government administration often doesn't staff as quickly as you think it will, so this is something to keep in mind.

### Developing an industrial base

Once you've established a strong position in Ethiopia and Somalia, you can start looking outside. The first and most important thing you'll want to do is to secure a domestic supply of coal. None of the Ethiopian, Eritrean, or Somali states have any domestic supply of coal. You can get some coal by colonizing the states of Ubangi Shari (to the south of Wadai) or the Rift Valley. Conquering [Madagascar](Madagascar.md) or [Gaza](Gaza.md) for coal is also a possibility if you can land them in a naval invasion.

The closest sulfur and lead producing states are Hedjaz and Yemen, respectively. It may be difficult to take full control of Yemen as you will be competing with the Great Powers (namely [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) and [Russia](Russia.md)), but the parts you get will usually be enough and you can use trade to make up for the rest. You can also expand your colonial empire into Indonesia to acquire coal, sulfur, and lead.

Defeating Egypt to acquire Eritrea will depend on how their inevitable scuffle against the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md) ends. Although it may be tempting to strike Egypt during their independence war, you will most likely be defeated even with Tewodros leading your charge, especially without Line Infantry. It's best to industrialize and build your technology and military. If Egypt has managed to free itself from the Turks, you might want to align yourself with Egypt's enemies such as the Ottomans and the British to eventually try get them to side with you. If Egypt is extraordinarily weak (also a possibility), you may even be able to beat them alone. After winning the first war to return Eritrea and potentially transfer or annex [Hedjaz](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Hedjaz), it becomes very easy to bully Egypt further.

You will also want to overbuild universities (more than you usually do), due to your incredibly low starting technology position. This will mean you can finally catch up to the rest of the world when it comes to tech and literacy rates.

### Expansion

At this point, you will have a strong industrial base in the heartland of Ethiopia, a burgeoning empire with ready access to resources, and a strong GDP and military. At this point, the world is your oyster. You can continue building tall to develop Ethiopia as an industrial powerhouse, bully Egypt to liberate Sudan, try kick the Europeans out of Africa, earn recognition, or anything else.
