**Oldenburg** is a minor power in North Germany. It is is able to form the [North German Federation](North German Federation.md) and [Germany](Germany.md). As an alternative, Oldenburg can also form the [Confederation of the Rhine](Confederation of the Rhine.md).

### Interest groups

Due to Oldenburg's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Oldenburg begins with the Kleinbürger and the Landowners in government. The Evangelical Church, the Intelligentsia, the Armed Forces, and Landvolk are in opposition. The Industrialists and Trade unions are marginalized.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.10. |

Oldenburg has the Professional Army law.

Oldenburg begins with 3 Battalions and can call upon no Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| Großherzoglich Oldenburgische Armee | 2 Line Infantry 1 Cannon Artillery | — | North Germany |

Oldenburg has no flotillas at the start of the game.
