*"Following the conclusion of the Kandyan Wars, Ceylon's fertile uplands have witnessed the rise of numerous plantations, fueled in part by the import of Tamil laborers from the mainland. Can Ceylon sustain this momentum, or will it fall to internal strife?"*

**Ceylon** is a minor power in South Asia.

It starts the game as a direct colony of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) and is administered separately from the [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company) on the mainland.

If it becomes independent, it will become **Sri Lanka**, with Sinhala as its new primary culture and Theravada as its state religion.

### Interest groups

Ceylon begins with the Landowners and Armed Forces in government. The Rural Folk and Intelligentsia are in opposition. The Anglican Church, Trade Unions, Petite Bourgeoisie and Industrialists are marginalized.

**Sri Lanka** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in South Asia. It can be released by any country that controls its sole core state, Ceylon.

Sri Lanka can also appear if British Ceylon becomes independent, in which case it remains a colonial type country.

As a Buddhist country, the Theravada Monks have the Buddhist Moralist ideology instead of the default Moralist.
