**Bulgaria** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in the Balkans. It may be released by any country that controls its capital or revolt as the cultural secession country of Bulgarians. At the start date, most of its states, including the capital, are controlled by the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md).

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.12. |
|---|---|

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Bulgaria
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
Since Bulgaria does not exist on the map in 1836, the player needs to find a way to release it. The main two ways are by releasing them as the [Ottoman Empire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey) or through an event as [Russia](Russia.md).

### Ottoman Setup

Playing as Bulgaria when starting as the Ottoman Empire is largely just a question of releasing Bulgaria as an independent state. Before doing so, it is possible to hobble the Ottoman Empire in a number of ways, such as by releasing subjects and territories, sabotaging its diplomacy, or signing away concessions to other countries. The player can also choose to delay releasing Bulgaria, and instead dedicate the Ottoman Empire's broader resources to developing Dobruca, Mizica and Kuzey Trakya until a later date.

Regardless of the other specifics, it is advisable to fully release all other possible countries in the Balkans, so as to provide an independent Bulgaria room to expand easily. Wallachia is an important early target for annexation, as it possesses the only sulfur deposits in the surrounding area.

### Russian Setup

This guide is also focused on the [Yugoslavia](Yugoslavia.md) path, but most suggestions are still useful no matter the choice made in the “Prussia of the Balkans” event.

As [Russia](Russia.md), the main goal is to make sure that Bulgaria can stand on its own, and that all of its neighbors are non-threats (either by weakening them or ensuring a lack of interest). To this end, the main targets will be [Austria](Austria.md) and the [Ottomans](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey).

Before unpausing there are some important first steps that can make achieving these goals easier. The first step is disbanding Russia’s [power bloc](Power bloc.md). A new one can be formed at any point, but it is important that Russia does not have a Sovereign Empire, as when the AI is in control, they will start puppeting the Balkans. The next step is securing allies, [Prussia](Prussia.md) can be fairly useful for bullying Austria, and dealing with the Ottomans is easier without [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) joining their side. So for these reasons, it is recommended to improve Prussian relations and unrival Great Britain. In addition, an interest in the Nile can make for a fairly useful way to meddle with the Ottomans when they go in on [Egypt](Egypt.md). Russia already owns part of Bulgaria in a tiny part of Dobrudja, so it is beneficial to put some development in that area, just basics like Construction Sectors and Logging Camps, but some utility buildings like Paper Mills, Universities, and Government Administrations can also help after switching.

The last recommendation before unpausing is that the Holy Alliance with Austria should be renegotiated to remove the defensive pact and replace it with something minor like [trade privlages](Treaty.md), along with shortening it to only 5 years. Unpausing the game at this point should have Austria accept fairly quickly, at which point it is useful to rival them for the influence. This can then in turn be funneled into improving relations with Great Britain.

### Playing as Russia

With that all set up, the game opens up, and suggestions become more agnostic on order along with allowing for significant freedom during downtime. A general trend is that it can be useful to start molding [Russia](Russia.md)’s politics to match the intended Bulgarian politics after the switch. For example, if the intent is to play Bulgaria as a republic, then working on making Russia a republic will ensure compatible governments. The [Springtime of the Peoples](Journal.md) can be fairly useful to enact any radical changes.

Economically, Russia could use a several more construction sectors (which can be placed in Russian Dobrudja), but otherwise does not require much economic development to be able to maintain its military against the Ottomans and support Bulgaria. Investing heavily in universities (and somewhat in railways) in Russian Dobrudja will give Bulgaria a significant boost to recovering from the research deficit that the Ottomans are liable to leave it in.

Before moving forward with any big conflicts, it can be useful to secure an alliance. If [Prussian](Prussia.md) relations have been improving, then it is fairly trivial to turn their Holy Alliance into one. An early available conflict is that if [Austria](Austria.md) is targeted with a war to liberate [Hungary](Hungary.md), then an allied Prussia is usually willing to join in the first year or two of the game. It is then fairly useful to add goals to liberate [Banat](Banat.md) and [Slovenia](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Slovenia), as they will be later Bulgarian targets. This will ensure the (ironic) balkanization of Austria, so any other war goals are fine, but liberating [Transylvania](Transylvania.md) will weaken Hungary (a future contender to Bulgaria's dominance). All of this should leave Austria in a state where they will not be a challenger for the remainder of the game. After the war it is then also useful to guarantee Slovenia (target of [Italy](Italy.md) and [Germany](Germany.md)) and Banat (target of [Serbia](Serbia.md) and [Hungary](Hungary.md)). Slovenia might even require some puppeting to prevent its annexation into Germany, just make sure to release them before switching to Bulgaria. Alternatively, if shooting for the Prussia of the Balkans achievement, the player may prefer to antagonize Prussia and reduce its prestige.

The Ottomans can then be targeted sometime around 1840, with two useful goals being Kars and [investment rights](Treaty.md). Kars is needed to switch to Bulgaria, and investment rights are useful for building up the Bulgarian economy now so that it isn’t so weak when starting. For any war with the Ottomans, it is recommended that fighting be restricted to the eastern front, so that Bulgarian lands sustain minimal [devastation](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Devastation). From there, the only thing to do is wait for the [Great Eastern Crisis](Turkish content.md), so just play whatever Russian game is desired (with possibly some further Ottoman antagonizations to either regain investment rights or just to keep the Ottomans weak). When the crisis does fire, the Ottomans should be targeted as soon as possible to liberate Bulgaria (and take Kars, if that hasn’t been done). This will lead to “The Bulgarian Revival” event, but before switching over, there are three final suggestions while the game is paused.

Russian Dobrudja should be traded to Bulgaria, which AI Bulgaria may counterintuitively be disinclined to accept, requiring further concessions from Russia. The last (and possibly most out-there) suggestion is to minorly balkanize Russia, enough that the AI feels like it can’t be a bully, but not so much that they aren’t strong enough to help. The released nations can be kept as puppets, and narratively it could be viewed as a move towards a confederation. It can also be helpful to have researched Pan-nationalism, both for the technology spread to Bulgaria, but also that it allows for more cohesive nation releases (such as [Siberia](Siberia.md) and [Transcaucasia](Transcaucasia.md)).

### Playing as Bulgaria

Even with the set-up, early days as Bulgaria can be fairly precarious, as it is basically a starting nation suddenly finding itself in possibly the early mid-game. Not to mention that there’s always the chance that the [Ottoman](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey) AI might possibly have made some odd choices. On account of this, it is usually useful to start by stabilizing the country both economically and politically. In terms of economy, the usual basics are fairly good choices (wood, tools, iron, paper, bureaucracy, universities, ect), but the army should also be steadily built up the whole time, as to meet the “Prussia of the Balkans” requirements. In terms of stabilizing politics, it is useful to modernize. This mostly means voting rights, abolishing slavery, and getting at least Cultural Exclusion, but if those are undesired then prepare for the Landowners to dominate and for Greeks and Turks to constantly revolt. Right of Assembly can also be useful for the technology spread.

In terms of research, military technologies are fairly useful for raising power projection, and thus marching towards the “Prussia of the Balkans”, but if aiming for [Yugoslavia](Yugoslavia.md), it is also important to work towards Pan-nationalism. Since Bulgaria inherits the Ottoman’s technology, they will be fairly behind, so it is useful to be familiar with tricks like the [Slingshot Method](Technology.md) and other ways to speed up research.

In terms of diplomacy, it is useful to make friends with the big players in the area. In this case, that will most likely be [Russia](Russia.md), [Prussia](Prussia.md)/ [Germany](Germany.md), and [Great Britain](Great Britain.md). Once they have Amicable relations, focus can be turned on the Balkan fellows. If the goal is Yugoslavia, it is pretty helpful to befriend all of the southern Slavs, as it is a [major unification](Formable countries.md) and so their backing will make things much easier. It is easy to assume that puppeting nations would also be useful, but this usually just harms their relations and it can be difficult to lower their [autonomy](Subject.md) enough, so it is not recommended. If there is a different goal, then befriend the nations that aren’t the most immediate targets. [Venice](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Venice), [Hungary](Hungary.md), and the [Ottomans](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey) all make for fairly good rivals, and will usually increase the goodwill of desired allies. Make sure to be strategic about it though, as it is undesirable to drive them all into each others’ arms.

Once the nation is stable enough, and the truce with the Ottomans is over, the goal should be regaining the remaining Bulgarian homelands. This should be fairly easy, as the Ottomans should now be well in decline, and hopefully Russia (or some other bigger nation) is willing to join. Liberating any remaining Balkan nations makes for a good secondary goal, as that gives a fairly large opinion boost that can be used for leverage (which is one of the reasons it was not encouraged to liberate them as Russia). “Prussia of the Balkans” should fire at roughly some point around this war, as either the build-up to it or the gaining of additional territory should build up the army enough to trigger it. The 100 prestige should be enough to make Bulgaria a major power, which in turn should make it a big fish in a small pond and allow for whatever path is desired.
