**Chitral** is an unrecognized power in northwestern India. It neighbors [Kafiristan](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kafiristan), [Kunduz](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kunduz), [Ladakh](Ladakh.md), [Kabul](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kabul), [Punjab](Punjab.md) and [China](China.md).

### Interest groups

Due to Chitral's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Chitral starts with the Zamindars in government. The Ryots, Sunni Ulema, and Armed Forces are in opposition, while the Trade Unions, Intelligentsia, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Industrialists are marginalized.

The Petite Bourgeoisie has the Modernizer ideology instead of the default Reactionary. The Intelligentsia has the Constitutionalist ideology instead of the default Republican.

### Diplomacy

Chitral starts with no diplomatic relations of note, and its only two active interests are in two home regions of Persia and Himilayas.

[British East India](East India.md) has claims on both of Chitral's two provinces, and [Jammu](Kashmir.md) has a claim on Chitrali Kashmir.
