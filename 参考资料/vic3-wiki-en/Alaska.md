**Alaska** (initially called *Russian-American Company*) is a minor power located in the Pacific Coast [strategic region](Strategic region.md) of North America and is a Chartered Company of [Russia](Russia.md). It borders the [Hudson’s Bay Company](Hudson’s Bay Company.md) and [Columbia District](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Columbia_District) as well as the decentralized nation of [Athabaska](Athabaska.md).

As a Russian country, Alaska can reform [Russia](Russia.md) if the latter ceases to exist.

With just 14,820 inhabitants, the Russian-American Company is the smallest playable country at the game.

## Historical Background

Russia began colonization of Alaska in the late 1700s, with the Russian-American Company being founded in 1799. The primary Russian settlement was New Archangel, now called Sitka.

By the 1860s, as the fur trade declined and Russia was overextended after the Crimean War, Russia sought to sell their colony, and in 1867, the USA purchased Alaska for a sum of $7.2 million (equivalent to $129 million in 2023).

## Formation

If it ceases to exist, Alaska can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country controlling its capital and only core state of Alaska.

## Scripted content

If [Russia](Russia.md) is AI controlled it is possible that [America](America.md) can buy Alaska and annex it via the [journal entry 'The Alaska Purchase'](American content.md), if Russia goes into debt. If Russia is player controlled, Russia itself can initiate the sale.

### Interest groups

Due to Alaska's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Alaska begins with the Armed Forces in government. The Petite Bourgeoisie, Rural Folk, Orthodox Church, Industrialists, and Intelligentsia may be in government or in opposition. The Trade Unions and Landowners are marginalized.

The Industrialists have the Colonialist ideology instead of the default Laissez-Faire.

Alaska starts off very poor but will get gold in only a few years. If you can get Multiculturalism or Council Republic, you can gain all of Athabaska for free and add Athabaskan as a primary culture on top of Russian (thus allowing the player to form [Turtle Island](Turtle Island.md)), when becoming independent. But it's much easier to just train one brigade and colonize them. Focus on maxing out migration attraction and building ports and later railways.

Note that Joint-Stock Companies is **not** useful at all, since Alaska while remaining a company cannot establish companies on their own until they earn their independence, which because Alaska is a Chartered company requires Civilizing Mission to be researched. Likewise, Bessemer Process is equally worthless at game start, as with Extraction Economy Alaska cannot build Steel Mills. However, getting off Extraction Economy is pretty easy, since most interest groups are opposed to it.
