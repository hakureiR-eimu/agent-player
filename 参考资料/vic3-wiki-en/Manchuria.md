**Manchuria** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in East Asia. It may be released by any country that controls its capital.

If [China](China.md) fails the *Fragile Unity* journal entry, Manchuria is released as a warlord state. Manchuria can reform [China](China.md) if the latter ceases to exist.
