**Transcaucasia** is a small [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in the Caucasus region. Transcaucasia includes the regions of [Armenia](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Armenia), [Azerbaijan](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Azerbaijan), and [Georgia (GEO)](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Georgia_(GEO)), which at game start are held by [Russia](Russia.md) and the [Ottoman Empire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey), with Kars split between the two and the rest under Russian control.

## Region

Transcaucasia is a region located in the South Caucasus, which includes countries such as Georgia, Armenia, and Azerbaijan. This region has been historically important due to its strategic location and its cultural and ethnic diversity. The region has three different religions in the game with Sunni, Oriental Orthodox and Orthodox. This could pose a problem when dealing with radicals.

## Formation

Four of the five core states are needed in order to form Transcaucasia; additional claims are Armenian homelands
The following countries can form Transcaucasia when they have researched Pan-nationalism and control four of its five core states. None exist at game start, and each can be released by a country which controls its capital state.

| Country | Capital | Primary Cultures | State Religion | Tier | Type | Core States |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Armenia** | Armenia | Armenian | Oriental Orthodox | Kingdom | Recognized | Armenia Elizavetpol Erzurum Kars |
| **Azerbaijan** | Baku | Azerbaijani | Shiite | Kingdom | Recognized | Baku Elizavetpol |
| **Georgia** | Tiflis | Georgian | Orthodox | Kingdom | Recognized | Tiflis |

Transcaucasia retains the state religion of the country that forms it, rather than changing to the default Shiite.

Transcaucasia can also be [released](Releasable countries.md) directly by any country that has researched Pan-nationalism and controls Tiflis and two other core states.

### Core states

Transcaucasia's core states are Armenia, Baku, Elizabetpol, Kars, and Tiflis. Armenia has an additional core state, Erzurum. Beyond these, there are an additional five states in Anatolia and Persia that become claimed as Armenian homelands.

## Caucasian War

|  | Available only with the [Sphere of Influence](Sphere of Influence.md) DLC enabled. |
|---|---|

If [Russia](Russia.md) fails the *Caucasian War* journal entry and has researched Pan-nationalism, it can release Transcaucasia as one option. It can also choose to release Georgia, Armenia, and Azerbaijan; either in their modern forms (with Nationalism) or as their historical ones. AI Russia never chooses any of these options, however.
