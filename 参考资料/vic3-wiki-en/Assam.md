**Assam** is an unrecognized power in South Asia. It is one of the "princely states", a puppet of the [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company), and thus a part of the [British Empire](Great Britain.md).

As an Assamese country, Assam is able to form [India](India.md).

### Interest groups

Assam starts with the Zamindars in government. The Hindu Priesthood, Armed Forces, Intelligentsia, and Ryots are in opposition. The remaining interest groups are marginalized.

The Hindu Priesthood has the Hindu Moralist ideology instead of the default Moralist. The Petite Bourgeoisie has the Modernizer ideology instead of the default Reactionary. The Intelligentsia has the Constitutionalist ideology instead of the default Republican.
