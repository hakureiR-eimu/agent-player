*"Calls for Romanian unification and political independence complicate regional politics, as both Russia and the Ottomans have an interest in enforcing the status quo. Can Romania win its freedom?"*

**Moldavia** is an insignificant power in Eastern Europe. It is able to form [Romania](Romania.md) and [Danubian State](Danubian State.md).

If Moldavia is not a Monarchy, it is renamed to *Moldova*.

## Historical background

The Principality of Moldavia took shape in the mid-14th century, traditionally founded under Bogdan I, who broke away from Hungarian authority and established an independent voivodeship east of the Carpathians. The new principality quickly grew in strength, taking advantage of its position between Poland-Lithuania, the Black Sea steppes, and the rising Ottoman Empire. Moldavia's golden age came in the 15th century under the long and celebrated reign of Stephen the Great in 1457–1504. Stephen defended Moldavia against repeated invasions by the Ottomans, Hungarians, Tatars, and Poles, securing major victories such as the Battle of Vaslui in 1475. His rule was also marked by extensive church patronage, including the iconic painted monasteries of northern Moldavia. Stephen's success ensured Moldavia's independence for decades, and he became a central figure of Romanian medieval statehood. After Stephen's death, the principality faced growing Ottoman pressure, eventually becoming a tributary state in the 16th century. Moldavia nonetheless retained a degree of internal autonomy. In 1600, Moldavia briefly came under the control of Michael the Brave, who united Wallachia, Transylvania, and Moldavia, though the union collapsed within a year after its establishment.

The 17th century brought internal struggles and shifting allegiances, but the most dramatic change came in the 18th century with the introduction of Phanariot rule (1711–1821). Like Wallachia, Moldavia was administered by Greek Phanariot princes appointed by the Ottoman Empire. This system intensified fiscal burdens and political instability but also opened the region to new administrative practices and early modern influences. Throughout the century, Moldavia became increasingly entangled in Russo–Ottoman conflicts, with Russian influence growing steadily. The principality suffered major territorial losses during this period: Bukovina was ceded to the Habsburg Empire in 1775, weakening Moldavia’s western flank, and Bessarabia was annexed by the Russian Empire in 1812 following one of the many Russo-Turkish Wars, depriving Moldavia of its eastern lands up to the Dniester. These losses dramatically reduced the principality’s size and strategic autonomy. By the early 19th century, Moldavia was a battleground of competing imperial interests, yet it also experienced a cultural and political revival. Local boyars and intellectuals began advocating for reform, modernization, and closer alignment with Wallachia. These currents culminated in the election of Alexandru Ioan Cuza as Prince of Moldavia in January 1859, followed shortly by his election in Wallachia, effectively uniting the two principalities into [Romania](Romania.md).

### Interest groups

Moldavia begins with the Boyars and Orthodox Church in government. The Armed Forces, Petite Bourgeoisie and Rural Folk are in opposition. The Intelligentsia, Industrialists and Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Orthodox Church has the Orthodox Patriarchy ideology instead of the default Patriarchal. The Landowners have the Pro-Slavery ideology due to the starting Legacy Slavery. The Petite Bourgeoisie have the Mercantile ideology due to the starting Traditionalism.

### Diplomacy

Moldavia starts as a Protectorate under the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md), and thus part of the *Devlet-i ‘Aliye* power bloc.

Moldavia starts with a claim on Bessarabia.

Moldavia starts with pro-country lobbies towards [Russia](Russia.md) () and [France](France.md) ().

It has a [treaty](Treaty.md) with [Russia](Russia.md) *Treaty of Adrianople*, where Russia supports Wallachian indepence in exchange for [power bloc embassy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Power_bloc_embassy) and Moldova committing to Organic Regulation.

### Starting conditions

Moldavia start in a precarious situation as the protectorate of the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md), together with fellow Romanian minor [Wallachia](Wallachia.md). The laws are extremely backwards, the [literacy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Literacy) is bad, the economic situation is bad, and most Romanian homelands are owned by three great powers (Ottomans, Russia and Austria). Initially, one can use every single [decree](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Decree), and have plenty authority left. Especially promoting education is important, as literacy is abysmal. Passing a school reform is important too.

The initial conditions depends a lot on which [attitude](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Attitude) the Ottomans take (protective or domineering), since it determines if Moldova can play friendly with them or not. There are both advantages and disadvantages to playing friendly with them.

#### Assuming the Ottomans are friendly

Moldavia can first puppet and later annex Wallachia via a [journal entry](Romania.md), if relations are good enough with all great powers with an interest in the area (including the Ottomans). Wallachia will become a personal union/ puppet[1] of Moldovia, and if they remain so for 10 years, they will be annexed. This can be done even while a protectorate of the Ottomans, assuming that Wallachia is also still a protectorate. So improving relations with all relevant [great powers](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Great_power) is important. There isn't many other uses for diplomatic capacity; Moldovia is not going to produce any [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy) anytime soon. The relevant great powers are usually just The Ottomans, Russia and [Austria](Austria.md), but it is also possible that [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) has also declared an interest. The tech Nationalism is needed to do so, but this is probably not the first priority. Stock Exchange and industrial techs are way more pressing concerns. *Note that Wallachia has the exact same possibility, so if Moldavia takes too long, they can find themselves suddenly under Wallachia.*

The problem is that if the Ottomans turn Wallachia from a protectorate to a puppet, all progress can be lost, as Ottomans will inherit all puppets, if they do so. So it is a fine balance to strike. Keeping the Ottomans around 0 relations are best. Expelling diplomats and various demands should be used if needed.

#### If they are not

It will be very difficult to get Wallachia via event, if Ottomans are not initially friendly, since relations starts cold. Then one would need to build up an army and the backing of a major power. [Russia](Russia.md) is already committed to help, and that should be enough assuming no one else (like the British) intervenes. Here it is important to add the [war goal](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/War_goal) transfer subject for Wallachia and secondarily annexing the Ottoman owned parts of Romania, since this will allow Moldova to continue with the journal entries.

It is very likely that the Ottomans will be allied to [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), or at least has friendly enough relations that Great Britain will come to their aid in a diplomatic play. Therefore it is important to time the attack, while GB is occupied elsewhere for example during the Opium Wars.

Of course one can choose the offensive option, despite the Ottomans being friendly.

### The Turkish Tanzimat reforms and Independence

The Ottoman [Tanzimat reforms](Turkish content.md) will grant Moldavia independence after 30 years, if The Ottomans fail to make the reforms. There is however not much, if anything, Moldavia can do to provoke it (or prevent it for that matter), but it is important to prepare for. Therefore the industry has to be a balance between finding a niche in the Ottoman market, while also prepare a well rounded industry for eventual and sudden independence. When independent, Moldavia will not have a coast (not even with Wallachia annexed), so trade is not reliable to fix the shortcomings in the short term.

#### Politics

Due to Moldova's unique law Manorialism, it is not as much of a concern to get away from Serfdom, as it otherwise would be. It is possible to implement a school system, and it does not strengthen the landowners as much as serfdom.

### Post independence

If you have acquired Moldova, a [journal entry](Romania.md) will make it possible to get hold of some of the Russian parts of Romania, if you have good relations with them and at least one other [Great Power](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Great_Power) with an [interest](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Interest) in the region. This will crucially give Romania access to some coastline. Russia will keep the most of it though.

When independent, Moldova will not have a coast (not even with Moldova annexed), so trade is not reliable to fix too many shortcomings in the very short term.

The economy will likely take a hit post independence, as the bigger market of the Ottomans is gone, and likely also some trade centers, that Moldova had been using until now. A consideration could be to join the Russian power bloc and market, since it is in Moldova's interest to be friendly with the Russians anyway, at least for the time being. This is dependent on which principles Russia has chosen for their power bloc, so it is important to keep track of.

With time an ally such as [Germany](Germany.md) will be good to turn on the Russians and take back the rest of Bessarabia.

If [Bulgaria](Bulgaria.md) achieves independence, they can be a good target. Especially if they have Dobrudja.
