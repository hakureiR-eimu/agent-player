**Zimbabwe** is a small [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in southern Africa. Its flag and core territory reflect the modern country of Zimbabwe.

## Historical context

Zimbabwe's history goes back centuries, from the medieval kingdom of Zimbabwe and its successors the kingdoms of Butua and Mutapa to the Rozvi Empire. In the early 1800s as part of the Mfecane, the Nguni *Ndebele* people moved into the region as they fled the Zulu, pushing the Shona people north. The region that Ndebele settled is now known as Matebeleland.

Zimbabwe came under British control in the 1890s, due to the efforts of Cecil Rhodes, for which the British colony was called Rhodesia. During decolonization, the white colonist controlled government declared independence from Great Britain in order to maintain minority control of government. This led to civil war which eventually resolved in 1980 with the establishment of a majority rule government as the Republic of Zimbabwe.

Swaziland is an unrecognized power; it starts with a split state in Transvaal, and it begins with the Landowners and Armed Forces in government, with the Devout, Rural Folk and Trade Unions in opposition. The remaining interest groups are marginalized.

#### Scripted content

Main article:
Southern Africa content
Swaziland shares a journal entry with nearby countries related to the struggle for dominance over the *South Africa* [geographic region](Geographic region.md). Specifically, Swaziland must ensure diplomatic recognition and coastal access to gain dominance over the other minor states in the region.
