*"Across Denmark new liberal and national pushes are changing the face of politics. Meanwhile they are also undergoing a golden age of art, literature and philosophy. What will the rest of the century bring?"*

Denmark is a minor power in Northern Europe. Denmark's position puts it in a balancing act between the major powers of [Prussia](Prussia.md) to the south, [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) across the North Sea and [Sweden](Sweden.md) to the east. Denmark holds small colonial possessions at the start of the game – Iceland and Greenland in the North Sea, and Danish Togo, Carnatic, Pegu, and the Danish West Indies in the tropics. Denmark starts as the overlord of [Schleswig](Schleswig.md) and [Holstein](Holstein.md) in personal unions.

Denmark can form the nation of [Scandinavia](Scandinavia.md) after Pan-nationalism has been researched.

## Historical background

Denmark is a small but old kingdom in Scandinavia, just north of [Prussia](Prussia.md). It is strategically positioned by and in the Skagerrak Strait, which connects the Baltic to the North Sea and the Atlantic. Historically, this allowed it to control a great amount of ![Lens option trade route export.png](/images/thumb/9/99/Treaty_goods_transfer.png/24px-Treaty_goods_transfer.png) Trade through the chokepoint in the Øresund. By 1836, however, the Skagerrak has lost this strategic advantage, and together with losses in the ![Invention napoleonic warfare.png](/images/thumb/3/30/Invention_napoleonic_warfare.png/24px-Invention_napoleonic_warfare.png) Napoleonic wars, Denmark has been reduced to a third-rank power. During the 18th century, Denmark suffered numerous losses to both [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) and [Prussia](Prussia.md). The loss of [Norway](Norway.md) in 1814 and [Schleswig](Schleswig.md)-[Holstein](Holstein.md) in 1864 marked the definitive end of Denmark as a player in European politics. The geopolitical losses sparked a golden age in Danish literature, philosophy and art, as well as the transition to democracy over the course of the game's period.

## Formation

If Denmark ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by a country controlling its capital state of Zealand and one of its other two core states. Denmark uses the Danish homelands as its cores.

### Interest groups

Denmark begins with the Landowners as the sole [interest group](Interest group.md) in government, with the Armed Forces, Church of Denmark, Petite Bourgeoisie, Intelligentsia, Rural Folk, and Industrialists in opposition. The Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Landowners have the Pro-Slavery (colonial) ideology due to the starting Colonial Slavery.

### Industry and Resources

**Industry**

Denmark does not have much industry at game start, with only two Shipyards and a Textile Mill in Zealand. Denmark's real power lies in its agricultural output, most of which is in Jutland which has increased throughput thanks to the Good Soils trait.

Note that at the beginning of the game, Denmark produces more than sufficient dye to improve the production method of the Textile Mills immediately.

**Resources**

Denmark can build plantations in its [colonies](State.md): West Indies, Togo, Carnatic, and Pegu. Here, goods such as coffee, sugar, and tobacco can be produced. Denmark does not have access to many resources critical to industrialization, including iron, coal and lead.

Whaling Stations can be built on Greenland and Iceland, which also have the Arctic Whaling trait, increasing their throughput. sulfur can be produced in Danish Togo and on Iceland.

At the beginning of the game, the state of Pegu is completely isolated, as it does not have a port.

Denmark

Denmark has good [state traits](State traits.md), but crucially lacks both iron, coal and lead, and sufficient wood will eventually also be a problem. Forming Scandinavia will solve these issues, as both [Norway](Norway.md) and [Sweden](Sweden.md) has plenty.

### The Balancing Act

While Denmark possesses a relatively strong army and navy, it is nowhere comparable to German or British forces. So one will probably have to cozy up to at least one of them. In Jutland, the Good Soils trait, which increases agricultural throughput, can help food security and allows for self-sufficient Food Industries. Since both the [German](Germany.md) and [British](Great Britain.md) markets will lack foodstuffs, it is an obvious choice to export food and agricultural products to either of those markets. Additionally, Dye Plantations in Danish Togo can allow for the domestic production of porcelain in Glassworks, which can then be exported to richer countries. Holding great powers close, but at an arm's reach is the key to securing success. From this secure position, it's definitely a good base for forming [Scandinavia](Scandinavia.md).

### Schleswig and Holstein

These two junior partners may hold trouble for Denmark as resolving the *Schleswig-Holstein Question* is key to German unification. For Denmark's part, demanding *Reduce Autonomy* to turn the two into puppets is advised, if only to extract more value out of them. Then, if Denmark becomes a great power, the two may be re-organized with other German minors to form the [Confederation of the Rhine](Confederation of the Rhine.md) as a consolidated Danish puppet.

If Prussia or Austria comes knocking in order to resolve the Question, intervention by other GPs is key to victory as it is very difficult for Denmark to stand alone. [France](France.md) here is an obvious choice of ally, but it might not be enough.

Another solution is to simply give up the states without a fight, at least temporarily. It does not prevent Denmark from forming Scandinavia, since of the required 11 states, Denmark still has 3 in Zealand, Jutland and Iceland, while Sweden (including Norway) has 8. This solution does not work in patches which exclude Iceland as a core. Also, [Lübeck](Lübeck.md) is required.

### Expansion in Africa

From Danish Togo, there is plenty of possibility to attack [Ashanti](Ashanti.md), [Dahomey](Dahomey.md) and the other west African countries in the area. The initial Danish army should be plenty capable to beat any of them as long as long as no one else interferes, and Great Britain and Netherlands are the only European countries with an actual interest in the area. Denmark has an interest in maintaining a healthy relationship with Great Britain anyway, so it is unlikely that they will pose any problems.

This can bolster the Danish armies and economy for the future.

### Regarding Danish Pegu

The split colony of Danish Pegu is isolated at the start of the game. The province is not worth a lot; arguably less than building and maintaining a harbour. It's an island so it can not even serve as a stepping stone for annexing something in South East Asia. A choice could be to simply sell the state to either Great Britain or [EIC](East India.md). On the other hand, they are not willing to pay any significant amount for it. Another could be to keep a close eye on [Burmese](Burma.md) Pegu, and launch an invasion as soon as they decide to build a port.

### Forming Scandinavia and the relation with Sweden

Once Pan-nationalism has been researched by every Scandinavian nation, the [major unification](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Major_unification) system kicks in. When this happens, it is likely that both Denmark and [Sweden](Sweden.md) will have the same power rank, [major power](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Major_power). If both countries become unification candidates, a war over leadership is needed to form Scandinavia. As it is unlikely that Sweden will fall to a lower rank, Denmark will have to rise to [great power](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Great_power) status before any Scandinavian country has researched Pan-nationalism to avoid a leadership war.

At the very beginning of the game, Denmark and Sweden have very cold relations but not actually a rivalry. Very soon, an AI controlled Sweden will likely either choose to be conciliatory or to declare a rivalry. This leads to a sort of prisoners' dilemma.

If Sweden decides on a rivalry and sticks to it, it is very difficult to ever form Scandinavia and at the same time avoid war. Turning the Swedish opinion around enough to get them to vote for Danish rule is simply not realistic. If Sweden chooses to be conciliatory, Denmark can then choose to meet their diplomatic efforts or choose the way of rivalry. If good diplomatic ties are established, Denmark can form Scandinavia by rising to great power status, improving relations with both Norway and Sweden without ever firing a single shot. Getting Sweden into a power bloc led by Denmark greatly increases the probability of forming Scandinavia.
