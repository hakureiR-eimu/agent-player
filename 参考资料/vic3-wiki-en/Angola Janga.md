**Angola Janga** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in the Brazil region. It can be released by any country which controls its capital and one other core state. At the start of the game, its core states are owned by [Brazil](Brazil.md).

## Scripted content

As an Afro-Brazilian country, Angola Janga receives the following [event](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Event) upon being released, if it was released from a country that had either Legacy Slavery, Colonial Slavery, or Slave Trade.

id

### A Constitution of Freedom

For centuries the [African Diaspora culture] people have toiled under the yoke of the whip, even while yearning for freedom. With the establishment of an independent [Country], the faint hope is now a mere pen-stroke away from becoming reality, as a proposal for an emancipatory constitution have been presented to the President.
---

Flavor text:
1) The people inhabiting [Country] hereby agree to form themselves into a free state sovereign and independent of any other power in the universe.
2) Every resident person in the territory of [Country] is free in full right.
3) Slavery in [Country] is forever abolished.
