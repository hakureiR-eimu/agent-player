**Banat** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in Danubia. It can be released by any country controlling its capital state of Vojvodina; at the start of the game, both of its core states are controlled by [Hungary](Hungary.md).

As a country with Serbian primary culture, Banat can form [Yugoslavia](Yugoslavia.md); as a country with South German primary culture, it can also form [Germany](Germany.md) or the [South German Federation](South German Federation.md), although this is unlikely to happen in-game due to its distance from core German states. Forming one locks the player out of forming the other.
