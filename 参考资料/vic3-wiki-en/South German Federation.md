**South German Federation** (often abbreviated to SGF) is a [formable nation](Formable countries.md) for any country with South German culture. The most likely country to form it is [Bavaria](Bavaria.md). The South German Federation can be formed either by the usual means or through a journal entry, which requires a South German country to be the only unification candidate for [Germany](Germany.md).

South German Federation can continue on to form [Germany](Germany.md).

9 of the 12 core states are needed in order to form South German Federation
The following countries can form South German Federation. [Baden](Baden.md), [Banat](Banat.md), [Hohenzollern](Hohenzollern.md), and [Transylvania](Transylvania.md) can form it regularly when they have researched Nationalism and own at least 9 of the 12 South German homelands. [Bavaria](Bavaria.md) and [Württemberg](Württemberg.md) can only form it through the journal entry outlined below.

All of these countries exist at game start except for Banat, and all of them can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country controlling their capital. If the South German Federation is formed by Württemberg, its state religion remains Protestant instead of changing to the default Catholic.

### Core states

South German Federation uses the South German homelands as its core states. These comprise the South Germany strategic region, except East and West Switzerland, as well as the states of Alsace-Lorraine, South Tyrol, and Slovenia. Most of these are controlled by [Austria](Austria.md) at game start, making regular formation very difficult.

## Strategy and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
South German Federation
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

### Race to a coast

Baden has access to East Switzerland; be sure to grab it once you are strong enough to defeat [Switzerland](Switzerland.md). Then, attack Sardinia-Piedmont. There are not many other viable conquests, due to Prussia in the North and Austria-Hungary to the south-east.

### Notes

Forming SGF usually means defeating both Prussia and Austria for German leadership. As such, both countries will be left out of future unification plays unless revolutions break out in the two countries.
