**Afghanistan** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in Central Asia.

Afghanistan can further form [Pakistan](Pakistan.md) or [Turkestan](Turkestan.md).

## Formation

Four of the eight core states are needed in order to form Afghanistan; the dashed states are additional Pashtun and Tajik homelands; only Kabulistan, Hazaristan, Herat, and Kandahar are used for release.
Afghanistan can be formed by the following nations when they control four of its eight core states. It can also be [released](Releasable countries.md) by a country that controls all four "inner" core states and has researched Pan-nationalism.

Tajikistan does not exist at the start and is the only releasable or formable country; Dir and Swat can be released by event. The rest all start with the journal entry *Afghan Reunification*. If the entry is active and Kunduz forms Afghanistan, it gains Uzbek as a primary culture; if Maimana forms Afghanistan, it gains both Uzbek and Turkmen as primary cultures, and if Kafiristan forms Afghanistan, it gains Chitrali as a primary culture. If Afghanistan is formed by Tajikistan, it remains recognized. For details on Uzbek and Turkmen homelands, see [Turkestan](Turkestan.md).

## Afghan minors

The Afghan minors are all unrecognized powers except for Kabul, which is an unrecognized regional power, and have similar basic stats, starting technology, and politics. Each starts with states or split states in Afghanistan's core states. They are all Principalities and all Sunni except Hindu [Kafiristan](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kafiristan). They generally start with the Landowners in government with a mix of the Armed Forces, Sunni Ulema, and either Intelligentsia or Rural Folk in opposition, and the remaining interest groups marginalized.

Kabul, Herat, and Kandahar are three of the eligible countries for the *Great Game* [objective](Objective.md), the others being [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), [Russia](Russia.md), and [Persia](Persia.md).

## Strategies and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Afghanistan
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
The most powerful of the Afghan countries in terms of military strength is Kabul. To form Afghanistan as Kabul, you need to go to war with Kandahar, Herat, and Chitral. When attacking there is the potential to engage in multiple wars, it is a good policy to avoid having multiple fronts. Focus on building units and armaments, the candidate should research military technologies such as rifling, annexing states allows the player to add conscripts.

It's recommended that you attack Chitral first as they are the weakest, giving the player time to train units; you can take all of their land but you only need Chitrali Kabulistan. Next I would attack Kandahar - Kabul needs the Hazaristan and Kandahar region, sway Persia with the war goal of retake/conquer state Kandahari Bampur, this is especially useful if all of the wars are in the same context because it places Persia on your side and they can't fight against you; if Britain or another major joins against you, don't bail – the Afghanistan region is landlocked and majors without a land border will struggle and give up. Next, you need to attack Herat and take the entirety of their country.

Alternatively, Afghanistan can also very easily be formed by Herat - by conquering the aforementioned regions from Kabal, Kandahar, and Chitral, Herat starts off weaker than Kabul, so it will require building up the military while attacking weaker powers first.
