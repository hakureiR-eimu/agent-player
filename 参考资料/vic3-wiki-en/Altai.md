**Altai** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in the West Siberia region. It can be released by any country controlling its capital. At the start of the game, it is controlled by [China](China.md).

Altai can also be released if China fails the [Fragile Unity](Chinese content.md) journal entry.
