**Waldeck** is an insignificant power in North Germany. It starts the game bordering [Nassau](Nassau.md), [Hesse](Hesse.md) and [Prussia](Prussia.md).

As a North German nation, it is able to form the [North German Federation](North German Federation.md) and [Germany](Germany.md). Alternatively, it can instead form [Confederation of the Rhine](Confederation of the Rhine.md).

### Interest groups

Due to Waldeck's lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Waldeck begins with the Landowners in government. The Kleinbürger, Evangelical Church, Intelligentsia, Landvolk and Armed Forces are in opposition. The Trade unions and Industrialists are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Waldeck starts in the *Zollverein* power bloc, lead by [Prussia](Prussia.md).

Waldeck has the Professional Army law.

Waldeck begins with 1 Battalion and can call upon no Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fürstlisches Waldecksches Füsilier-Bataillon | 1 Line Infantry | — | North Germany |

Waldeck has no flotillas at the start of the game, as they are landlocked.
