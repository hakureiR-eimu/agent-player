**United Baltic Provinces** is a minor power on the east coast of the Baltic Sea. It starts the game as the *Baltic Governorates* – a puppet under [Russia](Russia.md).

United Baltic Provinces' starting position is unique as its cultures at the start of the game are completely different from its defined cultures.

The Baltic Governorates, with its Russian primary culture, may form [Russia](Russia.md).

If it is reformed, United Baltic Provinces has Latvian, Estonian, and Lithuanian as primary cultures and – with Lithuanian – is able to form [Poland-Lithuania](Poland-Lithuania.md).

All of the seven core states are needed in order to form United Baltic Provinces
If United Baltic Provinces ceases to exist, the following countries can form it when they have researched Pan-nationalism and control all seven core states. None exist at game start, and each can be released by a country which controls its capital state.

| Country | Capital | Primary Cultures | State Religion | Tier | Type | Core states |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Estonia** | Talinn | Estonian | Protestant | Principality | Recognized | Talinn North Livonia |
| **Latvia** | South Livonia | Latvian | Protestant | Principality | Recognized | South Livonia Courland |
| **Lithuania** | Kaunas | Lithuanian | Catholic | Principality | Recognized | Lithuania Vilnius |

If Lithuania forms the United Baltic Provinces, its state religion remains Catholic instead of becoming Protestant.

United Baltic Provinces can also be [released](Releasable countries.md) directly by a country which has researched Pan-nationalism and controls South Livonia and at least two other core states. For release, East Prussia is not considered a core state.

### Interest groups

Due to United Baltic Provinces's general lack of historical IG leaders and its
Landed Voting law, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Baltic Governorates generally begins with the Landowners, Armed Forces, and the Evangelical Church in government as the Conservative Party. The Landvolk, Intelligentsia, and the Kleinbürger are in opposition. The Industrialists and Trade Unions are marginalized.

United Baltic Provinces has the Professional Army law.

United Baltic Provinces starts with 6 Battalions and can call upon 4 Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.
