*"Siam has established itself as the dominant regional power in South-East Asian under the Rattanakosin Dynasty. But will Siam manage to reform its economy and society for the modern age?"*

**Siam** is an unrecognized major power in Indochina. It borders [East India](East India.md) to the west, [Luang Prabang](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Luang_Prabang) and [Chiang Mai](Chiang Mai.md) to the north and [Dai Nam](Dai Nam.md), [Champassak](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Champassak) and [Cambodia](Cambodia.md) to the east, and [Perak](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Perak) and [Johore](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Johore) to the south.

## Formation

If Siam ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country controlling its capital state of Bangkok. Its core states are the Thai homelands.

### Interest groups

Siam begins with the Landowners in government. The Armed Forces, Theravada Monks, Intelligentsia, and Rural Folk are in opposition, while the Petite Bourgeoisie, Trade Unions, and Industrialists are marginalized.

The Theravada Monks have the Buddhist Moralist ideology instead of the default Moralist.

### Diplomacy

Siam rules over [Chiang Mai](Chiang Mai.md) and [Champassak](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Champassak) who are its vassals as well as [Luang Prabang](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Luang_Prabang) who is its tributary.

Siam and [Dai Nam](Dai Nam.md) have a mutual rivalry.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.10. |

Siam has the Peasant Levies law.

Siam starts with 45 Battalions and can raise up to 33 Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions comprise a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st Siamese Army | 45 Irregular Infantry | — | Indochina |

Siam starts with no flotillas.
