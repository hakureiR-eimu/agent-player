This page is intended to advise the player on how to play the [Prussia](Prussia.md) with the goal of forming [Germany](Germany.md), including Greater Germany and potentially [Central Europe](Central Europe.md).

Prussia starts in a powerful, but fickle position. It is on a trajectory of war against [France](France.md) for control over Alsace-Lorraine, and against [Austria](Austria.md) for leadership over Germany. If it can go on to form Germany, it is easily one of the most powerful countries in the world by default. Prussia is smaller than Austria, but generally has the upper hand due to better technologies, being more industrialized and due to already have the vast majority of the German states in their power bloc *Zollverein*.

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Prussia
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

## Initial industry

Lower Silesia (*Niederschlesien*) is initially one of the best states in the entire game, as it has a sizeable population, free infrastructure from the Oder River, and potential for every industrial resource (except oil and rubber), and it is thus a perfect place for a construction sector hub together with Westphalia (*Westfalen*) or Ruhr. Ruhr benefits from the Rhine River, while Westphalia has sulfur and very large deposits of its resources. In fact Prussia has so many states with coal and iron, that you can be really picky. The states with [state traits](State traits.md) boosting coal production are generally best, but West Prussia should be prioritized for motor industries despite lacking relevant potentials due to the [flavored company F. Schichau](List of flavored companies.md).

## Getting a Navy

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.10. |
|---|---|

Prussia starts with a very weak navy consisting of a single Flotilla, and should expand it greatly, if only to support her armies during naval invasions. Luckily the state of West Prussia has the Natural Harbors [state trait](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/State_trait), and so does Elbe, once Hanover, Bremen and Hamburg has joined Prussia. Annexing the coastal German minors via warfare should also be considered; [Oldenburg](Oldenburg.md), [Bremen](Bremen.md) and [Hamburg](Hamburg.md) can be attacked immediately due their relatively low relations. Grabbing Elbe from [Hanover](Hanover.md) also establishes a land connection with [Holstein](Holstein.md); do wait for the *Hanoverian Successsion* to fire so that [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) is no longer in a personal union with Hanover. One can however get them infamy free by unifying Germany so it is a solution for the impatient.

A navy would be vital to seize Spanish Al-Rif and Gibraltar for the lucrative Gibraltar toll.

## Making the most of the Zollverein

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.12. |
|---|---|

The amount of mandates a [power bloc](Power bloc.md) gets as well as how many different principles can be enacted is dependent on the number of members. 10 members are needed to get a fourth principle, which Prussia has at the start of the game, but will not have once Germany has formed. Therefore one should prioritize choosing a 3rd and 4th mandate over upgrading/repicking the already established ones, while North Germany is still split in a myriad of states.

## German Unification

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.9. |
|---|---|

There are several steps to be taken before Prussia can form the German Empire. Firstly 75% of German states has to have researched Nationalism, to complete the [German National Identity](Germany.md) journal entry. This comes very naturally, and should be the case after about 5 years. To help it along, nationalism is a good pick for a first technology for Prussia.

- Solving the Schleswig-Holstein question
- Getting [Hanover](Hanover.md) out of the British sphere of influence
- Convincing most if not all German states to join.
- Remove [Austria](Austria.md) as a possible contender.

Taking Alsace-Lorraine from France is not strictly necessary, and can wait until Germany is formed and stabilized, or it can be taken before hand, if [France](France.md) is found in a particularly weak spot or is outright attacking Prussia.

### Solving the Schleswig-Holstein question

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.12. |
|---|---|

[Denmark](Denmark.md) owns [Schleswig](Schleswig.md) and [Holstein](Holstein.md), which prevents completing the journal entry [The Schleswig-Holstein Question](German content.md), which in turn prevents the formation of Germany (and [NGF](North German Federation.md)). In order to solve the question, Prussia must own at least a part of the state, while Denmark (or any non-German state) owning none. Declaring war on Denmark to take Schleswig and release Holstein would do it (or vice versa; doing it this way is better though, because Schleswig border the Zollverein while Holstein does not). Another would be to release both and then annex [Lübeck](Lübeck.md) in a separate war either before or after. A third would be to simply protectorate (then puppet) Denmark. Another way to establish a front against Schleswig, Holstein and Denmark is to annex [Mecklenburg](Mecklenburg.md). Having Denmark as a protectorate can be lucrative due to the Sound toll; after Denmark becomes a puppet, consider making [Sweden](Sweden.md) a protectorate as well. If Sweden remains a Major Power, at least grab Scania to monopolize the Sound toll.

It is also advised to take Danish Togo while you are at it. In fact declaring the war for Danish Togo severely limits the amount of countries that can join. Taking the state of Jutland can also be considered, as it has a [state trait](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/State_trait) Good Soils, which boost agriculture, and Prussia is generally lacking in good agricultural provinces. This strategy will be difficult with the rework to Interests.

The war should be really simple to win since Prussia starts with military access through [Hanover](Hanover.md). Just plan an invasion into Holstein. The only potential issue is other great powers joining Denmark. Especially France can be keen on doing so. The war should however still be winnable, even with France on Denmark's side.

It is best to solve this entire issue before the German Unification mechanics kicks in, as Denmark will by then actually have something to offer Austria, namely German leadership. It's easier to take one war at a time.

### Hanover

Hanover will end their personal union with Britain sooner rather than later, usually between 1836 and 1840. When that happens, convincing Hanover to join the unification is just a matter of improving relations and inviting them to join the Zollverein, and they will eventually support Prussia. It is also possible to simply puppet them to get it over with quickly, but it is not necessary.

### Convincing German states to join

From game start, most if not all [influence](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Influence) should be spent on improving relations with all the German states, so they will actually vote for Prussia. Declaring inconsequential rivalries with Spain, Ottomans, Netherlands and/or France can provide even more influence. Mutual investment rights can also be useful. All ownership will end up in the unified Germany anyway.

First priority should be given to [Oldenburg](Oldenburg.md), [Hamburg](Hamburg.md), [Bremen](Bremen.md) as well as [Hanover](Hanover.md), [Schleswig](Schleswig.md) and [Holstein](Holstein.md) once they are independent, as they all start with a relation of **0**, while the rest of the German states has a relation of **20** to **30**. Doing so also have the secondary benefit of increasing cohesion in the *Zollverein*. Afterwards the South German states ( [Baden](Baden.md), [Württemberg](Württemberg.md), [Bavaria](Bavaria.md)) are the most important, since they are the closest aligned to Austria, and they should be prevented from supporting them in the eventual leadership play.

### Removing Austria as a contender

It is very difficult to convince all states to vote for you, as long as [Austria](Austria.md) exists as an alternative. Initially Prussia and Austria has a defensive pact [treaty](Treaty.md). Uniquely, leadership and unification plays bypasses the limits on wars due to good relations or defensive pacts, so one can actually win the war while also remaining pretty friendly with Austria. It is advised to time the war, so that most, if not all, other German states support Prussia when declaring war, since they will join the war on whichever side they prefer. It is a good idea to liberate [Hungary](Hungary.md) from Austria in the leadership war, since doing so will trigger an event that will likely cause Austria to retreat to their homelands, potentially dropping them to a minor power and thus opening the possibility of a "Super Germany" containing all German homeland states.

During the Springtime of the Peoples event, keep an eye on Austrian insurrections. The worst case scenario for Austria (best for Prussia) is that the minorities all achieve independence, which would almost permanently leave Austria as a Major Power at best. Another alternative is that Austria forms a dual monarchy with [Hungary](Hungary.md), removing herself as a unification candidate.

Both Austria and Prussia starts with a defensive pact with [Russia](Russia.md). This can often be renegotiated to a proper alliance. Calling Russia into the war, should make the war a formality.

Note that it is not impossible that [Bavaria](Bavaria.md) does so well that they end up as a contender, at least in name. Then they need the same treatment, maintain good relations, while winning a leadership war.

### Forming the North German Federation

Once Prussia is the only unification candidate, and has solved the Schleswig-Holstein question, they will form the [North German Federation](North German Federation.md) as an intermediate step via the [Journal Entry](North German Federation.md) of the same name. This is a big advantage, as it annexes all of the tiny North German states that support Prussia, unifying a lot of split states, and giving you control over all states. Once again it is important to get as many if not all states to support you, at this moment, in order to annex as many as possible. This is a one-time opportunity, so it is important to maximize the states gained.

#### Luxembourg

[Luxembourg](Luxembourg.md) is a North German state, but cannot join neither Germany nor NGF as they are in a personal union with the [Netherlands](Netherlands.md). So freeing them followed by intensive diplomatic lobbying will make Prussia able to get yet another state, when unifying NGF.

It is however so small, that Prussia might not consider it worth the effort.

## Russia

Germany starts with a [defensive pact](Treaty.md) with [Russia](Russia.md), which is generally beneficial, as Russia is one of the only Great powers whom Prussia has no immediate conflicting interests with. In fact, it is often a good idea to renegotiate the [treaty](Treaty.md) to a full alliance, and use this alliance against Austria and France.

## Bullying the Netherlands

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.10. |
|---|---|

Depending on the diplomatic situation, grabbing states off the [Netherlands](Netherlands.md) and releasing the [East Indies](East Indies.md) will make the Indonesian islands easier to conquer. In particular, Friesland is a coastal state and is accessible via land. Alternatively, Netherlands can be made a protectorate which keeps East Indies as an indirect subject. Netherlands cannot possibly defend itself, and is generally at odds with France and Great Britain, meaning they are often left defenseless. The Netherlands and Denmark share two colonies: the West Indies and Gold Coast.

Regarding the [Treaty of London](Belgium.md), Prussia has often an advantage of choosing the 24-point solution, as it improves relations with German states, which is Prussia's primary concern. On top of that, participating provides further [influence](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Influence), which is useful for influencing the German states.

## Confederation of the Rhine

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.10. |
|---|---|

It is in Prussia's interest to prevent the [Confederation](Confederation of the Rhine.md) from forming, as the German unification project cannot proceed while the Confederation exists. A great power can create the Confederation if it or its non-Personal Union subjects own at least 4 states in the Rhine, North Germany or South Germany strategic regions.

It is however unlikely, since Prussia already have good control over most of the states.

Especially Hanover should be kept a close watch on once they are free of Britain, since a great power puppeting them, would mean they already had 3 out of the 4 required states. It's an unlikely event, but one that should be prevented if it is in any way possible. Especially [France](France.md) is problematic, since they already have Alsace-Lorraine acting at the fourth state.

Should the Confederation of the Rhine be formed, it immediately becomes first priority to either tear it apart or completely annex it.

## Forming Großdeutschland

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.9. |
|---|---|

It is possible to get even Austria to vote for Prussia, but only if the Dual Monarchy with [Hungary](Hungary.md) is dissolved. Needless to say, this would instantly make Germany one of the most powerful states, if not the most powerful, in the entire world. Before doing so, it is important to improve relations with Austria all the way to **+50**, so that when the war ends relations with Austria are still pretty good, and there is no risk of Austria declaring a [rivalry](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Rivalry). If they declare a rivalry, it is close to impossible to turn them around get them to support Prussia. If Austria remains a Great Power after liberating Hungary, the Italian states can be liberated in another war.

It is important in the Brothers' War to not take anything other than leadership and maybe investment rights or monetary compensation. You will get it all when forming Germany anyway, and maintaining good relations with Austria is paramount. Once the war is over, you should start re-improving relations, and maybe giving Austria investment rights and taking on their debt (which you helped create). It should not take long, before they eventually vote for Prussia.

When Super-Germany forms with all the Austrian land, Germany will get an [event](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Event), where they have to choose between releasing all non-German countries, keeping them and receiving **70** [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy), or releasing them as puppets and getting **40** infamy. Therefore it is important to time the formation, so that Prussia has no more than 29.9 infamy, as well as at least decent relations with most other [great powers](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Great_power). Within a few years of stabilization, Super-Germany should be powerful enough to take on the entire world, but a *cut down to size* war immediately will be a problem. As long as Germany does not have more than 30 infamy, they should be fine though.

### From Großdeutschland to Central Europe

Once Super-Germany forms, it is important to take some time stabilizing and industrializing the Austrian states. Hungary, Slovakia, Czechia and Croatia will have many [unaccepted](Acceptance.md) and unemployed POPs, which will quickly turn angry. They should at least be given jobs, but Germany should probably also try to liberalize their citizenship laws to at least Racial Segregation to accept those POPs. Furthermore, the initial citizenship laws makes it impossible for those pops to work government jobs in Universities, Government Administration and such. It is also very likely, that Germany has a some of [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy) to get rid off. If not the initially wars should at least be limited to smaller endeavors in for example Africa or Asia.

Prussia can form [Central Europe](Central Europe.md) instead of or after forming Germany.[1] Indeed, Großdeutschland is just a few states away from forming the hegemony. There is incentive to do so as forming Central Europe makes North Italian, Czech and Slovene primary cultures and thus the waves of radicalization of those pops will stop.

It is advised to Puppet [Switzerland](Switzerland.md), [Sardinia-Piedmont](Sardinia-Piedmont.md), [Netherlands](Netherlands.md) and the [Papal States](Papal States.md) instead of annexing them, as it is cheaper in [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy). In the case of the Netherlands, it means that the Dutch colonies will now be Central European. However, Piedmont and the Papal States can be lost to other Italian nations due to *Risorgimento* mechanics. Because Central Europe has North but not South Italian as a primary culture [Italy](Italy.md) would be annexed, but not [Two Sicilies](Two Sicilies.md), it might be worth waiting for Italy to form before puppeting them so that the whole peninsula can be annexed.
