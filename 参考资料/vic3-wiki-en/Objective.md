When starting a new game, the player can select an objective or a sandbox game.
An **Objective** is an optional, overarching goal for the player to accomplish during a game. There are three regular objectives and one tutorial objective; only one objective can be chosen for a game, and it is also possible to start a game with no objective. Each objective recommends four starting countries, but any playable country can be chosen.

During the game, the objective creates journal entries which represent sub-goals on the path to completing the objective. These journal entries generally require certain conditions to unlock, as well as often completing an earlier sub-goal.

Completing the last sub-goal of each objective awards an [achievement](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Achievement). In tone with that, the opening tooltip in the "Learn the Game" objective states that "winning" Victoria 3 is not possible. Instead, one sets their own goals and pursues them as best as they can.

## Learn the Game

“Play a campaign guided by challenges that help you learn both how the game works and how to play it.”
This objective serves as a tutorial for Victoria 3, presenting the player with simple goals in order to familiarize new players with the mechanics of the game. Most journal entries for this objective also trigger a pop-up tutorial, or give an option to trigger one, to further explain the game mechanic(s) involved in that entry. Being a tutorial objective, the game recommends the following changes to the [game rules](Game rule.md):

- AI Behavior towards Players: Lenient
- AI Aggression: Low
- Loyalties Grace Period: Long

The recommended countries are [Sweden](Sweden.md), [Belgium](Belgium.md), [New Granada](Colombia.md) and [Dai Nam](Dai Nam.md).

There are 40 sub-goals for this objective, ranging from [building](Building.md) construction and management, to interacting with [interest groups](Interest group.md) and [laws](Laws.md), to [diplomacy](Diplomacy.md) and [war](Warfare overview.md). Most sub-goals require another to have been completed before they can trigger. Not all sub-goals must be completed to earn the Educated achievement, only those which directly lead to the final sub-goal, *Solidifying our Position*.

## Economic Dominance

“Aim to expand your production, market reach, and trade influence until the world economy is firmly within your control. Your ultimate goal will be to control the lion's share of global GDP.”
This objective pushes the player to become the dominant economy of the world, by controlling more than 25% of the global GDP within their [market](Market.md).

The recommended countries are [United States](America.md), [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), [Ottoman Empire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey) and [Brazil](Brazil.md).

There are 10 sub-goals for this objective, all focused on increasing economic power. There are two mutually exclusive sub-goals, each with a following sub-goal, so only eight sub-goals can actually be completed. The final sub-goal is *The Strong Market*.

## Hegemon

“Nothing short of complete domination of the global populace will satisfy you. Subjugate, conquer, or simply grow your own population until most of the world live inside your borders.”
This objective pushes the player to become the dominant power of the world, by controlling more than 40% of the world's [population](Pops.md), either directly or through [subjects](Subject.md).

The recommended countries are [Prussia](Prussia.md), [Spain](Spain.md), [Japanese Shogunate](Japan.md) and [Egypt](Egypt.md).

There are 11 sub-goals for this objective, all focused on increasing military power or global control. There are four mutually exclusive sub-goals, so only eight sub-goals can actually be completed. The final sub-goal is *The Hegemon*.

|---|---|---|---|
| A Colonial Power | — | — | Has an active Colonial affairs [institution](Institutions.md) |

| Maintain Subjects | A Colonial Power | — | Has five or more puppets, dominions, protectorates, or personal unions |
| African Colonies | A Colonial Power | — | Owns an entire, unincorporated state in Africa |
| Bolstering the Military | African Colonies | — | Has army size of at least 150 and navy size of at least 75 |
| Solidify Our Technological Advantage | Bolstering the Military | — | Has researched: Bolt-Action Rifle Dreadnought Malaria Prevention |
| Recognized Power | A Colonial Power | Started the game at unrecognized major power rank or below | Is recognized |
| Major Power | A Colonial Power | Started the game at or below minor power rank | Major power or higher rank |
| A Great Power | A Colonial Power | Started the game at major power rank | Great power rank |
| The Greatest Power | A Colonial Power | Started the game at great power rank | Number 1 country ranking |
| The Hegemon | Any of Recognized Power Major Power A Great Power The Greatest Power | — | More than **40%** of world population lives in the player's country or its subjects |

## Egalitarian Society

“Power to the people! Become a guiding beacon of liberty and equality for the world to look up to, by assuring opportunity and raising Standard of Living across your nation.”
This objective pushes the player to become a shining beacon for the world, by having less than 10% of their population as peasants, an average standard of living of 20 or higher, and literacy of 90% or higher.

The recommended countries are [France](France.md), [Russia](Russia.md), [Great Qing](China.md) and [Mexico](Mexico.md).

There are six sub-goals for this objective, all focused on improving standard of living and pop rights. The final sub-goal is *An Egalitarian Society*.

| Sub-goal | Prerequisite(s) | Other triggers | Completion trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Encourage Liberal Thought | — | — | The Intelligentsia are powerful and in the government |
| Liberate the Slaves | — | — | Has the law *Slavery Banned* enacted |
| Passing Laws | Encourage Liberal Thought *OR* Liberate the Slaves | — | Has the following laws enacted: Protected Speech Public Schools Multiculturalism Not State Religion |
| Public Services | Passing Laws | Has the following laws enacted: Protected Speech Public Schools Multiculturalism Not State Religion | The following institutions are all level 3 or higher: Education Health System Social Security Workplace Safety Office |
| Women and Children | Public Services | Has the following laws enacted: Protected Speech Public Schools Multiculturalism Not State Religion | Has the following laws enacted: Compulsory Primary School Women's Suffrage |
| An Egalitarian Society | Women and Children Liberate the Slaves | Has the following laws enacted: Protected Speech Public Schools Multiculturalism Compulsory Primary School Women's Suffrage Not State Religion | Less than 10% of country population are peasants Average Standard of Living is 20 or higher Literacy is 90% or higher |

“The new century marks the beginning of a long conflict waged across the world's largest continent, through intrigue, warfare, and deft diplomacy. Guide Russia and Britain to dominate Asia, or lead Afghanistan or Persia on the path to sovereignty.”

Unlike the other objectives, non-recommended countries cannot be selected.
