### Modernizer

Modernizer has two versions. The Modernization Movement starts with the first version. If the country has neither Peasant Levies nor Guild System, then the movement switches to the updated version; this causes the movement to lose its stances on Army Model and Labor Associations and gain stances on Education System and Children's Rights. Both versions share the same stances on Trade Policy, Economic System, Taxation and Land Reform.

### Carlist/Miguelist

Carlist and Miguelist have two versions. The movements start with the first version. After the country researches Corporatism, the movements switch to the modern version; this causes the movements to favor modernized economic systems and less intensely disapprove of non-autocratic distribution of power laws. Both versions share the same stances on Church and State, Citizenship, and Governance principles, with the exception of Theocracy.

### Bakufu

Bakufu has two versions. The movement starts with the first version; it changes to the second version when the [Sonnō Jōi](Japanese content.md) event fires. The second version favors more modern army and navy laws, and disapproves less of loosening the Edo status system. They share the same positions on Distribution of Power, Internal Security, and Labor Associations laws.
