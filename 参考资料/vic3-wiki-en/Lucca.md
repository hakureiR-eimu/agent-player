*"Torn from their rightful Parman lands, the house of Bourbon-Parma was granted Lucca until they could reclaim their rightful throne within Parma upon the death of Marie Louise. With Italian nationalism on the rise, how long can this aberration survive?"*

**Lucca** is a insignificant power in northern Italy. It is able to form [Italy](Italy.md).

Lucca starts with the Petite Bourgeoisie in government. The Catholic Church, Landowners, Intelligentsia, Rural Folk, and Armed Forces are in opposition, while the Industrialists and Trade Unions are marginalized.

## Strategy and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.10. |
|---|---|

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Lucca
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
**Starting Situation**

Lucca is a meme nation (that is, a nation that is not a serious contender at all, with no major strengths) at the start of the game. Almost all of its neighbors are far superior in size and power, leaving it unlikely (but not unable) to form [Italy](Italy.md). It is a nation that is played first and foremost for the challenge, and for the player to tell others they can.

**Grand Strategy**

Like all other meme nations, the key is dragging other countries into your wars to win your fights for you. This can be done in a variety of ways: giving out [obligations](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Obligations) like free candy; offering treaty ports, and then making peace prior to capitulation that do not give the nation the treaty port; and so forth.

Lucca does have a huge advantage compared to most other meme nations: the journal entry for [Italian unification](Italy.md) gives it a means to circumvent its initial weakness by peacefully annexing its neighbors. Peaceful unification is less disruptive than conquest, but does take significantly longer. Eventually, the player might hit a wall in diplomatic attitudes where they are unable to sway the other Italian states, and war will be the best way to unite Italy. Even if the player tries to remain peaceful to unite Italy, a strong military will almost certainly be needed eventually. Regardless of means, forming Italy is the most obvious way forward for a Lucca player.

Lucca can use treaty port offers and obligations to conquer chunks of land to improve its economic situation. However, the [Infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy) gains might make it more difficult to form Italy. Nearby targets of opportunity include the [Ionian Islands](Ionian Islands.md), [Tunis](Tunis.md), or [Morocco](Morocco.md). The player may be able to bargain with [France](France.md) to beat up [Spain](Spain.md) and take Spain's colonies for themselves. The player may be able to bargain with [Austria](Austria.md) to take parts of the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md) for themselves. Schemes like this are how meme nations thrive. However, if the player grabs up too much territory too fast, it may have issues with rebellions that they will be unable to suppress.

Pitting two powerful nations against each other to gain [War reparations](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/War_reparations) is more immediately useful, but territorial gains are more useful in the long run.

**Internal Politics**

Lucca's starting policies are passable. Lucca is too small for internal stability to be an issue within Lucca itself, but this status will go away as Lucca unites more and more of Italy.

**Diplomacy**

Lucca (like other Italian nations) is all about playing nice with the other Italian nations until playing nice isn't a viable option anymore. It is essential that Lucca remain independent to form Italy, but other than that the player can do as they wish. However, a player should avoid putting themselves in a situation where it is unable to call in other powers while their country is still weak. A [Defensive pact](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Defensive_pact) or [Alliance](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Alliance) is essential in this.

**Potential**

Lucca has a means to form Italy built-in, and while usually Italy isn't as strong as the other European majors, it is a respectable power in its own right.

The [Risorgimento](Italy.md) mechanics allows Lucca to actively join in Italian unification, by allowing *another* Italian power to annex it with enough radical pops of Italian culture. This annexation cannot be opposed by any power, and generates little infamy for Lucca's new controller.

**Weaknesses**

Lucca is small, weak, and vulnerable to aggression at game start. Much of the meme nation playstyle is finding ways to connive their way out of their initial situation.
