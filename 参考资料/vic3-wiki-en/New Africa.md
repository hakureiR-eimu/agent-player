**New Africa** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in North America. It may be released by a country that controls its capital or revolt as the cultural secession country of Afro-Americans. At the start date, all of its core states are controlled by the [United States of America](America.md).

## Scripted content

New Africa receives the following [event](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Event) upon being released, if the country it is being released from has either Legacy Slavery, Colonial Slavery, or Slave Trade.

id

### A Constitution of Freedom

For centuries the [African Diaspora culture] people have toiled under the yoke of the whip, even while yearning for freedom. With the establishment of an independent [Country], the faint hope is now a mere pen-stroke away from becoming reality, as a proposal for an emancipatory constitution have been presented to the President.
