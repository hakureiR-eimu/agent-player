**Finland** is a minor power in Eastern Europe. It starts as the *Grand Duchy of Finland* under a Personal Union with [Russia](Russia.md).

It can form [Sweden](Sweden.md) (after fully annexing it) and [Scandinavia](Scandinavia.md).

## Formation

If Finland ceases to exist, it can be released by any country that controls its capital and one other core state. Its core states are its starting states except Åland. These are also the Finnish homelands which start under Finnish control.

### Interest groups

Finland starts with the Landowners in government. The Church of Finland, Petite Bourgeoisie, Intelligentsia, Rural Folk, and Armed Forces are in opposition. The Industrialists and Trade Unions are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Finland starts as a personal union of [Russia](Russia.md), thus part of its *Rossiyskaya Imperiya* power bloc.

[Sweden](Sweden.md) starts with claims on all Swedish homelands owned by Finland; those are all of Finland's states except Kuopio.

### Industry and Resources

**Industry**

The only urban buildings at the start, without counting the Manors and Financial Districts, are 2 Furniture Manufacturies in Uusimaa. Finland is producing a good amount of wood, has some Rye Farms and Fishing Wharves producing food, and even an Iron Mine in Kuopio providing a bit of iron to the Russian Market.

**Resources**

Finland has access to a substantial amount of wood, as well as some iron, lead, and fish. All states except Åland have their wood output boosted by the *Scandinavian Forests* trait, although nearby provinces of other countries also have similar boosts.

### Industry and economy

All of Finland's states has the Scandinavian forest [state trait](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/State_trait). The issue is that Finland is in Russia's market, and since they also have plenty of state traits boosting forestry, there is not much to gain from it (and thus wood starts far below the base price). Since Russia starts with the [Russian-American Company](List of flavored companies.md) it is very likely, that they will simply build forestry for you anyway. Since Finland has a much higher literacy rate than Russia, specializing in much more advanced industry is probably better. As Finland will need Motor Industries and railways, sooner rather than later, those are probably a good bet. Alternatively, paper could be a good specialization, because of Finland's [unique company Nokia](List of flavored companies.md) and all the available cheap wood. As Russia has very inefficient laws they need an oversized bureaucracy to manage it, and thus Paper will likely be in very high demand. The downside here is that its prosperity bonus is increased Electrics Industries and Synthetics Plants throughput, which is not relevant for a long time.

### Immigration from Russia

At some point one might want to enact No Migration Controls or at least Cultural Exclusion to allow the many Russian and Ukrainian peasants to enter the country. This is a double edged sword however, as they are likely to significantly lower the average literacy. Enacting Freedom of Conscience to get a higher [acceptance](Acceptance.md) of Orthodox POPs is then a good idea.

### Declaring independence

If Finland wants to declare independence, it is mildly put difficult to win the war on its own. Even with Great powers backing Finland, it is still difficult. The most likely backer is [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) as they start the game with a mutual rivalry with Russia.

When declaring independence, [Sweden](Sweden.md) could become a problem, as they start with claims on all Swedish homelands owned by Finland; those are all of Finland's states except Kuopio.

### Perkeletankki

Tanks require the [Mobile Armor](Military technology.md) military technology, which is one of the final technologies you can unlock in the "military" tab, so be prepared for a long campaign. To produce 100 tanks, Finland needs 5 fully staffed Automotive Industries.[1] As automotive industries are practically always profitable when producing only cars, it is easy to get the factories up to full employment and then change the production method one week, even if there is no actual demand for tanks.
