**New Zealand** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in Oceania. It can be released by any country which controls its capital. New Zealand can form [Australia](Australia.md).

## Flags

- Default flag
- Has enacted Presidential Republic, Parliamentary Republic, or Corporate State
- Has enacted Presidential Republic, Parliamentary Republic, or Corporate State, and Multiculturalism
- Has enacted Monarchy, and Autocracy or Single-Party State
- Has enacted Theocracy
- Has enacted Presidential Republic or Parliamentary Republic, and Autocracy or Single-Party State
- Has enacted Presidential Republic, Parliamentary Republic, or Corporate State, and Autocracy, Oligarchy, or Single-Party State, and Corporate State, or ruler has Fascist or Integralist ideology

Note: New Zealand's flag will be a random communist flag if it enacts Council Republic.

## Strategy

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
New Zealand
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
When releasing New Zealand, it might be a benefit to remain a puppet of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) for now, in order to have the ["Federate Australia" events and mechanics](Australia.md) available. The only credible threat to New Zealand is Great Britain itself, so the extra defense is not really needed, but staying in the British market is also nice.
