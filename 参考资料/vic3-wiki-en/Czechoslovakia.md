**Czechoslovakia** is a small [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in Central Europe. Czechoslovakia represents a union of the Czech and Slovak peoples. Most of its four core states are controlled by [Austria](Austria.md) at game start.

Czechoslovakia is also able to form [Danubian State](Danubian State.md) and [Central Europe](Central Europe.md).[1]

## Historical context

In 1918, the First Czechoslovak Republic was established in the aftermath of World War I and the collapse of the Austro-Hungarian Empire. This new country was formed by the merging of several territories, including Bohemia, Moravia, Slovakia, and Carpathian Ruthenia. Twenty years later, in 1938, Czechoslovakia was annexed by Nazi Germany under the Munich Agreement. This allowed Hitler to take control of the Sudetenland region while the rest of the country was divided into a German protectorate and a Slovak puppet state.

After the end of World War II, Czechoslovakia was "liberated" by the Soviet Union and became a communist state under Soviet influence. The country was renamed the Czechoslovak Socialist Republic. In 1968, a period of political liberalization known as the Prague Spring took place in Czechoslovakia. However, this was suppressed by the Soviet Union and its Warsaw Pact allies, who invaded the country with their military forces.

In 1989, a peaceful transition to democracy occurred in Czechoslovakia, known as the Velvet Revolution. Negotiations for the peaceful separation of the Czech Republic and Slovakia into two independent nations began in 1992. The two countries were formally separated on January 1, 1993.

Three of the four core states are needed in order to form Czechoslovakia
Czechoslovakia can be formed by [Bohemia](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Bohemia) or [Slovakia](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Slovakia). The formation requires three of the following four core states: Bohemia, Moravia, Slovakia, and Ruthenia.

Czechoslovakia can also be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country which controls its capital and two other core states and has researched Pan-nationalism technology. Both Bohemia and Slovakia can also be released by any country controlling their capital.

### Core states

Czechoslovakia uses its cultures' homelands as its core states. The Czech homelands are in the South Germany region and the Slovak homelands are in the Danubia region.

Horní Slezsko (Upper Silesia) does not count towards the formation requirements, but counts as a core state when releasing the country and will be claimed after formation as a Czech homeland.
