**Panama** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in Central America – its single core state is initially controlled by [Colombia](Colombia.md). When released, it likely borders Colombia, as well as [Central America](Central America.md) or its breakaway [Costa Rica](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Costa_Rica), which it shares [primary culture](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Primary_culture) with.

If Central America has ceased to exist, Panama is able to reform it.

## Historical background

In 1836, Panama was a part of Colombia, one of the three successors to Gran Colombia. Gran Colombia was proclaimed in 1819 after Simón Bolívar's victory over [Spanish](Spain.md) forces near Bogotá and officially formed two years later when its constitution was adopted. After the war with Spain concluded, regional frictions resurfaced and eventually led to the break-up of Gran Colombia in 1830. Panama gained independence from Colombia in 1903.

## Core states

Panama has a single core state, also named Panama. The state begins with only a single level of a Coffee Plantation and Banana Plantation. While Panama does have access to coal, it lacks all other mined resources.

The Panama Canal can be constructed here after Civilizing Mission has been researched, by whoever owns the treaty port province. Great powers can purchase the treaty port or gain a claim on the state by taking the decision *Survey the Panama Isthmus*.
