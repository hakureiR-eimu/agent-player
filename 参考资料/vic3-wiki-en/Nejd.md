**Nejd** is an unrecognized power in the Arabia [strategic region](Strategic region.md). It borders the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md), [Jabal Shammar](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Jabal_Shammar), [Hedjaz](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Hedjaz), and [Bahrain](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Bahrain); it also neighbors but cannot access [Qazimid State](Azal.md), [Kathiri](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kathiri), [Mahra](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Mahra), [Oman](Oman.md), and the [Trucial States](Trucial States.md).

As an Bedouin country, Nejd can form [Arabia](Arabia.md).

If Nejd is a Monarchy and fully owns the state region of Hedjaz, it is called *Saudi Arabia*.

## Historical Background

The Emirate of Nejd was founded in 1824 as a kingdom ruled by the Saudi royal family. It was able to slowly expand across the 19th century but soon experienced decline, with the emirate collapsing in 1891 and the Saudi family being forced into exile after the Ottomans subjugated the Arabian peninsula. After World War I, the Saudi family returned and unified the remaining Arabian states into the modern nation of Saudi Arabia in 1932.

### Interest groups

Nejd begins with the Landowners in government. The Armed Forces, Sunni Ulema, and Rural Folk are in opposition. The Petite Bourgeoisie, Intelligentsia, Trade Unions, and Industrialists are marginalized.
