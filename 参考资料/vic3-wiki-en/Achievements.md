Victoria 3 has 141 different **achievements** with varying difficulty levels, ranging from very easy to very hard. They have no in-game effects and are used mainly to highlight different gaming experiences by providing players goals to strive for.

Once the requirements for an achievement have been completed, a notification pops up to notify the player – note that this happens only once per achievement (unless the achievements are removed from the player's profile using 3rd party tools). Depending on the gaming platform, the achievement can then be used as a vanity item to be displayed on the player's profile.

**Note:** For convenience purposes, this article references the Steam achievements mechanics, but they may be available on other platforms as well. On Steam, it is possible to see the achievements sorted by the percentage of users that managed to complete them by going to the game's [global achievements page](http://steamcommunity.com/stats/529340/achievements/) (for more precise numbers one can use the [Steam web API](http://api.steampowered.com/ISteamUserStats/GetGlobalAchievementPercentagesForApp/v0002/?gameid=529340)).

Many of names and/or pictures are history or pop culture references. See [Easter eggs#achievements](Easter eggs.md) for explanations.

## Achievement eligibility

An achievement can only be acquired if the potential conditions for that achievement are met and the achievement has not been acquired previously. Additionally, certain game conditions invalidate achievement eligibility.

The following block achievement eligibility:

- Multiplayer – This includes saves made in multiplayer then loaded in single-player.
- Debug mode
- [Game rules](Game rule.md) that block achievements – Specifically, *Achievements Blocked*.
- Switching your country via the game menu option – This does not include decisions or releasing countries via the diplomacy screen.
- After reaching the 1936 end screen – This might possibly be due to the player being taken to the country switching screen when picking "Continue Playing" at the end screen.

The following do not block achievement eligibility:

- [Older versions](Patches.md) of the game – As long as the achievement was present in that version.
- Non-Ironman mode – Unlike other Paradox titles.
- Changed checksum (e.g. mods)[1].

Specific achievements often have opaque requirements – many these are clarified in the list below. Here are some general notes on achievement requirements.

- Achievements which require starting as a certain country often set a variable on that country. Releasing and playing as another country typically prevents gaining those achievements, even if the achievement does not specifically require being country. Forming another country does not typically prevent such achievements.

## List of achievements

- The descriptions on this page are taken from the in-game achievement list; the descriptions on Steam (or other platforms) may be different.
- The **Notes** column includes clarifying information regarding the achievement and short, helpful tips. It should not be longer than 2-3 lines (in total).
- The column indicates whether an achievement requires a certain DLC to complete it.
- Achievements are categorized by the initial version that they appeared in and thus the earliest version that they can be completed on.

- The **DI** column indicates the achievement's difficulty level. The initial listing uses the game's difficulty categorization, however, it can be changed according to player consensus.

  - The difficulty levels are:E = Easy,M = Medium,H = Hard,VH = Very Hard,UC = Uncategorized.
