**Pomerania** (German: Pommern, Polish: Pomorze) is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in northeastern Germany on the Baltic Sea. It has a single core state – Pomerania – which is owned by the [Prussia](Prussia.md) at the start of the game.

As a North German state, it can form [North German Federation](North German Federation.md), [Confederation of the Rhine](Confederation of the Rhine.md), [Germany](Germany.md) and [Central Europe](Central Europe.md).

## Historical background

Pomerania has historically been a borderland between Poland and Germany. Since the Middle Ages, it has changed back and forth between independence, Swedish or Danish control. It became fully a part of Prussia in 1815 in the aftermath of the Napoleonic Wars.

After the Second World War, the Polish-German border was drawn along the Oder river (the Oder–Neisse line); today the area is split between the modern German state Mecklenburg-Vorpommern and Poland.

## Core states

Pomerania has a single core state – also named Pomerania. A major issue is the lack of iron and other resources needed for an industry. It has the *Oder River* state trait increasing [infrastructure](Infrastructure.md) by **15**. Due to the lack of industrial resources and obvious profit making products, it is unlikely that Pomerania can maintain a healthy [standard of living](Standard of living.md) in its own market.
