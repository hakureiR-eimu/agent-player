*"Depending on who you ask, the Austrian Empire is in a period of either perfect balance or worrying stagnation. Revolutionary movements are suppressed across the nation, but will this status quo last forever?"*

**Austria** is a great power in central Europe. A large multicultural Empire and the single largest country in the German speaking area, Austria is likely to compete with their northern rival [Prussia](Prussia.md) to form [Germany](Germany.md) through diplomacy or war.

Austria starts with Hungary, Transylvania, and Croatia as Crown Land subjects and Krakow as a Puppet. Through the [journal entry](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Journal_entry) *The Matter of Hungary* Austria can either make Hungary a sole Personal Union while annexing Transylvania and Croatia into Austria or Hungary under the Dual Monarchy, or annex all Austrian subjects if the Liberty Desire of all subjects is below 25. Should the Dual Monarchy be established Austria can later form the Triple Monarchy through a journal decision, releasing Croatia as a Personal Union. Should the Dual or Triple Monarchy be established, when Austria researches Pan-Nationalism they may choose to get a Journal Entry to form the United States of Greater Austria, gaining some combination of Czech, Slovak, Polish, Hungarian, Slovene, Croatian, Romanian, North Italian, Serbian, and Ukrainian as primary cultures. At least five primary cultures are necessary to form the United States of Greater Austria.

## Politics

Austria's initial government is a Kaiserreich based on the Monarchy and Autocracy laws. The initial ruler, Ferdinand von Habsburg supports the Austrian Aristocracy.

Austria and [Paraguay](Paraguay.md) are the only two countries to start the game with Secret Police.

### Interest groups

Austria begins with powerful Austrian Aristocracy and influential Armed Forces and Intelligentsia in government. The Catholic Church, Landvolk, Kleinbürger, and Industrialists are in opposition, while Trade Unions are marginalized. Because of the *Geheime Staatskonferenz* amendment, Austria cannot reform its government at game start.

The Intelligentsia have the Constitutionalist ideology instead of the usual Republican.

### Diplomacy

Austria has [Hungary](Hungary.md), [Croatia](Croatia.md), and [Transylvania](Transylvania.md) as crown lands and [Krakow](Krakow.md) as a puppet. It has trade privileges with Tuscany and Two Sicilies, and is guaranteeing the independence of Tuscany, Two Sicilies, Parma, and Modena. Austria also has a defensive pact with [Russia](Russia.md) and [Prussia](Prussia.md) (the [Holy Alliance](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Alliance)), and provides [Montenegro](Montenegro.md) with grain and fruit aid plus transit rights through a 40-year agreement.

Austria's starting interest levels are pervasive (level 4) in Balkans, Eastern Europe, and Southern Europe; influential (level 3) in Central Europe; and engaged (level 2) in Northern Europe and Western Europe.

Austria starts as the leader of the *Metternich System* power bloc. The other members of the bloc are Croatia, Hungary, Krakow, Transylvania, [Two Sicilies](Two Sicilies.md), [Tuscany](Tuscany.md), [Modena](Modena.md), and [Parma](Parma.md).

Austria starts with pro-country lobbies towards [Russia](Russia.md) () and [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) (), as well as an anti-country lobby towards [Prussia](Prussia.md) ().

## Strategies and Guide

Main article:
Strategy: Austria
Austria starts with some pretty good industries and tech, and is by far the easiest country that can form the Central Europe. Subjugating the Italian states and Switerland early can be good for your late game.

### Forming Germany

A map
by Reddit user u/Mu_Lambda_Theta showing which regions are safe to own before forming Germany, and which regions would cause the event giving you infamy.
It can be quite easy to form Germany as Austria, but beware some common pitfalls:

- You shouldn't fight Prussia for Unification. This will prevent annexing them peacefully. Rather, you should try to either befriend them or balkanize them, and eventually puppet them. Try to ask for puppeting one of their revolutions.
- You SHOULDN'T form Germany while holding:
  - Any North Italian Homeland
  - Any State in the Danubia Region
  - Croatia, Slavonia, Dalmatia and Bosnia

This will fire an event giving you either the choice to release them as independent, release them as puppets for 42 infamy, or keep them for 84 infamy.

### Achievements guide:

Warning: forming the United States of Greater Austria will prevent you from completing the other Austria-based achievements. I do believe that Viribus Unitis incompatible with Quill and Bayonet ( since you can't have the JE as anything other than Austria, and you need to not have any Personnal Unions ).

It's easy to do the Habsburg Resurgence , Quill and Bayonet, and Bierdemier achievements in a single run.

If you want to complete the Quill and Bayonet achievement, do the following:

- Suppress any Liberal/Radical movement and Bolster Absolutist ones
- Wait until you have:
  - Hungary that can be annexed at a click of a button
  - Get rid of Hereditary Bureaucrats and Serfdom
  - You should be in top 3 powers and either have more than 1.5x the GDP of Prussia or have beaten them up in a war ( for example, to take Silesia )
  - Improved your relations with the Papal States to amicable
  - No debt ( any amount of debt will cause the JE to fail faster ) and plenty of freed up bureaucracy

- Once you have all that, try to trigger a revolution liberal is the easiest one ), and wait for it to fire.
- As soon as the revolution fires, reset all your production methods to the lowest ones ( this will lower your GDP). Then, annex Hungary. This will trigger the "Autocracy in the Age of Liberty" JE.
- Wait for the JE to appear, click on all the buttons you can, restore your PM to the most efficient ones.

This should clear the JE as soon as possible, giving you the achievement.
