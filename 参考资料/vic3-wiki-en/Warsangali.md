**Warsangali** is an unrecognized power located in the Ethiopia [strategic region](Strategic region.md) of Africa at the Horn of Africa. It borders it [Isaaq](Isaaq.md), [Harar](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Harar), and [Majerteen](Majerteen.md).

### Interest groups

Due to Warsangali's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Warsangali starts with the Landowners in government. The Sunni Ulema, Rural Folk, and the Armed Forces are in opposition, while the Trade Unions, Petite Bourgeoisie, Intelligentsia, and Industrialists are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Warsangali starts with no diplomatic relations of note and its only active interest is in the Ethiopia region itself.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.10. |

Warsangali has the Peasant Levies law.

Warsangali starts with 2 Battalions and can raise 1 Conscript. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions comprise a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st Warsangali Army | 2 Irregular Infantry | *No General* | Ethiopia |

Warsangali starts with no flotillas.
