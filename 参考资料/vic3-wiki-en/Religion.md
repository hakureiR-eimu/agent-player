**Religion** is a feature of [countries](Countries.md) and [pops](Pops.md). Each country has a *state religion*, which can only change by effect (for example, [Japan](Japan.md) has a [decision](Decision.md) to change its state religion from Mahayana to Shinto) or through the Religious Convocation [power bloc](Power bloc.md) diplomatic action Impose State Religion. Religion is also a defining characteristic for pops, but individuals in a pop can convert to other religions depending on [acceptance](Acceptance.md) level.

## General mechanics

### Acceptance

The [Church and State laws](Power structure laws.md) determine how much [acceptance](Acceptance.md) each pop gains from its religion, by comparing it to the country's state religion. These laws also determine which acceptance statuses may convert in order to gain more acceptance. Pops may convert only if a more accepted religion is present in the state. Conversion speed can be increased by enacting Religious Schools or the Promote National Values decree.

### Taboos

Certain religions have one or more *taboos*. Pops with that religion purchase half as much of taboo [goods](Goods.md) compared to other pops, if other goods that can fulfill a given [need](Needs.md) via good substitution are available, even if the taboo good is comparatively cheaper. Unlike [obsessions](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Obsessions), taboos are fixed and do not change over the course of the game.

### Devout interest group

The Devout interest group can generally be supported only by pops with the country's state religion. Additionally, the name, [traits](Interest group.md), and [ideologies](Ideology.md) of the Devout often reflect a country's state religion, such as the Sunni Ulema or Orthodox Church.

### Atheism

If the law State Atheism is enacted, the country's state religion becomes Atheist, 10% of the country's population in [incorporated](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Incorporated) states immediately converts to Atheist, and all other religions have **−10** acceptance. If another Church and State law is enacted later, the state religion is reset to the country's default state religion. Atheist pops cannot support the Devout.

Outside of the Halloween easter egg [Eden](Eden.md), there are no intrinsically atheist countries.

### Religious convocation

|  | Available only with the [Sphere of Influence](Sphere of Influence.md) DLC enabled. |
|---|---|

Religious convocations are heavily entwined with religion, and the leader of a religious convocation can impose their state religion on members of the power bloc.

## Heritages

There are four heritage groups with one to three heritages each:

| Abrahamic | Eastern | Naturalistic | Non-Spiritual |
|---|---|---|---|
| Christian Islamic Jewish | Dharmic Taoic | Indigenous | Materialist |

Depending on the [Church and State laws](Power structure laws.md), the acceptance from religion is slightly higher for different religions within the same heritage group.
