**Arabia** is a [formable](Formable countries.md) nation in the Middle East. It can be formed by any nation with an "eastern" Arabic primary culture (Mashriqi, Bedouin, Misri, Yemenite).

## Historical background

The Arabian Peninsula was first politically unified under the Rashidun Caliphate, in the 7th century. The Rashidun Caliphate quickly expanded by conquering its neighboring lands, including the Sassanid Persian Empire and many provinces of the Byzantine Empire. After the Rashidun Caliphate was replaced by the Umayyad Caliphate, many of the areas under its control became more Arabic, adopting the language and culture along with Islam, with the notable exception of Persia.

Greater Arabic political unity ended with the Abbasid rebellion against the Umayyads in the mid 8th century. However, the Abbasids retained political control of most of the Arabic world until the mid 9th century, when outside conquests and internal rebellion reduced their power significantly. Following this, the Arab world was divided into smaller states, with a local Arab ruler or ruled by non-Arab powers, such as the Turkish Ottoman Empire.

In the Victorian era, many parts of the Arab world came under the control of the European great powers, particularly following World War I and the dissolution of the Ottoman Empire. The era also saw a rise in Pan-Arabism, though most efforts towards unification eventually failed, often due to rivalries between various states or their leaders.

If Arabia is formed by a Sunni or Ibadi country, its state religion remains Sunni or Ibadi instead of the default Shiite.

Arabia's core states are divided between Egypt, the Ottoman Empire, and the various minor countries of the Arabian peninsula at the start of the game. Egypt is in the best position to form Arabia, as the most powerful and controlling the most core states at the start.

All of the countries are [releasable](Releasable countries.md) and can be released by any country which controls its capital. Releasing Egypt requires two additional core states to be controlled.

Forming Arabia is one of the requirements for the Egyptian achievement, *Muhammed Ali's Ambition*.

### Core states

Arabia has 21 core states, comprising the Arabia strategic region, the southern part of the Near East strategic region and the modern borders of Egypt in the Nile Basin strategic region. These also comprise the homelands of its primary cultures, except for Bedouin, which has several other homeland states.

Arabia's core states are generally resource poor, having little access to mined resources, except oil. Except for certain fertile states, agriculture is also limited, both in amount and variety. The Tigris, Euphrates, and Nile Rivers provide much needed infrastructure to several states. Several other states lay within either the Arabian or Sahara Deserts, making industrialization much more difficult there.

## Strategies and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Arabia
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
[Egypt](Egypt.md) is easily the strongest candidate to form Arabia, already owning 11 of the required core states with the military power to conquer or subjugate the remaining Arabian minors.

Aside from Egypt, [Oman](Oman.md) and [Trucial States](Trucial States.md) are the next most likely. Oman is the next most powerful candidate, and Trucial States has the protection – and possible support – of the mighty [Great Britain](Great Britain.md).

Whichever country is selected, subjugating the other countries is the best method of controlling the required states, as it reduces the amount of infamy gained while still counting towards unification.

Note that although [Darfur](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Darfur) and [Sudan](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Sudan)'s core states are not required for Arabia, the countries are still annexed on formation if they are vassals. Similarly, Oman's other states can be annexed this way as well.

### The Eastern Question

Even before its formation, the Ottomans are a threat to the Arabian project, as they both occupy required states and have claims on states initially held by Egypt.

Once Arabia has been formed, the Turkish part of the empire is ideal for expansion (with relatively easy-to-accept pops and religions), while its European parts may come in handy as sways to get the European powers involved in its dismantling.

### Further expansion

Enacting some kind of [colonization law](Economy laws.md) is important to expand into Africa. The Ethiopian and Somalian States are an obvious choice of conquest. West Africa ( [Sokoto](Sokoto.md), [Benin](Benin.md) etc.) is also very valuable.
