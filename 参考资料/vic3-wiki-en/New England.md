**New England** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in North America. It may be released by any country that controls its capital. At the start of the game, all of its core states are controlled by the [USA](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/USA).

When New England is released, its Rural Folk leader is guaranteed to be a Luddite.[1]

As a Yankee country, New England may form the [Free States of America](Free States of America.md) or reform the [USA](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/USA).

## Achievements

New England is in a decent position for the *Amish Paradise* achievement, since it does not start with much industry and therefore a weak industrialist IG, thus making the Industry Banned law easier to pass, especially with its guaranteed Luddite Rural Folk leader. If New England is released as a puppet of USA, it can then specialize within the broader American market, using a multitude of [decrees](Decrees.md) on their main states.
