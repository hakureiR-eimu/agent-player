**Gobir** is an unrecognized power in West Africa. It directly borders [Sokoto](Sokoto.md) to the south, [Bornu](Bornu.md) to the east, and the decentralized [Kel Air](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kel_Air) and [Kel Dinnik](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kel_Dinnik) to the north.

### Interest groups

Due to Gobir's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Gobir starts with the Landowners in government. The Sunni Ulema and Armed Forces are in opposition, while the Intelligentsia, Trade Unions, Rural Folk, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Industrialists are marginalized.
