**Circassia** is an unrecognized power just north of the Caucasus Mountains.

## Formation

If Circassia ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country that controls its capital and sole core state, Kuban.

## Scripted content

With Sphere of Influence enabled, Circassia starts with the *Honor Before Life* journal entry, representing the Russo-Circassian war.

### Interest groups

Circassia begins with the Landowners in government. The Armed Forces, Sunni Ulema, and Rural Folk are in opposition. The Intelligentsia, Petite Bourgeoisie, Trade Unions, and Industrialists are marginalized.

Circassia has a truce with [Russia](Russia.md) until 1856. Circassia has a claim on Kuban, while Russia has claims on Kuban and North Caucasus. [Caucasian Imamate](Chechnya.md) also has a claim on North Caucasus.

## Military

Circassia has the Peasant Levies and Merchant Navy laws.

Circassia begins with 18 Battalions and may raise up to 7 conscripts at the start. Barracks start with No Organized Training. The battalions compose two armies.

## Strategies and guides

While Russia is blocked from immediately invading Circassia at game start by a Journal Entry, it is inevitable that Russia will be able to attack in the future, potentially as early as the 1850s. Preparing a defense against this is a top priority. One of the most reliable methods to secure Circassia against Russian invasion is to intentionally become a protectorate of Great Britain. While this can potentially be done by starting a Diplomatic Incident against Great Britain and hoping that it sets Make Protectorate as its demand, then immediately conceding, improving relations with Britain, joining the British Empire power bloc, and becoming vassalized is more reliable. Upgrading to a Professional Army should also be a priority.

### Gameplay as a British vassal

While joining the British market will immediately boost the Circassian economy, being a protectorate has drawbacks. In particular, it gives the player much less control during Diplomatic Incidents, as the moment that Britain gets called in, they take the reins and the player can no longer add further demands.

While still limited to the fraction of Kuban and North Caucasus that Circassia holds at game start, the player will need to sabotage relations with the British to be able to prevent becoming a puppet. However, once the rest of those (and likely other) states have been conquered, Circassia can field an army large enough to passively float liberty desire (especially with the occasional unreasonable request for market control, state support or knowledge sharing), allowing the player to improve relations again and benefit from increased British willingness to aid Circassia at war.

### Economy

While Circassia's starting position is modest, it does have a fair amount of wood, iron, and coal reserves, as does the rest of the Caucasus region more generally. Due to the priority of building up a military (both to defend against Russia and to build up clout for treaties with other countries), the player should consider expanding to a full two general's worth of military once the economy is producing 50 construction per week.

### Politics

Circassia starts with Parliamentary Republic, making it relatively easy to pass liberal reforms. Establishing elections will begin to boost Circassia towards recognition, even without signing any treaties with European powers. Combining that with ending slavery and improving literacy past 40% will set Circassia firmly on the road to recognition. If a protectorate, the GDP per capita and Standard of Living bonuses are likely to further contribute to gaining recognition.

### Expansion

Even without reforming its citizenship laws, the Caucasian Tradition bonus significantly boosts the acceptance of other groups in the region. Unintuitively, this does not quite extend to Azerbaijani pops, as their Anatolian heritage is in the Middle Eastern group, rather than Circassian's European group heritage. The malus from this will completely cancel out the Tradition boost while following Racial Segregation, but will still allow them to reach relatively high acceptance with Cultural Exclusion.

Beyond the Caucasus, Circassia is in the unusual situation of having European heritage and People of the Book Sunni Islam. Consequently, reforming citizenship but keeping the default religious law will allow Circassia the ability to integrate most of the pops in any neighboring region. The one putative exception to this is the state of Kalmykia, north of Dagestan, which is ruled by Russia and primarily home to Kalmyk pops that are in the Asian heritage group, speak a Mongolian language, and are a mix of Orthodox and Gelugpa at game start, meaning that with the aforementioned laws they will be limited to 0 acceptance (for Gelugpa pops) or 25 acceptance (for Orthodox pops). Additionally, while the Sunni Kurds and Caucasian Azerbaijanis of northern Persia can reach high acceptance with Cultural Exclusion, the Shia, Middle Eastern pops of the rest of the region will not have as high acceptance.

Due to the military focus inherent to a Circassia playthrough, as well as its position in Europe as a Muslim country that can rapidly modernize, Circassia is reasonably well-positioned to try to shoot for the Deconquista achievement, which will require diminishing Spain enough to turn it into a puppet, as well as forming a religious convocation and raising its cohesion high enough to impose Islam on them. Stealing parts of Greece and Italy en route may also be viable, particularly before Circassia is strong enough to continuously wage war against its more powerful neighbors and Spain.
