*"The Congress of Vienna established the German Confederation, a loose assortment of German intended to keep the peace in Central Europe. The Confederation in practice is dominated by the rivalry between Austria and Prussia. Can the smaller nations survive the coming conflict?"*

**Schleswig** is an insignificant power and one of the smaller countries in continental Europe. It is the junior member of a personal union with [Denmark](Denmark.md), together with its southern counterpart [Holstein](Holstein.md).

As a North German country, Schleswig can potentially form the [North German Federation](North German Federation.md), [Germany](Germany.md), [Confederation of the Rhine](Confederation of the Rhine.md) and [Central Europe](Central Europe.md). It can also form [Scandinavia](Scandinavia.md) since it also has Danish as a primary culture. However, due to the mechanics of nation formation replacing primary cultures with the formed nation's defined primary cultures, forming either one (likely Scandinavia because it is much easier) locks the player out of forming the other.

### Interest groups

Schleswig starts with the Landowners in government. The Church of Denmark, Landvolk, Intelligentsia, Armed Forces, and Kleinbürger are in opposition, while the Trade Unions and Industrialists are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Schleswig begins as a junior member of a Personal Union under [Denmark](Denmark.md).

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.9. |

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Schleswig
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

### Towards independence

Look at the [attitudes](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Attitude) of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), [Prussia](Prussia.md), [Austria](Austria.md) or [Russia](Russia.md) to find one (or more) that has a positive attitude towards you, the most preferable being Protective. Prussia/Austria are the most likely, since it is likely that there are [war goals](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/War_goals) against Denmark which they are interested in. Improve relations with them. Meanwhile, construct industrial buildings that require skilled workers/are profitable (and arms industries are almost always a good pick due to the likely upcoming war) and ports and trade centers for infrastructure and trade capacity; [and maybe a few barracks].

After at least the urban building is complete, declare a war of independence against Denmark, add War Reparations and potentially territory transfer. Holstein and Jutland are best, as Denmark has a relatively strong navy, so a war goal that does not require naval control is more likely. Sway the Great Power to your side as late as possible (just before or at 70 diplomatic play progression); this makes it less likely, that when Denmark switches from being cocky to being concerned, that they have time to sway someone.

Prepare to both mobilize and conscript, and man the frontline against Holstein. The idea is to occupy [Holstein](Holstein.md) as quickly as possible and thereby removing that frontline. But the situation will vary. It may be possible to use 2 generals, both with frontline orders against each side, or one with a majority of units (by promoting them so they have a greater share) and the other mobilized but idle, which will allow the units to act as a garrison, but also able to participate in battles due to being in the same region under "borrowed" units. At the very least, survive until the GP's armies reach you, after which it should be a cakewalk.

This is where a port and trade center comes in handy; you can keep your economy going for a bit by trading, which can pair well with an arms industry and keeping your units armed with guns but more importantly, artillery. In a pinch a [treaty](Treaty.md) for artillery transfer can also be useful.

### Post independence

After this, it is most preferable to join the GP's [power bloc](Power bloc.md) and thus market. Otherwise, become a Protectorate (only possible as a sway during diplomatic plays). Depending on relations, you can also wait if the GP is eager for a trade agreement after independence, or other pacts which can go further towards max relations improvement for a better chance to be in the customs union. The aim is to draw population out of their market and enrich yourself to continue to expand.

However, there is another issue, which is the migration potential if your Standard of Living (SoL) is not attractive enough; a state with a population close to 100k is vulnerable to become "emptied" population-wise, and remains vulnerable until maybe 200k. This is why Russia is preferable over Britain despite being more culturally distant; there are areas of high attractiveness in Britain but also some relative low areas (India), whereas Russia has a much tighter distribution of SoL, making it more reliably for pops from Russia to migrate over. As such, you need to consider your Religious law or Cultural Acceptance law as well as Migration law.

Once you are in their market, it is much smoother sailing. Continue to grow your GDP by becoming the haven of skilled workers for the market you are in (and factory goods). Expand into Sweden, Norway and Denmark. Puppeting them is ideal, but you can also annex some states. Integrate any conquered states immediately. Aim for states that contain factories or resources relevant for your buildings or the market you are in; don't forget about Administrative Centers for Bureaucracy and Universities for Innovation and Literacy for Research as well as Qualification access. Avoid building too many Farms; it removes "unused arable land"; more of which increases migration attractiveness rating.

You can also expand into Germany and take some states, but this will make Prussia increasingly hostile towards you for getting in the way of their German Unification.

Conquer and Puppet the Nordics, then turn to Germany proper. Carefully make plays against both Prussia and Austria and erode their influence in Germany. Cruelly continue to use one against the other for your own gain, and then deliver the final coup de grace, make a play for German Leadership, and then form Germany. This is almost certainly going to take a very, very long time... Maybe, just settle as being Scandinavia...
