The **Free States of America** (FSA) is an alternate history counterpart to the [Confederate States of America](Confederate States of America.md), where [America](America.md) expands slavery and abolitionists attempt to secede in order to keep slavery out of their states. It is also a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country).

Free States of America can also (re)form the full United States of America.

## Formation

75% of the 23 to 36 core states are needed in order to form America; dashed lines represent potential core states.
The Free States of America can be formed by any country below Kingdom tier with Yankee as a primary culture that has enacted Slavery Banned. The forming country must control at least 75% of Free American core states in order to form the FSA.

The following countries are capable of forming the Free States of America. Only Oregon (Columbia District) exists at game start; all of them are [releasable](Releasable countries.md).

### American Civil War

The Free States of America is most often formed through an Abolitionist secession in [America](America.md) while the journal entry [The Slavery Debate](American content.md) is active; this triggers an alternate version of the American Civil War.

### Secession

The FSA can form through secession of the Yankee culture. The American Civil War does not have to have occurred for the FSA to form this way.
