**South Peru** is an insignificant power in South America. It starts the game as a puppet of [Bolivia](Bolivia.md). It can form [Peru](Peru.md), and the [Federation of the Andes](Federation of the Andes.md) after Pan-nationalism has been researched.

Census Suffrage will be amended with Electoral Clientelism (), which increases authority but greatly reduces electoral confidence, representing the deeply entrenched electoral rigging system present in Peruvian politics.

On the other hand, if all [Bolivian](Bolivia.md) subjects become independent – which includes the two above as well as [Iquicha](Iquicha.md) – the player, or the Peruvian minor with highest GDP, will annex the other countries through a similar event – also offering the option to enact the laws outlined above. Additionally, South Peru shares several journal entries and events with its South American neighbors. These include journal entries related to Caudillismo, European immigration, and changing national culture.

South Peru is a Confederal State, with President Nicolás de Pierola y Flores supporting the Intelligentsia. South Peru starts with the *Age of Caudillos Age of Caudillos Country modifier +250% Political Strength of Officers +50% Armed Forces Political Strength +10% Armed Forces Pop Attraction* modifier, increasing the political strength of the Armed Forces greatly.

South Peru starts with **15%** of its population as radicals.

If South Peru gets a law that allows for voting in Elections (except for Single-Party State), the law will automatically be amended with Electoral Clientelism (), which increases authority but greatly reduces electoral confidence, representing the deeply entrenched electoral rigging system present in Peruvian politics.

### Interest groups

South Peru begins with the Intelligentsia in government. The Landowners, Catholic Church, Armed Forces and Rural Folk are in opposition, while the Petite Bourgeoisie, Industrialists and Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Armed Forces have the Caudillismo ideology in addition to their standard ideologies. The Landowners have the Elitist ideology instead of the default Paternalistic. The Landowners also have the Pro-Slavery ideology due to the starting Legacy Slavery.

### Diplomacy

South Peru starts as a puppet of [Bolivia](Bolivia.md). It is bordered by said overlord to the east, and by fellow subjects [North Peru](North Peru.md) and [Iquicha](Iquicha.md) to the north.

South Peru has poor relations with [Spain](Spain.md) and [New Granada](Colombia.md). It has cordial relations with its overlord [Bolivia](Bolivia.md). South Peru also starts with the modifier *Independence Not Recognized by Spain Independence Not Recognized by Spain Country modifier −10% Influence*, until [Spain](Spain.md) recognizes its independence, or South Peru becomes strong enough to disregard Spain.

South Peru has a anti-overlord lobby towards [Bolivia](Bolivia.md) ().
