*It took many bloody conflicts to establish the Third Burmese Empire, and it seems that the struggle is far from over as the British Empire makes its ambitions known. Will Burma become yet another British colony, or will it stand strong against the foreign invaders?*

**Burma** is an unrecognized regional power located in the Indochina [strategic region](Strategic region.md) of Asia. It borders its vassal [Shan](Shan.md), as well as [Chiang Mai](Chiang Mai.md), [China](China.md), [East India](East India.md), [Manipur](Manipur.md), and [Tibet](Tibet.md), and the decentralized nations of [Kuki](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kuki) and [Naga](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Naga).

## Formation

If Burma ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country that controls its capital, Mandalay. Its core states are the Burmese homelands, most of which start at least partially under its control.

### Interest groups

Burma begins with the Landowners in government. The Theravada Monks, Armed Forces, Intelligentsia, and Rural Folk in opposition. The Petite Bourgeoisie, Trade Unions, and Industrialists are marginalized.

The Theravada Monks have the Buddhist Moralist ideology instead of the default Moralist.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.10. |
|---|---|

Burma has the Peasant Levies law.

Burma starts with 34 Battalions and can call upon 39 Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tatmadaw | 30 Irregular Infantry 1 Cannon Artillery 1 Hussars 2 Cuirassiers | — | Indochina |

Burma starts with no Flotillas.
