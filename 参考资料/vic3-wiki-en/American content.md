## American Civil War

*The Slavery Debate* is active at the start of the game. The objective is for America to enact Slavery Banned and keep it active for a total of 10 years without triggering a revolution. Successfully completing this entry will remove the Missouri Compromise modifier and avoid the American Civil War altogether.

If a revolution by either the Pro-Slavery or the Anti-Slavery Movement breaks out, The Slavery Debate will fail and The American Civil War will start. The Missouri Compromise modifier is still removed in this case. If the revolutionaries are seceding, they become either the [Confederate States of America](Confederate States of America.md) (Pro-Slavery) or the [Free States of America](Free States of America.md) (Anti-Slavery). If the movement is not seceding, the American Civil War content is not used. The player is given a choice to either continue playing as America or switch control to the secessionist country. If the Confederate States of America formed and America has not yet passed Slavery Banned, then America receives the event The Emancipation Proclamation during the diplomatic play which gives America the option to immediately enact Slavery Banned and increase approval of every interest group that has a positive stance towards the law, choosing not to enact it instead decreases approval with the same.

The Confederate States of America becomes a releasable country after the American Civil War has occurred, regardless of which country actually formed. When released, it is given all states that are Dixie homelands which can include Arizona and New Mexico if Manifest Destiny has been completed. The Free States of America is not a releasable country, but can always be formed by a Yankee country below Kingdom tier that has enacted Slavery Banned.

## Reconstruction

If the Confederate States of America are defeated and re-annexed into America, the Reconstruction process will start. All states that are Dixie homelands but not also Yankee homelands will become unincorporated and gain the Unreconstructed StateUnreconstructed StateCountry modifier +20% Radicals from Political Movements modifier which increases radicals from standard of living decreases by 25%. America will have 12 years to complete three accompanying journal entries. Success or failure in these objectives will impact America's primary cultures post-Reconstruction.

- **Readmit the Secessionist States:** America must re-incorporate all states that have the Unreconstructed State modifier and raise the percentage of loyalists among the Dixie populations of these states to at least 20%. Failure to complete this objective will result in America losing Dixie as a primary culture when Reconstruction ends.
- **Equality for All:** America must pass a Citizenship law that accepts the Afro-American culture; Cultural Exclusion or Multiculturalism will satisfy this objective. Completing this objective will result in America gaining Afro-American as a primary culture when Reconstruction ends.
- **Wild, Wild West:** America must maintain less than 10% Turmoil and/or Devastation in all of its states that are Yankee and/or Dixie homelands for a total of 5 years.

## The Indian Removal

This entry is triggered by the event Worcester v Georgia, which occurs on January 22, 1836. As long as it is active, standard of living for Cherokee pops in America will be reduced by 3 and America will periodically get events regarding the forced migration of Cherokee pops to the [Indian Territory](Indian Territory.md), typically giving the player a choice on whether to give financial aid to reduce casualties. There are three different ways to complete this entry, each with a different effect:

- Enact Multiculturalism: America will annex the [Indian Territory](Indian Territory.md)
- Enact No Colonial Affairs: temporarily increase standard of living for Cherokee, Muskogean and Algonquian pops in America by 2
- No Cherokee pops remain in America: A random interest group whose leader is Bigoted or Jingoist will temporarily gain 2 approval, and Cherokee homelands in American states are removed.

This entry will be automatically removed if America loses control of Georgia or the Indian Territory ceases to exist or becomes independent.

## The First Transcontinental Railway

This entry becomes active when America owns all of California and Nebraska. The objective is to build a Railway in Nebraska, Wyoming, Utah, Nevada and California. This will trigger the event The First Transcontinental Railway which will give the player the choice of temporarily increasing migration attraction in either the Pacific Coast or Great Plains. This entry could theoretically be completed by any country owning the required states, though this is unlikely outside of the player conquering those states from America.

## Sale of Alaska

This pair of journal entries represent the historical [Alaska Purchase](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alaska_Purchase) between the [United States](America.md) and [Russia](Russia.md). In game, either the owner or overlord of Alaska can initiate the sale or a prospective power in North America can. While this is most often Russia and America, it can potentially be any two countries that meet the requirements.
