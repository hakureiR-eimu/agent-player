**Gaza** is an unrecognized regional power in the Southern Africa region. It is one of the stronger native African nations at game start.

Gaza begins with the Landowners and Armed Forces in government. The Devout are in opposition. The remaining interest groups are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.12. |
|---|---|

Gaza's active interests are in its home Southern Africa and Zanj regions. It neighbors the decentralized [Pedi](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Pedi), [Venda](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Venda), and [Rozvi](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Mashona) to the west, and [Manica](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Manica) to the north, as well as [Zulu](Zulu.md) and [Swaziland](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Swaziland) to the southwest and [Portugal](Portugal.md) to the east.

Gaza starts with no diplomatic ties.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.12. |

Gaza has the Peasant Levies army law.

Gaza starts with 25 Battalions and can call upon seven Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The batallions compose a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st Gazan Army | 25 Irregular Infantry | — | Southern Africa |

Gaza starts with no Flotillas.
