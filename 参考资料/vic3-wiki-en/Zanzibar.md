**Zanzibar** is a special country in east Africa that does not exist at the beginning of the game in 1836 but can appear through the [Rise of Zanzibar](Oman.md) event after the death of rule of [Oman](Oman.md), Said al Said in the [Fractured Oman](Oman.md) event. The events will allow the player to continue as either Oman or Zanzibar, and will either cede the Omani owned parts of the states of Ayika, Lindi, Kenya, or Rift Valley or give Zanzibar [claims](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Claims) on those states. At the beginning of the game, it is however only Zanzibar and parts of Kenya, that Oman actually owns.

If Zanzibar was released through the *Fracturing Oman* journal entry, it would start with Bedouin primary culture instead of Swahili, and Ibadi religion instead of Sunni.

With Bedouin primary culture, Zanzibar may form [Arabia](Arabia.md).
