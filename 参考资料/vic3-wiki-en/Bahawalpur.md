**Bahawalpur** is an unrecognized power in northwestern India. It neighbors the independent states of the [Khalsa Raj](Punjab.md) and [Sindh](Sindh.md), its own overlord, the [British East India Company](East India.md), and fellow subjects [Bikaner](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Bikaner) and [Jaisalmer](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Jaisalmer).

Bahawalpur is able to form [Punjab](Punjab.md), if it ceases to exist, [Pakistan](Pakistan.md) and [India](India.md).

## Politics

Bahawalpur is a Princely State, one of the many nominally sovereign realms in India under the indirect rule of the [British Empire](Great Britain.md). It is ruled by [Nawab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nawab) Bahawal Daudpotra, who supports the Zamindars.

### Interest groups

Bahawalpur starts with the Zamindars and in government, while the Armed Forces and the Sunni Ulema are in opposition. All other interest groups start marginalized.

The Petite Bourgeoisie have the Modernizer ideology instead of the default Reactionary.

### Diplomacy

Bahawalpur is the only Protectorate of the [British East India Company](East India.md) (all other subjects are Puppets.) This gives it more autonomy compared to its fellow subjects. Like all other princely states, Bahawalpur is Exempt from Service, allowing them to choose whether or not to join the [East India Company](East India.md)'s diplomatic plays.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Bahawalpur starts with no urban buildings.

**Resources**

The only resource building Bahawalpur has at the start is a single Livestock Ranch, though it can build many plantations for a country of its size. However, due to Punjab starting as a Split state, Bahawalpur **cannot** access any of the state's industrial resources.
