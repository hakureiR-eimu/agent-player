### Interest groups

Parma begins with the Landowners in government. The Catholic Church, Rural Folk, Armed Forces, and Intelligentsia are in opposition, while the Petite Bourgeoisie, Trade Unions, and Industrialists are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Parma starts in the *Metternich System* power bloc, lead by [Austria](Austria.md). Austria also guarantees Parma's independence.

Parma has both a pro-country lobby () and anti-country lobby () towards Austria.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.10. |

Parma has the Professional Army law.

Parma begins with three Battalions and can call upon one Conscript, using No Organized Training.The battalions compose a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| Truppe Reali Parmensi | 2 Line Infantry 1 Dragoon | Wilhelm Albrecht | Italy |

Parma is landlocked and has no flotillas at the start of the game.
