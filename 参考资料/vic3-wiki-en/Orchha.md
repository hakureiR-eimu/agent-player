**Orchha** is an unrecognized power in north central India. It neighbors [Jhansi](Jhansi.md), [Rewah](Rewah.md) and [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company).

It starts the game as a puppet of [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company).

Orchha is able to form [India](India.md).

### Interest groups

Orchha begins with the Zamindars in government. The Armed Forces, Hindu Priesthood, and Ryots are in opposition. The remaining interest groups are marginalized.

The Hindu Priesthood has the Hindu Moralist ideology instead of the default Moralist. The Petite Bourgeoisie has the Modernizer ideology instead of the default Reactionary. The Intelligentsia has the Constitutionalist ideology instead of the default Republican.

### Diplomacy

Orchha is a puppet of the [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company). Orchha starts with no declarable interests, and its only active interest is in the Central India region.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.12. |

Orchha has the Professional Army law.

Orchha starts with 5 Battalions and cannot raise any Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orchha Army | 4 Irregular Infantry 1 Lancer | — | Central India |

Orchha starts with no flotillas, as it is landlocked.
