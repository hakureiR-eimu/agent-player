**Surguja** is an unrecognized power in northeastern India. It neighbors [Rewah](Rewah.md), [Patna](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Patna), [Mayurbhanj](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Mayurbhanj) and [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company).

It starts the game as a puppet of [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company).

Surguja is able to form [India](India.md).

### Interest groups

Surguja begins with the Zamindars in government. The Armed Forces, Hindu Priesthood, and Ryots are in opposition. The remaining interest groups are marginalized.

The Hindu Priesthood has the Hindu Moralist ideology instead of the default Moralist. The Petite Bourgeoisie has the Modernizer ideology instead of the default Reactionary. The Intelligentsia has the Constitutionalist ideology instead of the default Republican.

### Diplomacy

Surguja is a puppet of the [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company). Surguja starts with no declarable interests, and its only active interest is in the Central India region.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.12. |

Surguja has the Professional Army law.

Surguja starts with 2 Battalions and can raise 1 Conscript. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| Surguja Army | 2 Irregular Infantry | — | Central India |

Surguja starts with no flotillas, as it is landlocked.
