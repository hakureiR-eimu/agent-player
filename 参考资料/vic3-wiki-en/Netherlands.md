*"The Netherlands lost Belgium to revolution six years ago and while still economically stable, their rate of industrialization is slow compared to some of their neighbors. Will the Netherlands be able to emerge as a powerhouse of the 19th century?"*

The **Netherlands** is a major power in the Rhine region. It neighbors [Hanover](Hanover.md) and [Prussia](Prussia.md) to the east and [Belgium](Belgium.md) to the south. The Netherlands starts with two overseas states, Dutch Guyana in South America and Dutch Ghana in West Africa. The Netherlands rules over [Luxembourg](Luxembourg.md) in a personal union and has the dominion of the [East Indies](East Indies.md) in Indonesia. The Netherlands and Belgium start as mutual rivals and have a truce until August 1, 1836.

If it reconquers Belgium, the Netherlands can form the [United Netherlands](United Netherlands.md).

## Historical background

The Kingdom of the Netherlands was established in 1815 following the Napoleonic Wars and the Congress of Vienna. This was the first time that the entire Lowlands were united in a single country since the Eighty Years' War during the 16th - 17th centuries. The united state of the Netherlands continued until the Belgian Revolution in 1830, where [Belgium](Belgium.md) seceded from the union.

## Formation

If the Netherlands ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by a country controlling its capital state of Holland. It uses the Dutch homelands as its core states, which include Holland, Friesland, and Gelre.

### Interest groups

Due to Netherlands's general lack of historical IG leaders and its
Census Suffrage law, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Netherlands typically begins with the Petite Bourgeoisie and Evangelical Church in government. They are typically joined by the Landowners as the Conservative Party. Typically in opposition are the Rural Folk as the Agrarian Party, and Intelligentsia as the Liberal Party; Armed Forces, Industrialists, and Trade Unions may lead their own parties in opposition or join another existing party.

The Landowners have the Pro-Slavery (colonial) ideology due to the starting Colonial Slavery.

### Diplomacy

Netherlands rules over [Luxembourg](Luxembourg.md) in a personal union and controls the [East Indies](East Indies.md) as a dominion. East Indies has several puppets in Indonesia as well.

Netherlands' starting interest tiers are pervasive (level 4) in Western Europe; influential (level 3) in Indonesia; and engaged (level 2) in Central America, Central Europe, Gran Colombia, Northern Europe, and Southern Europe.

Netherlands has claims on the states of Flanders, Gelre, and Wallonia. The Netherlands and [Belgium](Belgium.md) start as mutual rivals and have a truce until May 1, 1839.

Netherlands starts with a pro-country lobby towards [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) ().

It starts the game with a [diplomatic treaty](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Diplomatic_treaty) with [Japan](Japan.md): *Dejima Trade*, receiving 20 units of glass, 20 units of porcelain and 40 units of silk from them and transferring 30 units of liquor to them, binding for 23 years

The Netherlands has access to coal in Gelre and can grow some cash crops in its overseas states, but most of its resource access comes from the [East Indies](East Indies.md) and their subjects.

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.8. |
|---|---|

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Netherlands
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
The Netherlands is in an awkward position. Despite its Major Power status, it is a military minnow; even Belgium poses a challenge for the Dutch to reclaim. Much of the Netherlands' power depends on its dominion, the [East Indies](East Indies.md), which is also more powerful than its master. Its junior personal union partner, [Luxembourg](Luxembourg.md), is isolated by Belgium and Prussia, but is given its own market.

Great powers like France, Prussia and Great Britain will guarantee Belgium's independence after a successful Treaty of London, and may defend them if one attacks too early. In order to avoid a potentially catastrophic early-game war, one can follow a colonial strategy. Other important things to consider are improving relations with France, Great Britain, and Prussia, as they may be helpful allies, depending on the way that diplomacy across Europe develops, and will surely be terrible enemies if one is not careful.

### Tolls

Consider making [Denmark](Denmark.md) a protectorate and conquering Scania from [Sweden](Sweden.md) for the Sound toll. Grab Al-Rif in [Morocco](Morocco.md) to secure a forward base and then conquer Spanish Al-Rif and Gibraltar; this will allow the Gibraltar toll to be imposed.

### Colonisation

Continue to conquer minors across the globe; remember to have at least one conquered state per declared interest so that the region becomes an active interest, and thus freeing up the declared interest counter.

**Indonesia**

The minor countries in South-east Asia should be pushovers even for the militarily challenged Netherlands, and should not pose much [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy) gain. The main danger is a Great Power (most commonly, [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) or [France](France.md)) interfering.

**West Africa**

If one makes early moves to colonize in the Senegal and Niger strategic regions, it may be possible to secure rubber production, as well as other resources like iron, which may be hard to otherwise get without trade before conquering Belgium.

**Southern Africa**

Take note of [Zulu](Zulu.md) and [Gaza](Gaza.md), as they allow access to [Transvaal](Transvaal.md) and [Oranje](Oranje.md); the latter two's Boer homelands allows Netherlands to incorporate them quickly if desired. Though note that the Boer countries are guaranteed by [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) at the start.

**Guyana**

If one is willing to trade colonized states with Great Britain and France, then it is straightforward to acquire the whole of Guyana and benefit from the gold rushes there.

### Belgium

After the truce with Belgium is over, subdue Belgium by any means necessary. Once Belgium is fully mobilized, it can field a larger army than what the Netherlands can muster within Europe; the Netherlands can consider swaying a GP for assistance. It is advisable to fully conquer Belgium as quickly as possible, as the Netherlands can then form the [United Netherlands](United Netherlands.md). Note, however, that Belgium's independence is guaranteed by France. So much diplomatic negotiating must take place to keep those powers from intervening. Keep an eye on if Belgium joins the British Power Bloc.

Before invading, it is wise to increase bureaucracy generation to avoid going past one's limit, as Flanders and Wallonia cost a lot of bureaucracy to integrate because of large numbers of pops. Another difficulty in integrating them will be economic. It is likely that Belgium will have a highly developed construction industry and one may need to downsize some of those buildings to avoid a debt spiral. Additionally, many adjustments may need to be made to trade and production methods, as the economic situations of the Netherlands and Belgium are quite different. However, if one can successfully integrate Flanders and Wallonia, the possibilities for industrialization are enormous.

### East Indies

If the East Indies are not annexed after forming United Netherlands, consider using *Reduce Autonomy* on the dominion. If the Netherlands's military power is not strong enough, it will likely result in war. Netherlands can consider conquering small East Asian nations, and transfer them to the [East Indies](East Indies.md) to lower liberty desire.

### France's natural borders

|  | Available only with the [Voice of the People](Voice of the People.md) DLC enabled. |
|---|---|

If France activates the [journal entry](French content.md), it may receive claims on Flanders and Wallonia. Needless to say, this makes France extremely dangerous, both as a competitor for conquering Belgium and as a threat after Belgium has been annexed. It is advised to seek an ally or defensive pact with a GP rival of France (usually [Great Britain](Great Britain.md)).
