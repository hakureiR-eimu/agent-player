**Oyo** is an unrecognized power in West Africa. It directly borders [Borgu](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Borgu) and [Sokoto](Sokoto.md) to the north, [Dahomey](Dahomey.md) to the west, and [Benin](Benin.md) to the west.

## Formation

If Oyo ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country that controls its sole core state, Yoruba States.

### Interest groups

Due to Oyo's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Oyo begins with the Devout in government. The Armed Forces, Landowners, Petite Bourgeoisie and Rural Folk are in opposition while the rest are marginalized.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.10. |

Oyo has the Peasant Levies law.

Oyo starts with 15 Battalions and can call upon 15 Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions comprise a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st Oyo Army | 15 Irregular Infantry | *No Commander* | Senegal |

Oyo starts with no Flotillas.
