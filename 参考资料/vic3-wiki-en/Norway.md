*"Norway has spent centuries under Danish and Swedish rule, but now dreams of sovereignty. What will it take for Norway to win its independence?"*

**Norway** is a minor power in northern Europe. It starts as the junior member a personal union under [Sweden](Sweden.md).

Norway can form the nation of [Scandinavia](Scandinavia.md) after researching Pan-nationalism.

## Historical background

Norway had a long history of being ruled by its Nordic brothers, [Denmark](Denmark.md) and [Sweden](Sweden.md). After the Treaty of Kiel in 1814, a union between Sweden and Norway was established, but was little more than Sweden becoming overlord of the Norwegian fjords. In the 19th century, Norway was an agrarian country through and through, having not seen much of the industrialization other nations so eagerly chased. Despite being under Swedish rule, Norway enjoyed democratic elections, guaranteed by its constitution, which is celebrated every year on 17 May. The Norwegian people yearn for independence however, wanting to be in charge of their own fate and not be tied to foreign monarchs in Copenhagen or Stockholm.

## Formation

If Norway ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by a country controlling its capital state of Eastern Norway and one of its other two core states. Norway uses the Norwegian homelands as its core states, all of which are owned by it at game start.

As Norway is a personal union of [Sweden](Sweden.md), Norway is ruled by the Swedish king, Karl Johan Bernadotte, who supports the Aristocracy of Officials. It is therefore not possible to change the power structure law as long as it remains in a personal union. Norway is unique from Sweden in that it has Census Suffrage, making it the only Scandinavian country to have elections at game start. The next election is set for 8 November 1838.

Census Suffrage has the Tradition of Free Elections amendment (), increasing legitimacy, lowering enactment chance of laws that would abolish voting, and preventing electoral manipulation. Religious Schools law has the Prussian Education amendment (), increasing Armed Forces political strength, assimilation, and the number of conscriptable batallions by 10%.

### Interest groups

Norway starts with the Aristocracy of Officials and Petite Bourgeoisie in government, with the Armed Forces wanting to join as members of the Høyre. The other interest groups in opposition are the Church of Norway leading the Venstre, with the Rural Folk and Intelligentsia; and the Industrialists leading the Free-minded Venstre.

The Trade Unions are marginalized.

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Norway
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

### General overview

Norway starts out as the least-populated Scandinavian country, is resource-poor, and starts out under the yoke of [Sweden](Sweden.md). It has a burgeoning fisheries and whaling industry, although it starts with no coal and lead within its borders. It has a strong technological and political position, with early access to Stock Exchange and a relatively strong liberal coalition.

Although Norway has a weak military, Sweden itself is also militarily weak and ripe for invasion. With good diplomatic play, economy management and statecraft, Norway is a strong contender to form [Scandinavia](Scandinavia.md) and situate itself as the foremost power in Northern Europe.

### Pre-independence

Although you start under the Swedish Market, this is more of a boon in the early game than a detriment. Due to Norway's low population at game start, it's a good idea to maximize intermarket migration before declaring independence. You will want to liberally use the Greener Grass Campaign decree and potentially only keep taxes at High (as opposed to Very High) to keep SoL high and encourage more migration.

Diplomatically, [Denmark](Denmark.md) will often rival Sweden and is a great backer for your independence war. It's also worth looking around for other backers by checking who Sweden has rivalled: you can often get [Russia](Russia.md) backing you as well. With those two backing you and good economy building, your liberty desire will increase very quickly. Sweden will desperately grant you benefits to keep your LD down and may even upgrade you to a protectorate, and you may want to hold off the independence war to retain these benefits and the migration from the Swedish market.

When you initiate the independence war, it's a good idea to also demand the state of Scania in the war as it will be your only source of coal. You can also demand other high-GDP states like Gotland to cripple Sweden for future wars. If another Great Power (like [Prussia](Prussia.md) or [Austria](Austria.md)) decide to back Sweden for whatever reason, you can still win the war with Russian and Danish help. Conscript up units, play defensively, and strike quickly before your AI allies capitulate.

### Forming Scandinavia

Once all Scandinavian countries researches Pan-nationalism, the Scandinavian [major unification](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Major_unification) begins. Up to three Scandinavian countries become unification candidates; first only great powers, then only major powers if there are no great powers, and finally minor powers if there are no major powers. As Denmark often does not become a major power and Sweden will be crippled due to your independence war, Norway will often be the only Scandinavian major power (or even great power) at this point in the run.

After the independence war, it's a good idea to retain good relations with Denmark. As long as they remain a minor power, they won't become a unification candidate and will generally support your bid for Scandinavian unifications. However, Sweden may occasionally be irrevocably belligerent, especially since you've taken land from them. Because of this, it will be necessary to defeat Sweden in further wars to fully annex them.

Liberating [Finland](Finland.md) from Russia is optional before trying to form Scandinavia, but it makes for cleaner-looking borders. This will usually require another GP ally (like Prussia or Austria) backing you. Even if you do have a technological advantage over Russia, they will have mountains of troops ready to throw at you, so it's good to have another GP as a meat shield while you march onto Finland. You may also wish to take the states of East Karelia and Kola in this war, as they're both resource-rich and are Finnish culture.

Once you've formed Scandinavia, you will likely have a large economy with a luxurious standard of living, Great Power status (or on the precipice of attaining it), and a strong, high-quality military. This will make you an unmistakable powerbroker on the European mainland with the ability to project power anywhere you want.
