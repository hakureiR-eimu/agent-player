### Interest groups

Due to Bhutan's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Bhutan starts with the Landowners and Vajrayana Monks in government. The Armed Forces, Rural Folk, and Intelligentsia are in opposition. The remaining interest groups are marginalized. The Vajrayana Monks have the Buddhist Moralist ideology instead of the default Moralist.

### Historical characters

| Name | Type | Interest Group | Ideology | Traits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chhoki Gyaltsen | Ruler | Vajrayana Monks | Traditionalist | Persistent |

### Diplomacy

Bhutan borders [Sikkim](Sikkim.md) to the west, [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company) to the south, and the [Chinese](China.md) vassal of [Tibet](Tibet.md) to the north. It starts with no diplomatic relations of note.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.10. |
|---|---|

Bhutan has the Peasant Levies law.

Bhutan starts with 2 Battalions and can call on 1 Conscript. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st Bhutanese Army | 2 Irregular Infantry | — | Himalayas |

Bhutan starts with no Flotillas, as it is landlocked.
