**Zulu** is an unrecognized regional power in the Southern Africa strategic region. It is one of the few countries in the region with coastal access, and their starting army is significantly larger than its neighbors' as well.

## Scripted content

Main article:
Southern Africa content
Zulu shares a journal entry with nearby countries related to the struggle for dominance over the *South Africa* [geographic region](Geographic region.md). Specifically, Zulu must ensure diplomatic recognition and coastal access to gain dominance over the other minor states in the region. Additionally, there are several events specific for Zulu that revolve around its relation to the Boer states and the founding of [Natalia](Natalia.md).

### Interest groups

Zulu begins with the Armed Forces in government, with the Landowners and Devout in opposition. The Rural Folk, Petite Bourgeoisie, Trade Unions, Intelligentsia, and Industrialists are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Zulu's sole interest in its home Southern Africa region. It neighbors the decentralized [Ndebele](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Ndebele) and [Xhosa](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Xhosa), as well as [Winburg](Oranje.md) and [Basutoland](Basutoland.md) to the west, and the Nguni nations of [Swaziland](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Swaziland) and [Gaza](Gaza.md) to the north.

Zulu starts with no diplomatic ties.

## Military

Zulu has the Peasant Levies law.

Zulu starts with 20 Battalions and can call upon two Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st Zulu Army | 20 Irregular Infantry | — | Southern Africa |

Zulu starts with no Flotillas.
