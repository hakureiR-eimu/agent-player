*"The legacy of the Polish people rests heavily on the shoulders of the Free State of Krakow, distrusted by its constituting powers and occupied by Austria. Can a new Polish state rise from the ashes once again?"*

**Krakow** is an insignificant power in eastern Europe. It starts as a puppet of [Austria](Austria.md). It sits on the border between Austria, [Prussia](Prussia.md), and [Russia](Russia.md).

Krakow may go on to form [Galicia-Lodomeria](Galicia-Lodomeria.md), [Poland](Poland.md) and [Poland-Lithuania](Poland-Lithuania.md).

## Historical background

The **|Free, Independent, and Strictly Neutral City of Krakow with its Territory** was formed following the Congress of Vienna in 1815, as a remnant of the Duchy of Warsaw which was partitioned among Austria, Prussia and Russia. The city state had its autonomy reduced after the **November Uprising** in 1830. After the failed **Krakow Uprising** in 1846, the city state was fully annexed by the Austria.

### Interest groups

Due to Krakow's general lack of historical IG leaders and its
Wealth Voting law, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Krakow typically begins with the Intelligentsia and the Landowners in government leading the Liberal Party. In opposition, the Catholic Church and the Petite Bourgeoisie form the Conservative Party, Industrialists lead the Free Trade Party, and the Rural Folk may form the People's Party; any interest groups not affiliated with a party are also in opposition. The Armed Forces and Trade unions are marginalized.

The Landowners start with the Magnatial ideology, making them favour Wealth Voting, Oligarchy and Landed Voting over Autocracy.

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Krakow
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

### Starting Situation

Krakow is a meme nation for all intents and purposes. It is advisable for an Austrian player to annex Krakow immediately. Krakow is too small and weak to be capable of independent action, and the fact that it is a puppet (as opposed to a [Protectorate](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Protectorate)) handicaps it both diplomatically and economically.

On a more positive note, Krakow's starting laws are relatively good by the standards of game start, and the player will not need much effort to bring its laws to a competitive state. With a strong Intelligentsia (through level 3 Private Schools, Wealth Voting and 2 Universities), whose clout should be used to pass laws like Right of Assembly to increase Innovation, Cultural Exclusion to enable population growth by migration.

Breaking free from Austria is nearly impossible before the Defensive pacts with [Prussia](Prussia.md) and/or [Russia](Russia.md) are ended.

### Grand Strategy

Krakow's most sensible objectives at game start are to break away from Austria, and eventually to form [Galicia-Lodomeria](Galicia-Lodomeria.md), then [Poland](Poland.md), then [Poland-Lithuania](Poland-Lithuania.md). It is possible to form Poland-Lithuania after forming Poland.

Krakow will almost certainly need help from a third party (usually Prussia or Russia) to secure independence from Austria. After that, the player will have to choose which of the two to go after. Russia's land is usually easier to grab, but Prussia's lands are usually better developed. Either way, the player will need lands from both in order to form either Poland or Poland-Lithuania.

Once the lands are annexed, the player is still in a vulnerable situation and landlocked, sandwiched between three hostile nations that usually rather powerful.

### Before Unpausing

State Decrees on Krakow: Road Maintenance and Social Mobility. Improve relations with [Prussia](Prussia.md) and [Russia](Russia.md), then with other potential independence supporters.

Use Subject Interactions (with refreshes) to decrease opinion with [Austria](Austria.md) and increase liberty desire.

Research Railways and build a Steel Mill and a Motor Industry in the meantime, as Krakow has low infrastructure capacity.

Pass Per-Capita Taxation to increase income.

**Side Optional Strategy passing Isolationism**

Krakow is landlocked, so after obtaining independence it is unlikely to obtain Transit Rights from its neighbors, so Isolationism can be helpful before gaining a coastal state.

### Politics and laws

Krakow's Intelligentsia start in a strong position, bolstered by Private Schools, and its small size means that it will experience the political impacts of industrialization (increased power for Industrialists, Trade Unions, and Petite Bourgeoisie at the expense of the Landlords, Rural Folk, Catholic Church and Armed Forces) very quickly. This makes it easy to further liberalize its already relatively-progressive starting policies. However, reforming Citizenship from the starting National Supremacy to at least Racial Segregation will be vital before long in order to let the economy continue to expand with migration, immediately benefiting from a steady stream of immigrants inside the Austrian market.

As the leader of Ideological Union, [Austria](Austria.md) can [force a Regime Change](Diplomacy.md) on Krakow. Although it can set back progress in [Power Structure Laws](Power structure laws.md), it can be an opportunity to pass Technocracy and [Stamp Out Monarchism](Ideology content.md). Austria is also likely to impose Secret Police on the player, which can then be used to further the player's interests.

Due to the various factors favoring political modernization in Krakow, even the more conservative IGs in Krakow are likely to be led by Liberals, Radicals and Reformers, and it is relatively easy for the player to quickly pass reforms (and invest in economic development) that will lead to the marginalization of the Landowners and Catholic Church early on. As a consequence, while Austria can impose laws that are instantly adopted every few years, Krakow will be able to immediately set about re-liberalizing laws with starting success rates at 70% or even higher, with relatively few long-term consequences. Alternatively, the player can game the system and reload an earlier save in the event of regime change, then set unwanted Interest Groups in power so that they get evicted during the regime change.

### Christ of Nations

By researching Nationalism and adopting either Right of Assembly or Protected Speech (or becoming independent), Krakow has access to the *Christ of Nations* Journal Entry, which gives the player access to a decision that spends a small amount of money to increase Polish separatism in the Polish territories of one other country abroad. Prior to independence, Austria is not an option, severely limiting the usefulness of the event in the early game. The event's description includes a cost of a significant loss of relations with the target, which would make its use nearly prohibitive for a Krakow that is counting on its neighbors' support. However, in practice this penalty does not appear to be applied consistently.

### Independence War(s)

Breaking away from [Austria](Austria.md) will require the support of [Prussia](Prussia.md) (or successor states) and/or [Russia](Russia.md) to ensure that Krakow does not get occupied during the war. Additional war goals is take East and West Galicia to form [Galicia-Lodomeria](Galicia-Lodomeria.md) and Austrian Upper Silesia for its sulfur deposits (if [Austria](Austria.md) does not lose the split state when fighting [Prussia](Prussia.md)).

Although it is possible to have an army of few regiments on irregulars, it won't be strong fighting force. It can still be used to make the Armed Forces IG Influential to benefit from their happy trait to boost military research, and it is likely to make Austria more inclined to concede investment rights while Krakow is still a subject.

**1st War Increase Autonomy and form [Galicia-Lodomeria](Galicia-Lodomeria.md)**

Staying as Subject of [Austria](Austria.md) to keep benefiting from their market helps building up newly-formed [Galicia-Lodomeria](Galicia-Lodomeria.md).

**2nd War Independence**

Only after building a strong economic base, as Krakow will leave its overlord's market. If [Austria](Austria.md) sways [Prussia](Prussia.md) or [Russia](Russia.md) on its side, their states can be selected as war goals to form [Poland](Poland.md) after the war.
