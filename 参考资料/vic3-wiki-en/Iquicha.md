*"The autonomous state of Iquicha has been caught in the maelstrom of the Peruvian civil war, and stood on the side of Santa Cruz. Can it maintain its independence in this new Confederation?"*

**Iquicha** is an unrecognized power in South America. It starts the game as a puppet of [Bolivia](Bolivia.md). Iquicha is one of only three unrecognized countries in the Americas at game start (the others being [Indian Territory](Indian Territory.md) and [Miskitia](Miskitia.md)).

As a Quechua culture country (the only one in the game), Iquicha can form [Tawantinsuyu](Tawantinsuyu.md) - the empire of the Inca.

## Scripted content

Main article:
South America content
Due to Quechua not having the Hispanophone language trait, Iquicha does not have access to the majority of South American journal entries. However, it does have access to the [The Peru-Bolivian Confederation](South America content.md), as it is a member of it.

### Interest groups

Iquicha begins with the Rural Folk in government. The Landowners, Armed Forces, and Catholic Church are in opposition, while all other interest groups are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Iquicha starts as a puppet of [Bolivia](Bolivia.md), and has Amicable relations with them.

Iquicha is surrounded by fellow Bolivian subjects [North](North Peru.md) and [South Peru](South Peru.md), and is one of only two countries in South America to start without coastal access - the only other one being the independent [Paraguay](Paraguay.md).

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Iquicha has no industrial buildings at the start of the game.

**Resources**

Iquicha's only starting building is a Maize Farm, but its split state Ica has surprisingly decent agricultural potential - especially if Iquicha manages to take the rest of the state from [South Peru](South Peru.md). The state also has iron and wood.

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.9. |
|---|---|

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Iquicha
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
If Iquicha is controlled by the player, failing *The Peru-Bolivia Confederation* journal entry will result in Iquicha becoming independent instead of being annexed into [Peru](Peru.md), as it is when Iquicha is controlled by AI. Instead, [Peru](Peru.md) will be formed by [North Peru](North Peru.md), who will annex [South Peru](South Peru.md) (if the later exists). If the journal entry is instead completed, Iquicha is forcibly annexed into [Peru-Bolivia](Peru-Bolivia.md) regardless of player control. As such, it is recommended that the player starts building up their army and producing small arms as soon as possible, in preparation to declare independence.

One method used in the formation of [Tawantinsuyu](Tawantinsuyu.md) is to get independence support from [North Peru](North Peru.md) and any nearby minor or major power (usually [Argentina](Argentina.md), [Brazil](Brazil.md) and/or [Chile](Chile.md)). Then, declare a war against both [Bolivia](Bolivia.md) and [South Peru](South Peru.md). This lets the player conquer La Paz, Santa Cruz, Ica, and Arequipa in their independence war, allowing [Tawantinsuyu](Tawantinsuyu.md) to be formed immediately afterward, cutting down on the infamy required to get all the remaining core territories.
