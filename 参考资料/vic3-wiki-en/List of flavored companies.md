Many historical [companies](Companies.md) are represented as flavored companies. The AI is more likely to establish a flavored company over a basic one. These companies are typically tied to specific [states](State.md) or [cultures](Culture.md). As such they are heavily associated with certain [countries](Countries.md), but those tied to states are not technically limited to specific countries as in theory any country can conquer any state. The following are worth noting:

- If the (incorporated) state required does not specify full ownership, a split state will suffice.
- It is not possible for two countries to establish the same flavored company. It is always first come, first served.
- The required building levels must either be owned by the establishing country or its citizens (local workforce, Manor Houses, Financial Districts). Foreign ownership and rival (foreign) company ownership does not count.

The companies here are organized by the countries they are most commonly associated with. Ownership is Full Capitalist Ownership unless specifically noted otherwise.
