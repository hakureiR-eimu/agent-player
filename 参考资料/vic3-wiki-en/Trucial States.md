*"As the Ottoman Empire declines, the future of Arabia becomes ever more uncertain. Will a new power emerge from the desert?"*

The **Trucial States** are an unrecognized power in the Arabian Peninsula on the Persian Gulf. They are a protectorate of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md). Trucial States neighbor [Bahrain](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Bahrain), [Oman](Oman.md), and [Persia](Persia.md). While they do neighbor [Nejd](Nejd.md), the starting border is impassible.

As a Bedouin nation, Trucial States can form [Arabia](Arabia.md).

## Formation

If it ceases to exist, Trucial States can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country controlling its capital and only core state of Trucial Coast.

### Interest groups

Trucial States typically begins with the Landowners and Sunni Ulema in government, with the Armed Forces, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Rural Folk in opposition.

The Trade Unions, Industrialists and Intelligentsia are marginalized.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Trucial States start with only a single Fishing Wharf and no other industry.

**Resources**

Trucial Coast has access to fish and eventually a large amount of oil, but no other resources. The arable land is only usable for tobacco, subsistence pastures, or Livestock Ranches.

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.10. |
|---|---|

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Trucial States
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
**Starting Situation**

The Trucial states start the game politically, economically, and technologically backwards. They start with a relatively low population, few buildings, and sparse natural resources. On the flip side, they start as a protectorate under [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), so they are relatively safe from aggression. This status also provides British market access to acquire goods that the Trucial States do not produce themselves at the start.

**Grand Strategy**

The most obvious path forward is to leverage the relationship with Britain to conquer the other Arab states. The European powers (including Britain) may try to conquer the weak nations, so this should be done with some haste. Once the European nations gain a foothold in the Arabian peninsula, it will be difficult to get them out. The Trucial States will be able to field an army to conquer the other Arab nations, but will be too far behind in technology to fight the European powers. [Yemen](Yemen.md) should be a high priority target, as its population gives it much more growth potential than the rest of the Arabian peninsula.

After the Arab territories are integrated, the player will have to choose targets of opportunity for further expansion (if desired). [Oman](Oman.md)'s overseas territories can provide a springboard for future expansion. [Egypt](Egypt.md) or the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md) might be weak enough to attack directly after the Arabian Peninsula is united. The Horn of Africa is close by and relatively weak, but also underdeveloped.

**Internal Politics**

Like many other underdeveloped states at game start, the internal politics of the Trucial States will revolve around getting the Landowners out of power without breaking the country. As long as the player's economy continues to progress, the Landowner's power naturally begins to fade, so it can be a good idea to wait a while rather than fight a [revolution](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Revolution). Most laws are not worth fighting a revolution over.

**Diplomacy**

The diplomacy of the Trucial States is mostly about maintaining good relations with Britain until they are ready to break free. Breaking free isn't particularly hard from a military standpoint, but it can be devastating from an economic standpoint if the player is too dependent on goods produced elsewhere in the British market. Simultaneously, the player should avoid annexation by Britain, by keeping relations high and checking the British AI's diplomatic attitude. Getting too close to Britain so they can reduce autonomy is also a rissk.

**Potential**

The Trucial States can form [Arabia](Arabia.md), once the appropriate technologies have been researched. Once oil is discovered, Arabia can gain tremendous wealth.

**Weaknesses**

Population will be a problem, as most Arab states will rapidly run out of labor for their industries and armies. Access to raw resources will also be a problem.
