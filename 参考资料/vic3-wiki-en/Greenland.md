**Greenland** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in the North Atlantic. Its sole core state is large in size, but has a tiny population – around 11,000 individuals. It starts the game under [Danish](Denmark.md) rule.

As a Danish nation, Greenland is able to form [Scandinavia](Scandinavia.md). As an Inuit nation, Greenland can also form [Turtle Island](Turtle Island.md), although it would have to move its capital to North America.

## Core states

The state of Greenland starts without any buildings. Every single individual is employed in subsistence fishing.

The potential is extremely lacking as well. Only 3 units of arable land, which can be used only for Livestock Ranches. The only potential resources are fishing and whaling stations. The only upside is the Arctic Whaling trait, increasing the throughput of whaling stations. Unfortunately, meat and oil are likely the only resources not lacking in Greenland.

One single building is more than enough to employ the entire working population at the start of the game.
