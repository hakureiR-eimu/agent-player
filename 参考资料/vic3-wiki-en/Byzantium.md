**Byzantium** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in Southern Europe and Anatolia. It can be formed by any nation with Greek primary culture: these are the [Ionian Islands](Ionian Islands.md), [Crete](Crete.md), [Cyprus](Cyprus.md) and of course [Greece](Greece.md). Byzantium can only be formed with the [game rule](Game rule.md) *Formable Nations: All*.

## Historical background

Flag of the late Byzantine Empire
The Byzantine Empire was a continuation of the Roman Empire in the eastern Mediterranean region, which had its capital in Constantinople (modern-day Istanbul). The empire emerged from the division of the Roman Empire in 395 AD, when the Emperor Theodosius divided the empire into two parts: the Western Roman Empire with its capital in Rome, and the Eastern Roman Empire, with its capital in Byzantium, later renamed Constantinople. The Byzantine Empire spanned over a thousand years, from the fall of the Western Roman Empire in 476 AD to the capture of Constantinople by the Ottoman Turks in 1453. During this time, the empire went through many changes, both in its size and in its political and religious structure.

One of the most important developments in the history of the Byzantine Empire was the conversion of the emperor Constantine to Christianity in the 4th century AD. This led to the establishment of Christianity as the state religion of the empire and the development of the Eastern Orthodox Church, which became an important institution in Byzantine society. The Byzantine Empire faced many challenges throughout its history, including invasions by the Huns, Goths, and other barbarian tribes, as well as conflicts with the Persian Empire to the east. In addition, the empire had to contend with internal conflicts, such as the Iconoclastic Controversy, which pitted supporters of the use of religious icons against those who opposed them.

Despite these challenges, the Byzantine Empire remained a major power in the Mediterranean world for centuries, with a sophisticated culture and a highly developed system of government and administration. Its legacy can still be seen today in the art, architecture, and political institutions of many countries in Europe and the Middle East.

Of its 12 core states, two are controlled by Greece at game start and 10 are controlled by the [Ottoman Empire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey). While Cyprus, the Ionian Islands, and Crete can form Byzantium, their core states are not Byzantine core states.

Cyprus and Crete do not exist at game start and each can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country which controls its capital. Ionian Islands and Greece are also releasable; Greece requires the releasing country to control at least one additional core state.

### Core states

Byzantium has 12 core states, all of which except Northern Thrace and Albania are Greek homelands; however, several Greek homelands are not core states for Byzantium. Byzantium's core states are in the Balkan and Anatolia regions.

Byzantium's core states have a good amount of iron, but lack enough coal for the later part of the game, with only 48 available coal mines in Aydin. Sulfur and lead can be found in Attica, though not in great quantity. A small amount of oil can also be discovered in Albania later in the game. Additionally, the Byzantine core states can build several types of plantations, notably including silk and opium.

Eastern Thrace contains the Hagia Sophia monument.

## Strategy and guides

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Byzantium
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
The easiest way to form Byzantium is to play as the [Ottoman Empire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey) and annex Greece, and then later release as independent and select *play as*. That way you're only two states away from forming Byzantium because you acquire every Greek homeland state by doing this. You only miss Northern Thrace and Albania. While you're doing this, remember to switch capital as the Ottoman Empire as if you don't you won't get Eastern Thrace (Constantinople) because they can't give away their own capital.

The eastern half of Byzantium consists of Turkish-majority states; these [pops](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Pop) will not be accepted unless Multiculturalism is enacted.
