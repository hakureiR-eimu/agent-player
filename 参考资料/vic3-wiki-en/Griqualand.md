**Griqualand** – called *Waterboersland* at the start of the game – is a [captaincy](Government type.md) and unrecognized power in the Southern Africa strategic region. Its capital is the city of Kimberley in the state of Northern Cape, next to its twin state [Philippolis](Philippolis.md).

## Historical background

The Griqua are a mixed people who originated in the intermarriages between Dutch colonists in the Cape and the Khoikhoi already living there. They turned into a semi-nomadic Afrikaans-speaking nation of horsemen who migrated out of the Cape Colony and established short-lived states on the Colony's borderlands. In the 19th century, the Griqua controlled several political entities which were governed by Kapteins or Kaptyns (Dutch for "Captain", i.e. leader) and their Councils, with their own written constitutions.

Adam Kok I, the first Kaptein of the Griqua and recognised by the British, was originally a slave who had bought his own freedom. He led his people north from the interior of the Cape Colony. Probably because of discrimination against his people, they again moved north—this time outside the Cape, taking over areas previously controlled by San and Tswana people. This area, where most of the Griqua nation settled, was near the Orange River, just west of the Orange Free State, and on the southern skirts of the Transvaal. It came to be called Griqualand West, and the territory was centered on its capital "Klaarwater", later renamed Griekwastad ("Griquatown"). Later, [Andries Waterboer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andries_Waterboer) took over control and founded the powerful Waterboer dynasty. The Waterboer Kapteins ruled the region until the influx of Europeans accompanying the discovery of diamonds, and to some degree afterwards too. In 1834, the Cape Colony recognized Waterboer's rights to his land and people. It is for this reason that the country at start is known as "Waterboersland", instead of Griqualand or Griqualand West.

After several conflicts during the 19th century related to mining rights in the region, the British took control over the territory in 1871, and it was later formally annexed by the Cape Colony through an act 1877, which was promulgated in 1880.

## Politics

Griqualand is a Griqua Captaincy and an Autocracy, ruled by Captain Andries Waterboer, who supports the Landowners. The starting laws of Griqualand are very conservative, but they do start with Slavery Banned and Homesteading.

### Interest Groups

Griqualand starts with the Landowners and the Armed Forces in government, while the Rural Folk and the London Missionary Society are in opposition. The Trade Unions, Petite Bourgeoisie, Intelligentsia and Industrialists start marginalized.

The London Missionary Society have the Laissez-Faire ideology in addition to their starting ideologies. Additionally, they will be lead by characters of English and Scottish culture.

### Diplomacy

Griqualand's sole interest in its home Southern Africa region. It neighbors the decentralized [San](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/San) and [Tswana](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Tswana), as well as its twin Griqua state [Philippolis](Philippolis.md), the Boer republics of [Potchefstroom](Transvaal.md) and [Winburg](Oranje.md) and the much larger ![Flag GBR uk.png](/images/thumb/6/6e/Flag_GBR_uk.png/24px-Flag_GBR_uk.png) [Cape Colony](South Africa.md).

Griqualand starts with a treaty with [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) that guarantees the independence of the former and also gives them transit rights to allow for trade with the World Market, as Griqualand starts off landlocked. It also starts with a treaty with [Potchefstroom](Transvaal.md) that gives the latter transit rights for the same reason. Both treaties last for 10 years.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Griqualand has no starting industry and a single agricultural building, a Livestock Ranch.

**Resources**

The Northern Cape has access to a decent amount of iron, but as it starts as a split state, Griqualand can mine very little of it, and also has no access to the state's (already minuscule) wood. Furthermore, without Prospecting, it can't even make use of the state's Gold Fields.
