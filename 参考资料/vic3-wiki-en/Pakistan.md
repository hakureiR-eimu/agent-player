**Pakistan** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in Northern India. It can be formed by any Sunni country with a "Pakistani" culture (Kashmiri, Punjabi, Sindi, Baluchi, Pashtun) below empire tier.

Pakistan can form [India](India.md) (BHT).

## Historical background

Pakistan was formed as part of the dissolution of the British Raj in 1947. As part of the broader Indian independence movement, Muslim Indians – particularly those in majority Hindu areas – sought independence and self-determination as a separate Muslim majority country. Although the British Empire was opposed to partition of India and Pakistan, it agreed when it became clear that a union could not be accomplished. Pakistan initially included part of Bengal – as East Pakistan, which seceded in 1971 and became Bangladesh.

The name "Pakistan" comes from its five major regions: **P**unjab, **A**fghania (Pashtunistan), **K**ashmir, **S**indh, and Baluchis**tan**
