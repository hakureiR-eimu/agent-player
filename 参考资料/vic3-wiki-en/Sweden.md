*"After losing Finland and gaining Norway in a personal union, Sweden begun the slow process of industrialization, while becoming more liberal in some ways. What does the future of Sweden hold?"*

Sweden is a major power in northern Europe. Sweden is the overlord of neighboring [Norway](Norway.md), making it the most powerful nation in Scandinavia. Sweden has good trade relations with [Prussia](Prussia.md). To the east, [Russia](Russia.md) – with [Finland](Finland.md) under its grip – challenges Sweden's supremacy in the Baltic. Sweden is in the process of liberalization and industrialization.

Sweden can form the nation of [Scandinavia](Scandinavia.md), after Pan-nationalism has been researched.

Sweden, along with [Belgium](Belgium.md), [New Granada](Colombia.md), and [Dai Nam](Dai Nam.md), is one of the recommended countries for the *Learn the Game* [objective](Objective.md).

## Historical background

After the end of the Napoleonic wars, Sweden emerged as the most populous and wealthy country in Scandinavia, outmatching its old rival [Denmark](Denmark.md) and with [Norway](Norway.md) firmly under its boot. Despite losing [Finland](Finland.md) to Russia in 1809, Sweden has a long and proud history of military proficiency, even if the last time Sweden was at war was in 1814. The modern era was not kind to Sweden, with many of its poorer citizens leaving Sweden behind forever for a better life in [America](America.md). The strong farming culture and agrarian economy held Sweden back compared to the rapid industrialization seen in its neighbors. Only in 1870 did Sweden really enter the Industrial Era, during which a strong democracy emerged.

## Formation

Three of the four core states are needed in order to form Sweden
If Sweden ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country that controls its capital state (Svealand) and another core state: Götaland, Scania, or Norrland. It can also be [formed](Formable countries.md) by any Swedish culture country that controls three of those four states.

### Diplomacy

Sweden is the overlord of [Norway](Norway.md) through a personal union and has mutual trade privileges with [Prussia](Prussia.md), binding for 8 years.

Sweden's starting interest tiers are pervasive (level 4) in Northern Europe; and engaged (level 2) in Central Europe, Eastern Europe, Russia, and Western Europe.

Sweden has a pro-country lobby towards [Prussia](Prussia.md) ().

Sweden starts with claims on all Swedish homelands owned by [Finland](Finland.md), as well as West Karelia; those are all of Finland's states except Kuopio. Finland is however a Personal Union under [Russia](Russia.md).

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Sweden
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.

### Starting Economy

Sweden has good techs and a good [literacy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Literacy), and starts fairly industrialized. Their states are good, but population is low. Svealand (Stockholm) is the best state, and it has some unemployment, so that is probably where construction should start.

The state of Scania has an [infrastructure](Infrastructure.md) shortage. Railways is not researched yet, but is not far away, and until then the issue can be solved with Road Maintenance. As Scania is one of the richest states in Sweden and has the only tooling workshops in the country, using road maintenance is as good as mandatory.

The lack of coal is a long term concern, as there are only 8 levels available in Scania in both Sweden and Norway (and Denmark for that matter).

#### First company

Sweden starts with Corporate Charters already researched, but has not established any [company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Company). If a company is established on day 1, the two available are the basic forestry and basic mining companies. Both are good for the [construction loop](Beginner's guide.md). It is an option to wait a bit until something else comes up, but there is not really anything just around the corner.

Note that Sweden has a [unique company LKAB](List of flavored companies.md) for Iron (with [prestige iron](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Prestige_good)), but since that requires Open Hearth Process it is a little way off, and at that point the Basic Mining Company can be replaced.

#### Misc economy

Note that tools are very expensive and all the Rye Farms are using tools at game start, even though there are available and in Svealand even unemployment. Soon, labour saving methods will be needed however.

Due to Sweden's (and Scandinavia in general) low population, the basic food company is also a good choice for a company.

### Politics

As Sweden has a relatively low population, Charity Hospitals are a good choice for a first law.

### Finland

Sweden has claims on most [Finnish](Finland.md) states. If Sweden wants to take them, [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) is an obvious choice for an [ally](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Alliance), as they start with a mutual rivalry with Russia. If Sweden owns Finland when forming [Scandinavia](Scandinavia.md), Finnish will be added as a primary culture, but it can't be added afterwards. Even though most of Finland has Swedish homelands, this is not entirely insignificant as Finnish belongs to a different [language group](List of cultures.md).

### Forming Scandinavia

Once Pan-nationalism has been researched by every Scandinavian nation, the [major unification](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Major_unification) system kicks in. When that happens it is likely that at that time both [Denmark](Denmark.md) and Sweden will have the same power rank, that of [major power](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Major_power), assuming that Sweden has kept Norway and Denmark hasn't been on the wrong end of a war against a major German nation. Getting Denmark on board is enough to satisfy the requirements. If both countries become unification candidates, a war over leadership is more or less necessary to ever form Scandinavia. Denmark may at some point fall below major power rank, but it is not a given, so to be certain Sweden will have to rise to [great power](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Great_power) status before all Scandinavians research Pan-nationalism to avoid the leadership war.

At the very beginning of the game, Denmark and Sweden have very cold relations but not actually a rivalry. However, Denmark controls the other half of the Sound toll, and her colonies can provide resources which Sweden have difficulty securing. Consider making Denmark a protectorate.

If Denmark decides on a rivalry and sticks to it, it is very difficult to ever form Scandinavia and at the same time avoiding war. If Denmark choose to be conciliatory, Sweden can then choose to meet those diplomatic efforts or choose the way of rivalry. If good diplomatic ties are established, Sweden can potentially form Scandinavia by rising to great power status, improving relations with Denmark (and potentially a independent Finland) without ever firing a single shot. As long as Norway remains a junior partner of Sweden and at least decent relations are kept, their vote can be taken for granted. Getting Denmark (and an independent Finland) into a power bloc led by Sweden greatly increases the probability of forming Scandinavia.

Another quick and easy strategy is to declare an interest in the Niger before unpausing. Unpause, and when the interest activates on January 31, declare on Denmark to take Togo from them. Add "Make Denmark a protectorate" as a primary demand, then let the war tick. When you inevitably single-handedly stomp Denmark as you start with nearly double their army, just enforce the protectorate war goal; you don't have to actually take Togo. Tada, all of Sweden, Norway, Denmark, and Denmark's puppets as your subjects in 1836!

Note that in the patches which exclude Iceland as a core, if Prussia or Austria takes Schleswig-Holstein, it will prevent the formation of Scandinavia.
