Countries are the primary actors of Victoria 3. Each is controlled by a player or by the AI and pursues its own goals during a game. Each country has a set
type
- Recognized, Colonial, Company,
- Unrecognized, or Decentralized

and
tier
- Hegemony, Empire,
- Kingdom, Grand Principality,
- Principality, or City-State

and a dynamic
rank
- Great Power, Major Power, Minor Power, Insignificant Power,
- Unrecognized Major Power, Unrecognized Regional Power, Unrecognized Power,
- or Decentralized Power

.
There are 730 countries defined in the game: 443 of which are present at the 1836 start date. 74 countries are formable, of which 15 exist at the start (+12 more which can be formed only by effects); 394 are releasable, of which 205 exist at the start (+20 more which can be released only by effects). Countries can also appear on the map by culture secession or be generated as part of a revolution (48 can only appear in this way).
