**Peru** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in South America. At the start, its lands are split among [North Peru](North Peru.md), [Iquicha](Iquicha.md) and [South Peru](South Peru.md), all puppets of [Bolivia](Bolivia.md). Its land borders Bolivia to the east, [Ecuador](Ecuador.md) to the north and [Grão-Pará](Grão-Pará.md) to the northeast.

Peru is able to form [Federation of the Andes](Federation of the Andes.md).

On the other hand, if all [Bolivian](Bolivia.md) subjects become independent – which includes the two above as well as [Iquicha](Iquicha.md) – the player, or the Peruvian minor with highest GDP, will annex the other countries through a similar event – also offering the option to enact the laws outlined above.

Additionally, Peru shares several journal entries and events with its South American neighbors. These include journal entries related to Caudillismo, European immigration, and changing national culture.

## Formation

Four of the five core states are needed in order to form Peru
Peru can be formed by either North Peru or South Peru when they control at least four of its five core states. Peru can also be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country that controls Lima.

| Country | Capital | Primary Cultures | State Religion | Tier | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **North Peru** | Lima | South Andean | Catholic | Principality | Colonial |
| **South Peru** | Ica | South Andean | Catholic | Principality | Colonial |

North and South Peru both exist at the start of the game and neither can be released if they cease to exist.

### The Peru-Bolivian Confederation

If all [Bolivian](Bolivia.md) subjects become independent – which includes the two above as well as [Iquicha](Iquicha.md) – the player, or the Peruvian minor with highest GDP, will annex the other countries through a similar event – also offering the option to enact the laws outlined above.

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.11. |

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Peru
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
At game start, both [North Peru](North Peru.md) and [South Peru](South Peru.md) start as [Bolivia](Bolivia.md)n puppets. A lack of independent foreign policy makes it difficult to find powers to intervene in Peru's independence and unification.

You can be guaranteed independence, most likely by [Chile](Chile.md) and [Brazil](Brazil.md), by improving relations and drafting [treaties](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Diplomatic_Treaty) to guarantee independence. The more countries you can have guarantee your independence (including South Peru or [Iquicha](Iquicha.md)), the better. If you are short on influence, you can rival [Ecuador](Ecuador.md) or [Paraguay](Paraguay.md).

While doing so, research Napoleonic Warfare and enact the Enlistment Efforts decree. Train some Lancers when researched, and get a new general, searching for an Offensive Planner. Other useful early technologies are the ones that increases your minting, as you have gold mines.

Then launch the independence play 2 or 3 years before 1845 (the date the [War of the Confederation Journal Entry](South America content.md) ends). Be sure to release all of Bolivia's puppets to form Peru. If any subjects ask for Increasing Autonomy to switch to your side, reject it, as this overrides the Release Subject war goal. Sway every country that favors you, as most of them have claims on Bolivia (Jujuy for [Argentina](Argentina.md), Antofagasta for Chile, and both Amazonas and Mato Grosso for Brazil)

You can get Antofagasta if Chile doesn't ask for it and have some maneuvers to spare (losing this state removes Bolivia's World Market access, so you can later draft a good Treaty to warrant them Transit Rights, and also get gold). If Ecuador joins Bolivia's side, then ask for their main state, Ecuador (same logic of removing their World Market access, also the state has coal mines).

When you win, you will get an event to form Peru, or to form Peru and enact Census Suffrage and Right of Assembly.
