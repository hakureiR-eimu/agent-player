**Sulu** is an unrecognized power in north Indonesia and southwest Philippines. As a neighbor of both the [Bulungan](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Bulungan), [Brunei](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Brunei), [Maguindanao](Maguindanao.md) and [Philippines](Philippines.md).

### Interest groups

Due to Sulu's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Sulu starts with the Landowners in government. The Intelligentsia, Rural Folk, Sunni Ulema, and Armed Forces are in opposition, while the Trade Unions, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Industrialists are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Sulu starts with no diplomatic relations of note, and its only active interest is in Indonesia.
