**New Brunswick** is an insignificant power in northern America. It starts the game as a colony of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) and has the potential to form [Canada](Canada.md).

## Scripted content

All Canadian countries have access to the following journal entry and decision. Note that none of these actually form Canada, which must be formed manually through the usual mechanics.

### Interest groups

Due to New Brunswick's general lack of historical IG leaders and its
Wealth Voting law, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
New Brunswick begins with the Armed Forces and the Petite Bourgeoisie in government as the Liberal-Conservative Party, with the Anglican Church most likely joining them. In opposition, the Industrialists may form the Free Trade Party, while the Intelligentsia may lead the Liberal Party, joined by the Trade Unions and the Landowners - the latter due to their historical Reformer leader, Edward Chandler. The Rural Folk are also in opposition, and may form the Patrons of Industry or remain without a party.

### Diplomacy

New Brunswick is a colony of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) and thus part of the *British Empire* power bloc. New Brunswick neighbors fellow British colonies [Lower Canada](Quebec.md) to the north and [Nova Scotia](Nova Scotia.md) (with which it shares its split state of the Maritimes) to the south, as well as the independent [United States](America.md) to the west. It has an undeclared interest.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

New Brunswick begins with almost no buildings aside from a single Port.

**Resources**

New Brunswick has access to lead, wood and coal, and begins with a couple Lead Mines and Logging Camps to extract the first two. It has no iron, but there is a decent amount in nearby [Lower Canada](Quebec.md). Being part of the [British](Great Britain.md) market will likely account for any needs New Brunswick - and Canada - may have in other regards.
