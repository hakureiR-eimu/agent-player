This article is [timeless](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Category:Timeless) and should be accurate for any version of the game.

Like other games from [Paradox Interactive](Paradox.md), Victoria 3 contains **easter eggs**, references to history (outside that directly covered by the game) or other works of media.

## Achievements

Several [achievement](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Achievement) names or images are puns or references to historical or contemporary slogans or media.

**Amish Paradise**
  - The name of a parody song by [Weird Al Yankovic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%22Weird_Al%22_Yankovic); the Amish typically live an agrarian lifestyle and reject most modern technologies.

**Deeds, not words!**
  - The slogan of the British [suffragettes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suffragette) ([Women's Social and Political Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women%27s_Social_and_Political_Union)) in the early 1900s

**Go West, Young Man**
  - A phrase that represents American westward expansion in the 1800s

**I didn’t vote for Pedro**
  - A reference to a slogan from the movie *Napoleon Dynamite* and Brazilian emperor, [Dom Pedro II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_II_of_Brazil)

**Poor, Huddled Masses**
  - A reference to the poem *The New Colossus* by [Emma Lazarus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Lazarus), associated with the Statue of Liberty and immigration to the United States

**Anarchy in the UK**
  - A reference to the [Sex Pistols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_Pistols)' song of the same name

**Broken Promises**
  - The achievement may be a reference to Italy joining the Triple Entente in 1915 despite being a member of the Triple Alliance with Germany and Austria-Hungary.

**The Man Who Would Be King**
  - A reference to the book of the [same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Man_Who_Would_Be_King), said to have been inspired by Josiah Harlan among others

**All Quiet on the Western Front**
  - A reference to the book of the [same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Quiet_on_the_Western_Front)

**Diplomatic Victory**
  - A reference to the Computer game series *Civilization*, in which one of the win conditions is called a Diplomatic Victory

**Can't Touch This**
  - A reference to the rap song *U Can't Touch This* by [MC Hammer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MC_Hammer). The icon is based on the cover art for the single.

**For Twelve Years You Have Been Asking**
  - The name is a reference to the novel *Atlas Shrugged* by [Ayn Rand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayn_Rand)
  - The 5-3000 in the icon is a reference to the character [Liberty 5-3000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberty_5-3000) from the novella *Anthem*, also by Ayn Rand
  - Ayn Rand is known for supporting laissez-faire capitalism, among other things, which ties in with the achievement's requirement of all buildings being privatized.

**Great Game no re**
  - A reference to the online phrase [GG no re](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/GG_no_re). The icon features a former developer of the Victoria 3 team that used to play Starcraft 2 professionally, where the term "GG no re" is commonly used.

**Our Words are Backed…**
  - A reference an [urban legend regarding a bug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_Gandhi) in the Computer game *Civilization*, where Gandhi would supposedly become extremely aggressive

**Hyperpeace**
  - A reference to the [Finno-Korean Hyperwar](https://knowyourmeme.com/editorials/guides/what-was-the-finno-korean-hyperwar-ancient-finngolias-lore-explained) meme.

**I'm the Captain Now**
  - A reference to the 2013 movie *Captain Phillips*

**Champagne Socialist**
  - [Champagne socialist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champagne_socialist) is a pejorative political term commonly used in Great Britain, referring to self-described socialists with lavish lifestyles.

**Fordlandia**
  - A reference to a district in the Brazilian state of Pará, [Fordlândia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fordlândia), established by Henry Ford in 1928 to secure cultivated rubber

**Systembolaget**
  - [Systembolaget](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systembolaget) is a state-owned Swedish chain of liquor stores, created by merging regional alcohol monopolies in 1955. Even today it is the only retail store allowed to sell strong liquor in Sweden.

**Venice, Vidi, Vici**
  - A play on the Latin phrase [Veni, vidi, vici](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veni,_vidi,_vici)

**Yes, We Have Bananas!**
  - The name is a reference to the song *Yes! We Have No Bananas* by Frank Silver and Irving Cohn, which is said to have been inspired by the shortage of Gros Michel bananas.
  - The icon featuring a jester is likely a reference to the [Gros Michel Joker](https://balatrogame.fandom.com/wiki/Gros_Michel) card from the videogame [Balatro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balatro); the jester's design is quite similar to Balatro's jokers.

**Not yet lost**
  - The name of the achievement is a reference to the current national anthem of Poland: "[Poland is not yet lost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland_Is_Not_Yet_Lost)" ("Jeszcze Polska nie zginęła"). There is also an event with a similar name, *Is Poland not yet lost?*

**Deșteaptă-te, Române!**
  - The name of the achievement is Romanian for "Awaken Thee, Romanian!", and is the name of the [national anthem of Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deșteaptă-te,_române!) and former national anthem of Moldova.

**Declaration of Independence**
  - The picture depicts [Freddie Mercury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freddie_Mercury)

**Biedermeier**
  - The achievement is named after the period in art history [Biedermeier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biedermeier)

**David Slays Goliath**
  - Named after the [biblical story of David and Goliath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goliath)

**EXACTLY 100!!!**
  - Reference to the [It's Over 9000!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/It%27s_Over_9000!) meme

**Quill and Bayonet**
  - A play on the saying "The pen is mightier than the sword." The picture is a reference to the movie [The Matrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Matrix)

**Sorbia is Serbia**
  - The picture is probably a picture of a [Sorbet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbet), a type of dessert – A pun on Sorbia

**I’m Feeling Hungary**
  - A pun on Hungary / Hungry

**Prussia of the Balkans**
  - The picture is a [Pickelhaube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pickelhaube), a Prussian type of helmet

**Deconquista**
  - Pun on "Reconquista"

**Portugal is not a small country!**
  - Reference to historical propaganda during the Portuguese dictatorship under Antonio Salazar, comparing the size of Portugal's overseas empire to mainland Europe to argue Portugal was a great power

**Regeneration**
  - The icon is a reference to [the Doctor](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/the_Doctor)'s regenerations in *Doctor Who*.

**An Offer You Can't Refuse**
  - The icon is a reference to the [Trade Offer](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/trade-offer) meme

**Full Circle**
  - The achievement deals with the Carlists and the icon represents a team member of the Victoria 3 team, named Karl

**One More Time**
  - The name and the icon reference the song *One More Time* by [Daft Punk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daft_Punk)

**Mikasa es su Casa**
  - Pun on the Spanish saying 'Mi casa es us casa' (my house is your house) and the pre-dreadnought battleship *Mikasa*

**Martin Anward's Pirates**
  - Reference to Victoria 3's game director Martin Anward, and to the game [Sid Meier's Pirates!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sid_Meier%27s_Pirates!)

**Nobody Did Their Duty**
  - Reference to the phrase [England expects that every man will do his duty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_expects_that_every_man_will_do_his_duty), sent from the British flagship HMS Victory before the [Battle of Trafalgar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Trafalgar) against French and Spanish navies and its subsequent victory. The achievement flips the phrase on its head by requiring France or Spain to sink the British flagship.

**Knock knock**
  - References to the viral video [history of japan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mh5LY4Mz15o)

**Thing, Japan**
  - References the [Thing, Japan](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/thing-japan) meme

**Everybody disliked that**
  - References the [Everybody disliked that meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/everyone-disliked-liked-that)
  - The icon references the [Supa Hot Fire](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/people/supa-hot-fire) meme

**Thanks, Obama**
  - Reference to the [Thanks Obama meme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thanks,_Obama); Obama is also the name of a city located in the Hokushin'etsu region

**Railroad Taikun**
  - Pun on the [Railroad Tycoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Railroad_Tycoon) video game series and [Taikun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taikun), a title of the Japanese shogun

**Nothing personnel, kid**
  - Reference to [Coldsteel The Hedgeheg](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/coldsteel-the-hedgeheg) and the [*Teleports Behind You* Nothing Personal, Kid meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/teleports-behind-you-nothing-personal-kid) derived from the first art of him

**Golden Spirit**
  - Its Ainu focus is a reference to the manga *Golden Kamuy*

**Wealth, Fame, Power**
  - Reference to the opening of *One Piece*

## Characters

**Dracula**
  - With [fantastical content](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Game_rules) enabled, Dracula can appear for a Romanian country with a state in Northern Transylvania, during the out-of-game months of October and November. When he has appeared he is immortal, and there is a landmark of his castle added to the map.
**Frankenstein**
  - With [fantastical content](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Game_rules) enabled, Dr. Frankenstein can appear and the Monster can be recruited as a general for any country owning the state of Bavaria and having good relations with [Switzerland](Switzerland.md).
**Karl Marx in Estonia**
  - When the Communist Manifesto is written, any independent nation can support it, causing Karl Marx to appear in that country. If that happens in [Estonia](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Estonia), Marx's name changes to Kras Masov. This refers to a background character in the game *Disco Elysium* made by Estonian studio ZA/UM; this character is in turn a reference to the historical Marx.
**Michael Kundak**
  - [Michigan](Michigan.md) can get a politician called Michael Kundak, a self-insert of one of the developers, Mike Kundak-Cowall. Michigan itself can only exist if [historically plausable countries](Game rule.md) are turned off.
**Frederik Wester**
  - The Swedish [company](List of flavored companies.md) *John Paradox and Company* (see below) will get an executive called Frederik Wester. Wester is the CEO of Paradox Interactive.

## Countries

**Aggressor**
  - If [Iberia](Iberia.md) picks Esperanto as its national language and has a fascist governmentA fascist government requires: ● Corporate State, Presidential Republic, Parliamentary Republic, Council Republic, or Social Monarchy ● Autocracy, Oligarchy, or Single-Party State ● Corporate State, Social Monarchy, or Ruler has Fascist or Integralist, it changes name to "Aggressor", a reference to the fake country the US Army used in its wargames in mid-20th century that used Esperanto.

**America**
  - The in-game name for [America](America.md) is always abbreviated to "USA" regardless of the [government type](Government type.md), leading to names such as United Sovereign Archduchy ( Monarchy) or Unified Scientific Associations ( Technocracy).

**Byzantium**
  - [Byzantium](Byzantium.md) is a mainstay easter egg in both Victoria and Hearts of Iron, and is also a formable country in Victoria 3. It requires that [historically plausable countries](Game rule.md) is turned off.

**Cyberia**
  - If [Siberia](Siberia.md) becomes a Council Republic with Technocracy (cybernetic state), the name changes to the similarly pronounced Cyberia. The idea was initially proposed by a [post on Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/victoria3/comments/1je4k73/siberia_with_the_cybernetic_state_system_of/), which a dev saw.

**Eden**
  - The country of [Eden](Eden.md) can be created if [fantastical content](Game rule.md) is turned on, and one has decided to support Frankenstein and establish the company. (See above)

**Florida**
  - The flag of [Florida](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Florida) is a simplified variant of a [flag used at the inauguration](https://dos.fl.gov/florida-facts/florida-state-symbols/state-flag/floridas-historic-flags/state-flags/state-flag-1845/) of its first governor.

**France**
  - [France](France.md) has special [government type](Government type.md) names if it becomes a republic, in reference to the five historical republics of France. The First Republic existed before the game's start date, so it starts with the Second Republic. Switching from and back to a republic increments the number, up to fourth.

**Galicia**
  - [Galicia](Galicia.md) is the only country not on the map at the start to have a flavour text: *Galicia is a small and unremarkable country populated by cathedral enthusiasts. Will Galicia regain its past glory?* This is a reference to a pre-launch [AAR](Jargon.md) by one of the devs, with the aim of turning Galicia into a theocracy.

**Galicia-Lodomeria**
  - [Galicia-Lodomeria](Galicia-Lodomeria.md) (the Polish Galicia) can gain Galician (the Iberian culture) as a primary culture, if they have a Spanish speaking monarch.

**Greater Manchester**
  - Moving [Great Britain](Great Britain.md)'s capital to Lancashire renames the country to Greater Manchester and sets the flag to one resembling the the coat of arms of the actual city of Manchester (). Manchester was a major industrial center in Lancashire during the 1800s.

**Jan Mayen**
  - [Jan Mayen](Jan Mayen.md) is a tiny island north of [Norway](Norway.md). It has featured as an easter egg country in several Paradox Interactive games.

**Kalmar Union**
  - If [Scandinavia](Scandinavia.md) is formed by [Denmark](Denmark.md), it uses the name Kalmar Union along with the presumed flag of the same (). The [Kalmar Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalmar_Union) was a medieval personal union of the Scandinavian countries led by Denmark.

**Prussia**
  - If [Prussia](Prussia.md) enacts Anarchy, the map colour changes to yellow, a reference to Victoria 2 without DLC, where Prussia was indeed yellow, a rather unpopular choice among fans

**Union of Egoists**
  - If [Germany](Germany.md) has State Atheism, and the ruler is a Nihilist, it changes name to Union of Egoists; a reference to the German philosopher [Max Stirner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Stirner). Stirner proposed that a just society must be a "union of egoists"; a voluntary and non-systematic association rather than an actual state.

**Union of the New People**
  - If [Russia](Russia.md) has State Atheism, and the ruler is a Nihilist, it changes name to Union of the New People; a reference to the Russian philosopher and author [Nikolay Chernyshevsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolay_Chernyshevsky) and his novel *What is to be done?*.

## Companies

**John Paradox and Company**
  - The Swedish [flavored company](List of flavored companies.md) is, of course, a reference to Paradox Interactive itself. John Paradox is a fan given name to the man on the Victoria 3 loading screen. It is unlike all other other companies not listed in potential companies until the player has researched Telephone and Zeppelins, put down 20 Arts Academies and Electrics Industries, and constructed the Skyscraper in Svealand. The in-game company can produce Grand Strategy Games as a prestige variant of Fine Art. The company is very ahistorical, as Paradox Interactive was founded in 1999, 63 years after the end of the game.
  - If the Charters of Commerce DLC is active, the company comes with its own "historical" executive - Fredrik Wester, the current CEO of Paradox Interactive.
**Frankenstein et Cie., Établ. Électriques d’Ingolstadt**
  - With fantastical content enabled, a country can establish this company after the Frankenstein event has fired, and the country chose to support Dr. Frankenstein.

## Technologies

**Corporate Governance**
  - The description for the technology Corporate Governance contains a parody of corporate speak: *By synergistically introducing higher degrees of streamlining, professionalization, and scientification within our existing corporate culture, we can expect to leverage substantially maximized revenue and collaboratively evolve the industry through the seamless visualization of efficient initiatives.*

## Government types and ruler titles

**Archmagos - Warhammer 40K**
  - A Theocracy with the Technocracy Distribution of power law ([contechne](Government type.md)) will be lead by an "archmagos": In Warharmmer 40K an Archmagos is a leader of a forge world.

## Events and journal entries

**To Decapitate a Serpent - Star Wars**
  - In the [event](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Event) *To Decapitate a Serpent*, one of the options read: *Our allegiance is to a republic, to democracy!*, a paraphrase of a quote by Obi-wan Kenobi.

**A Stable Supply of Power - Star Wars**
  - In the event *A Stable Supply of Power*, one of the options read: *Unlimited power!*, a quote by Emperor Palpatine

**The Proclamation of the Republic - Star Wars**
  - In the [Brazilian event](Brazilian content.md) *The Proclamation of the Republic*, the option reads: *I love the Republic.*, a quote by Emperor Palpatine

**On the Mode of Communication of Cholera - A Song of Ice and Fire**
  - In the event *On the Mode of Communication of Cholera*, one of the options reads: *John Snow knows nothing.*, a quote by a lot of people in Game of Thrones. The doctor that discovered that Cholera spread by germs was also called [John Snow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Snow).

**A Strange Superstition - Twilight**
  - In the event *A Strange Superstition*, the population of a certain state will become afraid of a 'strange curse' – clearly implied to be vampires. Normally, the event says *Reports has come out of a strange illness harrying the members of same family*. If this event happens specifically in the state of Washington (the one owned by [Oregon](Oregon.md)), the event instead says *... harrying the members of the Cullen family*.

**Retaliation in [Statename]? - Rick and Morty**
  - In the description of the event *Retaliation in [Statename]?* is: *Let's go - it will be quick, in and out, 2-month adventure*, a paraphrase of a quote from the show.

**Among Us**
  - The [expedition event](Expeditions.md) *Among Us* is a reference to the game of the [same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Among_Us)

**The Best Damn Pet Shop in Town - The Simpsons**
  - As part of *The Temperance Movement* journal entry, the *The Best Damn Pet Shop in Town* event, and its description: *Pet shop, eh? Well. I just have one question. What kind of pet shop is filled with rambunctious yahoos and hectic music at 1:00 am?*, is a reference to a scene in an episode of The Simpsons.

**She can fix him**
  - The [Montenegrin](Montenegrin content.md) event *Redecorating* contains a reference to the ["I can fix him" meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/i-can-fix-him-i-can-fix-her).

**Lusty Maid - Elder Scrolls**
  - The flavor text of the event *Lewd Literature* contains a reference to the Elder Scrolls' in-game book *The Lusty Argonian Maid*.

## Graphical details

**Easter Islands**
  - On the Easter Island there are [Moai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moai) the size of skyscrapers
**Lobbies**
  - pro-country lobby and anti-country lobby icons are a reference to the [Drakeposting](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/drakeposting) meme

## Power bloc names

Several power bloc names are references to historical empires, alliances, or similar sorts of international organization from before or around the Victorian era.

**Moral International - Disco Elysium**
  - One of the names that can be picked for an [Ideological Union](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Ideological_Union) is *Moral International*, a political entity in the game *Disco Elysium*

**Order of the Colossus**
  - References Colossal Order, developer of the Cities: Skylines and Cities in Motion game series

**Organization of Free Nations - The New Order**
  - The name *Organization of Free Nations*, which is a name of a democratic Ideological Union that has rivaled a [fascist](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Fascist) country is a reference to the Hearts of Iron mod *The New Order: Last Days of Europe*, where such an organization exists
