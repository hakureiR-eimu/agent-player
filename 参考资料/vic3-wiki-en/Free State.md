**Free State** is a special country that does not exist in 1836 and cannot appear normally. It can appear if Africa is colonized through the *Free State Question* [journal entry](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Journal_entry) in any of the strategic regions in Africa, which requires that the country in question is a Monarchy or Social Monarchy and will create the Free State as a Personal Union under the country in question. The name and flag of the Free State will vary depending of the strategic region of Africa the Free State emerged from.

Its culture and religion are always Bakongo and Animist, despite the dynamic nature of the country.

## Historical Context

The Congo Free State, also known as the Independent State of the Congo (French: État indépendant du Congo, Dutch: Onafhankelijke Congostaat) was created in 1885 as the personal property of the King Leopold II of Belgium, but it was not consider a part of or belonging to the state of Belgium. King Leopold was able to seize the region by convincing other European states during Berlin Conference that he was involved in humanitarian and philanthropic work and would not tax trade. On 29 May 1885, he announced to name his possession the Congo Free State.

The Congo Free State was infamous for the colonial atrocities committed within its territory. In 1908, following decades of Leopold's ruthless personal rule over the Congo Free State, the Belgian parliament decided to annex the country as a recognized colony, after which it became known as Belgian Congo.
