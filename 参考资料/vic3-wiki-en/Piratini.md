*"The newly created state of Piratini, boldly declares its independence against Brazil, but how long can the fledgling nation last?*"

**Piratini** is an insignificant power in South America. It borders [Argentina](Argentina.md) to the west, [Uruguay](Uruguay.md) to the south and [Brazil](Brazil.md) to the northeast. When it is a republic, Piratini is called *Riograndense Republic*.

## Formation

If it ceases to exist, Piratini can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country controlling its capital and only core state of Rio Grande do Sul. It cannot be released if Brazil has [integrated the Sulista culture](Brazilian content.md) unless its core states are owned by a country other than Brazil.

## Scripted content

If Piratini conquers Santa Catarina from Brazil, it triggers an event that spawns the [Catharinense Republic](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Santa_Catarina), with an option to puppet, annex, or form a defensive pact with them. There is also an option for the player to switch to the Catharinense Republic, which forms a defensive pact with Piratini.

Giuseppe Garibaldi offers his services to Piratini shortly after the game starts.

### Interest groups

Due to Piratini's general lack of historical IG leaders and its
Wealth Voting law, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Piratini typically begins with the Landowners, Armed Forces, and Catholic Church in government as the Conservative Party. The Rural Folk and Intelligentsia are in opposition, while the Petite Bourgeoisie, Trade Unions, and Industrialists are marginalized.

## Strategies and guides

### Opening

Riograndese Republic's initial army composition consists of three Line Infantry, four Irregular Infantry, one Cuirassier and one Hussar. This number of battalions is *already enough* to win the war against [Brazil](Brazil.md), provided Irregulars and Hussars are upgraded.

The first steps are rather generic:

- set General Antônio de Sousa Neto to *Adamant Defence*,
- immediately upgrade the outdated battalions,
- increase military wages to very high,
- increase taxation to very high,
- set the army to high supply priority,
- enact any law that pleases Armed Forces and activates their trait Patriotic Fervor,
- issue *Enlistment Efforts* decree,
- only *after* the main army is upgraded, recruit four Cuirassier as Conscripts,
- issue *Greener Grass Campaign* decree to potentially receive a bit more meat fodder,
- start either expanding the Barracks for several aditional Cuirassier, or building a Trade Center and Arms Industries.

In order to upgrade the troops and perform a sustained defence with a successful counterattack, **Riograndese Republic** primarily needs small arms. The lack of those will cause the army to suffer a rapid decline in its organisation down to zero as soon as its supplies run dry within 5-10 weeks.

Therefore, the next priority is to arrange a Goods Transfer of small arms along with various other important goods, such as sugar, liquor, tobacco, and opium. At the beginning of the game, groceries are so scarce and none wants to trade them, so it's almost impossible to get an acceptable deal without running into a debt spiral.

Unfortunately, the deal acceptances will be *50/50* gamblings because **Riograndese Republic** doesn't have much to offer; a restart may be needed if the most crucial agreements are rejected. Nevertheless, a “fair” deal bonus between countries whenever they transfer only goods (and money) may smooth up the initial acceptance penalties. Since any country has to pay for the Goods Transfer only when the goods are demanded on their market, it is convenient to arrange for any other goods which can be potentially traded to a different country.

The only one who consistently agrees on a small arms deal without huge penalties is [Russia](Russia.md). A player can offer the bare minimum of coffee and ~200 per week, and ask for ~15 small arms and every other good the Russians can share; the acceptance will be 40% but it's much better than the guns with now powder.

Next will come the trade deals with anyone potentially offering liquor, tobacco, sugar, opium, and even groceries.

[Ecuador](Ecuador.md), [Uruguay](Uruguay.md), [Venezuela](Venezuela.md), [Dai Nam](Dai Nam.md), [Prussia](Prussia.md), and literally everyone else in the world can become an excellent trade partner.

While [Bolivia](Bolivia.md) can be a long-term partner and ally against Brazil, it is still worthwhile for the player to consider formally opposing it in the Peru-Bolivia Journal Entry, as the military boost will help against Brazil

The only major problem is that [The Great Wave](The Great Wave.md) has introduced the naval blockade mechanics which is constantly utilized by [Brazil](Brazil.md) causing non-fulfillment of agreements from Riograndese Republic's side. Luckily, the Brazilian fleet occasionally moves to the node near [Grão-Pará](Grão-Pará.md), thereby erratically lifting their blockade.

Depending on the available trade deals, a player can activate respective mobilization supplies and supplements. Having at least +30% of Morale recovery will give an upper hand to the player's army over Brazilian troops. The tactics itself is straightforward: staying on *Adamant Defence* until all battalions are upgraded and the advantage reaches 30-40%; then counterattack.

The next immediate targets are [Argentina](Argentina.md) and [Paraguay](Paraguay.md) for the precious iron needed to establish own small arms industry. It is advisable to annex Paraguay outright rather than turning it into a subject, as otherwise heavy investment into its iron deposits will develop its economy faster than the Riograndense Republic.

### Consolidation

Conquering Paraguay and Argentina will secure all of the resources needed to industrialize, but will likely leave the Republic severely in debt, and lacking both the population to industrialize and the SOL to draw migration. While avoiding and destroying Construction Sectors is advisable up until Argentina has been defeated and annexed, afterward it will be necessary to rapidly build several Construction Sectors, even while in deep debt, to get the economy moving.

Cycles of bankruptcy may be unavoidable. If radicalism gets high enough that it paralyzes the economy, it may be advisable to trigger a revolt and side with the rebels to reset radicalism (with Slave Revolt/Abolitionist/Slaver movements among the most likely to radicalize in the early to mid game in the Riograndense Republic). Once the existing economy and private sector investment are strong enough to keep the budget from bleeding itself dry, the player can halt government construction and allow the treasury to recover.

### Preparing to fight Brazil

Brazil is likely to launch additional invasions, but with modest upkeep and the momentum from conquering neighbors, it should be easy to hold the line by keeping armies on defensive. Selecting war demands that do not require occupying Brazil (such as nationalizing industry and revoking claims) can help win additional concessions from the war.

Courting a great power and joining their power bloc is liable to both help the player industrialize and increase the odds of bringing them into the war as an ally.

The player is unlikely to need more than one or two wars to surpass Brazil’s GDP for the achievement.

Prioritizing the Civilizing Mission technology will unlock the powerful *Populating the Anericas* Journal Entry, which can help attract mass immigration if the player has been struggling to do so.

It cannot be released if Brazil has [integrated the Sulista culture](Brazilian content.md) unless its core states are owned by country other than Brazil.
