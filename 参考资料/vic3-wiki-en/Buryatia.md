**Buryatia** is a recognized [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in the East Siberia strategic region. It may be released by a country that controls its only state or revolt as the cultural secession country of Buryats. At the start date, its sole core state is controlled by [Russia](Russia.md).
