**Cyprus** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in Anatolia. It can be released by a country which controls its capital, and at the start of the game it is part of the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md).

As a Greek nation, Cyprus can reform [Greece](Greece.md) if it ceases to exist or form [Byzantium](Byzantium.md) if the [game rule](Game rule.md) *Formable Nations: All* is enabled.
