**Rewah** is an unrecognized power in India. It is one of the "princely states", a puppet of the [East India Company](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/East_India_Company), and thus a part of the [British Empire](Great Britain.md).

As a Bagheli country, Rewah is able to form [India](India.md).

### Interest groups

Rewah starts with the Zamindars in government. The Hindu Priesthood, Ryots, and Armed Forces are in opposition. The remaining interest groups are marginalized.
