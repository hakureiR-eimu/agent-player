**Turkestan** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in central Asia. It can be formed by any nation with a "Central Turkic" primary culture (Uzbek, Kyrgyz, Kazakh, Turkmen, Tajik, or Uighur).

## Formation

7 of the 16 core states are needed in order to form Turkestan
Turkestan can be formed by the following nations when they control at least 7 of its 16 core states and have researched Pan-nationalism.

### Core states

Turkestan has 16 core states, which comprise its primary culture's homelands. Its core states comprise the Central Asia strategic region (15 states, listed below), as well the state of Southern Turkestan in the Greater Persia strategic region.

Forming Turkestan grants claims on all unowned primary culture homelands, which includes 6 non-core states in the Greater Persia region.

## Turkestani minors

The Turkestani minors are all unrecognized powers and have similar basic stats, starting technology, and politics. Each starts with only one or more states in Central Asia or Persia. They are all Principalities and Sunni. Most often they all start with the Landowners in government with a mix of the Armed Forces, Rural Folk, and Sunni Ulema in opposition, and the remaining interest groups marginalized.

They all start with the following technology and laws:
