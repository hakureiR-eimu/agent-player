**Maghreb** is a [formable nation](Formable countries.md) in North Africa. It can be formed by any country with Maghrebi, Amazigh, or Bidan as a primary culture that has researched Pan-nationalism. Maghreb comprises the entire region of North Africa plus a few adjacent states.

## Historical context

The term 'Maghreb' derives from Arabic 'maghrib' meaning "place where the sun sets; the west". It was used initially during the Muslim conquest of the region in the 7th century. While the precise definition varied, it generally came to mean the regions west of Alexandria. The Maghreb was initially unified under the Umayyad Caliphate. However, when the Umayyads were overthrown by the Abbasids, the region splintered into several competing emirates and kingdoms.

The Maghreb was inhabited by Amazigh (Berber) peoples since antiquity, the Muslim conquest brought Arab settlers as well as Arabizing the Amazigh. In the 11th century, the Fatimid Caliphate retaliated against the Amazigh Zirids' declaration of independence by sponsoring Bedouin Arabs to invade the region. This further shifted the culture of the Maghreb towards Arabic norms and introduced nomadism as well.

In the Early Modern era, most of the Maghreb came under loose Ottoman rule, largely as tributary or protectorate states. In the 19th and 20th centuries, the Maghreb came under increasing European control, particularly France, which controlled the largest share of the region. Following World War 2, the region was decolonized and returned to local control.
