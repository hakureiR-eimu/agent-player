**Australia** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in Oceania. It can be formed by any nation with Australian primary culture. All its constituent parts start as [crown colonies](Subject.md) in the [British Empire](Great Britain.md), and it is very likely that a fully formed Australia would also be a subject under the British crown.

## Historical background

Dutch explorers first encountered in Australia in the 1600s, mapping much of its coastline and naming it New Holland. British exploration came later, mainly in the 1700s, including the voyages of James Cook. European colonization began in 1788 when the British sent a fleet of settlers and convicts. British settlement continued and by 1827, Great Britain claimed the entirety of Australia. By 1836, there were four established colonial administrations – New South Wales, Van Diemen's Land, Western Australia, and South Australia. Two more divisions were made by 1860 – Victoria and Queensland.

While the initial colonies were primarily coastal, inland colonization grew over the mid-1800s, especially as ranching expanded and gold was discovered. The later half of the 1800s also saw the Australian colonies gain a measure of self-governance, establishing constitutions and parliaments. By the end of the 1800s, most Australian colonists were locally born and a new sense of Australian identity started to form. The idea of an Australian federation uniting the separate colonies also grew, though there was also opposition for many reasons. A proposed constitution was drafted in 1891 and adopted in 1900 – after two referenda were held in 1898 and 1899. The unified Commonwealth of Australia was proclaimed on January 1, 1901, with the six former colonies becoming states.

The colonies of Fiji and New Zealand had initially considered joining the federation, but decided against doing so early in the discussions.

Australia
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
[New South Wales](New South Wales.md) is generally the easiest to form Australia with, and they are the biggest and has the biggest economy.

### Forming Australia

1. Start improving relations with all of your fellow Australian subjects
2. Start colonizing the Māori and Aboriginal tribes
3. Research Nationalism
4. Once that's done research Central Banking
5. Improve relations with your neighbors
6. Research the colonization technologies to speed up colonizing
7. Every two years enter into a federation
8. Enjoy Australia, mate
