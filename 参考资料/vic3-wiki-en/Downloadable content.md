This article is [timeless](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Category:Timeless) and should be accurate for any version of the game.

**Downloadable content (DLC)** is content built by [Paradox Development Studio](Paradox.md) (PDS) as an extension or add-on to *Victoria 3*. They are modular in nature,[1] which means that a player can choose to play with or without a given DLC by checking them on or off in the launch menu. Each DLC is normally accompanied by a free [patch](Patches.md) that gives players most of the new content as not to hinder the playability of the game. As such, most DLCs are seen as optional content for players who wish to have a better gaming experience in exchange for some monetary support.

Multiplayer games also benefit from this compatibility, as if the host has gameplay DLC (expansions and flavor packs) that the player does not, the game acts as if the player has it.
