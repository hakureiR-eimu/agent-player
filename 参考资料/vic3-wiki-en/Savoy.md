**Savoy** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in the region Occitania. It can be released at the start of the game by [Sardinia-Piedmont](Sardinia-Piedmont.md), or it can be created through a Franco-Provençal secession.

## Historical background

Savoy is a cultural and historical region in western Europe. Savoy was a core part of [Sardinia-Piedmont](Sardinia-Piedmont.md) as the original homeland of the ruling House of Savoy.

For both cultural and strategic reasons, [France](France.md) had annexed Savoy during the Napoleonic wars. After Napoleon's defeat, the Congress of Vienna returned Savoy to Sardinia-Piedmont's control. France attempted unsuccessfully to annex Savoy again in 1848. Finally, in 1860, Sardinia-Piedmont ceded Savoy, along with Nice, to the second French empire, as compensation for French support for the unification of [Italy](Italy.md).

## Release

Savoy can be released by any country that controls its capital, the state region Savoy.

Savoy has two core states: Savoy and Rhône – both in the Southern Europe region.

A player playing Savoy should create strong ties with [France](France.md) or find strong allies, as France will potentially claim its only state due to the French Natural Borders [journal entry](French content.md). All potential Savoyard states are also Homelands of primary cultures of strong neighbors (namely Italy and France).

France can potentially take the state of Savoy with the decision [Treaty of Turin](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Treaty_of_Turin). This can be prevented however by making Savoy the [capital](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Capital).

## Trivia

Savoy has two dynamic names: *Lyon* if the capital is Rhône and *Romandy* if the capital is West Switzerland.

Savoy's flag has the same appearance as [Malta](Malta.md)'s in game.
