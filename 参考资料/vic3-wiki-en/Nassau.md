**Nassau** is an insignificant power in North Germany. It borders [Waldeck](Waldeck.md), [Hesse](Hesse.md) and [Prussia](Prussia.md) at game start.

As a North German nation, it is able to form the [North German Federation](North German Federation.md) and [Germany](Germany.md). Alternatively, it can form [Confederation of the Rhine](Confederation of the Rhine.md).

### Interest groups

Due to Nassau's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Nassau begins with the Kleinbürger and the Landowners in government. The Industrialists, Landvolk, Intelligentsia, Armed Forces and the Evangelical Church are in opposition. The Trade unions are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Nassau starts in the *Zollverein* power bloc, lead by [Prussia](Prussia.md).

Nassau has the Professional Army law.

Nassau begins with 3 Battalions and can call upon 1 Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
