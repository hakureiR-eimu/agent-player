**Anahuac** is a [releasable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Releasable_country) in the Mexico region. It can be released by any country controlling its capital. All its core states are initially owned by [Mexico](Mexico.md).

## Core states

Anahuac uses the homelands of its primary cultures as its core states.
