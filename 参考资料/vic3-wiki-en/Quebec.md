*"Once the colony of New France, Lower Canada has fallen under British rule. Tensions between the predominantly French population and the colonial government threaten to escalate in the near future, with little chance of peaceful resolution. Will Quebec win its freedom?"*

**Quebec** – called *Lower Canada* at the start of the game – is an insignificant power in North America. It starts the game as a colony of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) and has the potential to form [Canada](Canada.md).

## Formation

If Quebec ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by a country controlling its capital state of Quebec. It uses the Franco-Canadian homelands as its core states, which in addition to the state of Quebec, includes the Maritimes.

### Interest groups

Quebec generally begins with the Landowners, the Catholic Church, and the Armed Forces in government as the Conservative Party. In opposition, the Industrialists may form the Free Trade party or join the Intelligentsia's Liberal Party, while the Petite Bourgeoisie and Rural Folk are typically unaffiliated at game start. The Trade Unions are the only marginalized interest group.

### Political Movements

Quebec begins with a protesting Radical movement, due to the *Reformer Movement Fury Reformer Movement Fury Movement modifier +5% Political Movement Pop Attraction +20% Political Movement Activism* modifier, which lasts for 20 years. [Upper Canada](Ontario.md) starts in the same situation.

### Diplomacy

Quebec is a colony of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), thus it is a member of the *British Empire* power bloc. Quebec neighbors [Upper Canada](Ontario.md) to the west, Great Britain and the [Hudson’s Bay Company](Hudson’s Bay Company.md) to the north, the [United States](America.md) and [New Brunswick](New Brunswick.md) to the south, and its overlord [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) to the north in Newfoundland.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Quebec begins with both Shipyards and Military Shipyards, as well as a couple agricultural buildings, a Trade Center, and even a University.

**Resources**

Quebec starts with level 1 Logging Camps and Iron Mines, which allows for early industrialization. Coal and lead may be found in nearby [New Brunswick](New Brunswick.md), which makes it a good target for the first Confederation. Being part of the [British](Great Britain.md) market typically accounts for any needs Quebec – and Canada – may have in other regards.

## Military

Quebec has the Peasant Levies and Merchant Navy laws.

Quebec begins with 5 regular Battalions and can recruit 9 Conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | General | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st Lower Canadian Army | 5 Line Infantry | — | Canada |

Quebec has no flotillas at the start of the game.
