**Isaaq** is an unrecognized power located in the Ethiopia [strategic region](Strategic region.md) of Africa at the Horn of Africa. It borders it [Aussa](Aussa.md), [Harar](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Harar), and [Warsangali](Warsangali.md).

### Interest groups

Due to Isaaq's general lack of historical IG leaders, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Isaaq starts with the Landowners in government. The Sunni Ulema, Rural Folk, and the Armed Forces are in opposition, while the Trade Unions, Petite Bourgeoisie, Intelligentsia, and Industrialists are marginalized.

### Diplomacy

Isaaq starts with no diplomatic relations of note and its only active interest is in the Ethiopia region itself.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.10. |

Isaaq has the Peasant Levies law.

Isaaq starts with 2 Battalions and can raise up to 1 Conscript. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions comprise a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st Isaaqi Army | 2 Irregular Infantry | Faarax Guuleed | Ethiopia |

Isaaq starts with no flotillas.
