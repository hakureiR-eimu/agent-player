**Federation of the Andes** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in South America. It can be formed by any North Andean or South Andean country that has the active journal entry *Bolívar's Ambition*.

It is one of the few Hegemony-tier countries, the others being [Central Europe](Central Europe.md) and [India](India.md).

## Historical background

During the Spanish American wars of independence, the idea of a unified Spanish American nation was proposed by Francisco de Miranda and championed by Simón Bolívar. While this idea was never accomplished, there were several united regions at various points, including [Gran Colombia](Gran Colombia.md), [Peru-Bolivia](Peru-Bolivia.md), and [Rio de la Plata](Rio de la Plata.md), though none proved stable enough to last.

## Formation

80% of the 35 core states are needed to form Federation of the Andes
The following countries can form Federation of the Andes when they control 80% of its core states and have the active journal entry *Bolívar's Ambition*.

Federation of the Andes is a [major unification](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Major_unification) which means a country does not need to directly control its core states but can instead gain the backing of other North Andean and South Andean countries. Each of the following except Gran Colombia and Peru-Bolivia will change its primary culture from the regional North or South Andean to a national culture eventually. This removes that country from the unification

### Core states

Federation of the Andes' core states constitute the Gran Colombia, Andes, and La Plata strategic regions. There are 35 states within those regions; control of 28 states is needed to form the Federation of the Andes.

Federation of the Andes' defined capital state, Panama, is never a core state; however, this has no effect on forming the country.

## Strategy and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.10. |
|---|---|

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Federation of the Andes
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
As noted above, unlike other major unifications, countries with Andean or Platinean primary cultures may replace their regional culture with a local national culture after researching Nationalism. This reduces the number of countries who could potentially support you in a unification play.

Forming [Gran Colombia](Gran Colombia.md), [Peru-Bolivia](Peru-Bolivia.md), or [Rio de la Plata](Rio de la Plata.md) prevents a country from changing to a national culture, which can thus preserve at least some North Andean or South Andean homelands. Gran Colombia is the easiest to form of the three, especially as [New Granada](Colombia.md)

Although [Brazil](Brazil.md) is unrelated to the formation of the Federation, it is a major threat to all potential candidates. Strong candidates (e.g. Peru-Bolivia) should weaken Brazil by liberating nations from it and then try to annex the liberated nations.
