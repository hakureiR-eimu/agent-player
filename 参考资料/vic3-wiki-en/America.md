*"The United States of America has asserted the Americas' right to independence from European powers. However, the nation is also in the process of forcibly removing thousands of Native Americans from their homelands, and an intense debate rages between the states on the subject of slavery. What will the future for the United States look like?"*

**America**, also known as *United States of America*, is a Great Power in central-eastern North America, extending from the Atlantic coast west into the Great Plains. It borders [Canada](Canada.md) to the north (which in 1836 is still divided into multiple smaller colonies) and [Texas](Texas.md) and [Mexico](Mexico.md) to the south.

America starts with a relatively large geographic territory of 26 incorporated and 5 unincorporated states plus 8 in-progress colonies, relatively liberal laws, established early industry and the second highest literacy among the Great Powers, but a very small standing military of only 12 Battalions and 15 Flotillas.

The United States is one of four recommended countries for the *Economic Dominance* [objective](Objective.md), along with [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md) and [Brazil](Brazil.md).

## Historical Background

America's territory as of January 1, 1836. Texas is seceding from Mexico and Oregon (Columbia District) owns Oregon, Washington and Idaho
The United States of America is a relatively young country, barely turning 60 years old in 1836. It was founded when thirteen North American colonies of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) – dissatisfied with British rule – issued the Declaration of Independence on July 4, 1776, and its existence was cemented by the former colonies' victory over Great Britain in the American Revolutionary War. In the decades that followed, the USA doubled in size by purchasing [France](France.md)'s North American territory of Louisiana and earned formal recognition from its former overlord Great Britain after a second war in 1812.

During the Victorian period, the United States continued expanding westward to achieve "Manifest Destiny", the idea that the country was destined to stretch all across North America from the Atlantic to the Pacific Ocean. To achieve this, the United States negotiated for the Oregon Territory from Great Britain, displaced countless Native Americans from their lands, and entered into war with [Mexico](Mexico.md). In addition, the debate over the issue of slavery continued to plague the nation ever since its founding, with northern states generally avoiding practicing slavery while plantations in southern states relied on slave labor. Furthermore as the nation expanded west arguments broke out over whether the new territories should allow slavery, as each new state threatened to upset the balance of power between the two sides. The issue had been juggled through compromises such as the Missouri Compromise and the Fugitive Slave Act, but with the election of Abraham Lincoln as President of the United States the issue finally reached a breaking point and tore the country in two; South Carolina was the first state to secede on December 20, 1860 and was followed by ten more southern states, who together formed the [Confederate States of America](Confederate States of America.md). The American Civil War began in earnest on April 12, 1861 when Confederate forces attacked Fort Sumter. The war lasted over four years and remains the bloodiest conflict in United States history to this day. The war ended in a decisive Union victory with the surrender of the Confederate states and their re-integration into the United States. In 1865 shortly after the war's end the United States ratified the 13th Amendment to its Constitution which abolished slavery nationwide.

The decades following the American Civil War saw the United States continue to grow in economic and diplomatic power. The United States gained overseas territories, including [Hawaii](Hawaii.md) and the [Philippines](Philippines.md) as well as the purchase of Alaska from [Russia](Russia.md) and land in Panama to construct the Panama Canal. The United States gained a reputation as a "land of opportunity" and as such attracted many immigrants. By the beginning of the Second World War in Europe with the German invasion of Poland, the United States' national GDP exceeded the total GDPs of the combined belligerents of either side, including all the dominions and colonies in their respective empires.

## Formation

America's core territory at its greatest extent, after achieving Manifest Destiny and acquiring the Oregon Territory.
75% of the 33 to 49 core states are needed in order to form America; dashed lines represent potential core states.
America already exists at the start of the game. If it ceases to exist, it can be [formed](Formable countries.md) by a country with Yankee or Dixie as a primary culture. The forming country and its Puppets and Dominions must fully control at least 75% of American core state regions in order to form America; this percentage can increase from 25 state regions at game start up to 37 if all potential homelands are gained. Alternatively, America can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by any country which controls District of Columbia and nine other core states.

## Scripted content

Main article:
American content
- **The Slavery Debate, The American Civil War and Reconstruction:** These entries handle the lead up to and aftermath of the American Civil War. The Civil War can be avoided if Slavery Banned is passed and kept active for 10 years total without a revolution. Otherwise, if the Southern Planters start a revolution, then the American Civil War will begin. If the Union emerges victorious, the process of Reconstruction will begin, which can potentially add Afro-American as a primary culture or remove Dixie as a primary culture of America.
- **The Indian Removal:** While this entry is active, events will periodically happen that will forcibly move Cherokee pops from incorporated states to the [Indian Territory](Indian Territory.md). The player is typically given a choice of whether to render financial aid to reduce casualties.
- **Texan Statehood:** America can peacefully annex [Texas](Texas.md) if it is under AI control, or a human player of Texas may choose to become a Dominion of America. Texas must first win its initial war against [Mexico](Mexico.md), and then relations between Texas and America must be raised to at least Cordial.
- **Manifest Destiny:** This decision becomes available after researching Nationalism and gives America claims on multiple state regions held by [Mexico](Mexico.md). It also starts two new journal entries: Mexican Cession and Frontier Wars, which task America with fully controlling the western state regions and upon completion will add Yankee or Dixie homelands to these regions. Completing Frontier Wars will also automatically annex the [Indian Territory](Indian Territory.md).
- **The Oregon Border Dispute:** [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) can peacefully cede the states of Washington, Oregon and Idaho to America after the Oregon Trail has been mapped, America has fully colonized Montana and Wyoming and relations between Great Britain and America are at least Cordial. Washington, Oregon and Idaho will become Yankee homelands after America acquires the states.
- **The First Transcontinental Railway:** Building a Railway from California to Nebraska can temporarily increase migration attraction in either the Great Plains or Pacific Coast.
- **Negotiate the Alaska Purchase:** If [Russia](Russia.md) is in debt, America can offer to purchase the state of Alaska from it.

## Politics

America's initial government is a Republic, based on the Presidential Republic and Census Suffrage laws. The initial ruler is President Andrew Jackson who is also the leader of the Yeoman Farmers. Elections occur every four years starting from December 7, 1836. Presidential Republic and Census Suffrage results in a Presidential Elective transfer of power where the leader of the interest group with the most clout after each election becomes Ruler. Under Census Suffrage, pops of any profession can vote provided they have at least 15 wealth and their votes provide 30 political strength modified by the pops' literacy. America begins with the [amendment](Amendments.md) *Tradition of Free Elections* (), which increases legitimacy and prevents electoral fraud.

The homelands of America's two primary cultures, Yankee and Dixie, at their greatest extent after achieving Manifest Destiny and acquiring the Oregon Territory. Note that Missouri, Kentucky and Tennessee are homelands of both cultures. Starting from
Patch 10.3
, Delaware, District of Columbia, Maryland, and West Virginia are also homelands of both cultures.
America's primary cultures are Yankee and Dixie. Both primary cultures have the North American Settler and Anglophone traits. America's starting Citizenship law is Racial Segregation. America's state religion is Protestant and its initial Church and State law is Total Separation. Although America accepts all religions equally, cultures that do not have a European heritage will suffer very low acceptance in America.

America's core territory is home to numerous cultures of Indigenous American heritage who suffer Violent Hostility under Racial Segregation. The Cherokee in particular are in the process of being deported from American states to the [Indian Territory](Indian Territory.md) as part of *The Indian Removal*.

America starts with Legacy Slavery though only the southern Dixie states actually practice slavery. The slaves in America are almost exclusively Afro-Americans which have the African Diaspora and Anglophone traits; their shared language only elevates them to Cultural Erasure under Racial Segregation. The fate of slavery and Afro-Americans is a major point of contention in America during this period. America starts with opposing Abolitionist and Pro-Slavery movements; any attempt to abolish or expand slavery will cause the opposing movement to become revolutionary and risk starting the American Civil War. Unlike most ideological movements, including similar Abolitionist and Pro-Slavery movements in other countries, these movements will trigger cultural secessions rather than revolutions; the Pro-Slavery movement will form the Dixie [Confederate States of America](Confederate States of America.md) while the Abolitionist Movement will form the Yankee [Free States of America](Free States of America.md). Winning the historical American Civil War against the Confederate States will prompt a period of Reconstruction that can significantly change America's cultural identity; it presents an opportunity to elevate Afro-Americans to primary culture while also risking demoting Dixie from primary culture.

Jacksonian Democrat is a leader ideology unique to America and countries formed by civil war in America. This is the ideology of President Andrew Jackson. Other American characters can be generated with this ideology as long as Andrew Jackson is alive. This ideology notably supports a move to Universal Suffrage while maintaining the status quo of Presidential Republic, Elected Bureaucrats, Racial Segregation and most importantly Legacy Slavery.

America starts with a unique modifier called *The Missouri Compromise.* This modifier increases the political strength of the Southern Planters by **+25%** and the political strength of votes of aristocrats by **+50** and those of farmers by **+10**. These effects decay over 50 years, and the modifier is also removed when *The Slavery Debate* is resolved or a non-voting law is enacted. In addition, America starts with the *Fugitive Slave Act* (), an [amendment](Amendments.md) to Legacy Slavery that increases Southern Planters political strength by *+10%* and approval by **+1** while also increasing pop attraction to Abolitionist movements by **+15%** in free states.

Yankee pops start with an obsession for Coffee, Dixie are obsessed with Tobacco and Afro-Americans are obsessed with Liquor. Both Yankee and Dixie pops also start obsessed with Small Arms due to America's law [amendment](Amendments.md) *Second Amendment of the Constitution* (), which also increases training rate by **+10%** and colonial growth speed by **+5%** but also slightly increases mortality by **+1%** in unincorporated states and **+0.5%** to **+1.5%** in incorporated states with Turmoil.

Homesteading has significant impacts on America's interest groups. Under this law farmers enjoy **+25%** political strength and can gain increased wealth from dividends of farms and ranches through the Homesteading ownership production method. Farmers are also significantly more attracted to the Southern Planters and slightly more attracted to the Petite Bourgeoisie though the majority still align with the Yeoman Farmers. The increased wealth from dividends also helps ensure that farmers meet the 15 wealth threshold needed to be eligible to vote under Census Suffrage.

America starts with the Yeoman Farmers, Southern Planters, and Armed Forces in government as the Democratic Party. The Industrialists, Evangelicals, Intelligentsia, and Petite Bourgeoisie are in opposition as the Whig Party. The Trade Unions are marginalized.

America's Landowners are the Southern Planters. Their **Loyal** trait is *Patrician Philanthropy* instead of the usual *Noblesse Oblige*; this trait increases prestige by **+5%** and loyalists from standard of living increases by **+15%**. Between *The Missouri Compromise*, Legacy Slavery and level 2 Local Police Force they start with a total **+70%** political strength in addition to increased strength of votes of aristocrats and farmers. The Southern Planters are naturally aligned with the Democratic Party. The Southern Planters have the Elitist ideology instead of the usual Paternalistic which significantly changes their stances on several laws. They prefer Presidential Republic or Parliamentary Republic instead of Monarchy or Theocracy and they have no stances on Economic System or Trade Policy. They also have the Pro-Slavery ideology because America does not have Slavery Banned. They are the primary advocates for slavery in *The Slavery Debate.*

America's Rural Folk are the Yeoman Farmers. They have the unique Jeffersonian ideology instead of the usual Agrarian which adds stances on Distribution of Power laws; notably, they endorse a move to Universal Suffrage and oppose any move to reduce or eliminate the voting franchise. They also support Homesteading even more strongly than normal and oppose Collectivized Agriculture and Laissez-Faire more strongly than normal. The Yeoman Farmers are initially aligned with the Democratic Party largely because their leader is President Andrew Jackson. Andrew Jackson's Jacksonian Democrat ideology causes the Yeoman Farmers to support Legacy Slavery and oppose any effort to either ban or expand slavery, thus attempting to change the slavery law while Andrew Jackson is alive risks the Yeoman Farmers joining the secessionists in the American Civil War.

The Intelligentsia are primary advocates for abolishing slavery in *The Slavery Debate*. They are initially aligned with the Whig Party.

America's Devout are the Evangelicals. They are advocates of the Protestant religion and as such their **Loyal** trait is *Work Ethic* which increases throughput bonus from companies by **+5%**. America unusually starts the game with Total Separation which unless changed cripples this IG in the long run as non-Protestant pops do not convert.

The Armed Forces start with **−25%** political strength from National Militia and the same law greatly restricts the number of servicemen and officers that America can support, particularly in peacetime.

America has unique attraction modifiers for its political parties. American politics favor a two-party system largely between the Democratic Party and the Whig or Republican Party. As such several fringe parties that favor a specific interest group or ideology are significantly harder to form, with the notable exception of socialist parties. Notably this aspect of the two-party system only applies while America's Governance Principles law is Presidential Republic. The two-party system is even more heavily enforced while *The Slavery Debate* is active where Dixie and pro-slavery interest groups heavily favor the Democratic Party while Yankees and abolitionists favor the Whigs.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

America starts with relatively developed industry, including Chemical Plants and Steel Mills. Tooling Workshops are running Steel Tools, and most other industries are running second-tier base production methods. America is able to expand infrastructure as needed thanks to starting with the Railways technology.

Shipyards constructed in New York, New Jersey, and Massachusetts enjoy 10% additional throughput thanks to Natural Harbors; this same trait increases the max level of Ports by 3 and Naval Bases by 10 in these states.

Government Administrations built in District of Columbia enjoy 20% additional throughput thanks to the presence of the White House.

America's initial Economic System is Interventionism. Investment Pool funds may be used to pay for construction of any productive building whether agricultural, extraction or manufacturing as well as infrastructure. The player is allowed to subsidize any and all types of productive buildings and infrastructure. America starts with 40 construction capacity.

America's initial Trade Policy is Protectionism which allows roughly even control of tariffs for both imports and exports. America starts with export routes for fabric, ammunition, and explosives, and import routes for furniture, clothes, sugar, sulfur, coffee, dye, and luxury clothes.

America's initial Land Reform law is Homesteading. All non-plantation farms and ranches including subsistence buildings use the Homesteading ownership production method which means that farmers possess the bulk of the ownership shares of these buildings.

## AI Behavior

America has unique AI behaviors. It is programmed to have significantly increased confidence and aggression towards owner(s) of California, Nevada, Utah, Colorado, Arizona, New Mexico, Texas, Kansas and Oklahoma to facilitate its pursuit of Manifest Destiny. It is also very heavily weighted to adopt a "conquer" strategic desire towards the owner(s) of Washington, Oregon and Idaho, as well as Alaska, if offers for peaceful transfer of these states are rejected. In addition a hidden value for the Monroe Doctrine causes it to prioritize declaring interests in the western hemisphere.

## Trivia

[Texas](Texas.md) has an equivalent journal entry for Texan Statehood called Texan Independence, and in fact the latter journal entry is what actually governs the events whereas the American journal entry is purely informational.

The Monroe Doctrine was a stance against further expansion of European colonialism in the western hemisphere, which the prioritization of declared interests would facilitate in-game by allowing America to intervene in diplomatic plays in the region.

The Missouri Compromise was a solution to the proposed admittance of the territory of Missouri as a slave state in 1820, which by itself would have given slave states a majority in the US Senate. The compromise was to admit Missouri as a slave state while simultaneously admitting Maine as a free state to keep the two sides equal, and furthermore outlawing slavery above the 36º 30' latitude line in the remainder of the Louisiana Territory, though this latter condition would be repealed by the Kansas-Nebraska Act of 1854.

The District of Columbia does not contribute a star to America's default flag. In real life, the District of Columbia is not an actual state nor part of any other state and is meant to serve strictly as the meeting place for the United States federal government.

West Virginia also does not contribute a star to the flag; it was not a separate state until the American Civil War when it separated from Virginia.

The Emancipation Proclamation event allows the player to immediately enact Slavery Banned, but this is not a historically accurate effect. The proclamation only ordered the emancipation of slaves in rebel states. Slave states that sided with the Union, commonly referred to as "border states", were exempt from the order. The passage of Slavery Banned is more akin to the ratification of the 13th amendment to the United States Constitution which accomplished the nationwide abolition of slavery in real life.

The [American Buildings Pack](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/DLC) adds the US Capitol building to the map in the District of Columbia and the Central Park building to New York. They are purely decoration and serve no gameplay purpose.

There is a decision available to all Great Powers that can lead to the annexation of [Hawaii](Hawaii.md). In real life, the United States annexed the Hawaiian Islands on July 7, 1898 and the territory would become a full state on August 21, 1959.
