*"A newly reunited Vietnam emerges from centuries of division and strife. Will Dai Nam consolidate itself and build a great nation, or is it destined to be divided once more?"*

**Dai Nam** is an unrecognized regional power in Indochina. It borders [China](China.md) to the north and [Luang Prabang](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Luang_Prabang), [Siam](Siam.md), [Champassak](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Champassak) and [Cambodia](Cambodia.md) to the south and west.

Dai Nam is one of the recommended countries and the only unrecognized country for the *Learn the Game* [objective](Objective.md) along with [Sweden](Sweden.md), [New Granada](Colombia.md) and [Belgium](Belgium.md).

## Formation

If Dai Nam ceases to exist, it can be released by any country that controls its capital, Tonkin. Its core states are the Vietnamese homelands, which are also its starting states.

### Interest groups

Dai Nam begins with the Landowners in government. The Armed Forces, Rural Folk, Intelligentsia, and Petite Bourgeoisie are opposition; the Confucian Scholars may be in opposition or marginalized, and the Trade Unions, and Industrialists are marginalized.

The Confucian Scholars have the Confucian ideology instead of the default Moralist and Pious ideologies. The Petite Bourgeoisie have the Mercantile ideology in addition to the rest.

### Diplomacy

Dai Nam rules over [Cambodia](Cambodia.md) who is its tributary. Dai Nam and [Siam](Siam.md) have a mutual rivalry and a truce until 1839.

## Military

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.10. |
|---|---|

Dai Nam has the Peasant Levies law.

Dai Nam begins with 55 regular battalions and can call on 60 conscripts. All Barracks use No Organized Training. The battalions compose a single army.

| Army name | Battalions | Generals | HQ region |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st Vietnamese Army | 55 Irregular Infantry | — | Indochina |

Dai Nam has no flotillas at the start of the game.
