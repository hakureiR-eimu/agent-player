**Natalia** is a special country in Southern Africa, that can appear through a short event chain. Historically, it existed from 1838 to 1843, when it was annexed into the British Empire.

## Scripted content

Main article:
Southern Africa content
Natalia can spawn through an [event](Southern Africa content.md) that can happen if any Boer state (either [Potchefstroom](Transvaal.md) or [Winburg](Oranje.md)) borders [Zulu](Zulu.md). It will inherit [Winburg](Oranje.md)'s technologies and laws, if it exists. As a fallback, it will inherit [Potchefstroom](Transvaal.md)'s, ![Flag GBR uk.png](/images/thumb/6/6e/Flag_GBR_uk.png/24px-Flag_GBR_uk.png) [Cape Colony](South Africa.md)'s, or any neighboring centralized country's, in that order

Once it spawns, it will share a journal entry with nearby countries related to the struggle for dominance over the *South Africa* [geographic region](Geographic region.md). Specifically, Natalia must ensure diplomatic recognition and coastal access to gain dominance over the other minor states in the region. Through this journal entry, Natalia may seek confederation with other Boer states, such as [Potchefstroom](Transvaal.md) and [Winburg](Oranje.md).
