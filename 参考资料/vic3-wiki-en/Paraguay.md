**Paraguay** is an insignificant power in South America.

As a Platinean nation, Paraguay is able to form [Rio de la Plata](Rio de la Plata.md). However, like other nations in South America, the spread of Nationalism may prevent any pan-national aspirations, as a distinct Paraguayan identity begins to emerge.

## Historical background

Paraguay begins as one of the most eccentric nations in the world. Since 1814, it has been led by José Gaspar Rodriguez de Francia, an ideological devotee of Rousseau, who seeks to spread the ideals of the Enlightenment through dictatorship. The country accepts the indigenous Guarani people and opposes the Catholic Church, but is isolationist, autocratic, and makes use of a secret police. However, Francia is now in his seventies, and his death may pave the way for the collapse of his ideological project.

## Formation

If Paraguay ceases to exist, it can be [released](Releasable countries.md) by a country controlling its capital state of Bajo Paraguay. Its core states include Bajo Paraguay and Alto Paraguay, the latter of which is not part of Paraguay at game start.

## Politics

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.12. |

If Colossus of the South is enabled, Paraguay is a Perpetual Dictatorship, a unique type of government where the dictator has a designated Heir, the *Secretary-Amanuensis*; otherwise, it starts as a regular Presidential Dictatorship.

The country is initially led by José Gaspar Rodríguez de Francia of the Intelligentsia. Francia has the character modifier *Karai Guasu Karai Guasu Character modifier +60 Popularity +20 Legitimacy from including the Head of State In Government*. Uniquely and reflecting of Francia's ideology, Paraguay is one of only two countries to start with Secret Police enacted,[1] and the only recognized country to begin with Isolationism.

Paraguay starts with the *Age of Caudillos Age of Caudillos Country modifier +250% Political Strength of Officers +50% Armed Forces Political Strength +10% Armed Forces Pop Attraction* modifier, increasing the political strength of the Armed Forces greatly.

### Interest groups

Paraguay begins with the Intelligentsia in government. The Landowners, Armed Forces, Rural Folk, Industrialists, and Petite Bourgeoisie are in opposition, while the Catholic Church and Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Armed Forces have the Caudillismo ideology in addition to their standard ideologies. The Landowners have the Elitist ideology instead of the default Paternalistic. The Landowners also have the Pro-Slavery ideology due to the starting Legacy Slavery. The Petite Bourgeoisie have the Mercantile ideology due to the starting Isolationism.

If Colossus of the South is enabled, Paraguay also has the modifier *Pyraguës Pyraguës Country modifier +10% Authority −10% Enactment Success Chance −80% Devout Political Strength +200% Intelligentsia Political Strength −20% Radicals from Political Movements*, increasing the strength of the Intelligentsia while reducing the strength of the Catholic Church.

### Diplomacy

Paraguay borders the decentralized [Guarani](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Guarani_(GNI)) to the west, [Argentina](Argentina.md) to the southwest and [Brazil](Brazil.md) to the northeast. It is the only (independent) country in South America to start off without coastal access.

Paraguay also starts with the modifier *Independence Not Recognized by Spain Independence Not Recognized by Spain Country modifier −10% Influence*, until [Spain](Spain.md) recognizes its independence, or Paraguay becomes strong enough to disregard Spain.

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.12.4. |
|---|---|

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Paraguay
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
Paraguay starts with Isolationism, the only recognized country to do so. This makes trade with the world market impossible, limiting industrialization potential.

The player can opt to gamble and use the [Ragamuffin War](Brazilian content.md) to their advantage, declaring war on [Brazil](Brazil.md) for the state of Paraná. Brazil is much stronger than Paraguay but is distracted with its civil war, so the player can win this with a little bit of luck . The Enlistment Efforts decree will give you a few more conscripts to work with. You should also immediately begin incorporating Paraguayan Mato Grosso and improving [relations](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Relations) with [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) and [the US](America.md). Paraná gives you sea access but more importantly Coal Mines. It's also a Guarani homeland so the player should incorporate it before de Francia dies if possible.

Alternatively, the player can opt to improve relations with Brazil and trade for transit rights. Yet another route to the sea is available by marching south through Corrientes (in [Argentina](Argentina.md)) and ( [Uruguay](Uruguay.md)). Both countries are much weaker than Brazil, and the Argentine Confederation is likely to get pulled into wars that will make it easy to pick off states, first with Peru-Bolivia and later with the Mapuche and Tehuelche decentralized powers.

The first major [Journal Entry](South America content.md), following de Francia's death, will require in total 2 construction sectors, 3 iron mines, 2 logging camps, 2 arms industries, and 20 barracks (note that you won't be able to build beyond 5 barracks before getting off National Militia) in Bajo Paraguay so you can start building these as is convenient. Meanwhile, research your way to Nationalism to unlock the [Rio de la Plata Journal Entry](Rio de la Plata.md). You won't need to form [Rio de la Plata](Rio de la Plata.md), but you can use the extra maneuvers and claims to take land from [Argentina](Argentina.md). At the very least you should take enough states to cut them off from decentralized Guarani, you could also feasibly full annex Argentina in one war if you wanted to, though it's better to leave them as a rump state in Buenos Aires and/or Mendoza, otherwise their starting claims will disappear and [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) will start colonizing the region, which will be a major obstacle to forming Rio de la Plata.

If the player choose to continue Francia's legacy upon his death, the country will retain Guarani as primary culture at the expense of losing Paraguay's Journal Entry, and the culture will be inherited upon forming [Rio de la Plata](Rio de la Plata.md). However, Rio de la Plata will not inherit Guarani if the player managed to form it as Francia despite Guarani being a primary culture. The Guarani primary culture will also be lost if the player goes on to form [Federation of the Andes](Federation of the Andes.md) after forming Rio de la Plata.
