**Laos** is a small [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in East Asia. It may be formed by a Lao country below Kingdom tier that fully controls the state of Laos.

## Historical background

Prior to the 1700s, Laos was unified in the kingdom of Lan Xang, a pre-eminent power in the region. However, in the early 1700s, it was divided through a succession dispute into three kingdoms, from north to south: Luang Prabang, Vientiane, and Champassak. These successor kingdoms soon came under the influence of Siam and Burma, finally ending up as vassals of Siam near the end of the 1700s.

In 1826, the king of Vientiane, Anouvong, rebelled against Siam; Champassak was ruled by his son and joined the rebellion while Luang Prabang remained neutral. Anouvong was soon defeated, and Siam annexed Vientiane in response.

In the late 1800s, Luang Prabang sought help from the French against Chinese outlaws who sacked its capital. This resulted in France forcing Siam to cede all Laotian territory, which was then incorporated into French Indochina. Laos remained a French colony until World War II when it was occupied by Imperial Japan. After the war, Laos returned to French control, though with greater autonomy, and in 1953 gained full independence.

## Lao minors

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.10. |

The Lao minors are both unrecognized powers and have similar basic stats, starting technology, and politics. Each starts with a split state in Laos and are subjects of [Siam](Siam.md). They are both Principalities and Theravada. They generally start with the Landowners in government with a mix of the Armed Forces, Theravada Monks, Intelligentsia, and Rural Folk in opposition, and the remaining interest groups marginalized.

The Theravada Monks have the Buddhist Moralist ideology instead of the default Moralist.
