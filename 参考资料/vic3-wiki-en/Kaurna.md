**Kaurna** is an unrecognized power in Australia which starts as protectorate of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md).

Kaurna is the only centralized Aboriginal nation.

## Technology

Kaurna starts with almost no technologies.

| Production technology | Military technology | Society technology |
|---|---|---|
| Enclosure | Standing Army | Urbanization Democracy |

## Politics

At the start of the game, Kaurna is a Parliamentary Republic, one of just three countries to start as one (along with [Circassia](Circassia.md) and [Switzerland](Switzerland.md)).

### Interest groups

Due to Kaurna' complete lack of historical IG leaders and
Census Suffrage law, the starting interest groups in
government can vary.
Kaurna typically starts with Landowners, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Devout in government as the Conservative Party, while the Rural Folk make up the Agrarian Party, the Armed Forces the Patriotic Party, and the Industrialists are unaffiliated in opposition. The Intelligentsia and Trade Unions are marginalized.

### Historical characters

Kaurna starts with no historical characters

### Diplomacy

Kaurna is a Protectorate of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) and thus part of the *British Empire* power bloc.

## Military

Kaurna has the Peasant Levies law.

Kaurna starts with no Battalion and can call on no Conscripts.

Kaurna starts with no flotillas.
