*"Under Muhammad Ali, Egypt has become independent from the Ottoman Empire in all but the most technical respect. Recent gains in Syria however are sure to incite a response from the Sublime Porte. Can Egypt survive the coming storm?"*

**Egypt** is an unrecognized regional power in North Africa and the Middle East. Egypt controls [Hedjaz](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Hedjaz) as a tributary state, and is itself a protectorate of the [Ottoman Empire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey) with whom it has a mutual truce until 1840. The conflict with the Ottoman Empire is the foremost challenge for Egypt, but if it succeeds, Egypt is poised to become the predominant power in the region.

Egypt can form the nation of [Arabia](Arabia.md) after Pan-nationalism has been researched.

Egypt is one of four recommended countries for the *Hegemon* [objective](Objective.md), the others being [Prussia](Prussia.md), [Spain](Spain.md), and [Japan](Japan.md).

## Historical background

Egypt was an autonomous eyalet of the Ottoman Empire since the Mamluks were defeated in 1517. During the Napoleonic wars, [France](France.md) occupied Egypt from 1798 to 1801. Following the French withdrawal, there was a period fighting between the local Mamluk rulers, Ottoman troops already in Egypt, and an Ottoman force dispatched to restore order under the control of Muhammad Ali Pasha. After four years of fighting, in 1805, Muhammad Ali seized power in Egypt. The Ottoman Empire recognized him as *wāli* – governor, but Muhammad Ali claimed the title of *khedive* – viceroy. He spent the next decades reforming Egypt's economy, through land seizures and state monopolies, and notably the introduction of cotton to the Nile Delta.

Muhammad Ali also conducted several military campaigns, capturing Hedjaz from the [Saudis](Nejd.md) in Arabia, conquering [Sudan](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Sudan), and supporting the Ottoman Empire in the [Greek](Greece.md) revolution, sending both troops and ships. The Egyptian navy was sunk, and Muhammad Ali requested the eyalet of [Syria](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Syria) from the Ottoman Empire as compensation. As the Ottoman Empire declined this request, in 1831 he prepared to capture the region by force. Following a swift campaign and a resounding defeat of the Ottoman army, [Russia](Russia.md), France, and [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) intervened and brokered peace, granting Muhammad Ali control of Syria, Crete, and Hedjaz, but not official recognition of independence.

Egypt also holds the Sinai state which is required for the construction of the [Suez Canal](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Suez_Canal).

### Diplomacy

Egypt rules over [Hedjaz](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Hedjaz) as a tributary. It starts as a protectorate of and has a mutual truce with the [Ottoman Empire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey) until 1840.

Egypt neighbors many smaller countries, including the Ottoman's puppet [Tripolitania](Tripolitania.md), which adds an additional front to any war with the Ottoman Empire.

Egypt starts with active interests in Anatolia, Balkans, Arabia, Ethiopia and Nile, and declared interests in Congo, Niger, North Africa and Zanj.

The Ottoman Empire has claims on all of Egypt's states in Anatolia and Arabia.

Egypt has a pro-country lobby () towards [France](France.md).

Egypt starts with a [treaty](Treaty.md) with [France](France.md), the *French Mission to Egypt*, where France provides Military assistance and transfers 10 small arms and 5 artillery. In exchange Egypt transfers 120 fabric and gives France investment rights. It is bound for 8 years.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**
  - Egypt starts with a moderate amount of industry, notably its Arms Industry, along with a smaller number of Textile Mills, Glassworks, Paper Mills, and Shipyards. Resource production is effectively none, as Egypt begins with only a small number of Logging Camps and no mines of any type. However, it has a fairly developed agricultural sector with several cash crop plantations, notably Cotton Plantations and Tobacco Plantations.

**Resources**
  - Egypt has access to coal, sulfur, iron, and lead, though none are being exploited in 1836 and the potential resources are relatively sparse for a country of Egypt's size. It also has the ability to grow many cash crops, as every type except rubber can be grown in at least one starting state, even opium and silk.

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.9. |

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Egypt
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
Egypt begins in a precarious, but powerful position. It has a small amount of industry and the capability for more; a large, professional, and expensive, army; and many routes for conquest. It also is as good as guaranteed to come to conflict with the [Ottoman Empire](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Turkey) over Syria and the Levant. However, with careful expansion of its industry, conquest of its neighbors and careful choice of a weak Great (or Major) Power to gain recognition from, Egypt can easily become one of the Great Powers.

### Economy

Egypt has a well-developed agricultural sector and the ability to grow many cash crops, exports of these cash crops can provide a much needed revenue source for further industrialization. For resources, iron, coal, and wood are the key goods to develop initially. While building a decent domestic production of wood is necessary, do not try to be self-sufficient in this resource, as Egypt's desert terrain cannot support many logging camps. Instead, sign a goods transfer [treaty](Treaty.md) with a country with excess wood (usually [Russia](Russia.md)) so you can continue expanding your construction sectors.

Enacting consumption taxes on tobacco should provide enough income to offset the starting deficit, but more steady income will be needed for the long-term.

Once iron, coal, and wood production has increased, tools and steel are the next priorities. From there, industrialization can continue in the usual manner, expanding industries whose outputs are expensive or in high demand. Textile Mills are a great option for Egypt, as it has access to plentiful fabric and silk. Your textile industry can be further boosted by two companies: the Misr Spinning and Weaving Company, which can produce the prestige fabric Mit Afifi, and MM. Nicolas Portalis et Cie., which will increase your silk output and reduce radicalism from your (likely sizable) discriminated population. Deposits of sulfur and iron in northern Egypt will also enable you to support fertilizer and explosives factories.

If you conquer Libya, Iraq and/or the Arabian peninsula, you will have ample access to oil once you research Pumpjacks. Control of Baghdad will allow you to found the Turkish Petroleum Company (which you should promptly rename to something more appropriate), further boosting your oil output and easily making you one of the world's top producers.

Be mindful of infrastructure limits as before Railways are available, most states outside the Nile Delta have almost none.

### Politics

Egypt begins with few progressive laws. Notably, it starts with Serfdom and Slave Trade, which gives a total **+100%** increase to the clout of the Landowners. Meanwhile, Monarchy and Hereditary Bureaucrats give a total of **+50%** political strength to Aristocrats. That said, Egypt's starting laws are not quite as awful as most other unrecognized powers, as you at least start with Professional Army, Agrarianism, and Per-Capita Taxation.

As the Industrialists start marginalized, it is difficult to form a government which supports any meaningful change at first. Use law commitment [treaties](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Diplomatic_Treaty) to help pass early, less controversial reforms, such as Appointed Bureaucrats and Legacy Slavery, to start weakening the Landowners. Make sure you switch the production methods of your Government Administration and University buildings to secular administration and academia, to boost the Intelligentsia. Deliberately agitating the Peasant Movement can also help you pass Tenant Farmers relatively early. Combined with steadily developing your industry and bureaucracy, these strategies should help you strengthen the Industrialists and Intelligentsia to the point that you can pass more substantial reforms in the mid-game.

Egypt has a large number of pops suffering from discrimination, due to both religious and cultural differences. Passing Cultural Exclusion and Total Separation will relieve your large Sudanese and Christian minorities, respectively, but this can be difficult even in the mid-late game. Suppressing the National Supremacist and Sunni Traditionalist movements will likely be necessary to pass these laws. In addition, the player needs to enact Slavery Banned to unlock the most tolerant citizenship laws, which will likely itself face a large stall chance from angry Landowners.

Passing a colonization law is a good early move. Egypt can expand southward immediately, though slowly before Quinine is researched.

### Diplomacy and military

Egypt's starting army is equal to most major powers and can even contend with some great powers. Making effective use of this army early on is key to succeeding.

Subjugating the Arabian minor powers is a good first move. Should Egypt become independent, the Ottoman Empire may also intervene, but this is to Egypt's advantage, as the Ottoman military is weaker than the Egyptian military early on. If the Ottomans join this or later diplomatic plays, focus on liberating nations and revoking their claims, in order to minimize infamy. Egypt must resist Ottoman attempts to reduce it to a puppet, as well as any territorial demands. Enforcing cheap war goals on the Ottomans should enable you to become recognized relatively easily. If (and usually when) the Ottomans fail the Tanzimat reforms, they will become an unrecognized power, allowing you to seize more of their territory without raising too much infamy.

Keep in mind that the [Trucial States](Trucial States.md) is a Protectorate of [Great Britain](Great Britain.md), so don't make a play against them directly. Maintaining good relations with the European great powers will help keep them out of your wars with the Ottoman Empire, particularly Great Britain, [Russia](Russia.md), and [Austria](Austria.md).

After liberating [Iraq](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Iraq) and [Bulgaria](Bulgaria.md), the Ottoman Empire should be weak enough to be easily defeated in every war and can be dismantled or annexed at your leisure.

The [Ethiopia](Ethiopia.md)n states can also be a target. Egypt can vassalize [Shewa](Shewa.md) and hope that they continue to conquer their neighbours, giving Egypt a more powerful vassal at no infamy cost for Egypt. In any case, no GP shares a land border with Egypt; Egypt should avoid expanding too aggressively and becoming Russia's neighbour.

In Arabia, annexing [Oman](Oman.md), before [Zanzibar](Zanzibar.md) becomes independent, will allow a foothold in another part of East Africa.

When expanding westwards in North Africa, consider grabbing both Spanish Al-Rif and Gibraltar for the lucrative Gibraltar toll. Unlike other tolls, the Gibraltar toll can only be collected when one power controls *both* ends. The Suez and Bab-el-Mandeb tolls will also enrich Egypt's coffers; the two waterways are also vital to link the Mediterranean to the Indian Ocean.
