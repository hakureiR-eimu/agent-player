**Mali** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in West Africa. Its flag is that of the short-lived Mali Federation.

Four of the seven core states are needed in order to form Mali

| **Kaarta** | Western Mali | Bambara | Animist | Principality | Unrecognized |
| **Segou** | Western Mali | Bambara | Animist | Principality | Unrecognized |

| **Futa Toro** | Senegal | Fulbe Wolof | Sunni | Principality | Unrecognized |
| **Massina** | Eastern Mali | Fulbe | Sunni | Principality | Unrecognized |

All of the above exist at game start, and none of them are releasable. If Mali is formed by a Sunni country, it retains Sunni as its state religion instead of changing to Animist.

Mali's core states comprise the homelands of the Bambara culture along with a few additional neighboring states. Eastern Mali holds the site of the Mosque of Djenne.

## Malian minors

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this section. It was last verified for version 1.10. |

The Malian minors are all unrecognized powers and have similar basic stats, starting technology, and politics. Each starts with states or split states in Mali's core states, except Borgu. They are all Principalities and most are Sunni. They generally start with the Landowners in government with a mix of the Armed Forces, Sunni Ulema, and either Intelligentsia or Rural Folk in opposition, and the remaining interest groups marginalized. None start with any historical characters.

They all start with the following technology and laws, except as noted below. Animist minors start with State Religion instead of People of the Book.

## Strategy and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.10. |

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Mali
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
[Massina](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Massina) dominates its competitors with its 1.2M population; Compared to [Futa Jallon](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Futa_Jallon) with 421K, [Kaarta](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Kaarta) with 240K, [Segou](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Segou) with 220K and [Futa Toro](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Futa_Toro) with 180K. It should be noted however, that Massina only has 20 battalions whilst the rest each begin with 10 at game start, meaning a surprise upset is not out of reach.

Surprisingly, these nations don't have much wood. Massina, Sengou and Kaarta can expand into Mandinka (Guinea) for wood. Massina can also expand into Kabadougou (Windward coast), Kong (Ivory Coast) and Kong Volta/Mossi (Volta).

For iron, Massina can go into Kabadougou (Windward Coast). Futa Jallon does not border any decentralized neighbors with Iron. Kaarta and Segou can colonize Oualata (Outer Mauritania) for iron. Futa Toro begins with Iron.

For coal, Segou and Massina can go into Adagh (Timbuktu).

### Foreign powers

Futa Toro and Futa Jallon have a border with [France](France.md) and British Sierra Leone, respectively. The British in Gambia means they will soon border with Futa Jallon, Futa Toro and potentially even Kaarta from there. France has another colony in the Ivory coast, of which Massina is closest to, but it is quite a distance away.

The USA has [Liberia](Liberia.md) as a protectorate in the same strategic region Mali is in (Senegal), but it does not share a land border with any nation immediately. Futa Jallon, Kaarta, Segou and Massina can colonise Madinka to border Liberia; Massina can also land border them through Kabadougou (gets them closer to the French Ivory Coast, though).

As such, European intervention is possible and may aid or hinder the recreation of Mali by conquest.
