**Danubian State** is a [formable country](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Formable_country) in Central Europe. It represents a union of the non-German peoples of the [Austria](Austria.md)n Empire and can be formed by any country below empire tier with one of the "Danubian" cultures (South German, Hungarian, Romanian, Croatian, Czech, Slovak, Slovene).

Most of its core states are held by Austria or its subjects at game start; others are held by Bavaria and South German minors as well as the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md) and its subjects.

With National Awakening, [Austria](Austria.md) may become the Danubian State by completing the journal entry *The Federal Solution*. It that case, the country will be known as the *United States of Greater Austria* while it stays a Monarchy, and will have cultures integrated while the journal entry was active as primary ones.

Danubian State can also form [Central Europe](Central Europe.md).[1]

## Historical background

Following the Austro-Hungarian Compromise of 1867, the Kingdom of Hungary regained its co-equal status with Austria, bringing Hungarians back to co-equal status with Germans; however, the many other minorities, primarily various Slavic peoples, did not receive the same benefit.

As ethnic tensions grew over the following decades, a group of scholars – supported by Archduke Franz Ferdinand – proposed restructuring Austria-Hungary into a federal state with autonomous states for each major ethnic group. This plan was never realized, and following World War I, Austria-Hungary was dissolved into several separate states, largely along ethnic lines.

id
The Danubian State
Rising from the failed Austrian Empire, the Danubian State is an equal union of the Hungarian, Romanian, Czech, Croatian, Slovene, and Slovak peoples.
---

Flavor text:
"Good people of the Danube, of homes far and near, my brothers: I see in you the hearts of a thousand scorned women and men with nothing to lose. Indeed, our once reigning overlords had set us down a path with which we lost our way. Our path to greatness in history. We were once holding the knife by the blade, bloodying ourselves for another's gain. No more. No more! This day, we dictate our own path. Our future. Glory awaits!"
