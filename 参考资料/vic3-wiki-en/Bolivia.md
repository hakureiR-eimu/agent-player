*"Peru and Bolivia are close to the creation of a new confederation, but can the two countries come to an agreement? Will such a union be formed?"*

**Bolivia** is a minor power in South America.

As the leader of the Peru-Bolivian Confederation, Bolivia is one of the strongest countries in South America on game start. However, its infamous reputation makes it a target for all its neighbors, and the Confederation's survival is not certain.

## Formation

If Bolivia ceases to exist, it can be released by any country that controls La Paz. Bolivia's core states are La Paz, Santa Cruz, Potosí, Acre, and Antofagasta.

## Scripted content

Main article:
South America content
Bolivia shares several journal entries and events with its South American neighbors. These include journal entries related to Caudillismo, European immigration, and changing national culture.

Bolivia's main early game content revolves around the [The Peru-Bolivian Confederation](South America content.md) journal entry, through which Bolivia may form [Peru-Bolivia](Peru-Bolivia.md). To do so, however, Bolivia must reign in their Peruvian subjects of [North Peru](North Peru.md), [South Peru](South Peru.md), and [Iquicha](Iquicha.md), and defend against the attempts of other countries to prevent the unification of the Confederation - mainly [Chile](Chile.md) and [Argentina](Argentina.md).

### Interest groups

Bolivia begins with the Armed Forces in government. The Landowners, Catholic Church, Intelligentsia, Petite Bourgeoisie, and Rural Folk are in opposition; the Industrialists and Trade Unions are marginalized.

The Armed Forces have the Caudillismo ideology in addition to their standard ideologies. The Landowners have the Elitist ideology instead of the default Paternalistic.

### Diplomacy

Bolivia starts with [North Peru](North Peru.md), [South Peru](South Peru.md), and [Iquicha](Iquicha.md) as puppets.

Bolivia has **50** infamy at the start of the game.

Bolivia borders [Chile](Chile.md), [Argentina](Argentina.md) and [Guarani](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Guarani_(GNI)) to the south, its puppets [South Peru](South Peru.md) and [North Peru](North Peru.md) to the west, the rebel state of [Grão-Pará](Grão-Pará.md) to the north and [Brazil](Brazil.md) to the east.

Bolivia has cold relations with [Chile](Chile.md) and poor relations with [Spain](Spain.md) and [Argentina](Argentina.md). It will also likely start with negative relations with most of its neighbors, due to its high level of infamy. It has Amicable relations with its puppet [Iquicha](Iquicha.md) and cordial relations with its puppet [South Peru](South Peru.md). Bolivia also starts with the modifier *Independence Not Recognized by Spain Independence Not Recognized by Spain Country modifier −10% Influence*, until [Spain](Spain.md) recognizes its independence, or Bolivia becomes strong enough to disregard Spain.

[Chile](Chile.md) has a claim claim on the Bolivian state of Antofagasta, and [Argentina](Argentina.md) has a claim on the Bolivian half of the split state of Jujuy.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Bolivia has only two industrial buildings at the start of the game: a Tooling Workshop and a Furniture Manufactury, both in the state of La Paz.

**Resources**

Despite having many states, especially compared to its neighbors, Bolivia lacks any access to coal early on - although it is available in the market thanks to its subject [North Peru](North Peru.md). Annexation of its Peruvian subjects through the formation of [Peru-Bolivia](Peru-Bolivia.md) will give access to almost any industrial resource that may be needed.

Bolivia also begins with 6 Gold Mines, with the potential for more - especially after forming [Peru-Bolivia](Peru-Bolivia.md) and if conquering [Chile](Chile.md) and [Argentina](Argentina.md). However, its states lack any meaningful state traits early on, and most Peruvian states even have the negative *Andes* trait, reducing Infrastructure and construction efficiency.

In terms of agricultural potential, La Paz and Santa Cruz can build a very large amount of Coffee Plantations and Tobacco Plantations, but again, lack powerful traits to give Bolivia an edge over its competitors like [Brazil](Brazil.md).

## Strategies and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.11. |

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Bolivia
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
Bolivia starts the game with three puppets: [North Peru](North Peru.md), [Iquicha](Iquicha.md) and [South Peru](South Peru.md), and **50** [infamy](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Infamy). If Bolivia maintains control over at least one puppet and its government has at least 50 legitimacy for 10 years, the Peru-Bolivian Confederation will unify into a single country, [Peru-Bolivia](Peru-Bolivia.md). Due to the starting high infamy, it is best for the confederation to concentrate on developing its economy and only engage in warfare which will not increase its infamy. Improving relations with the puppets, especially [South Peru](South Peru.md), is important as they start with bad relations. Bolivia has more budget than it knows how to spend anyway. Therefore, lowering taxes and increasing military wages to increase both [SoL](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/SoL), legitimacy and armed forces approval. is a priority. Lowering subject payments can also be considered.

Bolivia also starts with a Bureaucracy deficit, but this is not actually a concern, since tax waste doesn't really do anything. The bureaucracy is due to the 12 battalion army having 3 generals, but one of them will disappear very quickly due to scripted events, solving the issue. And Bolivia doesn't need any more budget anyway.

### Early defense

It is very likely that either [Chile](Chile.md) and/or [Argentina](Argentina.md) will attack Bolivia within the first few years due to the claims on Antofagasta and Jujuy respectively, so be prepared for an early defensive war. Bolivia starts with a deficit of small arms, and they are needed to defend yourself. And iron is needed to produce small arms. Those are good bets for 2 starting buildings. Alternatively, a [treaty](Treaty.md) with [America](America.md) to import small arms, might be possible. Getting a trade center to mitigate the issue is also possible, but a bit more unstable than a domestic production, due to the only coastal state, Antofagasta, is the very state Chile will attack Bolivia for, and where the frontline will be.

If the prospect looks truly grim, luring [Brazil](Brazil.md) in with giving them Mato Grosso or Amazonia is possible. Those states are not worth a lot anyway. If Brazil starts on day 1 with a positive attitude, you may even be able to sway them into an alliance this way.

If you prioritize safety over all else, you can cede contested territories to North and South Peru: Acre and Bolivian Amazonas go to North Peru via the Grant State option, then use a treaty to give Antofagasta to South Peru and grant them Jujuy once the treaty goes into force. As for Mato Grosso, you can try to trade it for whatever you can get out of Brazil. This will force you to play tall, which will be rather annoying since Bolivia has negative construction modifiers from the Andes, but it almost ensures your territorial integrity up until forming Peru-Bolivia.

### Dealing with Brazil

Once the game is unpaused, Bolivia could start a diplomatic play liberating nations from Brazil: [Minas Gerais](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Minas_Gerais), [Confederation of the Equator](Confederation of the Equator.md), and [Paulistânia](Paulistânia.md) are all good options. This also adds to the possibility of [Grão-Pará](Grão-Pará.md) and [Piratini](Piratini.md) succeeding in their rebellions. Even if Brazil is successful in quelling the revolts, Bolivia shouldn't have a problem winning the war. Brazil should remain Bolivia's target until its GDP and army no longer threatens Bolivia.

Alternatively, Bolivia could simply sell the contested states for either [obligations](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Obligation) or money.

### Expansion targets

As long as the great powers don't intervene and Brazil has been dismembered, Bolivia should have little difficulty annexing states from [Chile](Chile.md) and [Argentina](Argentina.md). Once [Paraguay](Paraguay.md) and [Uruguay](Uruguay.md) are in range, they should be annexed once they are built up to near infrastructure limits. Forming [Federation of the Andes](Federation of the Andes.md) is a viable option.
