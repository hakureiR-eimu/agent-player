**Azal** (called **Qasimid State** at the start) is a landlocked unrecognized power in the Arabian Peninsula. It can form [Yemen](Yemen.md) and [Arabia](Arabia.md).

### Interest groups

Azal starts with the Landowners and Shi'a Ulema in government. The Rural Folk and Armed Forces are in opposition, while the Petite Bourgeoisie, Intelligentsia, Trade Unions, and Industrialists are marginalized.

### Industry and resources

**Industry**

Azal has no industry, and only a small number of agricultural buildings.

**Resources**

The state of Yemen lacks most resources, having only a small amount of lead and, later, oil.

## Strategy and guides

| ![Ambox outdated info.png](https://central.paradoxwikis.com/images/thumb/6/65/Ambox_outdated_info.png/22px-Ambox_outdated_info.png) | Please help with verifying or updating this guide. It was last verified for version 1.9. |
|---|---|

The below is
one of many
player suggested strategies for
Azal
. Bear in mind, due to the dynamic nature of the game, it may unfold differently for other players.
**Starting Situation**

Azal is unique among the Arabian minors in that it actually has a significant population at the start of the game. This gives it much more potential for vertical development than the other Arabian minors. Its military isn't good, but superior to almost all of its immediate neighbors, setting it up for early conquest.

**Grand Strategy**

Azal's formable is Arabia. There will be three main obstacles:

- [Hedjaz](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Hedjaz), who is a Tributary under [Egypt](Egypt.md) at game start. The Egyptian army is far larger at game start than any of the Arabian minors.
- [Trucial States](Trucial States.md), who are a Protectorate under [Great Britain](Great Britain.md) at game start.
- [Oman](Oman.md), who is relatively modernized as far as Arabian minors go.

The most immediate path forward is to conquer the other minors in the Yemen region. All of these are very small, but this will provide port access which Azal needs. Also, as Yemen is a split state, they will be incorporated states immediately. After that, the best targets of will probably be Oman or Nejd. Nejd won't be too difficult as long as the player has a land border (watch for impassable terrain). Alternatively, attacking Hejaz will be more tricky, but a player may be able to pull it off solo if Egypt is busy with the Ottomans or has outside help. Hejaz will allow the player to fully consolidate the Yemen territory.

There will be the [Ottoman Empire](Ottoman Empire.md) to the north, but they will probably be too busy with internal disfunction and grudge matches with Egypt to be a threat. Eventually, the player may want to expand into the Ottoman Empire or Egypt for land and resources, but both Anatolia and Egypt have a lot more population than Arabia does, so this may be difficult without outside help or a tech advantage.

Once the Arabian peninsula is more or less united, the player will have to choose where to go next. As population is an issue, conquest is probably a better idea than [Colonization](Colonization.md). Good options include:

- The Horn of Africa strategic region. It's close, and most of the nations here are very small and weak. The player may want the [Quinine](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Quinine) technology prior to going into this region due to [Malaria](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Malaria).
- Southeast Asia. Southeast Asia has a grab bag of weak nations and uncolonized land, but much of it is controlled by the [Netherlands](Netherlands.md) through the [Dutch East Indies](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Dutch_East_Indies).
- Central Asia. It's close, and also has small and weak nations, and the player may have a foothold already from the conquest of Oman. However, it likely won't have as many nations to grab up as the Horn of Africa.

**Internal Politics**

Azal's initial set of laws aren't great, but aren't uniquely bad. As per many minor nations, the primary issue will be getting the [Landowners](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Landowners) out of power and passing better [Laws](Laws.md).

**Diplomacy**

An Azal player's primary focus will be keeping the European majors out of the Arabian peninsula, if at all possible. The best means to do so is to maintain good relations with the colonial powers (Britain and [France](France.md)) and conquer the area directly. After that, it will be keeping the European majors from attacking them, which shouldn't be hard as long as relations are high. Natural [rivals](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Rivalry) are Egypt and the Ottoman Empire, but the player should avoid rivaling both at the same time.

**Potential**

If the player can consolidate Arabia and hold out until the discover [oil](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Oil), they can rake in the money. If the player can push into Anatolia and Egypt, they can become competitive with the European majors.

**Weaknesses**

While it starts off on much better footing than most of the Arabian minors do, Azal will eventually run out of labor to staff its industries and armies. The Arabian Peninsula, as a whole, is rather resource-poor and starts the game lightly populated. Next, Azal is Shitte rather than Sunni, which can create some issues with radicals. An Azal player will probably be forced to expand for population (rather than land or resources) in the late game. [immigration](https://vic3.paradoxwikis.com/Immigration) and birthrate boosts may partially cover for this, but economic expansion tends to outpace these factors. Azal's technology isn't great, but it has the population and resources to cover for this.
